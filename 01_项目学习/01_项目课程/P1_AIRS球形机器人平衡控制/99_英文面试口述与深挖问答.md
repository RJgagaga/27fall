## 一、口述

### 1.1 30 秒

> At AIRS I worked on balance control for a three-omni-wheel ballbot with a dual-arm upper body. I built a cascaded controller, with a position loop giving bounded tilt references to an attitude loop, allocated to the wheels through the kinematics, and tuned it on the robot. I also derived a linearised inverted-pendulum model and used LQR to analyse the tuning, added a centre-of-mass feedforward from the URDF, and implemented the safety loop: initialisation, output limits and fall handling.

中文：我在 AIRS 做三全向轮 ballbot（带双臂）的平衡控制。做了级联控制器：位置外环给出有界倾角参考、姿态内环跟踪、经运动学分配到三轮，并在实机整定；同时推导线性化倒立摆并用 LQR 分析整定；用 URDF 做质心前馈；实现了初始化、限幅、跌倒处理的安全闭环。

**关键词提示：** cascaded / bounded tilt reference / LQR as analysis / CoM feedforward / safety loop。**易说错点：** 不要说 "deployed LQR"；不要说 "I wrote the IMU filter"。

### 1.2 90 秒

> My internship at AIRS, from September 2025 to March 2026, was on a ballbot: a robot balancing on one ball, driven by three omni wheels, with two arms on top. The hard part is that it is unstable by itself, and when the arms move the centre of mass moves too.
>
> I was responsible for balance control. I used the IMU and wheel-speed feedback in a cascade: a position outer loop produces a bounded tilt reference, an attitude inner loop follows it using the angle and the gyro rate, and the output is allocated to the three wheels through the ball-wheel kinematics. I deployed and tuned it on the real robot at a hundred hertz.
>
> When tuning alone could not explain the behaviour, I went back to a model: a four-state linearised inverted pendulum, with LQR to study how the state weights trade stability against control effort. That became our reference for reading the deployed gains and for finding state anomalies and actuator saturation.
>
> For the arms, I estimated the centre-of-mass offset from the joint angles with Pinocchio and the URDF, fed a gravity feedforward into roll and pitch, and checked it by switching it on and off under fixed arm trajectories. Finally I made the loop safe: state initialisation, output limiting and fall detection.

中文：2025.09–2026.03 在 AIRS 做 ballbot：单球、三全向轮、双臂；难点是本身不稳定且手臂动会移动质心。我负责平衡控制：IMU 与轮速反馈、级联（有界倾角参考、角度与陀螺内环、经运动学分配三轮）、100 Hz 实机整定；整定解释不了行为时回到模型，用四状态线性倒立摆和 LQR 研究权重折中，作为读增益、找状态异常与执行器饱和的参照；臂用 Pinocchio+URDF 估质心偏移、做 roll/pitch 前馈并以开/关对比验证；最后做安全闭环。

**关键词提示：** one ball / unstable by itself / cascade / back to a model / on-off verification / safe loop。**易说错点：** "100 Hz" 是控制主循环，不是 IMU 以外的所有环节；"deployed" 只用于级联 PID。

### 1.3 3 分钟（在 90 秒基础上加深）

> （接 1.2）Let me add three details. First, why a cascade: to accelerate, a ballbot has to lean, so the tilt angle is effectively an acceleration request. The outer loop turns position and velocity errors into a tilt reference, bounded at 0.1 radians so that the acceleration stays in the range the linear model and the motors can handle; the inner loop uses the gyro rate as damping. The loops are separated in bandwidth: roughly 0.15 hertz outside, 1 hertz inside, and the motor driver's own speed loop at around 20 hertz.
>
> Second, the model work. I derived the equations with the Lagrange method, linearised around upright, and got a four-state model. It is non-minimum phase: to lean forward, the ball first has to move back, which limits how aggressive the position loop can be. With LQR I swept the weights and saw how a higher tilt weight raises the gains and the peak torque. I expanded the cascade into an equivalent state feedback to compare it with the LQR gains. I did not deploy LQR; it was an analysis tool, and it guided two concrete decisions: how much damping the inner loop needed, and how large the tilt reference could be before the wheels hit their limit.
>
> Third, the feedforward and safety. The arm pose shifts the true balance point, so the feedforward effectively moves the attitude set-point. I verified it by switching it on and off with identical arm trajectories, five runs each. For safety, the controller starts disabled, waits for valid sensor data, captures offsets with the robot held still, resets integrators and clears the references, clamps its outputs, and detects excessive tilt: half a radian for five consecutive cycles. The result: the chassis stayed balanced while both arms moved along their trajectories, which was the target my mentor set.

中文：补三点：①为何级联——加速要前倾，倾角即加速度请求；外环把位置/速度误差变成有界倾角参考（0.1 rad）；内环用陀螺做阻尼；带宽分离约 0.15 Hz / 1 Hz / 电机速度环 20 Hz。②模型——拉格朗日推导、线性化、四状态、非最小相位；LQR 扫权重、把级联展开为等效状态反馈比较；未上机，只作分析，指导了两个决定（内环阻尼、参考上限）。③前馈与安全——前馈等价于平移姿态设定点，同轨迹开/关各 5 次验证；安全闭环：默认失能、等有效数据、扶稳采偏置、清状态、限幅、0.5 rad 连续 5 周期判跌倒；结果：双臂按固定轨迹运动时底盘保持动态平衡。

**关键词提示：** acceleration request / non-minimum phase / two decisions / set-point shift / five runs each。**易说错点：** 不要说 "the numbers are from the teaching model" 当成实机；说 "decisions it guided" 而不是 "LQR gains we used"。

## 二、问答（L1 / L2 / L3，每段后附中文）

### A. 项目总览与职责边界

**Q1. Tell me about the project in one sentence.**

- **L1** — "I developed and tuned the balance controller of a three-omni-wheel ballbot with two arms: a cascaded PID with bounded tilt references, a model-based analysis with LQR, a centre-of-mass feedforward, and the safety loop that connects ROS to CAN motors."
  中文：我开发并整定了带双臂的三全向轮 ballbot 平衡控制器：有界倾角参考的级联 PID、LQR 模型分析、质心前馈，以及连接 ROS 与 CAN 电机的安全闭环。
- **L2** — *Which part was hardest?* — "Getting the interplay between the loops right, because a ballbot moves backwards first when you ask it to move forward, and the arm motion shifts the balance point."
  中文：最难的是各环的配合，因为向前走时它先后退，臂动还会移动平衡点。
- **L3** — *What would you do differently?* — "Log more from day one and automate the regression run, so every parameter change gets the same set of tests."
  中文：从第一天起多记录并把回归自动化，让每次改参数都跑同一套测试。

**Q2. What is a ballbot and why is it hard?**

- **L1** — "It balances on a single ball driven by three omni wheels. It is an inverted pendulum with one contact point and no passive support, so it must be actively stabilised in two tilt directions at once, and it can move in any direction."
  中文：单球、三全向轮驱动；是单接触点、无被动支撑的倒立摆，需同时在两个倾斜方向主动稳定，且可向任意方向运动。
- **L2** — *How fast does it fall?* — "Like e to the lambda t. For my teaching model lambda is about 2.6 per second, and for the real robot's order of magnitude about 4, so the tilt doubles roughly every 0.17 to 0.27 seconds."
  中文：按 $e^{\lambda t}$ 增长；教学模型 λ≈2.6/s，实机量级约 4/s，倾角约每 0.17～0.27 s 翻倍。
- **L3** — *What does that imply for your loop?* — "The total sensor-to-motor delay has to stay well below the inverse of lambda, so I kept it around 15 to 25 milliseconds and did not filter the fast path."
  中文：传感器到电机的总延迟要远小于 1/λ，所以控制在 15～25 ms，快路径不滤波。

**Q3. What exactly was your responsibility?**

- **L1** — "Balance control development and on-robot tuning, including the safety logic. The arm drivers, the hands and the mechanical design belonged to other people on the team."
  中文：平衡控制开发与实机整定，含安全逻辑；臂驱动、手和机械设计属于团队其他人。
- **L2** — *Which parts of the code did you write?* — "The balance controller side and the centre-of-mass node; the IMU driver is the vendor's package and the arm motor drivers came from the team."
  中文：平衡控制器侧和质心节点；IMU 驱动是厂商包，臂电机驱动来自团队。
- **L3** — *How did you draw that line with your mentor?* — "He gave the target, balance while the arms move on fixed trajectories, and I owned the controller; for anything on the arm or hand side I asked and agreed on interfaces first."
  中文：他给目标（臂按固定轨迹运动时保持平衡），我负责控制器；涉及臂和手的部分先问清并约定接口。

**Q4. How did you know it worked?**

- **L1** — "The chassis kept dynamic balance while both arms executed fixed trajectories, which was the objective set by my mentor. For the feedforward I compared on and off runs with the same trajectory, five runs each. Off, the peak tilt was about 0.06 radians, around 3.4 degrees. On, it dropped to about 0.026."
  中文：双臂按固定轨迹运动时底盘保持动态平衡（导师设定的目标）；前馈用同一轨迹开/关各 5 次对比：关时倾角峰值约 0.06 rad（3.4°），开时降到约 0.026。
- **L2** — *Which metrics?* — "Peak and RMS tilt, drift over 30 seconds, and the fraction of time the wheel commands sat at the limit. RMS went from 0.022 to 0.010, drift from about 0.30 to 0.10 metres, saturation from about 6 to 1 percent."
  中文：峰值与 RMS 倾角、30 s 漂移、命令贴限幅占比：RMS 0.022→0.010，漂移 0.30→0.10 m，饱和 6%→1%。
- **L3** — *How confident are you in those numbers?* — "They are from my own logs and a small sample, so I treat them as a clear direction, not a statistical claim."
  中文：来自自己的日志、样本较小，视为明确的趋势而不是统计结论。

**Q5. What were the main contributions in your own words?**

- **L1** — "Four: the cascade that kept it balanced, the model analysis that explained the gains and the saturation, the centre-of-mass feedforward for arm motion, and the safety loop that made repeated testing safe."
  中文：四项：保持平衡的级联、解释增益与饱和的模型分析、应对臂动的质心前馈、使反复测试安全的安全闭环。
- **L2** — *Rank them.* — "For the robot working, the cascade and the safety loop; for my own learning, the model analysis."
  中文：对机器人能工作，级联与安全闭环最关键；对我自己的成长，模型分析最重要。
- **L3** — *Which would you say is research-like?* — "The analysis: it turned tuning by feel into something I could predict and check."
  中文：分析部分——把凭感觉整定变成可预测、可检验。

**Q6. How long did it take and what were the milestones?**

- **L1** — "About six months. First sign and unit checks and a lifted-robot test, then the inner loop, then the outer loop with bounds, then the safety loop, then the model analysis, then the feedforward and arm trajectories."
  中文：约半年：先符号单位检查与悬空测试，再内环、带界外环、安全闭环、模型分析，最后前馈与臂轨迹。
- **L2** — *Where did you lose the most time?* — "On state problems such as the tilt offset and re-enable behaviour, not on gain tuning."
  中文：时间主要花在状态问题（倾角偏置、重新使能行为）而不是增益。
- **L3** — *What would you front-load next time?* — "The enable and initialisation logic, because it affects every later test."
  中文：把使能与初始化逻辑前置，因为它影响之后每次测试。

### B. 运动学

**Q7. How do you map the desired ball motion to the three wheels?**

- **L1** — "Each omni wheel presses on the ball at a known point and can drive the ball along its own tangential direction. The ball's surface velocity at the contact is omega cross r; I project it on the drive direction and divide by the wheel radius. Stacking the three wheels gives a three-by-three matrix J. Each row is the wheel's axle direction scaled by R over r."
  中文：每个轮在已知点压球，只能沿自己的切线方向驱动；接触点速度 ω×r 投影到驱动方向再除以轮半径；三轮叠成 3×3 的 J，每行是轮轴方向乘 R/r。
- **L2** — *When is J singular?* — "At contact latitude zero and ninety degrees. In between, the condition number tells how uneven the directions are; it equals one at about 54.7 degrees, where the axle vectors are orthogonal."
  中文：接触纬度 0° 与 90° 奇异；其间用条件数衡量各向异性，约 54.7° 时三轴正交、条件数为 1。
- **L3** — *What is yours?* — "About 45 degrees with the wheels 120 degrees apart; ball radius 0.2 metres, wheel radius 0.05, so the ratio is 4 and the condition number about 1.4."
  中文：约 45°、三轮隔 120°；球半径 0.2 m、轮半径 0.05，比值 4，条件数约 1.4。

**Q8. Velocity map versus torque map?**

- **L1** — "Velocities map with J. Torques map with J inverse transpose, by power conservation: wheel power equals ball power. I must never feed a torque into a velocity map."
  中文：速度走 J，力矩走 J 逆转置（功率守恒）；绝不能把力矩直接送进速度映射。
- **L2** — *What did your controller output?* — "A virtual command per axis, which I treat as a velocity-type command to the wheels; the motor driver's own speed loop turns it into current."
  中文：每轴一个虚拟量，按速度类命令发给轮子；电机驱动器的速度环把它变成电流。
- **L3** — *So is it a torque in newton-metres?* — "No. The variable names say torque, but the scaling is an empirical tuning gain, so I never quote it as a physical torque."
  中文：不是。变量叫 torque 但缩放是整定系数，所以不当作物理力矩来讲。

**Q9. How do you estimate the robot's speed?**

- **L1** — "Wheel speeds, inverse of J, give the ball's rate relative to the body; v equals R times omega; rotate by yaw and integrate. It's a local estimate: slip, body rotation and drift matter."
  中文：轮速经 J 逆得球相对机体角速度；v=Rω；按 yaw 旋转并积分；是局部估计，需考虑打滑、机体转动和漂移。
- **L2** — *Why does body rotation matter?* — "Wheels measure relative to the body, so I should add the body's gyro rate; a 0.2 radians-per-second pitch rate on 3 radians per second is about 6 percent."
  中文：轮子测相对机体，应加机体陀螺速率；3 rad/s 上叠 0.2 rad/s 约 6%。
- **L3** — *How much does it drift?* — "At rest about 5 centimetres per minute; walking, 3 to 5 percent of distance; turns and slips are worse. There is no external localisation."
  中文：静止约 5 cm/分；行走 3%～5% 路程；转弯打滑更差；无外部定位。

**Q10. Clip each wheel or scale the vector?**

- **L1** — "Scale the whole vector. The map is linear, so scaling preserves the direction of the ball's motion; clipping individual wheels changes the ratio and therefore the direction."
  中文：整体缩放；映射线性，缩放保持方向；逐轮截断改变比例和方向。
- **L2** — *Show a number.* — "Wheel command 9, 3, minus 6 with a limit of 7: clipping gives 7, 3, minus 6; scaling by 0.624 gives 5.6, 1.9, minus 3.7."
  中文：命令 [9, 3, −6]、上限 7：截断得 [7, 3, −6]；缩放 0.624 得 [5.6, 1.9, −3.7]。
- **L3** — *Downside of scaling?* — "One wheel at its limit slows all three; that's why I monitor the saturation fraction."
  中文：一个轮到限会让三个都变慢；所以监控饱和占比。

**Q11. How did you check the kinematics before powering the robot?**

- **L1** — "Single-axis tests with the ball lifted: pure yaw gives three equal wheel speeds; pure forward rolling leaves one wheel still and the other two equal and opposite; feeding the wheel speeds back through the inverse recovers the input. This catches unit, order and sign errors."
  中文：球离地做单轴测试：纯偏航三轮同速；纯前滚一个轮不转、另两个等速反向；把轮速代回逆矩阵应复原输入；可查出单位、轮序、符号错误。
- **L2** — *What did it catch?* — "The usual suspects: degrees given to a sine function, and the wheel order."
  中文：常见错误：把度数传给 sin、轮序不对。
- **L3** — *Why not trust the CAD?* — "Motor positive direction and wiring are not in the CAD, and the test takes five minutes."
  中文：电机正方向和接线不在 CAD 里，而测试只要 5 分钟。

**Q12. What is the maximum speed?**

- **L1** — "The kinematic limit from the wheel speed is about 1.6 metres per second, but I set the operational limit at 0.6 metres per second and about 0.5 metres per second squared, to keep the tilt reference and torque in range."
  中文：按轮速运动学上限约 1.6 m/s；实际限制为 0.6 m/s、约 0.5 m/s²，使倾角参考与力矩在范围内。
- **L2** — *How does the acceleration limit relate to tilt?* — "A bounded tilt gives a bounded acceleration: roughly the tilt bound times g over one plus R over l. For 0.1 radians that's about 0.8 metres per second squared at the limit."
  中文：有界倾角对应有界加速度：约 θmax·g/(1+R/l)；0.1 rad 时极限约 0.8 m/s²。
- **L3** — *Why is your limit lower?* — "Margin for the arms and for pushes; the bound is not meant to be used continuously."
  中文：为臂动与推扰留裕度；这个界不是用来长期顶着的。

### C. IMU 与状态

**Q13. Which signals do you use from the IMU?**

- **L1** — "Roll and pitch angles for the posture error, and the gyro rates for damping. The angle says how far we are from upright; the gyro says whether we are falling faster or recovering."
  中文：roll/pitch 做偏差，陀螺做阻尼；角度告诉偏离多少，陀螺告诉在加速倒下还是在恢复。
- **L2** — *Why not differentiate the angle?* — "The gyro is cleaner and has less delay."
  中文：陀螺更干净、延迟更小。
- **L3** — *Is the gyro exactly the derivative of roll?* — "Only for small angles; at the tilts we work in, below about 0.15 radians, it's a very good approximation."
  中文：仅小角度成立；在小于约 0.15 rad 的工作范围内近似很好。

**Q14. Who does the sensor fusion?**

- **L1** — "The module's onboard AHRS gives the orientation. My work was around it: checking validity, frame conventions, and offsets. I did not write my own attitude filter in this project; the only filtering I added was a light low-pass on the gyro, around 25 hertz, and a slower one on the wheel-speed velocity estimate."
  中文：姿态由模块板载 AHRS 给出；我围绕它做有效性、坐标约定、偏置；本项目没有自己写姿态滤波，只加了陀螺约 25 Hz 轻度低通和轮速速度估计的慢滤波。
- **L2** — *Where did you do fusion yourself?* — "In a competition project with a Mahony-type filter; that is a different project."
  中文：在竞赛项目里用过 Mahony 类滤波，那是另一个项目。
- **L3** — *What does the module's AHRS do about gyro bias?* — "It estimates it internally; I rely on that and handle only the mounting offset."
  中文：模块内部估计零偏；我依赖它，只处理安装偏置。

**Q15. How do you handle IMU zero offset?**

- **L1** — "I capture roll and pitch at enable, with the robot held upright and still, and subtract them; otherwise it balances around a wrong zero and drifts."
  中文：使能时在扶稳静止状态下采 roll/pitch 并扣除；否则围绕错误零点平衡并漂移。
- **L2** — *Quantify.* — "In my model the drift is the offset divided by the outer position gain: with 0.08 radians per metre, one degree gives about 0.22 metres. I average 200 samples, about two seconds, and discard the batch if the gyro reads above 0.05 radians per second."
  中文：漂移=偏置/外环位置增益：0.08 rad/m 下 1° 约 0.22 m；采 200 帧（约 2 s），陀螺超 0.05 rad/s 则丢弃重采。
- **L3** — *On re-enable?* — "Every enable re-captures it, because the robot may have been moved or the last capture may have been taken while it wobbled."
  中文：每次使能都重采，因为位置可能变了，或上次采集时在晃。

**Q16. Does filtering matter?**

- **L1** — "Yes, every low-pass adds delay. For a first-order filter the delay is about its time constant. Under one hertz of cutoff that's a fraction of a second, in a loop whose unstable pole is a few radians per second. So the gyro gets only a light filter around 25 hertz and the angle none; the only heavy filter, 0.8 hertz, sits on the slow wheel-speed velocity used by the outer loop."
  中文：每个低通都加延迟，一阶约一个时间常数；1 Hz 以下是零点几秒；所以陀螺只轻滤（约 25 Hz）、角度不滤，唯一重滤波（0.8 Hz）在外环用的慢速度估计上。
- **L2** — *Why is 0.8 hertz acceptable there?* — "The outer loop bandwidth is about 0.15 hertz, so the cutoff is about five times above it; the cost is roughly 0.2 seconds of delay."
  中文：外环带宽约 0.15 Hz，截止约为其 5 倍；代价约 0.2 s 延迟。
- **L3** — *What would happen if you filtered the gyro at 0.8 hertz?* — "In my teaching model the loop fails at about 0.12 to 0.14 seconds of delay, so a 0.2 second lag would destabilise it."
  中文：教学模型约 0.12～0.14 s 延迟失稳，0.2 s 滞后会导致失稳。

**Q17. How do you make sure the data are valid?**

- **L1** — "At the driver, CRC checks on the header and the data plus an end byte; in the controller, message age under 50 milliseconds for the IMU and wheels, quaternion norm near one, no NaNs, and all sources present before enabling."
  中文：驱动里头 CRC、数据 CRC 加帧尾；控制器里 IMU/轮反馈年龄 <50 ms、四元数模长≈1、无 NaN、使能前所有来源齐全。
- **L2** — *Is the message time the sample time?* — "No. In the driver I read, the stamp is the receive time, and the orientation can be one packet older than the gyro."
  中文：不是。驱动里时间戳是接收时刻，姿态可能比陀螺旧一个包。
- **L3** — *Impact at 100 hertz?* — "Up to about ten milliseconds of staleness on the orientation, small against the 25 millisecond worst-case budget, but worth knowing."
  中文：姿态最多旧约 10 ms，相对 25 ms 最坏预算较小，但要心里有数。

### D. 建模与 LQR

**Q18. Walk me through the model.**

- **L1** — "A planar inverted pendulum on a ball: states are ball position, ball velocity, body tilt and tilt rate. I wrote the kinetic and potential energy, used the Lagrange equations with the motor torque and its reaction on the ball, linearised around upright, and got a four-by-four A and a four-by-one B."
  中文：球上的平面倒立摆；状态为球位置、速度、机体倾角、倾角速度；写动能势能，用拉格朗日方程（含电机力矩及其对球的反作用），在竖直附近线性化得 4×4 的 A 与 4×1 的 B。
- **L2** — *Why is the reaction torque important?* — "Without it the second equation loses a term and the zeros and gains are wrong."
  中文：缺少它第二个方程会少一项，零点和增益都不对。
- **L3** — *Why a planar model for a 3D robot?* — "Roll and pitch are nearly decoupled for small tilts, and the planar model keeps the physics explainable."
  中文：小倾角下 roll/pitch 近似解耦，平面模型便于解释物理。

**Q19. Why linearise and when does it fail?**

- **L1** — "Linearisation gives me standard tools: poles, LQR, sensitivity. It's valid for small tilts, roughly below 0.2 radians. Beyond that, or when actuators saturate, the guarantees are gone."
  中文：线性化给出极点、LQR、灵敏度等标准工具；小倾角（约 0.2 rad 内）有效；超出或饱和时保证失效。
- **L2** — *What do you do about it?* — "Bound the tilt reference at 0.1 radians and check the design in a nonlinear simulation."
  中文：把倾角参考限在 0.1 rad，并用非线性仿真验证。
- **L3** — *What did the nonlinear check show?* — "The same trends; the maximum recoverable tilt for a given torque limit comes out about 0.11 times the limit in my teaching units."
  中文：趋势一致；给定力矩上限的最大可恢复倾角约为上限的 0.11 倍（教学单位）。

**Q20. What is non-minimum phase here?**

- **L1** — "The torque-to-position transfer function has a right-half-plane zero. To lean forward the ball first moves back, so the response starts in the wrong direction. It limits the achievable bandwidth of the position loop."
  中文：力矩到位置的传递函数有右半平面零点；前倾需球先后退，响应先反向；限制位置环可达带宽。
- **L2** — *How big is the undershoot?* — "In the teaching simulation, about 6 centimetres with a 0.1 radian tilt bound and 1.3 centimetres with 0.02."
  中文：教学仿真中，参考上限 0.1 rad 约 6 cm，0.02 rad 约 1.3 cm。
- **L3** — *So what is the trade-off?* — "A larger bound is faster but has a bigger undershoot and needs more torque."
  中文：界越大越快，但反向位移越大、力矩需求越高。

**Q21. What did you use LQR for?**

- **L1** — "As an analysis tool, not as the deployed controller. After the cascaded PID worked, I used LQR on the four-state model to see how state weights trade stability against control effort, and used that as a reference for reading the deployed gains, locating state anomalies and diagnosing saturation."
  中文：作为分析工具而非部署；级联 PID 工作后，用 LQR 看权重如何折中稳定与控制量，作为读增益、找状态异常、诊断饱和的参照。
- **L2** — *Give me one concrete decision.* — "The model suggested the damping gain should be about half the proportional gain, so I raised it and a 1.5 hertz oscillation disappeared."
  中文：模型提示阻尼增益应约为比例增益的一半，我提高后 1.5 Hz 振荡消失。
- **L3** — *Was that LQR advice or just PID practice?* — "Both agree; the model gave me the number to try and a reason for it."
  中文：二者一致；模型给了可试的数和理由。

**Q22. How exactly did it guide the PID tuning?**

- **L1** — "The cascade is a structured special case of full state feedback, so I expanded the PID gains into four equivalent state gains and compared their ratios and the closed-loop poles with the LQR solution. The sensitivity table said too little tilt-rate damping or too stiff a velocity term is dangerous, so I looked there first."
  中文：级联是全状态反馈的结构化特例；把 PID 增益展开成四个等效增益，与 LQR 比较比例和闭环极点；灵敏度表说明阻尼不足或速度项过硬最危险，所以先查这两处。
- **L2** — *Did you copy the LQR numbers?* — "No. Units and structure differ; I compared ratios and trends, then tuned on the robot."
  中文：没有。单位与结构不同；比较比例和趋势，再在实机整定。
- **L3** — *How big was the sensitivity?* — "In my teaching model, halving the rate gain made it unstable, and scaling all gains by 0.2 did too, while 0.3 was fine."
  中文：教学模型中，把速率增益减半会失稳，全部增益乘 0.2 也失稳，乘 0.3 则稳定。

**Q23. How did you choose Q and R?**

- **L1** — "Bryson's rule as a starting point: one over the square of the largest acceptable deviation for each state and one over the square of the largest acceptable torque. In my teaching model that gave weights 4, 4, 100 and 1, and R of 0.25. Then I swept them to see the effect."
  中文：以 Bryson 规则为起点：状态权重取容许最大偏差平方的倒数，R 取容许最大力矩平方的倒数；教学模型得 Q=diag(4,4,100,1)、R=0.25，再扫描看影响。
- **L2** — *What did the sweep show?* — "A larger R gives gentler, slower control; a tenfold tilt weight roughly doubles the peak torque, from 2.3 to 4.2 in teaching units."
  中文：R 大则更柔和更慢；倾角权重×10 使峰值力矩约翻倍（教学单位 2.3→4.2）。
- **L3** — *Is there a free lunch?* — "No, a faster response always costs larger gains, more torque, and more noise amplification."
  中文：没有；更快总要付出更大增益、力矩和噪声放大。

**Q24. Why didn't you deploy LQR?**

- **L1** — "The model is a single-plane approximation: it ignores the three-wheel geometry, slip, arm dynamics and actuator limits, and its input is a torque while the robot interface takes wheel commands. The PID structure was easier to tune on the real robot and to add limits, anti-windup and a feedforward. So LQR was the reference and PID the implementation."
  中文：模型是单平面近似，忽略三轮几何、打滑、臂动力学和执行器限制，输入是力矩而接口是轮命令；PID 更易在实机整定并加限幅、抗饱和、前馈；LQR 是参照，PID 是实现。
- **L2** — *Isn't LQR cheaper to compute?* — "Efficiency isn't the reason: solving Riccati is offline and applying it is four multiply-adds."
  中文：不是效率问题：Riccati 离线求解，在线只是 4 次乘加。
- **L3** — *Would you deploy it now?* — "With a better model, integral action and saturation handling, maybe as a gain-scheduled version; for this project the cascade was the right choice."
  中文：模型更好并有积分与饱和处理，也许做成增益调度版；本项目级联是正确选择。

**Q25. Saturation diagnosis?**

- **L1** — "For a given torque limit the model has a largest recoverable tilt. If the real wheel command hits the limit at a much smaller tilt, the problem is high gain or noise. If it hits near the predicted tilt, the actuator margin is the problem."
  中文：给定力矩上限有最大可恢复倾角；实际在远小于预测的倾角就贴限幅是增益过高或噪声；接近预测则是执行器余量。
- **L2** — *Which was your case?* — "A push of 20 to 30 newtons took the tilt to about 0.15 radians, where the commands hit the limit; my tilt-reference bound was also 0.15, so it was margin. I lowered the bound to 0.1."
  中文：20～30 N 推到约 0.15 rad 时贴限幅，而参考上限也是 0.15，是余量问题；把上限降到 0.1。
- **L3** — *How did you confirm?* — "Repeated the push: the peak tilt stayed about 0.15 but the saturation fraction fell below 5 percent."
  中文：重复推：倾角峰值仍约 0.15 rad，但饱和占比降到 5% 以下。

### E. 级联 PID

**Q26. Why a cascade? Why is the outer loop's output a tilt reference?**

- **L1** — "Position cannot be changed directly. To accelerate, the centre of mass has to be ahead of the contact point, so tilt is effectively an acceleration request. The outer loop turns position and velocity errors into a tilt reference, and the faster inner loop follows it while keeping the robot upright."
  中文：位置不能直接改变；加速需质心在接触点前方，倾角即加速度请求；外环把位置/速度误差变成倾角参考，更快的内环跟踪并保持直立。
- **L2** — *What are the bandwidths?* — "About 0.15 hertz outside, 1 hertz inside, and the motor driver's speed loop near 20 hertz."
  中文：外环约 0.15 Hz，内环约 1 Hz，电机速度环约 20 Hz。
- **L3** — *Why must the outer loop be slower?* — "It treats the inner loop as nearly instantaneous; if it is too fast, the inner loop's lag enters its phase margin and causes slow oscillation."
  中文：外环把内环当作近似瞬时；太快会使内环滞后进入其相位裕度，造成慢速振荡。

**Q27. Why is the tilt reference bounded?**

- **L1** — "Bounded tilt means bounded acceleration, it keeps the robot in the linear range, keeps torque within what the motors can deliver, and limits the non-minimum-phase undershoot. The cost is slower response, so the bound is a trade-off I tuned: 0.1 radians."
  中文：有界倾角即有界加速度；保持线性区；力矩在电机能力内；限制非最小相位反向位移；代价是响应变慢；取 0.1 rad。
- **L2** — *How did you pick 0.1?* — "I started at 0.03 and raised it while watching undershoot and saturation; 0.15 saturated during pushes, 0.1 did not."
  中文：从 0.03 起逐步放大并观察反向位移和饱和；0.15 在推扰时饱和，0.1 不会。
- **L3** — *Does it limit disturbance rejection?* — "It limits how hard the outer loop pushes, not the inner loop's response to a push, which is limited by the wheel command limit."
  中文：限制的是外环的"推力"，不是内环对扰动的响应，后者受轮命令限幅约束。

**Q28. What does the derivative term do?**

- **L1** — "It uses the gyro rate and acts as damping: if the body is already rotating toward the reference, the derivative term reverses the command before the angle gets there, so the controller brakes early. In my analysis halving the damping was enough to destabilise the model."
  中文：用陀螺速率做阻尼；若已朝参考转动，D 项在到达前反向，提前刹车；分析中阻尼减半足以使模型失稳。
- **L2** — *Numbers?* — "Proportional about 40 and derivative about 20 in command units; a worked example: tilt error 0.02, rate 0.1 radians per second gives 0.8 minus 2.0, so the output already flips sign."
  中文：命令单位下 P≈40、D≈20；例：误差 0.02、速率 0.1 rad/s 得 0.8−2.0，输出已反向。
- **L3** — *Noise?* — "Gyro noise times the D gain gives the command jitter; with a light 25 hertz filter it is small."
  中文：陀螺噪声乘 D 增益即命令抖动；加 25 Hz 轻滤波后很小。

**Q29. Is the attitude loop's output a torque or a velocity?**

- **L1** — "It is a virtual command mapped by the wheel kinematics to wheel commands. In our implementation the command sent to the drivers is a wheel velocity, and the motor's own velocity loop turns it into torque. I don't integrate torque into velocity in the controller; the physics does."
  中文：是虚拟量，经运动学映射到轮命令；实现中发给驱动器的是轮速，电机速度环把它变成力矩；控制器里不做力矩积分，物理做。
- **L2** — *Why the scale factor on the PID output?* — "It's an empirical gain between the PID output and the wheel command, tuned together with the PID gains."
  中文：是 PID 输出与轮命令之间的经验增益，与 PID 增益一起整定。
- **L3** — *Doesn't that make the gains hard to compare with LQR?* — "Yes, so I compare ratios, not absolute numbers."
  中文：是，所以我比较比例而非绝对数值。

**Q30. How do you prevent integrator windup?**

- **L1** — "I clamp the integral term and the output, stop accumulating when the output is at its limit, and reset the integrator on enable. I don't use back-calculation; that would be smoother and I'd consider it if saturation were frequent."
  中文：积分限幅+输出限幅，输出贴限时不再累加，使能时复位；不用反算，若饱和频繁会考虑。
- **L2** — *Show the problem.* — "With Ki 4 and an error of 0.5 for two seconds against an output limit of 1, the integral reaches 4; when the error flips to minus 0.2 it takes 5 seconds to unwind."
  中文：Ki=4、误差 0.5 持续 2 s、输出限 1，积分到 4；误差变 −0.2 后需 5 s 退回。
- **L3** — *Do you have an integral at all?* — "A small one, about 2 in command units, clamped, in the inner loop to absorb residual mounting offset; the outer loop has none."
  中文：内环有个很小的积分（命令单位约 2，带限幅）吸收残余安装偏置；外环没有。

**Q31. How do you tune it on the robot?**

- **L1** — "Safety first: lifted or on a gantry, low gains, e-stop in hand. Check signs, then the inner loop: raise P until a slight oscillation, back off to about half, add D with the gyro and watch the noise. Then the outer loop: velocity damping first, then position; then the bounds; then the feedforward and arm motion; and regression-test after every change."
  中文：安全第一（悬空/龙门架、低增益、急停）；先查符号；内环 P 加到轻微振荡后退到约一半，再加陀螺 D；外环先速度阻尼后位置；再界；再前馈与臂动；每次改动回归。
- **L2** — *What if it oscillates slowly?* — "Outer loop too fast relative to the inner: reduce the outer gain or the bound."
  中文：外环相对内环太快：降外环增益或界。
- **L3** — *And buzzing?* — "D noise or mechanical resonance: check the gyro noise times Kd and tighten the mounts."
  中文：D 噪声或机械共振：核对陀螺噪声×Kd，并紧固机械。

**Q32. What are the relative bandwidths and rates?**

- **L1** — "Outer at most a third to a fifth of the inner; sample at ten to thirty times the bandwidth. In our case the control loop ran at 100 hertz, the inner attitude loop had about 1 hertz of bandwidth, the outer position loop about 0.15 hertz, and the motor driver's speed loop about 20 hertz."
  中文：外环不超过内环的 1/3～1/5；采样为带宽的 10～30 倍；本项目 100 Hz 控制、内环约 1 Hz、外环约 0.15 Hz、电机速度环约 20 Hz。
- **L2** — *Why is 100 hertz enough?* — "It's 100 times the inner bandwidth and the delay budget is 15 to 25 milliseconds against an unstable time scale of a few hundred."
  中文：为内环带宽的 100 倍，延迟预算 15～25 ms 相对数百毫秒的不稳定时间尺度。
- **L3** — *What limits going faster?* — "Mostly the IMU rate and the motor loop, not the CPU."
  中文：主要受 IMU 频率和电机环限制，而非 CPU。

### F. 质心前馈

**Q33. How did you get the centre-of-mass offset?**

- **L1** — "I built a Pinocchio model from the URDF and ran a ROS node that subscribes to the left and right arm joint states, fills the position vector by joint name, and calls the centre-of-mass function. It publishes the horizontal x and y offset at up to 100 hertz. The centre of mass is the mass-weighted average of every link's local centre of mass in one frame."
  中文：用 URDF 建 Pinocchio 模型；ROS 节点订阅左右臂关节状态，按关节名填位置向量，调用质心函数；发布水平 x、y（最高 100 Hz）；质心是各连杆质量加权平均。
- **L2** — *Where does the URDF come from?* — "It came from the MuJoCo environment's model, originally exported from CAD, and I checked the total mass against the real weight to within about 5 percent."
  中文：来自 MuJoCo 环境的模型（最初由 CAD 导出），总质量与称重相差约 5% 以内。
- **L3** — *Which joints are included?* — "Four per arm; the hands are treated as fixed, so their pose change is ignored."
  中文：每臂 4 个关节；手视为固定，姿态变化被忽略。

**Q34. What is the sign logic?**

- **L1** — "The offset times total mass times g gives the gravity moment about the support. I add a roll term proportional to the lateral offset and a pitch term proportional to minus the forward offset, with a tuned gain. The signs come from r cross F: a forward offset gives a positive pitch moment, so the compensation is negative."
  中文：偏移×总质量×g 为重力矩；roll 项与侧向偏移成正比，pitch 项与前向偏移的负值成正比；符号来自 r×F：前向偏移产生正 pitch 力矩，补偿为负。
- **L2** — *Why does roll tip toward minus y?* — "Right-hand rule: positive roll rotates the upward axis toward minus y, so going left needs negative roll."
  中文：右手定则：正 roll 把向上轴转向 −y，向左需负 roll。
- **L3** — *Did you get it wrong once?* — "Yes, the sign was reversed at first and the feedforward made things worse; a static one-arm test found it."
  中文：是，最初符号反了，前馈让情况更糟；单臂静态实验发现。

**Q35. Why a feedforward and not just feedback?**

- **L1** — "The disturbance is predictable from the arm joint angles, so the feedforward acts before any tilt appears. For a ballbot there is a subtlety: the real balance point moves, because with the centre of mass shifted the body must lean back. The feedforward effectively shifts the attitude set-point."
  中文：扰动可由臂关节角预测，前馈在倾斜出现前就起作用；对 ballbot，质心偏移使真实平衡点移动（机体必须后仰），前馈相当于平移姿态设定点。
- **L2** — *How large is the shift?* — "In my teaching numbers a 90-degree raise of both arms moves the balance point by about 5 degrees, close to the 0.1 radian reference bound, which means the feedback alone would spend most of its authority."
  中文：教学数值中双臂前平举 90° 使平衡点移动约 5°，接近 0.1 rad 的界，反馈会耗掉大部分裕度。
- **L3** — *Why can't a motor torque hold the arm weight?* — "A constant torque would accelerate the ball indefinitely; the system can only cancel the gravity moment by changing the lean."
  中文：持续力矩会让球一直加速；只能通过改变倾角让重力矩自己归零。

**Q36. How did you verify the feedforward?**

- **L1** — "I ran the same fixed arm trajectory with the compensation on and off, five runs each, interleaved. I compared the peak and RMS tilt, the 30-second drift, and the fraction of samples with wheel commands at the limit. Off: peak about 0.06 radians, RMS 0.022, drift 0.30 metres, saturation 6 percent. On: 0.026, 0.010, 0.10 and 1 percent."
  中文：同一固定臂轨迹开/关各 5 次交错；比较峰值与 RMS 倾角、30 s 漂移、命令贴限幅占比；关：0.06/0.022/0.30 m/6%；开：0.026/0.010/0.10 m/1%。
- **L2** — *What was the trajectory?* — "Both arms raised to about 0.78 radians in 8 seconds and back in the next 8, with a cubic spline."
  中文：双臂 8 s 抬到约 0.78 rad，再用 8 s 回收，三次样条。
- **L3** — *What threatens the validity?* — "Battery level, motor temperature, a different starting state, and the gain itself; so I interleaved the runs, re-initialised each time, and calibrated the gain on static poses first."
  中文：电量、温度、起始状态、增益本身；所以交错运行、每次重新初始化、先用静态姿态标定增益。

**Q37. How is the gain set?**

- **L1** — "Empirically, because the controller output isn't a physical torque. I calibrate it with static arm poses: regress the steady correction needed against the computed moment; the slope is the gain. Normalised, it came out near 1.0."
  中文：经验标定，因为控制输出不是物理力矩；用静态臂姿态回归稳态修正量对计算力矩，斜率即增益；归一化后约 1.0。
- **L2** — *How many poses?* — "Five, including hanging, forward at two heights, and sideways."
  中文：五个姿态：下垂、前举两种高度、侧举等。
- **L3** — *What does a too-large gain look like?* — "The steady tilt goes the opposite way, so the sign of the static residual separates 'wrong sign' from 'too much gain'."
  中文：稳态倾角反向；静态残差的符号能区分"符号错"和"增益过大"。

**Q38. What are the limits of this approach?**

- **L1** — "It is quasi-static: it uses the centre-of-mass position, not the arm acceleration. For slow trajectories the dynamic part is a few percent of the static moment by my estimate, about 3.6 percent, but for fast motions it would matter. It also depends on the URDF masses and the model root, and the left and right joint states are not time-synchronised."
  中文：是准静态的，用质心位置而非臂加速度；慢轨迹动态项约占静态的 3.6%，快动作则重要；还依赖 URDF 质量和根坐标，左右关节状态没有时间同步。
- **L2** — *Is there a case where it failed?* — "Asymmetric extreme poses, one arm forward and one back with large reach, saturated the roll channel; I limited those trajectories and calibrated roll separately."
  中文：不对称极端姿态（一前一后大幅外伸）使 roll 通道饱和；限制了这些轨迹并单独标定 roll。
- **L3** — *Next improvement?* — "Add an acceleration term and time-synchronise the two arms."
  中文：加加速度项并同步两臂。

### G. 安全闭环

**Q39. What does your safety loop contain?**

- **L1** — "It starts disabled; on enable it waits for valid IMU and wheel data, captures offsets, resets integrators, filters and references, and sends zero first. It clamps outputs, detects excessive tilt, stops the output and requires a deliberate re-enable. A fall is roll or pitch above 0.5 radians for five consecutive cycles."
  中文：默认失能；使能时等有效数据、采偏置、复位积分/滤波/参考、先发零；限幅；检测过大倾角并停输出，需人为重新使能；跌倒=roll 或 pitch 超 0.5 rad 且连续 5 周期。
- **L2** — *Other triggers?* — "Stale IMU or wheel data older than 50 milliseconds, NaN values, wheel feedback missing for 50 milliseconds, or the wheel commands at the limit for half a second."
  中文：IMU/轮数据超 50 ms、NaN、轮反馈缺失 50 ms、命令贴限幅 0.5 s。
- **L3** — *Why latch the fault?* — "A fall may come from a loose cable; an automatic restart would hide the cause and might restart with people nearby."
  中文：跌倒可能源于接线松动；自动重启会掩盖原因，且旁边可能有人。

**Q40. Walk me through enabling the controller.**

- **L1** — "Disabled by default. On request it waits up to five seconds for fresh IMU and wheel data and a valid quaternion, averages 200 samples of roll and pitch with the gyro quiet, sets the target to the current position, clears integrators and filters, sends zero, and ramps the output over 0.3 seconds. If the data don't arrive it falls back to disabled with an alarm."
  中文：默认失能；最多等 5 s 新鲜数据和有效四元数；陀螺安静时平均 200 帧 roll/pitch；目标置当前；清积分与滤波；先零后 0.3 s 斜坡；超时回失能并报警。
- **L2** — *Why re-capture on every enable?* — "The robot may have been moved, and the last capture may have included a wobble."
  中文：位置可能变了，上次采集可能含晃动。
- **L3** — *What if the snapshot you worked from behaved differently?* — "I'd say so: the notes I worked from continued enabling after the timeout, and I'd list that as a hardening item."
  中文：如实说：所依据的笔记里超时后仍使能，我把它列为加固项。

**Q41. Why 0.5 radians and five cycles?**

- **L1** — "The threshold has to sit above the largest normal tilt, about 0.15 radians in a hard push, and below the point where the actuators can't recover it, which I estimated at around 0.6. Five cycles, 50 milliseconds, rejects a single bad sample without delaying the trip much."
  中文：阈值要高于正常最大倾角（硬推约 0.15 rad），低于执行器无法恢复的角（估计约 0.6）；5 周期（50 ms）可挡单帧坏点而不明显延迟。
- **L2** — *Cost of the debounce?* — "At an unstable pole near 4 per second the tilt grows by about 22 percent in 50 milliseconds, from 0.5 to 0.61 radians."
  中文：不稳定极点约 4/s 时 50 ms 内倾角增大约 22%（0.5→0.61 rad）。
- **L3** — *False trips?* — "A single 0.9-radian glitch in my replay does not trip it; five consecutive samples do."
  中文：回放中单个 0.9 rad 毛刺不触发，连续 5 个样本才触发。

**Q42. How do you know the motors really stop after a fault?**

- **L1** — "Resetting controller state isn't enough. I trace the whole path: zero command published, driver forwards it to CAN, driver watchdog, motor firmware timeout. I describe what I verified: for instance I killed the control node and watched the wheels stop within about a tenth of a second because of the driver watchdog."
  中文：重置状态不够；沿整条路径核对：零命令→驱动→CAN→看门狗→固件超时；只讲验证过的，如杀掉控制节点后约 0.1 s 内轮子停止（驱动看门狗）。
- **L2** — *What didn't you verify?* — "The motor firmware's own timeout with a cut CAN cable; I'd test it with the robot off the ground."
  中文：切断 CAN 线时电机固件自己的超时；应在离地条件下测。
- **L3** — *What would you add?* — "An explicit zero command on every fault path, a hardware emergency stop that cuts motor power independent of software, and a log of the last second before each fault."
  中文：每条故障路径显式发零命令；独立于软件的硬件急停切断电机电源；记录每次故障前一秒。

**Q43. Why limit the whole wheel vector rather than each wheel?** *(见 Q10)*

- **L1** — "Because the map is linear, scaling the whole vector keeps the ball's direction; per-wheel clipping bends it, which hurts balance exactly when the actuators are stretched."
  中文：映射线性，整体缩放保持方向；逐轮截断会拐弯，在执行器吃紧时伤害平衡。
- **L2** — *What is the limit?* — "About 8 in wheel-rate units, plus a per-cycle slew limit of 0.5."
  中文：轮速量级约 8，加每周期斜率限制 0.5。
- **L3** — *Why a slew limit?* — "It spreads a step over about 60 milliseconds, matching the motor speed loop, and prevents shock to the drive."
  中文：把阶跃展开到约 60 ms，匹配电机速度环，避免冲击。

**Q44. What happens when a command stops arriving?**

- **L1** — "A good design has a watchdog in the driver that zeros the output, because the controller may be what died. Ours was 100 milliseconds, and feedback missing for more than 50 milliseconds was treated as a motor-offline fault."
  中文：驱动里要有看门狗置零，因为死掉的可能正是控制器；本项目 100 ms；反馈缺失超 50 ms 判电机掉线故障。
- **L2** — *How far does it coast?* — "At 0.4 metres per second about 4 centimetres with the watchdog, against about 20 centimetres if the last command were held for half a second."
  中文：0.4 m/s 时约 4 cm；若保持最后命令 0.5 s 约 20 cm。
- **L3** — *Why not 20 milliseconds?* — "Normal scheduling jitter could trip it and zero the wheels mid-balance."
  中文：正常调度抖动可能误触发，在平衡中途让轮子归零。

**Q45. What is the end-to-end latency and how did you budget it?**

- **L1** — "About 15 milliseconds typical and 25 worst case: IMU sampling and serial about 5 and 10, parsing and caching 3 and 5, computation 1 and 2, CAN 1 and 2, the motor speed loop 5 and 6."
  中文：典型约 15 ms、最坏 25 ms：IMU 与串口 5/10，解析缓存 3/5，计算 1/2，CAN 1/2，电机速度环 5/6。
- **L2** — *Why does it matter?* — "The unstable time scale is a few hundred milliseconds; delay should be a small fraction of it."
  中文：不稳定时间尺度是数百毫秒；延迟要是其一小部分。
- **L3** — *How did you measure it?* — "Message timestamps along the chain, a bag at the controller, and CAN dump timestamps for the last hop."
  中文：沿链路的消息时间戳、控制器处录包、最后一跳用 CAN dump 时间戳。

### H. 调试与验证

**Q46. Tell me about a bug you found.**

- **L1** — "The robot slowly crept to one side at rest, always in the same direction, ending about 0.2 metres from the start. The pitch mean was about 0.9 degrees instead of zero, so it was a fixed offset. My model says the steady drift is the offset divided by the outer position gain, about 0.2 metres, matching what I saw. The offset had been captured before the robot was steady, so I changed the enable sequence; the drift fell to about 5 centimetres a minute."
  中文：静止缓慢爬行，固定方向，停在约 0.2 m；pitch 均值约 0.9°，是固定偏置；模型预言漂移=偏置/外环位置增益≈0.2 m，吻合；偏置采在机器人没稳时，于是改使能流程；漂移降到约 5 cm/分。
- **L2** — *How did you know it was not noise?* — "Same direction and distance every time, and adding half a degree in a replay changed the drift in proportion."
  中文：每次方向和距离相同；回放里加 0.5° 漂移按比例变化。
- **L3** — *Could gyro bias do this?* — "A gyro bias gives a ramp that grows with time; this was a constant offset."
  中文：陀螺零偏表现为随时间增长的斜坡；这是恒定偏置。

**Q47. How do you tell noise from lack of damping?**

- **L1** — "By frequency and timing. Noise shows as jitter near the control rate even at rest; lack of damping shows as oscillation near the loop bandwidth, 1 to 2 hertz here, after a push. Gyro noise times the D gain predicts the first."
  中文：看频率和时机：噪声在控制频率附近、静止就有；阻尼不足在环带宽附近（此处 1～2 Hz）、推后出现；陀螺噪声×D 增益可预测前者。
- **L2** — *The 1.5 hertz case?* — "I raised the damping gain to about half the proportional gain, guided by the model's damping ratio, and the oscillation went."
  中文：把阻尼增益提到比例增益的一半左右，参考模型阻尼比，振荡消失。
- **L3** — *How did you confirm it?* — "Same push, compared the decay ratio of successive peaks before and after."
  中文：同样的推力，比较前后相邻峰值的衰减比。

**Q48. The feedforward made things worse. How did you find the cause?**

- **L1** — "The peak pitch error grew from 0.06 to 0.09 radians when I switched it on. A static test with one arm raised showed the correction going the wrong way, while the centre-of-mass estimate matched my hand calculation. So the sign was wrong. I flipped it and calibrated the gain with five static poses; the peak then fell to 0.026."
  中文：开前馈后峰值 pitch 误差从 0.06 增到 0.09 rad；单臂静态实验显示修正方向反，而质心估计与手算一致；所以是符号错；翻转并用 5 个静态姿态标定后峰值降到 0.026。
- **L2** — *Why a static test first?* — "It separates the sign from the dynamics."
  中文：把符号与动态分开。
- **L3** — *In the model?* — "A reversed sign gives a steady offset of 0.92 metres, twice the no-feedforward 0.46."
  中文：模型中符号反使稳态偏移 0.92 m，是无前馈 0.46 m 的两倍。

**Q49. The robot lurched when you re-enabled it. Why?**

- **L1** — "Re-enabling only set a flag, so the target position, the integrator and the filters still held values from the previous run, and the tilt offset had been captured while I was steadying it by hand. I made enable a state machine with a five-step initialisation; repeated re-enables then gave peak tilt under 0.02 radians."
  中文：重新使能只置了标志，目标、积分和滤波仍是上次的值，偏置在手扶晃动时采的；改成状态机+五步初始化；重复重新使能倾角峰值<0.02 rad。
- **L2** — *Which cause mattered most?* — "They stacked; removing one reduced the lurch but did not remove it."
  中文：原因叠加；去掉一个只会减轻，不会消除。
- **L3** — *What test did you add?* — "Ten consecutive fall-and-re-enable cycles in the regression run."
  中文：回归里加入连续 10 次"跌倒-重新使能"。

**Q50. What is the first thing you check when it misbehaves?**

- **L1** — "Signs, units and timing before gains. A global error like wrong wheel order always diverges or drifts one way, so I run single-axis tests with the robot lifted. Then tilt and gyro, wheel commands against the limit, and message timestamps."
  中文：先符号、单位、时序，再增益；悬空单轴测试；再看倾角陀螺、轮命令与限幅、时间戳。
- **L2** — *Why not retune first?* — "Retuning around a sign error hides it."
  中文：围绕符号错误调参会掩盖它。
- **L3** — *Example where gains were the issue?* — "The 1.5 hertz oscillation."
  中文：1.5 Hz 振荡。

### I. ROS 与源码

**Q51. How does IMU data get into your controller?**

- **L1** — "The module streams binary frames over a 921600 baud serial link. The vendor driver looks for a head byte, checks type and length, verifies a CRC on the header and another on the data plus an end byte, and only then publishes. A failed frame is dropped and the parser resynchronises at the next head byte."
  中文：模块以 921600 波特率发二进制帧；厂商驱动找帧头、检查类型与长度、头 CRC、数据 CRC 与帧尾，通过后才发布；失败丢帧并在下一个帧头重新同步。
- **L2** — *Did you write the driver?* — "No, it's the vendor's; I read and used it and noticed a few weak points."
  中文：不是，是厂商的；我读过并使用，也注意到几处薄弱点。
- **L3** — *Which weak points?* — "The stamp is the receive time, the orientation can be one packet older than the gyro, and the error counters are never published."
  中文：时间戳是接收时刻；姿态可能比陀螺旧一个包；错误计数不发布。

**Q52. What does your centre-of-mass node do, and what are its limits?**

- **L1** — "It caches the latest left and right joint states, and a 100 hertz loop copies them into the Pinocchio vector by joint name, calls the centre-of-mass function, and publishes x and y."
  中文：缓存左右最新关节状态；100 Hz 循环按关节名写入 Pinocchio 向量，调用质心函数并发布 x、y。
- **L2** — *Limits?* — "The message has no timestamp, the arms are not synchronised, velocity reuses the position indices, a missing joint name is skipped silently, and nothing checks message age."
  中文：消息无时间戳；两臂不同步；速度复用位置索引；缺失关节名被静默跳过；不检查消息年龄。
- **L3** — *Fix?* — "Assert the mapping at startup, use a stamped message, and add an age check."
  中文：启动时断言映射；用带时间戳的消息；加年龄检查。

**Q53. How do you replay a fixed arm trajectory repeatably?**

- **L1** — "A ROS service takes a file name, loads timestamped joint targets from YAML, validates them, builds a clamped cubic spline so start and end velocities are zero, and publishes at 50 hertz using the clock, so the duration doesn't depend on loop jitter. It rejects a second request while one is playing."
  中文：服务接收文件名，读 YAML 时间戳关节目标并校验，建 clamped 三次样条（首末速度为零），以时钟按 50 Hz 发布（时长不受循环抖动影响）；播放期间拒绝新请求。
- **L2** — *What did you notice about it?* — "The old trajectory files use a key format the newer node rejects, and an all-zero arm trajectory is treated as not controlled."
  中文：旧轨迹文件的键格式被新节点拒绝；全零臂轨迹被当作不控制。
- **L3** — *Your fix?* — "I noted which node and format I used for the experiments; I'd add a version field and return a reason on failure."
  中文：我记录了实验所用节点和格式；建议加版本字段并在失败时返回原因。

**Q54. What is the ROS and CAN chain?**

- **L1** — "The controller publishes wheel commands; a driver node packs them into CAN frames at 1 megabit per second; feedback returns as joint states at about 100 hertz, in step with the controller. Arms and wheels sit on separate CAN channels, each well under 10 percent load."
  中文：控制器发布轮命令；驱动节点打包成 1 Mbit/s 的 CAN 帧；反馈以约 100 Hz 的 JointState 返回；臂与轮分在两个 CAN 通道，各自负载远低于 10%。
- **L2** — *Where were the real risks?* — "Not bandwidth: jitter, data freshness and fault handling."
  中文：不在带宽：抖动、新鲜度和故障处理。
- **L3** — *How do you start the arm bus?* — "Bring the interface down and up with the bitrate set, so no stale state remains."
  中文：先 down 再 up 并设置波特率，避免残留状态。

## 三、三个 STAR 案例

### STAR 1：回到模型分析

- **S（Situation）**：级联 PID 在实机上能站，但凭手感整定后，增益越来越难解释。具体：内环有一个约 1.5 Hz 的慢振荡，手推一下后轮命令偶尔贴限幅（约 2026 年 1 月前后）。
- **T（Task）**：找到一种能"预测"的方法，读懂已部署的增益、定位状态异常和执行器饱和。
- **A（Action）**：推导四状态线性化倒立摆（含反作用项）；用 Bryson 规则取 $Q=\mathrm{diag}(4,4,100,1)$、$R_u=0.25$；扫描权重；把 PID 展开成等效状态反馈，比较比例和极点；用灵敏度表确定"阻尼不足和速度项过硬最危险"；用"最大可恢复倾角"判断饱和性质。
- **R（Result）**：两个具体决定：①内环阻尼 $K_d$ 提到约 $K_p$ 的一半，1.5 Hz 振荡消失；②外环倾角参考上限从 0.15 降到 0.10 rad，20～30 N 推力下饱和占比降到 5% 以内。导师推荐信把它描述为"解释已部署控制器增益、定位状态异常和执行器饱和的参考"。

> I tuned the cascade by hand until it stopped explaining the robot's behaviour. So I derived a four-state linearised model and ran LQR with several weights. I expanded the PID into an equivalent state feedback and compared ratios and poles. The model said too little damping on the tilt rate was the dangerous direction, so I raised the damping gain to about half the proportional gain, and a slow 1.5 hertz oscillation went away. It also showed that a bigger tilt reference raises the peak control effort sharply, so I cut the bound from 0.15 to 0.1 radians, and the wheels stopped hitting their limit during pushes.

中文：S 手感整定后增益难解释（1.5 Hz 振荡、推后贴限幅）；T 找可预测的方法；A 推导模型、扫权重、PID 展开比较、灵敏度表、最大可恢复倾角；R 两个决定：阻尼提到 Kp 的一半、参考上限 0.15→0.10；导师信的描述。

### STAR 2：质心前馈的开关验证（含一次符号错误）

- **S**：臂一动，底盘漂移或倾斜；固定轨迹 `front_raise` 一类（0 s 零位，8 s 抬到约 0.78 rad，16 s 回零位，三次样条）。
- **T**：证明前馈有效，并把它做对。
- **A**：用 Pinocchio+URDF 估质心，加 roll/pitch 前馈；第一次开前馈峰值反而从 0.06 增到 0.09 rad（一次倒下）；做单臂静态实验发现方向反、质心估计与手算一致；翻转符号，用 5 个静态姿态回归标定增益（约 1.0）；再做开/关各 5 次交错对比。
- **R**：关→开：峰值 0.060→0.026 rad，RMS 0.022→0.010，30 s 漂移 0.30→0.10 m，贴限幅占比 6%→1%。

> The arm motion made the chassis drift and tilt. I estimated the centre of mass with Pinocchio and the URDF and fed a gravity feedforward into roll and pitch. The first time I switched it on, the peak tilt got worse, from 0.06 to 0.09 radians. A static test with one arm raised showed the correction going the wrong way, while the centre-of-mass estimate matched my hand calculation, so the sign was wrong. I flipped it, calibrated the gain on five static poses, and then ran the same fixed trajectory on and off, five runs each, interleaved. The peak tilt fell from about 0.06 to 0.026 radians, the 30-second drift from 0.30 to 0.10 metres, and the share of samples with the wheels at their limit from 6 to 1 percent.

中文：S 臂动导致漂移倾斜；T 证明并做对；A 首次开前馈反而更糟→单臂静态实验判定符号反→翻转+静态标定增益→开/关各 5 次交错；R 峰值 0.06→0.026，漂移 0.30→0.10 m，饱和 6%→1%。

### STAR 3：重新使能时猛冲（及五步初始化）

- **S**：早期整定时，每次跌倒测试后重新使能，机器人先往前猛冲约 0.3 m、倾角峰值 0.12 rad，有一次触发了第二次保护（约 2025 年 12 月）。
- **T**：查原因，让重新使能安全，因为下一步要在臂运动时测试。
- **A**：画使能瞬间的目标位置、位置估计、积分项；发现目标与积分都是上次的值，偏置是在手扶晃动时采的；写五步初始化（等数据；扶稳采 200 帧偏置，陀螺安静；目标=当前；清积分与滤波；先零后 0.3 s 斜坡）；把使能写成状态机，超时回失能；回归加"连续 10 次跌倒-重新使能"。
- **R**：重复 10 次重新使能倾角峰值均 <0.02 rad；成为标准流程：只在扶正后重新使能、每次重采偏置。

> Early in tuning, every time I re-enabled the controller after a fall test, the robot lurched forward for a moment before settling. I plotted the target position, the position estimate and the integrator around the enable instant. The target and the integrator still held values from the previous run, and the tilt offset had been captured while I was steadying the robot by hand. I wrote a five-step initialisation: wait for valid data, capture 200 samples of offset only when the gyro is quiet, set the target to the current position, clear integrators and filters, and send zero first, then ramp the output over 0.3 seconds. In ten repeated re-enables the peak tilt stayed under 0.02 radians, and it became our standard procedure.

中文：S 重新使能时先猛冲；T 查原因并使重新使能安全；A 画使能瞬间的目标、位置估计、积分，发现都是上次残留、偏置在晃动中采；写五步初始化；R 10 次重新使能倾角峰值<0.02 rad，成为标准流程。

## 五、最容易说错的 10 句话与绝对不能说的话

### 5.1 最容易说错的 10 句话（及正确说法）

| # | 容易说错 | 正确说法 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "I used LQR to control the robot." | "I used LQR as an analysis tool; the deployed controller was a cascaded PID." |
| 2 | "I wrote the IMU filter / driver." | "The orientation comes from the module's onboard AHRS; the driver is the vendor's; I added validity checks." |
| 3 | "The controller outputs torque." | "It outputs a virtual command mapped to wheel velocity commands; the scale is an empirical gain." |
| 4 | "The robot is guaranteed to stop on a fault." | "The design zeroes the command, the driver has a watchdog, and I verified the parts I could." |
| 5 | "Simulation proves it works on the robot." | "The model predicted the trend, and I saw the same kind of behaviour on the robot." |
| 6 | "We use back-calculation anti-windup." | "Integral clamping plus output limiting." |
| 7 | "The loop runs at 1 kHz." | "The main loop ran at 100 hertz; the motor driver's inner loops are faster." |
| 8 | "I fixed the arm trajectory node / the driver bugs." | "I noticed those issues in the code; I'd harden them but they weren't mine to change." |
| 9 | "The feedforward is the physical gravity torque." | "It is an empirically scaled gain calibrated on static poses." |
| 10 | "I measured exactly X in the field" (for any number you can't verify) | Use the honest templates in §六. |

### 5.2 绝对不能说的话

1. 不能说"我部署了 LQR/ MPC"；
2. 不能说"我们在龙门架下没有任何风险"或"保证不会伤人"；
3. 不能说教学模型的数字（0.12 s 延迟边界、$K=-46.11$ 等）是实机数据；
4. 不能把团队写的驱动、`arm_trajectory_node`、厂商 IMU 驱动说成自己写的；
5. 不能说使能超时一定回失能（快照里超时后仍使能），而要说"设计要求"和"已知隐患"；
6. 不能说"自己写了 Mahony/EKF"用于 P1（那是 P3 的经历）；
7. 不能报没有日志依据的精确数字；记不清用 §六 的模板。

## 六、诚实模板（只在你确实记不清时使用）

- "I don't have the exact figure in front of me, but it was on the order of ___, and I can describe how I measured it."
  中文：我手边没有确切数字，量级大约是 ___，我可以说明怎么测的。
- "That value was set by my teammate / by the vendor default; what I tuned was ___."
  中文：那个值是队友/厂商默认设的；我整定的是 ___。
- "I did not implement that part; I used the IMU's onboard filter."
  中文：那部分不是我实现的，我用的是 IMU 板载滤波。
- "I'd rather not quote a number I can't verify; the qualitative result was ___."
  中文：我不想报无法核实的数；定性结果是 ___。
- "In my teaching model it's about ___; the robot was in the same range but I wouldn't claim the same number."
  中文：教学模型里约 ___；机器人量级相近，但我不声称同一个数。

## 七、自测

1. 用 90 秒讲完项目，不看稿，并录音检查有没有说"deployed LQR"。
2. 五秒内说出：外环输出什么、为什么有界、内环 D 项干嘛。
3. 说出 3 个你"没做"的事（LQR 上机、自写姿态滤波、臂/手驱动）。
4. 把 Q46、Q48、Q49 三个调试故事各讲 60 秒，数字要和 README 自查表一致。
5. 随机抽 10 个问答，只看 L1 英文题面，口头回答 L1 和 L2。
6. 把本文所有英文数字与细节（README 自查表取值）读一遍，并与实机日志核对。
