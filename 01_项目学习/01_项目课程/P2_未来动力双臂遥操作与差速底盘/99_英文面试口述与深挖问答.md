## 1. 口述

### 1.1 30 秒

> At Shenzhen Future Power I worked on dual-arm and chassis control. I mapped VR controller motion to end-effector targets and solved constrained inverse kinematics with MuJoCo and Mink. I built leader–follower teaching, with gravity compensation on the leader arm and position–velocity tracking on the follower. And I closed wheel-speed PID loops with encoder feedback and odometry on a differential-drive base. Everything was validated on real hardware through a ROS 2 to CAN chain.

中文：在 Future Power 做双臂和底盘控制：把 VR 手柄运动映射成末端目标，用 MuJoCo/Mink 解约束逆运动学；做主从示教（主臂重力补偿、从臂位置–速度跟踪）；在差速底盘上用编码器反馈闭合轮速 PID 并做里程计；全部通过 ROS 2 到 CAN 链在真机上验证。

关键词：三条线 · 真机 · ROS 2–CAN。易说错：不要说“simulation only”；不要说“I wrote the firmware”。

### 1.2 90 秒

> My internship had three parts. First, VR teleoperation. The controller pose is relative: when the operator presses the trigger, I store the controller pose and the end-effector pose, and afterwards the target is the start pose plus the scaled controller displacement, rotated into the robot frame. Mink turns the two hand targets into one small quadratic program each cycle, with a low-weight posture task, damping and limits, and I integrate the joint velocity it returns. The damping keeps joint speed bounded near singularities.
>
> Second, leader–follower teaching. I built the dual-arm dynamics from the URDF. The leader arm gets only a gravity-compensation torque through the motor's MIT frame, with zero stiffness, so it is easy to drag by hand. The follower tracks the leader's joint position and velocity.
>
> Third, the base. Differential-drive kinematics turn body velocity into wheel targets, an encoder-based PID loop tracks them, and the wheel motion is integrated into odometry, for both teleoperated and autonomous modes. All of it ran on real hardware through ROS 2 and CAN.

中文：三部分。①VR 遥操作：相对位姿映射——按下触发键保存手柄与末端位姿，之后目标=起点+缩放位移并旋到机器人系；Mink 每周期把两只手的目标变成一个小 QP（低权重姿态任务、阻尼、限位），积分得到的关节速度；阻尼限制奇异附近的关节速度。②主从示教：URDF 建动力学，主臂经 MIT 帧只发重力补偿力矩、零刚度，手拖很轻；从臂跟踪主臂关节位置与速度。③底盘：差速运动学把车体速度变成轮速目标，编码器 PID 跟踪，轮运动积分成里程计，遥控与自动两种模式；全部 ROS 2–CAN 真机运行。

关键词：relative mapping · QP per cycle · gravity only · closed PID · odometry。易说错：重力补偿是“只发重力项”，不是“位置控制”。

### 1.3 3 分钟

> **[Context]** The goal was a dual-arm platform on a mobile base that a person could control in two ways: by a VR controller, or by hand-guiding a leader arm that a follower copies. Everything went through one ROS 2 to CAN execution chain.
>
> **[VR]** The key idea is relative mapping. Absolute controller coordinates would make the arm jump at the start and may be unreachable, so a clutch stores both start poses. The position target equals the start position plus a scale times a fixed axis-mapping matrix times the controller displacement; the scale is one by default and one half for fine work. The rotation is the controller's relative rotation, conjugated by the axis mapping and applied by left multiplication to the start orientation. Targets go to Mink as frame tasks. Each cycle it solves a small quadratic program over the joint increment, with a low-weight posture task to resolve redundancy, damping and limits, and then I integrate with a five-millisecond step. Near a stretched arm the smallest singular value of the Jacobian goes to zero and an ordinary inverse would amplify velocity by one over sigma. Damped least squares turns the gain into sigma over sigma squared plus lambda squared, which trades a little tracking for bounded joint speed.
>
> **[Leader–follower]** The dynamics are M q-double-dot plus C q-dot plus g equals tau. In hand guiding, gravity dominates, so the leader's motors output only the gravity torque computed from URDF masses and centres of mass; stiffness and damping are zero, so the operator feels only friction and inertia. The follower receives the mapped position and velocity with per-joint gains. Zero offsets and directions are handled per joint.
>
> **[Base]** Differential-drive kinematics: wheel speeds are v plus or minus omega times half the wheelbase. Encoder feedback closes a PID loop on the board, and odometry integrates the arc length of each wheel into a pose.
>
> **[Validation]** I validated on real hardware. The follower tracked the leader with about 0.01 radian RMS joint error, the end-effector steady error was under five millimetres, and the base drove a two-metre straight line with under five centimetres of sideways drift. One real fault was the base drifting sideways; I traced it to wheel-parameter error and fixed it with straight-line and in-place rotation tests.
>
> **[Takeaway]** I learned to localise faults by layer: algorithm, ROS, driver, bus, motor, and to make every layer's failure behaviour explicit.

中文：背景：移动底盘上的双臂平台，可用 VR 手柄控制，或手拖主臂、从臂复制，统一走 ROS 2–CAN 链。VR：相对映射（离合器保存两个起点；位置=起点+s·R_a·位移，s 默认 1、精细 0.5；旋转共轭后左乘）；Mink 每周期解小 QP（姿态任务、阻尼、限位）后以 5 ms 步长积分；伸直时最小奇异值趋零，普通逆放大 1/σ，DLS 增益 σ/(σ²+λ²) 以少量跟踪换有界速度。主从：M q̈+C q̇+g=τ，手拖时重力主导，主臂只发重力矩、刚度阻尼为零；从臂收位置与速度；逐关节处理零位与方向。底盘：v±ωb/2，板端编码器 PID，里程计积弧长。验证：RMS 约 0.01 rad、末端稳态 <5 mm、2 m 直线横偏 <5 cm；真实故障：底盘跑偏，查到轮参数误差，用直线与原地转测试修好。收获：按层定位，并让每层的失效行为显式。

易说错：别把“5 ms”说成“程序以 200 Hz 运行”（实测约 120 Hz）；别说 Mink 是“我写的求解器”。

## 2. 深挖问答

> 格式：**L1** = 20–40 秒口语回答；**L2/L3** = 面试官追问及回答。每题末尾有中文译文。

### A. 总览与职责

**1. What exactly was your role?**

- **L1.** I was the robotics software intern on dual-arm and base control. I owned the VR pose mapping and the Mink IK configuration and tuning, the gravity-compensation parameters and follower gains, and the differential-drive kinematics, wheel-speed PID and odometry integration with ROS 2. The motor driver wrapper, the STM32 CAN framework and the VR capture client already existed in the team.
- **L2.** *Which part is hardest to attribute to you?* The firmware. I read it closely and used it for validation, but I did not design its framework.
- **L3.** *What would you change in it?* The scheduler delay and the host-heartbeat logic, which I flagged to the team.

中文：L1：我是双臂与底盘控制的软件实习生，负责 VR 位姿映射与 Mink IK 的配置整定、重力补偿参数与从臂增益、差速运动学/轮速 PID/里程计与 ROS 2 集成；电机驱动封装、STM32 CAN 框架、VR 采集端是团队已有。L2：最难界定的是固件——我读懂并用于验证，没设计框架。L3：想改调度器延时和上位机心跳逻辑，我已向团队指出。

**2. Why three different control levels?**

- **L1.** Because the tasks differ. Pose targets need inverse kinematics, the leader has to be compliant so it gets a pure torque command, the follower must reproduce joints so it uses position and velocity, and the base needs a speed loop with feedback.
- **L2.** *Could one controller do all of it?* In principle impedance control could unify arms, but the interfaces and failure modes differ, so I kept them separate and simple.
- **L3.** *What is the shared part?* The execution chain: ROS 2 topics, the hardware node, CAN, and layered limits and timeouts.

中文：L1：任务不同——位姿目标要 IK；主臂要柔顺所以纯力矩；从臂要复现关节所以位置+速度；底盘要带反馈的速度环。L2：阻抗控制原则上可统一手臂，但接口与失效模式不同，所以分开保持简单。L3：共享的是执行链：话题、硬件节点、CAN、分层限幅与超时。

**3. What does "validated on real hardware" involve?**

- **L1.** Commands went through ROS 2 and CAN to the motors, and I checked encoder feedback. For the arms: joint direction against the encoder, follower RMS error of about 0.01 radian, end-effector steady error under 5 millimetres, and no joint touching its limit. For the base: a two-metre straight run with under 5 centimetres of sideways drift.
- **L2.** *How did you measure the follower error?* I overlaid leader and follower joint angle curves; steady error was under 0.02 radian, and during motion the lag was about three control cycles.
- **L3.** *Why is simulation not enough?* Sign errors, delays, friction and bus problems only show up on the real chain.

中文：L1：命令真的经 ROS 2–CAN 到电机，看编码器反馈：臂——关节方向与编码器一致、从臂 RMS 约 0.01 rad、末端稳态 <5 mm、无触限位；底盘——2 m 直线横偏 <5 cm。L2：叠加主从关节曲线；稳态 <0.02 rad，运动中滞后约 3 个周期。L3：符号、延迟、摩擦、总线问题只在真链路暴露。

**4. How did you localise faults?**

- **L1.** By layer. I look at the target on the ROS topic, then the CAN frame fields, then the encoder feedback. The fault sits between the first two layers where the data stops being right.
- **L2.** *Give an example.* If the topic target is right but the joint moves the wrong way, the sign is in the driver mapping.
- **L3.** *What tools?* `ros2 topic echo` and `hz`, `candump` with time deltas, link error counters, and rosbag for replay.

中文：L1：按层——话题上的目标、CAN 帧字段、编码器反馈；故障在数据开始出错的两层之间。L2：话题目标对、关节反向，则符号在驱动映射。L3：ros2 topic echo/hz、带时间差的 candump、链路错误计数、rosbag 回放。

**5. How big was the team and how did you work with them?**

- **L1.** I was one of the software people around the platform, with the mechanical and electrical engineers and the people who owned the VR client and the motor library. I agreed interfaces first — topic names, units, joint order — and then worked in my own nodes.
- **L2.** *Any disagreement?* On who owns safety limits; we settled on layered limits, each owner responsible for their layer.
- **L3.** *What did you learn?* Write interface decisions down; most integration bugs were unit or order mismatches.

中文：L1：我是平台软件人员之一，与机械、电气及维护 VR 客户端和电机库的同事合作；先定接口（话题名、单位、关节顺序）再在自己的节点开发。L2：分歧在安全限位归属——定为分层限幅各自负责。L3：接口决策要写下来；多数集成 bug 是单位或顺序不一致。

**6. Why did this internship interest you?**

- **L1.** It connects algorithms to physical execution: IK and dynamics on one side, CAN frames and wheel encoders on the other. I wanted to see how a plan becomes a safe physical motion.
- **L2.** *How does it connect to your later work?* My platform internship later focused on a safe-execution chain for task plans; this was the first time I saw why each layer needs its own limits and timeouts.
- **L3.** *What do you want to study further?* Learning-based control and perception, because I have mostly practical experience and want the theory behind it.

中文：L1：它把算法接到物理执行：一端是 IK 与动力学，一端是 CAN 帧与编码器；我想看计划如何变成安全的物理运动。L2：后来的平台实习聚焦任务计划的安全执行链，这次让我第一次理解每层都要有自己的限幅与超时。L3：想深入基于学习的控制与感知，补理论。

### B. VR 映射

**7. Why relative instead of absolute mapping?**

- **L1.** The operator's origin and range differ from the robot's. Absolute mapping would make the arm jump when the operator starts and could ask for unreachable targets. A clutch stores start poses, so only the displacement matters.
- **L2.** *What does the clutch do on release?* It clears the reference; pressing again stores new references so the arm never jumps.
- **L3.** *What if the trigger flickers?* I use hysteresis: press at 0.9, release at 0.7.

中文：L1：操作者与机器人的原点和范围不同；绝对映射起步会跳、目标可能不可达；离合器保存起点，只用位移。L2：松开清参考，再按重存，手臂不跳。L3：触发键用滞回 0.9/0.7。

**8. Write the position and rotation formulas.**

- **L1.** Position: p_d equals p_r0 plus s times R_a times the controller displacement. Rotation: R_d equals R_a times delta-R times R_a transpose, multiplied on the left of R_r0, where delta-R is the controller's relative rotation.
- **L2.** *What are R_a and s?* R_a maps controller axes to base axes: minus z forward, minus x left, y up in the XR frame goes to x forward, y left, z up; s is one by default and 0.5 for fine work.
- **L3.** *Do you scale rotation?* No, rotation is one to one; scaling a rotation needs axis-angle or SLERP.

中文：L1：位置 p_d=p_r0+s·R_a·位移；旋转 R_d=(R_a ΔR R_aᵀ)R_r0，ΔR 为手柄相对旋转。L2：R_a 把手柄轴映射到基座轴（XR 的 −z 前、−x 左、y 上 → x 前、y 左、z 上）；s 默认 1，精细 0.5。L3：旋转 1:1；缩放旋转需轴角或 SLERP。

**9. Why conjugate by R_a?**

- **L1.** The relative rotation is expressed in the VR frame. The similarity transform R_a ΔR R_a transpose expresses the same rotation about the corresponding axes in the robot frame, keeping the angle.
- **L2.** *What if you skipped it?* Turning the controller about its vertical axis would rotate the gripper about the wrong axis.
- **L3.** *How do you check?* Rotate 30 degrees about one controller axis at a time and see the gripper turn about the matching base axis.

中文：L1：相对旋转在 VR 系下表达，相似变换把同一旋转表达到机器人系，角度不变。L2：不做则绕错轴。L3：逐轴转 30° 看夹爪是否绕对应基座轴。

**10. Why left multiplication?**

- **L1.** Left multiplication rotates about fixed base axes; right multiplication rotates about the gripper's own axes, which would feel like the gripper rolls.
- **L2.** *Show with an example.* Gripper pointing forward, rotate about the vertical axis: left gives yaw, right gives roll if the gripper's own axis is horizontal.
- **L3.** *When would you use right multiplication?* When the increment is defined in the tool frame, such as jogging along the tool axis.

中文：L1：左乘绕固定基座轴，右乘绕夹爪自身轴，手感像夹爪在滚。L2：夹爪朝前绕竖轴转：左乘是偏航，右乘则变滚转。L3：增量定义在工具系时（如沿工具轴微动）用右乘。

**11. How do you verify R_a?**

- **L1.** Orthonormal with determinant plus one, checked by assertion at startup, and single-axis motion tests with small amplitude: forward, left, up.
- **L2.** *What does determinant minus one mean?* A mirror image, which no rotation can produce; the arm would move mirrored.
- **L3.** *How do you derive R_a?* Write each controller axis expressed in base coordinates as a column.

中文：L1：启动时断言正交且行列式 +1；小幅度单轴测试（前、左、上）。L2：−1 是镜像，旋转产生不了。L3：把手柄各轴在基座系下的方向写成列。

**12. Quaternion pitfalls?**

- **L1.** Two: component order, xyzw versus wxyz, and the sign ambiguity, since q and minus q are the same rotation, so linear blending can cancel.
- **L2.** *How do you fix the sign?* Before blending, flip one if their dot product is negative.
- **L3.** *Did you use SLERP?* No; a linear blend with normalisation is fine for small steps after the hemisphere fix.

中文：L1：分量顺序（xyzw/wxyz）与符号二义性（q 与 −q 相同，线性融合会相消）。L2：融合前点积为负则翻符号。L3：没用 SLERP；小步长下半球校正后线性融合并归一化即可。

**13. What filter did you use on the VR data, and what delay does it add?**

- **L1.** A first-order low-pass on the target. With alpha about 0.3 at 100 hertz, the time constant is about 23 milliseconds, enough to hide hand tremor without feeling laggy.
- **L2.** *Formula?* Time constant is T times one minus alpha over alpha.
- **L3.** *Why not a bigger filter?* More smoothing means more lag, which hurts the operator's sense of direct control.

中文：L1：对目标做一阶低通；100 Hz、α≈0.3 时时间常数约 23 ms，滤掉手抖又不觉得迟滞。L2：τ=T(1−α)/α。L3：更强滤波=更大延迟，伤害直接操控感。

**14. What is the TCP offset issue?**

- **L1.** The point the operator holds differs from the gripper's tool centre by a fixed transform, about 8 centimetres along the controller's minus z axis. Ignoring it makes rotations about the hand look like rotations about the wrist.
- **L2.** *How did you get the number?* Measured on the handle and the gripper and confirmed by a pure rotation test where the gripper centre should stay put.
- **L3.** *What if it is wrong?* The tool centre drifts during rotation.

中文：L1：握持点与夹爪 TCP 差一个固定变换（沿手柄 −z 约 8 cm）；忽略则绕手的旋转变成绕腕的旋转。L2：量得并用纯旋转测试确认夹爪中心不动。L3：错了则旋转时工具中心漂移。

### C. MuJoCo 与 Mink

**15. Why MuJoCo?**

- **L1.** It supplies the robot model, kinematics and Jacobians that Mink needs, plus a visualisation for checking targets. The real arm needs joint commands, not MuJoCo itself.
- **L2.** *Did you run physics simulation?* I used it as a kinematic model, not as the physics of the real arm.
- **L3.** *Why not KDL directly?* Mink is built on MuJoCo's model; KDL I used for dynamics.

中文：L1：提供 Mink 需要的模型、运动学与雅可比，并可视化检查目标；真机要的是关节命令。L2：当运动学模型用，不是真机物理。L3：Mink 基于 MuJoCo 模型；动力学用 KDL。

**16. qpos, qvel, ctrl?**

- **L1.** Generalised positions, generalised velocities, and actuator inputs. The meaning of ctrl depends on the actuator type.
- **L2.** *Why can nq differ from nv?* A free or ball joint has a quaternion: four position numbers but three velocity numbers.
- **L3.** *So how do you integrate?* With the library's integrate function, not by adding qvel times dt to qpos.

中文：L1：广义位置、广义速度、执行器输入，ctrl 含义取决于执行器类型。L2：自由/球关节用四元数：4 个位置数、3 个速度数。L3：用库的积分函数，不是 qpos+=qvel·dt。

**17. What are a FrameTask and a PostureTask?**

- **L1.** A frame task drives a frame to a target pose, weighted in position and orientation. A posture task is a low-weight preference to stay near a reference configuration, which resolves redundancy.
- **L2.** *Why a posture task for a seven-joint arm?* Six task dimensions and seven joints leave a null space; without a preference the elbow can drift.
- **L3.** *Your weights?* Position cost 1, orientation cost 0.5, posture 0.01, as configured values, not performance numbers.

中文：L1：帧任务把某坐标系驱动到目标位姿；姿态任务是低权重的偏好，消冗余。L2：6 维任务 7 个关节有零空间，没偏好肘部会漂。L3：权重 1/0.5/0.01，是配置值非性能指标。

**18. Why one QP for both hands?**

- **L1.** They share the decision variables if the torso joints are shared; even with independent arms, one problem gives a single solve, global constraints and consistent timing.
- **L2.** *Are the arms independent in your robot?* Yes, two independent seven-joint arms with no shared waist joint, so the problem is block-diagonal.
- **L3.** *Then why combine?* Convenience and a common limit/damping setup, and room to add a collision constraint between arms.

中文：L1：共享决策变量时必须合并；即使独立，一个问题也只需一次求解、全局约束、时序一致。L2：本机两臂独立 7 自由度，无共享腰，问题是分块对角。L3：合并是为了统一的限位/阻尼设置，并便于加双臂间碰撞约束。

**19. Why a 5 ms step?**

- **L1.** It is the integration period in the solver's configuration, not the measured loop rate. The real loop ran at about 120 hertz, and I publish targets at 100 hertz.
- **L2.** *What goes wrong if they differ?* If the loop is slower than the configured step, joints move less per cycle than intended and tracking feels slow.
- **L3.** *How do you check?* Log the real loop period and compare it to the step.

中文：L1：它是求解器配置的积分步长，不是实测频率；实际循环约 120 Hz，目标发布 100 Hz。L2：循环慢于配置步长则每周期位移偏小，跟随变慢。L3：记录实测周期与步长对比。

**20. Do the task weights mean percentages of importance?**

- **L1.** No. Position and orientation have different units, so the weights are relative preferences in a least-squares cost, not percentages.
- **L2.** *How do you tune?* Start with position dominant, add orientation until the gripper follows rotation without fighting position, keep posture tiny.
- **L3.** *What breaks it?* Making posture too large makes the end effector lag.

中文：L1：不是；位置与姿态单位不同，权重是最小二乘中的相对偏好。L2：位置为主，逐步加姿态至转动跟随且不抢位置，姿态任务很小。L3：姿态权重过大会让末端滞后。

**21. What if the solver fails?**

- **L1.** My wrapper returns the current configuration for that step. That is not a safe stop, so I count failures and the hardware node has its own timeouts.
- **L2.** *What threshold?* Three consecutive failures raise a warning; more than ten escalate to hold-and-alarm.
- **L3.** *Why not stop at once?* A single failure is common near limits; escalating avoids false stops.

中文：L1：包装层返回当前构型——不是安全停机，所以计失败次数，硬件节点另有超时。L2：连续 3 次告警，超过 10 次升级为保持并报警。L3：单次失败在限位附近常见，分级避免误停。

### D. 逆运动学与奇异

**22. What is the Jacobian?**

- **L1.** The map from joint velocities to end-effector velocity. Column j is the effect of joint j alone.
- **L2.** *Dimensions?* Six by seven for this arm.
- **L3.** *What does the null space mean?* Joint motions that leave the end effector unchanged, one dimension here.

中文：L1：关节速度到末端速度的映射，第 j 列是第 j 个关节单独的效果。L2：本臂 6×7。L3：零空间=不改变末端的关节运动，这里一维。

**23. What is a singularity and why does it hurt?**

- **L1.** A pose where the Jacobian loses rank, for example a fully stretched arm. The inverse gain is one over the smallest singular value, so a tiny target move needs a huge joint speed.
- **L2.** *Numbers?* For a two-link arm, 1 metre per second needs about 1 radian per second at a 90-degree elbow but about 22 at 5.7 degrees.
- **L3.** *Does the real arm reach it?* Near it, yes; so the log tracks the smallest singular value.

中文：L1：雅可比降秩的位姿（如完全伸直）；逆增益=1/σ_min，目标微动需巨大关节速度。L2：两连杆例：肘 90° 约 1 rad/s，5.7° 约 22 rad/s。L3：真机会靠近，所以日志记录最小奇异值。

**24. Explain damped least squares.**

- **L1.** It minimises the task error squared plus lambda squared times joint speed squared. The gain per singular direction becomes sigma over sigma squared plus lambda squared, with a maximum of one over two lambda.
- **L2.** *What do you pay?* A tracking residual in the weak directions.
- **L3.** *How to choose lambda?* Large enough to cap speed near singularity, small enough not to hurt tracking elsewhere; in our config 0.05 per task and a small global damping of 1e-3, with an adaptive option.

中文：L1：最小化任务误差平方+λ²·关节速度平方，每个奇异方向增益 σ/(σ²+λ²)，最大 1/(2λ)。L2：代价是弱方向跟踪残差。L3：足够限速又不伤别处跟踪；配置里每任务 0.05、全局 1e-3，也可自适应。

**25. Is damping a joint limit?**

- **L1.** No. Damping is a soft penalty on speed, limits are hard constraints. Damping does not stop a joint at its bound.
- **L2.** *So how do you enforce limits?* Inside the QP as bounds on the increment, plus clamps at the driver.
- **L3.** *Why both?* The QP can fail or be bypassed; the driver clamp is the last software guard.

中文：L1：不是；阻尼是软惩罚，限位是硬约束，阻尼拦不住关节到边界。L2：QP 内对增量加界，驱动层再钳位。L3：QP 可能失败或被绕过，驱动钳位是最后软件保护。

**26. How did you monitor singularity?**

- **L1.** The smallest singular value, maximum joint speed and task residual in the log. I flagged anything below about 0.05, and joint speed spikes only appeared below that.
- **L2.** *What did you do on a flag?* Slowed the target near that region.
- **L3.** *Alternative?* Manipulability measure; similar information.

中文：L1：日志记录最小奇异值、最大关节速度、任务残差；低于约 0.05 标红，尖峰只在其下出现。L2：标红时降低目标速度。L3：也可用可操作度，信息类似。

**27. A seven-joint arm is redundant. How do you use that?**

- **L1.** The posture task pulls the null-space motion toward a comfortable elbow, so the end-effector task has priority and the elbow does not wander.
- **L2.** *Null-space projection?* N equals identity minus J-plus J; adding motion in N does not disturb the end effector.
- **L3.** *Risk?* Near singularity the projection is poorly conditioned; damping helps.

中文：L1：姿态任务把零空间运动拉向舒适肘位，末端任务优先，肘不漂。L2：N=I−J⁺J，在 N 内加运动不扰动末端。L3：近奇异投影病态，需阻尼。

### E. 约束

**28. Which hard constraints did you use?**

- **L1.** Position and velocity bounds on the joint increment: joint speed limits of 2.5 radians per second for joints one to four and 4 for five to seven, and position limits pulled in 0.05 radian from the URDF limits.
- **L2.** *Why lower than the URDF speed?* The URDF numbers are 36, 14, 7; the teleoperation limits are a safety choice.
- **L3.** *Collision?* Optional distance constraints; I kept work-space box limits instead.

中文：L1：对关节增量的位置/速度界：J1–4 取 2.5 rad/s、J5–7 取 4 rad/s；位置限位在 URDF 内缩 0.05 rad。L2：URDF 是 36/14/7；遥操作限制是安全取值。L3：碰撞约束可选；我用了工作空间盒。

**29. Why not clip afterwards?**

- **L1.** Clipping changes the direction of motion. In a small example, clipping after gave a 16-degree direction error versus 8 degrees with the bound inside the QP.
- **L2.** *Why does it change direction?* Each joint is scaled independently.
- **L3.** *Any case for clipping?* As a last-resort guard, yes.

中文：L1：事后裁剪改变运动方向；小例子中事后裁剪方向误差 16°，QP 内加界 8°。L2：各关节被独立缩放。L3：作为最后一道保护可以。

**30. How do position limits work in the QP?**

- **L1.** I bound the increment by a gain smaller than one times the remaining distance to the limit, so the joint approaches the limit asymptotically and never crosses it.
- **L2.** *Margin?* 0.05 radian inside URDF limits.
- **L3.** *Drawback?* Slow final approach.

中文：L1：增量上界=小于 1 的系数×到限位的剩余距离，关节渐近靠近而不越过。L2：URDF 内缩 0.05 rad。L3：末段接近变慢。

**31. What are the motor-side limits?**

- **L1.** MIT ranges for position, speed and torque, and clamping before packing. Unclamped packing wraps and can flip torque sign, for instance plus 11 newton metres on a 10 newton metre motor decoding as about minus 9.
- **L2.** *Who fixed this?* The team's newer driver version clamps; I found the problem by reading and unit-testing.
- **L3.** *What else?* Torque rate limits upstream.

中文：L1：MIT 位置/速度/力矩量程及打包前钳位；不钳位会回绕翻符号（+11 N·m 在 10 N·m 电机上解码约 −9）。L2：团队新版驱动已钳位；我是读码+单测发现。L3：上游还有力矩斜率限制。

### F. 动力学与主从

**32. Why can the leader be pushed so easily?**

- **L1.** Stiffness and damping are zero and the motors cancel gravity, so the operator feels only friction and a little inertia.
- **L2.** *Why not zero friction too?* Friction is partly compensated with a small smooth term, but over-compensation causes instability.
- **L3.** *What does the operator still feel?* Gearbox friction and inertia.

中文：L1：刚度阻尼为零且电机抵消重力，只剩摩擦与少量惯性。L2：摩擦只用小的平滑项补偿，过补偿会不稳定。L3：仍有减速器摩擦与惯性。

**33. Derive the gravity torque of two links.**

- **L1.** Differentiate potential energy with respect to each joint angle. For unit masses, unit lengths at the horizontal configuration, the shoulder needs 19.6 and the elbow 4.9 newton metres.
- **L2.** *Why does the shoulder need more?* It carries both links.
- **L3.** *For the real arm?* KDL computes it from URDF masses and centres of mass.

中文：L1：势能对各关节角求偏导；单位质量/长度水平位形，肩 19.6、肘 4.9 N·m。L2：肩承担两连杆。L3：真机由 KDL 用 URDF 质量与质心算。

**34. Why gravity only?**

- **L1.** Coriolis scales with speed squared; hand guiding is slow, so gravity dominates. I keep Coriolis and friction as small optional terms.
- **L2.** *How much Coriolis did you use?* A 0.1 scale, mostly off.
- **L3.** *Why not more?* Larger gains excited oscillation with the control delay.

中文：L1：科氏项∝速度平方，手拖很慢，重力主导；科氏与摩擦仅作为小的可选项。L2：科氏比例 0.1，基本关闭。L3：更大增益与控制延迟配合会激励振荡。

**35. What did URDF/KDL give you?**

- **L1.** Masses, centres of mass and joint axes, from which a tree gives the generalised gravity torques for the current joint angles.
- **L2.** *Your repo uses Pinocchio?* The reference code uses Pinocchio; my CV says KDL; the maths is the same.
- **L3.** *Which numbers are uncertain?* Payload mass and centre of mass at the last joint.

中文：L1：质量、质心、关节轴，据此由运动树得到当前关节角的广义重力矩。L2：参考代码用 Pinocchio，CV 写 KDL，数学相同。L3：最不确定的是末端关节的负载质量与质心。

**36. What can go wrong with gravity compensation?**

- **L1.** Sign, joint order, zero offset and inaccurate masses. Sign errors make the arm heavier; mass errors make it drift.
- **L2.** *How do you test the sign?* Hold the arm horizontal, command only the gravity torque for one joint, and see if it floats or falls.
- **L3.** *Then?* Raise the compensation ratio from 0.5 toward one in steps.

中文：L1：符号、关节顺序、零位偏置、质量不准；符号错手臂更重，质量错会漂。L2：臂水平、单关节只发重力项，看飘还是掉。L3：补偿比例从 0.5 逐步升到 1。

**37. Why is the compensation ratio 0.8 on the last joint?**

- **L1.** Its payload — gripper or handle — has the least certain mass and centre of mass. Slightly under-compensating is safer than over-compensating, which makes the joint creep upwards.
- **L2.** *How did you choose 0.8?* I raised the ratio from 0.5; above about 0.9 the joint drifted up slowly after release, so I settled on 0.8 with a 10 to 20 percent margin.
- **L3.** *Other joints?* One.

中文：L1：末端负载（夹爪/手柄）质量质心最不确定；略欠补偿比过补偿（上漂）安全。L2：从 0.5 往上调，约 0.9 以上松手后缓慢上漂，定 0.8 留 10–20% 余量。L3：其余关节为 1。

**38. What are the MIT fields?**

- **L1.** Desired position, desired velocity, Kp, Kd and feed-forward torque; the motor computes torque as Kp times position error plus Kd times velocity error plus feed-forward.
- **L2.** *Which does the leader use?* Kp zero, Kd small or zero, feed-forward gravity torque.
- **L3.** *Which does the follower use?* Position and velocity from the leader with per-joint Kp and Kd.

中文：L1：期望位置、速度、Kp、Kd、前馈力矩；τ=Kp·e+Kd·ė+τff。L2：主臂 Kp=0、Kd 小或 0、前馈重力矩。L3：从臂用主臂的位置速度及逐关节 Kp/Kd。

**39. What gains did the follower use?**

- **L1.** Kp of 50 for joints one to four and 10 for five to seven; Kd of 1.2 for the first four, then 0.3, 0.2, 0.3. It has velocity feed-forward but no gravity feed-forward.
- **L2.** *Consequence of no gravity feed-forward?* A steady sag of about g over Kp, so a high Kp is needed to keep the error under 0.02 radian.
- **L3.** *What would you add?* Gravity feed-forward to cut the sag without raising Kp.

中文：L1：Kp 前四关节 50、后三 10；Kd 1.2×4 后 0.3、0.2、0.3；有速度前馈、无重力前馈。L2：稳态下垂≈g/Kp，故需较高 Kp 把误差控制在 0.02 rad 内。L3：加重力前馈以不抬 Kp 而减下垂。

**40. Why send velocity to the follower?**

- **L1.** It removes tracking lag: the steady lag in constant motion drops from about b omega over Kp to about Kd omega over Kp, relative to a plain position command with damping.
- **L2.** *Noise?* The leader velocity is low-pass filtered first.
- **L3.** *Risk?* Velocity noise injects jitter.

中文：L1：消除跟踪滞后：匀速时稳态滞后明显减小（相对仅位置+阻尼）。L2：主臂速度先低通。L3：速度噪声会注入抖动。

**41. End-to-end latency leader to follower?**

- **L1.** About 30 milliseconds: around 5 to read the leader, 5 to publish and receive, 2 on the bus, and 15 to 20 for the follower motor's inner loop.
- **L2.** *How do you measure it?* A quick small step on the leader, both joint curves on one timeline, and the time difference between their 10 and 50 percent points.
- **L3.** *Effect on stability?* Delay adds phase lag, so gains cannot be raised freely.

中文：L1：约 30 ms：读主臂约 5、发布接收约 5、总线约 2、从臂电机内环 15–20。L2：主臂快速小阶跃，同一时间轴叠两条曲线，读 10%–50% 处时间差。L3：延迟增加相位滞后，增益不能随意加大。

### G. 差速底盘

**42. Derive differential-drive kinematics.**

- **L1.** With wheelbase b, forward speed v and turn rate omega, the wheel speeds are v minus omega b over two for the left and v plus omega b over two for the right; inversely v is the mean and omega the difference over b.
- **L2.** *Example?* v 0.3, omega 0.5, b 0.75 gives 0.1125 and 0.4875 metres per second.
- **L3.** *Constraint?* Non-holonomic: no sideways velocity.

中文：L1：左=v−ωb/2，右=v+ωb/2；反之 v 为均值、ω 为差/b。L2：v=0.3、ω=0.5、b=0.75 得 0.1125 与 0.4875 m/s。L3：非完整约束，无侧向速度。

**43. How is the wheel loop closed?**

- **L1.** The board reads wheel speed from the driver in rpm at 200 hertz and runs a PID per wheel with Kp 5 and Ki 0.5, an integral limit of 80 and an output limit of 200. The driver's own current loop is not mine.
- **L2.** *Why add integral?* With proportional only there is a steady-state error; integral removes it, with a limit against windup.
- **L3.** *Mode switches?* Clear the integrators and the command.

中文：L1：板端 200 Hz 读轮速 rpm，每轮 PID：Kp 5、Ki 0.5，积分限幅 80，输出限幅 200；驱动器自带电流环不归我调。L2：仅比例有稳态误差，积分消除，限幅防饱和。L3：模式切换清积分与命令。

**44. How do you convert wheel rpm to metres per second?**

- **L1.** Multiply by two pi over sixty and the wheel radius. With a radius of 0.13 metres, one rpm is 0.0136 metres per second, so 48 rpm is 0.65.
- **L2.** *Wheel parameters?* Radius 0.13, wheelbase 0.75.
- **L3.** *How were they calibrated?* A five-metre straight run for radius, ten in-place turns for wheelbase, three repeats each.

中文：L1：×2π/60×半径；r=0.13 时 1 rpm=0.0136 m/s，48 rpm≈0.65。L2：半径 0.13、轮距 0.75。L3：5 m 直线标半径、原地转 10 圈标轮距，各重复 3 次。

**45. How is odometry integrated?**

- **L1.** Each sample, each wheel's distance is its speed times a constant; the mean is forward distance and the difference over the wheelbase is the heading change; I rotate the body step into the world frame using the mid-angle.
- **L2.** *Accuracy?* About 2 percent over a 5-metre round trip and roughly 30 degrees accumulated over ten in-place turns.
- **L3.** *Why does it drift?* Slip, wheel-size error and a fixed sample time; fusing an IMU yaw would help.

中文：L1：每次采样每轮位移=速度×常量，均值为前进距离，差/轮距为航向增量，用中间角旋转到世界系。L2：5 m 往返约 2%，原地转 10 圈累积约 30°。L3：打滑、轮径误差、固定采样时间；融合 IMU yaw 可改善。

**46. Teleoperated versus autonomous mode?**

- **L1.** Only the source of the velocity command differs: joystick mapping with a dead zone in remote mode, a CAN command from the ROS 2 side in autonomous mode. Both feed the same kinematics, wheel PID and odometry.
- **L2.** *Protect mode?* Forces zero speed and clears integrators.
- **L3.** *Switching safety?* Zero commands on every switch.

中文：L1：只有速度命令来源不同——遥控摇杆（带死区）与自动模式下 ROS 2 经 CAN 的命令；后面共用运动学/轮速 PID/里程计。L2：保护模式强制零速并清积分。L3：每次切换先清零。

**47. What happens if the host stops sending?**

- **L1.** On the ROS side, the bridge sets zero speed after 300 milliseconds without a velocity command. The board has a heartbeat rule, but I found while reading that its flag is never updated, so I did not rely on it.
- **L2.** *Did you fix the firmware?* No; I reported it and covered the risk on the host side.
- **L3.** *Better design?* A heartbeat counter reset by each valid frame, checked with greater-or-equal.

中文：L1：ROS 侧 300 ms 无速度命令则零速；板子有心跳规则，但读码发现标志位从未更新，所以不依赖。L2：没改固件，已上报并在上位机侧覆盖。L3：更好的设计：每个有效帧清零计数器，用 ≥ 判断。

**48. Why is the right wheel sign flipped?**

- **L1.** The right motor is mounted mirrored, so a positive forward command must be negated. The sign convention has to be consistent across reference, feedback and output.
- **L2.** *Classic bug?* Negating the reference and also the feedback, so the PID compares opposite signs and becomes positive feedback.
- **L3.** *Fix?* Run PID in one positive-forward convention and flip only at the output.

中文：L1：右电机镜像安装，向前命令需取负；给定/反馈/输出符号约定必须一致。L2：经典 bug：给定取负、反馈又取负，PID 比较反号，成正反馈。L3：PID 在统一正方向下算，只在输出处翻转。

### H. ROS2-CAN 与固件

**49. Describe your ROS 2 to CAN chain.**

- **L1.** Algorithm nodes publish joint targets or velocity commands. A hardware node keeps the latest target, and on a fixed 100 hertz timer maps joints to motors, clamps, packs frames and sends over SocketCAN. Replies are decoded and published as joint states or odometry.
- **L2.** *Why a timer?* Stable CAN period, and it still runs if the upstream dies so I can check target age.
- **L3.** *QoS?* Best effort, depth one for commands.

中文：L1：算法节点发目标；硬件节点缓存最新目标，100 Hz 定时器映射、钳位、打包并经 SocketCAN 发送；回包解码后发布。L2：周期稳定，上游挂了仍可检查目标年龄。L3：命令用 best effort、深度 1。

**50. What happens if a node dies?**

- **L1.** Layered timeouts: the hardware node freezes the arm after 100 milliseconds without a target and disables it after 500; the base zeroes after 300 without a velocity command; the motors disable themselves after about a second of bus silence.
- **L2.** *Why freeze first?* A disabled arm falls under gravity; 100 milliseconds is usually jitter.
- **L3.** *What if the hardware node dies?* The motors' own timeout is the last layer.

中文：L1：分层超时：100 ms 冻结、500 ms 失能；底盘 300 ms 零速；电机总线静默约 1 s 自失能。L2：失能会下坠，100 ms 多为抖动。L3：硬件节点也挂了，则靠电机自带超时。

**51. Why two CAN buses?**

- **L1.** One arm needs about 16 frames per cycle, around 1.8 milliseconds, limiting a single bus to a few hundred hertz. Two arms on separate buses double capacity and isolate faults.
- **L2.** *Bus rate?* 1 megabit per second, classic 8-byte frames, no data-rate switching.
- **L3.** *Load?* About 36 percent at 200 hertz per arm.

中文：L1：每臂每周期约 16 帧（约 1.8 ms），单总线上限几百 Hz；两臂分总线容量翻倍并隔离故障。L2：1 Mbit/s，经典 8 字节帧，无速率切换。L3：每臂 200 Hz 时负载约 36%。

**52. How do you encode an MIT frame?**

- **L1.** Map each value linearly to an unsigned integer over its range — 16 bits for position, 12 bits for the other four — and pack into eight bytes. Clamp before quantising.
- **L2.** *Resolution?* About 0.02 degrees for position, and 5 milli-newton-metres for torque on the small motor.
- **L3.** *Why does it matter?* Torque quantisation sets the smallest gravity-compensation step.

中文：L1：各量在量程内线性量化（位置 16 位，其余 12 位），打包 8 字节；先钳位。L2：位置分辨率约 0.02°，小电机力矩约 5 mN·m。L3：力矩量化决定重力补偿的最小台阶。

**53. Walk me through the chassis firmware.**

- **L1.** An STM32H7 with a cooperative scheduler running two tasks. One reads the SBUS remote and selects protect, remote or auto mode; the other maps speeds to wheel targets, sends CAN speed commands, reads feedback in the CAN interrupt, integrates odometry every 5 milliseconds and reports it to the host.
- **L2.** *CAN timing?* 80 megahertz kernel clock, 80 time quanta per bit, one microsecond, 75 percent sample point.
- **L3.** *Downsides of the scheduler?* No preemption, and the delay function probably does nothing in that snapshot.

中文：L1：STM32H7，协作式调度两个任务：一个读 SBUS 选模式，一个映射轮速、发 CAN 命令、中断读反馈、每 5 ms 积分里程计并上报。L2：80 MHz 内核、每位 80 量子=1 µs、采样点 75%。L3：无抢占，快照里延时函数很可能无效。

**54. What risks did you find in the firmware?**

- **L1.** The host heartbeat flag is never updated, so the lost-host rule cannot fire; remote-lost detection only checks one channel; the odometry is packed as 16-bit millimetres and wraps near 32 metres. I found them by reading code, not from a failure, and covered the first on the host side.
- **L2.** *Anything on the bus?* An uninitialised frame is sent in the auto branch.
- **L3.** *How would you prioritise?* Fix the heartbeat first, because it is a safety path.

中文：L1：心跳标志不更新、遥控失联只查一个通道、里程计 int16 毫米约 32 m 回绕；读码发现而非故障，第一条在上位机侧覆盖。L2：自动分支发送了未初始化的帧。L3：先修心跳，因为是安全路径。

### I. 调试、验证与局限

**55. Tell me about a time a robot behaved unexpectedly.**

- **L1.** Near full arm extension one joint sped up. I plotted the smallest singular value next to maximum joint speed and saw spikes only below about 0.05. So it was near-singular amplification. I added damping, joint speed limits and slowed the target there, then repeated the stretch twenty times.
- **L2.** *Why not just clip speed?* Clipping changes direction; bounds inside the optimisation keep it consistent.
- **L3.** *How do you know it did not hide another problem?* I compared tracking error with and without damping; the residual rose only slightly.

中文：L1：伸直时某关节加速；奇异值与最大速度并排画，低于约 0.05 才有尖峰；加阻尼、速度约束、降低目标速度，并重复 20 次。L2：裁剪改方向；优化内加界方向一致。L3：对比有无阻尼的跟踪误差，残差仅略升。

**56. What are the limitations of your work?**

- **L1.** The follower has no gravity feed-forward, so it needs a high gain; the wheel odometry has no IMU fusion; and the VR pipeline depends on the quality of controller tracking.
- **L2.** *What would you do next?* Add gravity feed-forward on the follower and fuse an IMU yaw into odometry.
- **L3.** *Anything on safety?* Replace "hold" behaviours with explicit state machines.

中文：L1：从臂无重力前馈需高增益；轮式里程计无 IMU 融合；VR 管线依赖手柄追踪质量。L2：下一步给从臂加重力前馈、给里程计融合 IMU yaw。L3：把“保持”行为改为显式状态机。

**57. How did you validate tracking without ground truth?**

- **L1.** I used internal consistency: leader and follower curves overlaid, encoder feedback against command, end-effector steady error by a ruler at a held pose, and for the base a measured two-metre line.
- **L2.** *Limits of that?* No absolute accuracy claim; it shows repeatability and relative error.
- **L3.** *Better?* A motion-capture or external camera reference.

中文：L1：内部一致性——主从曲线叠加、编码器对指令、保持位姿时用尺量末端稳态误差、底盘量 2 m 直线。L2：不声称绝对精度，只表示重复性与相对误差。L3：更好用动捕或外部相机作参考。

**58. How do you log and reproduce?**

- **L1.** Every real-hardware run is recorded with rosbag: targets, joint states, the smallest singular value and measured loop periods. Fixed faults get a reproduction step and a pass criterion.
- **L2.** *Parameters?* In a versioned config file, not in code.
- **L3.** *Why?* So a bad run can be replayed with the exact parameters.

中文：L1：每次真机运行用 rosbag 记录目标、关节状态、最小奇异值与实测周期；修复的故障保留复现步骤与通过判据。L2：参数放有版本的配置文件。L3：这样坏数据可用同样参数复现。

**59. How do you decide a loop rate?**

- **L1.** Start from the physics and the interface: motor inner loop in kilohertz, my command around 100 to 200 hertz, VR data at 90 hertz. A command faster than its source adds no information.
- **L2.** *Measured rates?* IK about 120 hertz, gravity compensation about 200, targets published at 100.
- **L3.** *What limits them?* IK solve time and CAN round trip waits.

中文：L1：从物理与接口出发：电机内环 kHz 量级，我的命令 100–200 Hz，VR 数据 90 Hz；比数据源更快不增加信息。L2：IK 约 120 Hz、重力补偿约 200 Hz、目标发布 100 Hz。L3：受 IK 求解时间和 CAN 往返等待限制。

### J. 与 PS/推荐信的呼应与诚实回答

**60. How does this internship relate to your statement of purpose?**

- **L1.** My statement is about how a high-level decision becomes a safe physical action. In this internship I saw the physical end: layered limits, timeouts and explicit failure behaviour between a command and a motor.
- **L2.** *Concrete link?* A stale command must not be reused, which is the same principle as gating a plan before execution.
- **L3.** *What do you want to add?* Learning-based components on top of such a safe chain.

中文：L1：我的陈述关注高层决策如何变成安全的物理动作；这次实习让我看到物理端：命令到电机之间的分层限幅、超时与显式失效行为。L2：陈旧命令不能复用，与执行前给计划设门是同一原则。L3：想在这样的安全链上加入基于学习的部分。

**61. You don't remember a number. What do you say?**

## 3. STAR 案例（3 个）

### STAR 1：近奇异时关节暴冲

- **Situation.** During VR teleoperation tests on the real arm, whenever the operator reached far, the arm stretched out and one joint suddenly sped up, shaking the end effector.
- **Task.** Find the cause and keep joint speed bounded without losing tracking in normal poses.
- **Action.** I logged the smallest singular value of the Jacobian, the maximum joint speed and the task residual. The speed spikes only appeared when the smallest singular value fell below about 0.05, which pointed to near-singular amplification rather than noise. I raised the damping in the QP, enforced joint speed limits of 2.5 radians per second for the first four joints and 4 for the rest, and slowed the target near that region. I repeated a slow stretch toward the boundary twenty times.
- **Result.** Joint speeds stayed within the limits, the end effector moved smoothly near full extension, and tracking elsewhere was unchanged. I kept the stretch test as a regression check for later parameter changes.

中文：S：真机 VR 遥操作中，伸得远时手臂伸直，某关节突然加速、末端抖。T：找原因，保持关节速度有界且不损失常规位姿跟踪。A：记录最小奇异值、最大关节速度、任务残差；尖峰只在最小奇异值低于约 0.05 时出现，指向近奇异放大；QP 提高阻尼，J1–4 取 2.5 rad/s、其余 4 rad/s 速度约束，并在该区域降低目标速度；缓慢伸向边界重复 20 次。R：关节速度在限内，伸直附近末端平滑，其余区域跟踪不变；该伸展测试作为回归检查保留。

### STAR 2：底盘向一侧跑偏

- **Situation.** The differential base drifted sideways when commanded straight; over two metres it was off by roughly ten-plus centimetres, and the odometry heading was also wrong after a full turn.
- **Task.** Separate command problems from physical ones and bring the drift under five centimetres over two metres.
- **Action.** I fed both wheels equal targets and compared the feedback to rule out the command path. Then I calibrated the wheel radius with a five-metre straight run and the wheelbase with ten in-place turns, three repeats each, and checked the sign convention of the mirrored wheel. I wrote the calibrated values into a config file.
- **Result.** Sideways drift over two metres fell below five centimetres, the five-metre round-trip distance error was about two percent, and the heading accumulated about thirty degrees over ten turns. I noted that an IMU yaw would be the next improvement.

中文：S：差速底盘直行命令时向一侧偏，2 m 偏十几厘米，转一整圈里程计航向也不对。T：区分指令问题与物理问题，把 2 m 偏移降到 5 cm 内。A：给两轮相同目标比较反馈排除指令路径；5 m 直线标轮半径、原地转 10 圈标轮距（各 3 次），检查镜像轮符号；标定值写入配置文件。R：2 m 横偏 <5 cm；5 m 往返距离误差约 2%；10 圈航向累积约 30°；下一步是融合 IMU yaw。

### STAR 3：CAN 间歇断连

- **Situation.** During long tests one arm intermittently stopped responding; the reply wait timed out and joint states froze, and replugging fixed it for a while.
- **Task.** Find the root cause and make the system fail safely if it recurs.
- **Action.** I read the link error counters and watched the bus with timestamps, measured the termination resistance with power off, and wiggled the connectors while watching the counters; the error count jumped at one connector. I replaced it and made sure both bus ends had a 120-ohm resistor. In the hardware node I added a rule: a missing reply for more than three cycles raises a warning and more than ten disables the whole arm.
- **Result.** A 30-minute run showed no error growth and no missing replies, and the new rule meant a future loss would end in a controlled disable instead of control on stale data.

中文：S：长时间测试中一条臂间歇不响应，等回包超时、关节状态冻结，重插后恢复一段时间。T：找根因，并在复发时安全失效。A：读链路错误计数、带时间戳看总线、断电量终端电阻、边晃接头边看计数——某接头处计数跳升；更换并确保两端各 120 Ω；硬件节点加规则：回包缺失 >3 周期告警、>10 周期失能整臂。R：30 分钟运行无错误增长、无回包缺失；以后再丢会受控失能而不是用陈旧数据继续控制。

| # | 容易说错的 | 应该说 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | “Mink is my solver.” | “I configured and tuned Mink, a quadratic-programming IK library, for our robot.” |

## 5. 绝对不能说的话

- 不能把读码发现的隐患（心跳标志、未初始化帧、`task_delay`、int16 溢出）说成自己发现“并修复”的成果；只能说“读码发现并上报/在上位机侧兜底”。
- 不能说固件框架、电机驱动封装、VR 采集端是自己写的。
- 不能说 P2 是“纯仿真”或“没有真机”（CV：real hardware）。
- 不能给没有依据的精确数字（成功率、毫米级精度、毫秒级延迟）；只用本文件与 README 自查表的取值，并说明量级与测量方法。
- 不能把 5 ms 步长说成实测频率；不能把 Mink 的内部实现说成自己的。
- 不能把教学推算（如端到端预算、总线负载）说成实测。
- 不能声称有 CV 之外的成果（例如论文、发布）。
