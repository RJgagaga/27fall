# 99 英文面试口述与深挖问答（P3 RoboMaster 英雄机器人）

> **一句话定位**：把 P3 变成可以"说出来"的英文：30 秒、90 秒、3 分钟三种口述，58 个按 **L1 / L2 / L3 三层**组织的问答（每问都带中文译文），4 个 STAR 案例，以及"记不清细节时"的诚实回答模板。所有事实以 CV 为准；具体数字取自 [README 自查表](./README.md#实机细节自查表)的统一设计取值，与实机记忆不同时以实机为准并同步修改。

## 本章学完你能……

- 不看稿说出 30 秒、90 秒、3 分钟三种版本；
- 对 58 个问题中的任何一个，先给 L1（30 秒内的直接回答），被追问时给 L2（机制与理由），被深挖时给 L3（取舍、数字与局限）；
- 用一套固定的"现状 → 风险 → 改进"结构回答弱点类问题；
- 在不确定时用诚实模板，而不是编数字。

## 使用方法

1. 先背第一部分的三种口述；
2. 按章节过第二部分的问答。**L1** 是面试官问出口后你的第一反应（两三句）；**L2** 是常见追问，需要讲清机制；**L3** 是深挖，需要给出数字、取舍和局限；每问末尾有中文译文用于核对意思，不要背中文；
3. 工程化类问题（周期、版本、团队）见第二部分 H 组，并与 [20 工程化实践](./20_工程化实践_频率周期与问题解决.md) 末尾的 12 个工程化问答配合；
4. 用第四部分的诚实模板处理"我不记得具体数字"。

> **口径提醒**：控制任务周期按本项目设计取值约 3 ms（约 333 Hz），**不说 1 kHz**；IMU 任务由 DMA 通知唤醒、约 1 kHz；3V3 二等奖是团队成绩，不归于英雄机器人本身。

## 第一部分：三种口述

### 1. 30 seconds

> "I was responsible for the embedded closed-loop control of our university team's hero robot in RoboMaster. On an STM32F407 with FreeRTOS and CAN, I built three sets of loops: a cascaded angle–rate PID for the gimbal, speed and position loops for the launcher, and power-aware wheel-speed control for the Mecanum chassis. The robot competed in the 2025 national final and won third prize."

中文：负责英雄机器人嵌入式闭环控制；STM32F407/FreeRTOS/CAN 上做云台角度–角速度串级 PID、发射机构速度/位置环、麦轮底盘功率感知轮速控制；进入 2025 国赛获三等奖。

### 2. 90 seconds

> "RoboMaster is a university robotics competition, and our hero robot fires large 42-millimetre projectiles, so every shot has to be accurate and consistent. I worked on the low-level control on an STM32F407 running FreeRTOS, with motors on CAN.
>
> For the gimbal, I built a cascaded PID for pitch and yaw: the outer loop turns the angle error into a target angular rate, and the inner loop turns the rate error into a motor command. Feedback came from the IMU with an attitude filter, plus the motor encoders. Remote-control and vision targets are turned into continuous attitude commands, so aiming stays stable and tracking is smooth.
>
> For the launcher, the friction wheels have a speed loop and the feeder has a position–velocity dual loop. I iterated the wheel controller from PID to variable-gain PID to sliding-mode control, and used muzzle-velocity feedback to keep continuous firing stable.
>
> For the chassis, I allocated the body velocity to four Mecanum wheels, estimated the power demand from power and buffer-energy feedback, and scaled the wheel speeds so the robot stayed manoeuvrable under the power limit. We reached the national final and won third prize."

中文：同 30 秒，展开三个子系统，各一句核心。

### 3. 3 minutes（结构：背景 → 三个回路 → 难点 → 收获）

> "**Context.** In RoboMaster, teams build robots that shoot projectiles at each other under a referee system that enforces limits on heat, chassis power and muzzle speed. Our hero robot shoots 42-millimetre projectiles at a low rate, so aiming and shot consistency matter a lot. My job was the embedded control of the gimbal, the launcher and the chassis on an STM32F407 with FreeRTOS and CAN.
>
> **Gimbal.** The gimbal is a pair of inertial axes with gravity and disturbances from the chassis and the recoil. I used a cascaded structure. The inner loop is an angular-rate loop that uses the gyro directly, so I don't need to differentiate an angle; the outer loop is an angle loop. The inner loop should be several times faster than the outer, so I tuned the inner first, then the outer, then added feedforward for gravity and target velocity. Targets come from the remote or from the vision computer. The vision targets are delayed and arrive at a lower rate, so I smooth them and switch modes without a jump, keeping the commands continuous.
>
> **Launcher.** The friction wheels must hold their speed even when a projectile takes energy from them, and the muzzle velocity must stay inside the rule limit. I started with PID, moved to a variable-gain PID, and then to sliding-mode control, and used the reported muzzle velocity to correct the wheel target. The feeder uses a position loop on top of a velocity loop so each shot is a fixed step, and a jam is detected when it fails to reach the target in time, followed by one reverse move.
>
> **Chassis.** Body velocity is mapped to four wheel speeds with the Mecanum kinematics. Because the referee limits chassis power, I estimated the power demand from power and buffer-energy feedback and scaled all four wheel speeds by a common factor, which keeps the direction of motion while reducing the demand.
>
> **What I learned.** The biggest lesson was that timing is part of the controller: the loop period, the delays and the data freshness all affect stability. I also learned to test disturbances on the real robot, not just step responses. We reached the national final and won third prize."

中文：背景—云台—发射—底盘—收获（时序是控制器的一部分、实机扰动测试）。

## 第二部分：英文问答（58 题，L1 / L2 / L3）

> 格式：每题 **L1**（直接回答）→ **L2**（追问与机制）→ **L3**（深挖：数字、取舍、局限）→ **中文**（三层要点）。数字取自 README 自查表。

### A. 项目总览与背景

**Q1. Tell me about your hero robot project.**

- **L1**: "I did the embedded control of our hero robot: the gimbal, the launcher and the chassis loops, on an STM32F407 with FreeRTOS and CAN. We reached the 2025 national final and won third prize."
- **L2** — *Which part was hardest?* "The launcher. A 42-millimetre projectile takes energy from the friction wheels, so the wheel speed dips on every shot, and the muzzle speed must stay under the cap."
- **L3** — *What did you change over the season?* "I went from PID to variable-gain PID to sliding-mode control on the wheels, then added a slow muzzle-speed correction from the referee data. The spread came down from about plus or minus one metre per second to about plus or minus 0.3."

中文：L1 职责与成绩；L2 最难的是发射（掉速）；L3 PID→变增益→滑模，再加弹速修正，离散 ±1 降到 ±0.3 m/s。

**Q2. What was your personal contribution versus the team's?**

- **L1**: "The robot was a team effort with mechanical, vision and strategy members. My part was the embedded control: three sets of loops on the STM32."
- **L2** — *Who wrote the vision code?* "A teammate. I defined and implemented the target interface on my side: the frame format, the target error angles with a timestamp, and the attitude I send back."
- **L3** — *Did you write all the firmware?* "No. The framework was a team codebase. I owned the control logic for the three mechanisms and the way they are tuned and tested. If a question is about a module a teammate wrote, I say so and explain what I understood of it."

中文：团队 vs 个人：我负责三类电控回路与视觉接口；视觉由队友写；固件框架是团队代码，不夸大。

**Q3. What is a hero robot and why is control hard?**

- **L1**: "It fires large 42-millimetre projectiles at a low rate. The heavy projectile makes the wheel speed dip, feeding jams are more likely, the machine is heavy so the chassis hits the power limit, and the gimbal has large inertia."
- **L2** — *Why does a low fire rate make accuracy more important?* "Each shot is worth a lot, so I cannot spray and correct. The first shot of a burst has to be right."
- **L3** — *Which of those four is the most limiting?* "For scoring it is shot consistency, so the wheel loop. For survival it is power: if the buffer energy runs out the chassis is penalised."

中文：大弹丸→掉速、卡弹、功率、惯量；低射频意味着每发要准；得分看弹速一致性，生存看功率。

**Q4. How do the rules shape your controller?**

- **L1**: "Heat limits gate the feeder, chassis power and buffer energy force wheel-speed scaling, and the muzzle-velocity cap is why I use velocity feedback."
- **L2** — *What is buffer energy?* "A small energy pool the referee tracks. It drains when the chassis draws more than the power limit; if it empties you are penalised."
- **L3** — *How do you stay robust when the rules change each season?* "I keep limits as parameters from the referee data, not constants. In 2025 the hero's cap is 16 metres per second; heat and power limits come from the referee at run time, and my local thresholds are conservative."

中文：规则决定三种门控；缓冲能量是裁判跟踪的能量池；限值用裁判上报而不是写死。

**Q5. Describe the system architecture.**

- **L1**: "Two RoboMaster Development Board Type C boards, a gimbal board and a chassis board, each with an STM32F407 running FreeRTOS. Motors are on CAN, the IMU is on SPI with DMA, the remote and referee are on UART, and vision is on USB."
- **L2** — *How do the two boards talk?* "Over CAN2 at 1 Mbps. The gimbal board sends velocity commands, the chassis mode and the relative yaw angle at 100 Hz; the chassis board sends referee data such as muzzle speed, heat and power limit at 50 Hz."
- **L3** — *What if the link fails?* "If no inter-board frame arrives for 100 milliseconds, the chassis relaxes and the gimbal relaxes and turns off the friction wheels. Relax means zero command, cleared integrators, and the CAN heartbeat keeps running."

中文：双板，云台板+底盘板；CAN2 互联 1 Mbps，100 Hz/50 Hz；100 ms 失联即 RELAX。

**Q6. Why STM32F407 and FreeRTOS?**

- **L1**: "The Cortex-M4F has an FPU, so quaternion math and PID run in floating point; it has two CAN controllers and enough DMA. FreeRTOS gives priorities and clean synchronisation between event-driven and periodic work."
- **L2** — *What are the downsides of an RTOS?* "Scheduling jitter, stack memory, and priority inversion. For a few control loops a bare-metal timer interrupt could be tighter."
- **L3** — *Would you do it differently now?* "I would run the control loops from a fixed-period timer or `vTaskDelayUntil` with measured jitter, and keep CAN transmission in a dedicated task, so the period is a design number and not a side effect of delays."

中文：F407 有 FPU、双 CAN、DMA；RTOS 带来抖动与栈开销；现在会用固定周期并单独发送任务。

### B. FreeRTOS 与时序

**Q7. What tasks did you have and what priorities?**

- **L1**: "Gimbal, chassis, IMU, detect, USB, decode, UI, LED and capacitor tasks. The gimbal, USB and decode tasks have higher priority, the chassis task is lower, and the IMU task is woken by a notification from the DMA completion."
- **L2** — *Why is the IMU task not the highest?* "In the version I used it was set low. It works because it is notification driven and the CPU load is light. The risk is a delayed attitude update under high load."
- **L3** — *How would you fix it?* "Raise the attitude task to at least the control task's priority, count notifications to detect lost samples, and measure the time from notification to finished update with a GPIO pin."

中文：任务清单与优先级；IMU 偏低可工作但有风险；改进=提高优先级+丢样计数+测量。

**Q8. How fast were your control loops?**

- **L1**: "The task loop ran at about 3 milliseconds, around 333 hertz, not 1 kilohertz. The attitude task is separate, woken by the IMU at about 1 kilohertz."
- **L2** — *Why 3 milliseconds and not 1?* "The loop has three one-tick delays, so each cycle takes about three ticks. It was not a deliberate choice at first; I tuned the gains at that period, which made it part of the design."
- **L3** — *Is that fast enough?* "The inner gimbal loop has about 35 hertz bandwidth, so the sampling is about ten times the bandwidth, the lower end of the usual rule. Adding about two milliseconds of delay costs about 25 degrees of phase at 35 hertz. So I would not push the bandwidth higher without a faster, fixed period."

中文：约 3 ms/333 Hz，不是 1 kHz；起初是延时累加的结果，随后在该周期下整定；采样/带宽≈10 倍，处于下沿。

**Q9. What is the difference between vTaskDelay and vTaskDelayUntil?**

- **L1**: "`vTaskDelay` waits relative to the call time, so the period drifts with execution time. `vTaskDelayUntil` keeps an absolute schedule, so the average period is fixed."
- **L2** — *What about `osDelay`?* "It is the CMSIS wrapper of `vTaskDelay`, so the same relative behaviour."
- **L3** — *What happens if a task overruns its period with `vTaskDelayUntil`?* "It returns immediately, and the wake time catches up; I would log overruns with a counter so a missed deadline is visible, not silent."

中文：相对 vs 绝对延时；osDelay 同相对；超时要计数而不是静默。

**Q10. How do you get IMU data into a task?**

- **L1**: "The DMA completion interrupt sends a task notification; the task decodes the data and runs the filter."
- **L2** — *What if the task is slow?* "Notifications can coalesce, so samples can be missed. I would count them."
- **L3** — *Why not do the filter in the interrupt?* "Interrupts should be short. The filter takes a few hundred microseconds on this core; doing it there would block lower-priority interrupts such as CAN reception."

中文：DMA 中断→任务通知；通知可合并丢样；滤波不放在中断里以免阻塞其他中断。

**Q11. What is priority inversion?**

- **L1**: "A low-priority task holds a lock that a high-priority task needs, and a medium task preempts the low one, so the high one waits too long. Mutexes with priority inheritance bound this."
- **L2** — *Did you see it?* "I designed to avoid it: control tasks share data by copying snapshots in short critical sections rather than long-held locks."
- **L3** — *What is the cost of a critical section?* "It masks interrupts, so I keep it to a few copied words. Longer shared structures use a double buffer or a sequence counter."

中文：优先级反转与继承；设计上用短临界区快照避免；长结构用双缓冲/序号。

**Q12. What happens to a PID if the loop period changes?**

- **L1**: "If dt is not in the equations, the effective integral and derivative gains scale with the period, so the tuning is tied to the period."
- **L2** — *Was dt in your code?* "No, the gains were bound to the period, so I tuned at a fixed period. Some internal constants such as the sliding-mode and filter dt were hard-coded at 0.001."
- **L3** — *What is the effect of that mismatch?* "With a hard-coded 1 millisecond and a real 3 millisecond cycle, a derivative-like term is overestimated about three times and an integrated gyro under-integrated by two thirds. I would use the measured dt."

中文：增益绑定周期；dt 写死的名义常数导致 3 倍偏差；应使用实测 dt。

**Q13. How do you avoid reading half-updated shared data?**

- **L1**: "Copy the shared structure in a short critical section, or use a double buffer with a sequence number."
- **L2** — *Which variables are at risk?* "Multi-field structures such as the attitude quaternion and the vision target; a single aligned 32-bit word is read atomically on a Cortex-M4."
- **L3** — *Do you check data freshness?* "Yes. Every message has a timestamp, and the detect task compares its age with a timeout of 50 to 100 milliseconds depending on the source."

中文：临界区拷贝或双缓冲；多字段结构有风险；时间戳判新鲜度。

**Q14. How do you measure loop period and jitter?**

- **L1**: "Toggle a GPIO pin at the top of the loop and look at it with a logic analyser, or read the DWT cycle counter."
- **L2** — *What do you record?* "Period minimum, maximum and standard deviation over several seconds, and the worst-case execution time of each task."
- **L3** — *How much jitter is acceptable?* "A rule of thumb is under 10 percent of the period. At 3 milliseconds that is about 0.3 milliseconds; our worst case should be checked under load, with vision and referee traffic running."

中文：GPIO+逻辑分析仪或 DWT；记录最大/最小/标准差；抖动目标 <10% 周期。

### C. CAN 与电机

**Q15. Why CAN?**

- **L1**: "Differential signalling for noise immunity, multi-node broadcast, arbitration by ID, hardware CRC and retransmission, and short fixed frames for predictable latency."
- **L2** — *How does arbitration work?* "Dominant bits win, so a lower ID has higher priority, non-destructively."
- **L3** — *Any downsides?* "Eight bytes per frame and limited bandwidth, so the bus load must be calculated and the motors split across buses."

中文：差分抗扰、多节点、ID 仲裁、CRC 重发、定长帧；缺点是带宽与 8 字节，需算负载。

**Q16. How did you estimate bus load?**

- **L1**: "A standard 8-byte frame is about 111 bits, so about 111 microseconds at 1 Mbps. Frames per second times 111 microseconds gives the load."
- **L2** — *What was your split?* "The gimbal board has five motors on CAN1, about 63 percent; the chassis board has four on CAN1, about 48 percent; the board-to-board link on CAN2 is under 2 percent."
- **L3** — *Is 63 percent too high?* "It is within the 50 to 70 percent rule of thumb. With worst-case bit stuffing it rises, so I did not add more frames, and I sent commands at the 3 millisecond control rate, not every millisecond."

中文：111 µs/帧；云台板 63%、底盘板 48%、CAN2<2%；63% 在经验范围内，命令按控制周期发。

**Q17. What does the motor feedback contain and how do you convert it?**

- **L1**: "Single-turn rotor angle 0 to 8191, signed rpm, torque current and temperature. rpm times 2π over 60 gives rad/s; divide by the gear ratio for the output shaft."
- **L2** — *What about the command?* "A signed integer of plus or minus 16384 for the M3508 driver, mapping to plus or minus 20 amps; the GM6020 takes a voltage-mode command up to plus or minus 30000."
- **L3** — *Where is the current loop?* "Inside the motor driver. My loops output current or voltage commands, so my loop bandwidth is bounded by it and by the CAN delay."

中文：反馈量与换算；指令范围；电流环在电调内。

**Q18. How do you get a multi-turn angle?**

- **L1**: "Compare consecutive samples; a jump larger than half a turn means a wrap, so adjust a turn counter."
- **L2** — *When does it fail?* "If more than half a turn happens between two samples. For the M3508 rotor at 8000 rpm, three lost frames can alias."
- **L3** — *Where do you use it?* "The feeder position loop, with one slot as 26225 counts, about 60 degrees of output shaft."

中文：半圈阈值法；丢帧会混叠；拨盘位置环用累计计数，一格 26225。

**Q19. How do you detect a motor offline?**

- **L1**: "Timestamp in the receive callback; a 10-millisecond monitor compares the age to a threshold, 50 milliseconds for motor feedback."
- **L2** — *What happens then?* "That mechanism goes to relax: command zero, integrators cleared, the CAN heartbeat kept."
- **L3** — *What are the other thresholds?* "100 milliseconds for the remote, 100 for vision, 100 for the inter-board link. The motor one is shortest because the feedback arrives every millisecond."

中文：时间戳超时；离线→RELAX；DBUS/视觉/板间 100 ms，电机 50 ms。

**Q20. How do you send CAN commands safely?**

- **L1**: "Pack the four motor commands of a group into one 8-byte frame, and send it once per control cycle, with a small gap between frames."
- **L2** — *Why a gap?* "In our version a one-tick delay between frames avoids filling the transmit mailboxes; there are only three."
- **L3** — *What would you improve?* "A dedicated transmit task or a queue, so a full mailbox never blocks a control task, and a counter of dropped frames."

中文：同组 4 个电机打包一帧；帧间 1 tick；改进=独立发送任务/队列+丢帧计数。

### D. IMU 与姿态

**Q21. How do you estimate gimbal attitude?**

- **L1**: "Gyro and accelerometer over SPI, bias and mounting correction, then a Mahony complementary filter updating a quaternion, converted to Euler angles for control."
- **L2** — *Which sensor?* "A BMI088, gyro range plus or minus 2000 degrees per second, accelerometer plus or minus 6 g, kept at a constant 40 degrees by a heater."
- **L3** — *Why the heater?* "Gyro bias drifts with temperature. A constant temperature keeps the start-up calibration valid: I average 2000 still samples at power-up."

中文：BMI088+Mahony；恒温 40 °C 保证零偏标定有效；上电静止采 2000 个样本。

**Q22. Explain the Mahony filter.**

- **L1**: "Predict gravity from the quaternion, cross it with the measured acceleration direction, feed the error back into the gyro rates with a PI term, integrate and normalise."
- **L2** — *What are Kp and Ki?* "Kp sets how fast the accelerometer pulls the estimate; Ki learns the gyro bias. I used Kp of 0.5 and turned Ki off."
- **L3** — *Why Ki off?* "The start-up calibration plus the constant temperature already remove most of the bias, and an integral term can wind up during a long manoeuvre. The crossover is roughly Kp over 2π, about 0.08 hertz."

中文：叉乘误差回馈陀螺；Kp=0.5、Ki=0；交越频率约 Kp/2π。

**Q23. Why quaternions?**

- **L1**: "No gimbal lock, and the integration is cheap and stable. Euler angles are only used for output."
- **L2** — *Where does gimbal lock matter for you?* "Pitch near 90 degrees; our gimbal does not go there, but the representation is still safer."
- **L3** — *What do you watch for?* "Normalisation after every update, and the sign and axis mapping between the sensor frame and the gimbal frame, which cost me a debugging session."

中文：无万向锁、积分稳定；每次更新要归一化；轴映射/符号是坑。

**Q24. Why does yaw drift?**

- **L1**: "Gravity cannot observe yaw. Without a magnetometer, yaw is only the integrated gyro, so any bias becomes drift."
- **L2** — *How much?* "About 1 degree per minute at rest in our setup, which is acceptable over a match because aiming uses relative targets."
- **L3** — *How could you reduce it?* "Better start-up calibration, constant temperature, a still-detection bias update, or a magnetometer in a clean environment; the IST8310 was on the board but not enabled because the motors disturb it."

中文：yaw 不可观测；静置漂移约 1°/min；可用静止更新零偏/磁力计，但电机干扰大未启用。

**Q25. Mahony vs Madgwick vs Kalman?**

- **L1**: "Mahony and Madgwick are cheap nonlinear complementary filters; a Kalman filter weights sensors optimally but needs noise models and more compute."
- **L2** — *Which did you use where?* "Mahony on the MCU for the gimbal. In my navigation project I used an EKF."
- **L3** — *When would you switch?* "If the accelerometer were often disturbed or I needed bias as a proper state with covariance, I would use an error-state Kalman filter."

中文：Mahony/Madgwick 轻量；Kalman 最优但需模型；本项目 Mahony，导航项目用 EKF。

**Q26. What did you do about accelerometer disturbances?**

- **L1**: "When the acceleration magnitude deviates from gravity, the correction should be reduced."
- **L2** — *Did your filter do that?* "Our Mahony version normalised the vector; I would add a magnitude gate as an improvement."
- **L3** — *What does it look like in practice?* "During a hit or a hard stop the pitch estimate briefly deflects. Because the gyro is the main source at high frequency, the effect is short, and the inner rate loop is not affected at all."

中文：加速度幅值门限是改进项；冲击时姿态短暂偏移，但内环直接用陀螺不受影响。

### E. 云台

**Q27. Why cascaded PID?**

- **L1**: "The inner rate loop uses the gyro directly, so there is no differentiation, it makes the plant look like an integrator for the outer loop, it rejects disturbances quickly, and it lets me limit the rate."
- **L2** — *Why not a single PID with a D term?* "Differentiating angle is noisy and delayed; the gyro gives a clean rate for free."
- **L3** — *What are the values?* "The outer loop is proportional only: Kp of 28 for yaw and 25 for pitch, output limited to 240 and 120 degrees per second. The inner loop is PI: 3200 and 10 for yaw, 2800 and 8 for pitch, integral limit 3000, voltage output limit 30000."

中文：串级=直接用陀螺、免微分、抗扰；外环 P：yaw 28/pitch 25；内环 PI；限幅。

**Q28. How did you choose the bandwidths?**

- **L1**: "The inner loop at least four to ten times the outer. In our design the inner is about 30 to 40 hertz and the outer about 4 to 5 hertz."
- **L2** — *How do you get the outer bandwidth?* "With a proportional outer loop and a fast inner loop, the closed-loop bandwidth is about Kp over 2π: 28 gives 4.5 hertz for yaw, 25 gives 4 hertz for pitch."
- **L3** — *What limits the inner bandwidth?* "Delay, noise and current limits. A 2 millisecond delay costs about 25 degrees at 35 hertz and the zero-order hold another 19, so I did not raise it further."

中文：内环 30–40 Hz、外环 4–5 Hz；外环带宽≈Kp/2π；内环受延迟/噪声/限幅限制。

**Q29. How did you tune it?**

- **L1**: "Signs first with small commands, then the rate loop P, a small I, then the angle loop, then feedforward, then disturbance tests: push, spin the chassis, fire."
- **L2** — *How do you know when to stop?* "When the step shows little overshoot and the disturbance recovery is fast without audible buzzing."
- **L3** — *Did you keep records?* "Yes. Each change was logged with the error curve, the current peak and the motor temperature, so I could compare versions and go back."

中文：先符号、再内环、再外环、再前馈、再扰动测试；每次改参数记录曲线与温度。

**Q30. How do you handle yaw wraparound?**

- **L1**: "Wrap the error to plus or minus 180 degrees so the gimbal takes the short way; to average angles, use sine and cosine."
- **L2** — *What failed before?* "Averaging 179 and minus 179 gave 0, so the gimbal swung the wrong way."
- **L3** — *Where else does it matter?* "The vision target filter and the chassis-follow angle, both in radians wrapped to (−π, π]."

中文：误差折到 ±180°；平均用 sin/cos；视觉滤波与底盘跟随也要折角。

**Q31. How do you handle vision latency?**

- **L1**: "The vision side anchors each target to the attitude at the capture time using timestamps, then extrapolates. The MCU only low-passes and rate-limits."
- **L2** — *How large is the problem?* "End-to-end latency is about 30 to 50 milliseconds; at 20 degrees per second the target moves 0.8 degrees in 40 milliseconds, about 70 millimetres at 5 metres."
- **L3** — *What do you do on the control side?* "A velocity feedforward with a gain of about 0.6, below 1 because the vision velocity is noisy. With the outer Kp of 28, a 20 degree per second ramp leaves a lag of 0.71 degrees, reduced to 0.29 with feedforward."

中文：延迟补偿在视觉侧；电控低通+限速；速度前馈 0.6；稳态滞后 0.71°→0.29°。

**Q32. How do you avoid a jump when switching modes?**

- **L1**: "Initialise the filtered target to the current command and rate-limit toward the new one; on exit set the manual target to the current attitude."
- **L2** — *What if vision is lost?* "After 100 milliseconds without a valid target, the gimbal falls back from auto to active mode and holds the current target angle."
- **L3** — *Why hold the current angle?* "Chasing a stale target would make the gimbal move without a reason. Holding is the safest continuous command."

中文：滤波目标初始化为当前值+限速；视觉丢失 100 ms 退回主动模式并保持角度。

**Q33. How do you reject chassis spinning?**

- **L1**: "Use the IMU's absolute rate and angle as feedback so the loop sees the world frame; then test with the chassis spinning."
- **L2** — *What is the inner-loop feedback axis for yaw?* "The gyro z axis directly, without projection, since pitch swing is small: cos 20 degrees is 0.94, a 6 percent gain change."
- **L3** — *What does spinning do to the chassis-follow command?* "At the spinning mode of about 3.5 rad/s the translation vector is rotated by the relative angle so the robot still moves in the commanded direction."

中文：IMU 绝对量作反馈；yaw 速度取陀螺 z 轴不做投影；小陀螺时平移矢量按相对角旋转。

**Q34. How do you compensate gravity on pitch?**

- **L1**: "A feedforward proportional to the cosine of pitch, about 3500 voltage units in our design, added to the inner-loop output."
- **L2** — *Why not just the integrator?* "3500 is larger than the integral limit of 3000, and the integrator would lag when the pitch changes."
- **L3** — *Is the load constant?* "The projectile state changes it a little, so the feedforward covers the main part and the integral term trims the rest."

中文：pitch 重力前馈∝cos(pitch)≈3500；大于积分限幅所以必须前馈；积分微调剩余。

**Q35. How do you prevent integral windup?**

- **L1**: "Limit the integral term and the total output, clear the integrator on relax, and stop integrating during large errors."
- **L2** — *Show a case.* "If the gimbal is held by hand, the integrator reaches the 3000 limit and, without a limit, would accumulate to about 17,000 and overshoot when released."
- **L3** — *Why not back-calculation?* "In the stuck case the output was below the 30000 limit, so back-calculation would not act; the integral clamp does."

中文：积分限幅、RELAX 清零、大误差停积分；无限幅时被卡住会累到约 17000，松手过冲。

### F. 发射

**Q36. Describe the launcher control.**

- **L1**: "Two friction wheels with a speed loop, a feeder with a position loop over a velocity loop, a state machine for firing, heat gating and jam handling."
- **L2** — *What speed?* "The base target is about 6000 rpm, a soft limit of 7000, which gives a surface speed near 19 metres per second for a 16 metre-per-second cap."
- **L3** — *Which motors?* "Two M3508 motors direct drive, 60 millimetre wheels, and an M3508 feeder with a 19.2 to 1 gearbox, six slots."

中文：摩擦轮速度环+拨盘位置/速度环+状态机；基础转速 6000 rpm、限 7000；M3508 直驱。

**Q37. Why did the wheel speed drop when a projectile passed?**

- **L1**: "Energy. The projectile's kinetic energy, about 5.8 joules for 45 grams at 16 metres per second, comes from the wheels' rotation."
- **L2** — *Can the controller prevent the dip?* "No. It sets how fast the speed recovers and how much it overshoots."
- **L3** — *How big was the dip?* "About 600 to 900 rpm in our case, and with the PID about 120 milliseconds to recover, almost the whole interval between shots of about 150 milliseconds."

中文：弹丸动能来自轮子转动；控制器只能改恢复速度；掉 600–900 rpm，PID 恢复 120 ms。

**Q38. Why iterate from PID to variable-gain PID to sliding mode?**

- **L1**: "With the fixed-gain PID the wheel dipped about 600 to 900 rpm and took about 120 milliseconds to recover, so the muzzle speed spread was about plus or minus 1 metre per second."
- **L2** — *What did each step add?* "Variable gain improved the large-error response but the gain near zero error was hard to tune. Sliding mode added robustness to load changes through a switching term with a boundary layer."
- **L3** — *What was the result?* "Recovery of about 60 to 80 milliseconds and a spread of about plus or minus 0.3. Before matches the measured muzzle speed was 15.2 to 16.0 metres per second. They are an iteration history; they never ran at the same time."

中文：PID 恢复慢→变增益改善大误差→滑模抗负载变化；恢复 60–80 ms，离散 ±0.3；三者是迭代历史。

**Q39. What is your variable-gain PID?**

- **L1**: "The proportional gain depends on the error magnitude: Kp equals k0 times log10 of k1 times the absolute error, plus k2."
- **L2** — *What are the values?* "k0 equals 1, k1 equals 8, k2 equals 5, so the gain goes from about 5.9 at an error of 1 to 8.9 at 1000."
- **L3** — *What is the pitfall?* "The log tends to minus infinity as the error goes to zero, so the error needs a lower bound and the gain upper and lower limits."

中文：Kp=k0·log10(k1|e|)+k2，k=(1,8,5)；e→0 时需下限保护。

**Q40. Explain your sliding-mode controller.**

- **L1**: "The sliding variable is s equals the error rate plus c times the error; on s equals zero the error decays exponentially with rate c."
- **L2** — *The control law?* "An equivalent part proportional to the error and a switching part of ρ times a saturation of s over ε, with c equal 5, ρ equal 2, ε equal 1, output limited to 10000."
- **L3** — *Is it stable?* "I did not prove it formally. The boundary layer makes it a quasi-sliding mode, and I checked it by tests and a teaching simulation. A known weakness: the internal dt was hard-coded to 0.001, not the real 3 milliseconds."

中文：s=ė+ce；等效项+ρ·sat(s/ε)；c=5、ρ=2、ε=1；未做严格证明，dt 写死是弱点。

**Q41. What is chattering and how do you reduce it?**

- **L1**: "High-frequency switching of the control caused by the discontinuous term plus delay and sampling."
- **L2** — *How do you reduce it?* "A boundary layer or saturation, a smaller switching gain, and filtering of the derivative."
- **L3** — *How do you size the boundary layer?* "Larger than the ripple caused by the gain times the sampling period; otherwise the output still flips each cycle."

中文：抖振=不连续项+延迟+采样；边界层/减小切换增益/滤波；边界层大于纹波。

**Q42. Is it really sliding mode?**

- **L1**: "With a boundary layer it is a quasi-sliding-mode controller. Inside the layer it acts like a high-gain proportional law; outside, it applies the full switching term."
- **L2** — *Why use it?* "Robustness to the energy drain and battery voltage changes, with a simple structure."
- **L3** — *What is missing?* "An integral or disturbance observer for a steady load, which I list as an improvement."

中文：带边界层=准滑模；层内高增益比例；缺少积分/扰动观测器。

**Q43. How does muzzle-velocity feedback work?**

- **L1**: "The referee reports each shot's speed. After each shot, if it exceeds 16.0 metres per second I lower the wheel speed by 50 rpm; if it is below 15.7 I raise it by 50."
- **L2** — *Why not feed it into the speed loop?* "It is event-driven, delayed and noisy, so it is a slow outer correction on the target, not a loop."
- **L3** — *Any problem found?* "At full battery we twice saw 16.2 metres per second, over the cap, which is why the lowering logic exists. The two thresholds are independent ifs, so in an odd case both could act."

中文：裁判上报弹速，>16.0 降 50 rpm，<15.7 升 50 rpm；慢速外层修正；满电 16.2 超限促成下调。

**Q44. How does the feeder work?**

- **L1**: "A position loop on the accumulated encoder count outputs a speed reference, clamped; a velocity loop outputs current. One shot is a fixed count step."
- **L2** — *Values?* "Position Kp of 0.1 counts to rpm, clamped at 2500 rpm; the speed loop Kp 8 and Ki 0.3, current limited to 10000. One shot is about 150 milliseconds."
- **L3** — *The step size?* "8192 times 19.2 over 6 is about 26214 counts; the calibrated value is 26225."

中文：位置环→速度环→电流；一发=26225 计数；Kp=0.1、速度环 Kp=8/Ki=0.3；约 150 ms。

**Q45. How do you detect and clear a jam?**

- **L1**: "If the residual error stays above 5000 counts for more than one second, I declare a jam, reverse two slots, 52450 counts, once."
- **L2** — *If it fails again?* "The state becomes SHOOT_FAIL: stop firing and report. I do not retry forever."
- **L3** — *Why not use current alone?* "Current is high during normal starts. And SHOOT_FAIL is not a current cut-off; only relax gives zero current. I would add a latch with a current cut-off after a second failure."

中文：误差>5000 计数且 >1 s 判堵转；反退 52450 一次；失败进 SHOOT_FAIL；不单看电流。

**Q46. How do you respect the heat limit?**

- **L1**: "Automatic fire requires the fire command, auto mode, the 42-millimetre heat below 100 and more than 500 milliseconds since the last auto shot."
- **L2** — *Where does heat come from?* "The referee reports it; each 42-millimetre projectile adds 100. Limits and cooling follow the season's rulebook, and my local threshold is conservative."
- **L3** — *What about a local estimate?* "It would add heat at each shot immediately and decay at the cooling rate, and take the max with the referee value, because the referee data arrives late."

中文：自动发射四条件；热量以裁判为准，本地保守阈值；本地估计是改进。

**Q47. What is the difference between Fire-off and relax?**

- **L1**: "Fire-off sets the target to zero and the controller may still brake; relax zeros all commands and clears the integrators."
- **L2** — *When is relax used?* "On offline detection, on mode exit to safe, and on inter-board link loss."
- **L3** — *Does relax keep the CAN heartbeat?* "Yes, the frames still go out with zero command, so the driver sees a live master and recovery is quick."

中文：Fire-off 仍可制动；RELAX 零指令+清积分；RELAX 保持 CAN 心跳。

### G. 底盘与功率

**Q48. Derive the Mecanum kinematics.**

- **L1**: "Wheel contact velocity is body velocity plus ω cross r; the roller axis fixes the direction each wheel can push, so wheel speed is the projection onto that direction divided by the radius."
- **L2** — *The formula?* "For an X layout, wheel speeds are proportional to vx minus vy minus d times ω for one wheel and so on, with d equal to a plus b, 0.38 metres here."
- **L3** — *The inverse?* "The pseudo-inverse; the columns are orthogonal so it is simple. See chapter 09."

中文：投影法推导；X 型布局 d=a+b=0.38 m；逆解用伪逆。

**Q49. Why scale all four wheels together?**

- **L1**: "Independent clipping changes the ratios and the robot goes in a different direction; a common factor keeps the direction and only slows it."
- **L2** — *Any exception?* "The rotation command in spinning mode can be reduced first, because keeping the heading of translation matters more."
- **L3** — *How is the factor found?* "The power is a quadratic in the factor k, so a·k² + b·k + c equals the cap, solved in closed form, then limited to between 0 and 1."

中文：统一缩放保方向；小陀螺时先降 wz；功率对 k 为二次，闭式求根。

**Q50. How does your power control work?**

- **L1**: "I estimate the power demand from the wheel loops and measured power and buffer energy, then compute one scale factor so the predicted power fits the limit, and apply it to the wheel speed references."
- **L2** — *The limit?* "The referee's chassis power limit minus 5 watts; 70 watts is a common setting."
- **L3** — *Is it a hard guarantee?* "No. Model error and recovery smoothing can exceed the limit briefly, so the buffer energy is the safety net."

中文：功率预测→统一缩放系数→应用于轮速参考；上限=裁判上限−5 W；非硬保证，缓冲能量兜底。

**Q51. What is buffer energy and how do you use it?**

- **L1**: "A 60-joule reserve the referee tracks; it drains when you exceed the power limit. Low buffer makes the limiter more conservative."
- **L2** — *Thresholds?* "Below 30 joules I add extra compression; below 10 joules I force the factor down to 0.3."
- **L3** — *Why feedback from the buffer?* "The power model has errors; the buffer is the true accumulated result, so it closes the loop on the prediction."

中文：缓冲 60 J；<30 J 额外压缩；<10 J 强降到 0.3；缓冲是对模型误差的闭环修正。

**Q52. How does the scale factor recover?**

- **L1**: "It drops immediately but recovers by 0.01 per cycle, about 0.3 seconds to return to 1."
- **L2** — *Why?* "A fast recovery makes the power bounce back above the limit and the scale oscillate."
- **L3** — *Cost?* "A slight delay in regaining full speed after a power spike, acceptable for stability."

中文：下降立即、恢复 +0.01/周期（约 0.3 s）；避免来回振荡。

**Q53. How does the chassis follow the gimbal?**

- **L1**: "The gimbal yaw relative angle from the encoder is the error; a controller turns it into a chassis rotation command."
- **L2** — *Values?* "Kp 4.0, Kd 0.2, output limited to 6 rad/s, dead band 2 degrees."
- **L3** — *Why the dead band?* "Without it the chassis keeps hunting near zero, and under power limits it causes shaking."

中文：云台相对角→底盘 wz；Kp=4.0、Kd=0.2、限 6 rad/s、死区 2°。

### H. 工程化与反思

**Q54. Tell me about the hardest bug.**

- **L1**: "During continuous fire the friction wheels dipped by 600 to 900 rpm and the muzzle speed spread was about plus or minus 1 metre per second."
- **L2** — *How did you find the cause?* "I logged wheel speed against the referee-reported bullet speed and confirmed that the dip, not noise, caused the spread."
- **L3** — *What did you change?* "The controller from PID to sliding mode and added the muzzle-speed correction, which reduced the spread to about plus or minus 0.3."

中文：连发掉速；日志对比轮速与弹速；PID→滑模+弹速修正。

**Q55. How did you debug on the competition site?**

- **L1**: "Before each match I ran a fixed checklist: wiring, sensor calibration, motor directions, the muzzle-speed test, and a safe-state test."
- **L2** — *What do you do if something fails?* "Change one thing at a time, compare with the last known good parameter set, and fall back to it if needed."
- **L3** — *What did the checklist catch?* "Wiring faults and supply drops that make a motor go offline, which I handle with offline detection."

中文：赛前固定清单；一次只改一处，可回退；清单能发现接线/掉电问题。详见 [20 章](./20_工程化实践_频率周期与问题解决.md)。

**Q56. How did you manage versions and parameters?**

- **L1**: "Git with a branch per feature, a tag for each competition build, and the parameters in one header so a build is reproducible."
- **L2** — *How do you avoid conflicts in a team repository?* "Each person owns separate source files; shared interfaces change only through a short review."
- **L3** — *What did you record per tuning session?* "The commit hash, the parameter set, the battery voltage, and the log file, so any result can be reproduced."

中文：Git 分支/比赛打 tag；参数集中；每次调参记录哈希、参数、电压、日志。

**Q57. Tell me about a conflict in the team and how you resolved it.**

- **L1**: "The vision and control sides disagreed about who compensates for latency. We compared measurements and agreed that vision compensates using timestamps, and control only filters."
- **L2** — *How did you decide?* "We ran the same tracking test with both options and picked the lower steady error."
- **L3** — *What did you learn?* "Settle interfaces with data and a written frame definition, not opinions."

中文：视觉 vs 电控延迟补偿归属；用同一测试比较；接口用数据和书面定义解决。

**Q58. What would you improve?**

- **L1**: "Fixed-period loops with explicit dt, a dedicated CAN transmit task, a latched fail state for the feeder with a current cut-off, a speed-ready gate before feeding, and systematic logging for A/B tests."
- **L2** — *Which first?* "The fixed period with explicit dt, because it affects every loop and several hard-coded constants."
- **L3** — *How would you verify it?* "Measure the period with a GPIO pin, re-run the step and disturbance tests, and compare the logs with the old build."

中文：改进=固定周期+显式 dt、独立发送任务、堵转锁存、弹速就绪门、系统日志；先做固定周期；用实测验证。

## 第三部分：STAR 案例（对应关键故障 Top3 与一个备选）

> 下面四个案例使用自查表第 30 项的关键故障，数字为教材统一设计取值；与实机记忆不同时以实机为准并同步修改。

**STAR 1：连发掉速 → 弹速离散（促成 PID → 变增益 PID → 滑模）**
- S: 连续发射时，每发 42 mm 弹丸擦过摩擦轮，转速跌落约 600–900 rpm，恢复约 120 ms，弹速离散约 ±1 m/s（PID 阶段，Kp=12、Ki=0.2）。
- T: 让弹速落在 16 m/s 上限以内的稳定窗口。
- A: 记录轮速与裁判上报的 bullet_speed，确认是掉速后恢复慢，而不是测量噪声；先试变增益 PID（$K_p=k_0\log_{10}(k_1|e|)+k_2$，k0=1、k1=8、k2=5），大误差响应改善，但近零误差增益难调；再改滑模（s=ė+5e，ρ=2，ε=1，输出限幅 10000）；最后加弹速分档反馈（高于 16.0 m/s 减 50 rpm，低于 15.7 m/s 加 50 rpm）。
- R: 掉速恢复缩短到约 60–80 ms，弹速离散约 ±0.3 m/s，赛前实测 15.2–16.0 m/s；满电时曾 2 次测到 16.2 m/s 超限，引入下调逻辑后解决。
- English: "In our setup, the fixed-gain PID let the wheel speed dip for about 120 ms after each shot. Moving to sliding mode cut that to about 60 to 80 ms and the spread to about plus or minus 0.3 m/s."

**STAR 2：大弹丸拨盘堵转**
- S: 42 mm 大弹丸供弹时，拨盘偶发卡死，电机持续堵转发热。
- T: 自动检测并恢复，且不能无限堵转。
- A: 用"残余误差大于 5000 计数且持续超过 1000 ms"判堵转，不单看电流（正常起步电流也高）；判定后反向 52450 计数（两格）只尝试一次；再失败进入 SHOOT_FAIL，停止发射并上报，由操作手处理；SHOOT_FAIL 不等于断电流，只有 RELAX 才零电流。
- R: 偶发卡弹可以自动恢复，不再因长时间堵转而过热；一次反退仍失败的情况有明确的失败状态，不会无限重试。
- English: "A jam is declared when the position error stays large for a second; we reverse two slots once, and if that fails we latch a fail state instead of retrying forever."

**STAR 3：yaw 在 ±180 度跳变 / 视觉角度平均错误**
- S: 自瞄或模式切换时，yaw 在 ±180° 附近云台突然反向大幅转动；原因是把 179° 和 −179° 直接平均得到 0°。
- T: 让目标角和滤波输出连续，云台走最短路径。
- A: 误差先绕回到 ±180°；滤波时把角度映射到单位圆（sin/cos 分别滤波）再用 atan2 还原；视觉丢失（超过 100 ms 无有效目标）时目标角保持当前值，避免接管跳变。
- R: ±180° 附近的跳变消失，视觉接管和退出时云台没有突跳。
- English: "We filtered the sine and cosine of the angle instead of the angle itself, so crossing plus or minus 180 degrees no longer produces a jump."

**STAR 4（备选）：CAN 偏线 / 供电掉电导致电机离线**
- S: 调试和搬运后，偶发某个电机反馈消失，对应机构突然失控或无力；原因是 CAN 接线偏线、接触不良，或供电瞬间掉电。
- T: 一旦离线立刻进入安全状态，并方便排查。
- A: detect 任务以 10 ms 周期按时间戳判超时（电机反馈 50 ms、DBUS 100 ms、视觉 100 ms、板间 100 ms）；任一关键源离线则对应机构进入 RELAX（电流或电压指令置 0，清 PID 积分与历史，保持 CAN 发送心跳）；同时重新检查线束与供电。
- R: 离线时机构能安全失能，恢复后需要重新使能，不再出现失控飞车。
- English: "Each feedback source has a timestamp and a timeout; when one goes stale, the related mechanism is put into a relax state with zero current."

## 本章要点速记

1. 先背 30s/90s/3min；每题 L1 → L2 → L3 逐层展开。
2. 数字用自查表的统一取值，记不清用模板，不说没核对的数字。
3. 个人贡献 = 云台、发射、底盘三类控制回路；3V3 是团队成绩。
4. 周期（约 3 ms）、延迟、数据新鲜度是亮点，不说 1 kHz。
5. yaw 外环 Kp=28（4.5 Hz）、pitch 25（4.0 Hz），内环 30–40 Hz。
6. 迭代历史 PID → 变增益 → 滑模，三者不同时运行。
7. 反思"what I would improve"体现深度。
