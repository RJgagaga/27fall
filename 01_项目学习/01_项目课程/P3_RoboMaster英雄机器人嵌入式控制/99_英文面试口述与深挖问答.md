# 99 英文面试口述与深挖问答（P3 RoboMaster 英雄机器人）

> **一句话定位**：把 P3 变成可以"说出来"的英文：30 秒、90 秒、3 分钟三种口述，50 个带追问的问答，4 个 STAR 案例，以及"记不清细节时"的诚实回答模板。所有事实以 CV 为准；凡涉及具体数字，只有你亲自核对过才能说，否则用模板。

## 使用方法

1. 先背三种口述（第一部分），能不看稿说出来；
2. 按章节过问答（第二部分），每题先说**主答案**，再准备**追问**；
3. 用第四部分的诚实模板处理"我不记得具体数字"；
4. 把 [README 自查表](./README.md#实机细节自查表) 填完，再回来把 `[ ]` 里的内容换成真实值。

## 第一部分：三种口述

### 1. 30 seconds

> "I was responsible for the embedded closed-loop control of our university team's hero robot in RoboMaster. On an STM32F407 with FreeRTOS and CAN, I built three sets of loops: a cascaded angle–rate PID for the gimbal, speed and position loops for the launcher, and power-aware wheel-speed control for the Mecanum chassis. The robot competed in the 2025 national final and won third prize."

中文：负责英雄机器人嵌入式闭环控制；STM32F407/FreeRTOS/CAN 上做云台角度-角速度串级 PID、发射机构速度/位置环、麦轮底盘功率感知轮速控制；进入 2025 国赛获三等奖。

### 2. 90 seconds

> "RoboMaster is a university robotics competition, and our hero robot fires large 42-millimetre projectiles, so every shot has to be accurate and consistent. I worked on the low-level control on an STM32F407 running FreeRTOS, with motors on CAN.
>
> For the gimbal, I built a cascaded PID for pitch and yaw: the outer loop turns the angle error into a target angular rate, and the inner loop turns the rate error into a motor current command. Feedback came from the IMU and an attitude filter plus the motor encoders. Remote-control and vision targets are turned into continuous attitude commands, so aiming stays stable and tracking is smooth.
>
> For the launcher, the friction wheels have a speed loop and the feeder has a position–velocity dual loop. I iterated the wheel controller from PID to variable-gain PID to sliding-mode control, and used muzzle-velocity feedback to keep continuous firing stable.
>
> For the chassis, I allocated the body velocity to four Mecanum wheels, estimated the power demand from power and buffer-energy feedback, and scaled the wheel speeds so the robot stayed manoeuvrable under the power limit. We reached the national final and won third prize."

中文：同 30 秒，展开三个子系统，各一句核心。

### 3. 3 minutes（结构：背景→三个回路→难点→收获）

> "**Context.** In RoboMaster, teams build robots that shoot projectiles at each other under a referee system that enforces limits on heat, chassis power and muzzle speed. Our hero robot shoots 42-millimetre projectiles at a low rate, so aiming and shot consistency matter a lot. My job was the embedded control of the gimbal, the launcher and the chassis on an STM32F407 with FreeRTOS and CAN.
>
> **Gimbal.** The gimbal is a pair of inertial axes with gravity and disturbances from the chassis and the recoil. I used a cascaded structure. The inner loop is an angular-rate loop that uses the gyro directly, so I don't need to differentiate an angle; the outer loop is an angle loop. The inner loop should be several times faster than the outer, so I tuned the inner first, then the outer, then added feedforward for things like gravity and target velocity. Targets come from the remote or from the vision computer. The vision targets are delayed and arrive at a lower rate, so I smooth them and switch modes without a jump, keeping the commands continuous.
>
> **Launcher.** The friction wheels must hold their speed even when a projectile takes energy from them, and the muzzle velocity must stay inside the rule limit. I started with PID, moved to a variable-gain PID, and then to sliding-mode control, and used the measured muzzle velocity to correct the wheel target. The feeder uses a position loop on top of a velocity loop so each shot is a fixed step, and a jam is detected when it fails to reach the target in time, followed by a reverse move.
>
> **Chassis.** Body velocity is mapped to four wheel speeds with the Mecanum kinematics. Because the referee limits chassis power, I estimated the power demand from power and buffer-energy feedback and scaled all four wheel speeds by a common factor, which keeps the direction of motion while reducing the demand.
>
> **What I learned.** The biggest lesson was that timing is part of the controller: the loop period, the delays and the data freshness all affect stability. I also learned to test disturbances on the real robot, not just step responses. We reached the national final and won third prize."

中文：背景—云台—发射—底盘—收获（时序是控制器一部分、实机扰动测试）。

## 第二部分：英文问答（50 题）

> 格式：**Q** → **A（英文主答案）** → *中文要点* → **Follow-up**（追问方向）。带 `[ ]` 的是要换成你真实数字的位置。

### A. 项目总览与背景

**Q1. Tell me about your hero robot project.**
A: 用 90 秒口述前两段。*中文：背景+职责+三回路+成绩。* Follow-up: Which part are you most proud of? → 选一个你能讲最深的（建议发射或云台）。

**Q2. What was your personal contribution versus the team's?**
A: "The robot was a team effort with mechanical, vision and strategy members. My part was the embedded control: the gimbal, launcher and chassis loops on the STM32." *中文：团队 vs 个人要清楚。* Follow-up: Who wrote the vision? → 视觉同学；我定义并实现了与它的目标接口。【待你补全：团队分工】

**Q3. What is a hero robot and why is control hard?**
A: "It fires large 42-millimetre projectiles at a low rate. The heavy projectile makes the wheel speed dip when it passes, feeding jams are more likely, the machine is heavy so the chassis hits power limits, and the gimbal carries a big inertia." *中文：大弹丸→掉速/卡弹/功率/惯量。*

**Q4. How do the rules shape your controller?**
A: "Heat limits gate the feeder, chassis power and buffer energy force wheel-speed scaling, and the muzzle-velocity cap is why I use velocity feedback." Follow-up: What is buffer energy? → 超功率时消耗的缓冲池，耗尽会被惩罚。

**Q5. Describe the system architecture.**
A: "An STM32F407 with FreeRTOS; motors on CAN; IMU over SPI with DMA; remote and referee over UART; vision over USB. Tasks for attitude, gimbal, chassis, communication and monitoring." Follow-up: single or dual board? → 【待你补全】

**Q6. Why STM32F407 and FreeRTOS?**
A: "The Cortex-M4F has an FPU, so quaternion math and PID run in floating point; it has two CAN controllers and enough DMA. FreeRTOS gives priorities and clean synchronisation between event-driven and periodic work." Follow-up: downsides of RTOS? → 抖动、栈内存、优先级反转。

### B. FreeRTOS 与时序

**Q7. What tasks did you have and what priorities?**
A: "[Attitude task, gimbal task, chassis task, communication tasks, monitoring, low-priority UI]. The attitude task is notified by the IMU DMA completion." Follow-up: why that priority? → 用"现状→风险→改进"结构，见第四部分。

**Q8. How fast were your control loops?**
A (honest): "The tick was 1 ms, but I know the effective loop period was longer because of one-tick delays inside the loop. I would measure it with a GPIO toggle; my estimate was a few milliseconds." Follow-up: What would you change? → `vTaskDelayUntil` 固定周期，把发送移到专职任务。

**Q9. vTaskDelay vs vTaskDelayUntil?**
A: "`vTaskDelay` waits relative to the call time, so the period drifts with execution time and aligns to tick boundaries. `vTaskDelayUntil` keeps an absolute schedule, so the average period is fixed." *中文：相对 vs 绝对。*

**Q10. How do you get IMU data into a task?**
A: "DMA completion triggers an interrupt that sends a task notification; the task decodes and runs the filter. Notifications can coalesce, so a slow task can miss samples; I'd count them." Follow-up: portYIELD_FROM_ISR? → 立即切换，省 0～1 ms。

**Q11. What is priority inversion?**
A: "A low-priority task holds a lock needed by a high-priority one, and a medium task preempts the low one. Mutexes with priority inheritance bound it." Follow-up: Did you see it? → 若没有就说 "I designed to avoid it".

**Q12. What happens to a PID if the loop period changes?**
A: "If dt isn't in the equations, the effective integral and derivative gains scale with the period, so I tune at a fixed period; better is to use dt explicitly and keep the period stable." Follow-up: effect on delay/phase margin → 相位滞后 = ω·T_d。

### C. CAN 与电机

**Q13. Why CAN?**
A: "Differential signalling for noise immunity, multi-node broadcast, arbitration by ID, hardware CRC and retransmission, and short fixed frames for predictable latency."

**Q14. How did you estimate bus load?**
A: "A standard 8-byte frame is about 111 bits, so about 111 µs at 1 Mbps. Four motors reporting at 1 kHz plus one command frame is roughly 55 percent; eight motors on one bus would exceed 100 percent, so motors are split across the two buses." Follow-up: your split? → 【待你补全】

**Q15. What does the motor feedback contain and how do you convert it?**
A: "Single-turn rotor angle 0–8191, signed rpm, torque current and temperature. rpm × 2π/60 gives rad/s; divide by the gear ratio for the output shaft." Follow-up: current command scale → ±16384 ↔ ±20 A for the M3508 driver.

**Q16. How do you get a multi-turn angle?**
A: "Compare consecutive samples; a jump larger than half a turn means a wrap, so adjust a turn counter. It assumes less than half a turn between samples, so I watch frame intervals." Follow-up: failure? → 8000 rpm 丢 3 帧即混叠。

**Q17. Where is the current loop?**
A: "Inside the motor driver. My loops output current commands."

**Q18. How do you detect a motor offline?**
A: "Timestamp in the RX callback; a monitor compares age to a threshold of a few feedback periods and moves the subsystem to a safe state."

### D. IMU / AHRS

**Q19. How do you estimate gimbal attitude?**
A: "Gyro and accelerometer over SPI, bias and mounting correction, then a Mahony-style complementary filter updating a quaternion, converted to Euler angles for control."

**Q20. Explain the Mahony filter.**
A: "Predict gravity from the quaternion, cross it with the measured acceleration direction, feed the error back into the gyro rates with a PI term, integrate and normalise. Kp sets convergence speed, Ki learns bias." Follow-up: choose Kp? → fc≈Kp/2π。

**Q21. Why quaternions?**
A: "No gimbal lock, cheap and stable integration; Euler angles are used only for output."

**Q22. Why does yaw drift?**
A: "Gravity can't observe yaw; without a magnetometer, only gyro integration, so bias becomes drift. For stabilisation and relative vision targets it is acceptable over a match." Follow-up: how to reduce? → 标定、恒温、静止零偏更新。

**Q23. Mahony vs Madgwick vs Kalman?**
A: "Mahony and Madgwick are cheap nonlinear complementary filters; a Kalman filter weights sensors optimally but needs noise models and compute. I used the cheap one on the MCU and an EKF in my navigation project."

**Q24. What did you do about accelerometer disturbances?**
A: "Gate or reduce the correction when the acceleration magnitude deviates from gravity." 若你的实现没有：说 "I would add".

### E. 云台

**Q25. Why cascaded PID?**
A: "The inner rate loop uses the gyro directly, so no differentiation, makes the plant look like an integrator for the outer loop, rejects disturbances quickly and lets me limit the rate."

**Q26. How did you choose the bandwidths?**
A: "Inner at least four to ten times the outer. For a PI inner loop that cancels the mechanical pole, the bandwidth is Kt·Kp/J, and the outer gain for critical damping is a quarter of that." Follow-up: limits on inner bandwidth → 延迟、噪声、电流限幅。

**Q27. How did you tune?**
A: "Signs first with small current, then rate loop P, small I, then angle loop, then feedforward, then disturbance tests: push, spin the chassis, fire."

**Q28. How do you handle yaw wraparound?**
A: "Wrap the error to ±180 degrees so the gimbal takes the short way; averaging angles uses sine and cosine."

**Q29. How do you handle vision latency?**
A: "Anchor the target to the attitude at capture time using timestamps, then extrapolate; otherwise a 40 ms delay at 90 deg/s gives 3.6 degrees of error." Follow-up: where done? → 【待你补全】

**Q30. How do you avoid a jump when switching modes?**
A: "Initialise the filtered target to the current command and rate-limit toward the new one; on exit set the manual target to the current attitude."

**Q31. How do you reject chassis spinning?**
A: "Use the IMU's absolute rate and angle for feedback so the loop sees the world frame; test with the chassis spinning."

### F. 发射

**Q32. Describe the launcher control.**
A: 见主答案：friction wheel speed loop, feeder position loop on velocity loop, state machine, heat gating, jam handling.

**Q33. Why did the wheel speed drop when a projectile passed?**
A: "Energy: the projectile's kinetic energy, about 5.8 J for 45 g at 16 m/s, comes from the wheels' rotation. The controller can't prevent the dip, only set recovery speed and overshoot."

**Q34. Why iterate from PID to variable-gain PID to sliding mode?**
A: "[Your real symptoms]. In general, a fixed-gain PID trades small-error noise against large-error responsiveness and has integral windup at spin-up; variable gain schedules the proportional gain with the error; sliding mode adds robustness to load and parameter changes via a switching term with a boundary layer." Follow-up: evidence? → 【待你补全：数据】

**Q35. What is variable-gain PID in your case?**
A: "The proportional gain depends on the error magnitude, for example a logarithmic law Kp = k0·log10(k1·|e|)+k2, bounded below and above; chosen from desired gains at a small and a large error." Follow-up: why log? → 增长慢，不暴涨。

**Q36. Explain your sliding-mode controller.**
A: "Sliding variable s = ė + c·e; ideally s=0 gives exponential decay of the error with rate c. The control has an equivalent part proportional to the error and a switching part ρ·sat(s/ε); the saturation boundary layer limits chattering." Follow-up: units/stability → 诚实：参数靠调，稳定性靠边界层分析与实测。

**Q37. What is chattering and how do you reduce it?**
A: "High-frequency switching of the control caused by the discontinuous term plus delay and sampling. Use a boundary layer or saturation, smaller switching gain, filtering of the derivative, and make the boundary layer larger than the ripple k·T."

**Q38. Is it really sliding mode with a boundary layer?**
A (honest): "With a boundary layer it is a quasi-sliding-mode controller. Inside the layer it behaves like a high-gain proportional law; outside it applies the full switching term. Its robustness comes from the gain and the saturation, and I verified it empirically." 

**Q39. How does muzzle-velocity feedback work?**
A: "The referee reports each shot's speed. I correct the wheel speed target by a fraction of the speed error after each shot, with a dead band, step limit and a cap below the rule limit. Convergence needs 0<Kv·κ<2." Follow-up: why not feed into the speed loop? → 事件触发、延迟、噪声。

**Q40. How does the feeder work?**
A: "A position loop on the accumulated encoder count outputs a speed reference, clamped; a velocity loop outputs current. One shot is a fixed count increment: 8192 × gear ratio × 60/360."

**Q41. How do you detect and clear a jam?**
A: "If the feeder doesn't reach the target within a time window, I declare a jam, reverse the target, and re-check; failing again I flag a fault. Not current alone, since current is high during normal starts." Follow-up: improvements → 锁存 FAIL、重试次数、电流+位置组合。

**Q42. How do you respect the heat limit?**
A: "A local heat estimate adds per-shot heat immediately and decays at the cooling rate; I take the max with the referee's value and only feed if the next shot fits under the limit minus a margin."

**Q43. Fire_OFF vs relax?**
A: "Fire_OFF sets target zero but the controller may still brake; relax zeros all currents."

### G. 底盘与功率

**Q44. Derive the Mecanum kinematics.**
A: "Wheel contact velocity is body velocity plus ω×r; the roller axis fixes the direction in which each wheel can push, so wheel speed is the projection onto that direction divided by the radius. For an X layout: [vx−vy−dω, vx+vy+dω, vx+vy−dω, vx−vy+dω]/r with d=a+b." Follow-up: inverse → 伪逆，列正交，v=(r/4)Σ…。

**Q45. Why scale all four wheels together?**
A: "Independent clipping changes the ratios and the robot goes in a different direction; a common factor keeps the direction and only slows it."

**Q46. How does your power control work?**
A: "I estimate the power demand from the wheel loops and the measured power and buffer energy, then compute a common scale factor so that the predicted power fits the limit, and apply it to the wheel speed references before the wheel PIDs. A quadratic model a·k²+b·k+c in the scale k makes the scale solvable in closed form." Follow-up: hard guarantee? → 不是，模型误差与恢复平滑可能超限。

**Q47. What is buffer energy and how is it used?**
A: "A small energy reserve the referee tracks: it drains when you exceed the power limit. Keeping it above a threshold avoids penalties, so low buffer makes the limiter more conservative."

**Q48. How does the chassis follow the gimbal?**
A: "The gimbal yaw relative angle from the encoder is the error; a controller turns it into a chassis rotation command, and the translation command is rotated from the gimbal frame to the chassis frame."

### H. 调试与反思

**Q49. Tell me about the hardest bug.**
A: 用 STAR（第三部分）；【待你补全真实案例】。

**Q50. What would you improve?**
A: "Fixed-period loops with explicit dt, a dedicated CAN transmit task, a latched fail state for the feeder with current cut-off, a speed-ready gate before feeding, systematic logging for A/B tests, and an integral term or disturbance observer for the sliding-mode wheel loop."

**STAR 2：卡弹**
- S: 拨盘偶发卡弹，电机发热。

- R: 【待你补全】

**STAR 3：功率超限被罚**

## 本章要点速记

1. 先背 30s/90s/3min。2. 每题"主答案+追问"。3. 数字只用核对过的。4. 记不清用模板。5. 个人贡献=三类控制回路。6. 周期、延迟、数据新鲜度是亮点。7. 反思"what I would improve"体现深度。

## 自测

1. 不看稿说出 90 秒版本。2. 用 2 句话解释串级 PID。3. 解释为何统一缩放四轮。4. 推导弹速外环收敛条件。5. 说出 3 个你会改进的点。
