# 99 P5 英文面试口述与深挖问答

> **用法**：先背熟三个口述版本（30 秒/90 秒/3 分钟），再按主题刷问答。每题给出英文答案、中文要点和可能的追问。文中的具体数字取自 [README](./README.md) 的实机细节自查表；开口时用 about、roughly 这类说法，被追问来源时要能讲清楚怎么测的。记不清时用文末的诚实模板。

**前置**：[README](./README.md)（自查表）、各章正文。

**难度分级**：A–H 组为按主题的基础与追问题（相当于 L1/L2，每题的"追问"即 L2）；I–L 组（第 69–104 题）明确标注 L1 基础、L2 追问、L3 深挖，并聚焦 ROS2 实现、定位与时间、规划与控制、频率与工程化。工程化专题的 12 道问答见 [20 工程化实践](./20_工程化实践_频率周期与问题解决.md) 末尾。ROS 相关回答统一口径：ROS 2 Humble，Nav2 体系；如被问及 ROS1 的 move_base，只在对比语境下说明差异。

## 一、三个口述版本

### 30 秒版

> In my lab project, I worked on autonomous navigation for a car-like robot. I was responsible for the algorithm chain: EKF and AMCL localisation fusing IMU, wheel encoders and 2D LiDAR; Hybrid A-star for global planning under the vehicle footprint and turning radius; TEB for local trajectory optimisation; and an MPC controller to track that trajectory. We ran the whole chain on the real vehicle.

（中文：车式机器人自主导航；负责定位—全局—局部—MPC 全链；实车运行。）

### 90 秒版

> The project was a car-like mobile robot, supervised by Professor Fu. My job was the algorithm chain from localisation to tracking. For localisation, I fused wheel encoders, an IMU and a 2D LiDAR: an EKF gave a smooth state, and AMCL corrected it against the map. The tricky part was inconsistent timing, coordinate frames and motion direction, which caused drift and pose jumps, so I made those consistent and kept the output continuous. For global planning I used Hybrid A-star, which searches over position and heading with kinematic primitives and checks the vehicle footprint and the minimum turning radius. Locally, TEB optimised a time-parameterised trajectory against the global path and the local costmap and gave pose and curvature references. Finally an MPC on a kinematic bicycle model tracked it under position, heading, speed and steering constraints. The result was closed-loop autonomous navigation on the real vehicle.
> In the lab hall and corridor, about 25 by 15 metres, the car ran full goal-to-goal missions at up to 0.8 metres per second. At 0.5 metres per second the mean cross-track error was about 6 centimetres, and 20 out of 22 recorded runs succeeded.
> 中文：在约 25×15 m 的实验室大厅与走廊实跑，最高 0.8 m/s；0.5 m/s 下横向误差均值约 6 cm，22 次成功 20 次。

3. 定位（40 秒）：EKF 融合；AMCL；跳变的成因（时间、坐标、方向）与处理。

## 二、问答（按主题）

### A. 项目与角色

**1. What was your role?**
> I was responsible for the navigation algorithm chain: localisation, global planning, local trajectory optimisation and MPC tracking, and for getting it working on the real vehicle.
> 中文：负责从定位到 MPC 跟踪的算法链并上实车。追问：哪些是别人做的？
> I did the localisation fusion, the planner integration and tuning, and the MPC from modelling to on-vehicle tuning. A labmate built the chassis and the STM32 motor and servo firmware, and Professor Fu reviewed progress every week.
> 中文：定位融合、规划集成调参、MPC 全程是我；底盘机械与下位机固件是同学做的；傅老师每周评审。

**2. Why a car-like robot, and what makes it different from differential drive?**
> A car-like robot has a minimum turning radius and cannot rotate in place or move sideways, so planning and control must respect non-holonomic constraints.
> 追问：举例说明对规划的影响——需要在 (x,y,θ) 搜索、曲率约束、可能要倒车。

**3. Describe the system architecture.**
> Sensors feed localisation; localisation provides the vehicle state; the global planner makes a path on the map; TEB turns it into a local timed trajectory; MPC tracks it; the chassis executes. Odom is for local modules, map for global ones.

**4. What were the biggest challenges?**
> Consistency between modules: timing, frames and motion direction. Also making the planned trajectory drivable and tracked smoothly on the real vehicle.
> The hardest one was a pose jump when turning and reversing. It turned out to be three small inconsistencies at once: a laser timestamp about 60 milliseconds late, an unsigned wheel speed that lost the reverse direction, and a steering sign convention that differed between modules.
> 中文：最难的是转弯和倒车时的位姿跳变，实为三个小不一致叠加：激光时间戳晚约 60 ms、轮速无符号丢了倒车方向、转向符号约定不一致。

**5. What would you improve?**
> Better time synchronisation at the hardware level, more systematic parameter tuning from logged data, and evaluating robustness in more scenes.

### B. 运动学与传感器

**6. Derive the bicycle model.**
> Rear-axle reference, rolling without slip, the instantaneous centre where the perpendiculars meet; the triangle gives tan δ = L/R, so yaw rate = v tan δ / L.
> 追问：何时失效？高速/大侧向加速度。

**7. How do you compute the minimum turning radius?**
> R_min = L / tan δ_max. For example L = 0.5 m and 30° gives 0.87 m (teaching numbers).

**8. How does reversing change the model?**
> v becomes negative, so the same steering angle turns the heading the opposite way. Signs must be consistent in odometry, planner and controller.

**9. Why integrate with arcs rather than Euler?**
> Euler uses the heading at the start of the step and drifts outwards in turns; the exact arc keeps the radius.

**10. How do you get odometry from encoders?**
> Count ticks, convert to distance with wheel radius, divide by time for speed, and use steering angle or gyro for yaw rate. Calibrate wheel radius and steering mapping.
> 追问：误差？标度误差 2% 走 100 m 差 2 m；航向 1° 走 50 m 横向 0.87 m。

**11. What are the main IMU errors?**
> Gyro bias and noise. Bias integrates linearly into heading error, so it dominates; I don't integrate the accelerometer for position.

**12. What are the LiDAR issues?**
> Motion distortion during a scan, glass or dark surfaces, dynamic objects, and mounting offset. Timestamps and static transform must be right.

**13. How do you check a sensor frame is correct?**
> Rotate the car and check signs, drive straight and check axes, and verify static transforms by aligning a known wall.

**14. What does a timestamp mean and what goes wrong?**
> It should be the measurement time. Using arrival time adds latency; error is roughly speed times delay, for example 80 ms at 0.5 rad/s is 2.3° of heading.

**15. How do you find a time offset between sensors?**
> Cross-correlate two estimates of the same quantity, such as gyro yaw rate and yaw rate from wheel odometry, and find the lag of the peak.

**16. What is the reversing sign problem?**
> If the encoder has no direction or the sign is lost in the interface, dead reckoning moves forward while the LiDAR sees backward motion, causing drift and big corrections.
> 追问：怎么发现？倒车直线实验、对比 odom 速度符号与激光位移方向。
> In our case the lower controller sent the wheel speed as an unsigned magnitude and the direction was a separate bit that my parser ignored. In a 3-metre reverse test, odometry said forward 3 metres while scan matching said backward 3 metres. I switched to a signed integer and added a gear check, and afterwards the reverse distance matched a tape measure within about 5 centimetres.
> 中文：下位机把速度当无符号幅值发，方向是单独一位，我的解析忽略了它；倒车 3 m 测试 odom 说前进、激光说后退；改为带符号并加挡位校验，之后与卷尺相差约 5 cm。

### C. EKF

**17. Explain the EKF.**
> Predict with the motion model and grow covariance with FPFᵀ + Q; update with a measurement using the Kalman gain from the prediction and measurement uncertainties; linearise nonlinear models with Jacobians.

**18. Why FPFᵀ?**
> New states are linear combinations of old ones, so covariance propagates as F P Fᵀ. It also creates correlations, such as heading error leaking into lateral position.

**19. How do you choose Q and R?**
> R from sensor statistics at rest; Q from input noise mapped through the Jacobian plus model error, then check innovation consistency.
> About 0.05 metres per second for wheel speed and 0.01 radians per second for the gyro, with process noise on the diagonal around 1e-3 to 1e-2. I set the gyro value above its resting noise of about 0.003 to cover temperature drift, and checked NIS.
> 中文：轮速约 0.05 m/s、陀螺约 0.01 rad/s，过程噪声对角元约 1e-3~1e-2；陀螺比静止噪声 0.003 放大以覆盖温漂，并用 NIS 检查。

**20. What states did you use?**
> Typically x, y, yaw and speed, maybe gyro bias. In our setup the filter ran at 30 Hz in 2D mode, with position, yaw and the body-frame velocities as state, fed by wheel speed, a yaw rate from the bicycle model and the gyro.
> 中文：我们是 30 Hz、2D 模式，状态含位置、偏航与机体系速度，输入为轮速、自行车模型的偏航角速度和陀螺。

**21. What did you fuse and what not?**
> Wheel speed and gyro yaw rate for the continuous state; avoided fusing integrated position or unreferenced yaw as absolute measurements.

**22. What is the Mahalanobis gate?**
> The squared innovation weighted by S⁻¹; reject if it exceeds a chi-square threshold, for example 5.99 for two degrees of freedom at 95%.

**23. What is a common EKF bug?**
> Not wrapping yaw innovation; zero covariance in the incoming messages; double TF publishers.

**24. Why doesn't the x variance shrink when only y and yaw are measured?**
> Those measurements carry no information about x unless x is correlated with them in the prior. It's an observability point.

**25. How do you handle delayed measurements?**
> Keep a short history, roll back to the measurement time, update, and replay the later inputs.

### D. AMCL 与协同

**26. How does AMCL work?**
> Particles hold pose hypotheses; the motion model moves them; the likelihood-field observation model weights them by the distance of scan endpoints to map obstacles; resampling keeps good ones; KLD sampling adapts the number.

**27. Why a particle filter and not an EKF?**
> The observation model is strongly nonlinear and the posterior can be multimodal.

**28. What is the likelihood field model?**
> Look up the distance from each beam endpoint to the nearest obstacle in a precomputed distance map, give a Gaussian plus uniform probability. Fast but ignores occlusion.

**29. What is KLD sampling?**
> It chooses the number of particles so that the approximation error stays under a bound; spread-out distributions need more particles.

**30. What is the kidnapped robot problem?**
> The robot is moved without the filter knowing. Recovery injects random particles when the short-term average weight drops below the long-term one.

**31. What happens to AMCL when the car reverses?**
> The odometry motion model sees a ±180° rotation and noise explodes unless forward and backward are treated symmetrically. I check the model and the sign chain.

**32. Why AMCL publishes map→odom?**
> base_link already has odom as its parent. AMCL multiplies its map pose by the inverse of odom→base_link.

**33. What causes pose jumps and how did you deal with them?**
> Accumulated drift corrected in steps, particle cluster switches, bad initial pose, timing, wrong frames, reversing signs, duplicate TF. I fixed data consistency first, then smoothed or gated corrections, and kept control modules in odom.
> Concretely, I rate-limited the map-to-odom correction to roughly twenty percent of the vehicle speed, and any single correction above half a metre or ten degrees had to be confirmed three times with a tight particle cloud before I applied it.
> 中文：具体做法是把 map→odom 修正限速到约车速的 20%；单次超过 0.5 m 或 10° 的修正要连续 3 次确认且粒子云收敛才应用。

**34. How is the output continuous?**
> The EKF part is smooth by construction; corrections live in map→odom and are rate-limited or confirmed; planners and controllers use odom.

**35. What are the trade-offs of smoothing the correction?**
> It protects control but delays the true correction.

**36. How do you debug a pose jump?**
> Log a bag, compute step sizes of map→odom, correlate with turning, reversing, starting or relocalising. Yaw-rate-proportional means timing; reverse-only means sign; constant offset means static transform.

### E. 代价地图与 Hybrid-A*

**37. What is a costmap?**
> A grid with obstacle cost, inflated around obstacles with exponentially decaying cost, layered from static map and live sensors.

**38. Why check the footprint?**
> A car is a rectangle; collision depends on heading. I use oriented footprint checks, such as a few circles looked up in a distance map.

**39. Why can't you just use A-star?**
> No heading, no curvature limit, no vehicle shape; a corner has zero radius.

**40. Explain Hybrid A-star.**
> Nodes keep continuous (x,y,θ); expansion uses short arcs from steering primitives; a 3D grid keeps only the cheapest node per cell.

**41. How do you choose the step length?**
> Larger than the cell diagonal, so a child leaves the parent cell.

**42. Heuristics?**
> Maximum of a non-holonomic heuristic without obstacles (Reeds-Shepp) and a 2D grid shortest path with obstacles.

**43. Analytic expansion?**
> Periodically try to connect the current node to the goal with a Reeds-Shepp curve; if collision-free, finish.

**44. Is it optimal?**
> No, it is feasible and usually good; discretisation and heuristics break optimality.

**45. How do you ensure the minimum turning radius?**
> Bounded steering in primitives, and a curvature check after smoothing.

**46. How do you handle reversing in planning?**
> Reverse primitives with a reverse penalty and a direction-change penalty; downstream must handle cusps.

**47. Why would a planned path go through a wall?**
> Costmap and map metadata mismatch, frames, stale obstacle layer, unknown space allowed, or a footprint not applied.

### F. TEB

**48. Explain TEB.**
> A trajectory of poses with time intervals, optimised with weighted penalties for path, obstacles, speed, acceleration, non-holonomic kinematics, turning radius and time, solved with g2o.

**49. How does it choose the way around an obstacle?**
> By the gradient of the penalty terms: poses are pushed away from obstacles on the side where the initial trajectory lies; the weights decide the balance.

**50. How does it keep the trajectory drivable?**
> A term forces consecutive poses onto a circular arc and another penalises curvature above the limit. They are soft, so I leave margin and verify.

**51. What are homotopy classes?**
> Topologically different ways around obstacles; TEB is a local optimiser, so it plans several candidates and picks the best, with hysteresis to avoid oscillation.

**52. What does TEB output to the controller?**
> Time-stamped poses with speed and curvature.

**53. Why TEB then MPC?**
> TEB generates a timed obstacle-free trajectory; MPC handles the vehicle model, limits and delay in closed loop.
> In our case the TEB output at 10 Hz sometimes switched homotopy class, and its first velocity command did not account for about 120 ms of actuator delay. The MPC smoothed that, respected steering rate limits and compensated the delay.
> 中文：TEB 10 Hz 偶尔切换同伦类，其第一个速度指令也没考虑约 120 ms 执行延迟；MPC 负责平滑、限转角速率并补偿延迟。

**54. How is TEB different from DWA?**
> DWA samples velocities over a short window; TEB optimises the entire timed trajectory with constraints.

### G. MPC

**55. Describe your MPC.**
> Receding horizon on a kinematic bicycle model, linearised around the reference, quadratic cost on tracking error, effort and effort change, constraints on steering, rate, speed; solve a QP, apply the first input.
> Fifteen steps of 0.1 seconds, so a 1.5 second horizon. State is x, y, yaw and speed; inputs are acceleration and steering angle. Steering is limited to 0.5 radians and 0.6 radians per second, speed from minus 0.3 to 1.0 metres per second, and I solve it with OSQP in about 2 to 5 milliseconds at 20 hertz.
> 中文：15 步×0.1 s，转角限 0.5 rad、0.6 rad/s，速度 −0.3~1.0 m/s，OSQP 20 Hz、约 2–5 ms。

**56. Why N actions and N+1 states?**
> Each action advances one step; the initial state is measured.

**57. Who picks the control values?**
> The optimiser; they are the decision variables. The previous solution is only a warm start.

**58. What is the terminal cost?**
> It represents the cost beyond the horizon, ideally the LQR Riccati matrix, for stability.

**59. How do you pick weights?**
> Start from one over the acceptable error squared, then tune by observing tracking error and steering smoothness.

**60. How do you align the reference in time?**
> Resample the timed trajectory at the prediction times, interpolate with arcs when points are sparse, and add curvature feedforward.

**61. How do you compensate delay?**
> Predict the state forward by the total latency using the commands already sent, and start the optimisation from there.

**62. What if the solver fails?**
> Reuse the shifted previous solution for a few cycles, then slow down and stop.

**63. What causes steering chatter?**
> Noisy or jumping state, small change penalty, discontinuous reference, delay, or switching active constraints.

**64. Compare MPC with Pure Pursuit.**
> Pure Pursuit is geometric with no constraints; MPC includes constraints and prediction, at higher computational cost.

### H. 综合与反思

**65. How did you validate the system?**
> Step by step: sensors, localisation, then planning in RViz, then tracking at low speed, then full runs. The test area was the lab hall and corridor; metrics were cross-track error, goal position and heading error, and success rate over 22 recorded runs.
> 中文：测试场景为实验室大厅与走廊；指标为横向误差、到点位置与航向误差、22 次的成功率。

**66. What failure did you learn most from?**
> See the STAR stories below.

**67. How would you extend to dynamic obstacles?**
> Predict obstacle motion in TEB, use dynamic obstacle terms, and add safety margins and speed limits.

**68. How does this relate to your other projects?**
> The state consistency ideas (timestamps, frames, validity) reappear in my platform work; the control ideas connect to PID, LQR and MPC in my balance projects.

### I. ROS2 与系统实现（L1 基础 / L2 追问 / L3 深挖）

> **分级说明**：L1 是面试官开场会问的定义题，答案 2–3 句；L2 是"你具体怎么做"的追问，要给出本项目取值；L3 是挑战理解深度的问题（取舍、边界、推导），答不上来时用第四节的诚实模板，再给出你的思路。每个问答都有中文译文。

**69. [L1] Which ROS version did you use, and which packages?**
> I used ROS 2 Humble on Ubuntu 22.04. For localisation I used robot_localization for the EKF and nav2_amcl for AMCL. For global planning I used a Hybrid A-star planner, a typical implementation is the Smac planner in Nav2. For the local trajectory I used a ROS 2 port of teb_local_planner, and the MPC tracker was my own node.
> 中文：ROS2 Humble、Ubuntu 22.04；定位用 robot_localization 与 nav2_amcl；全局 Hybrid-A*（典型实现如 Nav2 的 Smac）；局部用 TEB 的 ROS2 移植；MPC 是自己写的节点。

**70. [L2] How are the nodes connected? Walk me through the topics.**
> Wheel odometry and the IMU go into the EKF node, which publishes the filtered odometry and the odom-to-base transform at 30 hertz. The laser scan goes into AMCL and into the local costmap. AMCL gives a pose, and a small node turns it into the smoothed map-to-odom transform. The global planner publishes a path, TEB turns it into a timed local trajectory at 10 hertz, and my MPC node reads that trajectory plus the filtered odometry at 20 hertz and sends the speed and steering command to the chassis bridge node.
> 中文：轮速+IMU→EKF（30 Hz，发布滤波里程计和 odom→base）；激光→AMCL 与局部代价地图；AMCL 位姿→平滑节点→map→odom；全局路径→TEB（10 Hz）→MPC（20 Hz）→底盘桥接节点。

**71. [L3] A node is running but receives no messages. How do you debug it in ROS 2?**
> The first suspect is QoS incompatibility, because in ROS 2 a reliable subscriber will silently not match a best-effort publisher. I run `ros2 topic info -v` on the topic to compare the reliability and durability of both endpoints. Then I check the topic name and namespace, then whether the node is a lifecycle node that is still inactive. Only after that do I look at the data itself. We had exactly this once: the laser driver was best-effort and the costmap subscribed reliably, so the local costmap stayed empty.
> 中文：先查 QoS 不兼容（Reliable 订阅者不会匹配 BestEffort 发布者，且不报错），用 `ros2 topic info -v`；再查话题名/命名空间；再查生命周期节点是否仍 inactive。我们遇到过激光驱动 BestEffort、代价地图 Reliable 订阅，局部代价地图一直为空。

**72. [L1] What is tf2 and why does the transform tree need a single parent per frame?**
> tf2 keeps a buffer of timed transforms between frames, so any node can ask where one frame was relative to another at a given time. The tree has one parent per frame because the transform between two frames must be unambiguous. If two nodes publish the same child frame, the pose flickers between two values.
> 中文：tf2 缓存带时间的坐标变换；每个坐标系只能有一个父节点，否则同一变换有两个来源，位姿在两个值间闪烁。

**73. [L2] Who publishes which transform in your system?**
> The EKF publishes odom to base_link. For map to odom, AMCL computes it, and in our setup a small node smooths it and republishes, so AMCL's own broadcast is switched off. The sensor frames come from the URDF through robot_state_publisher. I check at start-up that there is only one publisher for each child frame.
> 中文：EKF 发 odom→base_link；map→odom 由 AMCL 计算，经平滑节点发布（关掉 AMCL 自身广播）；传感器静态变换来自 URDF；启动自检确认每个子坐标系只有一个发布者。

**74. [L3] Why must the pose in the AMCL message be combined with the odom transform at the laser time, not at the current time?**
> Because the AMCL pose describes where the car was when the scan was taken, about 0.1 second ago. If I multiply it by the current odom-to-base transform, I mix in the distance the car has moved since. At 0.5 metres per second that is 5 centimetres, and in a turn it also adds an angle error. So I look up the odom-to-base transform at the scan stamp with tf2.
> 中文：AMCL 位姿对应扫描时刻（约早 0.1 s）；若乘当前的 odom→base，会混入这段时间的位移（0.5 m/s 时 5 cm，转弯还带角度误差）；应在扫描时间戳查 odom→base。

**75. [L1] What is a lifecycle node and why does Nav2 use them?**
> A lifecycle node has explicit states such as unconfigured, inactive and active. Nav2 uses them so a manager can bring the servers up in a controlled order and shut them down cleanly, and so a node will not publish before its dependencies are ready.
> 中文：生命周期节点有 unconfigured/inactive/active 等显式状态；Nav2 用它们让管理器按序启动、有序关闭，依赖未就绪时不发布。

**76. [L2] How did you manage parameters?**
> All tunables live in YAML files loaded by Python launch files, one file per node group. I may change a value online with `ros2 param set` to try it, but a result only counts when it is written back to the YAML and committed. Each experiment bag stores the parameter dump, so I can reproduce the run.
> 中文：所有参数在 YAML 里，由 Python launch 加载；在线 `ros2 param set` 只用于试验，结论必须写回 YAML 并提交；每个实验的 bag 附参数转储，可复现。

**77. [L3] How would you make the MPC node real-time safe in ROS 2?**
> I would run the solve in its own thread with a fixed-rate timer, avoid dynamic memory allocation in the loop by preallocating the solver matrices, and keep the callback that receives the trajectory separate from the one that solves, using a mutex-protected latest-value buffer. I would also measure the solve time and raise a warning if it passes a budget, in our case 10 milliseconds, as half of the 50-millisecond period is the limit I would not cross. For a hard guarantee I would use a real-time kernel and a real-time scheduling class, but on our vehicle the margin was large enough that a normal kernel was fine.
> 中文：独立线程+固定频率定时器；预分配求解器矩阵避免动态内存；接收轨迹与求解回调分离，用互斥保护的"最新值"缓存；监控求解耗时，超过预算（我们取 10 ms，周期 50 ms 的一部分）告警；硬实时可用实时内核+实时调度类，但本车余量足够，普通内核即可。

### J. 分级深挖：定位与时间

**78. [L1] Why use both an EKF and AMCL?**
> The EKF is smooth and fast but drifts. AMCL uses the map so it does not drift, but it updates at a low rate and can jump. I keep the EKF output as the continuous vehicle state and use AMCL to correct the map-to-odom transform.
> 中文：EKF 平滑高频但会漂；AMCL 不漂但低频会跳；EKF 做连续状态，AMCL 修正 map→odom。

**79. [L2] What are the update rates in the localisation chain and why?**
> The IMU runs at 100 hertz, the wheel encoder report at 50 hertz, the laser at 10 hertz. The EKF runs at 30 hertz, which is below the IMU rate but above the 20 hertz MPC rate, and AMCL updates when the car has moved 0.15 metres or turned 0.2 radians, so in practice around 3 to 10 hertz. The control layer reads the filtered odometry at 20 hertz for MPC.
> 中文：IMU 100 Hz、编码器 50 Hz、激光 10 Hz；EKF 30 Hz；AMCL 按位移 0.15 m/转角 0.2 rad 触发，实际约 3–10 Hz；MPC 20 Hz 读滤波里程计。

**80. [L3] If the EKF runs at 30 Hz but the IMU at 100 Hz, are you throwing data away?**
> Not exactly. robot_localization processes each measurement when it arrives, using its own timestamp, and then outputs the state at the fixed filter frequency. So all 100 hertz of IMU messages are fused; the 30 hertz is the rate at which the state is published and extrapolated. The cost is that each IMU message costs a small update, which is cheap in 2D mode.
> 中文：并不丢数据；robot_localization 按每条测量的时间戳逐条融合，30 Hz 是状态输出/外推的频率；2D 模式下每条更新很便宜。

**81. [L2] How did you fix the laser timestamp problem?**
> The driver stamped each scan with the time the full revolution arrived. A scan takes 100 milliseconds, so the first points were old. I changed the stamp to the start of the scan and compensated a fixed driver delay of about 60 milliseconds, and put all machines on one clock with chrony. I measured the offset by cross-correlating the gyro yaw rate with the yaw rate from scan matching, and the offset dropped from about 60 milliseconds to within 5.
> 中文：驱动打整圈到达时刻；扫描本身 100 ms；改为扫描起始时刻+补偿固定延迟约 60 ms，chrony 统一时钟；用陀螺偏航角速度与扫描匹配偏航角速度互相关估偏移，从约 60 ms 降到 5 ms 内。

**82. [L3] Why is a timestamp error of 60 ms visible in a turn but not on a straight line?**
> The error in position is speed times delay, and the error in heading is yaw rate times delay. On a straight line the car moves along the scan direction, so the points shift a few centimetres along the walls, which scan matching barely notices. In a turn at 30 degrees per second, 60 milliseconds is 1.8 degrees, and at 5 metres that is about 16 centimetres of sideways error on the wall, which is large and changes with the turn.
> 中文：位置误差=速度×延迟，航向误差=角速度×延迟；直行时点沿墙方向平移几厘米，匹配几乎无感；30°/s 转弯时 60 ms 对应 1.8°，5 m 处墙面横向约 16 cm 且随转弯变化。

**83. [L2] How do you set the AMCL parameters for a car-like robot?**
> I use the likelihood field model with 500 to 3000 particles and about 60 beams. The update thresholds are 0.15 metres and 0.2 radians. The odometry noise alphas are around 0.15, 0.10, 0.20 and 0.10. These are deliberately a bit larger than the real noise, because a car with steering slip is less accurate than a differential robot, and it also helps recovery after a bad start.
> 中文：似然场模型、粒子 500–3000、约 60 束；触发阈值 0.15 m/0.2 rad；α 约 0.15/0.10/0.20/0.10，有意略大于实际噪声，因车式转向打滑，也有助于恢复。

**84. [L3] Why can a small covariance still mean a wrong pose?**
> Covariance only measures how tightly the particles cluster, not whether the cluster is in the right place. In a glass-walled area the laser sees the wall behind the glass, many particles agree on a wrong place, and the cloud becomes tight. That is how we got a wrong relocalisation near a glass door. So I treat covariance as necessary but not sufficient: I also compare against the short-term odometry prediction and avoid global relocalisation in that area.
> 中文：协方差只表示粒子聚集程度，不代表聚在正确位置；玻璃墙让激光看到后面的墙，粒子在错误位置一致，协方差变小；我们在玻璃门处因此重定位错误；所以协方差只是必要条件，还要对照里程计预测，并避免在该区域使用全局重定位。

**85. [L2] How do you smooth the map-to-odom correction in practice?**
> I rate-limit it. The correction speed is about 20 percent of the vehicle speed, clipped between 0.05 and 0.2 metres per second, and the angle at 0.15 radians per second. A correction larger than 0.5 metres or 10 degrees must be seen three times in a row, with a particle covariance trace below 0.3, before I apply it. The price is a delay of a second or so, which I accept.
> 中文：限速平滑：速率约车速 20%（0.05–0.2 m/s），角度 0.15 rad/s；>0.5 m 或 10° 的修正需连续 3 次且协方差迹<0.3 才应用；代价是约 1 s 延迟，可接受。

**86. [L3] Is it better to fuse the AMCL pose into a second EKF instead of smoothing?**
> It is more principled: the Kalman gain limits the correction according to the covariances, and the Mahalanobis gate rejects outliers. The weakness is that AMCL's covariance is often optimistic, so I would have to inflate it by hand, and the second filter has its own tuning. I chose the simple smoother because it is easy to explain, easy to test, and its behaviour is bounded by construction. With more time I would test the second EKF and compare step sizes on the same bag.
> 中文：第二个 EKF 更有理论依据（增益自动限幅、马氏门限）；但 AMCL 协方差偏乐观需手动放大，且多一个滤波器要调；我选了平滑器：好解释、好测试、行为有界；有时间会用同一 bag 对比。

### K. 分级深挖：规划、TEB 与 MPC

**87. [L1] Why does a car-like robot need Hybrid A-star instead of grid A-star?**
> Grid A-star ignores heading and assumes the robot can turn on the spot, so its paths have corners the car cannot follow. Hybrid A-star searches over position and heading, expands with motion primitives that respect the steering limit, and checks the footprint, so the path is drivable.
> 中文：栅格 A* 不含航向、假设可原地转，路径有尖角车走不了；Hybrid-A* 在位置+航向上搜索，用满足转向极限的运动基元，并检查 footprint，路径可行驶。

**88. [L2] What are your planner settings and its typical run time?**
> The grid resolution is 0.05 metres, there are 72 heading bins of 5 degrees, and the turning radius is 0.8 metres, a bit above the vehicle's 0.69 metre limit to leave margin. The primitives are left, straight, right, plus reverse. A typical plan takes 20 to 80 milliseconds, the worst narrow turn-around about 300 milliseconds, and I cap it at one second.
> 中文：分辨率 0.05 m、72 个航向 bin（5°）、转弯半径 0.8 m（实车极限 0.69 m 留余量）；基元为左/直/右+倒车；典型 20–80 ms，窄口调头最坏约 300 ms，上限 1 s。

**89. [L3] Hybrid A-star is not complete or optimal. What does that mean for your system?**
> The state is discretised by heading bins, and each cell keeps only one continuous state, so some feasible paths are pruned. It can fail in very tight spots where a continuous solution exists, and its cost is only near-optimal. In practice I handle it with a fallback chain: relax the penalties, allow reversing, and retry once with a finer heading discretisation. And because the later TEB and MPC layers smooth and re-optimise, small path suboptimality does not matter, but a missing path does. That is why I monitor the failure rate and keep the time cap.
> 中文：航向离散且每个格只保留一个连续状态，会剪掉部分可行路径，窄处可能无解、代价只是近似最优；对策是降级链：放宽惩罚、允许倒车、一次细化重试；后面 TEB/MPC 会再优化，次优无妨、无解才是问题，所以监控失败率并设时间上限。

**90. [L1] What does TEB optimise?**
> It optimises a sequence of poses with time intervals between them. The cost includes time, distance to obstacles, closeness to the global path, velocity and acceleration limits, and kinematic constraints for the car. It is solved as a sparse graph optimisation with g2o.
> 中文：优化带时间间隔的位姿序列；代价含时间、障碍距离、贴近全局路径、速度加速度限制、车辆运动学约束；用 g2o 稀疏图优化求解。

**91. [L2] What TEB parameters mattered most?**
> The minimum obstacle distance of 0.3 metres, the obstacle weight 50, the non-holonomic kinematics weight 1000 and the minimum turning radius 0.8 metres. Raising the kinematics weight was what made the trajectory respect the car's turning limit. I allowed four homotopy candidates and added hysteresis to stop switching between them.
> 中文：min_obstacle_dist 0.3 m、障碍权重 50、非完整运动学权重 1000、最小转弯半径 0.8 m；提高运动学权重让轨迹守住转弯极限；4 个同伦候选+选择滞回防止切换。

**92. [L3] Why is the turning radius a soft constraint in TEB, and what do you do about it?**
> Because TEB puts all constraints as penalties in one least-squares problem, so none is a hard bound. If the obstacle penalty is stronger, the optimiser trades the turning radius for clearance. I deal with it in three ways: set the planner radius larger than the vehicle limit, 0.8 against 0.69; raise the weight; and clip steering again in the MPC, which has hard constraints. The last layer is the one that guarantees feasibility.
> 中文：TEB 把所有约束作为二次惩罚放进同一个最小二乘，没有硬约束；障碍惩罚更强时会牺牲转弯半径；对策：规划半径取大于实车极限（0.8 vs 0.69）、提高权重、MPC 里有硬约束再限幅，最后一层保证可行。

**93. [L2] How does the MPC receive the trajectory from TEB?**
> TEB publishes a trajectory with a time-from-start for every pose at 10 hertz. My MPC node keeps the latest one and resamples it at its own prediction times: 15 steps of 0.1 seconds from now. If the points are sparse I interpolate along an arc, not a straight line, and add a curvature feedforward.
> 中文：TEB 以 10 Hz 发布带 time_from_start 的轨迹；MPC 保存最新一条，按自己的预测时刻（从现在起 15 步×0.1 s）重采样；点稀疏时圆弧插值并加曲率前馈。

**94. [L3] How do you compensate for the 120 ms of delay in the MPC?**
> I take the state at the measurement time and propagate it forward through the vehicle model using the commands that have already been sent but not yet applied, over the 120 milliseconds. The solver then starts from this predicted state. The delay is about 20 milliseconds for the serial link and low-level controller, and about 100 milliseconds for the servo and motor response. If the delay estimate is wrong by 40 milliseconds, the predicted position is off by about 2 centimetres at 0.5 metres per second, which the feedback absorbs. A second option is to put the delay into the model as extra states, but the forward prediction was simpler.
> 中文：从测量时刻状态出发，用已发出但未生效的指令按模型前推约 120 ms，再从预测状态求解；延迟约 20 ms（串口+下位机）+约 100 ms（舵机电机）；估计偏 40 ms 时 0.5 m/s 下位置偏约 2 cm，反馈可吸收；另一方案是把延迟放进模型状态，前推更简单。

**95. [L2] How is the MPC problem made into a QP?**
> I linearise the bicycle model around the reference trajectory at each step and discretise it, then stack the states over the horizon. With a quadratic cost and linear constraints on steering, steering rate, speed and acceleration, it becomes a QP that OSQP solves with warm start in two to five milliseconds.
> 中文：沿参考轨迹逐步线性化并离散化，堆叠成预测方程；二次代价+线性约束（转角、转角速率、速度、加速度）→QP，OSQP 热启动 2–5 ms。

**96. [L3] What happens if the QP becomes infeasible, for example after a large pose jump?**
> I use soft constraints with slack variables on the state constraints, so the problem stays feasible, and I keep the hard bounds only on the inputs, because those are physical limits. If the solver still fails or times out, I reuse the shifted previous solution for up to three cycles, then command a smooth stop. I also log every fallback event, because a growing fallback rate is an early sign of an upstream issue.
> 中文：状态约束用松弛变量软化以保持可行，只对输入保留硬约束（物理限制）；仍失败则复用平移后的上一解最多 3 个周期，再平滑停车；记录每次降级事件，降级率上升是上游问题的早期信号。

**97. [L3] Why did the steering chatter, and why did increasing Rd alone not fix it?**
> There were two causes. The TEB reference jumped when its homotopy class switched, and the 120 milliseconds delay made the controller react to old state, so it over-corrected. A bigger steering-rate weight Rd smooths the output but cannot remove the jump in the reference or the phase lag; it only trades off tracking accuracy. The full fix was three changes together: a larger Rd, the delay compensation, and a hysteresis in TEB's class selection.
> 中文：两个原因：TEB 同伦类切换使参考跳变；120 ms 延迟使控制器基于旧状态过度修正；只加大 R_d 只能平滑输出，去不掉参考跳变和相位滞后；完整修复=加大 R_d+延迟补偿+TEB 选择滞回。

### L. 分级深挖：频率、性能与工程化

**98. [L1] What are the control and planning rates in your system?**
> The MPC runs at 20 hertz, TEB at 10, the global planner replans at 1 hertz when needed, the EKF at 30, and the local costmap updates at 10. The chassis link is 50 hertz.
> 中文：MPC 20 Hz、TEB 10 Hz、全局规划需要时 1 Hz 重规划、EKF 30 Hz、局部代价地图 10 Hz、底盘通信 50 Hz。

**99. [L2] How did you choose 20 Hz for the MPC?**
> The vehicle's dominant dynamics, the servo response, are around 100 milliseconds, so a 50-millisecond period is a reasonable fraction of that. The solve takes at most 5 milliseconds, so there is a large margin. Going higher gives little benefit because the TEB reference only updates at 10 hertz and the chassis link at 50. Going lower, to 10 hertz, increased tracking error in bends.
> 中文：舵机主响应约 100 ms，50 ms 周期是其合理分数；求解最多 5 ms，余量大；再高无益（TEB 只有 10 Hz、底盘 50 Hz）；降到 10 Hz 弯道误差增大。

**100. [L3] How would you budget end-to-end latency from an obstacle appearing to the wheels reacting?**
> I add the stages: the laser scan takes up to 100 milliseconds to collect, waiting for the costmap update averages 50, TEB reads it up to 100 milliseconds later, then the MPC period of 50, plus 120 for the actuation delay. The worst case is about 420 milliseconds, the mean around 300. At 0.5 metres per second that is 15 to 21 centimetres of travel, which is why the minimum obstacle distance is 0.3 metres and why I limit the speed in narrow areas.
> 中文：逐级相加：激光采集最多 100 ms，等代价地图更新平均 50，TEB 最多晚 100，MPC 周期 50，执行延迟 120；最坏约 420 ms、平均约 300 ms；0.5 m/s 下对应 15–21 cm 行驶，因此最小障碍距离取 0.3 m，窄处限速。

**101. [L2] How did you test the system before running on the real vehicle?**
> First unit tests for the maths: SE(2) composition, the bicycle model, the step detector. Then replay of recorded bags through localisation. Then simulation of the full stack with the same parameter files and a fixed seed. Only then did I run at 0.3 metres per second on the vehicle with a person holding the emergency stop, and after that 0.5 and 0.8.
> 中文：先对数学做单元测试（SE(2) 组合、自行车模型、阶跃检测）；再回放 bag 测定位；再用同一套参数在仿真里跑全栈（固定随机种子）；最后实车 0.3 m/s，人手持急停，再到 0.5、0.8。

**102. [L3] Your result is 20 out of 22 runs. How would you convince me it is meaningful?**
> I would not claim statistical significance with 22 runs. I would say what the runs were: one environment, mostly static, with fixed start-goal pairs, and what the two failures were: a relocalisation error at a glass door and a time-out in a narrow turn-around. Both are understood and have known mitigations. The honest conclusion is that the system works reliably in that setting, and that I have not yet tested dynamic obstacles or other buildings.
> 中文：22 次不足以谈统计显著；说明条件：单一环境、基本静态、固定起终点对；两次失败原因明确（玻璃门重定位错误、窄口调头超时）并有对策；诚实结论：在该场景下可靠，未测动态障碍和其他建筑。

**103. [L2] What did you do about safety on the real vehicle?**
> Several layers. A hardware emergency stop, a speed cap during early tests, a watchdog in the lower controller that stops the car if no command arrives within 200 milliseconds, validity flags in the vehicle state that reduce the speed when localisation is uncertain, and the MPC input limits as the last software layer.
> 中文：硬件急停；早期测试限速；下位机看门狗（200 ms 无指令即停车）；车辆状态的有效性标志（定位不确定时降速）；MPC 输入限幅作为软件最后一层。

**104. [L3] What would you change if you had to run at 2 m/s?**
> Everything with a delay would matter more. At 2 metres per second, 120 milliseconds is 24 centimetres. I would model the tyre side slip, because the kinematic model breaks down at high lateral acceleration, use a dynamic model in the MPC, raise the control rate and the TEB rate, shrink the sensor latency with hardware timestamps, and extend the planning horizon since stopping distance grows with the square of the speed.
> 中文：所有延迟的影响放大（2 m/s 时 120 ms=24 cm）；需要考虑轮胎侧偏，运动学模型在高侧向加速度下失效，MPC 改用动力学模型；提高控制与 TEB 频率；用硬件时间戳降低传感器延迟；停车距离随速度平方增长，需延长规划时域。

## 三、STAR 案例（用你的真实经历替换细节）

**STAR 1：位姿跳变（对应 CV）**
- Situation：Localisation output jumped, causing steering jerks. 
- Task：Make the vehicle state continuous.
- Action：I logged data, correlated jumps with turning and reversing, found inconsistencies in timing, frames and motion direction, fixed them at the source, and limited how correction reached the controller.
- Result：Pose steps between control cycles dropped from about 0.2 to 0.4 metres to below 0.05 metres, steering jerks disappeared, and we could run full missions.
- 中文：相邻周期阶跃从约 0.2–0.4 m 降到 0.05 m 以下，转向抽动消失，可以跑完整任务。
- 要点：先数据后参数；每一步有证据。

**STAR 2：倒车漂移**
- S：Position drifted during reversing. T：Find the cause. A：Reverse straight test, compared odometry sign with LiDAR motion, corrected the sign handling. R：Reverse distance matched a tape measure within about 5 centimetres, the particle cloud no longer blew up when reversing, and we could use reverse manoeuvres in narrow spaces.（倒车距离与卷尺相差约 5 cm，粒子云不再炸开，窄位可倒车调头。）

**STAR 3：MPC 抖动或滞后**
- S：Tracking was jittery/lagging. T：Smooth it. A：Checked state noise, reference time alignment, delay; tuned change penalty. R：Raising the steering-change weight from 0.5 to 5, compensating the roughly 120 millisecond delay and adding hysteresis to TEB's homotopy choice removed the steering chatter of about 3 degrees, and tracking error fell by about a third.（转角变化权重 0.5 提到 5、补偿约 120 ms 延迟、TEB 选择滞回后，约 3° 的转向抖动消失，误差下降约三分之一。）
