## 二、这份 PS 的主线与可能的追问

### 2.1 PS 主线（引用原句）

MAIR 的 PS 只有约 600 词，但每句都是证据。面试官会把它当目录来翻：「你说的这点，现场讲给我听」。

> "The ballbot I worked on at AIRS had to stay balanced while two arms moved on top of it and shifted its centre of mass continuously."
>
> **中文：** 我在 AIRS 做的球形机器人，必须在上面两条手臂运动、持续改变其质心时保持平衡。

> "…every model, state estimate and control output has to survive real constraints, and the tools I relied on, linear models and hand-set gains, have limits I could not push past without more mathematics, optimisation and learning."
>
> **中文：** ……每个模型、状态估计和控制输出都必须经受真实约束，而我依赖的工具——线性模型和手工设定的增益——有我在缺乏更多数学、优化和学习的情况下无法突破的局限。

> "I have not yet had a formal course in manipulation, optimisation as a design tool or reinforcement learning, and I want to reason about these systems with optimisation and probability."
>
> **中文：** 我还没有上过操作学、作为设计工具的优化或强化学习的正式课程，我想用优化和概率来推理这些系统。

> "Robot Manipulation and Math Fundamentals for Robotics are exactly the foundations my dual-arm work lacked; the core courses in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning will give me a principled basis for the learning-based perception I have so far only deployed…"
>
> **中文：** 机器人操作和机器人数学基础，正是我双臂工作所缺的基础；AI 与机器学习核心课程会为我至今只部署过的学习型感知提供有原则的基础……

> "…the electives in Mobile Robotics, Reinforcement Learning and Optimization map directly onto my navigation and control experience."
>
> **中文：** ……移动机器人、强化学习和优化选修课直接对应我的导航与控制经验。

**推荐信与 CV 如何呼应：** 傅向华老师在 MAIR 版信中写：你的学业知识在 2023 级 CS 同届前 5%，对本项目的适合度在前 10%，并概括为「能从控制理论走到嵌入式实现再回来」；陈淡生工程师写：在其直接指导过的 9 位本科实习生中，你在技术知识与研究生阶段适合度两项均排第 1。这些评价可作为你「为什么觉得自己适合」的外部依据，但面试里请用自己的话说，不要引用排名数字。

### A. 开场与动机

#### Q1. Please introduce yourself and say why AI and robotics.

**中文：** 请介绍自己，并说明为什么选 AI 与机器人。

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> Hello, I am Yongyi Lai, a final-year Computer Science student at Shenzhen Technology University, ranked 6th of 156. At AIRS, I worked on a ballbot that has to stay balanced while two arms move on top of it. I built a PID cascade, and used a linearised model with LQR to understand the tuning. After that, I worked on a wheel-legged robot, a car-like navigation vehicle, and dual-arm teleoperation. I found that the tools I relied on, linear models and hand-set gains, have limits. I want the mathematics, optimisation and learning to go beyond them, and MAIR combines exactly these two sides.
>
> **中文译文：** 您好，我是赖咏怡，深圳技术大学计算机科学大四学生，排名 156 人中第 6。在 AIRS，我做过一台必须在两条手臂运动时保持平衡的球形机器人。我做了 PID 级联，并用线性化模型和 LQR 去理解调参。之后我做了轮腿机器人、车式导航车和双臂遥操作。我发现自己依赖的工具——线性模型和手工增益——存在局限。我想用数学、优化和学习去突破它们，而 MAIR 正好结合这两方面。

> Because my evidence and my gaps are both in robotics. I have built control, navigation and manipulation modules on hardware, but I lack formal courses in manipulation, in mathematics for robotics and in optimisation as a design tool. The Robotics concentration fills those. I still want the AI and machine-learning core, since I have only deployed learning-based perception, and I will take reinforcement learning as an elective. > **中文译文：** 因为我的证据和缺口都在机器人。我在硬件上做过控制、导航和操作模块，但缺少操作学、机器人数学和作为设计工具的优化的正式课程，机器人方向正好补这些。我仍然想要 AI 与机器学习核心课，因为我只部署过学习型感知，并会把强化学习作为选修。所以我把机器人方向看作根基，AI 是下一步的工具。

### B. 围绕 PS 的追问

#### Q6. What exactly are the limits of linear models and hand-set gains that you ran into?

**中文：** 你遇到的线性模型和手工增益的局限具体是什么？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> A linear model is only valid near the operating point. On the ballbot, the linearised four-state model helped me understand the deployed gains, but the real robot has arm disturbance, saturation and friction that the model leaves out. Hand-set gains worked on the trajectories I tested, but I had no systematic way to know how far they could be pushed, or how they would change if the arm configuration changed. On the wheel-legged robot, I had to schedule gains with leg length because one linear model was not enough. Those are the limits that make me want proper nonlinear, optimisation-based and learning-based tools. For example, on a real robot the timing and ground contact never match the model exactly, so a controller that worked on paper still needed tuning on site.
>
> **中文译文：** 线性模型只在工作点附近有效。在球形机器人上，线性化四状态模型帮助我理解已部署的增益，但真机有手臂扰动、饱和和摩擦，模型没有涵盖。手工增益在我测试过的轨迹上有效，但我没有系统方法知道它们能被推到多远，或手臂构型变化时它们会如何变化。在轮腿机器人上，我必须随腿长调度增益，因为一个线性模型不够。这些局限让我想学习正规的非线性、基于优化和基于学习的工具。 例如在真机上，时序和地面接触从来不会与模型完全吻合，纸面上可行的控制器仍然需要现场调参。


#### Q7. Where have you already used optimisation without a formal course?

**中文：** 你在没有正式课程的情况下，已经在哪里用过优化？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> Quite a few places, which is why I want to learn it properly. LQR minimises a quadratic cost. MPC solves a constrained optimisation at every step for vehicle tracking. The inverse kinematics for VR teleoperation was a constrained problem solved with Mink, to keep tracking while limiting velocity near singularities. TEB optimises a trajectory under constraints. In all of them I understood the formulation and used a solver, but I could not say much about convexity, conditions for optimality or why a solver fails. That is the gap. The one I know best is the constrained inverse kinematics in the VR teleoperation: the cost is the end-effector pose error with a damping term, the constraints are joint and velocity limits, and the solver is the QP solver used through the Mink toolchain. I did not write the solver myself.
>
> **中文译文：** 不少地方，这也是我想认真学它的原因。LQR 最小化二次代价；MPC 在车辆跟踪中每步解一个带约束的优化；VR 遥操作的逆运动学是用 Mink 求解的约束问题，用来在奇异点附近限制速度同时保持跟踪；TEB 在约束下优化轨迹。这些我都理解形式并用了求解器，但对凸性、最优性条件、求解器为什么失败说不出太多。这就是缺口。 我最熟悉的是 VR 遥操作里的约束逆运动学：代价是末端位姿误差加阻尼项，约束是关节和速度限制，求解器是通过 Mink 工具链使用的 QP 求解器。求解器不是我写的。


#### Q8. Have you used reinforcement learning on a robot?

**中文：** 你在机器人上用过强化学习吗？

> **参考回答（English）**

Reinforcement learning is something I want to study, and I would not describe it as part of my existing projects. What I can say is why I find it interesting: my controllers rely on models I derived by hand, and learning could help where those models are hard to get, for example contact-rich situations. > **中文译文：** 没有。我目前的机器人控制工作使用的是传统和基于模型的方法：PID、LQR、VMC 和 MPC。强化学习是我想学的方向，我不会把它说成已有项目的一部分。我能说的是我为什么感兴趣：我的控制器依赖手工推导的模型，而学习方法在模型难以获得的场合（比如接触丰富的情形）可能有帮助。我需要先把理论学扎实，再尝试一个简单的仿真任务。


#### Q9. You say your ML experience is practical. What exactly is missing?

**中文：** 你说你的机器学习经验偏实践，具体缺什么？

For example, I can run an inference chain and read a confidence score, but I cannot yet explain in depth why a model's confidence may be miscalibrated, or design a proper evaluation across conditions. I see this as a specific learning goal, and I do not want to overstate my experience.

### C. 项目深挖

#### Q10. Why did you go back to a model and LQR after the PID cascade was already running?

**中文：** PID 级联已经跑起来了，为什么还要回到模型和 LQR？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> Because hand tuning stopped explaining what I saw on the robot. I modelled the ballbot as a linearised four-state inverted pendulum, with position, velocity, tilt and tilt rate, and used LQR to study how the state weights trade stability against control effort. To be clear, the deployed controller was the PID cascade; the LQR analysis was my model reference. It helped me interpret the gains I had deployed, spot abnormal states, and locate actuator saturation instead of just turning gains up and down. The way I used it: when the output sat at its limit, the LQR weights showed that the tuning was asking for more control effort than the actuators could give, so I looked at saturation instead of raising the gains again. I will not quote specific numbers.
>
> **中文译文：** 因为手工调参已经解释不了机器人上看到的现象。我把球形机器人建成线性化的四状态倒立摆（位置、速度、倾角、倾角速度），用 LQR 研究各状态权重如何在稳定性和控制量之间权衡。需要说清楚：实际部署的是 PID 级联，LQR 是我的模型参照。它帮助我解释已部署的增益、发现异常状态、定位执行器饱和，而不是盲目地调大调小增益。 我的用法是：当输出长时间贴着限幅时，LQR 权重的分析说明当前调参要求的控制量超过了执行器能给的，于是我去查饱和，而不是再加大增益。具体数值我不报。


#### Q11. How did you deal with the arms disturbing the balance?

**中文：** 手臂运动会扰动平衡，你是怎么处理的？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> When the arms move, the centre of mass of the whole robot shifts, so the balance point shifts too. I estimated the centre-of-mass offset from the arm joint states using the URDF model and Pinocchio, and fed it into the balance controller as roll and pitch gravity feedforward. To check that it really helped, I compared the balance response with the compensation switched on and off while the arms followed the same fixed trajectories. It showed me how tightly upper-body motion and base balance are coupled. With compensation off, the chassis needed more correction when the arms moved; with it on, the tilt disturbance was smaller. I describe this qualitatively and do not quote numbers.
>
> **中文译文：** 手臂运动时整机质心会移动，平衡点也随之移动。我用 URDF 模型和 Pinocchio 根据手臂关节状态估计质心偏移，并作为 roll、pitch 方向的重力前馈加入平衡控制器。为了验证它确实有效，我在手臂执行同一条固定轨迹时，对比开启和关闭补偿时的平衡响应。这让我体会到上身运动与底盘平衡耦合得有多紧。 关闭补偿时，手臂运动会让底盘需要更多修正；开启补偿后倾角扰动更小。我只定性描述，不报数值。


#### Q12. Why did you use gain-scheduled LQR on the wheel-legged robot?

**中文：** 轮腿机器人为什么用增益调度的 LQR？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> The robot can change its leg length, and the leg length changes the dynamics: the effective pendulum length and inertia are different at different heights. So one fixed set of LQR gains is not a good fit across the whole range. I built the body-pitch and motion states from IMU, wheel-speed and joint feedback, scheduled the LQR gains by leg length, and converted the balance and motion errors into wheel-hub and hip torques. The robot kept its body balanced and tracked velocity commands while the legs extended and retracted. The gains are computed for several leg-length points and interpolated at run time. I do not quote the number of points.
>
> **中文译文：** 机器人可以改变腿长，而腿长会改变动力学：不同高度下等效摆长和转动惯量都不同，所以一组固定的 LQR 增益无法覆盖整个范围。我用 IMU、轮速和关节反馈构造机体俯仰与运动状态，按腿长调度 LQR 增益，把平衡和运动误差转换为轮毂力矩和髋关节力矩。腿伸缩时机器人仍保持机体平衡并跟踪速度指令。 增益在若干个腿长点上求得，运行时做插值。点的数量我不报。


#### Q13. If the vehicle behaved badly, how would you find out which module is responsible?

**中文：** 如果车的行为不对，你怎么判断是哪个模块的责任？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> I would go from the bottom up through the chain. First, is the state estimate continuous and consistent? If the pose jumps, nothing downstream can be trusted. Then, is the global path feasible for the vehicle? Then, does the local trajectory respect the local costmap and give continuous pose and curvature? Finally, is the tracker saturating on steering or speed? In my project, most mysterious problems were upstream, in timing and frames, not in the planner or controller. A case I can describe is a pose that jumped. I compared the timestamps of odometry, IMU and LiDAR, then checked the frames and the motion direction. Once these were consistent the pose became continuous, and I confirmed it by driving again and watching the pose stay smooth.
>
> **中文译文：** 我会沿着链条自下而上排查。第一，状态估计是否连续一致？位姿一跳，下游都不可信。第二，全局路径对车是否可行？第三，局部轨迹是否满足局部代价地图，位姿和曲率是否连续？最后，跟踪器在转向或速度上是否饱和？在我的项目里，大多数“神秘”问题出在上游的时间和坐标系，而不是规划器或控制器。 我能描述的一次是位姿跳变。我先对比里程计、IMU 和激光雷达的时间戳，再检查坐标系和运动方向；这些一致之后位姿变得连续，我再次实车运行并确认位姿保持平滑。


#### Q14. How does your MPC tracker work?

**中文：** 你的 MPC 跟踪控制器是怎么工作的？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> It is based on a car-like kinematic model. At every step, the controller solves a receding-horizon problem: it predicts the vehicle motion over a short horizon, and minimises tracking error to the planned trajectory subject to constraints on position, heading, speed and steering. It applies only the first control, then solves again at the next step. This closed the loop from the planned trajectory to real vehicle motion on the physical car. I chose MPC because steering and speed limits are explicit constraints, not something to patch afterwards. I do not have the exact horizon, sample time or weights in front of me. The trade-offs I can explain: a longer horizon anticipates curves but costs more computation, and the weight on steering change trades tracking accuracy for smoothness. The problem is solved with a QP-type solver.
>
> **中文译文：** 它基于车式运动学模型。每一步求解一个滚动时域问题：在短时域内预测车辆运动，在位置、航向、速度和转向约束下最小化对规划轨迹的跟踪误差；只执行第一个控制量，下一步再重新求解。这样在实车上把规划轨迹到车辆运动的闭环打通了。选 MPC 是因为转向和速度限制是显式约束，而不是事后修补。 具体的预测时域、采样周期和权重我手头没有。我能解释的取舍：时域越长越能预见弯道但计算更重；转向变化的权重在跟踪精度和平滑性之间取舍。问题用 QP 类求解器求解。


#### Q15. How did you stop the arms from jumping near singularities during VR teleoperation?

**中文：** VR 遥操作时，如何避免手臂在奇异点附近突然乱动？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> I mapped the VR controller motion to end-effector targets and solved a constrained inverse kinematics problem with MuJoCo and Mink. Near a singularity, a naive inverse of the Jacobian makes the joint velocity blow up for a small end-effector motion. Solving IK as a constrained optimisation, with limits and regularisation, keeps the end-effector tracking while suppressing that velocity amplification. All of this was validated on real hardware through the ROS2-CAN chain. The constraints are joint position and velocity limits, and the regulariser is a damping term that limits joint velocity near singularities. The controller input is smoothed before use. I do not quote the control rate.
>
> **中文译文：** 我把 VR 手柄的运动映射成末端目标，用 MuJoCo 和 Mink 求解带约束的逆运动学。靠近奇异点时，直接求雅可比的逆会让很小的末端运动对应巨大的关节速度。把 IK 作为带约束和正则的优化问题来解，就能在保持末端跟踪的同时抑制速度放大。整个流程都通过 ROS2-CAN 链路在真机上验证。 约束是关节位置和速度限制，正则项是在奇异点附近限制关节速度的阻尼项，控制器输入在使用前做了平滑。控制频率我不报。


### D. 数学、控制与学习基础

#### Q16. Compare PID, LQR and MPC. When would you use each?

**中文：** 比较 PID、LQR 和 MPC，各自什么时候用？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> PID is simple and model-free in practice, and works well for single-loop problems, but it does not handle coupled multi-state systems or constraints explicitly. LQR assumes a linear model and finds the state-feedback gain that minimises a quadratic cost of state and effort; it handles coupled states naturally and gives a clear trade-off through the weights, but it does not handle constraints directly. MPC optimises over a finite horizon at each step with explicit constraints, at the price of computation. I used PID cascades on the ballbot, gain-scheduled LQR on the wheel-legged robot, and MPC for vehicle tracking, each for a different reason.
>
> **中文译文：** PID 简单，实际中几乎不需要模型，适用于单回路问题，但不能显式处理多状态耦合和约束。LQR 假设线性模型，求出使状态和控制量二次代价最小的状态反馈增益；它自然地处理耦合状态，并通过权重给出清晰的权衡，但不能直接处理约束。MPC 每一步在有限时域内带显式约束做优化，代价是计算量。我在球形机器人上用了 PID 级联，在轮腿机器人上用了增益调度 LQR，在车辆跟踪上用了 MPC，各有不同原因。


#### Q17. What is the Jacobian, and what happens at a singularity?

**中文：** 什么是雅可比矩阵？奇异位形会发生什么？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> The Jacobian maps joint velocities to end-effector velocity: v = J(q) q-dot. By the principle of virtual work, its transpose maps end-effector forces to joint torques, which is what I used in VMC. At a singularity, J loses rank, so some end-effector directions cannot be moved, and the inverse needed in inverse kinematics blows up: a small desired motion asks for huge joint speeds. Remedies include damped least squares, constrained optimisation with joint-velocity limits, and avoiding such configurations. In my dual-arm teleoperation, I solved a constrained IK to suppress velocity amplification near singularities.
>
> **中文译文：** 雅可比矩阵把关节速度映射到末端速度：v = J(q) q̇。根据虚功原理，它的转置把末端力映射为关节力矩，这是我在 VMC 里用到的。在奇异位形处 J 降秩，某些末端方向无法运动，逆运动学需要的逆会发散：很小的期望运动要求巨大的关节速度。补救办法包括阻尼最小二乘、带关节速度限制的约束优化，以及避开这类位形。在双臂遥操作中，我用带约束的 IK 抑制了奇异点附近的速度放大。


#### Q18. What is gravity compensation, and how is it computed?

**中文：** 什么是重力补偿？怎么计算？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> A manipulator's dynamics can be written as M(q) q-ddot + C(q, q-dot) q-dot + g(q) = tau. If we command tau = g(q), the gravity torque is cancelled, and the arm floats: a person can move it with little force. The term g(q) is computed from the robot model: link masses, centres of mass and kinematics, which come from the URDF, using a library such as KDL or Pinocchio. The compensation is only as good as the model, so errors in mass or friction leave residual forces. I used this for the leader arm in teaching, and used the same kind of model to estimate the centre-of-mass shift on the ballbot.
>
> **中文译文：** 机械臂动力学可写成 M(q) q̈ + C(q, q̇) q̇ + g(q) = τ。若令 τ = g(q)，重力力矩被抵消，手臂就会“悬浮”：人只需很小的力就能移动它。g(q) 由机器人模型（连杆质量、质心和运动学，来自 URDF）通过 KDL 或 Pinocchio 等库计算。补偿的效果取决于模型准确度，质量或摩擦误差会留下残余力。我在示教中用它做主臂，并用同类模型估计球形机器人上的质心偏移。


#### Q19. What do the eigenvalues of the matrix A tell you about a linear system? How do you use rank?

**中文：** 矩阵 A 的特征值告诉你线性系统什么？秩有什么用？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> For x-dot = Ax, the eigenvalues of A are the system's poles. If all real parts are negative, the system is asymptotically stable; a positive real part means instability; the imaginary part gives oscillation. With state feedback u = -Kx, the closed-loop matrix is A - BK, so we choose K to move the eigenvalues. Rank matters in two places: the controllability matrix must have full rank for the system to be controllable, and the rank of a manipulator Jacobian drops at a singularity, which means some end-effector directions cannot be moved.
>
> **中文译文：** 对 x' = Ax，A 的特征值就是系统的极点。所有实部为负则渐近稳定；实部为正则不稳定；虚部给出振荡。使用状态反馈 u = −Kx 时，闭环矩阵是 A − BK，所以通过选 K 来移动特征值。秩在两处重要：可控性矩阵满秩系统才可控；机械臂雅可比的秩在奇异点下降，意味着某些末端方向动不了。


#### Q20. What is a convex optimisation problem, and why does convexity matter?

**中文：** 什么是凸优化问题？凸性为什么重要？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> A convex problem minimises a convex function over a convex set. Its key property is that any local minimum is a global minimum, so algorithms like gradient descent or interior-point methods can find the global solution reliably. A quadratic program with a positive semi-definite cost matrix and linear constraints is convex, which is why linear MPC and many IK formulations can be solved quickly and predictably. A non-convex problem, for example a neural-network loss or a nonlinear MPC, may have many local minima, so initialisation and solver choice matter more.
>
> **中文译文：** 凸问题是在凸集上最小化凸函数。其关键性质是任何局部最小都是全局最小，所以梯度下降或内点法可以可靠地找到全局解。代价矩阵半正定、约束线性的二次规划是凸的，这也是线性 MPC 和许多 IK 形式能快速且可预期地求解的原因。非凸问题（如神经网络损失、非线性 MPC）可能有很多局部极小，因此初始化和求解器选择更重要。


#### Q21. Explain reinforcement learning, and why it is hard to apply to real robots.

**中文：** 请解释强化学习，以及为什么难用在真实机器人上。

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> Reinforcement learning models a problem as a Markov decision process: an agent observes a state, takes an action, receives a reward, and the environment moves to a new state. The goal is a policy that maximises expected cumulative reward. Methods include value-based ones such as Q-learning and policy-gradient ones such as PPO. On real robots it is hard because data is expensive and unsafe to collect, rewards are hard to design, and training in simulation creates a sim-to-real gap. Common remedies are domain randomisation, safe exploration, and combining learning with classical controllers. I have not applied RL myself; this is my understanding from study.
>
> **中文译文：** 强化学习把问题建模为马尔可夫决策过程：智能体观察状态、采取动作、获得奖励，环境转移到新状态；目标是得到使期望累计奖励最大的策略。方法包括 Q-learning 这类基于价值的，和 PPO 这类策略梯度的。在真实机器人上之所以难，是因为数据昂贵且采集不安全、奖励难设计、仿真训练存在 sim-to-real 差距。常见补救是域随机化、安全探索，以及把学习与经典控制器结合。我自己没有应用过强化学习，这是我学习得到的理解。


#### Q22. What is overfitting, and how do you detect and reduce it?

**中文：** 什么是过拟合？如何发现并缓解？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> Overfitting means the model fits the noise and quirks of the training data, so it does well on training data and badly on new data. I detect it by comparing training error with validation error: when training error keeps falling and validation error starts rising, the model is overfitting. To reduce it, I can use more or more varied data, data augmentation, regularisation such as weight decay or dropout, a simpler model, or early stopping. The related idea is the bias-variance trade-off: a very simple model underfits (high bias), a very flexible one overfits (high variance).
>
> **中文译文：** 过拟合是指模型拟合了训练数据中的噪声和特殊性，所以在训练集上表现好、在新数据上表现差。我通过比较训练误差和验证误差来发现它：训练误差持续下降而验证误差开始上升，就是过拟合。缓解的办法包括更多或更多样的数据、数据增强、权重衰减或 dropout 等正则化、更简单的模型、提前停止。相关概念是偏差-方差权衡：过于简单的模型欠拟合（高偏差），过于灵活的模型过拟合（高方差）。

## 五、该校特有风险点与对策

MAIR 竞争高（公开画像显示多数来自 C9/985/211/QS 前 200），所以你的差异化要靠「真机证据 + 诚实的缺口」。

| 风险点 | 为什么是风险 | 对策（可直接执行） |
|---|---|---|
| Assessment 形式未知 | 官方不预设为面试 | 口试 + 数理笔试两手准备；邮件一到按通知再聚焦 |
| 数学深度 | PS 自己说缺优化和概率推理 | 每周手推：特征值/稳定性、协方差更新、QP 的 KKT；能在纸上讲 |
| 机器学习只部署过 | PS 已写明 | 不被诱导说训练经验；准备 t_overfit/t_metrics/t_quant 的回答 |
| 强化学习没用过 | PS 列为未来方向 | 学会 MDP、PPO 的基本思路与 sim-to-real，明确没做过 |
| 双非背景 | 公开画像偏 C9/985/211 | 不辩解；用实机证据 + 推荐信中的诊断能力说话 |
| PS 约 600 词，对应不上课程名 | 课程名必须与官网当年一致 | 面试前对照官网逐条核对 Robot Manipulation 等名称 |


## 六、5 道模拟题

第 1、3 题请在白纸上边写边讲，练习「口述 + 推导」。

**模拟题 1.** Show that the closed-loop poles of x-dot = Ax + Bu with u = -Kx are the eigenvalues of A - BK, and explain how LQR picks K.

**中文：** 说明 u = −Kx 时闭环极点是 A − BK 的特征值，并解释 LQR 如何选 K。（要点：代入；Q、R 的物理意义；Riccati；可控性）

**模拟题 2.** A 2-link planar arm is at full extension. What happens to the Jacobian and what would you do in teleoperation?

**中文：** 二连杆平面臂完全伸直，雅可比如何？遥操作中怎么办？（要点：降秩；奇异；阻尼最小二乘/约束优化/限速）

**模拟题 3.** You have a detector with 95 percent accuracy on a balanced test set. Is it ready for a robot?

**中文：** 检测器在平衡测试集上精度 95%，可以上机器人吗？（要点：指标选择、场景、延迟、误检代价、基线）

**模拟题 4.** What is the difference between localisation and SLAM, and which one did you do?

**中文：** 定位和 SLAM 的区别？你做的是哪个？（要点：已知地图 vs 同时建图；EKF/AMCL；诚实）

**模拟题 5.** Why do you want reinforcement learning, and how would you try it safely?

**中文：** 你为什么想学强化学习？如何安全地尝试？（要点：仿真先行、安全约束、与经典控制器结合、没做过）

## 七、本章要点速记

1. 主线：线性模型与手工增益的局限，需要数学、优化和学习去突破。
2. 选 Robotics concentration：证据与缺口都在机器人；AI 是下一步工具。
3. Assessment/exam 形式未知：口试与数理笔试都要准备。
4. 隐含用过优化：LQR、MPC、约束 IK、TEB；但缺凸性与 KKT 的正式理解。
5. 强化学习与训练经验：没有就如实说，不夸大。
6. 课程名只用 PS 与官网都出现的，2027 名单待确认。
7. 外部评价（推荐信）只用来建立信心，不在面试里背数字。
