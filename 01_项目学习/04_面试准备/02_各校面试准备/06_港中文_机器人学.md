### B. 围绕 PS 的追问

#### Q6. You say you want to connect the separate modules into a principled understanding of whole systems. What does 'whole system' mean to you?

**中文：** 你想把分散模块连成对整个系统的有原则的理解。你说的「整个系统」是什么意思？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> For me, a whole robotic system is the chain from sensing and state estimation to planning and control to actuators, with the constraints that couple them. In my navigation robot, a bad pose estimate ruins the planner and the tracker, even though each module is individually correct. On the ballbot, the arm motion changes the centre of mass and so the balance. A principled view would let me say, before building, what each interface must guarantee, such as timing, frames and limits, and how errors propagate. At the moment I find that out by debugging.
>
> **中文译文：** 对我来说，整个机器人系统是从传感、状态估计，到规划、控制，再到执行器的链条，以及耦合它们的约束。在导航机器人上，一个坏的位姿估计会毁掉规划器和跟踪器，尽管每个模块单独看都是对的。在球形机器人上，手臂运动改变质心，进而改变平衡。有原则的视角能让我在建造之前就说出每个接口必须保证什么（时间、坐标系、限制）以及误差如何传播。目前我是靠调试发现这些的。


#### Q7. What does embodied intelligence mean to you?

**中文：** 你如何理解具身智能？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> To me it means intelligence that acts through a physical body. A robot has to connect perception, decision and motion in one loop, while respecting timing, uncertainty and hardware limits. In my projects that loop showed up in different ways: balance control with arm disturbance, navigation from localisation to tracking, and in the Kaihong chain, where an AI decision must pass checks before it becomes physical action. A learning method for a robot is only useful if it works inside that whole loop.
>
> **中文译文：** 对我来说，它指的是通过物理身体行动的智能。机器人必须在一个闭环里把感知、决策和运动连接起来，同时尊重时间、不确定性和硬件限制。在我的项目里，这个闭环以不同方式出现：带手臂扰动的平衡控制、从定位到跟踪的导航，以及深开鸿那条链——AI 决策在变成物理动作之前必须经过检查。机器人上的学习方法，只有在整个闭环内能工作才有用。


#### Q8. Explain reinforcement learning, and why it is hard to apply to real robots.

**中文：** 请解释强化学习，以及为什么难用在真实机器人上。

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> Reinforcement learning models a problem as a Markov decision process: an agent observes a state, takes an action, receives a reward, and the environment moves to a new state. The goal is a policy that maximises expected cumulative reward. Methods include value-based ones such as Q-learning and policy-gradient ones such as PPO. On real robots it is hard because data is expensive and unsafe to collect, rewards are hard to design, and training in simulation creates a sim-to-real gap. Common remedies are domain randomisation, safe exploration, and combining learning with classical controllers. I have not applied RL myself; this is my understanding from study.
>
> **中文译文：** 强化学习把问题建模为马尔可夫决策过程：智能体观察状态、采取动作、获得奖励，环境转移到新状态；目标是得到使期望累计奖励最大的策略。方法包括 Q-learning 这类基于价值的，和 PPO 这类策略梯度的。在真实机器人上之所以难，是因为数据昂贵且采集不安全、奖励难设计、仿真训练存在 sim-to-real 差距。常见补救是域随机化、安全探索，以及把学习与经典控制器结合。我自己没有应用过强化学习，这是我学习得到的理解。


#### Q9. What is the sim-to-real gap, and how can you reduce it?

**中文：** 什么是 sim-to-real 差距？如何缩小？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> A simulator is a simplified model: friction, contact, actuator delay, sensor noise and mass parameters never match the real robot exactly. A policy or controller tuned in simulation can therefore fail on hardware. To reduce the gap, we can identify the real parameters, randomise physical parameters and noise during training so that the policy is robust (domain randomisation), model actuator dynamics and latency, and finally fine-tune or validate on the real robot with safety limits. In my own work, I validated on real hardware each time, which is why I care about this gap.
>
> **中文译文：** 仿真器是简化模型：摩擦、接触、执行器延迟、传感器噪声和质量参数都不可能与真实机器人完全一致，所以在仿真里调好的策略或控制器可能在硬件上失效。缩小差距的办法包括：辨识真实参数；在训练中随机化物理参数和噪声使策略更鲁棒（域随机化）；建模执行器动力学和延迟；最后在真机上带安全限制地微调或验证。我自己的工作每次都在真机上验证，所以很在意这个差距。


### C. 项目深挖

#### Q10. Walk me through the balance controller you built on the ballbot.

**中文：** 请讲讲你在球形机器人（ballbot）上做的平衡控制器。

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> The robot is a three-omni-wheel ballbot with a dual-arm upper body, so the base is dynamically unstable and the arms keep moving its centre of mass. I built the control state from IMU and wheel-speed feedback. The controller is a cascade: a position outer loop produces a bounded attitude reference, and the attitude inner loop tracks it. The resulting action is then allocated to the three wheels through the wheel kinematics. I deployed it on the real robot and tuned it there, and in the end the chassis kept dynamic balance while both arms followed fixed trajectories. I do not have the exact loop rates or gains in front of me. The structure I can describe: the position outer loop outputs a bounded tilt reference, the faster attitude inner loop tracks it, and the state is built from IMU tilt and rate plus wheel speed. I tuned the inner loop first and the outer loop after.
>
> **中文译文：** 这台机器人是带双臂上身的三全向轮球形机器人，底盘本身动态不稳定，手臂运动还会不断移动质心。我用 IMU 和轮速反馈构造控制状态。控制器是级联结构：位置外环给出有界的姿态参考，姿态内环去跟踪它，输出再通过轮子运动学分配到三个轮子。我在真机上部署并调参，最终在双臂按固定轨迹运动时，底盘保持了动态平衡。 具体的回路频率和增益我手头没有。我能讲清结构：位置外环输出有界的倾角参考，更快的姿态内环去跟踪它，状态由 IMU 的倾角与角速度加轮速构成。调参时先内环，后外环。


#### Q11. Why did you go back to a model and LQR after the PID cascade was already running?

**中文：** PID 级联已经跑起来了，为什么还要回到模型和 LQR？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> Because hand tuning stopped explaining what I saw on the robot. I modelled the ballbot as a linearised four-state inverted pendulum, with position, velocity, tilt and tilt rate, and used LQR to study how the state weights trade stability against control effort. To be clear, the deployed controller was the PID cascade; the LQR analysis was my model reference. It helped me interpret the gains I had deployed, spot abnormal states, and locate actuator saturation instead of just turning gains up and down. The way I used it: when the output sat at its limit, the LQR weights showed that the tuning was asking for more control effort than the actuators could give, so I looked at saturation instead of raising the gains again. I will not quote specific numbers.
>
> **中文译文：** 因为手工调参已经解释不了机器人上看到的现象。我把球形机器人建成线性化的四状态倒立摆（位置、速度、倾角、倾角速度），用 LQR 研究各状态权重如何在稳定性和控制量之间权衡。需要说清楚：实际部署的是 PID 级联，LQR 是我的模型参照。它帮助我解释已部署的增益、发现异常状态、定位执行器饱和，而不是盲目地调大调小增益。 我的用法是：当输出长时间贴着限幅时，LQR 权重的分析说明当前调参要求的控制量超过了执行器能给的，于是我去查饱和，而不是再加大增益。具体数值我不报。


#### Q12. Why did you use gain-scheduled LQR on the wheel-legged robot?

**中文：** 轮腿机器人为什么用增益调度的 LQR？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> The robot can change its leg length, and the leg length changes the dynamics: the effective pendulum length and inertia are different at different heights. So one fixed set of LQR gains is not a good fit across the whole range. I built the body-pitch and motion states from IMU, wheel-speed and joint feedback, scheduled the LQR gains by leg length, and converted the balance and motion errors into wheel-hub and hip torques. The robot kept its body balanced and tracked velocity commands while the legs extended and retracted. The gains are computed for several leg-length points and interpolated at run time. I do not quote the number of points.
>
> **中文译文：** 机器人可以改变腿长，而腿长会改变动力学：不同高度下等效摆长和转动惯量都不同，所以一组固定的 LQR 增益无法覆盖整个范围。我用 IMU、轮速和关节反馈构造机体俯仰与运动状态，按腿长调度 LQR 增益，把平衡和运动误差转换为轮毂力矩和髋关节力矩。腿伸缩时机器人仍保持机体平衡并跟踪速度指令。 增益在若干个腿长点上求得，运行时做插值。点的数量我不报。


#### Q13. What is virtual model control, and how did you use it?

**中文：** 什么是虚拟模型控制（VMC）？你怎么用它？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> Virtual model control lets you think in task space instead of joint space. From the closed-chain leg kinematics I computed a virtual leg length and posture. Then I specified the desired support force and pitch torque along that virtual leg, and mapped them to the joint torques through the Jacobian. I coordinated leg extension with a leg-length PID plus force feedforward. This way the balance controller can ask for a force on the virtual leg, without caring how the real joints produce it. The Jacobian maps the virtual leg force to joint torques through the closed-chain leg geometry, tau equals J transpose times F. The feedforward force is mainly the share of body weight that the leg must support.
>
> **中文译文：** VMC 让你在任务空间而不是关节空间思考。我先由闭链腿运动学算出虚拟腿长和姿态，再沿这条虚拟腿给出期望的支撑力和俯仰力矩，通过雅可比映射到关节力矩。腿的伸缩由腿长 PID 加力前馈协调。这样平衡控制器只需要对虚拟腿提出受力要求，不必关心真实关节如何实现。 雅可比通过闭链腿的几何关系把虚拟腿上的力映射为关节力矩，即 tau = J 转置乘 F。前馈力主要是这条腿需要承担的那部分体重。


#### Q14. How did you build the localisation for the car-like robot, and what went wrong?

**中文：** 车式机器人的定位是怎么做的？遇到过什么问题？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> I fused IMU, wheel encoders and 2D LiDAR for map-based localisation, using an EKF together with AMCL, and the output was a continuous vehicle state. The real problem was drift and pose jumps. They came from inconsistent timing, coordinate frames and motion direction between the sensors. So I spent a lot of effort aligning timestamps and frames and checking the sign of the motion direction, before touching any filter parameter. The lesson was that a fancy estimator cannot repair inconsistent inputs. The EKF state typically holds position, heading and velocity. It fuses wheel odometry and IMU between corrections, and AMCL provides a map-based pose that corrects the drift. I found the direction problem by comparing the sign of the wheel-encoder speed with the actual direction of motion.
>
> **中文译文：** 我融合 IMU、轮式编码器和二维激光雷达做基于地图的定位，使用 EKF 加 AMCL，输出连续的车辆状态。真正的问题是漂移和位姿跳变，原因是各传感器之间时间、坐标系和运动方向不一致。所以我在动滤波参数之前，先花大量精力对齐时间戳和坐标系，检查运动方向的符号。教训是：再好的估计器也修不了不一致的输入。 EKF 的状态通常包含位置、航向和速度；它在两次修正之间融合轮式里程计和 IMU，AMCL 提供基于地图的位姿来修正漂移。我是通过对比轮速编码器的符号与实际运动方向发现方向问题的。


#### Q15. Why Hybrid-A* instead of plain A* for the global path?

**中文：** 全局路径为什么用 Hybrid-A* 而不是普通 A*？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> A car-like vehicle cannot turn on the spot or move sideways, so a path on a plain grid may be impossible to drive. Hybrid-A* searches over continuous vehicle states, including heading, and expands nodes with motion primitives that respect the kinematic model. In my project it generated feasible global paths under the vehicle footprint, the minimum turning radius and obstacle cost. The price is a more expensive search than grid A*. The standard design uses a heuristic that combines a Euclidean distance with a non-holonomic cost, discretises the grid and the heading, and adds an analytic expansion such as Reeds-Shepp near the goal. I can explain this design; I do not have my exact resolutions.
>
> **中文译文：** 车式机器人不能原地转向，也不能横移，所以普通栅格上的路径可能根本开不出来。Hybrid-A* 在包含航向的连续状态上搜索，用符合运动学模型的运动基元扩展节点。在我的项目里，它能在车身轮廓、最小转弯半径和障碍代价的约束下生成可行的全局路径。代价是搜索比栅格 A* 更贵。 标准设计用结合欧氏距离与非完整约束代价的启发函数，对栅格和航向离散化，并在靠近目标处加入 Reeds-Shepp 这样的解析扩展。我能讲清这个设计；我自己的具体分辨率手头没有。


#### Q16. What does TEB do, and how did it fit with the global planner?

**中文：** TEB 做什么？它和全局规划器怎么配合？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> TEB, the timed elastic band, optimises a time-parameterised local trajectory. It starts from the global path and deforms it under the local costmap and kinematic constraints, so when local obstacles change, the vehicle gets an updated trajectory. In my system it output continuous pose and curvature references to the controller. So the global planner gives the rough route, TEB gives something locally smooth and feasible, and MPC tracks it. The TEB parameters that matter are obstacle clearance, maximum velocity and acceleration, and minimum turning radius. More clearance gives safer but longer paths; lower limits give smoother but slower motion.
>
> **中文译文：** TEB（timed elastic band）优化的是带时间参数的局部轨迹。它以全局路径为起点，在局部代价地图和运动学约束下对路径变形，局部障碍变化时车辆就得到更新的轨迹。在我的系统中，它向控制器输出连续的位姿和曲率参考。所以全局规划给出大致路线，TEB 给出局部平滑可行的轨迹，MPC 负责跟踪。 影响最大的 TEB 参数是障碍物间隔、最大速度与加速度、最小转弯半径。间隔越大路径越安全但越长；限值越低运动越平滑但越慢。


### D. 机器人学核心概念

#### Q17. Explain PID and integral windup. How did you deal with saturation?

**中文：** 解释 PID 和积分饱和（windup）。你如何处理饱和？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> PID computes the control from the present error (P), the accumulated error (I) and the rate of change of error (D). P gives the main response, I removes steady-state error, D adds damping. Windup happens when the actuator saturates: the error persists, the integral keeps growing, and when the system comes back, it overshoots badly. Typical remedies are clamping the integral, conditional integration, or back-calculation anti-windup, plus resetting the integrator at re-enable. In my balance projects, I used output limiting and state initialisation for similar reasons. I used output limiting and clamping of the integral term while the output is saturated; I do not claim a more elaborate back-calculation scheme.
>
> **中文译文：** PID 根据当前误差（P）、累积误差（I）和误差变化率（D）计算控制量。P 提供主要响应，I 消除稳态误差，D 增加阻尼。执行器饱和时会出现积分饱和：误差持续存在，积分不断增大，系统恢复时就会严重超调。典型办法是积分限幅、条件积分或反算抗饱和，并在重新使能时复位积分器。在我的平衡项目里，基于类似原因使用了输出限幅和状态初始化。 我用的是输出限幅，以及输出饱和时钳住积分项；我不声称用过更复杂的反算抗饱和方案。


#### Q18. Why EKF instead of a plain Kalman filter, and what are its weaknesses?

**中文：** 为什么用 EKF 而不是普通卡尔曼滤波？它有什么弱点？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> A plain Kalman filter needs linear models. Vehicle motion and many measurements are nonlinear, since the heading enters through sine and cosine. The EKF linearises the motion and measurement models around the current estimate using Jacobians. Its weaknesses: the linearisation can be poor under large uncertainty or strong nonlinearity, the Gaussian assumption can be wrong, it needs reasonable noise covariances, and it is sensitive to inconsistent inputs, such as timestamps or frames that do not match, which I experienced in the navigation project.
>
> **中文译文：** 普通卡尔曼滤波要求线性模型。车辆运动和许多测量是非线性的，因为航向通过正弦和余弦进入。EKF 利用雅可比矩阵，在当前估计点附近线性化运动模型和测量模型。弱点：不确定性大或非线性强时线性化可能很差；高斯假设可能不成立；需要合理的噪声协方差；对不一致的输入（如时间戳或坐标系不匹配）非常敏感——这正是我在导航项目中遇到的。


#### Q19. What is the Jacobian, and what happens at a singularity?

**中文：** 什么是雅可比矩阵？奇异位形会发生什么？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> The Jacobian maps joint velocities to end-effector velocity: v = J(q) q-dot. By the principle of virtual work, its transpose maps end-effector forces to joint torques, which is what I used in VMC. At a singularity, J loses rank, so some end-effector directions cannot be moved, and the inverse needed in inverse kinematics blows up: a small desired motion asks for huge joint speeds. Remedies include damped least squares, constrained optimisation with joint-velocity limits, and avoiding such configurations. In my dual-arm teleoperation, I solved a constrained IK to suppress velocity amplification near singularities.
>
> **中文译文：** 雅可比矩阵把关节速度映射到末端速度：v = J(q) q̇。根据虚功原理，它的转置把末端力映射为关节力矩，这是我在 VMC 里用到的。在奇异位形处 J 降秩，某些末端方向无法运动，逆运动学需要的逆会发散：很小的期望运动要求巨大的关节速度。补救办法包括阻尼最小二乘、带关节速度限制的约束优化，以及避开这类位形。在双臂遥操作中，我用带约束的 IK 抑制了奇异点附近的速度放大。


#### Q20. Compare A*, Hybrid-A*, RRT* and TEB. What does each solve?

**中文：** 比较 A*、Hybrid-A*、RRT* 和 TEB，各解决什么问题？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> A* finds a shortest path on a discrete graph with an admissible heuristic, but ignores vehicle kinematics. Hybrid-A* keeps a continuous state, including heading, inside each grid cell, and expands with kinematically feasible motions, so the path is drivable by a car-like vehicle. RRT* is sampling-based: it works well in high-dimensional spaces and is asymptotically optimal, but paths can be irregular and need smoothing. TEB is a local optimiser: it deforms a path as a time-parameterised trajectory under costmap and dynamic constraints. In my chain, Hybrid-A* gave the global path, TEB the local trajectory, and MPC tracked it.
>
> **中文译文：** A* 在离散图上用可采纳启发函数求最短路径，但忽略车辆运动学。Hybrid-A* 在每个栅格内保留包含航向的连续状态，并用运动学可行的动作扩展，因此路径对车式车辆是可行驶的。RRT* 基于采样，适合高维空间并渐近最优，但路径可能不规则，需要平滑。TEB 是局部优化器：在代价地图和动力学约束下把路径变形为带时间参数的轨迹。在我的链条里，Hybrid-A* 给出全局路径，TEB 给出局部轨迹，MPC 负责跟踪。


#### Q21. Why do coordinate frames and timestamps matter so much in robotics?

**中文：** 为什么坐标系和时间戳在机器人里这么重要？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> Every measurement is expressed in some frame and taken at some time. If I fuse a LiDAR scan with odometry from a different time, or treat a pose in one frame as if it were in another, the result is wrong in a way no filter can fix; it appears as drift, jumps or oscillation. So a good system fixes a frame convention, such as map, odom and base frames, uses a consistent clock, interpolates or buffers to match times, and checks validity. This was the common thread of my navigation problems and of the state-management work at Kaihong.
>
> **中文译文：** 每个测量都在某个坐标系下、某个时刻取得。如果我把激光雷达扫描与另一时刻的里程计融合，或把一个坐标系的位姿当作另一个坐标系的位姿，得到的结果是任何滤波器都修不好的错误，表现为漂移、跳变或振荡。所以好的系统要固定坐标系约定（如 map、odom、base），使用一致的时钟，通过插值或缓冲匹配时间，并检查有效性。这是我导航问题和深开鸿状态管理工作的共同主线。


#### Q22. Derive the differential-drive kinematics.

**中文：** 推导差速驱动的运动学。

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> Let the wheel radius be r, the track width L, and the left and right wheel angular speeds omega_L and omega_R. The wheel linear speeds are v_L = r*omega_L and v_R = r*omega_R. The body linear speed is v = (v_R + v_L)/2 and the yaw rate is omega = (v_R - v_L)/L. Inversely, given v and omega, the wheel targets are v_R = v + omega*L/2 and v_L = v - omega*L/2. Odometry integrates this: x-dot = v*cos(theta), y-dot = v*sin(theta), theta-dot = omega. These are textbook formulas; the real values of r and L need calibration.
>
> **中文译文：** 设轮半径为 r，轮距为 L，左右轮角速度为 ω_L 和 ω_R。轮的线速度为 v_L = r·ω_L，v_R = r·ω_R。车体线速度 v = (v_R + v_L)/2，横摆角速度 ω = (v_R − v_L)/L。反过来，给定 v 和 ω，轮目标为 v_R = v + ω·L/2，v_L = v − ω·L/2。里程计对此积分：ẋ = v·cosθ，ẏ = v·sinθ，θ̇ = ω。这些是教科书公式，r 和 L 的真实值需要标定。

## 六、5 道模拟题

第 2、3 题建议拿纸笔画图再讲，练习「边画边说」。

**模拟题 1.** Describe the transition from your model to the real robot on one project, and what changed.

**中文：** 描述某个项目从模型到真机的过渡，以及发生了什么变化。（要点：P1 饱和/摩擦/手臂扰动，P7 着地切换，P5 时间不一致；不编数字）

**模拟题 2.** Draw the chain from sensors to actuators in your navigation robot and mark where an error would hurt most.

**中文：** 画出导航机器人从传感器到执行器的链条，标出误差伤害最大的位置。（要点：状态估计→规划→跟踪；上游错误下游放大；约束）

**模拟题 3.** Derive the forward kinematics and Jacobian of a 2-link planar arm.

**中文：** 推导二连杆平面臂的正运动学与雅可比。（要点：x,y 表达式；偏导；det J = l1 l2 sin(q2)；奇异点）

**模拟题 4.** Why can a controller that is stable in simulation fail on the real robot?

**中文：** 为什么仿真里稳定的控制器在真机上可能失效？（要点：未建模动态、延迟、饱和、传感噪声、接触；sim-to-real）

**模拟题 5.** What would your first project in this programme look like?

**中文：** 你在项目里的第一个项目会是什么样？（要点：小而明确：平台、指标、基线、时间表）

## 七、本章要点速记

1. 主线：平衡 → 足式 → 导航 → 操作；想把分散模块连成有原则的系统理解。
2. 核心项目：P7、P5、P1；会讲「模型到真机时发生了什么变化」。
3. 实验方法是你的缺口：指标先行、基线比较、如实报告失败。
4. 机器人学习与医学机器人：诚实说没做过。
5. 学费与日期有不一致，以系统和 offer 为准。
6. 一年制：早规划、早启动实践项目。
7. 官方没规定面试：按技术面谈准备，邮件为准。

## 八、自测题

1. 二连杆臂的奇异点在哪？
   - **要点：** q2 = 0 或 π（完全伸直 / 折叠），det J = l1 l2 sin q2 = 0
2. PS 里「分散模块」指什么？
   - **要点：** 平衡、运动、导航、操作各自独立实现的模块
3. 官方对 CUHK Robotics 面试怎么说？
   - **要点：** 公开页面未规定所有人必面；可根据情况要求进一步评估
4. 学费口径有什么不一致？
   - **要点：** HK$354,200（核对版）与 HK$308,000（旧表）；以官网为准
5. 你有机器人学习经验吗？
   - **要点：** 没有；是想学的方向
