## 二、这份 PS 的主线与可能的追问

### 2.1 PS 主线（引用原句）

UM RAS 的 PS 与港理工、CUHK 共享前三段，第四段改为「广度整合」：你的项目覆盖了平衡、足式、定位、规划、跟踪、操作与边缘 AI，需要「共同原理」。面试官最可能追问这一段。

> "My projects have covered balance control, legged locomotion, localisation, planning, trajectory tracking, manipulation and edge AI, but these experiences were developed on different platforms."
>
> **中文：** 我的项目覆盖了平衡控制、足式运动、定位、规划、轨迹跟踪、操作和边缘 AI，但这些经历是在不同平台上形成的。

> "A programme that brings together machine vision, sensor fusion, robot pose estimation, SLAM, nonlinear motion estimation and control, and trajectory planning would help me understand the common principles behind these systems."
>
> **中文：** 一个将机器视觉、传感器融合、机器人位姿估计、SLAM、非线性运动估计与控制、轨迹规划结合起来的项目，会帮助我理解这些系统背后的共同原理。

> "I am particularly interested in the connection between autonomous perception and control: a planner is only useful when the state estimate is trustworthy and the controller can execute the resulting trajectory under physical constraints."
>
> **中文：** 我特别感兴趣的是自主感知与控制之间的联系：只有当状态估计可信、且控制器能在物理约束下执行所得轨迹时，规划器才有用。

> "…while using the programme to strengthen my mathematical modelling, sensor-fusion and autonomous-system design skills."
>
> **中文：** ……同时利用该项目加强我的数学建模、传感器融合和自主系统设计能力。

**推荐信与 CV 如何呼应：** 傅向华老师的信写：导航项目中你负责 EKF/AMCL 定位（含处理导致位姿跳变的时间与坐标系不一致）、Hybrid-A*、TEB 与 MPC，并「在本科阶段很少见到学生承担从定位到规划到控制并跑通整车」。这是 UM 版最重要的外部证据，你要对 P5 的每一环都能讲。

#### Q3. What are the common principles you hope to find across your platforms?

**中文：** 你希望在各平台中找到什么共同原理？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> I already see three. First, every system is a loop of estimate, decide and act, and the loop is only as good as its weakest link: consistent timing and frames for estimation, feasible references for planning, and actuator limits for control. Second, models are valid in a region, so we need linearisation, scheduling or constraints to stay inside it. Third, validation on hardware changes the problem: friction, delay, saturation and contact are real. I want the mathematics that makes these principles explicit instead of lessons I learned case by case.
>
> **中文译文：** 我已经看到三条。第一，每个系统都是「估计、决策、执行」的闭环，闭环的好坏取决于最薄弱的一环：估计需要一致的时间和坐标系，规划需要可行的参考，控制需要考虑执行器限制。第二，模型只在一个区域内有效，所以需要线性化、调度或约束来留在其中。第三，硬件验证会改变问题：摩擦、延迟、饱和和接触都是真实的。我想要能把这些原则变得明确的数学，而不是一个个案例学到的教训。

### B. 感知—估计—规划—控制

#### Q5. If the vehicle behaved badly, how would you find out which module is responsible?

**中文：** 如果车的行为不对，你怎么判断是哪个模块的责任？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> I would go from the bottom up through the chain. First, is the state estimate continuous and consistent? If the pose jumps, nothing downstream can be trusted. Then, is the global path feasible for the vehicle? Then, does the local trajectory respect the local costmap and give continuous pose and curvature? Finally, is the tracker saturating on steering or speed? In my project, most mysterious problems were upstream, in timing and frames, not in the planner or controller. 【待你补全：一次真实的排查过程】
>
> **中文译文：** 我会沿着链条自下而上排查。第一，状态估计是否连续一致？位姿一跳，下游都不可信。第二，全局路径对车是否可行？第三，局部轨迹是否满足局部代价地图，位姿和曲率是否连续？最后，跟踪器在转向或速度上是否饱和？在我的项目里，大多数“神秘”问题出在上游的时间和坐标系，而不是规划器或控制器。


#### Q6. Which of your systems are nonlinear, and how did you handle that?

**中文：** 你的系统中哪些是非线性的？你怎么处理？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> The ballbot and the wheel-legged robot are inverted-pendulum-like, so I linearised around the upright position and kept the tilt small, which is why I bounded the attitude reference. For the wheel-legged robot, the linearised dynamics change with leg length, so I scheduled the LQR gains. The car-like vehicle has nonlinear kinematics through its heading, and the MPC works from a car-like kinematic model with constraints. The launcher friction wheel faced varying load, and I moved from PID to variable-gain PID and sliding-mode control. I have not formally studied nonlinear control such as feedback linearisation or Lyapunov design, which I would like to learn here. 【待你补全：MPC 是否对运动学模型线性化（如围绕参考轨迹）；没有确认就说“我可以说明思路”】
>
> **中文译文：** 球形机器人和轮腿机器人类似倒立摆，所以我在直立位置附近线性化，并保持倾角较小，这也是我限制姿态参考的原因。轮腿机器人的线性化动力学随腿长变化，所以我调度 LQR 增益。车式车辆的运动学通过航向是非线性的，MPC 基于带约束的车式运动学模型。发射机构的摩擦轮面临变化的负载，我从 PID 迁移到变增益 PID 和滑模控制。我没有正式学过反馈线性化或李雅普诺夫设计等非线性控制，希望在这里学到。


#### Q7. Tell me about the edge-vision part of your gardening system.

**中文：** 讲讲园艺系统里的边缘视觉部分。

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> I built a YOLOv5 inference chain on the RK3588, quantised to INT8 with RKNN. It converts camera frames into structured observations, each with a confidence and a timestamp, so that the decision layer does not receive raw images but something it can reason about. My contribution was the deployment and integration of the chain. 【待你补全：模型是现成权重还是你训练的、数据集规模与类别、量化前后精度/速度差异——没有实测数据就如实说“没有系统测过”】
>
> **中文译文：** 我在 RK3588 上搭建了 YOLOv5 推理链，并用 RKNN 做 INT8 量化。它把摄像头帧转换成带置信度和时间戳的结构化观测，让决策层拿到的不是原始图像，而是可以推理的信息。我的工作是这条链的部署与集成。


#### Q8. What is the difference between localisation and SLAM? Which did you do?

**中文：** 定位和 SLAM 有什么区别？你做的是哪个？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> Localisation estimates the robot pose in a known map. SLAM estimates the map and the pose at the same time, which is harder because errors in the pose corrupt the map and vice versa; it needs loop closure to correct accumulated drift. In my car-like robot project, I did map-based localisation with EKF and AMCL on a given map, not full SLAM. I understand the SLAM idea, for example a pose graph or filter-based approach, but I have not implemented one, and I would like to study it more deeply.
>
> **中文译文：** 定位是在已知地图中估计机器人位姿。SLAM 同时估计地图和位姿，更难，因为位姿误差会污染地图，反之亦然；还需要回环检测来修正累积漂移。在车式机器人项目中，我是在给定地图上用 EKF 和 AMCL 做基于地图的定位，不是完整的 SLAM。我理解 SLAM 的思想（如位姿图或基于滤波的方法），但没有实现过，希望更深入地学习。


#### Q9. How do you estimate attitude from an IMU?

**中文：** 你怎么从 IMU 估计姿态？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> A gyroscope gives angular rate, which can be integrated to get attitude, but the integration drifts because of bias and noise. An accelerometer measures gravity direction, which gives roll and pitch without drift, but it is noisy and affected by linear acceleration. A complementary filter combines them: trust the gyro at high frequency and the accelerometer at low frequency. The Mahony filter does this with a proportional-integral correction on the error between the estimated and measured gravity direction, which also estimates gyro bias. I used this kind of AHRS estimate in my robot projects. Yaw needs a magnetometer or another reference. 【待你补全：在哪个项目、什么库/自写、你调了哪些增益】
>
> **中文译文：** 陀螺仪给出角速度，积分可得到姿态，但积分会因偏置和噪声而漂移。加速度计测量重力方向，可得 roll 和 pitch 且无漂移，但噪声大并受线加速度影响。互补滤波把两者结合：高频信任陀螺，低频信任加速度计。Mahony 滤波用估计与测量重力方向之间误差的比例-积分修正来实现，同时估计陀螺偏置。我在机器人项目中使用过这类 AHRS 估计。偏航需要磁力计或其它参考。


### C. 项目深挖

#### Q10. How did you build the localisation for the car-like robot, and what went wrong?

**中文：** 车式机器人的定位是怎么做的？遇到过什么问题？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> I fused IMU, wheel encoders and 2D LiDAR for map-based localisation, using an EKF together with AMCL, and the output was a continuous vehicle state. The real problem was drift and pose jumps. They came from inconsistent timing, coordinate frames and motion direction between the sensors. So I spent a lot of effort aligning timestamps and frames and checking the sign of the motion direction, before touching any filter parameter. The lesson was that a fancy estimator cannot repair inconsistent inputs. 【待你补全：EKF 的状态向量、AMCL 与 EKF 如何衔接（谁修正谁）、你怎么发现“运动方向不一致”】
>
> **中文译文：** 我融合 IMU、轮式编码器和二维激光雷达做基于地图的定位，使用 EKF 加 AMCL，输出连续的车辆状态。真正的问题是漂移和位姿跳变，原因是各传感器之间时间、坐标系和运动方向不一致。所以我在动滤波参数之前，先花大量精力对齐时间戳和坐标系，检查运动方向的符号。教训是：再好的估计器也修不了不一致的输入。


#### Q11. Why Hybrid-A* instead of plain A* for the global path?

**中文：** 全局路径为什么用 Hybrid-A* 而不是普通 A*？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> A car-like vehicle cannot turn on the spot or move sideways, so a path on a plain grid may be impossible to drive. Hybrid-A* searches over continuous vehicle states, including heading, and expands nodes with motion primitives that respect the kinematic model. In my project it generated feasible global paths under the vehicle footprint, the minimum turning radius and obstacle cost. The price is a more expensive search than grid A*. 【待你补全：启发函数的具体选择、栅格/航向离散分辨率、是否使用解析扩展（Reeds-Shepp）】
>
> **中文译文：** 车式机器人不能原地转向，也不能横移，所以普通栅格上的路径可能根本开不出来。Hybrid-A* 在包含航向的连续状态上搜索，用符合运动学模型的运动基元扩展节点。在我的项目里，它能在车身轮廓、最小转弯半径和障碍代价的约束下生成可行的全局路径。代价是搜索比栅格 A* 更贵。


#### Q12. How does your MPC tracker work?

**中文：** 你的 MPC 跟踪控制器是怎么工作的？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> It is based on a car-like kinematic model. At every step, the controller solves a receding-horizon problem: it predicts the vehicle motion over a short horizon, and minimises tracking error to the planned trajectory subject to constraints on position, heading, speed and steering. It applies only the first control, then solves again at the next step. This closed the loop from the planned trajectory to real vehicle motion on the physical car. I chose MPC because steering and speed limits are explicit constraints, not something to patch afterwards. 【待你补全：预测时域、控制周期、求解器（如 OSQP/qpOASES/ipopt）、代价权重的取舍】
>
> **中文译文：** 它基于车式运动学模型。每一步求解一个滚动时域问题：在短时域内预测车辆运动，在位置、航向、速度和转向约束下最小化对规划轨迹的跟踪误差；只执行第一个控制量，下一步再重新求解。这样在实车上把规划轨迹到车辆运动的闭环打通了。选 MPC 是因为转向和速度限制是显式约束，而不是事后修补。


#### Q13. Why did you go back to a model and LQR after the PID cascade was already running?

**中文：** PID 级联已经跑起来了，为什么还要回到模型和 LQR？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> Because hand tuning stopped explaining what I saw on the robot. I modelled the ballbot as a linearised four-state inverted pendulum, with position, velocity, tilt and tilt rate, and used LQR to study how the state weights trade stability against control effort. To be clear, the deployed controller was the PID cascade; the LQR analysis was my model reference. It helped me interpret the gains I had deployed, spot abnormal states, and locate actuator saturation instead of just turning gains up and down. 【待你补全：一个具体的“LQR 分析帮你定位问题”的例子，比如某次饱和或抖动】
>
> **中文译文：** 因为手工调参已经解释不了机器人上看到的现象。我把球形机器人建成线性化的四状态倒立摆（位置、速度、倾角、倾角速度），用 LQR 研究各状态权重如何在稳定性和控制量之间权衡。需要说清楚：实际部署的是 PID 级联，LQR 是我的模型参照。它帮助我解释已部署的增益、发现异常状态、定位执行器饱和，而不是盲目地调大调小增益。


#### Q14. Why did you use gain-scheduled LQR on the wheel-legged robot?

**中文：** 轮腿机器人为什么用增益调度的 LQR？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> The robot can change its leg length, and the leg length changes the dynamics: the effective pendulum length and inertia are different at different heights. So one fixed set of LQR gains is not a good fit across the whole range. I built the body-pitch and motion states from IMU, wheel-speed and joint feedback, scheduled the LQR gains by leg length, and converted the balance and motion errors into wheel-hub and hip torques. The robot kept its body balanced and tracked velocity commands while the legs extended and retracted. 【待你补全：增益表怎么得到（离线求解几个腿长点？插值方式？）】
>
> **中文译文：** 机器人可以改变腿长，而腿长会改变动力学：不同高度下等效摆长和转动惯量都不同，所以一组固定的 LQR 增益无法覆盖整个范围。我用 IMU、轮速和关节反馈构造机体俯仰与运动状态，按腿长调度 LQR 增益，把平衡和运动误差转换为轮毂力矩和髋关节力矩。腿伸缩时机器人仍保持机体平衡并跟踪速度指令。


#### Q15. How did you stop the arms from jumping near singularities during VR teleoperation?

**中文：** VR 遥操作时，如何避免手臂在奇异点附近突然乱动？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> I mapped the VR controller motion to end-effector targets and solved a constrained inverse kinematics problem with MuJoCo and Mink. Near a singularity, a naive inverse of the Jacobian makes the joint velocity blow up for a small end-effector motion. Solving IK as a constrained optimisation, with limits and regularisation, keeps the end-effector tracking while suppressing that velocity amplification. All of this was validated on real hardware through the ROS2-CAN chain. 【待你补全：约束与正则项的具体设置、控制频率、手柄抖动的滤波】
>
> **中文译文：** 我把 VR 手柄的运动映射成末端目标，用 MuJoCo 和 Mink 求解带约束的逆运动学。靠近奇异点时，直接求雅可比的逆会让很小的末端运动对应巨大的关节速度。把 IK 作为带约束和正则的优化问题来解，就能在保持末端跟踪的同时抑制速度放大。整个流程都通过 ROS2-CAN 链路在真机上验证。


### D. 概念与基础

#### Q16. Why EKF instead of a plain Kalman filter, and what are its weaknesses?

**中文：** 为什么用 EKF 而不是普通卡尔曼滤波？它有什么弱点？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> A plain Kalman filter needs linear models. Vehicle motion and many measurements are nonlinear, since the heading enters through sine and cosine. The EKF linearises the motion and measurement models around the current estimate using Jacobians. Its weaknesses: the linearisation can be poor under large uncertainty or strong nonlinearity, the Gaussian assumption can be wrong, it needs reasonable noise covariances, and it is sensitive to inconsistent inputs, such as timestamps or frames that do not match, which I experienced in the navigation project.
>
> **中文译文：** 普通卡尔曼滤波要求线性模型。车辆运动和许多测量是非线性的，因为航向通过正弦和余弦进入。EKF 利用雅可比矩阵，在当前估计点附近线性化运动模型和测量模型。弱点：不确定性大或非线性强时线性化可能很差；高斯假设可能不成立；需要合理的噪声协方差；对不一致的输入（如时间戳或坐标系不匹配）非常敏感——这正是我在导航项目中遇到的。


#### Q17. Why do coordinate frames and timestamps matter so much in robotics?

**中文：** 为什么坐标系和时间戳在机器人里这么重要？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> Every measurement is expressed in some frame and taken at some time. If I fuse a LiDAR scan with odometry from a different time, or treat a pose in one frame as if it were in another, the result is wrong in a way no filter can fix; it appears as drift, jumps or oscillation. So a good system fixes a frame convention, such as map, odom and base frames, uses a consistent clock, interpolates or buffers to match times, and checks validity. This was the common thread of my navigation problems and of the state-management work at Kaihong.
>
> **中文译文：** 每个测量都在某个坐标系下、某个时刻取得。如果我把激光雷达扫描与另一时刻的里程计融合，或把一个坐标系的位姿当作另一个坐标系的位姿，得到的结果是任何滤波器都修不好的错误，表现为漂移、跳变或振荡。所以好的系统要固定坐标系约定（如 map、odom、base），使用一致的时钟，通过插值或缓冲匹配时间，并检查有效性。这是我导航问题和深开鸿状态管理工作的共同主线。


#### Q18. How do you linearise an inverted pendulum and check that LQR is applicable?

**中文：** 如何线性化倒立摆？怎么检查能用 LQR？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> I write the nonlinear equations of motion, choose the upright equilibrium, and take a first-order Taylor expansion, which for small tilt means sin theta is approximately theta and cos theta is approximately 1. That gives a linear state-space model x-dot = Ax + Bu. LQR needs the pair (A, B) to be controllable, which I check with the rank of the controllability matrix. The linear model is only valid near upright, so the controller should keep the tilt small, which is one reason to bound the attitude reference. In my ballbot, the four states were position, velocity, tilt and tilt rate.
>
> **中文译文：** 我先写出非线性运动方程，选直立平衡点，做一阶泰勒展开；小倾角下 sin θ≈θ，cos θ≈1，得到线性状态空间模型 x' = Ax + Bu。LQR 要求 (A, B) 可控，用可控性矩阵的秩来检查。线性模型只在直立附近有效，所以控制器要让倾角保持较小，这也是限制姿态参考幅度的原因之一。在我的球形机器人里，四个状态是位置、速度、倾角和倾角速度。


#### Q19. Compare A*, Hybrid-A*, RRT* and TEB. What does each solve?

**中文：** 比较 A*、Hybrid-A*、RRT* 和 TEB，各解决什么问题？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> A* finds a shortest path on a discrete graph with an admissible heuristic, but ignores vehicle kinematics. Hybrid-A* keeps a continuous state, including heading, inside each grid cell, and expands with kinematically feasible motions, so the path is drivable by a car-like vehicle. RRT* is sampling-based: it works well in high-dimensional spaces and is asymptotically optimal, but paths can be irregular and need smoothing. TEB is a local optimiser: it deforms a path as a time-parameterised trajectory under costmap and dynamic constraints. In my chain, Hybrid-A* gave the global path, TEB the local trajectory, and MPC tracked it.
>
> **中文译文：** A* 在离散图上用可采纳启发函数求最短路径，但忽略车辆运动学。Hybrid-A* 在每个栅格内保留包含航向的连续状态，并用运动学可行的动作扩展，因此路径对车式车辆是可行驶的。RRT* 基于采样，适合高维空间并渐近最优，但路径可能不规则，需要平滑。TEB 是局部优化器：在代价地图和动力学约束下把路径变形为带时间参数的轨迹。在我的链条里，Hybrid-A* 给出全局路径，TEB 给出局部轨迹，MPC 负责跟踪。


#### Q20. What is the connection between Bayes' rule and a Kalman filter?

**中文：** 贝叶斯公式和卡尔曼滤波有什么联系？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> A Kalman filter is a recursive Bayesian estimator for linear systems with Gaussian noise. The prediction step propagates the previous belief through the motion model, which widens the uncertainty. The update step applies Bayes' rule: it combines that prior with the likelihood of the new measurement. Because everything is Gaussian, the posterior is also Gaussian, and the Kalman gain tells how much to trust the measurement versus the prediction. The EKF linearises a nonlinear model around the current estimate; AMCL uses particles to represent a belief that is not Gaussian. I used EKF and AMCL in my navigation project.
>
> **中文译文：** 卡尔曼滤波是针对线性、高斯噪声系统的递归贝叶斯估计器。预测步把上一时刻的置信度通过运动模型传播，不确定性变大；更新步应用贝叶斯公式，把这个先验与新测量的似然结合。由于一切都是高斯的，后验也是高斯，卡尔曼增益决定更相信测量还是预测。EKF 在当前估计点附近线性化非线性模型；AMCL 用粒子表示非高斯的置信度。我在导航项目里用过 EKF 和 AMCL。


#### Q21. Derive the differential-drive kinematics.

**中文：** 推导差速驱动的运动学。

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> Let the wheel radius be r, the track width L, and the left and right wheel angular speeds omega_L and omega_R. The wheel linear speeds are v_L = r*omega_L and v_R = r*omega_R. The body linear speed is v = (v_R + v_L)/2 and the yaw rate is omega = (v_R - v_L)/L. Inversely, given v and omega, the wheel targets are v_R = v + omega*L/2 and v_L = v - omega*L/2. Odometry integrates this: x-dot = v*cos(theta), y-dot = v*sin(theta), theta-dot = omega. These are textbook formulas; the real values of r and L need calibration.
>
> **中文译文：** 设轮半径为 r，轮距为 L，左右轮角速度为 ω_L 和 ω_R。轮的线速度为 v_L = r·ω_L，v_R = r·ω_R。车体线速度 v = (v_R + v_L)/2，横摆角速度 ω = (v_R − v_L)/L。反过来，给定 v 和 ω，轮目标为 v_R = v + ω·L/2，v_L = v − ω·L/2。里程计对此积分：ẋ = v·cosθ，ẏ = v·sinθ，θ̇ = ω。这些是教科书公式，r 和 L 的真实值需要标定。


#### Q22. Explain the pinhole camera model. Why does calibration matter?

**中文：** 解释针孔相机模型。为什么要标定？

> **参考回答（English）**
>
> In the pinhole model, a 3D point in the camera frame (X, Y, Z) projects to pixel coordinates u = fx * X / Z + cx and v = fy * Y / Z + cy, where fx, fy are focal lengths in pixels and (cx, cy) is the principal point. These are the intrinsics. The extrinsics, a rotation and translation, relate the camera frame to the robot or world frame. Real lenses add distortion. Calibration, for example with a checkerboard, estimates these parameters. Without it, a pixel cannot be turned into a reliable ray or position, so errors flow into the estimator and the planner. 【待你补全：你是否做过标定；没有就说明是概念理解】
>
> **中文译文：** 在针孔模型中，相机坐标系下的三维点 (X, Y, Z) 投影到像素坐标 u = fx·X/Z + cx，v = fy·Y/Z + cy，其中 fx、fy 是以像素为单位的焦距，(cx, cy) 是主点，这些是内参。外参（旋转和平移）把相机坐标系与机器人或世界坐标系联系起来。真实镜头还有畸变。标定（例如用棋盘格）估计这些参数。没有标定，像素就无法转换为可靠的射线或位置，误差会流入估计器和规划器。

## 六、5 道模拟题

第 1 题请先画一张 5 个方框的链路图，再口述。

**模拟题 1.** Describe your navigation chain end to end, and say which module's failure is most dangerous.

**中文：** 完整描述你的导航链，并说明哪个模块的失效最危险。

**评分要点：** 定位→规划→局部→跟踪；估计失效最危险；一致性

**模拟题 2.** A pose estimate jumps by half a metre. What are the possible causes?

**中文：** 位姿估计突然跳变半米，可能的原因有哪些？（练习假设）

**评分要点：** 时间/坐标系/运动方向/粒子退化/传感器异常；排查顺序

**模拟题 3.** Explain the Kalman filter's predict and update steps and what P, Q and R mean.

**中文：** 解释卡尔曼滤波的预测与更新，以及 P、Q、R 的含义。

**评分要点：** 公式与直觉；增益；协方差

**模拟题 4.** What is the difference between SLAM and localisation, and would you say you have done SLAM?

**中文：** SLAM 与定位的区别？你是否做过 SLAM？

**评分要点：** 区别；没有做过完整 SLAM；诚实

**模拟题 5.** What would you do in your project report at UM?

**中文：** 你在澳大的项目报告会做什么？

**评分要点：** 小而聚焦：估计—规划—控制联系；指标与基线

## 七、本章要点速记

1. 主线：感知、估计、规划、控制必须整体设计；估计不可信，规划器无用。
2. P5 是主证据：EKF/AMCL → Hybrid-A* → TEB → MPC；问题多出在时间与坐标系。
3. 诚实缺口：机器视觉（只有 P4 部署）、完整 SLAM、非线性控制理论。
4. 共同原理三条：闭环链条、模型有效域、硬件验证改变问题。
5. UM 2027 的日期、费用、形式均未公布，以邮件为准。
6. 对学校/教授的了解只说真实的。
7. 互补滤波/Mahony 要能讲清漂移与偏置。
