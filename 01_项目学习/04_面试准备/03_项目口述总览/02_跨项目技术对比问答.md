## A. PID、LQR、MPC、滑模：怎么选

### Q1. In your projects you used PID, LQR and MPC. How do you decide which one to use?

**参考回答**
"I decide by three questions: how well do I know the model, how many coupled states and constraints are there, and how much compute and time do I have. On the RoboMaster hero robot the loops were single-input, the plant was a motor with friction and load changes, and I needed it running on an STM32 quickly — so cascaded PID was the natural choice. On the wheel-legged robot the plant is an unstable inverted pendulum with several coupled states, and its dynamics change with leg length — that's what LQR does well, so I used gain-scheduled LQR. On the car-like robot, the main difficulty is following a path under steering, speed and heading constraints, and MPC handles constraints explicitly, so I used MPC for tracking. So it's not 'which is better' — each fits a different structure."

中文：我用三个问题选：模型了解多少？耦合状态和约束多不多？算力与时间如何？英雄机器人是单输入回路，对象是有摩擦与负载变化的电机，要在 STM32 上快速跑起来，所以级联 PID；轮腿机器人是多状态耦合的不稳定倒立摆，动力学随腿长变化，适合增益调度 LQR；车式机器人主要难点是在转向/速度/航向约束下跟路径，MPC 显式处理约束，所以跟踪用 MPC。不是谁更好，而是各自匹配结构。

**追问方向**：Why not use MPC on the wheel-legged robot？（见 Q3）

### Q2. What exactly is the difference between PID and LQR, in one sentence each, and in terms of what you tune?

**参考回答**
"PID is an output-feedback structure: you pick the structure, three gains per loop, and tune them by response — you usually tune each loop separately. LQR is a full-state feedback law that comes from a model and a cost: you choose weights Q and R that say how much you care about each state versus control effort, and the gain matrix comes out of solving a Riccati equation. So with PID I tune gains; with LQR I tune *preferences*, and the gains are computed. In my AIRS work I used this difference: LQR told me how the weights trade stability against control effort, and that helped me explain the PID gains I had deployed."

中文：PID 是输出反馈结构：选好结构，每个回路三个增益，按响应调，通常各回路分别调。LQR 是由模型和代价函数得到的全状态反馈律：选权重 Q、R 表达对各状态与控制量的偏好，增益矩阵通过解 Riccati 方程得到。所以 PID 调的是增益，LQR 调的是"偏好"，增益由计算得出。AIRS 里我利用了这点：LQR 告诉我权重如何权衡稳定性与控制量，帮助解释已部署的 PID 增益。

**追问方向**：What does R do？What happens when R is too small？（控制量大，易饱和）

### Q3. Why didn't you use MPC for balance on the wheel-legged robot, if MPC handles constraints like torque limits?

**参考回答**
"Good question. MPC can handle torque limits, but it needs an online optimisation at every control step, and the balance loop on an STM32H7 runs at a high rate with FreeRTOS tasks sharing the CPU. Gain-scheduled LQR is a precomputed gain lookup, so it's cheap and deterministic. Also, the real problems I traced were state inconsistency, torque limits and contact transitions, and I handled the torque limits by limiting the output. I'm not claiming MPC wouldn't work — it's a good option for a more powerful controller, and it's something I'd like to study in a master's. But for this project, LQR gave me a predictable real-time cost. 【待你补全：团队/导师当时是否讨论过 MPC】"

中文：MPC 能处理力矩限制，但每个控制周期都要在线优化；STM32H7 上平衡环频率高，还有 FreeRTOS 任务共用 CPU。增益调度 LQR 是预先算好的增益查表，便宜且确定。而且我实际追到的问题是状态不一致、力矩限制和接触切换，力矩限制通过输出限幅处理。我不是说 MPC 不行，它是更强的选择，也是我想在硕士学习的；但这个项目里 LQR 的实时开销可预测。

**追问方向**：What is the real-time cost of MPC and how do embedded MPC solvers reduce it？（见基础文档 [MPC](../../02_基础知识/04_控制理论/05_MPC模型预测控制.md)）

### Q4. In P5 you used MPC for tracking. Why not just Pure Pursuit or a PID on the lateral error?

**参考回答**
"Pure Pursuit and Stanley are simple and robust, and for gentle paths they work. I chose MPC because the CV project needed constraints on position, heading, speed and steering to be part of the problem, and the reference from TEB is a time-parameterised trajectory with curvature, not just a path. MPC can use the vehicle's kinematic model and look ahead over a horizon, so the steering is planned, not just reactive. The price is computation and the need for a decent model. For a car-like vehicle with a kinematic bicycle-type model that price is acceptable. 【待你补全：MPC 的预测时域、求解器、控制频率】"

中文：Pure Pursuit/Stanley 简单鲁棒，缓路径上好用。我选 MPC，是因为需要把位置、航向、速度、转向约束放进问题里，且 TEB 给的是带曲率的时间参数化轨迹，不仅是路径。MPC 用车辆运动学模型向前看一个时域，转向是规划出来的而非仅反应。代价是计算量与对模型的要求；对车式运动学模型这个代价可接受。

**追问方向**：What is the horizon and how did you choose it?

### Q5. Cascaded PID shows up in P1, P2 (wheel loop), P3. What makes a cascade better than a single PID?

**参考回答**
"A cascade splits a hard problem into a fast inner loop and a slower outer loop. The inner loop — for example angular rate in the gimbal, or attitude in the ballbot — makes the plant respond quickly and more linearly, so the outer loop sees a simpler system. It also lets me limit each stage separately. In P1, the position outer loop outputs a bounded attitude reference. That bound is a safety feature: even if the position error is big, the robot is never asked to lean too far. The rule of thumb is that the inner loop should be clearly faster than the outer one. 【待你补全：P1/P3 内外环的实际频率关系】"

中文：级联把难问题拆成快的内环与慢的外环。内环（如云台角速度、ballbot 姿态）让对象响应快且更线性，外环看到更简单的系统；还能分别对各级限幅。P1 的位置外环输出有界的姿态参考，这个界是安全设计：位置误差再大也不会要求倾得太多。经验法则：内环明显快于外环。

**追问方向**：What happens if the inner and outer loops have similar bandwidth?（相互耦合、振荡）

### Q6. When does PID fail, in your experience?

**参考回答**
"In my experience it fails — or at least stops being the easiest tool — in three situations. One: strong coupling between states, like the ballbot where position, velocity and tilt all interact; I could tune for a long time without understanding why. Two: parameters that change a lot with operating point, like the wheel-legged robot's leg length, where one fixed gain is a compromise. Three: constraints that must be respected, like torque limits and steering limits; PID doesn't know about them, so you get windup and saturation unless you add anti-windup and limiting. For the first two I went to a model-based method, for the third I added output limiting."

中文：我的经验里三类情况：一，状态强耦合（ballbot 位置/速度/倾角相互作用），调很久也不知道为什么；二，参数随工作点变化大（轮腿腿长），固定增益是折中；三，必须遵守的约束（力矩、转向限制），PID 不知道，需防积分饱和与限幅。前两类我转向基于模型的方法，第三类加输出限幅。

**追问方向**：How do you do anti-windup?  `【待你补全：你实际用的抗积分饱和方式】`

### Q7. What is saturation, and where did you meet it?

**参考回答**
"Saturation means the actuator can't deliver what the controller asks for — the motor torque or current hits its limit. I met it in three places. In P1, the LQR analysis helped me diagnose actuator saturation: the weights show how aggressive the control effort is. In P7, torque limits were one of the three root causes of balance anomalies. In P3, the chassis power limit effectively limits the wheel speeds, so I scale them by the estimated power demand. The common rule: when saturated, the linear analysis no longer applies, so you must limit the output deliberately and design with that in mind."

中文：饱和指执行器给不出控制器要求的量（力矩/电流到限）。三处遇到：P1 用 LQR 分析帮助诊断执行器饱和（权重反映控制量激进程度）；P7 力矩限制是平衡异常三个根因之一；P3 底盘功率限制等效限制轮速，我按功率需求估计缩放轮速。共同规则：饱和后线性分析不再适用，需有意限幅并据此设计。

**追问方向**：What does saturation do to an integrator?

### Q8. Compare sliding-mode control with PID in plain words. Why did you move the launcher to sliding mode?

**参考回答**
"A PID reacts to the error with fixed gains; if the load changes, the same gains can be too slow or too strong. Sliding-mode control defines a surface — a combination of error and its rate — and drives the system onto it with a strong switching action, which is robust to bounded disturbances and parameter changes. In the launcher, projectiles passing through the friction wheels change the load, and I needed stable continuous firing with muzzle-velocity feedback. So I iterated from PID to variable-gain PID to sliding-mode control. The known cost of sliding mode is chattering, so in practice one softens the switching. 【待你补全：你的滑模面、抖振处理、与 PID 对比的效果】"

中文：PID 用固定增益响应误差，负载变化时可能太慢或太猛；滑模定义由误差及其变化率组成的"面"，用强切换作用把系统驱到面上，对有界扰动与参数变化鲁棒。发射机构里弹丸通过摩擦轮改变负载，需要用初速反馈实现稳定连发，所以从 PID → 变增益 PID → 滑模。代价是抖振，实际常软化切换。

**追问方向**：How do you reduce chattering? （边界层、饱和函数、高阶滑模）

### Q9. Rank your comfort: PID, LQR, MPC, sliding mode — what do you really know, and what are you still learning?

**参考回答**
"Honestly: PID and cascades I know best because I tuned them on real motors, in P1, P2 and P3. LQR I know well at the level of modelling, weights and gain scheduling, from P1 and P7. Sliding mode I used in one subsystem and I'd say I understand the idea and the practical side, but not the full theory. MPC I used in P5 as part of a navigation chain, and I know the structure — model, horizon, constraints — but I'm still learning the theory of solvers and stability guarantees. My statement says my learning experience is practice-first, and this is one area where I want to build theory in a master's programme."

中文：诚实讲：PID/级联最熟（P1/P2/P3 真机调过）；LQR 在建模、权重、增益调度层面熟（P1/P7）；滑模只在一个子系统用过，理解思想和实际做法，不算完整理论；MPC 在 P5 作为导航链一环，知道结构（模型、时域、约束），求解器与稳定性理论仍在学。PS 说我的学习以实践为先，这是想在硕士补理论的地方。

**追问方向**：Show me how you would derive the LQR gain for a simple system.

## B. P1 与 P7：同一个问题的两条路

### Q10. What is the relationship between your cascaded PID in P1 and your LQR in P7?

**参考回答**
"They're two attempts at the same problem — balancing an inverted-pendulum-like robot — with different amounts of model. In P1, I deployed a cascaded PID and then went back to the model to analyse it: a four-state linearised pendulum and LQR, to understand how weights trade off stability and control effort. That was my 'model as explanation' step. In P7 I used that mindset from the start: the LQR *is* the controller, and since the leg length changes the model, I scheduled the gains by leg length. So P1 taught me why a model is useful; P7 is where I let the model lead."

中文：这是同一问题（平衡类倒立摆机器人）的两次尝试，用的模型量不同。P1 先部署级联 PID，再回到模型做分析（四状态线性化倒立摆 + LQR，理解权重如何权衡稳定性与控制量）——"模型作为解释"。P7 一开始就带着这个思路：LQR 就是控制器，腿长改变模型，所以按腿长调度增益。P1 教我模型为什么有用，P7 让模型主导。

**追问方向**：What is the difference in what you can verify on the real robot between P1 and P7?

### Q11. In P1 the LQR was for analysis, not deployed. Why not deploy it?

**参考回答**
"I should be accurate here: my CV says I used LQR for modelling and analysis, as a model reference for the PID tuning and for saturation diagnosis, and the deployed controller was the cascaded PID. I used it that way because the cascaded PID was already working on the robot with the hardware safety loop around it, and the LQR analysis answered the questions I needed: why a gain behaves as it does, and when the actuators saturate. Deploying a full-state LQR would need reliable estimates for all four states and a good model of the arms moving; that's a bigger step. 【待你补全：当时为什么没有部署 LQR（时间/状态可靠性/团队决定）】"

中文：要准确：CV 写的是 LQR 用于建模与分析，作为 PID 整定的模型参考和饱和诊断，部署的是级联 PID。因为级联 PID 已在机上工作且有硬件安全回路，LQR 分析回答了我需要的问题（增益为何如此、何时饱和）。部署全状态 LQR 需要四个状态的可靠估计和手臂运动的良好模型，是更大的一步。

**追问方向**：If you had to deploy it, what would you need?

### Q12. Both P1 and P7 balance a pendulum-like robot. What is physically different?

**参考回答**
"In P1 the base is a three-omni-wheel ballbot: it balances in two directions, and the balance is coupled with the arms because the arm motion shifts the centre of mass. In P7 the body is on a wheel-legged mechanism, and the key variable is leg length — it changes the pendulum's effective length and so the dynamics, and the legs are closed-chain mechanisms with joint torques. So in P1 my special tool was centre-of-mass feedforward from arm joints; in P7 it was gain scheduling and VMC. Both needed a consistent state and respected actuator limits."

中文：P1 底座是三全向轮 ballbot，在两个方向平衡，且与手臂耦合（臂动改变质心）；P7 是轮腿机构，关键变量是腿长——改变等效摆长与动力学，腿是闭链机构、用关节力矩。所以 P1 的特色工具是臂关节的质心前馈，P7 是增益调度与 VMC。两者都要求状态一致并尊重执行器极限。

**追问方向**：Where does feedforward appear in P7？（腿长 PID + 力前馈）

### Q13. Feedforward appears in P1 (CoM), P2 (gravity compensation), P7 (force). What do they have in common?

**参考回答**
"In all three, I use a model to predict a disturbance I can compute, and cancel it before the feedback loop sees it. In P1 the arm joint states predict the centre-of-mass shift, so I feed roll and pitch gravity feedforward. In P2 the leader arm's gravity torque is computed from the URDF and KDL dynamics, so the human doesn't feel the arm's weight. In P7 the leg-length loop has force feedforward, since the support force is predictable. The common point: feedback corrects errors after they appear, feedforward prevents the predictable part. And you verify it the same way: toggle it on and off under the same motion, as I did in P1."

中文：三处都是用模型预测可计算的扰动，在反馈回路看到之前抵消。P1 由臂关节状态预测质心偏移，加 roll/pitch 重力前馈；P2 由 URDF/KDL 动力学算 leader 臂重力力矩，让人感觉不到臂重；P7 腿长环有力前馈（支撑力可预测）。共同点：反馈在误差出现后纠正，前馈提前消除可预测部分；验证方法也一致：同一运动下开关补偿，如 P1。

**追问方向**：What if the model is wrong? （前馈引入误差，反馈要兜底）

## C. P3：滑模、变增益与 PID

### Q14. In P3, why a cascade for the gimbal but a different approach for the launcher?

**参考回答**
"They are different plants. The gimbal's job is to point to an attitude, and the physical structure is natural for a cascade: an angular-rate inner loop from the gyro, an angle outer loop from the IMU attitude. The launcher's job is to keep a wheel speed constant under a load that changes every time a projectile passes. A cascade doesn't solve that — what matters is robustness to the load change. That's why I iterated there: PID, then variable-gain PID, then sliding mode, using the muzzle-velocity feedback."

中文：对象不同。云台目标是指向姿态，结构天然适合级联（陀螺角速度内环、IMU 姿态角外环）；发射机构目标是弹丸每次通过时负载变化下保持转速恒定，级联解决不了，关键是对负载变化的鲁棒，所以在这里迭代 PID→变增益 PID→滑模，并用初速反馈。

**追问方向**：How did you know the muzzle velocity? （初速反馈，传感器细节 `【待你补全：初速测量方式】`）

### Q15. What is variable-gain PID, and how is it different from the gain-scheduled LQR in P7?

**参考回答**
"They're the same idea at different levels. In both, the gains change with an operating variable, so one fixed gain doesn't have to fit everywhere. In P7, gains are computed from a model at different leg lengths and scheduled by leg length. In P3, variable-gain PID changes the PID gains depending on the situation, for example the size of the error — closer to an empirical design. 【待你补全：P3 变增益的调度变量与规则】 The difference is where the gains come from: from a model-based calculation, or from tuning experience."

中文：思想相同，层次不同。两者都让增益随工作变量变化。P7 的增益由模型在不同腿长下计算，并按腿长调度；P3 的变增益 PID 根据情形（例如误差大小）改变 PID 增益，更偏经验设计。区别在于增益来自模型计算还是调参经验。

**追问方向**：What is the risk of gain scheduling? （切换处的平滑、插值）

### Q16. Sliding mode (P3) vs PID (P3, P2) vs LQR (P7): which is the most robust and why do you not use it everywhere?

**参考回答**
"Sliding mode has strong robustness to bounded disturbances, but it brings chattering and high switching activity, which wears actuators and excites unmodelled dynamics. PID is cheap, intuitive and good enough for most single loops. LQR is optimal only for the model you give it — if the model is wrong, the guarantee is weaker than it looks. So I use each where its strengths fit: sliding mode where load changes dominate (launcher), PID where the loop is simple (gimbal, wheel speed), LQR where state coupling matters (balance). Robustness has a cost, so I don't pay it unless I need it."

中文：滑模对有界扰动鲁棒性强，但有抖振与高频切换，磨损执行器、激发未建模动态；PID 便宜直观，对多数单回路足够；LQR 只对给定模型最优，模型错了保证没看上去那么强。所以按长处选：负载变化主导用滑模（发射机构），简单回路用 PID（云台、轮速），状态耦合用 LQR（平衡）。鲁棒是有代价的，不需要就不付。

## D. 估计：P5 的 EKF/AMCL 与姿态估计

### Q17. P5 uses an EKF. Other projects use an IMU with AHRS or complementary filtering. What's the difference in purpose?

**参考回答**
"They answer different questions. A complementary filter, like the Mahony-type AHRS on the IMU, estimates attitude by blending the gyro, which is smooth but drifts, with the accelerometer, which is noisy but gives a gravity reference — it's cheap and fits an MCU. The EKF in P5 estimates the vehicle pose — position and heading — by fusing IMU, wheel encoders and LiDAR-based localisation, with covariances, in a model-based way. So: complementary filter = attitude from one IMU, cheap, fixed blend; EKF = pose from several sensors, probabilistic, more flexible but needs a model and noise tuning. 【待你补全：你具体在 P1/P3/P7 哪个项目使用了 Mahony 类 AHRS，若由现成库提供则如实说】"

中文：回答的问题不同。互补滤波（如 IMU 上的 Mahony 类 AHRS）融合陀螺（平滑但漂移）与加速度计（噪声大但有重力参考）估计姿态，便宜，适合 MCU。P5 的 EKF 融合 IMU、编码器、激光定位来估计车辆位姿（位置+航向），带协方差，基于模型。互补滤波 = 单 IMU 估姿态、廉价、固定混合；EKF = 多传感器估位姿、概率化、更灵活但需要模型与噪声整定。

**追问方向**：Can a complementary filter be seen as a special Kalman filter? （稳态增益固定的近似）

### Q18. What is the main difference between EKF and AMCL, and why use both?

**参考回答**
"AMCL is a particle filter: it keeps many pose hypotheses and weights them by how well the LiDAR scan matches the map. It can handle multi-modal uncertainty — useful for global localisation — and gives a map-referenced pose, but it updates in discrete steps and can be jumpy. The EKF is a Gaussian filter: smooth and good at fusing high-rate inertial and wheel data, but it assumes unimodal uncertainty. So I used the EKF for continuity and AMCL for the map reference. The practical difficulty I faced was that when timestamps, frames or motion direction were inconsistent, these two disagreed and gave drift and pose jumps."

中文：AMCL 是粒子滤波：保持许多位姿假设，按激光与地图匹配程度加权；能处理多峰不确定性（全局定位有用），给出相对地图的位姿，但更新离散、可能跳变。EKF 是高斯滤波：平滑，擅长融合高频惯性与轮速数据，但假设单峰。我用 EKF 保证连续性，用 AMCL 提供地图参考。实际困难是时间戳、坐标系或运动方向不一致时，两者不一致，出现漂移与位姿跳变。

**追问方向**：What is the "kidnapped robot" problem?

### Q19. In P5 you said pose jumps came from inconsistent timing, frames and motion direction. Give me a concrete way you'd detect each one.

**参考回答**
"Generic detection methods, which are also what I would build into a state manager: for timing — compare sensor timestamps with the receive time and with each other; check for gaps and out-of-order messages, and look at whether the pose jump happens at the same moment the delay grows. For frames — verify the transform tree: plot the same landmark in the odom and map frames and check for a constant offset or a rotation. For motion direction — compare the sign of the wheel-encoder velocity with the commanded direction and the IMU acceleration; a sign error shows up as the robot appearing to move the wrong way when reversing. 【待你补全：你在 P5 实际遇到并解决的具体一次不一致】"

中文：通用检测方法（也是我会放进状态管理器的做法）：时间——比较传感器时间戳与接收时间及彼此，查间隙与乱序，看位姿跳变是否与延迟增大同时出现；坐标系——核对变换树，在 odom 与 map 系下画同一个地标，看是否常量偏移或旋转；运动方向——比较编码器速度符号、指令方向与 IMU 加速度，符号错会表现为倒车时看起来往错误方向动。

### Q20. Why does an EKF need a motion model and a measurement model? Where do those come from in P5?

**参考回答**
"The motion model predicts how the state changes between measurements; the measurement model says what sensor reading to expect given a state. The filter compares the prediction with the actual measurement and corrects using the Kalman gain, which depends on the covariances. In P5, the motion model is the car-like kinematics driven by wheel odometry and IMU, and the measurements come from the localisation outputs. If either model is wrong — for example, the wrong motion direction — the correction fights the prediction and you see jumps. 【待你补全：状态向量与协方差设置】"

中文：运动模型预测测量之间状态如何变化；测量模型说明给定状态应预期什么读数。滤波器比较预测与实际测量，用依赖协方差的卡尔曼增益校正。P5 里运动模型是由轮速里程计和 IMU 驱动的车式运动学，测量来自定位输出。任一模型错（如运动方向错），校正会与预测"打架"，出现跳变。

### Q21. What is similar between estimating attitude on an STM32 (P1/P3/P7) and estimating pose on a robot with ROS (P5)?

**参考回答**
"Both are about trusting the right sensor at the right frequency. A gyro is good at high frequency, an accelerometer or a map is good for the long term, and the filter blends them. 中文：两者都是在正确频率信任正确传感器：陀螺高频好，加速度计或地图适合长期，滤波器混合它们；都需要干净的时间基准与一致的坐标系。区别在平台：MCU 上每步开销固定且小、时序确定，常用简单滤波；ROS 一侧算力多、有消息时间戳与变换树，挑战转向同步与坐标系。所以我在 Kaihong 的状态管理像是对两者的推广。

## E. 奇异性：P2 逆运动学与 P7 五连杆

### Q22. You dealt with singularities in P2 (arm IK) and in P7 (closed-chain legs). Are they the same thing?

**参考回答**
"Mathematically they have the same flavour: the Jacobian loses rank, so some motion directions are lost or amplified. In P2, near an arm singularity, a small end-effector motion can demand a huge joint velocity; my constrained IK with MuJoCo and Mink suppresses that amplification. In P7, the leg is a closed-chain mechanism, and I use the Jacobian to map support force and pitch torque to joint torques — near a singular configuration the same Jacobian can make joint torques blow up or lose controllability. So the same concept, but in P2 I worry about velocity amplification going from task to joint space, in P7 about force-to-torque mapping and keeping the leg in a safe configuration range. 【待你补全：P7 中你如何限制腿的构型范围】"

中文：数学上同一类：雅可比失秩，某些运动方向丢失或被放大。P2 在臂奇异点附近，小的末端运动会要求巨大关节速度，我的 MuJoCo/Mink 约束 IK 抑制这种放大；P7 腿是闭链机构，用雅可比把支撑力和俯仰力矩映射到关节力矩，在奇异构型附近同一个雅可比会使关节力矩激增或失去可控性。概念相同：P2 关心任务空间到关节空间的速度放大，P7 关心力到力矩的映射及把腿保持在安全构型范围。

**追问方向**：Show a 2-link arm example. （见基础文档 [奇异性](../../02_基础知识/03_坐标变换与运动学/04_奇异性_阻尼最小二乘_零空间.md)）

### Q23. How do you suppress velocity amplification near a singularity? Name the standard methods.

**参考回答**
"The standard methods are: damped least squares — add a damping term so the pseudo-inverse stays bounded near singularities, at the cost of some tracking error; velocity or joint-limit constraints in a QP, so the solution simply isn't allowed to exceed limits; and task prioritisation or null-space motion, to use redundancy to stay away from singular configurations. In P2 I used constrained inverse kinematics with MuJoCo and Mink, where constraints are part of the optimisation. 【待你补全：Mink 里你启用的任务/约束/阻尼参数】"

中文：标准方法：阻尼最小二乘——加阻尼使伪逆在奇异点附近有界，代价是一些跟踪误差；QP 中加速度/关节限位约束，使解不允许超限；任务优先级或零空间运动，利用冗余远离奇异构型。P2 用 MuJoCo/Mink 的约束逆运动学，约束是优化的一部分。

**追问方向**：What is the cost of damping? （方向性误差、精度下降）

### Q24. Why did you need constrained IK for VR teleoperation instead of directly using an analytical IK?

**参考回答**
"A VR controller gives an arbitrary human motion, and humans don't know the robot's limits. The target can leave the workspace, hit joint limits, or pass near a singularity. An analytical IK gives the exact solution if there is one, but it doesn't tell you what to do near the limits, and a dual-arm robot with redundancy may have no closed form anyway. A constrained, optimisation-based IK always returns the best feasible joint motion under the constraints, which is what a teleoperation system needs. 【待你补全：你们的臂自由度与是否冗余】"

中文：VR 手柄给出任意的人类运动，人不知道机器人极限：目标可能超出工作空间、触碰关节限位、靠近奇异点。解析 IK 有解时给精确解，但在极限附近不告诉你怎么办，冗余双臂也可能没有闭式解。基于优化的约束 IK 总能在约束下返回最佳可行关节运动，这正是遥操作需要的。

**追问方向**：How do you handle the operator moving too fast?

### Q25. In P7, why are closed-chain kinematics harder than the arm kinematics in P2?

**参考回答**
"For a serial arm, forward kinematics is a direct chain of transforms, and inverse kinematics is the hard direction. In a closed-chain leg, such as a five-bar linkage, the joints are constrained by loop closure, so the relationship between motor angles and foot position is a different structure: you solve for the passive joints through the loop constraint, and the Jacobian maps motor space to the virtual leg's length and angle. The upside is that the mass is concentrated near the body, so the leg can be light and fast; the downside is more complex kinematics and its own singular configurations. 【待你补全：你们腿的具体构型与解法】"

中文：串联臂正运动学是变换直接相乘，逆运动学是难的方向。闭链腿（如五连杆）的关节受闭环约束，电机角到足端位置的关系结构不同：通过闭环约束解被动关节，雅可比把电机空间映射到虚拟腿的长度与角度。好处是质量集中在机身附近，腿轻快；坏处是运动学更复杂，也有自己的奇异构型。

## G. 状态一致性：P5 / P6 / P7 的共同点

### Q32. You keep saying "state consistency". What exactly do you mean, and where does it appear in P5, P6, P7?

**参考回答**
"I mean that all the numbers the controller or planner uses should describe the same moment, in the same frame, with the same sign convention, and should be valid. In P5, pose jumps came from inconsistent timing, frames and motion direction. In P7, balance anomalies came from state inconsistency, among other causes, like torque limits and contact transitions. 中文：我指的是控制器或规划器使用的所有数字应描述同一时刻、同一坐标系、同一符号约定，且有效。P5 的位姿跳变来自时间、坐标系、运动方向不一致；P7 的平衡异常其中一个根因是状态不一致（另有力矩限制和触地切换）；P6 把它做成平台功能：标准化时间戳、坐标系和有效性检查，运动控制层与任务层得到一个可信状态。三个项目一个教训：调控制器前先查状态。

### Q33. What would a "validity check" look like in code, in general terms?

**参考回答**
"Generic examples of what a state manager checks: is the timestamp recent enough compared with the current time; is it monotonic; is the frame ID the expected one, or is a transform available; are the values finite and inside physical ranges; and has the source stopped updating — a timeout. If a check fails, the state is marked invalid, not silently used, and downstream layers decide to hold, slow down or stop. 【待你补全：P6 里你实现的实际检查项与阈值，不要在面试里编数值】"

### Q34. How do timestamps matter on an MCU (P7) compared with ROS (P5, P6)?

### Q35. How does the "consistent state" idea relate to the P1 centre-of-mass feedforward?

**参考回答**
"The CoM feedforward depends on the arm joint states. If those joint states are late or in the wrong frame relative to the IMU attitude, the feedforward torque gets applied at the wrong moment or direction — and the compensation could make things worse. That's why I verified it by toggling the compensation under the same trajectory: if it's consistent, the toggle shows improvement. So feedforward isn't free: it raises the requirement on the state's timing and frames."

中文：质心前馈依赖臂关节状态。若关节状态相对 IMU 姿态有延迟或坐标系不对，前馈力矩会在错误时刻或方向施加，补偿可能更糟。所以我在同一轨迹下开关补偿验证：状态一致时，开关能看到改善。前馈不是免费的：它提高了对状态时序与坐标系的要求。

**追问方向**：How did you define roll/pitch signs? `【待你补全】`

## H. CAN 总线：P1 / P2 / P3 / P7

### Q36. CAN appears in four projects. What is common, and what is different?

**参考回答**
"Common: CAN is the physical link between a controller and the motor drivers; I send torque, current or speed commands and receive feedback like angle, speed and status. In P3 it's the STM32F407 talking to the gimbal, launcher and chassis motors. In P7 it's FDCAN on the STM32H7 for the wheel and hip motors. In P1 and P2 the CAN motor execution sits at the end of a ROS state-processing chain, so the path is ROS node → CAN → motor. The difference is who owns the real-time loop: in P3 and P7 the loop runs on the MCU itself; in P1 and P2 ROS/ROS2 is in the loop, so latency and jitter in the chain matter more. 【待你补全：P1/P2 中 CAN 通信的节点/频率/驱动类型】"

中文：共同点：CAN 是控制器与电机驱动之间的物理链路，发送力矩/电流/速度指令，接收角度、速度、状态反馈。P3 是 STM32F407 与云台/发射/底盘电机；P7 是 STM32H7 的 FDCAN 驱动轮与髋电机；P1、P2 里 CAN 电机执行位于 ROS 状态处理链末端，路径是 ROS 节点 → CAN → 电机。区别在于谁拥有实时回路：P3、P7 回路在 MCU 上；P1、P2 链路中有 ROS/ROS2，延迟与抖动更重要。

**追问方向**：What is CAN arbitration? （基础文档 [CAN](../../02_基础知识/08_嵌入式与通信/03_CAN_FDCAN与电机通信.md)）

### Q37. What's the difference between CAN and FDCAN, and why does P7 use FDCAN?

**参考回答**
"Classic CAN has an 8-byte payload and a bitrate up to 1 Mbit/s. CAN FD, which is what FDCAN peripherals implement on STM32H7, allows larger payloads and a faster data phase. In my project the STM32H7 has an FDCAN peripheral, so I used it for the motor communication; whether I used the FD features or only classic frames depended on the motor drivers. 【待你补全：电机驱动是否支持 CAN FD，波特率，帧格式】 I'd rather say that than guess."

中文：经典 CAN 载荷 8 字节，速率最高 1 Mbit/s；CAN FD（STM32H7 的 FDCAN 外设实现）允许更大载荷与更快的数据段。我的 STM32H7 有 FDCAN 外设，所以用于电机通信；是否用上 FD 特性取决于电机驱动。（具体需补全，不要猜。）

**追问方向**：What happens on bus-off?

### Q38. What goes wrong on a CAN bus in a real robot, and how did you handle it?

**参考回答**
"Generic failure modes that I check for: lost or late frames, which show up as feedback that stops updating or jumps; bus overload if too many nodes talk at high rate; wiring and termination problems; and a node that stops responding. In control terms, a stale feedback frame is dangerous because the controller acts on old data. In my hardware safety work on P1 I implemented output limiting and fall-recovery safeguards, and in P7 I traced anomalies partly to state inconsistency. The same attitude applies: treat the feedback as having a freshness, and don't let the control output go unbounded. 【待你补全：你遇到过的 CAN 具体故障】"

中文：我检查的通用故障：丢帧或延迟（反馈停止更新或跳变）、节点太多/速率太高导致总线过载、接线与终端电阻问题、节点失去响应。从控制角度看，过期反馈危险，因为控制器基于旧数据行动。P1 的硬件安全回路里我做了输出限幅与跌倒恢复保护，P7 里把异常部分追到状态不一致。同样态度：反馈要有"新鲜度"，控制输出不能无界。

**追问方向**：How do you detect a missing motor feedback frame?

### Q39. P2 mentions a "ROS2-CAN execution chain validated on real hardware". Why does it matter to say "real hardware"?

**参考回答**
"In simulation like MuJoCo, an IK solution is correct if the maths is right. On real hardware, you also find delays, friction, joint limits, communication jitter, and the fact that a wheel or an arm doesn't do exactly what you commanded. Validating on the real chain means the VR target really goes through ROS2, CAN, drivers and motors, and the arm and wheels behave as expected. That's why I describe the work as 'validated on real hardware through the ROS2-CAN chain' — and I'm careful to describe what I validated, not to claim more. 【待你补全：具体验证了什么现象】"

中文：MuJoCo 仿真里，数学对 IK 就对。真机上还有延迟、摩擦、关节限位、通信抖动，轮子或手臂不会精确按指令动。在真实链路验证意味着 VR 目标真经 ROS2、CAN、驱动和电机，手臂和轮子表现符合预期。所以我说"通过 ROS2-CAN 链在真机上验证"，并谨慎描述验证了什么，不多说。

**追问方向**：What failed in simulation but worked on hardware, or the other way round?

## I. 调试方法论对比

### Q40. Compare how you debugged in P1, P5 and P7.

**参考回答**
"A common pattern: I try not to tune before I understand. In P1, when tuning couldn't explain the behaviour, I went back to a linearised model and used LQR to understand the gain trade-offs and diagnose saturation. In P5, I traced drift and pose jumps to inconsistent timing, frames and motion direction rather than the filter. In P7, I traced balance anomalies to state inconsistency, torque limits and contact transitions, and tuned only after that. So the method is: classify the problem — model, state, actuator, or mode — then fix the cause, then tune."

中文：共同模式：理解之前不乱调。P1 调参解释不了时回到线性化模型，用 LQR 理解增益权衡并诊断饱和；P5 把漂移与位姿跳变追到时间、坐标系、运动方向不一致而非滤波器；P7 把平衡异常追到状态不一致、力矩限制、触地切换，之后才调参。方法：先分类（模型/状态/执行器/模式），修根因，再调参。

**追问方向**：Tell me about a bug where your first diagnosis was wrong.

### Q41. What tools did you use to debug on the real robots?

**参考回答**
"Mostly logging and plotting the signals around the loop: reference, measurement and output, plus the actuator limits. For ROS-based projects, I look at topics and timestamps, and the transform tree. And the specific technique I used in P1: a controlled comparison — the same arm trajectory with the CoM compensation on and off. I also like simple unit checks, for example signs of the axes, before blaming the controller. 【待你补全：你实际使用的日志、绘图工具、示波器或调试器】"

中文：主要是记录并画出回路附近的信号：参考、测量、输出以及执行器限制。ROS 项目查话题、时间戳、变换树。P1 的具体技巧是受控对比：同一手臂轨迹开关质心补偿。我也喜欢先做简单检查（如坐标轴符号）再怪控制器。

**追问方向**：How do you log at high rate on an MCU without disturbing the loop?

### Q42. What do you do first when a real robot "misbehaves"?

**参考回答**
"My checklist, in order: first, is the data sane — timestamps, frames, signs, ranges. Second, is the actuator doing what's commanded — saturation, torque limits, communication. Third, is the system in the right mode — for example ground contact, initial state, manual or automatic. Fourth, only then, is the controller's gain or model wrong. That order comes from P5, P7 and P6: most of my 'mysterious' problems were in the first three."

中文：我的清单按顺序：一，数据是否合理（时间戳、坐标系、符号、范围）；二，执行器是否按指令动（饱和、力矩限制、通信）；三，系统模式对不对（触地、初始状态、手动/自动）；四，最后才是增益或模型。该顺序来自 P5、P7、P6：多数"神秘"问题出在前三项。

**追问方向**：Give an example of each of the four categories.

### Q43. Simulation vs real robot: how do your projects compare?

**参考回答**
"In P2, MuJoCo/Mink is part of the solver chain, but the CV states the results were validated on real hardware. In P5 and P7, the controllers were run on the real vehicle and robot, and the tuning was done on the robot. I treat simulation as good for checking the structure and the signs, and the real robot as the final judge for delays, saturation and contact. 【待你补全：哪些项目你用过仿真（Gazebo/MuJoCo等），做了什么】"

中文：P2 里 MuJoCo/Mink 是求解链的一部分，但 CV 写明结果在真机验证；P5、P7 控制器在真车/真机运行，整定在机上完成。我把仿真看作检查结构与符号的工具，真机则是延迟、饱和、接触的最终裁判。

**追问方向**：What was the biggest sim-to-real gap you saw?

## J. 数据驱动 vs 模型驱动

### Q44. Your projects are mostly model-based. Why do you want to study AI / learning-based methods?

**参考回答**
"My projects taught me both what models can do and where they stop. Model-based methods — LQR, VMC, MPC — give me guarantees I can explain and tune. But a model needs the world to be modelled: contacts, unknown objects, perception in a messy scene. That is where learning-based methods can help. My P4 project used a learned perception model, YOLOv5, and a decision chain; at Kaihong my safe-execution chain exists exactly because AI decisions will reach robots. My statement is honest: my machine-learning experience is practice-first, and I want to build the theory — optimisation, generalisation, reinforcement learning — in the master's programme."

中文：项目让我知道模型驱动能做什么及何处受限。LQR、VMC、MPC 给出我能解释和调的保证；但模型需要世界可建模：接触、未知物体、杂乱场景的感知，正是学习方法可以帮助之处。P4 用了学习的感知模型 YOLOv5 和决策链；Kaihong 的安全执行链之所以存在，正是因为 AI 决策将到达机器人。PS 里我坦承：机器学习经验以实践为先，想在硕士补理论——优化、泛化、强化学习。

**追问方向**：Which learning method would you apply first to one of your projects?

### Q45. Where could learning replace or help your model-based parts?

**参考回答**
"I'd answer carefully, as suggestions rather than things I did. In P7, a learned policy could handle terrain and contacts that my linear model doesn't capture, with LQR as a baseline and a safety fallback. In P5, learned components could help perception, or provide a better cost for local planning, while keeping MPC as the tracker with constraints. In P2, learned retargeting could make VR mapping more natural. In every case I'd keep a model-based safety layer — the same thinking as my P6 chain: learning proposes, rules and feedback dispose."

中文：谨慎回答，是建议而非我做过的。P7 里学习的策略可处理线性模型没捕捉到的地形与接触，LQR 做基线与安全回退；P5 里学习组件可帮助感知或提供更好的局部规划代价，MPC 仍做带约束的跟踪；P2 里学习式重定向可使 VR 映射更自然。每种情况都保留基于模型的安全层——与 P6 链一致：学习提案，规则与反馈裁决。

**追问方向**：What is the sim-to-real problem for RL on wheel-legged robots?

### Q46. How does your experience connect to "embodied AI"?

**参考回答**
"Embodied AI means intelligence that acts through a body in the physical world. I've worked on three layers of that body: control — keeping robots stable and tracking motions; estimation and planning — knowing where it is and where to go; and the safe interface — how a decision reaches an actuator. I have not trained large models for robots yet. What I bring is the ability to make the body reliable, so that when a learned brain is added, the whole system can be tested and trusted. That's what I'd like to deepen at your programme."

中文：具身智能是通过身体在物理世界行动的智能。我做过身体的三层：控制（稳定与跟踪运动）、估计与规划（知道在哪、去哪）、安全接口（决策如何到达执行器）。我还没训练过机器人大模型；我带来的是让身体可靠的能力，这样加入学习的"大脑"后，整个系统能被测试和信任。这是我想在贵项目深化的。

## 本章要点速记

1. 选控制器的三个问题：模型知道多少？状态耦合与约束多不多？算力与时间够不够？
2. PID 调增益，LQR 调偏好（Q、R），MPC 调模型+时域+约束，滑模调面与切换。
3. P1 的 LQR = 解释与诊断；P7 的 LQR = 部署控制器；二者是"模型先后顺序"的不同。
4. 前馈（P1 质心、P2 重力、P7 力）：模型预测可计算扰动，反馈兜底，用开关对比验证。
5. 奇异性：雅可比失秩；P2 担心速度放大，P7 担心力到力矩的映射与构型范围。
6. P4 → P6：agent 只提案，设备/策略层有最终发言权，反馈闭环。
7. 状态一致性：同一时刻、同一坐标系、同一符号、有效——先查状态再调参。
8. CAN：P3/P7 回路在 MCU 上，P1/P2 链中有 ROS，注意新鲜度与限幅。
9. 调试顺序：数据 → 执行器 → 模式 → 增益。
10. 数据驱动 vs 模型驱动：学习提案、模型与规则守底线。
