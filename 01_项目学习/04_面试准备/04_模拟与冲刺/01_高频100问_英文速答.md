# 高频 100 问：英文速答（60–90 秒版）

> **一句话定位**：这是面试里最可能出现的 100 个问题，每个都给一份可以直接说出口的英文参考答案（约 60–90 秒）和中文译文。答案全部基于 CV、PS、推荐信；没有依据的细节用 `【待你补全】` 标出，请先补全再背。

**本章学完你能……**

- 对"自我介绍 / 动机 / 项目 / 技术 / 行为 / 反问"六类问题，各有一套不翻车的回答；
- 每个答案在 60–90 秒内说完，结构清楚：结论先行 → 两三个依据 → 一句收束；
- 把 100 题用"主题卡片"的方式反复练习，而不是逐字背诵。

**前置知识**：[英文自我介绍多版本](../01_面试总指南/02_英文自我介绍_多版本.md)、[核心叙事线](../01_面试总指南/03_核心叙事线_为什么读研_为什么这个方向.md)、[七个项目口述](../03_项目口述总览/01_七个项目30秒_90秒_3分钟口述.md)、[跨项目技术对比](../03_项目口述总览/02_跨项目技术对比问答.md)。

## 使用方法

1. **三遍法**：第一遍看中文理解；第二遍照英文大声读（录音）；第三遍只看问题，用自己的话答，对照要点修改。
2. **语速**：110–120 词/分钟。每个答案约 90–150 词，对应 60–90 秒。
3. **别逐字背**：背"结论句 + 两个依据 + 收束句"。现场用自己的话连接。
4. **口径锚点**：排名统一 "6th out of 156, top 4%"；均分 87.63；IELTS 6.5；动词分寸见 [口径与事实基线](../../00_申请画像/04_口径与事实基线.md)。
5. 带 `【待你补全】` 的答案，补全前不要当事实说。

Each of my projects sits on one part of this chain, and each one taught me that the parts have to be consistent with each other."
中文：主线是从感知到动作的链：底层是稳定的控制回路；中层是机器人可信的状态（时间、坐标系、有效性）；顶层是可能来自规划器或 AI 的决策，须通过安全接口到达执行器。每个项目对应链的一环，也教我各环必须彼此一致。

## C. 项目经历（Q26–Q55）

### Q26. Which project are you most proud of?
"The ballbot balance control at AIRS, because it's where I learned to combine practice and theory. I deployed a cascaded PID that worked, but I wasn't satisfied with 'it works': I went back, modelled it as a four-state inverted pendulum and used LQR to understand the trade-offs behind my gains and the actuator saturation. It changed how I work: build, then explain."
中文：AIRS 的 ballbot：我部署了可用的级联 PID，但不满足于"能用"，回头建了四状态倒立摆并用 LQR 理解增益背后的权衡与执行器饱和。它改变了我的工作方式：先做，再解释。

### Q27. Which project was the most challenging?
"The wheel-legged robot, because the dynamics change with leg length and the failure modes were mixed. A balance problem could come from state inconsistency, torque limits or ground-contact transitions, and each looks like 'bad gains'. I had to learn to separate these causes before tuning. `【待你补全：一个具体案例】`"
中文：轮腿机器人：动力学随腿长变化，且失效模式混杂；平衡问题可能来自状态不一致、力矩限制、触地切换，看起来都像"增益不好"，我学会先分离根因再调参。

### Q28. Tell me about your RoboMaster project.
"It was our university team's hero robot, running on an STM32F407 with FreeRTOS and CAN. I was responsible for three control loops: a cascaded PID for the gimbal, the launcher loops which I iterated from PID to variable-gain PID to sliding-mode control, and the Mecanum chassis with power control. Our team won third prize at the national final."
中文：校队英雄机器人，STM32F407 + FreeRTOS + CAN。我负责三条回路：云台级联 PID；发射机构（PID→变增益 PID→滑模迭代）；麦轮底盘功率控制。团队获全国总决赛三等奖。

### Q29. What was your specific contribution in the RoboMaster team?
"The gimbal, launcher and chassis control loops of the hero robot. Other teammates handled other subsystems, like vision, mechanical design and the referee interface `【待你补全：队内分工】`. I'd describe the team's result as ours, and my part as those three loops."
中文：英雄机器人的云台、发射、底盘控制回路；其他由队友负责（视觉、机械、裁判系统等，补全）。成绩是团队的，我的部分是三条回路。

### Q30. Tell me about your internship at AIRS.
"I was a robotics software intern from September 2025 to March 2026, working on the dynamic balance of a three-omni-wheel ballbot with a dual-arm upper body. I was responsible for balance control development and on-robot tuning. I built a cascaded PID, did LQR modelling and analysis, added centre-of-mass feedforward from the arm joints, and a hardware safety loop for output limiting and fall recovery."
中文：2025.9–2026.3 机器人软件实习，做带双臂上身的三全向轮 ballbot 的动态平衡。负责平衡控制开发与机上整定：级联 PID、LQR 建模分析、臂关节质心前馈、输出限幅与跌倒恢复的硬件安全回路。

### Q31. What is a ballbot, and why is it hard to control?
"A ballbot balances on a spherical or, in my case, omni-wheel base, like an inverted pendulum that can fall in any direction. It's unstable by itself, and with arms moving, the centre of mass keeps changing. So it needs fast feedback, good state estimates, and care about actuator limits. `【待你补全：底盘具体构型，不要与 CV 冲突——CV 写 three-omni-wheel ballbot】`"
中文：ballbot 像能向任意方向倒的倒立摆，本身不稳；手臂运动使质心不断变化，因此需要快速反馈、良好状态估计和对执行器限制的关注。

### Q32. Explain the cascaded PID in your ballbot.
"The outer loop takes the position error and wheel-speed feedback and outputs an attitude reference. I bound that reference, so the robot is never asked to lean too far. The inner loop takes the IMU attitude and produces the body-level command, and the kinematics allocates it to the three wheels. The inner loop must be faster than the outer one."
中文：外环取位置误差和轮速反馈，输出姿态参考并限界，避免要求倾得过大；内环取 IMU 姿态，产生机体级指令，运动学分配到三轮；内环须比外环快。

### Q33. Why did you use LQR for analysis rather than as the controller?
"The PID was already deployed and working with the safety loop around it. LQR gave me the answers I needed: how the weights on position, velocity, tilt and tilt rate trade off stability against control effort, and when the actuators would saturate. It served as a model reference for my PID tuning. In the wheel-legged project, by contrast, the LQR was the main controller."
中文：PID 已部署且有安全回路；LQR 回答了我需要的问题——位置、速度、倾角、倾角速率权重如何权衡稳定性与控制量、何时饱和——作为 PID 整定的模型参考。轮腿项目里 LQR 则是主控制器。

### Q34. How did you verify the centre-of-mass feedforward?

### Q43. Why Hybrid-A* instead of plain A*?

## D. 技术问题（Q56–Q80）

### Q56. Explain PID to a non-expert.
"A PID controller looks at the error — how far you are from where you want to be — and decides the action from three things: P, the present error; I, the accumulated past error; D, how fast the error is changing. Like steering a car: P turns the wheel in proportion to how far you're off, I fixes a constant drift, D stops you from overshooting."
中文：PID 看误差（离目标多远），用三项决定动作：P 当前误差，I 累积的过去误差，D 误差变化速度。像开车：P 按偏离程度打方向，I 纠正持续偏移，D 防止过冲。

### Q57. What is the difference between P, I and D terms in practice?
"P gives speed of response but too much causes oscillation. I removes steady-state error but can cause windup and overshoot. D adds damping but amplifies noise. In practice, I tune P first, add D for damping, then a small I if needed, and always limit the output and the integrator."
中文：P 带来响应速度，过大振荡；I 消除稳态误差，但可能积分饱和和超调；D 增阻尼但放大噪声。实践中先调 P，加 D 抑制，必要时加小 I，并始终限幅输出与积分。

### Q58. What is integral windup and how do you prevent it?
"Windup is when the integrator keeps accumulating while the actuator is saturated, so when the error finally reverses, the controller overshoots badly. Common fixes are clamping the integral term, stopping integration while saturated, or back-calculation. `【待你补全：你在项目中实际用的方式】`"
中文：积分饱和：执行器饱和时积分项仍累积，误差反向时严重超调。常见办法：积分限幅、饱和时停止积分、反算法。

### Q59. What is a cascade control loop and why use it?
"A cascade has an inner fast loop and an outer slower loop. The outer loop's output is the inner loop's reference. It makes the plant look simpler to the outer loop, and lets me limit each stage. For example, a gimbal angle loop outside a rate loop, or in my ballbot a position loop outside the attitude loop."
中文：级联有快的内环与慢的外环，外环输出作为内环参考；使外环面对更简单的对象并可分级限幅。如云台角度环外套速率环；ballbot 位置环外套姿态环。

### Q60. What is LQR?
"LQR finds the state-feedback gain K that minimises a cost: the integral of x-transpose Q x plus u-transpose R u. Q says how much you care about state errors, R how much you care about control effort. K comes from solving the algebraic Riccati equation. It needs a linear model, and it's optimal for that model."
中文：LQR 求使代价（x'Qx + u'Ru 的积分）最小的状态反馈增益 K；Q 表示对状态误差的在意，R 表示对控制量的在意；K 由代数 Riccati 方程得出。需要线性模型，对该模型最优。

### Q61. How do you choose the Q and R matrices?
"I start with Bryson's rule: weight each state by one over its maximum acceptable value squared, same for inputs. Then iterate by looking at responses and actuator usage. In the ballbot analysis, I looked at how changing the weights on position, velocity, tilt and tilt rate moves the trade-off. `【待你补全：你的实际权重和迭代】`"
中文：从 Bryson 法则起步：每个状态权重取其最大可接受值平方的倒数，输入同理；再按响应和执行器使用迭代。ballbot 分析里看各状态权重变化如何改变权衡。

### Q62. What is gain scheduling?
"When the plant changes with an operating variable, you design controllers at several points and switch or interpolate between them. In my wheel-legged robot, the variable is leg length: I schedule the LQR gains by leg length. The risk is that stability between points and during fast changes isn't guaranteed by the design itself."
中文：对象随工作变量变化时，在若干点设计控制器，在其间切换或插值。轮腿里变量是腿长，按腿长调度 LQR 增益；风险是点间与快速变化时设计本身不保证稳定。

### Q63. What is MPC?
"Model predictive control repeatedly solves an optimisation: predict the future with a model over a horizon, choose the control sequence that minimises a cost under constraints, apply the first step, and repeat with new measurements. Its strength is handling constraints explicitly. The cost is computation. I used it for vehicle tracking."
中文：MPC 反复解优化：用模型预测一个时域，在约束下选使代价最小的控制序列，应用第一步，再用新测量重复。强项是显式处理约束，代价是计算量；我用于车辆跟踪。

### Q64. What is sliding-mode control?
"You define a sliding surface, for example a combination of the error and its derivative, and use a switching control law to drive the state to the surface and keep it there. It is robust to bounded disturbances and parameter changes, but switching causes chattering, so people soften it with a boundary layer. I used it in the launcher."
中文：定义滑模面（如误差与其导数的组合），用切换律驱动状态到面上并保持；对有界扰动和参数变化鲁棒，但切换带来抖振，常用边界层软化。我用在发射机构。

### Q65. What is feedforward and when do you use it?
"Feedforward adds a control term computed from a model of a known disturbance or reference, before the error appears. I used it for centre-of-mass gravity compensation in the ballbot, gravity compensation of the leader arm, and force feedforward in the leg-length loop. Feedback then only has to deal with what the model misses."
中文：前馈按已知扰动或参考的模型，在误差出现前加控制项。用于 ballbot 质心重力补偿、leader 臂重力补偿、腿长环力前馈；反馈只需处理模型漏掉的部分。

### Q66. What is an inverted pendulum and how do you linearise it?
"A pendulum balanced upright is unstable. Its nonlinear dynamics involve sine and cosine of the angle. Around the upright point, I take a first-order Taylor expansion — sin(θ) ≈ θ, cos(θ) ≈ 1 — to get a linear model ẋ = Ax + Bu, which is what LQR needs. It's valid for small angles."
中文：直立的摆不稳定，非线性动力学含角度的正弦余弦。在直立点一阶展开（sinθ≈θ，cosθ≈1）得线性模型 ẋ=Ax+Bu，LQR 所需；仅小角度有效。

### Q67. What is a Kalman filter in simple terms?
"It estimates the state by combining a prediction from a model with a measurement, weighing each by its uncertainty. When the measurement is noisy, it trusts the prediction more; when the model is poor, it trusts the measurement more. The weight is the Kalman gain. The EKF linearises a nonlinear model at each step."
中文：它把模型预测与测量按不确定性加权结合来估计状态；测量噪声大就更信预测，模型差就更信测量，权重即卡尔曼增益；EKF 在每步对非线性模型线性化。

### Q68. What is a complementary filter?
"It combines a gyro, accurate over short times but drifting, with an accelerometer, noisy but drift-free for gravity direction. A low-pass on the accelerometer and a high-pass on the gyro give a stable attitude estimate. Mahony's filter does this in a way that feeds the error back to correct the gyro bias."
中文：结合陀螺（短时精确但漂移）和加速度计（噪声大但重力方向无漂移）：对加速度计低通、对陀螺高通得到稳定姿态；Mahony 滤波把误差反馈修正陀螺偏置。

### Q69. What is AMCL?
"Adaptive Monte Carlo Localisation is a particle filter: it keeps many pose hypotheses (particles), weights them by how well the LiDAR scan matches the map, resamples, and adapts the number of particles. It can handle global localisation and ambiguous situations, and it provides a pose in the map frame."
中文：自适应蒙特卡洛定位是粒子滤波：保留大量位姿假设，按激光与地图匹配度加权，重采样并自适应粒子数；可处理全局定位与歧义，提供地图系下位姿。

### Q70. What is the difference between global and local planners?
"A global planner finds a feasible path across the whole known map, like my Hybrid-A*. A local planner works in a small window around the robot on the latest costmap and adjusts to nearby obstacles, like TEB. The controller then tracks the local result, in my case with MPC."
中文：全局规划在整个已知地图上找可行路径（如 Hybrid-A*）；局部规划在机器人周围小窗口、最新代价地图上调整以避近处障碍（如 TEB）；控制器跟踪局部结果（如 MPC）。

### Q71. What is inverse kinematics, and what is a singularity?
"IK computes joint angles for a desired end-effector pose. A singularity is a configuration where the Jacobian loses rank, so some motions are impossible and others need huge joint velocities. The standard ways to handle them include damping, constraints and avoiding those configurations."
中文：IK 由期望末端位姿求关节角；奇异是雅可比失秩的构型，某些运动不可能、另一些需要极大关节速度；处理方式包括阻尼、约束、避开。

### Q72. What is a Jacobian?
"It's the matrix that maps joint velocities to end-effector velocity: v = J(q) q̇. Its transpose maps end-effector forces to joint torques: τ = Jᵀ F. I use the second relation in the wheel-legged robot's VMC to turn support force into joint torques."
中文：雅可比把关节速度映射为末端速度 v=J(q)q̇；其转置把末端力映射为关节力矩 τ=JᵀF。轮腿 VMC 用后者把支撑力变成关节力矩。

### Q73. What is differential-drive kinematics?
"With wheel separation b, the left and right wheel speeds are v_L = v − ωb/2 and v_R = v + ωb/2, where v is forward speed and ω the turn rate. Odometry goes the other way: v = (v_L + v_R)/2 and ω = (v_R − v_L)/b, which I integrate for the pose."
中文：轮距 b 时，v_L=v−ωb/2，v_R=v+ωb/2（v 前进速度，ω 转向率）；里程计反向：v=(v_L+v_R)/2，ω=(v_R−v_L)/b，积分得位姿。

### Q74. What is CAN bus and why is it used in robots?
"CAN is a robust serial bus where multiple nodes share two wires. Messages have identifiers; arbitration lets higher-priority messages win without collisions. It's noise-robust and has error detection, which suits motors and sensors in a robot. I used it in P3 with the STM32F407 and FDCAN with the STM32H7."
中文：CAN 是鲁棒的串行总线，多节点共享两线；报文有标识符，仲裁使高优先级报文无冲突获胜；抗噪、有错误检测，适合机器人的电机与传感器。

### Q75. What is FreeRTOS, and how did you use it?
"FreeRTOS is a real-time operating system for microcontrollers. It schedules tasks by priority, with queues, semaphores and timers for communication. I used it on the STM32F407 and STM32H7 to run control loops in periodic tasks and communication tasks. `【待你补全：你的任务划分、周期与优先级】`"
中文：FreeRTOS 是微控制器实时操作系统，按优先级调度任务，用队列、信号量、定时器通信。我在 STM32F407/H7 上用周期任务运行控制回路与通信任务。

### Q76. What is the difference between a process and a thread?
"A process has its own address space; a thread shares the address space with other threads in the same process. Threads are lighter to create and switch, but sharing memory needs synchronisation, such as mutexes. Processes are better isolated. In ROS, nodes are typically separate processes."
中文：进程有独立地址空间，线程共享同进程地址空间；线程创建切换更轻，但共享内存需同步（互斥量）；进程隔离更好。ROS 的节点通常是独立进程。

### Q77. What is ROS2, and how is it different from ROS1?
"ROS2 is a robotics middleware framework. Compared to ROS1, it uses DDS for communication, has no central master, supports real-time-friendly designs and quality-of-service settings, and works on more platforms. In my work, I mapped ROS/ROS2 commands to drivers at Kaihong and used ROS2 with CAN at Future Power."
中文：ROS2 是机器人中间件框架；与 ROS1 相比，用 DDS 通信、无中心 master、支持 QoS 与更实时友好的设计、平台更广。我在 Kaihong 做 ROS/ROS2 指令映射，在未来动力用 ROS2+CAN。

### Q78. What is YOLO and what does INT8 quantisation do?
"YOLO is a one-stage object detector: one network pass predicts boxes and classes. INT8 quantisation maps weights and activations from floating point to 8-bit integers, which cuts memory and compute, at the price of some accuracy that must be checked with calibration data. I deployed YOLOv5 as INT8 on an RK3588 using RKNN."
中文：YOLO 是单阶段检测器，一次前向预测框与类别；INT8 量化把权重与激活从浮点映射为 8 位整数，减少内存与计算，代价是需用校准数据检查的精度损失。我用 RKNN 在 RK3588 部署 INT8 YOLOv5。

### Q79. What is LangGraph?
"It's a framework for building agent workflows as graphs: nodes are steps, edges are transitions, and a shared state flows through them, with conditional branches and loops. I used it for the gardening decision chain, with a rule-based fast path next to the model-based nodes."
中文：把 agent 工作流建为图的框架：节点是步骤，边是转移，共享状态流经，支持条件分支与循环。我用于园艺决策链，在模型节点旁加规则快速路径。

### Q80. What is the difference between supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning?
"Supervised learning learns from labelled examples, like YOLO from labelled images. Unsupervised learning finds structure without labels, like clustering. Reinforcement learning learns a policy by interacting with an environment and receiving rewards. My own practice is mostly in supervised models used for perception; RL is something I want to study properly."
中文：监督学习从带标签样本学（如 YOLO）；无监督在无标签时找结构（如聚类）；强化学习通过与环境交互获取奖励学策略。我的实践多是用于感知的监督模型；RL 想系统学习。

## E. 行为问题（Q81–Q93）

### Q81. Tell me about a time you failed.
"In the navigation project, my localisation showed drift and pose jumps, and I spent a long time adjusting the filter, assuming the algorithm was wrong. The real cause was inconsistent timing, frames and motion direction across sensors. I learned to check the data before the algorithm. Since then I always start with consistency checks, and that became part of my state-management work at Kaihong."
中文：导航项目里定位漂移和位姿跳变，我花很久调滤波器，以为算法错；真因是各传感器时间、坐标系、运动方向不一致。我学会先查数据再查算法，此后总从一致性检查开始，也成为 Kaihong 状态管理工作的一部分。

### Q82. Tell me about a conflict with a teammate.
"`【待你补全：真实案例】` A template: in the RoboMaster team, a disagreement about interfaces between control and another module. I listed what each side needed, proposed a simple interface with clear units and update rate, and we tested it together. The outcome was a clearer interface and a better relationship."
中文：模板：战队里控制与另一模块在接口上有分歧；我列出各方需求，提出带明确单位与更新率的简单接口，一起测试，结果接口更清晰、关系更好。（务必换成真实案例。）

### Q83. Tell me about a time you led.
"In the RoboMaster team I took ownership of the hero robot's three control loops: I coordinated with the mechanical and vision teammates about what they needed from the loops, and I kept the launcher iteration going until it was stable. `【待你补全：是否有带人/协调的具体经历】`"
中文：战队里我负责英雄机器人三条回路，与机械、视觉队友协调需求，推进发射机构迭代到稳定。（补全带人经历。）

### Q84. How do you handle tight deadlines?
"I prioritise what the system needs to run safely first, then improve. In competition, I'd get a working PID first, then iterate. I also keep things testable, so I can find faults quickly. And I tell my team early if I see a risk."
中文：先保证系统能安全运行，再改进；比赛中先做出可用 PID 再迭代；保持可测试便于快速定位故障；见风险及早告知团队。

### Q85. How do you handle criticism?
"I treat it as data. In my tuning work the robot gives feedback all the time, and in review, I try to ask what the person observed. If the criticism is right, I fix it and note what I'll do differently; if I disagree, I explain my reasoning calmly with evidence."
中文：当作数据；调参中机器人一直在反馈，评审中我会问对方观察到什么；对则改并记下改进，不同意则带证据平静解释。

### Q86. Tell me about a time you learned something quickly.
"At Future Power I had about two and a half months to deliver VR teleoperation, teaching and base control on real hardware. I had to learn Mink and the dual-arm dynamics with KDL quickly. I started from small examples in simulation, then moved to the real chain step by step. `【待你补全：学习路径细节】`"
中文：在未来动力约两个半月内要在真机上交付 VR 遥操作、示教、底盘控制；需快速学 Mink 和 KDL 双臂动力学。先在仿真里做小例子，再逐步上真链路。

### Q87. How do you work in a team vs alone?
"In a team, I define interfaces clearly and communicate what I need. Alone, I'm disciplined with logs and notes. In P4 I worked alone and built the whole loop, while in P3, P5 and P7 I worked in teams and owned specific modules."
中文：团队中把接口定义清楚并沟通需求；独自时用日志与笔记自律。P4 独自搭建整个闭环；P3/P5/P7 在团队中负责具体模块。

### Q88. How do you prioritise when you have many tasks?
"I ask what blocks others and what blocks safety. A safety loop or a shared interface comes first, then features, then polish. On the wheel-legged robot, I fixed state consistency before tuning."
中文：问什么阻塞他人、什么关乎安全：安全回路与共享接口优先，其次功能，最后打磨。轮腿上先修状态一致再调参。

### Q89. Describe a time you took initiative.
"At AIRS the PID worked, but I wasn't satisfied with not knowing why. I took the initiative to build the four-state model and run an LQR analysis. It wasn't required, but it gave a reference for tuning and helped diagnose saturation."
中文：AIRS 里 PID 可用，但我不满足于不知道为什么，主动建四状态模型并做 LQR 分析；非必需，却给整定提供参考并帮助诊断饱和。

### Q90. How do you stay up to date?
"I read documentation and papers related to my current project, follow open-source robotics code, and try small experiments in simulation. For learning-based robotics, I'm building a reading list. `【待你补全：具体来源】`"
中文：读与当前项目相关的文档与论文，看开源机器人代码，仿真里做小实验；学习型机器人方向正在建阅读清单。

### Q91. How do you deal with ambiguity?
"I write down what I know, what I assume, and what I can test quickly. Then I test the cheapest assumption first. In the navigation project, the cause of the jumps was ambiguous, so I tested timing, frames and direction one by one."
中文：写下已知、假设、可快速测试的；先测最便宜的假设。导航里跳变原因不明，我逐一测时间、坐标系、方向。

### Q92. What is the most important lesson from your experience?
"That a control problem is often a state problem or an interface problem. Many 'bad gains' were really inconsistent states, saturated actuators or wrong modes. So I check the chain from sensors to actuators before I tune."
中文：控制问题常常是状态问题或接口问题；许多"增益不好"其实是状态不一致、执行器饱和或模式错误。所以调参前先查从传感器到执行器的链。

### Q93. How do you handle stress?
"I break the problem into small testable steps and focus on what I can control. In competition week, I made a checklist of the control loops and tested them in order. I also try to sleep and keep my routine. `【待你补全：你自己的减压方式】`"
中文：把问题拆成小的可测步骤，专注可控部分；比赛周列控制回路清单逐个测试；也注意休息和作息。

## 本章要点速记

1. 每个答案：结论句 → 两三个依据 → 一句收束；60–90 秒。
2. 动机主线：项目让我看到理论薄弱处 + AI 决策到物理动作的接口 + 大湾区产业。
3. 弱点诚实：理论深度、口语；并给出具体改进。
4. 项目答案不含 CV 以外的数字；缺的细节标 `【待你补全】`。
5. 行为题用项目真实经历：失败（P5 定位跳变）、主动（P1 LQR 分析）、团队（P3）。
6. 反问：研究方向、课程、资源、去向、与我方向相关的教师。
