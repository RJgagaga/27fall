# 模拟面试脚本：预录 AI 视频面试（5 套）

> **一句话定位**：预录视频面试没有人回应、不能打断、限时作答。本文给出 5 套模拟题（每套 5–6 题），每题有准备/作答时限、英文参考答案（含中文译文）和自评表，帮你练"一次说完、结构清楚、不超时"。

**本章学完你能……**

- 在 30 秒准备内搭好"结论—依据—收束"的骨架，并在 90 秒内说完；
- 对镜头自然讲话，不盯稿；
- 用自评表发现口头禅、超时、结构散乱等问题。

**前置知识**：[面试形式与评分维度](../01_面试总指南/01_面试形式_流程与评分维度.md)、[高频 100 问](./01_高频100问_英文速答.md)、[七个项目口述](../03_项目口述总览/01_七个项目30秒_90秒_3分钟口述.md)。

## 1. 预录视频面试的规则与策略

| 项 | 常见情况 | 你的对策 |
|---|---|---|
| 准备时间 | 约 30 秒（有的平台 0–60 秒） | 只写 3 个关键词，不写句子 |
| 作答时间 | 约 60–120 秒；超时自动截断 | 目标用满 80%，留 1 句收束 |
| 重录 | 通常不可，或次数有限 | 说错一个词继续，不要停下道歉 |
| 镜头 | 没有面试官表情 | 看镜头不看屏幕，想象对着一位教授 |
| 评分 | 内容 + 表达 + 英语（可能有 AI 辅助评分） | 结构清晰、语速稳、术语准确 |

**万能骨架（PREP）**：Point（结论）→ Reason（依据 1–2 个）→ Example（项目例子）→ Point（收束）。

**三条纪律**：

1. 开口第一句直接回答，不重复题目；
2. 数字只说 CV 里有的（6 of 156、87.63、6.5）；
3. 卡壳时用"过渡短语"：*"Let me put it this way…"*、*"The key point is…"*、*"For example, in my ballbot project…"*。

## 2. 第 1 套：自我介绍与动机（准备 30 秒 / 作答 90 秒）

### 1.1 Introduce yourself.
**关键词**：thread / three layers / master's。
"I'm Yongyi Lai, a computer science student at Shenzhen Technology University, ranked sixth out of 156. My projects follow one thread: stable control loops, a state the robot can trust, and safe paths from decisions to actuators. I started with the RoboMaster hero robot, then balance control at AIRS and on a wheel-legged robot, navigation on a car-like robot, VR teleoperation at Future Power, and now a robotics platform at Kaihong. I'm applying for a master's to build the theory behind this work."
中文：我是赖咏怡，深圳技术大学计算机专业，排名 6/156。主线：稳定回路、可信状态、从决策到执行器的安全通路。依次做过英雄机器人、AIRS 与轮腿平衡、车式导航、未来动力 VR 遥操作、Kaihong 平台。申请硕士是为补背后的理论。

### 1.2 Why do you want to study this programme?
**关键词**：theory / learning / Greater Bay Area。
"I want to turn my practice into understanding. I can tune and deploy, but I want to study optimisation, estimation and learning properly. Your programme combines robotics with AI, and it is close to the Greater Bay Area industry where I want to work as an algorithm engineer. `【按学校补一条具体课程或教授】`"
中文：我想把实践变成理解；能调能部署，但想系统学优化、估计、学习；贵项目结合机器人与 AI，且靠近我想工作的大湾区产业。

### 1.3 What is your biggest achievement?
**关键词**：ballbot / model / explain。
"My biggest achievement is the ballbot balance control. I deployed a cascaded PID, and then I went back to the model: a linearised four-state pendulum with LQR, to explain my gains and diagnose actuator saturation. It taught me to combine practice with theory."
中文：最大成就是 ballbot 平衡：部署级联 PID，再回到线性化四状态模型，用 LQR 解释增益并诊断饱和；教会我实践与理论结合。

### 1.4 What is your weakness?
**关键词**：theory / spoken English / plan。
"Theory depth. I usually build first and study the maths afterwards. For example, I've used MPC and sliding-mode control, but I haven't studied their stability theory. I'm addressing it with the courses I have, and a master's is the next step. I also practise spoken English daily."
中文：理论深度。我先做后学数学，如用过 MPC 与滑模但没学过稳定性理论；用已有课程补，硕士是下一步；口语每天练。

### 1.5 Where do you see yourself in five years?
**关键词**：algorithm engineer / own a stack。
"In a robotics team in the Greater Bay Area, owning part of the algorithm stack, such as navigation or control, and able to apply recent learning-based methods safely."
中文：在大湾区机器人团队负责一块算法栈（导航或控制），并能安全应用较新的学习方法。

**自评重点**：第一句是否直接答题？有没有超时？有没有 "um/uh"？

## 3. 第 2 套：控制项目（准备 30 秒 / 作答 90 秒）

### 2.1 Describe your balance control work on the ballbot.
**关键词**：cascade / bounded / LQR analysis / CoM feedforward。
"At AIRS I built a cascaded PID: a position loop gives a bounded attitude reference to the attitude loop, and the output is allocated to three wheels. Then I modelled a four-state inverted pendulum and used LQR to analyse the weights, as a reference for tuning and saturation diagnosis. I also added centre-of-mass feedforward from the arm joints and verified it by toggling it on and off."
中文：外环位置给内环有界姿态参考，输出分配到三轮；建四状态倒立摆，LQR 分析权重作整定与饱和诊断参考；加臂关节质心前馈，开关对比验证。

### 2.2 Why gain-scheduled LQR on the wheel-legged robot?
**关键词**：leg length / dynamics change / torque。
"Because the leg length changes the effective pendulum length, so one fixed gain is a compromise. I scheduled LQR gains by leg length, and converted balance and motion errors into wheel-hub and hip torques."
中文：腿长改变等效摆长，固定增益是折中；按腿长调度 LQR 增益，把平衡与运动误差转成轮毂与髋力矩。

### 2.3 What is virtual model control?
**关键词**：virtual leg / Jacobian / support force。
"I compute a virtual leg length and posture from the closed-chain kinematics. Then I specify a support force and a pitch torque on the virtual leg, and the Jacobian maps them to joint torques. A leg-length PID with force feedforward handles extension."
中文：由闭链运动学算虚拟腿长与姿态；在虚拟腿上指定支撑力与俯仰力矩，雅可比映射到关节力矩；腿长 PID + 力前馈负责伸缩。

### 2.4 How did you debug balance problems on the real robot?
**关键词**：three causes / consistency / then tune。
"I traced anomalies to three causes: state inconsistency, torque limits and ground-contact transitions. I fixed the cause first, then tuned parameters on the robot. The order matters, because bad states look like bad gains."
中文：把异常追到三类：状态不一致、力矩限制、触地切换；先修根因再调参；顺序重要，因为坏状态看起来像坏增益。

### 2.5 Tell me about your launcher control in RoboMaster.
**关键词**：friction wheel / feeder / PID → variable-gain → sliding mode。
"The launcher has friction-wheel speed loops and a feeder with a position–velocity double loop. I iterated from PID to variable-gain PID to sliding-mode control with muzzle-velocity feedback, aiming at stable continuous firing, because the load changes every time a projectile passes."
中文：摩擦轮速度环与拨弹位置-速度双环；PID→变增益→滑模，初速反馈，目标稳定连发，因为每次弹丸通过负载都变。

### 2.6 When would you choose PID, LQR or MPC?
**关键词**：model / coupling / constraints。
"PID for simple loops with unknown models; LQR for coupled states with a decent model, like balance; MPC when constraints are central, like steering and speed limits in vehicle tracking."
中文：简单回路、模型不明用 PID；状态耦合、模型尚可（如平衡）用 LQR；约束为核心（如车辆转向速度限制）用 MPC。

## 4. 第 3 套：导航与估计（准备 30 秒 / 作答 90 秒）

### 3.1 Describe your navigation stack.
**关键词**：EKF/AMCL / Hybrid-A* / TEB / MPC。
"On a car-like robot: EKF and AMCL fuse IMU, wheel encoders and 2D LiDAR for localisation; Hybrid-A* plans a feasible global path under footprint and turning-radius constraints; TEB optimises local trajectories; MPC tracks them. It achieved autonomous navigation on the real vehicle."
中文：EKF/AMCL 融合定位；Hybrid-A* 在轮廓与转弯半径约束下规划；TEB 局部优化；MPC 跟踪；真车自主导航。

### 3.2 What caused pose jumps and how did you fix them?
**关键词**：timing / frames / direction。
"The jumps came from inconsistent timing, frames and motion direction across sensors, not from the filter. I aligned them, and the output became a continuous vehicle state."
中文：来自各传感器时间、坐标系、运动方向不一致而非滤波器；对齐后得到连续状态。

### 3.3 Why Hybrid-A* rather than A*?
**关键词**：heading / turning radius。
"A car has heading and a minimum turning radius. Hybrid-A* includes heading in the search with feasible motions, so the path is drivable."
中文：车有航向与最小转弯半径；Hybrid-A* 把航向放进搜索并用可行运动，路径可行驶。

### 3.4 What is MPC doing in your tracker?
**关键词**：receding horizon / constraints。
"It predicts with a kinematic model, optimises under position, heading, speed and steering constraints, applies the first step and repeats."
中文：用运动学模型预测，在位置/航向/速度/转向约束下优化，应用第一步并重复。

### 3.5 Explain singularity in VR teleoperation.
**关键词**：Jacobian rank / velocity amplification。
"Near a singularity the Jacobian loses rank, so a small end-effector motion demands huge joint velocity. My constrained IK with MuJoCo and Mink suppresses this amplification."
中文：奇异点附近雅可比失秩，小末端运动要求巨大关节速度；MuJoCo/Mink 约束 IK 抑制该放大。

### 3.6 How did you build wheel-speed control?
**关键词**：differential drive / encoder PID / odometry。
"Body velocity commands become wheel targets through differential-drive kinematics, a PID loop closes on encoder feedback, and the wheel motion is integrated into odometry for teleoperated and autonomous modes."
中文：车体速度经差速运动学成为轮目标，编码器反馈 PID 闭环，轮运动并入里程计，用于遥操作与自主。

## 5. 第 4 套：AI 与系统（准备 30 秒 / 作答 90 秒）

### 4.1 Tell me about your gardening system.
"I independently built a loop from vision to decision to irrigation: YOLOv5 INT8 on RKNN on an RK3588 produces observations with confidence and timestamps; a LangGraph agent decides, with a rule-based fast path; a Hi3861 pump state machine has independent start and stop thresholds and manual priority."
中文：见 P4 口述。

### 4.2 Why does an AI agent not control the pump directly?
"Because the device must stay safe if the agent is wrong or offline, and a human must be able to override. The agent issues recommendations or constrained requests, and the device has its own thresholds."
中文：agent 错误或离线时设备须安全，人须可覆盖；agent 只发建议或受约束请求，设备有自己阈值。

### 4.3 What is the safe-execution chain at Kaihong?
"Action proposal, policy check, execution, state feedback. A plan never goes straight to the robot."
中文：提案、策略检查、执行、状态反馈；计划不直达机器人。

### 4.4 What is a unified capability model?
"A common C++ description of sensors, actuators and controllers, so motion-control code uses capabilities rather than specific drivers and can be reused across hardware."
中文：C++ 统一描述，运动控制代码用能力而非具体驱动，跨硬件复用。

### 4.5 What do you mean by a consistent robot state?
"Same moment, same frame, valid values. I standardised timestamps, frames and validity checks for the motion and task layers."
中文：同一时刻、同一坐标系、有效值；为运动与任务层标准化时间戳、坐标系、有效性检查。

### 4.6 How do you see AI in robotics?
"Learning can help perception and decisions, but the interface to the actuator needs structure and safety. I want to study that boundary and the theory of learning-based control."
中文：学习可助感知与决策，但与执行器的接口需结构与安全；想研究该边界与学习控制理论。

## 6. 第 5 套：行为与综合（准备 30 秒 / 作答 90 秒）

### 5.1 Tell me about a failure.
"In navigation, I spent a long time tuning the filter before realising the inputs were inconsistent in time, frame and direction. I learned to check data before algorithms."
中文：导航里花很久调滤波器，才发现输入的时间/坐标系/方向不一致；学会先查数据再查算法。

### 5.2 Tell me about teamwork.
"In RoboMaster I owned three loops that depend on teammates' mechanical, vision and referee-interface parts, so I agreed on interfaces and tested together. `【补全具体事例】` We won third prize at the national final."
中文：战队里三条回路依赖队友的机械、视觉等部分，先约定接口再联调；获全国三等奖。

### 5.3 How do you handle pressure?
"I break the work into testable steps and fix safety first. In competition I tested the loops in order."
中文：拆成可测步骤，安全优先；比赛中按顺序测回路。

### 5.4 Why should we choose you?
"I have hands-on experience from control to navigation to platform software, delivered on real hardware, and I know what I still need to learn."
中文：从控制到导航到平台软件的真机经验，且清楚自己还需学什么。

### 5.5 What will you contribute to the group?
"Practical robot experience, careful debugging, and willingness to work on whichever platform the group needs."
中文：实践经验、细致排错，愿意在课题组所需平台上工作。

### 5.6 Anything to add?
"Thank you. I would like to emphasise that I'm honest about my gaps, and I learn fast by building and then explaining."
中文：感谢；强调我对不足坦诚，通过先做再解释快速学习。

## 7. 自评表与计时训练法

### 7.1 自评表（每题打 1–5 分）

| 维度 | 1 分 | 3 分 | 5 分 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 首句直答 | 重复题目、绕 | 有结论但拖 | 第一句即答案 |
| 结构 | 散 | 有依据无收束 | 结论—依据—例子—收束 |
| 事实准确 | 有编造数字 | 偶有模糊 | 全部对应 CV |
| 用时 | 超时被截断 | 剩余 >30% | 用满 80–95% |
| 英语流畅 | 频繁停顿 | 偶有停顿 | 平稳、自然 |
| 镜头 | 一直看稿 | 偶尔看镜头 | 稳定看镜头 |

### 7.2 训练法

1. 手机录屏，计时 30 秒准备 + 90 秒作答；
2. 回看时标记口头禅与超时；
3. 同一题连录 3 次，第 3 次不看稿；
4. 每套练完把得分填表，目标：所有维度 ≥ 4。

## 本章要点速记

1. PREP 骨架：结论—依据—例子—收束。
2. 准备 30 秒只写关键词；作答用满 80%。
3. 只说 CV 里有的数字；说错继续，不道歉停顿。
4. 看镜头，语速 110–120 词/分钟。
5. 五套覆盖：自我介绍动机、控制、导航估计、AI 系统、行为综合。

## 自测题

1. 作答 90 秒时，你如何分配时间？
2. 卡壳时用哪三个过渡短语？
3. 第 2 套 2.4 题的"三类根因"是什么？
4. 为什么首句要直接答题？
5. 你的自评表里最低的维度是什么，下一轮怎么改？

**答案要点**：1. 结论 10 秒、依据 40 秒、例子 30 秒、收束 10 秒；3. 状态不一致、力矩限制、触地切换。
