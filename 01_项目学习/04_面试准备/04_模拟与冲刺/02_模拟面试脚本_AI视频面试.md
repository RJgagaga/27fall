# 模拟面试脚本：预录 AI 视频面试（5 套 × 6 题）

> **一句话定位**：预录视频面试没有人回应、不能打断、限时作答。本文给出 5 套共 30 道模拟题；每题都有英文题面与中文译文、准备 30 秒要写下的关键词、90 秒英文参考答案与中文译文、单题自评行和常见翻车点，帮你练到"一次说完、结构清楚、不超时、数字不出错"。

**本章学完你能……**

- 在 30 秒准备内只写关键词，搭好"结论—依据—例子—收束"的骨架，并在 90 秒内说完；
- 对镜头自然讲话，不盯稿，不在口误后停下道歉；
- 用自评表按"内容 / 结构 / 语速 / 发音 / 时间"五个维度发现自己的问题，并逐轮改进；
- 把每个项目的数字稳定在 CV 与各项目文档的统一口径上（说 roughly / in my notes / in our design，不报精确到小数的"实测"）。

**前置知识**：[面试形式与评分维度](../01_面试总指南/01_面试形式_流程与评分维度.md)、[高频 100 问](./01_高频100问_英文速答.md)、[七个项目口述](../03_项目口述总览/01_七个项目30秒_90秒_3分钟口述.md)、[英文自我介绍多版本](../01_面试总指南/02_英文自我介绍_多版本.md)。

## 1. 预录视频面试的规则、时间表与万能骨架

| 项 | 常见情况 | 你的对策 |
|---|---|---|
| 准备时间 | 约 30 秒（有的平台 0–60 秒，有的无准备） | 只写 3 个关键词，不写句子；无准备时用心里默念三个词 |
| 作答时间 | 约 60–120 秒；超时自动截断 | 目标用满 80%–95%，最后留一句收束 |
| 重录 | 通常不可，或次数有限 | 说错一个词就继续，不要停下道歉 |
| 镜头 | 没有面试官表情反馈 | 看镜头不看屏幕，想象对着一位听得懂的教授 |
| 评分 | 内容 + 表达 + 英语（可能有机器辅助评分，如语速、停顿、关键词覆盖） | 结构清晰、语速稳、术语准确、关键词出现 |
| 设备 | 笔记本摄像头与耳机麦克风 | 提前测光线、收音、网络，见 [面试当天检查清单](./05_面试当天检查清单.md) |

**万能骨架（PREP）**：Point（结论）→ Reason（依据 1–2 个）→ Example（项目例子）→ Point（收束）。

**90 秒时间分配**：结论 10 秒 + 依据 35 秒 + 例子 35 秒 + 收束 10 秒。英语口语常速约 110–130 词/分钟，所以 90 秒的合适篇幅是 **190–260 词**，本文的参考答案都落在这个范围。

**四条纪律**：

1. 开口第一句直接回答，不重复题目，不说 "That's a great question"；
2. 数字只说 CV 里有的（6 of 156、top 4%、87.63、IELTS 6.5、各奖项），项目内部数值只说各项目文档"实机细节自查表"里的统一取值，并用 *roughly* 引出；
3. 动词有分寸：你"负责 / 独立做"的才说 I built / I was responsible for，团队成果说 our team；
4. 卡壳时用过渡短语：*"Let me put it this way…"*、*"The key point is…"*、*"For example, in my ballbot project…"*。

> **面试要点**：预录面试里"停下来想"和"说错后道歉"最伤印象。宁可用一个过渡短语换取 2 秒思考，也不要沉默。

**如何使用每题的"自评行"**：录完回放后，对五个维度各打 1–5 分，填在题末的 `自评` 行里（可直接在文件里改，也可抄到第 7 节的汇总表）。

## 2. 第 1 套：自我介绍与动机

### 1.1 Introduce yourself.

**题面中文**：请做自我介绍。

**准备 30 秒要点**：thread（一条主线）/ three layers（三层）/ master's（为何读硕）。

**90 秒参考答案**：

"I'm Yongyi Lai, a computer science student at Shenzhen Technology University. I'm ranked sixth out of 156, in the top four percent, with an average of 87.63. My projects follow one thread: control loops that stay stable, a robot state that can be trusted, and a safe path from a decision to an actuator. I started with the RoboMaster hero robot, where I was responsible for the gimbal, launcher and chassis control loops, and our team won third prize at the national final. Then at AIRS I worked on balance control for a three-omni-wheel ballbot with a dual-arm upper body, and on a wheel-legged robot in the lab. Next came a car-like robot with a full navigation chain, from localisation to MPC tracking. At Future Power I built VR teleoperation, leader-follower teaching and wheel-speed control on real hardware. And now, at Kaihong, I work on device abstraction, state management and a safe-execution chain for a multi-robot platform. I can tune and deploy controllers, but I want to understand the theory behind them: optimisation, estimation and learning. That is why I'm applying for a master's, and I hope to work as a robotics algorithm engineer in the Greater Bay Area afterwards."

**中文译文**：我是赖咏怡，深圳技术大学计算机专业学生，排名 156 人中第 6（前 4%），均分 87.63。我的项目有一条主线：稳定的控制回路、可信的机器人状态、从决策到执行器的安全通路。我从 RoboMaster 英雄机器人开始，负责云台、发射、底盘三类控制回路，团队获全国总决赛三等奖；随后在 AIRS 做带双臂上身的三全向轮球形机器人平衡控制，并在实验室做轮腿机器人；接着做车式机器人的完整导航链（从定位到 MPC 跟踪）；在未来动力，我在真机上完成 VR 遥操作、主从示教和轮速控制；现在在深开鸿做多机器人平台的设备抽象、状态管理和安全执行链。我能调试和部署控制器，但想理解背后的理论：优化、估计和学习。这就是我申请硕士的原因，毕业后希望在大湾区做机器人算法工程师。

**自评**：内容 □/5　结构 □/5　语速 □/5　发音 □/5　时间 □/5

**常见翻车点**：① 按时间顺序逐个念项目，没有主线，一分钟后听众就丢了；② 把"团队获奖"说成"我获奖"；③ 把项目名都念一遍导致超时，应只点到名字和一个动词；④ 把 Kaihong 说成"已结束"，它是在职（Jun 2026 – Present）。

### 1.2 Why do you want a master's degree now, and not after working for a few years?

**题面中文**：为什么现在读硕士，而不是工作几年再读？

**准备 30 秒要点**：practice first / theory gap / time window。

**90 秒参考答案**：

"Because my practice has run ahead of my theory, and I'd like to close that gap while I can still study full-time. In the last three years I have deployed cascaded PID on a ballbot, gain-scheduled LQR on a wheel-legged robot, and MPC on a real car-like vehicle. In each case I could make it work, but I often could only explain part of why. For example, at AIRS I built the controller first, and only afterwards went back to the model, linearised a four-state inverted pendulum, and used LQR to understand how the weights trade stability against control effort. That analysis helped me explain the gains I had tuned and find actuator saturation. It was useful, but it came late. I want to learn that way of thinking systematically: optimisation, state estimation, and the basics of learning-based control, which I have so far used mainly through practice. If I went to work first, I would probably become faster at building, but I would meet these questions under deadline pressure, with less time to study them. A master's gives me a focused year or two to do it, with supervision, and then I can go back to industry with stronger foundations. I am not leaving practice behind. I'm planning to use my engineering experience as the starting point for my projects and coursework."

**中文译文**：因为我的实践走在了理论前面，想趁还能全职学习时补上。三年里我部署过球形机器人的级联 PID、轮腿机器人的增益调度 LQR、车式机器人的 MPC，每次都能让它跑起来，却往往只能解释一部分原因。比如在 AIRS，我先做控制器，之后才回到模型，线性化四状态倒立摆，用 LQR 理解权重如何在稳定性与控制量之间权衡；这帮我解释了调出来的增益并找到执行器饱和，但来得晚了。我想系统学习这种思维：优化、状态估计，以及目前主要靠实践接触的基于学习的控制。若先工作，我会更会"做"，但会在交付压力下遇到这些问题，没时间学。硕士给我一两年专注、有导师指导的时间，之后带着更扎实的基础回到产业。我不是离开实践，而是以工程经验为起点去做课题和课程。

**自评**：内容 □/5　结构 □/5　语速 □/5　发音 □/5　时间 □/5

**常见翻车点**：① 说"我想逃避工作"类的负面动机；② 贬低实践或贬低学校；③ 只讲"想要学历"，不讲具体想补什么；④ 把"LQR 部署在 ballbot 上"说出口——CV 口径是 LQR 用于**分析**，部署的是 PID 级联。

### 1.3 Why are you interested in our programme?

**题面中文**：你为什么对我们的项目感兴趣？

**准备 30 秒要点**：courses / labs / location。

**90 秒参考答案**：

"Three things attract me. First, the curriculum. Your programme combines robotics with AI, and that is exactly the combination I need: I have the hands-on side of control and navigation, and I want the learning and optimisation side, so courses on machine learning, optimisation and robot perception would fit my gaps. Second, the research environment. I'd like to join a group that works on real robots, because my own strength is building on hardware, and I want to do research where algorithms are tested on a physical platform, not only in simulation. Third, the location. I want to work in the Greater Bay Area afterwards, which has many robotics companies and research institutes, and I have already interned at AIRS, Future Power and Kaihong in Shenzhen. Studying close to that community would let me keep learning from engineers and mentors there. Before this interview I also read your programme description and some faculty pages, and I'd be glad to discuss which directions are the best match with my background, for instance control and navigation for mobile robots, or safe execution for robot platforms. **中文译文**：三点吸引我。其一是课程：贵项目把机器人和 AI 结合，正是我所需：我有控制与导航的动手经验，想补学习与优化这一侧，机器学习、优化、机器人感知课程正对我的缺口。其二是科研环境：我想加入做真实机器人的课题组，因为我的强项是在硬件上做东西，想做能在实体平台上检验算法的研究，而不止仿真。其三是地点：我毕业后想留在大湾区，这里机器人企业和研究机构多，我已在深圳的 AIRS、未来动力、深开鸿实习过；离这个圈子近，我可以继续向工程师和导师学习。面试前我读过项目介绍和部分教师主页，也乐意讨论哪些方向与我的背景最匹配，例如移动机器人控制与导航，或机器人平台的安全执行。我也明白硕士还要学会做研究，想在好导师指导下培养这项能力。

### 1.5 What are your strengths and your weaknesses?

**题面中文**：你的优点和缺点是什么？

**准备 30 秒要点**：strength = debug on hardware / weakness = theory depth / plan。

**90 秒参考答案**：

"My main strength is careful debugging on real hardware. When a robot misbehaves, I try to separate the causes before touching any gains. For example, on the wheel-legged robot, I traced balance problems to three families: state inconsistency, torque limits, and ground-contact transitions. In one case, the robot crept forward slowly after being lifted and put down. The cause was a small pitch offset of roughly 1.5 degrees and sensor data that were not sampled at the same moment, not the gains. After I calibrated the offset and took one snapshot of the state per control cycle, the drift over thirty seconds was below three centimetres. My second strength is that I finish what I start: in the RoboMaster team I kept iterating on the launcher until continuous firing was stable. My weakness is theory depth. I tend to build first and study the mathematics afterwards. I have used MPC and sliding-mode control successfully, but I have not studied their stability theory in depth. I'm working on this in three ways: I use the control courses I have taken as a base, I write short derivation notes after each project, for instance the four-state LQR analysis at AIRS, and I plan to take optimisation and advanced control courses in the master's. I think knowing this gap, and having a plan, is better than hiding it."

**中文译文**：我的主要优点是在真机上细致排错：机器人出问题时先分离原因再动增益。例如轮腿机器人，我把平衡问题追到三类：状态不一致、力矩限制、触地切换。有一次吊起放下后机器人缓慢向前爬行，原因是约 1.5° 的俯仰偏置，以及传感器数据不是同一时刻采样的，而不是增益；标定偏置并改为每个控制周期取一次状态快照后，30 秒漂移小于 3 厘米。第二个优点是有始有终：在战队里我一直迭代发射机构，直到连发稳定。缺点是理论深度：我倾向先做后学数学；MPC 和滑模我用成功了，但没深入学过它们的稳定性理论。我从三方面补：以已修的控制课为基础，每个项目后写简短推导笔记（如 AIRS 的四状态 LQR 分析），并计划在硕士阶段修优化和高等控制课。知道缺口并有计划，比掩饰更好。

### 1.6 What is your biggest achievement so far?

**题面中文**：到目前为止你最大的成就是什么？

**准备 30 秒要点**：ballbot / model back / explain & diagnose。

**90 秒参考答案**：

"My biggest achievement, from a technical point of view, is the balance control of the ballbot at AIRS, and I'd like to explain why it matters to me. A ballbot balances on a ball, and in our case a three-omni-wheel base, like an inverted pendulum that can fall in any direction. On top of that, it has a dual-arm upper body, so when the arms move, the centre of mass changes. I designed and deployed a cascaded PID controller: the position loop gives a bounded attitude reference to the attitude loop, and the output is allocated to the three wheels through the kinematics. The chassis stayed balanced while both arms moved along fixed trajectories. I also added centre-of-mass feedforward estimated from the arm joint states, and I verified it by switching it on and off with the same arm trajectory. In my notes, the tilt peak dropped from about 0.06 to about 0.03 radians. What I'm proudest of is the next step. I went back to the model, linearised a four-state inverted pendulum, and used LQR to analyse how the weights trade stability against control effort. That analysis explained my gains and helped me diagnose actuator saturation. It taught me that practice and theory should support each other, and it is the kind of work I want to do more of."

**中文译文**：技术上我最大的成就是 AIRS 的球形机器人平衡控制，我想讲讲它为何重要。球形机器人（此处为三全向轮底座）像一个能向任意方向倒的倒立摆，加上双臂上身，手臂一动质心就变。我设计并部署了级联 PID：位置环给姿态环有界的姿态参考，输出经运动学分配到三个轮子，双臂沿固定轨迹运动时底盘保持平衡。我还加了由臂关节状态估计的质心前馈，并用同一臂轨迹开关对比验证；笔记里倾角峰值从约 0.06 降到约 0.03 rad。我最自豪的是下一步：回到模型，线性化四状态倒立摆，用 LQR 分析权重如何在稳定性与控制量间权衡，它解释了我的增益并帮我诊断执行器饱和。它让我明白实践与理论应互相支撑，这也是我想多做的工作。

**自评**：内容 □/5　结构 □/5　语速 □/5　发音 □/5　时间 □/5

**常见翻车点**：① 说成"我部署了 LQR"，与 CV 不符；② 把"开关对比"说成"统计显著"，只说是固定轨迹下的对比；③ 把成就说成团队整体成果而没有交代自己负责的部分。

## 3. 第 2 套：控制项目（P1 / P7 / P3）

### 2.1 Walk me through your cascaded balance controller on the ballbot.

**题面中文**：请讲讲你在球形机器人上的级联平衡控制器。

**准备 30 秒要点**：outer position / bounded tilt reference / inner attitude / allocation。

**90 秒参考答案**：

"The controller has two main loops and an allocation step. The outer loop is a position loop. It looks at the position and velocity error and outputs a tilt reference, and the key point is that this reference is bounded, roughly 0.1 radians in my design. That bound matters: if the outer loop asks for a large tilt, the robot would need a large wheel command and could saturate and fall. The inner loop is an attitude loop. It uses the IMU tilt and tilt rate, and outputs a command to balance the robot around the reference. The structure is a PD law with a very small, clamped integral term to absorb a mounting offset. Finally, the output is a planar command, and the kinematics of the three omni wheels allocate it to three wheel commands. Bandwidth separation is also important: the inner loop is about six or seven times faster than the outer loop, so the outer loop sees the inner loop as nearly ideal. In my notes the outer loop is around 0.15 hertz and the inner loop around 1 hertz. These are design values, and the main idea is the structure."

### 2.3 How did you verify the centre-of-mass feedforward?

**题面中文**：你如何验证质心前馈有效？

**准备 30 秒要点**：URDF / Pinocchio / toggle / same trajectory。

**90 秒参考答案**：

"The idea is simple. ### 2.4 Explain gain-scheduled LQR and VMC on the wheel-legged robot.

**题面中文**：请解释轮腿机器人上的增益调度 LQR 与 VMC。

**准备 30 秒要点**：leg length / six states / Jacobian / torque。

**90 秒参考答案**：

"On the wheel-legged robot, leg length changes the effective pendulum length, so the dynamics change when the leg extends. One fixed gain is therefore a compromise: it is fine near the design point, and loses stability margin far from it. My solution was gain-scheduled LQR. The state has six elements: leg angle and its rate, position and velocity, and body pitch and its rate. I computed LQR gains offline for several leg lengths, and fitted each of the twelve gains with a cubic polynomial of leg length, so there are 48 coefficients, valid for roughly 0.1 to 0.3 metres, with the leg length clamped online. The LQR output is converted into wheel torque and a leg torque. Virtual model control handles the leg itself. From the closed-chain kinematics, I compute a virtual leg length and posture. Then I specify a support force and a pitch torque on the virtual leg, and the Jacobian maps them to the two hip joint torques. A leg-length PID plus a force feedforward, roughly the weight carried by each leg divided by the cosine of the leg angle, controls extension. All of this ran at 500 hertz on an STM32H7 with FreeRTOS and FDCAN. I tuned it on the robot, and the numbers I gave are my design values."

**中文译文**：轮腿机器人上，腿长改变等效摆长，所以腿伸缩时动力学改变，固定增益只是折中：设计点附近可以，远离设计点稳定裕度下降。我的方案是增益调度 LQR：状态六维（腿角及其速率、位置与速度、机身俯仰及其速率）；对若干腿长离线算 LQR 增益，把 12 个增益各用腿长的三次多项式拟合，共 48 个系数，约适用于 0.1–0.3 m，在线对腿长限幅；LQR 输出转换为轮力矩和腿力矩。腿本身用 VMC：由闭链运动学计算虚拟腿长与姿态，在虚拟腿上给定支撑力和俯仰力矩，由雅可比映射到两个髋关节力矩；腿长 PID 加力前馈（约为每条腿承重除以腿角余弦）控制伸缩。整个系统在 STM32H7 + FreeRTOS + FDCAN 上以 500 Hz 运行，在机器人上整定；给出的数字是设计取值。

### 2.5 How did your launcher control evolve from PID to sliding-mode control?

### 2.6 How did you control the chassis power on the hero robot?

### 3.2 What caused the pose jumps, and how did you fix them?

**题面中文**：位姿跳变的原因是什么？你怎么解决的？

**准备 30 秒要点**：timestamp / sign of speed / static transforms / smoothing。

**90 秒参考答案**：

"When turning and reversing, the map-to-odom transform had steps of roughly 0.2 to 0.4 metres and 5 to 10 degrees, and the steering command twitched with them. My first reaction was to tune the filter, and I lost time on that. The real cause was inconsistent inputs, in three ways. First, time: the LiDAR driver stamped each scan with its arrival time, about 60 milliseconds after the scan started, so during a turn the scan was matched against the wrong pose. I changed the stamp to the scan start time with a fixed delay compensation and synchronised the clocks, and the cross-correlation offset dropped from about 60 milliseconds to within about 5. Second, direction: the lower-level board reported wheel speed as an unsigned magnitude, so when reversing, the odometry still integrated forward, and the particle cloud spread. I parsed the speed as signed, added a gear check, and fixed the sign convention for steering. A 3 metre reverse then matched a tape measure to about 5 centimetres. Third, frames: some static transforms were inconsistent. After that I added a rate limit and a gate on the map-to-odom correction. ### 3.3 Why Hybrid-A* and not plain A*? How does it differ from TEB?

**题面中文**：为什么用 Hybrid-A* 而不是普通 A*？它和 TEB 有何区别？

**准备 30 秒要点**：heading / turning radius / continuous state / global vs local。

**90 秒参考答案**：

"Plain A* searches on a grid of positions and moves between neighbouring cells, so the path has sharp corners that a car cannot follow. A car has a heading and a minimum turning radius, and it cannot move sideways. Hybrid-A* adds the heading to the search state, and expands nodes with feasible motion primitives, such as turning left, going straight, turning right and reversing, with the continuous pose stored inside each discrete cell. So the path it finds respects the kinematics. In my configuration the grid is 0.05 metres, and the heading is split into 72 bins of 5 degrees. I also used penalties for reversing, changing direction and non-straight motion, plus an analytic expansion near the goal with Reeds-Shepp curves. The heuristic is typically the larger of a non-holonomic cost that ignores obstacles and a grid cost that ignores kinematics, so it is not globally optimal, because of discretisation, but it is feasible. TEB is different: it is a local planner. It starts from the global path and optimises a time-parameterised trajectory as an elastic band, with obstacle distance, speed, acceleration, and turning-radius terms, and it runs at about 10 hertz as obstacles change. In short, Hybrid-A* decides where to go, globally, and TEB decides how to move now, locally."

**中文译文**：普通 A* 在位置网格上搜索、在相邻格子间移动，路径有尖角，车跟不了；车有航向和最小转弯半径，不能侧移。Hybrid-A* 把航向加入搜索状态，用可行运动基元（左转、直行、右转、倒车）扩展节点，并在离散格内保存连续位姿，所以找到的路径满足运动学。我的配置里栅格 0.05 m，航向分为 72 个 5° 的桶；对倒车、换向、非直行加了惩罚，目标附近用 Reeds-Shepp 曲线做解析扩展。启发函数通常取"忽略障碍的非完整代价"与"忽略运动学的栅格代价"中较大者，所以因离散化而不全局最优，但可行。TEB 不同，它是局部规划器：从全局路径出发，把带时间参数的轨迹作为弹性带优化，目标含障碍距离、速度、加速度、转弯半径等项，随障碍变化约 10 Hz 运行。总之，Hybrid-A* 决定全局"去哪"，TEB 决定局部"现在怎么动"。

### 3.4 How does your MPC tracker work, and what happens if the solver fails?

**题面中文**：你的 MPC 跟踪器怎么工作？求解失败怎么办？

**准备 30 秒要点**：model / horizon / constraints / delay / fallback。

**90 秒参考答案**：

"At each step, the MPC predicts the vehicle motion over a short horizon with a car-like kinematic model. The state is x, y, heading and speed, and the inputs are acceleration and steering angle. It finds the best input sequence under constraints on position, heading, speed and steering, applies only the first input, and repeats at the next step. In my configuration the horizon is 15 steps of 0.1 seconds, running at 20 hertz, and the cost penalises tracking error, the input size, and the input change, with the change penalty on steering large so that the steering does not chatter. The constraints are roughly plus or minus 0.5 radians of steering angle, a steering rate of 0.6 radians per second, a speed from minus 0.3 to 1.0 metres per second, and limited acceleration. I solve it with OSQP, warm-started, and one solve takes only a few milliseconds. The real vehicle has a delay of roughly 120 milliseconds from command to motion, mostly from the servo and motor, so I predict the state forward using the commands already sent. If a solve fails, I reuse the previous solution, shifted in time, for up to three cycles, and then slow down and stop. When the steering chattered early on, the causes were jumps between TEB homotopy classes and this delay, and I fixed it with a larger input-change weight, delay compensation, and hysteresis in the TEB choice."

**中文译文**：每一步，MPC 用车式运动学模型预测短时域内的车辆运动；状态是 x、y、航向、速度，输入是加速度和转向角；在位置、航向、速度、转向约束下求最优输入序列，只应用第一个输入，下一步重复。我的配置中时域 15 步、每步 0.1 s，20 Hz 运行；代价惩罚跟踪误差、输入大小和输入变化，转向的输入变化权重大，避免转向抖动。约束大致为转向角 ±0.5 rad、转向速率 0.6 rad/s、速度 −0.3 到 1.0 m/s、加速度受限。用 OSQP 热启动求解，单次只需数毫秒。真车从命令到运动约有 120 ms 延迟（主要来自舵机和电机），所以用已发出的命令前推状态。若求解失败，复用时间平移后的上一解最多 3 个周期，然后减速停车。早期转向抖动的原因是 TEB 同伦类切换使参考跳变加上这段延迟，解决办法是加大输入变化权重、做延迟补偿、给 TEB 选择加滞回。

### 3.5 What is a singularity in VR teleoperation, and how did you handle it?

**题面中文**：VR 遥操作里的奇异点是什么？你如何处理？

**准备 30 秒要点**：Jacobian rank / velocity amplification / damping & limits。

**90 秒参考答案**：

"In the VR teleoperation at Future Power, the controller pose of the operator is mapped to an end-effector target for each arm, and inverse kinematics turns the target into joint motion. A singularity is a joint configuration where the Jacobian loses rank, for example when an arm is fully stretched. There, a small end-effector motion in some direction would require a huge joint velocity, or may be impossible at all, so the arm jerks. I handled it in three ways with MuJoCo and Mink. First, I solved the inverse kinematics as a constrained optimisation each step, not as a plain pseudo-inverse. Second, I used a damping term, roughly 0.001, so that near a singularity the solution gives up a bit of tracking accuracy to avoid a joint velocity explosion; this is the damped-least-squares idea. Third, I added limits: joint positions pulled in by roughly 0.05 radians from the hard limits and joint speed caps of roughly 2.5 radians per second for joints one to four and 4 for joints five to seven, applied at the target side and in the driver node, with an end-effector speed cap of 0.5 metres per second. I also freeze the target if the VR data is more than 200 milliseconds old. The price is some tracking accuracy near the singularity, which is the trade-off I accepted for safety."

**中文译文**：未来动力的 VR 遥操作中，操作者手柄位姿被映射为各臂的末端目标，逆运动学把目标变成关节运动。奇异点是雅可比失秩的关节构型，例如手臂完全伸直；此时某方向上的小末端运动需要巨大的关节速度，甚至不可能，手臂就会猛抽。我用 MuJoCo 和 Mink 从三方面处理：其一，每步把逆运动学作为带约束的优化来解，而非朴素伪逆；其二，加阻尼项（约 0.001），奇异点附近以牺牲一点跟踪精度换取避免关节速度爆炸，这就是阻尼最小二乘的思想；其三，加限幅：关节位置比硬限位内缩约 0.05 rad，关节速度上限关节 1–4 约 2.5 rad/s、关节 5–7 约 4 rad/s（在目标端和驱动节点施加），末端速度上限 0.5 m/s；VR 数据超过 200 ms 无更新则冻结目标。代价是奇异点附近跟踪精度略降，这是我为安全接受的权衡。

### 4.2 Why doesn't the agent control the pump directly?

"Because a language-model agent can be wrong, slow or offline, and a pump is a physical device. If the agent had direct control, a bad output could flood the plant or leave it dry. ### 4.3 What does INT8 quantisation do to your model, and how did you check the accuracy loss?

### 4.5 What is a unified capability model, and what do you mean by a consistent robot state?

**题面中文**：什么是统一能力模型？你说的"一致的机器人状态"是什么意思？

**准备 30 秒要点**：sensor/actuator/controller / ROS mapping / same time-frame-valid。

**90 秒参考答案**：

"A unified capability model is a common C++ description of what a device can do, whether it is a sensor, an actuator or a controller. Each capability has an identifier, a type such as a lidar, a wheel velocity or a trajectory follower, units, a frame, limits, a command rate, a timeout and a safety level. The motion-control code then uses capabilities and not specific drivers, so the same code runs on different hardware. I also mapped ROS and ROS2 motion commands, for example velocity commands, to the low-level drivers through adapters, so existing robot software could use the platform without changes in its logic. The second part is a consistent robot state, meaning the same moment, the same coordinate frame, and valid values. Different sources report at different rates with different clocks and frames. I standardised the timestamps, using a monotonic clock inside a device, the coordinate frames following the usual convention of x forward, y left, z up, and validity checks. The checks include finite values, ranges, freshness, a limit of around three sensor periods, and frozen-value detection. Each message then carries a validity level, so the motion layer and the task layer see a state they can trust, or are told clearly that they cannot. Thresholds are our design values, roughly."

### 5.1 Tell me about a failure and what you learned.

**题面中文**：讲一次失败以及你的收获。

**准备 30 秒要点**：S-T-A-R / wrong layer / check data first。

**90 秒参考答案**：

"In my navigation project, the situation was that the localisation output jumped when the car turned or reversed, and the steering command jerked with it. 1. PREP 骨架：结论—依据—例子—收束；90 秒约 190–260 词。
2. 准备 30 秒只写三个关键词；作答用满 80%–95%，最后留一句收束。
3. 只说 CV 里有的荣誉与排名；项目数值带 roughly，并说明来源是笔记或设计取值。
4. 区分个人与团队：战队的三条回路是我的，奖项是团队的；3V3 成绩不归于英雄机器人。
5. LQR 在 P1 是分析参照，在 P7 才是主控制器；不要混用。
6. 平台类项目（P6）只讲思路与自己负责的边界，不讲内部源码与完整字段表。
7. 说错一个词就继续，不停下道歉；卡壳用过渡短语。
8. 五套覆盖：自我介绍动机、控制、导航估计操作臂、AI 与系统、行为综合。

## 自测题

1. 90 秒作答时，你如何分配四段时间？
2. 卡壳时可用哪三个过渡短语？
3. 第 2 套 2.2 题，LQR 帮你做了哪两件具体的事？
4. 第 3 套 3.2 题，位姿跳变的三类根因是什么？
5. 第 4 套 4.2 题，为什么 agent 不直接控制水泵？设备端有哪些兜底？
6. 你的自评表里最低的维度是什么，下一轮怎么改？

**答案要点**：1. 结论约 10 秒、依据约 35 秒、例子约 35 秒、收束约 10 秒。2. *Let me put it this way…*、*The key point is…*、*For example, in my ballbot project…*。3. 提示内环微分增益应提到比例增益的约一半；判断倾角参考上限过大并从约 0.15 降到 0.10 rad。4. 时间戳（到达时刻）、方向（轮速无符号）、坐标系（静态变换不一致）。5. agent 可能出错或离线；白名单、参数限幅、新鲜度检查、动作 ID 去重，以及设备本地阈值、最长开泵时间、传感失效停泵、手动优先。
