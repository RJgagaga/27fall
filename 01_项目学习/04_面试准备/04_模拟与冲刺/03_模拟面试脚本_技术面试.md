# 模拟面试脚本：教授技术面试（6 套）

> **一句话定位**：教授面试不会按题库走，而是顺着你的一句话一层层追问。本文给出 6 套脚本（控制 / 估计规划 / AI-ML / 嵌入式-CS / 机器人综合 / 系统与安全），每套含追问树（3–4 层）、参考回答、评分要点、常见卡壳点与应急说法。

**本章学完你能……**

- 预判教授沿一个项目会追到第几层、每层问什么；
- 在每层用"一句结论 + 一个依据"回答，不被带偏；
- 卡壳时用应急说法保住分数并诚实标出边界。

**前置知识**：[CV 逐句英文表述与追问](../03_项目口述总览/03_CV逐句英文表述与追问.md)、[跨项目技术对比](../03_项目口述总览/02_跨项目技术对比问答.md)。

## 0. 通用规则与应急说法

- 每层回答 30–45 秒；先结论后依据；教授不追就停。
- 事实只用 CV 内容；细节缺失处用 `【待你补全】` 先补。

| 卡壳场景 | 应急说法 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 想不起细节 | "I don't have the exact number in front of me, but the trend was… and I can explain how I'd check it." | 记不清数字，但趋势是……可说怎么核对 |
| 没学过 | "I haven't studied that formally. My understanding is… Could you tell me if I'm on the right track?" | 没系统学过，我的理解是……方向对吗 |
| 想一下 | "Let me think for a second." | 让我想一下 |
| 答偏了 | "Let me go back to your question." | 回到您的问题 |
| 超出个人负责范围 | "That was handled by a teammate; my part was…" | 那是队友做的，我负责…… |

## 1. 第 1 套 控制（P1 / P7 / P3）

### 追问树

```text
L0 Describe your ballbot balance control.
 ├─ L1 Why a cascade? Why bound the reference?
 │   └─ L2 What if the inner loop is not fast enough?
 │       └─ L3 How would you detect and fix that?
 ├─ L1 What does your LQR analysis show?
 │   ├─ L2 How do Q and R change the result?
 │   │   └─ L3 Why not deploy LQR? What would you need?
 │   └─ L2 What does actuator saturation do to LQR's guarantees?
 └─ L1 How did you verify CoM feedforward?
     └─ L2 What if the URDF masses are wrong?
```

### 参考回答
- **L0**："Cascaded PID: position outer loop gives a bounded attitude reference to the attitude inner loop; output is allocated to three wheels. Then LQR on a linearised four-state pendulum as a model reference. CoM feedforward from arm joints."
- **L1 Why bound**："So a large position error never requests a dangerous lean. The inner loop must be clearly faster than the outer one."
- **L2 inner not fast**："The loops couple and oscillate. I'd look at the response of each loop separately and lower the outer gain or speed up the inner loop."
- **L2 Q/R**："Larger weight on tilt makes it hold upright harder but uses more control effort; larger R reduces effort and slows the response."
- **L3 why not deploy**："The PID was working with its safety loop; deploying LQR would need reliable estimates of all four states. `【补全真实原因】`"
- **L2 saturation**："Once saturated, the linear analysis no longer holds; the analysis helped me see where saturation would occur, and I limit outputs deliberately."
- **L2 URDF wrong**："Feedforward would over- or under-compensate; feedback covers the residual, and the on/off test shows it."

**评分要点**：能区分分析与部署；知道 Q/R 含义；诚实面对饱和与模型误差。
**卡壳点**：Riccati 推导；Q/R 数值。应急：说结构与定性影响，承认数值需查记录。

### 另一分支：P7 与 P3
- "Why does gain scheduling by leg length help?" → 等效摆长变，动力学变，固定增益折中。
- "What happens between scheduling points?" → 插值；快速变化时无严格保证，故实机验证 `【补全】`。
- "Why sliding mode on the launcher?" → 弹丸通过引起负载变化，要求对负载鲁棒；代价抖振，需软化。

## 2. 第 2 套 估计与规划（P5）

### 追问树

```text
L0 Walk me through your navigation stack.
 ├─ L1 Why EKF and AMCL together?
 │   └─ L2 What caused the drift / jumps?
 │       └─ L3 How would you detect each cause automatically?
 ├─ L1 Why Hybrid-A*?
 │   └─ L2 What is the heuristic? Is it optimal?
 │       └─ L3 What fails in narrow spaces?
 └─ L1 Why MPC for tracking?
     └─ L2 Horizon, constraints, solver?
         └─ L3 What if the solver is late?
```

### 参考回答
- **L0**：见 [P5 口述](../03_项目口述总览/01_七个项目30秒_90秒_3分钟口述.md)。
- **EKF+AMCL**："EKF gives smooth fusion of inertial and wheel data; AMCL gives a map-referenced pose. Together: continuity plus map reference."
- **跳变根因**："Inconsistent timing, frames and motion direction. After alignment the state became continuous."
- **自动检测**："Compare timestamps with receive time; check the transform tree for constant offsets; compare wheel velocity sign with commanded direction."
- **Hybrid-A* 启发式**："Typically the max of a non-holonomic-aware cost and an obstacle-aware cost; not globally optimal because of discretisation. `【补全你用的】`"
- **MPC**："Receding horizon on a car-like kinematic model with position, heading, speed and steering constraints. `【补全时域/求解器】` If late, reuse the previous solution or slow down."

**评分要点**：说明各模块存在理由；定位问题归因于一致性；知道算法局限。
**卡壳点**：EKF 的雅可比；Hybrid-A* 启发函数细节；MPC 求解器。应急：说结构，诚实标记细节待补。

## 3. 第 3 套 AI/ML（P4 / P6）

### 追问树

```text
L0 Tell me about your edge vision pipeline.
 ├─ L1 Why INT8? What accuracy cost?
 │   └─ L2 How do you calibrate?
 │       └─ L3 What if lighting changes?
 ├─ L1 What does your LangGraph agent do?
 │   └─ L2 Why a rule-based fast path?
 │       └─ L3 What can go wrong with LLM-based decisions?
 └─ L1 How does this connect to your Kaihong work?
     └─ L2 What is checked in the policy check?
         └─ L3 Can the check itself fail?
```

### 参考回答
- **INT8**："Cuts memory and compute for the NPU; cost is quantisation error that must be validated on calibration data. `【补全你自己的精度对比】`"
- **光照变化**："Confidence thresholds and multi-frame confirmation; the decision layer shouldn't trust one frame."
- **Fast path**："Extreme states shouldn't wait for a slow, uncertain model; rules are deterministic."
- **LLM 风险**："Hallucination, inconsistency, latency; so outputs are structured and constrained, and the device has its own floor. `【补全 agent 是否使用 LLM】`"
- **Kaihong 连接**："P4's device-side floor becomes a platform-level proposal–policy-check–execution–feedback chain."
- **策略检查失效**："Keep it simple, deterministic, and default-deny on failure. `【补全规则类型】`"

**评分要点**：知道量化、置信度、结构化输出；能说出 AI 的风险；坦承 ML 理论是想补的部分。
**卡壳点**：量化原理；Transformer 细节。应急："My practice is deployment-focused; here's what I understand… I want to study the theory."

## 4. 第 4 套 嵌入式/CS（P3 / P7 / P6）

### 追问树

```text
L0 How did you structure firmware on the STM32?
 ├─ L1 Which FreeRTOS tasks? Priorities and periods?
 │   └─ L2 How do tasks share data safely?
 │       └─ L3 Priority inversion? How avoid?
 ├─ L1 How does CAN communication work?
 │   └─ L2 What if a feedback frame is late?
 │       └─ L3 Bus-off / overload?
 └─ L1 (P6) How would you design a capability abstraction?
     └─ L2 What are the trade-offs of abstraction?
```

### 参考回答
- **任务**："Periodic control tasks and communication tasks under FreeRTOS. `【补全任务表：名称/周期/优先级】`"
- **共享数据**："Queues, mutexes or critical sections; keep ISR work short."
- **优先级反转**："A low-priority task holds a mutex needed by a high-priority task while a medium task preempts. Priority inheritance in mutexes mitigates it."
- **CAN 延迟帧**："Treat feedback as having freshness; if stale, limit output."
- **Bus-off**："Node goes off-bus after repeated errors; recover by re-init; check wiring and load."
- **抽象取舍**："Reuse and isolation versus an extra layer, and differences that can't be abstracted need explicit parameters."

**评分要点**：任务划分清晰；理解同步与实时；知道通信故障。
**卡壳点**：具体优先级、波特率。应急：说原则，诚实补全细节。

## 5. 第 5 套 机器人综合（P2 / P7）

### 追问树

```text
L0 Explain constrained IK in VR teleoperation.
 ├─ L1 What is a singularity? How do you suppress amplification?
 │   └─ L2 Damping vs constraints vs null space?
 │       └─ L3 Cost of damping?
 ├─ L1 Gravity compensation: how computed?
 │   └─ L2 Model error effect?
 └─ L1 (P7) Closed-chain kinematics: what is a virtual leg?
     └─ L2 How do you map virtual force to joints?
         └─ L3 Near-singular leg configurations?
```

### 参考回答
- **奇异**："Jacobian loses rank; small Cartesian motion demands huge joint velocity. Damped least squares or QP constraints keep it bounded, at some tracking cost."
- **零空间**："For redundant arms, null-space motion keeps joints away from limits without affecting the end-effector task."
- **重力补偿**："From URDF and KDL: g(q) feedforward on the leader arm; model error causes drift or heaviness."
- **虚拟腿**："Length and angle computed from closed-chain kinematics; τ = Jᵀ F maps virtual force to joints."
- **近奇异**："Torques blow up, so keep leg configuration in a safe range and limit output. `【补全限位策略】`"

**评分要点**：Jacobian 的两种用途；奇异处理方法；知道局限。
**卡壳点**：五连杆公式推导。应急：描述思路——环闭合约束、被动关节、雅可比。

## 6. 第 6 套 系统与安全（P6 / P4）

### 追问树

```text
L0 What is your safe-execution chain?
 ├─ L1 What does each stage do?
 │   └─ L2 What does the policy check consider?
 │       └─ L3 What if state is stale or invalid?
 ├─ L1 Why DDS-style coordination?
 │   └─ L2 Discovery / registration / dispatch flow?
 └─ L1 How would you extend it for AI planners?
     └─ L2 Failure modes of an AI planner?
         └─ L3 How do you test the chain?
```

### 参考回答
- **各阶段**："Proposal describes the intended action; policy check allows or blocks based on state and rules; execution acts; feedback reports the real state."
- **状态失效**："Mark invalid, don't silently use; default to not allowing new actions, hold or stop."
- **DDS**："Data-centric publish–subscribe suits many devices sharing state; discovery lets devices find each other, registration declares capabilities, dispatch sends tasks. `【补全 M-DDS 细节】`"
- **AI 规划器风险**："Wrong or outdated assumptions, hallucinated actions; so the chain adds a deterministic gate and feedback."
- **测试**："Inject invalid states and unsafe proposals, check they're blocked; replay recorded states. `【补全实际做法】`"

**评分要点**：链的逻辑；fail-safe；与 AI 的连接；不夸大个人范围。
**卡壳点**：公司内部细节。应急："I can't share internal details, but the design principle is…"

## 本章要点速记

1. 追问沿一句话向下：做了什么 → 为什么 → 失败怎么办。
2. 每层 30–45 秒，结论先行。
3. 不会就诚实说边界并给出你确实懂的部分。
4. 六套覆盖控制、估计规划、AI、嵌入式、操作臂/腿、系统安全。

## 自测题

1. 说出第 1 套 L3 的问题与你的回答。
2. 优先级反转怎么发生，如何缓解？
3. 为什么策略检查失败要默认不放行？
4. 阻尼最小二乘的代价是什么？
5. 你最怕被问到哪一层？为它写应急说法。

**答案要点**：2. 低优先级持锁、中优先级抢占，优先级继承；3. fail-safe；4. 方向性跟踪误差。
