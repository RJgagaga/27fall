# 模拟面试脚本：技术面试（6 套 × 追问树）

> **一句话定位**：技术面试官会从你 CV 上的一句话出发，一层一层往下追问。本文给出 6 套模拟面试，每套含面试官开场、3–4 层追问树（每层有问题、英文参考回答及中文译文、评分要点）、卡壳时的应急说法和结束时的反问；用来练"被追问到第三层还不乱"。

**本章学完你能……**

- 面对"一句话 → 原理 → 参数 → 取舍"的四层追问，每层都能用英文在 60–90 秒内答清；
- 区分"我做了的事实"与"典型做法"，不越过 CV 口径（P1 的 LQR 是分析参照，P7 的 LQR 才是主控制器）；

## 2. 第 1 套：控制（P1 / P7 / P3）

**面试官开场**："I see you worked on balance control for a ballbot and a wheel-legged robot. Tell me about the control structure you used."
中文：我看到你做过球形机器人和轮腿机器人的平衡控制，请讲讲你用的控制结构。

```text
开场：控制结构
 ├─ L1 级联 PID：外环给内环什么？
 │    └─ L2 为什么参考要有界？带宽怎么分？
 │         └─ L3 输出饱和、积分饱和怎么处理？
 │              └─ L4 既然有 LQR 分析，为什么不部署？轮腿为什么要调度？
```

### L1：你的级联结构是什么？

**参考回答**：

"On the ballbot, the controller is a cascade. The outer loop is a position loop. It takes the position and velocity error and outputs a tilt reference. The inner loop is an attitude loop. It takes the IMU tilt and tilt rate and outputs a planar command that keeps the robot balanced around that reference. Then the kinematics of the three omni wheels allocate the command to three wheel commands. I deployed this structure on the real robot while both arms moved along fixed trajectories. In my notes the outer loop has a proportional gain of about 0.08 radians per metre and a derivative gain of about 0.2, and the inner loop is PD with a very small clamped integral term."

中文：球形机器人上是级联：外环位置环由位置/速度误差输出倾角参考；内环姿态环用 IMU 倾角与倾角速率输出平面命令；三全向轮运动学把命令分配到三个轮子。该结构在双臂沿固定轨迹运动时部署在真机上。笔记里外环比例约 0.08 rad/m、微分约 0.2，内环为 PD 加极小的限幅积分项。

**评分要点**：
- 说清"外环输出倾角参考，内环跟踪参考"的因果；
- 提到运动学分配到三轮；
- 明确是部署在真机上的控制器；
- 数值带 roughly / in my notes。

### L2：为什么参考要有界？带宽怎么分配？

**参考回答**：

"Because the outer loop asks the inner loop for a tilt. If it asks for too much tilt, the inner loop needs large wheel commands, saturates, and the robot falls. So I bound the reference, roughly 0.1 radians in my design, and I also limit the speed and acceleration. About bandwidth: the inner loop must be much faster than the outer loop, so the outer loop sees an almost ideal tilt response. In my notes the inner loop is about 1 hertz and the outer loop about 0.15 hertz, a ratio of six or seven. If the ratio is too small, the two loops interact, and you can get a slow oscillation that grows. The motor speed loop inside the driver is faster still, around 20 hertz, so the three layers are separated in frequency."

中文：因为外环向内环要的是倾角；要得太大，内环需要大轮命令而饱和并倒下，所以参考有界（设计中约 0.1 rad），并限速度与加速度。带宽方面，内环必须比外环快得多，外环才能把内环看作近似理想的倾角响应；笔记里内环约 1 Hz、外环约 0.15 Hz，比值约 6–7。比值太小则两环耦合，可能出现增长的慢振荡。驱动器内部电机速度环更快（约 20 Hz），三层在频率上分离。

**评分要点**：
- 把有界参考与"防止饱和"联系起来；
- 给出带宽分离的理由（外环视内环为理想）；
- 能说出分离不足的后果。

### L3：输出饱和与积分饱和怎么处理？

**参考回答**：

"There are three pieces. First, output limiting: I limit the norm of the wheel command as a whole and scale it proportionally, so the direction of the command is preserved, plus a slope limit per cycle. If I clipped each wheel separately, the direction would change and the robot would be pushed the wrong way. Second, integral windup: I use clamping of the integral term, and I stop adding to it when the output sits at the limit. I did not use back-calculation. Windup is when the integrator keeps growing while the actuator is saturated, so when the error finally reverses, the controller overshoots badly. Third, I diagnosed saturation with the LQR analysis: when commands often hit their limits, the peak control effort predicted by the analysis suggested that the tilt reference cap was too large, so I lowered it from about 0.15 to 0.10 radians. I can also say that in the notes about six percent of cycles saturated with the feedforward off and about one percent with it on."

中文：三部分。其一输出限幅：对轮命令整体限制范数并等比缩放，保持命令方向，再加每周期斜率限制；若逐轮单独截断，方向会变，机器人会被推向错误方向。其二积分饱和：采用积分限幅，输出贴限幅时停止累加，没有用反算法。积分饱和是执行器饱和时积分器继续增长，误差最终反向时严重超调。其三用 LQR 分析诊断饱和：命令常贴限幅时，分析预测的峰值控制量显示倾角参考上限过大，于是从约 0.15 降到 0.10 rad。笔记里关前馈时约 6% 周期饱和，开前馈后约 1%。

**评分要点**：
- 区分输出限幅与积分抗饱和；
- 说出"整体 L2 等比缩放以保持方向"的理由；
- 不说成 back-calculation；
- 把 LQR 分析用于诊断而非部署。

### L4：既然做了 LQR 分析，为什么不部署？轮腿机器人为什么要调度增益？

**参考回答**：

"On the ballbot, LQR needs a reliable estimate of all four states, and mine was position from wheel odometry, which drifts, so the PID cascade with its safety loop was the more robust choice, and I used the LQR as a model reference to explain and tune the gains. I'm careful to say deployed PID, analysed LQR. On the wheel-legged robot, the situation is different: the dynamics depend on leg length, and the model is good enough, so LQR is the main controller. One fixed gain is a compromise, and it loses stability margin far from the design point. I scheduled the gains by leg length: I solved LQR at several leg lengths offline and fitted each of the twelve gains with a cubic polynomial, so there are 48 coefficients, valid between roughly 0.1 and 0.3 metres, with clamping online. The limitation is that scheduling is a heuristic: between the design points, I have no formal stability guarantee if the leg length changes quickly, so I tested it on the robot. A stronger approach would be to study the stability of the scheduled system, and that is one thing I want to learn in a master's."

中文：球形机器人上，LQR 需要四个状态的可靠估计，而我的位置来自会漂移的轮速里程，所以带安全回路的 PID 级联更稳健，我把 LQR 作为模型参照用于解释和整定；我会明确说"部署的是 PID，分析的是 LQR"。轮腿机器人则不同：动力学依赖腿长，模型足够好，LQR 是主控制器。固定增益是折中，远离设计点稳定裕度下降。我按腿长调度：离线在若干腿长上解 LQR，把 12 个增益各用三次多项式拟合（共 48 个系数），约适用于 0.1–0.3 m，在线限幅。局限是调度只是启发式：设计点之间若腿长变化很快，没有形式化稳定性保证，所以我在机器人上测试。更强的做法是研究调度系统的稳定性，这正是我想在硕士阶段学习的。

**评分要点**：
- 解释不部署的真实理由（状态估计质量），保持 CV 口径；
- 轮腿 LQR 是主控制器；
- 能说出调度的局限并提出改进方向。

**卡壳点与应急说法**：
- 被问 LQR 的 Riccati 方程细节：*"I know the structure: the gain is K equals R inverse B transpose P, where P solves the algebraic Riccati equation. I used a solver rather than deriving it by hand, and I'd like to study the derivation more formally."*
- 被问某个增益的确切值：*"I don't have the exact figure in front of me, but roughly it was of that order, and it came from tuning on the robot."*
- 被问 P1 是否用了 LQR 增益：*"The deployed controller is the cascaded PID. The LQR was a model reference."*

**结束时你的反问**：
1. "Which control methods does your group use most for legged or wheeled robots, and how do you validate them on hardware?"
2. "How do students usually combine model-based control with learning-based methods in your lab?"

## 3. 第 2 套：估计与规划（P5）

**面试官开场**："Your CV says you built EKF and AMCL localisation, Hybrid-A*, TEB and MPC on a car-like robot. Walk me through how the pieces fit together."
中文：CV 上写你在车式机器人上做了 EKF/AMCL 定位、Hybrid-A*、TEB 与 MPC，请讲讲这些部分如何配合。

```text
开场：整条导航链
 ├─ L1 EKF 与 AMCL 为什么都要？分工是什么？
 │    └─ L2 EKF 的状态/观测/噪声怎么定？
 │         └─ L3 位姿跳变的根因与处理
 │              └─ L4 Hybrid-A* 启发式、TEB 同伦、MPC 求解失败怎么办
```

### L1：EKF 与 AMCL 的分工

**参考回答**：

"They solve different problems. The EKF fuses the wheel odometry, the gyro and the IMU at a high rate, around 30 hertz in my setup, and gives a smooth, continuous state in the odometry frame. It drifts over time because it has no absolute reference. AMCL is a particle filter that matches the 2D LiDAR scan against the map and estimates the global pose, but it updates less often and can jump when the scan is ambiguous. So I use them together: the EKF output is the continuous odom to base transform, which the controller uses, and AMCL publishes the correction between the map frame and the odom frame. The controller never sees the jumps of AMCL directly, because it works in the odom frame. If I used AMCL alone, the control would see every jump, and if I used the EKF alone, the position would drift away from the map."

中文：它们解决不同问题。EKF 以较高频率（我的配置约 30 Hz）融合轮速里程计、陀螺与 IMU，在 odom 系中给出平滑连续的状态，但没有绝对参考，会漂移。AMCL 是粒子滤波，把 2D LiDAR 扫描与地图匹配估计全局位姿，更新较慢，扫描有歧义时可能跳变。所以二者配合：EKF 输出作为连续的 odom→base 变换供控制器用，AMCL 发布 map 与 odom 之间的校正。控制器在 odom 系中工作，看不到 AMCL 的跳变。单用 AMCL，控制会看到每次跳变；单用 EKF，位置会相对地图漂移。

**评分要点**：
- 说出连续性 vs 全局一致性的分工；
- 说出 TF 层级 map→odom→base_link；
- 说明控制器为何放在 odom 系。

### L2：EKF 的状态、观测和噪声怎么定？

**参考回答**：

"I used the robot_localization EKF in 2D mode. The state is the planar pose, heading and velocities. The process model is a motion model, and the measurements are the wheel speed, a yaw rate that I compute from the speed and the steering angle using the bicycle model, and the gyro yaw rate. The measurement noise reflects how much I trust each sensor: roughly 0.05 metres per second for the wheel speed and 0.01 radians per second for the gyro in my configuration, and the process noise is a small diagonal, around 0.001 to 0.01. The way I choose them is by looking at the sensors' behaviour when the robot is still, and then adjusting so the innovation looks reasonable. A rule of thumb: if the filter lags behind a real turn, the measurement noise is too high or the process noise is too low; if the estimate is noisy, the opposite. I also check consistency: the covariance should grow when no measurement arrives. One limitation is that an EKF linearises, so large heading errors hurt it."

中文：我用 robot_localization 的 2D EKF。状态是平面位姿、航向和速度；过程模型是运动模型；观测为轮速、由速度与转向角用自行车模型算出的偏航角速度、陀螺偏航角速度。观测噪声反映对各传感器的信任：我的配置中轮速约 0.05 m/s、陀螺约 0.01 rad/s，过程噪声为小的对角阵，约 0.001–0.01。选取方法是观察机器人静止时传感器的表现，再调到新息看起来合理。经验法则：滤波器在真实转弯时滞后，则观测噪声偏大或过程噪声偏小；估计太抖则相反。我也检查一致性：无观测时协方差应增长。局限是 EKF 线性化，大航向误差会使其变差。

**评分要点**：
- 能写出状态/过程/观测三件事；
- 说明噪声参数的来源与调法；
- 提到 EKF 的线性化局限。

### L3：位姿跳变的原因和处理

**参考回答**：

"When the car turned or reversed, the map-to-odom transform had steps of about 0.2 to 0.4 metres and 5 to 10 degrees. I found three input problems, not a filter problem. First, timing: the LiDAR driver stamped each scan with its arrival time, about 60 milliseconds late, so during a turn the scan was matched to the wrong pose. I changed it to the scan start time, compensated the fixed delay, and synchronised the clocks, which brought the offset to within about 5 milliseconds. Second, direction: the lower-level board sent wheel speed as an unsigned magnitude, so reversing integrated forward, and the particles spread. I parsed it as signed and added a gear check. Third, inconsistent static transforms. Then I added a rate limit, roughly twenty percent of vehicle speed, and a gate on the map-to-odom correction: a large jump needs several consistent updates and a small covariance. Afterwards, adjacent steps were generally below 5 centimetres."

中文：车转弯或倒车时，map→odom 变换出现约 0.2–0.4 m、5°–10° 的阶跃。我发现是三个输入问题，而不是滤波问题。其一时间：LiDAR 驱动给每帧打到达时刻，晚约 60 ms，转弯时扫描匹配到错误位姿；改成扫描起始时刻、补偿固定延迟、同步时钟，偏移降到约 5 ms 内。其二方向：下位机轮速是无符号幅值，倒车时向前积分，粒子扩散；改为带符号解析并加挡位校验。其三静态变换不一致。随后对 map→odom 校正加限速（约车速的 20%）与门限：大跳变需要连续数次一致更新且协方差小。处理后相邻步一般小于 5 cm。

**评分要点**：
- 能分出时间、方向、坐标系三类根因；
- 能说出具体修复动作；
- 区分"修输入"与"后处理平滑"，并说明后者只是兜底。

### L4：Hybrid-A* 的启发式、TEB 的同伦、MPC 失败怎么办

**参考回答**：

"For Hybrid-A*, the heuristic is typically the maximum of two: a non-holonomic cost that ignores obstacles, computed from Reeds-Shepp lengths, and a 2D grid distance that ignores kinematics. Each is admissible, and their maximum is tighter. It is not globally optimal, because of the discretisation of the heading into bins and of the continuous state into cells, but it is feasible. For TEB, the optimiser can end in different homotopy classes, for example passing an obstacle on the left or on the right. If the chosen class flips between cycles, the reference jumps. So I keep several candidates, around four, and add hysteresis to the choice, which also helped with a steering chatter I saw in the MPC. For the MPC, if a solve fails or times out, I reuse the previous solution, shifted in time, for up to three cycles, and then decelerate to stop. The solve time is just a few milliseconds at a horizon of 15 steps, so the failures are rare, but a fallback is needed on a real vehicle. A possible improvement is a proper fallback controller, or a robust formulation of the delay."

中文：Hybrid-A* 的启发式通常取两者最大值：忽略障碍的非完整代价（由 Reeds-Shepp 长度算出）与忽略运动学的二维栅格距离；两者都可采纳，取最大更紧。因航向分桶与连续状态离散化，它不是全局最优，但可行。TEB 的优化可能落入不同同伦类，如从障碍左边或右边通过；若所选类在周期间翻转，参考就会跳，所以我保留约 4 个候选并给选择加滞回，这也缓解了 MPC 中看到的转向抖动。MPC 求解失败或超时时，复用平移后的上一解最多 3 个周期，然后减速停车。时域 15 步时求解只要数毫秒，失败很少，但真车必须有兜底。可能的改进是设计专门的兜底控制器，或对延迟采用鲁棒形式。

**评分要点**：
- 启发式取最大值且知道可采纳性；
- 解释同伦类与滞回；
- 有求解失败的兜底并承认局限；
- 不夸大"最优"。

**卡壳点与应急说法**：
- EKF 雅可比：*"I don't derive the Jacobians by hand in this setup, because I used the robot_localization package. Conceptually, it linearises the motion model around the current estimate; I can write the structure for a simple bicycle model."*
- Hybrid-A* 启发细节：*"I can describe the structure, which is the maximum of the non-holonomic and the grid heuristics, and I'd rather not guess the exact weights."*
- MPC 求解器：*"I used OSQP with warm start; the formulation is a linear-time-varying quadratic program around the reference."*

**结束时你的反问**：
1. "How does your group evaluate localisation quality on real vehicles, apart from visual checks?"
2. "Do you see a role for learned components in the navigation stack, for example in the cost maps or the local planner?"

## 4. 第 3 套：AI / ML 与边缘部署（P4）

**面试官开场**："Tell me about the vision and decision parts of your gardening project. What did you do yourself?"
中文：请讲讲园艺项目的视觉与决策部分，哪些是你自己做的？

```text
开场：视觉 + 决策 + 灌溉
 ├─ L1 YOLOv5 推理链：从相机帧到结构化观测
 │    └─ L2 INT8 量化：校准、精度损失、NPU 速度
 │         └─ L3 Agent 为什么只给建议？怎么保证安全？
```

### L1：从相机帧到结构化观测

**参考回答**：

"The camera gives a 1280 by 720 frame. I resize it with a letterbox, which keeps the aspect ratio and pads with grey to 640 by 640, convert from BGR to RGB, and send it to the NPU as 8-bit data. The model outputs three feature maps at different scales. I decode the boxes on the CPU, filter by objectness and class score, roughly 0.25, apply non-maximum suppression per class with an IoU threshold of 0.45, and then map the boxes back to the original image using the scale and the padding. A common bug is forgetting to subtract the padding, which shifts all the boxes, and I had exactly that bug once. Then I turn detections into events: the same class must appear in three consecutive frames with a confidence of at least 0.5, and an event of the same class is at most once every five seconds. The event carries a class, a confidence, a box and a timestamp. This makes the output a structured observation, not just pictures."

中文：相机给出 1280×720 帧。我用 letterbox 缩放（保持长宽比，用灰边填充到 640×640），BGR 转 RGB，以 8 位数据送入 NPU。模型输出三个尺度的特征图；我在 CPU 上解码框，按 objectness 与类别分数过滤（约 0.25），按类别做 IoU 0.45 的非极大值抑制，再用缩放比例与填充量把框映射回原图。常见的错误是忘记减去填充，使所有框整体偏移，我就踩过一次。随后把检测转成事件：同一类需连续 3 帧置信度至少 0.5，同类事件最多每 5 秒一次。事件含类别、置信度、框和时间戳，这样输出是结构化观测，而不只是图片。

**评分要点**：
- 说清预处理（letterbox、颜色顺序）与后处理（解码、NMS、坐标还原）；
- 提到事件门而不是单帧触发；
- 承认并说明了真实踩过的 bug。

### L2：INT8 量化

**参考回答**：

"Quantisation maps floating-point weights and activations to 8-bit integers using a scale and a zero point per tensor or per channel. It reduces memory and bandwidth and lets the NPU run integer operations, but it adds error. The key step is calibration: the toolkit runs sample images through the model to measure the range of each activation, so the scales are chosen well. I used 200 calibration images, 150 from the training set and 50 from the field, with fill light and at night. If the calibration set doesn't match the deployment data, some activations clip, and accuracy drops. I used RKNN-Toolkit2 with asymmetric 8-bit quantisation, per-channel weights, and I did not use mixed quantisation. In my notes, mAP at 0.5 dropped from about 0.86 to 0.83, and the recall on the harder classes dropped more, wilting from 0.84 to 0.80 and disease spot from 0.78 to 0.73. The NPU time is about 28 milliseconds per frame, and about 15 frames per second end to end."

中文：量化用 scale 与 zero point（逐张量或逐通道）把浮点权重与激活映射为 8 位整数，减少内存与带宽并让 NPU 跑整数运算，但引入误差。关键步骤是校准：工具包让样本图过模型，统计各激活范围，从而选好 scale。我用 200 张校准图（150 张来自训练集、50 张来自现场补光与夜间）。校准集若与部署数据不匹配，部分激活会被截断，精度下降。我用 RKNN-Toolkit2 的非对称 8 位量化、权重逐通道，没有用混合量化。笔记中 mAP@0.5 约从 0.86 降到 0.83，较难类别召回掉得更多：wilting 0.84→0.80，disease spot 0.78→0.73。NPU 单帧约 28 ms，端到端约 15 FPS。

**评分要点**：
- 解释 scale/zero point 与校准的目的；
- 说明校准集要覆盖部署分布；
- 诚实给出精度损失，并指出集中在难类别；
- 不声称做了混合量化。

### L3：Agent 为什么只给建议？

**参考回答**：

"The agent is a language-model decision chain in LangGraph, and it can be wrong, slow or offline. So it never drives the pump directly. It produces structured recommendations or constrained requests, with a fixed output format. Before anything is executed, the request goes through a whitelist and a policy check: the action must be allowed, the parameters must be within limits, such as a maximum pumping time of 60 seconds, and the data must be fresh, for example soil data within ten seconds and a vision observation within two seconds. Requests have an action ID so that retries don't repeat the action, and the device sends an acknowledgement. There is also a rule-based fast path for extreme states, like very dry soil, which does not wait for the model. On the device, the Hi3861 has its own state machine with start and stop thresholds, 56 and 65 in my notes, so there is a hysteresis band of 9, and manual commands have priority. The model is used with a low temperature, around 0.2, and a structured output schema. The long-term memory and a vector retrieval were not implemented, only the last few event summaries."

中文：agent 是 LangGraph 里的语言模型决策链，可能出错、变慢或离线，所以它从不直接驱动水泵：只产生结构化建议或受约束请求，输出格式固定。任何执行之前，请求要过白名单与策略检查：动作必须被允许，参数在限制内（如最长开泵 60 秒），数据必须新鲜（如土壤数据 10 秒内、视觉观测 2 秒内）。请求带动作 ID，重试不会重复执行，设备回执确认。另有对极端状态（如土壤极干）的规则快速通道，不等模型。设备端 Hi3861 有自己的状态机，启停阈值笔记中为 56 和 65（滞回带 9），手动命令优先。模型温度较低（约 0.2）并使用结构化输出 schema。长期记忆与向量检索没有实现，只有最近若干条事件摘要。

### L1：任务划分与优先级

**参考回答**：

"On the hero robot, two boards, one for the gimbal and one for the chassis, each running FreeRTOS on an STM32F407. I split the work into periodic tasks by function: an IMU task that reads the sensor and updates the attitude, control tasks for the gimbal, the launcher or the chassis, a CAN communication task, a task for the remote control and the vision link, and a detect task every 10 milliseconds that checks for offline sources. The priorities follow the deadline: sensing and control are higher, logging and monitoring are lower. On the wheel-legged robot with an STM32H7, there is a 500 hertz control and estimation task at the top priority, an FDCAN task, and a lower-priority task for logging and commands. I avoid blocking calls in the control task, and I pass data between tasks with queues or with a snapshot taken at the start of each cycle. A problem I watch for is priority inversion, where a low-priority task holds a lock that a high-priority task needs. Mutexes in FreeRTOS use priority inheritance to limit it, but the best approach is to avoid sharing."

中文：英雄机器人有两块板（云台板、底盘板），各在 STM32F407 上运行 FreeRTOS。我按功能划分周期任务：读传感器并更新姿态的 IMU 任务、云台/发射/底盘控制任务、CAN 通信任务、遥控与视觉链路任务，以及每 10 ms 检查掉线源的 detect 任务。优先级按截止期：感知与控制高，日志与监控低。轮腿机器人用 STM32H7，有最高优先级的 500 Hz 控制与估计任务、FDCAN 任务以及低优先级的日志与命令任务。控制任务里避免阻塞调用，任务间用队列或每周期开始时取的快照传数据。要警惕优先级反转：低优先级任务持有高优先级任务所需的锁；FreeRTOS 互斥量用优先级继承限制它，但最好的办法是避免共享。

**评分要点**：

### L2：CAN 总线负载

**参考回答**：

"I estimate the load with a rough frame size. A standard CAN frame with 8 data bytes is about 111 bits including stuffing and overhead, so at 1 megabit per second it takes about 111 microseconds. On the gimbal board, five motors on one bus report feedback at 1 kilohertz each, so 5000 frames per second, and the controller sends two command frames, one for the 0x200 group and one for the 0x1FF group, at about 333 hertz each. Total: 5000 plus 667 is about 5667 frames per second, times 111 bits is about 629 kilobits per second, which is about 63 percent of the bus. The chassis board has four motors, so about 4000 plus 333 frames, about 48 percent. The link between the two boards is a separate bus with only a few frames, below two percent. A load around 60 percent is acceptable but leaves less room for retransmission and added devices; above about 70 to 80 percent, latency of low-priority messages increases. Priority on CAN is by identifier, so critical messages have low identifiers."

中文：我用粗略的帧大小估算负载。8 字节数据的标准 CAN 帧含填充与开销约 111 位，在 1 Mbit/s 下约 111 µs。云台板一条总线上 5 个电机反馈各 1 kHz，即 5000 帧/s，控制器发两个命令帧（0x200 组与 0x1FF 组）各约 333 Hz；合计约 5667 帧/s，乘 111 位约 629 kbit/s，约占总线 63%。底盘板 4 个电机，约 4333 帧/s，约 48%。两板之间是独立总线，只有少量帧，低于 2%。约 60% 的负载可以接受，但对重传与新增设备余量变小；高于约 70%–80% 时，低优先级报文延迟上升。CAN 按标识符仲裁优先级，所以关键报文用小标识符。

**评分要点**：
- 会做帧大小与帧率的手算；
- 知道 CAN 按 ID 仲裁；
- 能说出负载高的后果。

### L3：IMU 与 AHRS

**参考回答**：

"The hero robot uses a BMI088 IMU, read over SPI with DMA. The gyroscope is precise over short times but has a bias that drifts. The accelerometer gives a gravity direction that is noisy during motion but does not drift. The Mahony filter combines them: it integrates the gyro, and corrects the attitude with the error between the measured gravity and the estimated gravity, through a proportional gain, with an integral gain for the bias. I set Kp to 0.5 and Ki to 0, so no integral term, and I removed the gyro bias at start-up by averaging 2000 still samples, and the IMU temperature is held at about 40 degrees Celsius with a heater and a PID. The yaw angle has no absolute reference without a magnetometer, which I did not enable, so yaw drifts, about 1 degree per minute in my notes, and it is only a relative angle. Because the yaw wraps at plus and minus 180 degrees, I once had a jump in the gimbal when filtering, and fixed it by filtering sine and cosine on the unit circle."

中文：英雄机器人用 BMI088 IMU，通过 SPI+DMA 读取。陀螺短时精确但偏置会漂移；加速度计给出重力方向，运动中有噪声但不漂移。Mahony 滤波器把二者结合：积分陀螺，并用测得重力与估计重力之间的误差通过比例增益校正姿态，积分增益用于偏置。我取 Kp=0.5、Ki=0（不用积分项），启动时对 2000 个静止样本取平均去除陀螺偏置，并用加热电阻 PID 把 IMU 温度恒定在约 40°C。没有磁力计（我未启用）时 yaw 没有绝对参考，会漂移，笔记中约 1°/min，只是相对角。因为 yaw 在 ±180° 回绕，我曾在滤波时遇到云台跳变，用单位圆上的 sin/cos 滤波解决。

**评分要点**：
- 解释陀螺与加速度计的互补性；
- 能讲出 Mahony 的结构与 Kp/Ki 的作用；
- 知道 yaw 漂移与角度回绕问题；

### L4：数字 PID 与实时性陷阱

**参考回答**：

"A digital PID has a few details that matter. First, the gains are tied to the sampling period, since in my implementation the integral and derivative are not scaled by dt, so I tuned them for a fixed period, and if the period changes, the effective gains change. A cleaner version uses the measured dt. Second, I limit the integral term, 3000 in the gimbal inner loop, and also the total output, 30000 for the motor voltage command. Third, the derivative acting on the error causes a kick when the setpoint steps, so one can use the derivative on the measurement. Fourth, in the relaxed state I clear the integral and the history so the controller does not jump when it re-enables. And a real-time trap: the sliding-mode and the AHRS code used a fixed dt of 0.001 as a nominal constant, while the real loop period was a few milliseconds, because several one-tick delays accumulate. So I don't claim a 1 kilohertz loop. If I measured the period, I'd use that. The gimbal inner loop bandwidth is around 30 to 40 hertz and the outer loop around 4 to 5 hertz."

中文：数字 PID 有几个关键细节。其一，增益与采样周期绑定：我的实现里积分和微分没有乘除 dt，所以是针对固定周期整定的，周期变化则有效增益改变；更干净的做法是用实测 dt。其二，限制积分项（云台内环 3000）和总输出（电机电压命令 30000）。其三，微分作用在误差上会在给定阶跃时产生冲击，可改为对测量值微分。其四，在松弛（RELAX）状态清除积分与历史，使控制器重新使能时不跳变。另一个实时性陷阱：滑模与 AHRS 代码把 dt 写死为 0.001 的名义常数，而实际循环周期是几毫秒（多处一 tick 延时累加），所以我不声称 1 kHz 回路；若实测了周期就用实测值。云台内环带宽约 30–40 Hz、外环约 4–5 Hz。

**评分要点**：
- 提到积分限幅、输出限幅、重新使能清状态；
- 知道微分冲击；
- 诚实说明 dt 名义常数，避免夸大频率。

**卡壳点与应急说法**：
- 被问 RTOS 调度内部：*"I know the priority-based preemptive model and the time slicing among equal priorities; I haven't read the kernel source in detail."*

### L1：位姿映射

### L2：约束 IK、阻尼与奇异点

**参考回答**：

"At each step I solve for a joint velocity that moves the end effector towards the target. The relation is that the end-effector velocity equals the Jacobian times the joint velocity. A plain pseudo-inverse blows up near a singularity, where the Jacobian loses rank and some direction needs a huge joint velocity. With Mink, the problem is posed as a small quadratic program, a task cost on the end-effector error, plus a damping term on the joint velocity, which is the damped least squares idea: the solution is J transpose times the inverse of J J transpose plus lambda squared identity. Near a singularity, the damping gives up some tracking in the bad direction, instead of an explosion. On top of that there are constraints: the joint position limits, pulled in by 0.05 radians, and velocity limits. In my configuration the step is 5 milliseconds, and the real loop ran at about 120 hertz including the solve and the messaging. The damping is global, roughly 0.001, so it does not adapt to how near the singularity is, which is a limitation. I do not claim any version's default behaviour in the library."

中文：每一步我求一个关节速度，使末端向目标移动，关系是末端速度等于雅可比乘关节速度。朴素伪逆在奇异点附近会爆炸（雅可比失秩，某方向需要巨大关节速度）。Mink 把问题表述为小的二次规划：末端误差的任务代价加上关节速度的阻尼项，即阻尼最小二乘思想，解为 $J^{T}(JJ^{T}+\lambda^{2}I)^{-1}$ 的形式；奇异点附近，阻尼让出坏方向的一点跟踪，避免爆炸。此外有约束：关节位置限位（内缩 0.05 rad）与速度限位。我的配置中步长 5 ms，含求解与消息收发的实际循环约 120 Hz。阻尼是全局的，约 0.001，不会随接近奇异点的程度自适应，这是个局限。我不断言库在某版本的默认行为。

**评分要点**：
- 写出速度级 IK 的关系；
- 说清阻尼最小二乘的作用与代价；
- 知道约束放进优化中；
- 承认全局阻尼的局限。

### L3：重力补偿与主从跟踪

**参考回答**：

"For the leader arm, I want it to feel weightless, so the motors cancel gravity. I built the dual-arm dynamics from the URDF with KDL and computed the gravity torque at the current joint angles about 200 times per second, and sent it as a feed-forward torque with zero stiffness. The compensation ratio is 1 for most joints and 0.8 for the last joint. The reason: the mass and centre of mass of the gripper or handle there are the least certain, and when I raised the ratio to about 0.9 and above, that joint started to drift upward slowly. So I left a 10 to 20 percent margin. The follower arm tracks the leader's joint positions with a position-velocity law, stiffness 50 on the first four joints and 10 on the last three, damping 1.2 down to 0.2, with a velocity feed-forward and no gravity feed-forward. The end-to-end delay is about 30 milliseconds, and the follower lags the leader by about three control cycles during motion, with a steady error below 0.02 radians in my notes."

中文：主臂要手感"无重力"，所以电机抵消重力。我用 KDL 从 URDF 建立双臂动力学，约每秒 200 次按当前关节角计算重力力矩，作为零刚度下的前馈力矩下发。补偿比例多数关节为 1，最后一个关节为 0.8，原因是那里夹爪或手柄的质量与质心最不确定，比例提到约 0.9 以上时该关节开始缓慢上漂，所以留 10%–20% 余量。从臂用位置-速度律跟踪主臂关节位置，前四个关节刚度 50、后三个 10，阻尼 1.2 到 0.2，带速度前馈、不带重力前馈。端到端延迟约 30 ms，运动中从臂滞后主臂约 3 个控制周期，笔记里稳态误差小于 0.02 rad。

**评分要点**：
- 说清重力补偿原理与"为什么不是 1"；
- 区分主臂零刚度前馈与从臂 PD 跟踪；
- 能量化延迟与跟踪误差，且带 roughly。

### L4：差速底盘、里程计与系统失效处理

**参考回答**：

"For the differential-drive base, the left and right wheel speeds are the linear speed minus or plus the angular speed times half the track width, divided by the wheel radius. My values are a wheel radius of 0.13 metres and a track width of 0.75 metres. I close a PID loop on the encoder speed at 200 hertz, in my notes with Kp 5, Ki 0.5, Kd 0, and output and integral limits. Odometry integrates the wheel speeds: the heading changes with the difference of the two wheels over the track width, and the position moves along the heading. I calibrated the wheel radius on a 5 metre straight line and the track width with ten in-place turns, and the error was about 2 percent on 5 metres, and about 30 degrees of heading after ten turns. For failures, the host sets the wheel speed to zero if the velocity command is older than 300 milliseconds, the arm driver freezes at the current position if the target is older than 100 milliseconds and disables after 500 milliseconds. I read the base board firmware too, and its own heartbeat timeout does not actually trigger, so the host-side timeout is the real safeguard; I report that as something I found, not something I fixed."

中文：差速底盘中，左右轮速为线速度减/加角速度乘半轮距，再除以轮半径。我的取值是轮半径 0.13 m、轮距 0.75 m。我在编码器速度上以 200 Hz 闭环 PID（笔记中 Kp 5、Ki 0.5、Kd 0，带输出和积分限幅）。里程计积分轮速：航向随两轮差除以轮距变化，位置沿航向移动。我用 5 m 直线标定轮半径、用原地转 10 圈标定轮距，5 m 误差约 2%，转 10 圈后航向误差约 30°。失效处理：速度命令超过 300 ms 无更新则上位机把轮速置零；臂驱动在目标超过 100 ms 时冻结在当前位置，500 ms 后失能。我也读过底盘板固件，其自身心跳超时实际不会触发，所以真正的兜底是上位机超时；我把它报告为读码发现，而不是我修复的东西。

**评分要点**：
- 差速运动学与里程计公式正确；
- 提到标定方法；
- 失效处理分层，且诚实区分"发现"与"修复"。

**卡壳点与应急说法**：
- 被问雅可比的推导：*"For a planar two-link arm I can derive it; for my seven-degree-of-freedom arm I used the library to compute it, and I understand how its rank relates to singularities."*
- 被问 KDL 递归牛顿-欧拉：*"The gravity term comes from the dynamics model; I used KDL's computation rather than implementing the recursion by hand."*
- 被问 URDF 参数来源：*"The masses come from the model and were checked against the real robot; I don't claim they are identified."*

**结束时你的反问**：
1. "Does your lab study teleoperation and learning from demonstration together, for example using teaching data to train policies?"
2. "What does the lab consider the main open problem in making manipulation reliable on real robots?"

## 7. 第 6 套：系统与安全（P6 / P4）

**面试官开场**："Your CV says you built a safe-execution chain on a multi-robot platform. What problem does it solve, and what did you build?"
中文：CV 上写你在多机器人平台上做了安全执行链，它解决什么问题？你做了什么？

```text
开场：安全执行链
 ├─ L1 提案→策略检查→执行→反馈：各阶段做什么
 │    └─ L2 一致的机器人状态：时间戳、坐标系、有效性
 │         └─ L3 发现/注册/同步/分发：QoS、租约、幂等
 │              └─ L4 怎么测试？哪些做不到？如何避免夸大
```

### L1：四阶段链

**参考回答**：

"The problem is that a high-level task plan, which might come from a scheduler, an operator, or an AI planner, is not safe to send directly to a robot. A plan can be stale, out of range, or simply not allowed in the current mode. The chain has four stages. First, an action proposal: a structured object with an ID, a source, an action type, parameters, a frame, an issue time, a validity time and an idempotency key. Second, a policy check, which returns accept, modify by clamping to the allowed range, reject, or ask for confirmation. The checks are typically about the schema, the permission, an action whitelist, the validity time, the mode, such as manual or emergency stop, the freshness of the robot state, and numeric ranges. Third, execution: the gateway only accepts commands that carry a short-lived approval token tied to the proposal, so there is no back door. Fourth, state feedback, from received and accepted to executing and done or failed. I developed and integrated this chain, and I'd describe the rules and numbers as our design."

中文：问题在于高层任务计划（可能来自调度器、操作员或 AI 规划器）不能安全地直接发给机器人：计划可能过期、超范围，或在当前模式下不被允许。链有四个阶段。其一动作提案：含 ID、来源、动作类型、参数、坐标系、签发时间、有效期与幂等键的结构化对象。其二策略检查，返回接受、钳位修改、拒绝或请求确认；检查典型涉及 schema、权限、动作白名单、有效期、模式（手动/急停）、机器人状态新鲜度与数值范围。其三执行：网关只接受带与提案绑定的短时批准票据的命令，所以没有后门。其四状态反馈，从已接收、已接受到执行中、完成或失败。我开发并集成了这条链，规则与数值我按"我们的设计"来讲。

**评分要点**：
- 讲清问题动机（计划来源不可信）；
- 四阶段含义准确；
- 无后门（票据）；
- 用词符合 CV：developed and integrated。

### L2：一致的机器人状态

**参考回答**：

"The policy check and the controller are only as good as the state they see. Sensors and actuators report at different rates, with different clocks and frames, so I standardised three things. Time: inside a device I use a monotonic clock, and across devices I synchronise with NTP, which gives an accuracy of a few to about ten milliseconds, so I use cross-device time only for logging and rough alignment, and never inside a control loop. Frames: I follow the common convention of x forward, y left, z up, with the frames map, odom, base link, laser link and imu link, and the extrinsics sit in one device description file that the state manager applies once at the entry. Validity: values must be finite and in range, the frame must be declared, the timestamps must be monotonic, the age must be below about three sensor periods, so 30 milliseconds for a 100 hertz IMU, and a value that repeats exactly for five frames is treated as frozen. Each message then gets a validity level: valid, stale, suspect or invalid."

中文：策略检查与控制器只和它们看到的状态一样好。传感器和执行器以不同频率、时钟、坐标系上报，所以我标准化三件事。时间：设备内用单调时钟，跨设备用 NTP 同步，精度约几毫秒到十几毫秒，所以跨设备时间只用于日志和粗对齐，绝不放进控制回路。坐标系：遵循 x 前、y 左、z 上的通用惯例，使用 map、odom、base_link、laser_link、imu_link，外参集中放在一个设备描述文件里，由状态管理器在入口一次性应用。有效性：值必须有限且在范围内，坐标系必须已声明，时间戳单调，年龄小于约 3 个传感器周期（100 Hz IMU 为 30 ms），连续 5 帧完全相同视为冻结。每条消息据此得到有效性等级：valid、stale、suspect、invalid。

**评分要点**：
- 三件事：时间、坐标系、有效性；
- 知道 NTP 精度限制，控制环不依赖跨设备时间；
- 有效性规则含阈值依据。

### L3：发现、QoS、租约与幂等

**参考回答**：

"On the M-DDS data layer, I describe it as a publish-subscribe middleware with domains, topics and quality-of-service policies, and I used it as a user, I did not change its internals. For discovery, a device announces itself every second with its ID, a capability summary hash and a version. If the lease of three seconds, three periods, expires, the device is marked offline and the upper layers are told. When it comes back, it registers its capabilities again, and a full state synchronisation runs. For QoS, high-rate state uses best effort, volatile and keep-last-one, with a deadline of about twice the period, since an old state is useless. Capabilities and health use reliable and transient-local, so a late joiner still gets the last value. Task commands and receipts use reliable with a small history. For tasks, delivery is at least once and the receiver deduplicates by the idempotency key, with a dedup window of about twice the validity time, and each robot has one task owner at a time; a higher priority task can pre-empt, and emergency stop is always highest. This fixed a bug where a task was sent twice after a reconnect."

中文：在 M-DDS 数据层上，我把它描述为带域、主题和 QoS 策略的发布订阅中间件，我是使用者，没有改它的内部。发现：设备每秒公告一次，含 ID、能力摘要哈希与版本；3 秒（3 个周期）租约到期则标记离线并通知上层；重新上线时重新注册能力并做一次完整状态同步。QoS：高频状态用 best effort、volatile、keep-last-1，deadline 约两倍周期，因为旧状态没用；能力与健康用 reliable + transient-local，使晚加入者仍能拿到最后一个值；任务命令与回执用 reliable 加小历史。任务：至少一次投递，接收方按幂等键去重，去重窗口约为有效期的两倍；每台机器人同一时刻只有一个任务所有者，高优先级可抢占，急停永远最高。这修复过重连后任务被重复下发的问题。

**评分要点**：
- 把 M-DDS 说成"使用者角度"，不乱讲内部；
- QoS 选择有理由；
- 至少一次 + 幂等的组合；
- 租约与重新上线流程。

### L4：测试、局限与"不夸大"

**参考回答**：

"For testing, unit tests use fake clocks and fake devices, so I can test timeouts and ordering without waiting. Contract tests run the same cases against every adapter, including a kinematics round trip, so a new adapter cannot break the interface. For integration, there is a bench with three small robots and a simulator. Fault injection uses network delay and loss with tc netem, killed processes and clock jumps, and I replay recorded state streams. The tests I did myself are on the capability model, the state, the coordination and the safety chain. Now the limits, honestly: the numbers I gave are design values, not measured performance; the end-to-end latency budget of under 100 milliseconds is a budget, not a measurement; and the safety chain is connected to the scheduler path, but I'm still adding test cases and audit fields. I also don't share the internal source, the full topic names or the customer information. If I were to improve it, I would add formal checks on the policy rules, for example testing that no accepted proposal can violate the limits, and measure the latency distribution properly."

中文：测试方面：单元测试用假时钟与假设备，无需等待即可测超时与顺序；契约测试对每个适配器跑同一套用例（含运动学往返），新适配器不会破坏接口；联调有 3 台小车加仿真的台架；故障注入用 tc netem 的网络延迟与丢包、杀进程、时钟跳变，并回放录制的状态流。我亲手做的是能力模型、状态、协同和安全链相关的测试。再诚实说局限：我给的数字是设计取值，不是实测性能；端到端延迟小于 100 ms 是预算，不是测量值；安全链已接入调度器路径，但我仍在补测试用例与审计字段。我也不透露内部源码、完整主题名与客户信息。若要改进，我会对策略规则加形式化检查（例如验证任何被接受的提案都不会违反限制），并认真测量延迟分布。

**评分要点**：
- 测试分层（单元、契约、联调、故障注入、回放）；
- 明确区分设计取值与实测；
- 不泄露内部细节；
- 提出具体改进。

**卡壳点与应急说法**：
- 被问 M-DDS 内部实现：*"I used it as the data layer and I'd rather not describe the internals; I can explain the standard DDS concepts it is based on."*
- 被问某个具体数值：*"In our design it's roughly that, but I don't have a measured figure to quote."*
- 被问是否多机器人编队：*"The coordination I built is at the information and task level: discovery, registration, state synchronisation and dispatch, not formation control."*

**结束时你的反问**：
1. "How does your group think about safety for robots driven by learned policies or language-model planners?"
2. "Are there opportunities in the programme to work with industry partners on robot platforms?"

## 本章要点速记

1. 追问树是"一句话 → 原理 → 参数 → 取舍"，每层 60–90 秒。
2. P1 的 LQR 是分析参照，P7 的 LQR 是主控制器；不要混用。
3. EKF 给连续状态，AMCL 给全局一致性，控制器工作在 odom 系。
4. 位姿跳变先查输入（时间、方向、坐标系），再动算法。
5. INT8 量化的关键是校准集覆盖部署分布；诚实报告精度损失。
6. Agent 只给建议，执行层有白名单、限幅、新鲜度、幂等，设备端有独立兜底。
7. CAN 负载手算：约 111 位/帧，按帧率累加；PID 增益与周期绑定，不要夸大回路频率。
8. 设计取值不是实测；被追问就说 "in our design, roughly"，并区分"发现"与"修复"。

## 自测题

1. 说出级联 PID 中"参考有界"的理由，以及内外环带宽比的作用。
2. 为什么控制器要放在 odom 系，而不是 map 系？
3. 解释 Hybrid-A* 的启发式为什么取最大值。
4. 云台板 CAN 总线约 63% 负载是怎么算出来的？
5. 阻尼最小二乘在奇异点附近换来了什么、付出了什么？
6. 如何回答"你的安全执行链延迟是多少"才不夸大？

**答案要点**：1. 防止要求过大倾角导致饱和；内环快 6–7 倍外环才视其为理想。2. 避免 AMCL 跳变进入控制。3. 两者都可采纳，取最大更紧。4. (5×1000 + 2×333) × 111 ≈ 629 kbit/s。5. 换来关节速度不爆炸，付出奇异方向的跟踪精度。6. 说 "under 100 ms is our design budget, not a measurement"，并说明怎么测。
