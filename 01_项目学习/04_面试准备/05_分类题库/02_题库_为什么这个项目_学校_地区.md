# 分类题库 02：为什么这个项目 / 学校 / 地区（Why this programme / university / Hong Kong or Macau / Greater Bay Area）

> **一句话定位**：这是"动机类"问题的题库：为什么选这个项目、这所学校、香港或澳门、大湾区，为什么读授课型硕士而不是读博或工作，为什么是现在。先给 16 道各校通用题，再给 11 个申请项目各 6 道专属题，共 82 题，每题都有英文回答、中文翻译和两个追问。

**本章学完你能……**

- 用"缺口 + 项目特征 + 对应关系 + 我能带来什么"的公式回答任何 "Why this programme?"；
- 针对 11 个项目，各说出至少三处有依据的具体特征（课程方向、学制、培养目标），并能诚实地说明哪些细节你还没核对；
- 回答港澳大湾区相关的地域问题，而不显得套话或暗示"港澳学位更容易"；
- 在被问到"是否申请了其他学校""是不是第一志愿"时，答得稳，不说谎，也不拆台。

**前置知识**：[核心叙事线](../01_面试总指南/03_核心叙事线_为什么读研_为什么这个方向.md)、[PS 与推荐信主线](../../00_申请画像/02_PS与推荐信主线.md)、[目标项目与面试画像总表](../../00_申请画像/05_目标项目与面试画像总表.md)、[各校面试准备](../02_各校面试准备/README.md)。

## 1. 本文件怎么用

1. **题号**：通用题用 Q1–Q16，项目专属题继续编号到 Q82。查找时按第 2 节的速查表找到你的项目，再去对应小节。
2. **回答公式**：缺口（一句，来自 PS）→ 项目的两个具体特征（只用有依据的）→ 它们如何对应缺口 → 我能带来什么。每题 40–60 秒。
3. **"我了解到……"原则**：学校官网的课程名、学分、面试形式很多尚未更新到 2027 轮次。凡不确定的信息，只用这类安全说法：
   - "From the programme page, my understanding is that ..."
   - "As far as I know, ... and I would be glad if you could tell me more."
   - "I have not seen the full course list yet, so I don't want to guess."
   不要说 "I know that you offer course X"，除非 X 在 PS 或官方核对表里有依据（速查表里标了来源）。
4. **不拆台、不比较**：不要说别的学校差、不要说排名。被问到比较时，只谈自己的缺口与项目的匹配。
5. **数据与事实**：和 CV 保持一致。项目细节数字（如果出现）取自各项目 README 的自查表。
6. **面试前核对清单**：每个项目小节末尾给出"面试前核对"一行，写明哪些事实还要到官网确认。核对前这些信息一律用"我了解到"表述。
7. **与其他题库的分工**：职业规划与研究兴趣见 [03 题库](./03_题库_职业规划与研究兴趣.md)；优缺点与压力题见 [04 题库](./04_题库_个人特质_优缺点_压力题.md)；英语与沟通见 [05 题库](./05_题库_英语能力与沟通.md)；自我介绍与追问见 [01 题库](./01_自我介绍_通用与按项目定制.md)。

> **注意**：HKUST(GZ) 具身智能项目的名称与当轮招生状态以官方为准（见 [画像总表](../../00_申请画像/05_目标项目与面试画像总表.md)）。面试里只谈"具身智能方向"，不断言项目细节。

## 3. 第一部分：各校通用题

### Q1. Why do you want to do a master's in Hong Kong or Macau, rather than on the mainland or somewhere else?

**中文题意**：为什么选择在香港或澳门读硕士，而不是内地或其他地方？

**考察点**：地域动机是否真实、是否暗含"港澳学位更容易"的想法。

**English answer**：There are three reasons. First, the programmes I applied to are taught in English and have an international environment, and I want to use technical English every day. Second, many of them are one or two years, which fits my plan to return to industry soon after I build the foundations I need. Third, the region is close to the robotics industry where I already work. I do not want to move far away from the hardware and the people I know. Other places have strong programmes too, but for my goal, this combination of language, length and location matters most to me.

**中文翻译**：有三个原因。第一，我申请的项目是英语授课、环境国际化的，我想每天使用技术英语。第二，很多项目是一到两年，符合我打好所需基础后尽快回到产业的计划。第三，这个地区靠近我已经工作的机器人产业，我不想远离我熟悉的硬件和人。别的地方也有很强的项目，但对我的目标来说，语言、学制和地点的组合最重要。

**追问 1**
- Q: Did you consider studying in another country?
- A: I looked mainly at this region, because my plan is to work in the Greater Bay Area. Studying here keeps me close to the industry.
- 中文：考虑过去别的国家读吗？——我主要看这个地区，因为我计划在大湾区工作，在这里读书能让我贴近产业。

**追问 2**
- Q: Is it easier to get into a Hong Kong programme?
- A: I don't think so. Programmes here are competitive, and I prepared for them seriously. My choice is about the curriculum and the location.
- 中文：港澳项目更容易进吗？——我不这么认为。这里的项目竞争激烈，我也认真准备；我的选择是基于课程和地点。

**关键词与易错点**：English-taught、international、close to industry。不要说 "easier"、"cheaper"、"for a visa"、"for the future of my family" 等动机。

### Q2. Why do you want to stay in the Greater Bay Area after graduation?

**中文题意**：为什么毕业后想留在大湾区？

**考察点**：职业目标的真实性与连贯性。

**English answer**：All three of my internships were in Shenzhen: a research institute, a robotics company and a platform team. So I have seen the region's robotics industry from research to products to platforms. It has a dense chain of hardware, from motors and sensors to manufacturing, and that is important for algorithm work, because I can test on real robots quickly. My goal is to become a robotics algorithm engineer in this region, working on perception, navigation and learning-based control. Studying here helps me keep close to people and problems that I will work on later.

**中文翻译**：我的三段实习都在深圳：一个研究院、一家机器人公司和一个平台团队。所以我从研究到产品再到平台，近距离看过这个地区的机器人产业。这里有从电机、传感器到制造的密集硬件产业链，这对算法工作很重要，因为我能很快在真机上测试。我的目标是在这个地区成为机器人算法工程师，做感知、导航和基于学习的控制。在这里读书，能让我保持与未来工作的人和问题的接近。

**追问 1**
- Q: Which companies would you like to work for?
- A: I don't have a single company in mind. I want a team that builds mobile or legged platforms and has real hardware to test on.
- 中文：想去哪些公司？——我没有锁定某一家；我想去做移动或足式平台、有真机可测的团队。

**追问 2**
- Q: Would you work outside the Greater Bay Area?
- A: My main goal is here, but I'm open to good opportunities elsewhere.
- 中文：会去大湾区以外工作吗？——主要目标在这里，但对好机会保持开放。

**关键词与易错点**：research to products to platforms、real hardware、close to industry。不要列举你没有联系过的公司名，不要把"薪资"当作理由。

### Q3. How will you adapt to living and studying in Hong Kong?

**中文题意**：你将如何适应在香港的学习和生活？

**考察点**：适应能力与对生活的现实认识。

**English answer**：I am used to living in Shenzhen, which is close to Hong Kong, so the distance and the basic culture feel familiar. I expect some differences, for example a faster pace, smaller living spaces, and daily life in English or Cantonese. For study, I will use the first weeks to review my mathematics, and to find a study group. For daily life, I will plan housing early, because I understand that it is expensive and limited. I will also join one or two student activities, so that I meet people outside my programme.

**中文翻译**：我习惯在深圳生活，深圳离香港很近，所以距离和基本文化感觉熟悉。我预期会有些不同，比如节奏更快、居住空间更小、日常用英语或粤语。学习上，我会用头几周复习数学并找学习小组。生活上，我会提早规划住宿，因为我了解到住宿昂贵且紧张。我也会参加一两个学生活动，认识项目以外的人。

**追问 1**
- Q: Do you speak Cantonese?
- A: Classes are in English, so I expect no problem in class. I'm happy to learn more Cantonese for daily life.
- 中文：你会粤语吗？——课堂是英语，我预期课堂上没问题；日常生活中我乐意多学粤语。

**追问 2**
- Q: What if housing is hard to find?
- A: I would apply for campus housing as early as possible, and have a plan B nearby. I'll check the university's housing information.
- 中文：找不到住宿怎么办？——我会尽早申请校内住宿，并准备附近的备选；我会核对学校的住宿信息。

**关键词与易错点**：familiar、plan housing early、study group。不要抱怨香港的生活；不确定的信息用 "I understand ... and will check"。

### Q4. What is your impression of Macau, and how will you integrate into study and life there?

**中文题意**：你对澳门的印象如何？打算怎样融入当地的学习和生活？

**考察点**：对地点的了解和适应态度（澳门院校面试的真实高频题）。

**English answer**：To be honest, my impression is general. Macau is a small, international city with a mix of Chinese and Portuguese heritage, and it is close to Zhuhai and Hengqin, so it connects well with the Greater Bay Area. I also know that universities there are building AI and robotics programmes. For life, I would live near the campus, join student activities, and explore the city on weekends. Because the city is small, I expect it to be easy to get around and meet classmates. Classes are in English, so I think language will not be a barrier in study.

**中文翻译**：说实话，我的印象比较概括。澳门是一座小而国际化的城市，有中国和葡萄牙文化的融合，靠近珠海和横琴，与大湾区联系紧密。我也知道那里的大学正在发展 AI 和机器人项目。生活上，我会住在校园附近，参加学生活动，周末探索城市。城市小，我预期出行和认识同学都比较方便。课堂是英语授课，我认为语言不会成为学习的障碍。

**追问 1**
- Q: Macau is small. Are you worried about the industry connection?
- A: Not much. It is close to Zhuhai and the Greater Bay Area, and I can use projects, events and internships to stay connected.
- 中文：澳门小，担心产业联系吗？——不太担心；它靠近珠海和大湾区，我可以通过项目、活动和实习保持联系。

**追问 2**
- Q: Do you know any special feature of Macau's universities?
- A: I know they are small and have close contact between students and faculty, but I have not studied the details, so I would not like to guess.
- 中文：知道澳门大学的特色吗？——我知道规模较小、师生接触较多，但没有深入研究，所以不想乱猜。

**关键词与易错点**：Chinese and Portuguese heritage、close to Zhuhai、honest about limited impression。"师生接触较多" 是常见印象但未核对，保持 "I know ... but" 的试探口吻。不要谈赌场；不要说 "Macau is just a place for tourism"。

### Q5. Why would you like a campus in Shenzhen, Dongguan or Guangzhou rather than in Hong Kong itself?

**中文题意**：为什么愿意去深圳/东莞/广州的校区，而不是香港本部？

**考察点**：对内地校区的认可度，避免显得"退而求其次"。

**English answer**：For me, the campus matters less than the curriculum and the community around it. Shenzhen, Dongguan and Guangzhou are all inside the Greater Bay Area, where I plan to work. A campus here puts me close to manufacturing, hardware suppliers and robotics teams, so I can see real problems during my studies. I also understand that these programmes are taught by the same universities and in English. I would not see a mainland campus as a second choice. I see it as a way to learn where the industry is.

**中文翻译**：对我来说，校区不如课程和周边的社区重要。深圳、东莞和广州都在我计划工作的大湾区内。这里的校区让我接近制造业、硬件供应商和机器人团队，学习期间就能看到真实问题。我也了解到这些项目由同一所大学、英语授课。我不会把内地校区看成第二选择，而看成接近产业所在地的方式。

**追问 1**
- Q: Do you care about the degree certificate?
- A: I care more about what I learn. I understand the programmes are run by the universities, and I will check the details of the award.
- 中文：你在意证书吗？——我更在意学到什么；我了解项目由大学开设，也会核对学位授予的细节。

**追问 2**
- Q: Would you move to the main campus later?
- A: I have not decided. If the programme allows exchange, I would be happy to consider it.
- 中文：以后会去本部吗？——我没有决定；如果项目允许交流，我乐意考虑。

**关键词与易错点**：same universities、English-taught、close to industry。不要贬低任何校区，也不要暗示"内地校区更容易进"。

### Q6. Why do you want a taught master's, rather than a PhD?

**中文题意**：为什么选择授课型硕士，而不是博士？

**考察点**：对研究与工程路径的清醒认识。

**English answer**：Because I do not have research experience yet, and I think I need two things first. One is a systematic foundation in optimisation, learning and manipulation. The other is a chance to try research on a small project, before I decide on years of it. My projects were engineering projects. My referees also say that I arrived at models robot by robot and tuned gains on the machine, and that the general theory is not something my undergraduate curriculum gave me. A taught master's is the right size for that. If I find during the programme that I love research, I would seriously consider a PhD later.

**中文翻译**：因为我还没有研究经验，我认为首先需要两样东西：一是最优化、学习和操作的系统基础；二是在决定投入多年之前，先在一个小项目里尝试研究。我的项目都是工程项目。我的推荐人也指出，我是逐台机器人推出模型、在机器上调增益，一般理论本科课程没有提供。授课型硕士的体量刚好适合。如果在项目中发现我热爱研究，之后我会认真考虑读博。

**追问 1**
- Q: Why not an MPhil, which has more research?
- A: I want broad coursework first. I'm not yet sure which research question I would commit to.
- 中文：为什么不读研究型硕士？——我想先有宽的课程基础，我还不确定会投入哪个研究问题。

**追问 2**
- Q: What research question interests you now?
- A: How to combine a model-based controller with learning, and how to keep perception robust to timing and frame errors.
- 中文：你现在对什么研究问题感兴趣？——如何把基于模型的控制器与学习结合，以及如何让感知对时序和坐标系误差更鲁棒。

**关键词与易错点**：foundation、small research project、open to PhD。推荐人的话要说 "my referees say"，不要念原文；不要说 "I hate research" 或 "PhD is too long"。

### Q7. You already have strong internship experience. Why not work instead of studying?

**中文题意**：你已有很强的实习经验，为什么不直接工作？

**考察点**：读研的必要性。

**English answer**：The internships are how I found my gaps. At Kaihong, I design a policy check for task plans, but I cannot yet evaluate a learned component in a principled way. At AIRS, I analysed gains with a linear model, but I could not go beyond linear models. In a company the focus is on what works before a deadline, so it is hard to study theory properly while delivering a product. A master's gives me structure and time. My plan is still to return to industry afterwards, in the Greater Bay Area, as a stronger algorithm engineer.

**中文翻译**：实习正是我发现缺口的方式。在深开鸿，我为任务计划设计策略检查，但还不能以有原则的方式评估一个学习得到的部件。在 AIRS，我用线性模型分析增益，但无法超越线性模型。在公司，重点是在期限前做出能用的东西，所以交付产品的同时很难系统学理论。硕士给我结构和时间。我的计划仍然是之后回到大湾区的产业，成为更强的算法工程师。

## 4. 第二部分：11 个项目专属题

### 4.1 PolyU — MSc in Intelligent Robotics Engineering

### Q17. Why Intelligent Robotics Engineering at PolyU, rather than a Computer Science programme?

**中文题意**：为什么选港理工智能机器人工程，而不是计算机科学项目？

**考察点**：专业选择的逻辑，以及对机器人"整机视角"的需要。

**English answer**：My work is on robots with real hardware, and I want a programme that treats the robot as a whole. A Computer Science programme would give me strong software, but less on mechanisms and sensing, and those are exactly where I lack structured training. In control and planning I have practice, but I want the theory behind it. So this programme fits both: it fills a gap, and it deepens what I already do.

**中文翻译**：我的工作是在有真实硬件的机器人上，我想要一个把机器人当成整体的项目。从项目页看，我的理解是它把机构、控制、运动规划和具身智能放在一个工程框架里。计算机科学项目能给我扎实的软件，但机构和传感方面较少，而这恰恰是我缺乏结构化训练的地方。在控制和规划上我有实践，想要背后的理论。所以这个项目两方面都匹配：补缺口，也深化我已在做的事。

**追问 1**
- Q: Why not a control or automation master's?
- A: Robotics brings together control, planning, mechanisms and AI. Control alone would miss navigation and the embodied part.
- 中文：为什么不读控制或自动化硕士？——机器人学把控制、规划、机构和 AI 放在一起；只读控制会缺少导航和具身部分。

**追问 2**
- Q: Are you applying to Computer Science programmes too?
- A: Yes, a few, because my work also has a software side. But for the robot as a whole, this programme is the closest fit.
- 中文：也申请计算机类项目吗？——是的，有几个，因为我的工作也有软件一面；但就机器人整体而言，这个项目最贴近。

**关键词与易错点**：mechanisms, control, motion planning, embodied intelligence、structured training。不要说 "CS is boring" 或 "mechanical is easy"。院系名称若不确定只说 "an engineering department"。

### Q18. This programme sits in an engineering department, and you have no mechanical background. How will you cope?

**中文题意**：项目属于工程系，你没有机械背景，怎么跟上？

**考察点**：对短板的自知与补救方案。

**English answer**：You are right that my coursework is in computer science. But my projects forced me to learn the hardware side: motors and CAN buses, IMUs, torque limits, and closed-chain kinematics on a wheel-legged robot. I also used URDF and gravity compensation on a dual-arm robot. What I have not had is structured training in mechanisms, and that is something I want from this programme. Before the term, I will review statics and rigid-body dynamics, so I can follow the mechanism courses. I think my control and software background will also help classmates with mechanical training.

**中文翻译**：您说得对，我的课程是计算机科学。但项目迫使我学习硬件方面：电机与 CAN 总线、IMU、力矩限幅，以及轮腿机器人上的闭链运动学；我也在双臂机器人上用过 URDF 和重力补偿。我没有的是机构学的结构化训练，这是我想从这个项目获得的。开学前我会复习静力学和刚体动力学，以便跟上机构类课程。我也认为我的控制和软件背景可以帮助有机械训练的同学。

**追问 1**
- Q: Have you designed any mechanism yourself?
- A: No. I worked on given platforms. I analysed their kinematics, but I did not design the mechanisms.
- 中文：自己设计过机构吗？——没有，我用的是现成平台；我分析过它们的运动学，但没有设计机构。

**追问 2**
- Q: Explain the leg of your wheel-legged robot in simple words.
- A: It is a five-bar linkage with two motors. Forward kinematics gives the foot position from the motor angles, and the Jacobian maps force at the foot to motor torques.
- 中文：简单讲讲轮腿的腿。——五连杆、两个电机；正运动学由电机角度得到足端位置，雅可比把足端力映射成电机力矩。

**关键词与易错点**：closed-chain kinematics、structured training、statics and dynamics。腿杆尺寸等数字不用报。不要说 "mechanics is not important for algorithms"。

### Q19. Which of our core courses interest you most, and why?

**中文题意**：核心课程里你最感兴趣的是哪些？为什么？

### Q43. You say your machine learning experience is practical. What do you expect from "Machine Learning: Principles and Practice"?

**中文题意**：你说机器学习偏实践，你期待"机器学习：原理与实践"带来什么？

**追问 1**
- Q: What is overfitting and how do you prevent it?
- A: The model fits noise in the training data and does poorly on new data. We use validation data, regularisation, more data, and early stopping.

**追问 2**

- 中文：精确率和召回率？——精确率是检测结果中正确的比例；召回率是真实目标中被找到的比例。

**关键词与易错点**：principles、evaluation、baseline、failure modes。不要宣称在训练上有深入经验；你的 mAP 数字只在被问到时说，并强调是小规模自测。

**考察点**：能否解释自己写的金句，并举例。

**English answer**：A planner assumes it knows where the robot is. **中文翻译**：规划器假设它知道机器人在哪里。如果位置估计跳变，规划出的路径就不再从真实位置出发，控制器可能跟随一条错误的路径。在我的车式机器人上，转弯或倒车时 map→odom 修正会跳 0.2–0.4 m、5°–10°，转向指令随之抽动。原因是输入不一致：激光时间戳、倒车速度的符号和坐标系。我修复它们并对修正做平滑后，阶跃变小。所以好规划的第一步是一致的状态。

**关键词与易错点**：consistent state、timestamps、sign of reverse speed、coordinate frames、rate limit。数值取 P5 自查表第 12 项（教材统一设计取值），说 "about"。

### Q81. In your statement you say "algorithms that remain reliable outside simulation". What are the main differences between simulation and a real robot?

**中文题意**：你说想让算法"在仿真之外保持可靠"，仿真与真机的主要差别是什么？

**考察点**：sim-to-real 的理解，用自己的例子说。

**English answer**：I see four main differences. First, actuator limits and delay: a simulator may send torque instantly, but a real motor saturates and responds with a lag. Third, contact and friction: they are hard to model, and they switch suddenly, for example when a wheel leaves the ground. Fourth, safety and recovery: a real robot can fall and be damaged. My examples are the ballbot saturation and the wheel-legged contact switching. A learned policy trained in simulation will meet all of these, so evaluation outside simulation is essential.

**中文翻译**：我看到四个主要差别。第一，执行器限制和延迟：仿真可以瞬时给力矩，真实电机会饱和并有滞后。第二，传感器噪声、偏置和时序：真实 IMU 有偏置，信号到达时间不同。第三，接触和摩擦：难以建模，且会突然切换，比如轮子离地时。第四，安全和恢复：真实机器人会倒下、会损坏。我的例子是球形机器人的饱和和轮腿的接触切换。在仿真里训练的学习策略会遇到所有这些，所以在仿真之外评估是必需的。

**追问 1**
- Q: How would you reduce the gap?

- 中文：怎样缩小差距？——在仿真里加入真实的延迟和噪声，参数随机化，并在硬件上带安全限制逐步测试。

- 中文：做过域随机化吗？——没有；我知道这个想法，想在项目里尝试。

**关键词与易错点**：actuator limits and delay、sensor bias、contact、safety。"domain randomisation" 只说没做过；不要为了显得懂而扩展到没有依据的细节。

| 第 5 天 | 其他同类项目的专属题各 1 道（避免串校） | 45 分钟 |
| 第 6 天 | 模拟：朋友随机抽 5 题，只给英文题目，限时作答 | 45 分钟 |
| 第 7 天 | 核对官网未核对项；更新你的"我了解到"清单 | 60 分钟 |

### 5.3 常见错误回顾

1. 把一个项目的课程名带进另一个项目。
2. 说出没有核对过的学制、学费和面试形式。
3. 对澳门/香港生活做过度的断言。
4. 回答太长：超过 60 秒的动机题会被打断。
5. 在 "Why us" 里提到别的学校的名字。
