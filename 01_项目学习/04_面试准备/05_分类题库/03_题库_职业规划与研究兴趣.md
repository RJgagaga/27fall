# 分类题库 03：职业规划与研究兴趣（Career plan, role, research interests and trends）

> **一句话定位**：这一份题库覆盖"你想做什么、想学什么、怎么看行业"：短中长期规划、机器人算法工程师这个岗位（感知、导航、基于学习的控制）、研究兴趣、读研后想补的理论基础、对 embodied AI / VLA / 人形与轮腿机器人的看法，以及对"AI 与安全执行"的立场。共 56 题，每题有英文回答、中文翻译和两个追问。

**本章学完你能……**

- 用同一条主线（做过真机上的控制与平台；缺系统理论；回到大湾区做机器人算法）回答任何职业规划类问题，且短、中、长期互相一致；
- 把"感知 / 导航 / 基于学习的控制"三个方向各讲出自己已有的东西和缺的东西；
- 对研究兴趣给出"兴趣 + 动机 + 想验证的问题"，而不假装有成熟课题；
- 回答行业趋势类开放题时，用"观察、挑战、我的看法、我会做什么"的结构，并避免预言和绝对化；
- 把 P6 的"动作提议、策略检查、执行、状态反馈"链讲成自己对 AI 与安全的立场。

## 1. 本文件怎么用

1. **主线只有一条**：我做过让机器人在真机上稳定运转的控制、导航和平台软件；下一步要补上让这些系统"被设计而不仅是被调出来"的数学与学习基础；然后回到大湾区做机器人算法工程师（感知、导航、基于学习的控制），长期成长为能掌管机器人产品算法栈的人。所有职业规划题都是这条线的不同切面。
2. **措辞要和 PS 一致**。CityU 东莞 CS 的 PS 写的是 "software engineer for robot platforms and motion algorithms"，其余多为 "robotics algorithm engineer"。口头统一为"机器人平台与运动算法方向的工程师"，不要自相矛盾。
3. **趋势题没有标准答案**：评委看的是你是否有观察、有判断、有边界。凡是外部事实（某公司、某论文、某年份、某数据）一律不说，除非有可靠依据；用 "I think"、"as I understand it"、"it seems that"。本文件所有趋势类答案中的外部判断都属于"我的理解"，未逐条核对，面试前如果你读到了更具体的材料，可以替换成更具体的一句，但不要编造。
4. **研究兴趣不夸大**：你没有论文，没有结构化研究经历。答案里的研究问题全部是"来自项目的问题"，不是已做成果。
5. **诚实模板**：被问到你没做过的（强化学习、VLA、人形机器人、论文阅读等），先说"没有"，再说你的理解和想怎么学。
6. **每题格式**：英文题目、中文题意、考察点、英文回答（口语，可直接说）、中文翻译、两个追问、关键词与易错点。
7. 配套阅读：[自我介绍](./01_自我介绍_通用与按项目定制.md)、[为什么这个项目](./02_题库_为什么这个项目_学校_地区.md)、[个人特质与压力题](./04_题库_个人特质_优缺点_压力题.md)。

> **注意**：不要在趋势题里说"X 公司的 Y 机器人很厉害"之类你无法展开的话；面试官一追问就露馅。

## 2. A 组：职业规划

### Q1. What is your short-term career plan after graduation?

**中文题意**：毕业后的短期职业规划是什么？

**考察点**：目标清晰度，与申请项目的一致性。

**English answer**：My short-term plan is to work as a robotics algorithm engineer in the Greater Bay Area, in a team that builds mobile or legged robots and has real hardware to test on. I would like to work on perception, navigation or learning-based control. In the first two years, I want to become reliable in one module, for example state estimation and control of a platform, and to learn how a team ships a robot product. I know that this plan needs stronger foundations in optimisation and learning, and that is why I am applying for this master's first.

**中文翻译**：我的短期计划是在大湾区做机器人算法工程师，加入做移动或足式机器人、有真机可测的团队，做感知、导航或基于学习的控制。头两年，我想在一个模块上变得可靠，比如平台的状态估计与控制，并学会团队如何交付机器人产品。我知道这个计划需要更扎实的最优化和学习基础，所以我先申请读这个硕士。

**追问 1**
- Q: How do you plan to achieve it?
- A: By studying the foundations in the master's, doing a project with real data, and keeping contact with robotics teams here.
- 中文：打算怎样实现？——在硕士里学基础，做一个用真实数据的项目，并与这里的机器人团队保持联系。

**追问 2**
- Q: Which module do you want to own first?
- A: State estimation or balance control, because I have the most experience there.
- 中文：想先负责哪个模块？——状态估计或平衡控制，因为我在这里经验最多。

**关键词与易错点**：Greater Bay Area、real hardware、perception, navigation, learning-based control。不要说"随便做什么"，也不要承诺具体公司。

### Q2. Where do you hope to be professionally in five years?

**中文题意**：五年后希望在职业上处于什么位置？

**考察点**：规划的现实性与成长路径。

**English answer**：In five years, I hope to be an engineer whom a robotics team trusts with a complete module. For example, the localisation and control of a mobile or legged platform. I would like to have brought at least one learning-based component into a real system after careful testing and with safety checks around it. I also want to be someone who can explain why an algorithm works and when it will fail, to teammates and to people outside the team. That is not a fixed title. It is the kind of trust that I want to earn.

**中文翻译**：五年后，我希望成为一个被机器人团队信任、负责完整模块的工程师，比如移动或足式平台的定位与控制。我希望至少把一个基于学习的部件在仔细测试和安全检查之后带进真实系统。我也希望成为能向队友和团队之外的人解释算法为何有效、何时会失效的人。这不是一个固定头衔，而是我想赢得的信任。

**追问 1**
- Q: Do you want to become a manager?
- A: Not as a first goal. I want to be technically strong first, and then help others grow.
- 中文：想当管理者吗？——不是第一目标；先在技术上扎实，再帮助他人成长。

**追问 2**
- Q: What would be a sign that you are on track?
- A: When I can design a method for a new problem, not only tune an existing one.
- 中文：怎样算走在轨道上？——当我能为新问题设计方法，而不只是调现有的方法。

**关键词与易错点**：trusted with a module、learning-based component、explain when it fails。不要说具体职位头衔或薪资目标。

### Q3. What is your long-term goal?

**中文题意**：你的长期目标是什么？

**考察点**：愿景是否与现有经历连贯，不夸大。

**English answer**：In the long term, I would like to grow into someone who can own the algorithm stack of a robot product, and who understands how algorithms, software and hardware fit together. I think my background in control and platform software, with a stronger foundation in learning and optimisation, is a good preparation for that. I do not want to say it as a title. It means that I can make technical decisions across perception, planning and control, and be responsible for the quality of the whole system. I know it takes many years, and I'm ready to build it step by step.

**中文翻译**：长期来看，我希望成长为能掌管机器人产品算法栈、理解算法、软件和硬件如何配合的人。我认为我在控制与平台软件的背景，加上更扎实的学习和最优化基础，是很好的准备。我不想把它说成头衔，它意味着我能在感知、规划和控制之间做技术决策，并对整个系统的质量负责。我知道这需要许多年，我准备一步步建设。

**追问 1**
- Q: What would stop you from reaching it?
- A: Staying too narrow, or losing contact with real hardware. I will keep both breadth and hands-on practice.
- 中文：什么会阻碍你？——变得太窄，或脱离真机；我会保持广度和动手实践。

**追问 2**
- Q: Is that realistic?
- A: I think it is a direction, not a promise. I'll check it every year against what I can actually do.
- 中文：这现实吗？——我把它看作方向而不是承诺；每年对照我实际的能力做检查。

**关键词与易错点**：own the algorithm stack（PS 原句风格）、across perception, planning and control。CS 版的变体是 "lead the software side of a robot product"。长期规划轻描淡写，不要夸大。

### Q4. How will this master's degree help you reach your career goals?

**中文题意**：这个硕士怎样帮你达到职业目标？

**考察点**：学位价值的具体性。

**English answer**：It helps in three ways. First, the courses give me the foundations I lack: optimisation, probability and estimation, and learning. Second, projects let me test those ideas on a real system and in a fair way, which I have not done at that level. Third, it gives me time and supervision to practise the research habits I will need: reading papers, defining a question, and writing down results. After that, I can join a robotics team and work on harder problems, because I can design and evaluate methods, not only tune them.

**中文翻译**：它在三方面有帮助。第一，课程给我缺少的基础：最优化、概率与估计、学习。第二，项目让我在真实系统上公平地检验这些想法，我还没在那个层次上做过。第三，它给我时间和指导，练习我需要的研究习惯：读论文、定义问题、记录结果。之后我可以加入机器人团队去做更难的问题，因为我能设计和评估方法，而不只是调参。

**追问 1**
- Q: Which one matters most?
- A: The foundations, because projects and habits depend on them.
- 中文：哪一个最重要？——基础，因为项目和习惯都依赖它。

**追问 2**
- Q: Can you reach the same goal without the degree?
- A: Slowly, maybe. A programme is a shorter and more structured path.
- 中文：没有学位能达到吗？——也许可以但较慢；项目是更短、更有结构的路径。

**关键词与易错点**：foundations、fair tests、research habits。不要把学位说成"拿到文凭"；要说学到什么、做什么。

### Q5. What skills do you still lack for your target role?

**中文题意**：你离目标岗位还缺哪些技能？

**考察点**：自我评估的诚实度与具体度。

**English answer**：I see four gaps. The first is optimisation and learning: I have used LQR, MPC and inverse kinematics as tools, but I do not have the theory, and I have not trained a policy on a robot. The second is probabilistic estimation at a deeper level than using filters. The third is perception beyond deploying a detector, for example geometry and evaluation. The fourth is the research method: setting up fair experiments and reporting them. I am also working on a softer skill: explaining my work in English in a short and clear way. These gaps are the reason for my application.

**中文翻译**：我看到四个缺口。第一是最优化和学习：我把 LQR、MPC 和逆运动学当工具用，但没有理论，也没在机器人上训练过策略。第二是比"使用滤波器"更深的概率估计。第三是除部署检测器之外的感知，比如几何和评估。第四是研究方法：设置公平的实验并报告。我也在练一项软技能：用英语简短清楚地讲我的工作。这些缺口就是我申请的原因。

**追问 1**
- Q: Which gap will you close first?
- A: Optimisation, because it supports learning, estimation and control.
- 中文：先补哪个？——最优化，因为它支撑学习、估计和控制。

**追问 2**
- Q: How will you know that the gap is closed?
- A: When I can derive a method, implement it, and explain its limits on a task I have not seen.
- 中文：怎么知道补上了？——当我能在没见过的任务上推导方法、实现它，并说明其局限。

**关键词与易错点**：four gaps、optimisation and learning、research method。不要把四个缺口说成"我什么都不会"；先说已有的再说缺的。

### Q6. Would you consider doing a PhD after the master's?

**中文题意**：硕士后会考虑读博吗？

**考察点**：对研究兴趣的真实度，不绝对化。

**English answer**：I'm open to it. My immediate plan is to work in industry, because I enjoy building robots and my goal is a robot product team. But if during the master's I find that I enjoy research questions enough, and I have a supervisor to work with, I would seriously consider a PhD. At the moment, I don't want to commit before I have done a real research project. I think that is the honest way to decide, because I have not done structured research yet.

**中文翻译**：我持开放态度。我的近期计划是进入产业，因为我喜欢做机器人，目标是机器人产品团队。但如果硕士期间发现自己足够喜欢研究问题，并有导师合作，我会认真考虑读博。现在我不想在做过真正的研究项目之前下承诺。我认为这是诚实的决定方式，因为我还没做过结构化研究。

**追问 1**
- Q: What would make you choose a PhD?
- A: If I find a question I want to spend years on, and a supervisor who supports it.
- 中文：什么会让你选择读博？——找到一个愿意花数年的问题，以及支持它的导师。

**追问 2**
- Q: What would make you choose industry?
- A: If I enjoy building and testing more than writing papers, which is what I think now.
- 中文：什么会让你选择工作？——如果我更享受做和测，而不是写论文，这也是我现在的想法。

**关键词与易错点**：open to it、immediate plan is industry、real research project。不要说"一定读博"或"绝不读博"。

### Q7. Do you want to start your own company someday?

**中文题意**：你以后想创业吗？

**考察点**：现实感与团队意识。

**English answer**：I haven't planned it, and I don't want to say yes just because it sounds ambitious. What I enjoy is building a robot system with a team and making it reliable. In the next years, I want to learn how a product is built, from algorithms to hardware to testing, and I think that is needed before any company of my own. If a chance appears later, with a team and a real problem, I would think about it carefully. For now, I want to learn from a strong team.

**中文翻译**：我没有规划过，也不想为了听起来有雄心就说是。我享受的是与团队一起搭建机器人系统并使其可靠。未来几年，我想学习产品是怎么做出来的，从算法到硬件到测试，我认为在有自己的公司之前需要这个。如果以后出现机会，有团队和真实问题，我会认真考虑。现在，我想向强的团队学习。

**追问 1**
- Q: What skills would you need?
- A: Product thinking, leading people and managing cost. I don't have those yet.
- 中文：需要什么技能？——产品思维、带人、成本管理；我现在都还没有。

**追问 2**
- Q: Have you led a team?
- A: Not formally. In the RoboMaster team I was responsible for three control loops, but I did not manage people.
- 中文：带过团队吗？——没有正式带过；在 RoboMaster 战队我负责三类控制回路，但没有管理人。

**关键词与易错点**：haven't planned、learn from a team、product thinking。注意 CV 只写 "responsible for" 三类回路，不写 led，所以不要说自己带过团队。

### Q8. What would a perfect job look like for you?

**中文题意**：你理想的工作是什么样的？

**考察点**：价值观与岗位匹配。

**English answer**：A perfect job has four parts. First, real robots that I can test on, because I learn fastest from hardware. Second, a team with strong people and mentors, who ask precise questions and give honest feedback. Third, a hard problem that mixes algorithms and systems, such as navigation or balance control for a platform that will actually ship. Fourth, a culture that treats safety and documentation as part of engineering. Salary matters, but in the first years, learning speed is more important to me.

**中文翻译**：理想的工作有四个部分。第一，有可测试的真实机器人，因为我从硬件学得最快。第二，有强的同事和导师，会问精准的问题并给出诚实的反馈。第三，一个把算法和系统混合在一起的难题，比如真会交付的平台的导航或平衡控制。第四，把安全和文档当作工程一部分的文化。薪水重要，但在最初几年，学习速度对我更重要。

**追问 1**
- Q: Large company or start-up?
- A: Either, as long as it has real hardware and good mentors. I've seen a research institute and two companies, and I learned from each.
- 中文：大公司还是创业公司？——都可以，只要有真机和好导师；我见过一个研究院和两家公司，各有所学。

**追问 2**
- Q: What would make you leave a job?
- A: If I could no longer learn, or the team did not care about safety.
- 中文：什么会让你离开？——如果不再能学习，或团队不重视安全。

**关键词与易错点**：real robots、mentors、hard problem、safety and documentation。不要说"钱多事少"。

### Q9. What would you do if your first job is not in robotics?

**中文题意**：如果第一份工作不在机器人领域怎么办？

**考察点**：弹性与长期坚持。

**English answer**：I would try to keep the connection to robotics. Many skills transfer: embedded and C++ programming, real-time systems, data pipelines and testing. I would choose a role where those skills grow, and I would keep my own robotics projects going on weekends, with ROS 2 and simulation. I would also stay in touch with the robotics community and apply again for robotics roles when I have a stronger profile. I don't see it as a failure. I see it as a longer path to the same goal.

**中文翻译**：我会尽量保持与机器人的联系。很多技能可以迁移：嵌入式和 C++ 编程、实时系统、数据管线和测试。我会选择能让这些技能增长的角色，并在周末继续做自己的机器人项目，用 ROS 2 和仿真。我也会与机器人社区保持联系，在背景更强时再申请机器人岗位。我不把它看作失败，而是通往同一目标的更长路径。

**追问 1**
- Q: What would you do on those weekends?
- A: A small navigation or balance project in simulation, with clear tests, and a short write-up.
- 中文：周末做什么？——在仿真里做一个小的导航或平衡项目，有清晰的测试和简短的总结。

**追问 2**
- Q: How long would you wait?
- A: I would review every six months and decide what to improve.
- 中文：会等多久？——每半年回顾一次，决定改进什么。

**关键词与易错点**：skills transfer、weekend projects、longer path。不要表现得对"非机器人工作"不屑。

### Q10. How will you keep learning after graduation?

**中文题意**：毕业后怎样继续学习？

**考察点**：学习习惯的可信度。

**English answer**：I have three habits that I plan to keep. First, I read the documentation and the source of the tools I use, because that is how I learned ROS 2, OpenHarmony and the chip manuals. Second, I try small examples by hand before using a method on a robot, and I write short notes about what I learned. Third, I ask precise questions to people who know more, and I try to explain what I learned to others. I'd add a fourth after the master's: to read research papers regularly, and to reproduce one result every few months.

**中文翻译**：我有三个想保持的习惯。第一，读我所用工具的文档和源码，我就是这样学 ROS 2、OpenHarmony 和芯片手册的。第二，在把方法用到机器人上之前先手算小例子，并写简短的笔记。第三，向懂得更多的人问精准的问题，并尝试把所学讲给别人。读完硕士后我会加第四个：定期读研究论文，每隔几个月复现一个结果。

**追问 1**
- Q: How do you read a paper?
- A: I read the abstract and figures first, then the method, and I try to reproduce a key equation or result in a small example.
- 中文：怎么读论文？——先读摘要和图，再读方法，并尝试用小例子复现关键公式或结果。

**追问 2**
- Q: What did you learn recently by yourself?
- A: On the job, how timestamps and validity checks make the robot state consistent across sensors.
- 中文：最近学了什么？——在工作中学到时间戳和有效性检查如何让多传感器的机器人状态保持一致。

### Q30. Why optimisation? How have you used it already?

**中文题意**：为什么要学最优化？你已经怎么用过？

**考察点**：把"工具使用"提升到"需要理论"。

**English answer**：I have used optimisation as a tool in three places. **中文翻译**：我在三个地方把最优化当工具用过。双臂遥操作中，逆运动学被作为带约束的二次规划求解，阻尼减小了奇异点附近的速度尖峰。在 AIRS，我用 LQR 分析稳定性与控制量的权衡。在车式机器人上，我用了 MPC，它在短时域内求解带约束的优化。但我是在没有理论的情况下用的：凸性、对偶性以及求解器为何收敛。我想知道一个问题何时容易求解、何时不容易，以及如何表述我自己的问题。

**追问 1**

- 中文：MPC 用什么求解器？——OSQP，带热启动；单次求解几毫秒。

### Q31. Why probability and estimation?

**中文题意**：为什么要学概率与估计？

### Q33. Why do you want to learn manipulation and dynamics more formally?

### Q38. Why do you find wheel-legged robots interesting, and what is their future?

**中文题意**：为什么觉得轮腿机器人有意思？它的前景如何？

**考察点**：把项目经验转化成观点。

**English answer**：A wheel-legged robot combines two things: the speed and efficiency of wheels, and the adaptability of legs. On my lab project, the leg length could change while the robot kept its body balanced and followed velocity commands. That means the robot can change its height and absorb some changes in the ground. The control is interesting because the model changes with leg length, which is why I used gain scheduling, and because contact switching matters. As I understand it, such robots may be useful where the floor is mostly flat but has obstacles, though I think the application is still being explored.

**中文翻译**：轮腿机器人结合了两样东西：轮子的速度和效率，以及腿的适应性。在我的实验室项目里，腿长可以变化，机器人仍保持机身平衡并跟踪速度指令。这意味着机器人可以改变高度并吸收部分地面变化。控制之所以有趣，是因为模型随腿长变化，所以我用了增益调度，也因为接触切换很重要。据我的理解，这类机器人可能适用于地面大体平坦但有障碍的场景，不过我认为应用仍在探索。

**追问 1**
- Q: How does gain scheduling work in your project?
- A: I fitted the LQR gains as polynomials of the leg length, and I used the current leg length to choose the gains online.
- 中文：你项目里增益调度怎么做？——把 LQR 增益拟合成腿长的多项式，在线用当前腿长选增益。

**追问 2**
- Q: What are the weaknesses of this design?
- A: More mechanical complexity, and the control must handle contact changes, such as leaving the ground.
- 中文：这种设计的弱点？——机械更复杂，控制要处理接触变化，比如离地。

**关键词与易错点**：wheels + legs、leg length changes the model、gain scheduling、contact switching。不要说你的机器人能跳跃或越障（CV 没有）；增益拟合细节（三次多项式、12 个增益）取自 P7 自查表，被问到再说。

### Q39. Will learning-based control replace classical control?

**中文题意**：基于学习的控制会取代经典控制吗？

**考察点**：平衡的观点。

**English answer**：I don't think it will replace it. I think they will combine. Classical control has strong points: we understand it, we can analyse its stability, and it is predictable and fast. Learning can handle effects that are hard to model, and it can adapt from data. But a learned policy needs data, may fail in new situations, and is harder to certify. In a real robot, I would expect a mixed design: a classical controller as a base and for safety, and learning where the model is weak. That is also why I want to study both, to know where each is the right tool.

**中文翻译**：我不认为会取代，我认为它们会结合。经典控制有强项：我们理解它，可以分析它的稳定性，它可预测且快。学习能处理难以建模的效应，并能从数据中适应。但学习策略需要数据，在新情况下可能失败，也更难认证。在真实机器人上，我预期是混合设计：经典控制器作基础并保障安全，学习用在模型薄弱的地方。这也是我想两者都学的原因，知道各自什么时候是合适的工具。

**追问 1**
- Q: Which situations favour learning?
- A: Contact-rich tasks and unmodelled effects, like friction, where a good model is hard to write.
- 中文：哪些情形有利于学习？——接触丰富的任务和未建模效应（如摩擦），难以写出好模型的地方。

**追问 2**
- Q: How would you certify a learned controller?
- A: I don't know the full answer. I'd start with limits, monitors and many tests in simulation and on hardware.
- 中文：怎么认证学习得到的控制器？——我不知道完整答案；会从限幅、监控器以及仿真和硬件上的大量测试开始。

**关键词与易错点**：combine not replace、classical as base and safety、learning for what the model misses。第二追问承认不知道比编造好。

### Q40. How much can simulation replace testing on a real robot?

**中文题意**：仿真能在多大程度上替代真机测试？

**考察点**：对仿真价值与局限的看法。

**English answer**：Simulation is very useful for development: it is fast, safe and repeatable. I used MuJoCo to check signs and trajectories, and for inverse kinematics. But I think it cannot replace real tests for validation. A simulator does not contain everything: actuator limits, delay, sensor bias and contact are only partly modelled. In my projects, many failures appeared first on the real robot: saturation on the ballbot, ground-contact transitions on the wheel-legged robot. So I use simulation to prepare and to find mistakes early, and I use the real robot, step by step and with safety limits, to decide whether the design works.

**中文翻译**：仿真对开发非常有用：快速、安全、可重复。我用 MuJoCo 检查符号和轨迹，并做逆运动学。但我认为它不能替代真机验证。仿真器没有包含全部：执行器限制、延迟、传感器偏置和接触只是部分被建模。在我的项目里，很多故障先在真机上出现：球形机器人的饱和、轮腿机器人的触地切换。所以我用仿真做准备并尽早发现错误，用真机、逐步并带安全限制，来判断设计是否有效。

**追问 1**
- Q: What should a good simulation include?
- A: Realistic delay, noise and actuator limits, and parameters that can vary.
- 中文：好的仿真应包含什么？——真实的延迟、噪声和执行器限制，以及可变化的参数。

**追问 2**
- Q: Did you use simulation to tune gains?
- A: No. I used simulation to check signs and directions. I tuned the gains on the real robot.
- 中文：用仿真整定过增益吗？——没有；我用仿真检查符号和方向，增益是在真机上整定的。

**关键词与易错点**：fast, safe, repeatable、cannot replace validation、step by step。这与 P1 口径一致（仿真用于符号/轨迹检查，增益以实机为准）。

### Q41. What role can large language models play in robotics?

**中文题意**：大语言模型在机器人领域能起什么作用？

**考察点**：对 LLM 的合理定位。

**English answer**：I think the best role today is in high-level understanding and planning: turning a natural-language request into a task plan, or helping with the interface between people and robots. They are less suitable for low-level, fast and safety-critical control. The risks are known: they can produce plausible but wrong plans, they are not always consistent, they have latency, and they do not know the physical state unless we give it to them. So I would use them as a source of proposals, and put validation, limits and feedback around them. That is the approach in my gardening system and in the platform work at Kaihong.

**中文翻译**：我认为目前最好的角色是高层理解与规划：把自然语言请求变成任务计划，或帮助人与机器人的界面。它们不太适合底层、快速、关乎安全的控制。风险是已知的：可能给出看似合理却错误的计划，不总是一致，有延迟，且除非我们提供，否则不知道物理状态。所以我把它们当作提议的来源，并在周围加上校验、限制和反馈。这就是我的园艺系统和深开鸿平台工作的做法。

**追问 1**
- Q: How did your gardening system use a language model?
- A: It combined observations and soil data to produce recommendations or constrained requests. The commands still passed a whitelist and a policy check.
- 中文：园艺系统怎么用语言模型？——它结合观测和土壤数据给出建议或受约束的请求；命令仍要通过白名单和策略检查。

**追问 2**
- Q: What would be a dangerous use?
- A: Letting a model send motor commands directly, with no checks or limits.
- 中文：什么用法是危险的？——让模型直接发电机命令，没有检查或限制。

### Q42. Robot data is expensive. What do you think about imitation learning and data collection?

**中文题意**：机器人数据很贵，你怎么看模仿学习与数据采集？

**考察点**：对数据瓶颈的理解。

**English answer**：I think data is one of the main bottlenecks, because collecting robot data takes time, hardware and people. Imitation learning is attractive because a human can show the task, for example by teleoperation. Its main problem is distribution shift: the robot can reach states that the demonstrations did not cover, and errors then grow. My own work with VR teleoperation of a dual-arm robot is a way a person can control a robot naturally, though I did not use it to collect training data. If I did, I would care about the quality of the data, its timestamps and consistency, because I know from my projects how bad data breaks a system.

**中文翻译**：我认为数据是主要瓶颈之一，因为采集机器人数据需要时间、硬件和人力。模仿学习有吸引力，因为人可以示范任务，比如通过遥操作。它的主要问题是分布偏移：机器人可能到达示范没覆盖的状态，误差随之增大。我做的双臂 VR 遥操作是人自然控制机器人的一种方式，不过我没有用它采集训练数据。如果做，我会重视数据质量、时间戳和一致性，因为我从项目里知道坏数据会如何破坏系统。

**追问 1**
- Q: What is distribution shift?
- A: The data seen at test time is different from the training data, so the model makes errors it never learned to correct.
- 中文：什么是分布偏移？——测试时的数据与训练数据不同，模型出现它没学过如何纠正的错误。

**追问 2**
- Q: How would you improve the data quality?
- A: Consistent timestamps and frames, checks for bad frames, and clear logging of the setup.
- 中文：怎么提高数据质量？——一致的时间戳和坐标系、坏帧检查，以及清晰记录实验设置。

**关键词与易错点**：data bottleneck、imitation learning、distribution shift、data quality。明确"没用遥操作采集过训练数据"。

### Q45. How will AI tools change the work of robotics engineers?

**中文题意**：AI 工具会怎样改变机器人工程师的工作？

**中文翻译**：我认为 AI 工具会让一些工作更快，比如写样板代码、读文档、探索想法。但责任仍在工程师。机器人是物理系统，限制或坐标系的一个错误可能造成损坏。所以重要的技能会是判断结果对不对、仔细测试、理解系统。我谨慎地用 AI 工具做解释和初稿，但会通过运行测试或手算检查结果。我认为理解基础的工程师会比不理解的人更好地使用这些工具。

- Q: Do you allow AI to write your control code?
- A: I might use it for a first draft, but I read every line and test it. I never run unchecked code on hardware.
- 中文：你让 AI 写控制代码吗？——可能用于初稿，但每一行我都读并测试；从不在硬件上运行未经检查的代码。

**追问 2**
- Q: How should a student use AI tools in a course?
- A: To learn and to check understanding, within the course rules, not to replace the learning.
- 中文：学生在课程里该怎么用 AI 工具？——在课程规则内用来学习和检查理解，而不是替代学习。

**关键词与易错点**：faster but responsibility remains、check by running tests、fundamentals。不要说"我完全依赖 AI"；也不要否认使用。

### Q46. What do you think about open-source in robotics, such as ROS 2?

**中文题意**：你怎么看机器人领域的开源，比如 ROS 2？

**考察点**：对开源生态的态度与诚实的贡献边界。

**English answer**：Open-source is very important in robotics, because the field needs many components and no team can build all of them. I used ROS 2 in the navigation project, and open-source packages for filtering and planning. The benefits are speed, shared knowledge and testing by many users. The risks are quality differences, versions that change, and unclear documentation. So I read the documentation and the source, and I write tests around the parts I depend on. I have not yet contributed code upstream. That is something I would like to do, maybe with a small fix or documentation improvement, during or after the programme.

**中文翻译**：开源在机器人领域非常重要，因为这个领域需要许多组件，没有团队能全部自己做。我在导航项目里用了 ROS 2，以及用于滤波和规划的开源包。好处是速度快、知识共享、被很多用户测试。风险是质量差异、版本变化和文档不清。所以我读文档和源码，并对依赖的部分写测试。我还没有向上游贡献过代码；我想在项目期间或之后，以小的修复或文档改进开始。

**追问 1**
- Q: Which ROS 2 distribution did you use?
- A: Humble, on Ubuntu 22.04.
- 中文：你用哪个 ROS 2 发行版？——Humble，Ubuntu 22.04。

**追问 2**
- Q: What is the main difference between ROS and ROS 2?
- A: ROS 2 is built on DDS with configurable quality of service, a node lifecycle, and better real-time support. ROS 1 had a central master.
- 中文：ROS 与 ROS 2 的主要区别？——ROS 2 基于 DDS，有可配置服务质量、节点生命周期和更好的实时支持；ROS 1 有中心 master。

**关键词与易错点**：open-source benefits and risks、read documentation、have not contributed upstream。P5 使用 ROS2 Humble；P1/P2 的 ROS 版本以各项目为准（P2 为 ROS2）。不要说贡献过开源。

## 7. F 组：AI 与安全执行

> 这一组是你 PS（AI 版）的核心：高层任务计划不能直接发给机器人，必须经过 "action proposal, policy check, execution, state feedback" 这条链。回答时用 "in a design like this"、"typically"、"in our design" 引导，不报内部参数；链路的细节见 [P6 第 08 章](../../01_项目课程/P6_深开鸿M-Robots平台软件/08_安全执行链.md) 和 [第 09 章](../../01_项目课程/P6_深开鸿M-Robots平台软件/09_LLM_AI规划进入物理执行的风险.md)。

### Q47. How should AI be used in safety-critical physical systems?

**中文题意**：AI 应该怎样用在关乎安全的物理系统里？

**考察点**：对 AI 与安全的总体立场。

**English answer**：My position is that a model's output should be a proposal, not a command. Between the proposal and the actuator, there should be a layer that checks it: is it allowed, is it fresh, and is it within limits? After execution, the system should confirm with feedback that the action did what was expected. Where the cost of a mistake is high, a simple rule or a person should have the final say. I think AI is very useful here, but it should be used with clear boundaries, and with the ability to fall back to a safe state. That is the design idea I work with at Kaihong.

**中文翻译**：我的立场是：模型的输出应当是提议，而不是命令。在提议和执行器之间，应该有一层检查：它被允许吗？它够新吗？它在限制之内吗？执行后，系统应通过反馈确认动作达到了预期。在错误代价很高的地方，简单规则或人应有最终决定权。我认为 AI 在这里非常有用，但要在清晰的边界内使用，并有能力回退到安全状态。这就是我在深开鸿所用的设计思想。

**追问 1**
- Q: Is that too conservative?
- A: It can be slower, but in physical systems a mistake is not always reversible. I accept a small cost for that safety.
- 中文：这样是否太保守？——可能更慢，但在物理系统中错误并不总是可逆的；我接受为安全付出小代价。

**追问 2**
- Q: Does that apply to all AI?
- A: It depends on the risk. A low-risk suggestion needs light checks. A motion command needs strong ones.
- 中文：适用于所有 AI 吗？——取决于风险；低风险的建议轻检查，运动命令要强检查。

**关键词与易错点**：proposal not command、check、feedback、safe state。避免说"AI 不可信"这种绝对话；说 "depends on the risk"。

### Q48. Explain "action proposal, policy check, execution, state feedback" to someone who is not an engineer.

**中文题意**：向非工程师解释"动作提议、策略检查、执行、状态反馈"。

**考察点**：把技术链路讲给外行。

**English answer**：Think of a robot as a new employee in a factory. A planner, which can be a person or a program, suggests a job: that is the proposal. Before the employee starts, a supervisor checks the job against the rules: is it allowed, is it still valid, and is it safe right now? That is the policy check. Then the employee does the job: the execution. At the end, we check whether the job was really done and whether everything is fine: the state feedback. If something is wrong at any step, the job stops. The key idea is that nobody can send the robot a job without the checks.

**中文翻译**：把机器人想象成工厂里的新员工。规划者（可以是人或程序）建议一项工作：这是提议。员工开始前，主管按规则检查这项工作：它被允许吗？还有效吗？现在安全吗？这是策略检查。然后员工做这项工作：执行。最后我们检查工作是否真的完成、一切是否正常：状态反馈。任何一步有问题，工作就停止。关键思想是：没有检查，任何人都不能给机器人派活。

**追问 1**
- Q: Who is the "supervisor" in a real system?
- A: A separate program, with its own rules, that every proposal must pass. It is not the same program that makes the proposal.
- 中文：真实系统里"主管"是谁？——一个有自己规则的独立程序，每个提议都必须通过；它与提出提议的程序不是同一个。

**追问 2**
- Q: Why separate them?
- A: So that a mistake in the planner cannot skip the checks.
- 中文：为什么要分开？——这样规划器的错误无法绕过检查。

**关键词与易错点**：proposal, check, execute, feedback、new employee analogy、no path without checks。类比是为外行准备的，面对技术评委要换回技术说法。

### Q49. Can an AI model be trusted to control a robot directly?

**中文题意**：AI 模型可以被信任直接控制机器人吗？

**考察点**：立场与理由。

**English answer**：Not without conditions. For some tasks, with a lot of testing, a learned policy can control a robot well, and I would not say it is impossible. But "trust" needs evidence: many tests in the conditions where it will be used, knowledge of how it fails, and safeguards that work even when it fails. For a general-purpose language model, I would not let it send motor commands directly, because it can be wrong or inconsistent, and it does not have the physical state. So my answer is: trust has to be earned in a specific task with evidence, and the safeguards remain.

**中文翻译**：并非无条件。对一些任务，经过大量测试，学习得到的策略可以很好地控制机器人，我不会说不可能。但"信任"需要证据：在将要使用的条件下做许多测试、了解它如何失败，以及在它失败时仍然有效的保障。对通用语言模型，我不会让它直接发电机命令，因为它可能出错或不一致，也不具备物理状态。所以我的回答是：信任必须在具体任务上以证据赢得，保障依然保留。

**追问 1**
- Q: What evidence would you want?
- A: Success rates over many trials, results on unseen cases, and a clear list of failure modes.
- 中文：你想要什么证据？——许多次试验的成功率、没见过情况上的结果，以及清晰的失效模式清单。

**追问 2**
- Q: Do you have such evidence for your gardening system?
- A: No. I only did a small test on my own dataset and one live trial. I would not claim trust from that.
- 中文：你的园艺系统有这样的证据吗？——没有；我只在自己的数据集上做了小测试和一次现场试验，不会据此声称可信。

**关键词与易错点**：trust needs evidence、specific task、safeguards remain。第二追问如实承认证据有限，这是加分项。

### Q50. What are the failure modes of a learned component in a robot?

**中文题意**：机器人里的学习部件有哪些失效模式？

**考察点**：对失效的系统性思考。

**English answer**：I would list five. The first is distribution shift: the input is different from the training data, for example a new light or an unseen object. The second is overconfidence: the model gives a high confidence for a wrong answer. The third is latency and timing: the output arrives late or at an irregular rate. The fourth is brittleness: a small change in the input changes the output a lot. The fifth is silent failure: nothing crashes, but the behaviour is wrong. In my gardening system, quantisation also reduced the recall of some classes, which is another kind of failure.

**中文翻译**：我会列五个。第一，分布偏移：输入与训练数据不同，比如新的光线或没见过的物体。第二，过度自信：模型对错误答案给出高置信度。第三，延迟与时序：输出晚到或速率不规则。第四，脆弱性：输入的小变化引起输出的大变化。第五，静默失败：没有崩溃，但行为是错的。在我的园艺系统里，量化还降低了某些类别的召回，这是另一种失效。

**追问 1**
- Q: How do you detect silent failure?
- A: By monitoring outputs against simple expectations, and by checking the physical result with feedback.
- 中文：怎么发现静默失败？——对照简单预期监控输出，并用反馈检查物理结果。

**追问 2**
- Q: Which one worries you most?
- A: Overconfidence, because it makes a bad output look reliable.
- 中文：最担心哪个？——过度自信，因为它让坏输出看起来可靠。

**关键词与易错点**：distribution shift、overconfidence、latency、brittleness、silent failure。这是一般性知识，不是你的实验结论；你的量化数据只是小测试。

### Q51. How would you test a learned component before putting it on a robot?

**中文题意**：把学习部件放上机器人前，你怎么测试？

**考察点**：验证方法。

**English answer**：I would use stages. First, offline tests on a held-out dataset with clear metrics, and with cases that are different from the training data. Second, simulation tests with realistic delay, noise and actuator limits, compared with a simple baseline. Third, hardware tests in a safe order: with the robot lifted, low limits, then a gantry or a restricted area, then the real setting. At every stage I would write down the failure cases, and set a clear rule for when to stop. This is close to what I did with my controllers, where I moved in steps from single-axis tests to the ground.

**中文翻译**：我会分阶段。第一，在留出数据集上做离线测试，有清晰指标，并包含与训练数据不同的情况。第二，在带真实延迟、噪声和执行器限制的仿真中测试，并与简单基线比较。第三，按安全顺序做硬件测试：机器人悬空、低限幅，再到龙门架或受限区域，最后到真实环境。每个阶段我都会记录失败案例，并设定明确的停止规则。这与我对控制器所做的接近：从单轴测试逐步走到落地。

**追问 1**
- Q: What is a held-out dataset?
- A: Data that the model never saw during training or tuning, used only to measure performance.
- 中文：什么是留出数据集？——模型在训练或调参时从未见过的数据，只用来衡量性能。

**追问 2**
- Q: What is a stop rule?
- A: A clear condition, such as a limit exceeded or an unexpected behaviour, that ends the test at once.
- 中文：什么是停止规则？——明确的条件，如超过限制或出现意外行为，立刻结束测试。

**关键词与易错点**：offline, simulation, hardware stages、baseline、stop rule。把你自己做过的（控制器分级测试）与设想的（学习部件测试）分清。

### Q52. What safeguards do you put around an uncertain model?

**中文题意**：你在不确定的模型周围加哪些保障？

**考察点**：具体的保障设计，结合 P4。

**English answer**：In my gardening system, I used five. First, a confidence threshold, and a rule that an event is accepted only after several consecutive frames. Second, a rule-based fast path that handles extreme states directly, so the pump does not wait for the model. Third, a whitelist of allowed actions, and a policy check before any command. Fourth, a freshness check: old data is not used. Fifth, a pump controller that is safe on its own, with a maximum running time, and where a manual override has priority. The idea is that if the model is wrong or silent, the system still stays safe.

**中文翻译**：在我的园艺系统里，我用了五项。第一，置信度阈值，并规定事件需连续多帧才被接受。第二，规则快速通道直接处理极端状态，使水泵不等模型。第三，允许动作的白名单，以及任何命令前的策略检查。第四，新鲜度检查：不使用过期数据。第五，水泵控制器自身安全，有最长运行时间，手动覆盖优先。想法是：即使模型出错或沉默，系统仍保持安全。

**追问 1**
- Q: What is the maximum running time?
- A: In my design, 60 seconds, then a cooling period.
- 中文：最长运行时间是多少？——在我的设计里是 60 秒，之后有冷却期。

**追问 2**
- Q: Why does manual override have priority?
- A: A person can see things that the system does not, so the person must be able to override it.
- 中文：为什么手动覆盖优先？——人能看到系统看不到的东西，所以人必须能覆盖系统。

**关键词与易错点**：confidence threshold + consecutive frames、fast path、whitelist、freshness、maximum run time。数字取 P4 自查表（最长开泵 60 s、冷却 120 s）；"手动优先"的细节注意：语音关闭可能恢复自动，不要说"手动关闭永久有效"。

### Q53. Should a human always be in the loop?

**中文题意**：人应该始终在回路中吗？

**考察点**：对人机分工的看法。

**English answer**：I think it depends on the risk, the speed and the reliability of the system. For high-risk actions, I think a person should confirm, or at least be able to stop the system at any time. For fast control loops, a human cannot be in the loop, so the safety must be built into the system: limits, watchdogs and safe states. For low-risk, routine actions, a person who reviews logs afterwards may be enough. In my platform work, there are decisions where the system asks for confirmation, and an emergency stop that always has the highest priority.

**中文翻译**：我认为这取决于风险、速度和系统的可靠性。对高风险动作，应有人确认，或至少能随时停止系统。对快速控制回路，人无法在回路中，所以安全必须内建于系统：限幅、看门狗和安全状态。对低风险的例行动作，事后由人查看日志可能就够了。在我的平台工作里，有些决策系统会请求确认，也有始终最高优先级的急停。

**追问 1**
- Q: What is a watchdog?
- A: A timer that triggers a safe action if the system does not give a signal in time.
- 中文：什么是看门狗？——如果系统没有及时给出信号，就触发安全动作的定时器。

**追问 2**
- Q: Can a human be a weak point too?
- A: Yes. People can be tired or make mistakes, so a good design also checks human commands.
- 中文：人也可能是薄弱点吗？——可能；人会疲劳也会出错，所以好的设计也要检查人的命令。

**关键词与易错点**：depends on risk、confirm or stop、watchdog、emergency stop priority。"急停最高优先级"是通用设计原则，与 P6 的设计取值一致。

### Q54. If an AI-controlled robot causes harm, who is responsible?

**中文题意**：AI 控制的机器人造成伤害，谁负责？

**考察点**：伦理与责任意识，不越界。

**English answer**：I'm an engineer, not a lawyer, so I can only give a view. I think responsibility cannot be handed to the model. People who design, test, approve and operate the system share it: the engineers who set the limits and the tests, the organisation that decides to deploy it, and the operators who use it. That is why I think design must include logs and clear records, so we can understand what happened. It is also a reason to keep strong safeguards and to be honest about the limits of a system, instead of claiming that it is safe in all cases.

**中文翻译**：我是工程师，不是律师，所以只能给出一个看法。我认为责任不能交给模型。设计、测试、批准和运行系统的人共同承担：设定限制和测试的工程师、决定部署的组织以及使用它的操作员。所以我认为设计必须包括日志和清晰的记录，以便理解发生了什么。这也是保持强保障、并诚实说明系统局限的理由，而不是声称它在所有情况下都安全。

**追问 1**
- Q: What would you do as an engineer to reduce harm?
- A: Test carefully, write down limits, add safeguards and logs, and refuse to ship something I think is unsafe.
- 中文：作为工程师你会怎样减少伤害？——仔细测试、写明限制、加保障和日志，并拒绝发布我认为不安全的东西。

**追问 2**
- Q: Have you faced such a decision?
- A: Not in a real product. In my projects, I made safety checks before each hardware test.
- 中文：你遇到过这样的决定吗？——没在真实产品中；我的项目里，每次硬件测试前我都做安全检查。

**关键词与易错点**：shared responsibility、logs and records、honest about limits。不要作法律判断；"拒绝发布不安全的东西"是原则表态，不要说成已做过。

### Q55. What does your policy check actually check?

**中文题意**：你的策略检查具体检查什么？

**考察点**：设计的具体度与保密边界。

**English answer**：I can describe the general design and not the internal details. In a design like this, a policy check typically asks seven kinds of questions. Is the message well formed? Does the sender have the permission? Is the action on the whitelist? Is the proposal still valid in time? Is the robot in a mode that allows it, for example not in an emergency stop? Is the state fresh enough? And are the numbers within the allowed ranges? A decision is accept, modify to a safe range, reject, or ask for confirmation, and each rejection has a reason code and a log entry.

**中文翻译**：我可以描述一般设计，不讲内部细节。在这类设计里，策略检查通常问七类问题：消息格式正确吗？发送者有权限吗？动作在白名单上吗？提议在时间上仍有效吗？机器人所处模式允许吗，比如不在急停中？状态足够新吗？数值在允许范围内吗？判决有四种：接受、修改到安全范围、拒绝或请求确认，每次拒绝都有原因码和日志。

**追问 1**
- Q: Which check is the hardest to get right?
- A: Freshness, because it depends on timestamps and clocks, which can be wrong.
- 中文：哪项检查最难做对？——新鲜度，因为它依赖时间戳和时钟，而这些可能出错。

**追问 2**
- Q: Can you share the real rule set?
- A: No, that is internal. I can discuss how I would design such rules in general.
- 中文：能分享真实规则集吗？——不能，那是内部的；我可以讨论如何一般性地设计此类规则。

**关键词与易错点**：seven kinds of checks（格式、权限、白名单、有效期、模式、新鲜度、数值范围）、accept/modify/reject/confirm、reason codes。这是 P6 自查表第 15、18 项的设计取值，说成 "typically / in a design like this"，不要说成内部事实或实测。

### Q56. What are the limits of a policy check?

**中文题意**：策略检查有哪些局限？

**考察点**：对自己设计的批判性认识。

**English answer**：I see four limits. First, it can only check what it can see: if the state is wrong or old, a correct rule can still pass a bad action. Second, there is a gap in time: the state may change between the check and the execution, so the execution must check again for what matters. Third, it cannot judge meaning: a command can be allowed and in range, yet a bad idea for the task. Fourth, the rules themselves can be wrong or incomplete, so they need review and testing. A policy check is a layer of protection, not a guarantee. I would add feedback and a watchdog behind it.

**中文翻译**：我看到四个局限。第一，它只能检查它能看到的：如果状态是错的或过期的，正确的规则仍可能放行坏动作。第二，时间上有间隙：检查与执行之间状态可能变化，所以执行端要对关键内容再次检查。第三，它不能判断含义：一个命令可以被允许且在范围内，却对任务是个坏主意。第四，规则本身可能出错或不完整，需要评审和测试。策略检查是一层保护，不是保证。我会在它后面加反馈和看门狗。

**追问 1**
- Q: What is the gap between check and execution called?
- A: Time-of-check to time-of-use. Re-checking at execution time, with a short-lived approval, reduces it.
- 中文：检查与执行之间的间隙叫什么？——检查时到使用时（TOCTOU）；在执行时再检查并使用短时批准可减小它。

**追问 2**
- Q: How would you test the rules?
- A: With unit tests, fault injection like delays and clock jumps, and replays of recorded state.
- 中文：怎样测试规则？——单元测试、延迟和时钟跳变这类故障注入，以及回放录制的状态。

**关键词与易错点**：only sees what it can see、TOCTOU、not a guarantee。能诚实说出局限比夸大安全性更好；不要说"策略检查保证安全"。

## 8. 自测与练习安排

### 8.1 自测清单

| 检查项 | 通过标准 |
|---|---|
| 主线一致 | 短期、五年、长期三题的回答能连成一条线，没有互相矛盾 |
| 三方向 | Q14 的"导航现在能贡献、学习要成长、感知要建设"能 40 秒说完 |
| 研究兴趣 | Q21 说出三个问题；任选一个，用"问题、方法、基线、指标"四步讲 60 秒 |
| 趋势题 | 任抽 3 个趋势题，每题按"观察、挑战、我的看法、我会做什么"回答，且没有报任何公司或论文名 |
| AI 与安全 | 能用 30 秒给外行讲清四步链路，也能给技术评委讲七类检查与四个局限 |
| 红线句 | 不说做过强化学习/VLA/人形；不说发过论文；不说 "AI is totally safe" |

### 8.2 练习安排

| 天 | 内容 | 时间 |
|---|---|---|
| 第 1 天 | A 组 Q1–Q12：只练 Q1、Q2、Q3、Q4 的 45 秒版，其余通读 | 60 分钟 |
| 第 2 天 | B 组 Q13–Q20，重点 Q14、Q15、Q17 | 60 分钟 |
| 第 3 天 | C 组 Q21–Q28；写下三个研究问题，各 30 秒口述 | 60 分钟 |
| 第 4 天 | D 组 Q29–Q34；复习对应基础（最优化、概率、RL 概念） | 60 分钟 |
| 第 5 天 | E 组 Q35–Q46；每题只说"观察 + 看法"两句，训练短答 | 60 分钟 |
| 第 6 天 | F 组 Q47–Q56；找朋友当"外行"，练 Q48 | 45 分钟 |
| 第 7 天 | 综合：随机 8 题，限时 60 秒，录音回听 | 45 分钟 |

### 8.3 常见错误回顾

1. 职业目标措辞前后不一（工程师/研究员/创业）。
2. 趋势题里报出你无法展开的名词（公司、论文、数据）。
3. 把研究设想说成已完成的工作。
4. 对 AI 过度乐观（"完全可以信任"）或过度悲观（"绝对不能用"）。
5. 把 P6 的内部设计当作可公开的细节。
