# 分类题库 04：个人特质、优缺点、压力题与敏感题（Personal qualities, weaknesses, pressure and sensitive questions）

> **一句话定位**：这一份题库覆盖"你是什么样的人"：优缺点、压力与失败、时间管理、学习方式、兴趣爱好、价值观，以及最让人紧张的敏感题（成绩、英语 6.5、实习时长与中断、排名、"项目不创新"等），最后是怪题和开放题。共 66 题，每题有英文回答、中文翻译和两个追问。

**本章学完你能……**

- 用"承认、证据、计划、回到目标"的四步公式回答任何弱项题，不防御、不夸大；
- 为每一个特质（严谨、好奇、可靠、会求助）准备一个来自项目的具体例子，而不是形容词；
- 回答敏感题时保持平静，30–45 秒说完，说完就停；
- 遇到怪题和开放题时，先停顿，用结构化方式回答，并把话题带回自己有依据的经历；
- 不编造任何兴趣爱好、家庭背景或经历。

**前置知识**：[弱项与敏感问题应对](../01_面试总指南/05_弱项与敏感问题应对.md)、[行为面试 STAR](../01_面试总指南/04_行为面试_STAR与常见问题.md)、[口径与事实基线](../../00_申请画像/04_口径与事实基线.md)。

## 1. 本文件怎么用

1. **事实来源**：成绩、排名、雅思、实习日期、奖项一律取自 [CV 事实基线](../../06_维护说明/CV事实基线.md)：均分 87.63、专业排名 6 of 156（top 4%）、IELTS Academic 6.5；AIRS 2025.09–2026.03、Future Power 2026.03–2026.05、Kaihong 2026.06 至今。雅思小分（听力 6.0、阅读 8.0、写作 6.0、口语 5.5）取自申请总览，见 [口径与事实基线 2.3](../../00_申请画像/04_口径与事实基线.md)，只在被直接问到时说。
2. **习惯类答案的来源**：本文件里"我平时怎么做"的内容，取自你各项目的工作方式记录（每周与导师同步、先手算小例子、先悬空再落地的测试顺序、记录基线、脚本化回归、提精准问题等），属于项目自查表里的统一取值。如果与你的真实习惯有出入，请改成你真实的做法，**不要说自己做不到的**。
3. **不编造**：没有依据的兴趣爱好、家庭情况、家乡、志愿活动、读过的书和看过的电影，一律不编。本文件对这类题给出诚实的回答结构（用你确有的经历：做小项目、读技术文档、向同学讲解）。如果你有真实的爱好，可以加一句，但不要为面试编造。
4. **四步公式**：承认（一句话，直接）、证据（一两个事实）、计划（具体的补救）、回到目标（与读研的关系）。弱项题 30–45 秒，说完就停。
5. **压力题的原则**：先停顿 3 秒；不防御；不反问"你为什么这样问"；用事实回答；如果对方说你错了，先确认再修正。
6. **敏感信息**：雇主保密的内容不讲；他校申请不列名单；不评价他人和他校。
7. **每题格式**：英文题目、中文题意、考察点、英文回答、中文翻译、两个追问、关键词与易错点。

> **面试要点**：评委问弱项，不是要你否认，而是看你是否自知、是否有计划、是否诚实。承认之后给证据，承认就变成"冷静"，而不是"示弱"。

## 2. A 组：优缺点与自我认知

### Q1. What are your greatest strengths?

**中文题意**：你最大的优点是什么？

**考察点**：自我认知和证据。

**English answer**：I would name three. The first is that I check the whole system, not only the algorithm. On my navigation project, a localisation problem came from timestamps, frames and the sign of reverse speed, not from the filter, and I found it because I looked at the inputs. The second is that I go back to a model when tuning stops explaining the robot, as I did at AIRS. The third is that I treat safety and documentation as part of the design. I add limits and checks before I test on hardware. These come from my projects, and they are habits I keep.

**中文翻译**：我会说三点。第一，我检查整个系统，而不只是算法。在导航项目里，一个定位问题来自时间戳、坐标系和倒车速度的符号，而不是滤波器，我是因为检查输入才找到的。第二，当调参不再能解释机器人时，我会回到模型，就像在 AIRS 那样。第三，我把安全和文档当作设计的一部分，在硬件测试前先加限制和检查。这些来自我的项目，是我保持的习惯。

**追问 1**
- Q: Which strength is the most useful in a master's programme?
- A: Going back to a model, because courses ask us to explain why methods work.
- 中文：哪个优点对读硕士最有用？——回到模型，因为课程要求我们解释方法为何有效。

**追问 2**
- Q: How do others see these strengths?
- A: My mentor at AIRS said that I asked precise questions and worked independently once the direction was clear.
- 中文：别人怎么看这些优点？——AIRS 的导师认为我会提精准的问题，方向明确后能独立工作。

**关键词与易错点**：whole system、go back to a model、safety and documentation。每个优点后面必须有一个例子；不要说"我很聪明/勤奋"。

### Q2. What is your biggest weakness?

**中文题意**：你最大的缺点是什么？

**考察点**：诚实和改进计划，同时不暴露致命弱点。

**English answer**：My biggest weakness is that I rely on practice more than on theory. I can make a controller work, but I often arrive at the model robot by robot and tune the gains on the machine. I don't have a general theory that lets me design a controller from first principles. I also have less experience in optimisation and in machine learning theory. I'm working on it. I use small examples by hand, such as the LQR analysis on the ballbot, and I plan to study the foundations systematically in this master's. I consider this weakness a clear reason why I am applying.

**中文翻译**：我最大的缺点是依赖实践多于理论。我能让控制器工作，但常常是逐台机器人推出模型，并在机器上调增益。我没有能从第一性原理设计控制器的一般理论。我在最优化和机器学习理论上经验也较少。我在改进：用小的手算例子，比如球形机器人上的 LQR 分析；并计划在这个硕士里系统学习基础。我把这个缺点看作我申请的清晰理由。

**追问 1**
- Q: Isn't that a big weakness for a master's student?
- A: It is a gap, not a block. I have the mathematics base, and a master's is meant to fill it.
- 中文：这对硕士生不是大缺点吗？——是缺口，不是障碍；我有数学基础，硕士正是用来补它。

**追问 2**
- Q: What have you done about it so far?
- A: I took two control courses, I used LQR analysis on the ballbot, and I keep reading about optimisation and learning.
- 中文：到目前为止你做了什么？——修了两门控制课，在球形机器人上做 LQR 分析，并持续阅读最优化与学习相关内容。

**关键词与易错点**：practice over theory、gap not block、concrete steps。这个缺点与推荐信和 PS 完全一致，不要换成别的"假缺点"（如"太完美主义"）。

### Q3. How are you working on this weakness?

**中文题意**：你怎样改进这个缺点？

**考察点**：改进计划的具体性。

**English answer**：In three ways. First, I use the method of small examples: when I meet a new method, I work through a small example by hand before I use it on a robot. Second, I write down the model and its assumptions before I tune. For example, the four-state model of the ballbot told me which assumptions I made. Third, I plan to review convex optimisation and probability before the programme, with exercises I can check. I keep a short list of questions that I cannot answer yet, and I look for the answers. In the master's, I would bring that list to the lecturers.

**中文翻译**：三种方式。第一，小例子法：遇到新方法，先手算一个小例子，再用到机器人上。第二，调参之前先写下模型及其假设。比如球形机器人的四状态模型告诉我自己做了哪些假设。第三，我计划开学前复习凸优化和概率，做可自检的习题。我保留一份还不能回答的问题清单，并去找答案。读硕士时，我会把这份清单带给授课教师。

**追问 1**
- Q: Give an example from the list.
- A: Why does a larger tilt weight in LQR produce a larger gain, and when does that become too large for the motor?
- 中文：从清单里举个例子。——LQR 中更大的倾角权重为何产生更大增益，以及何时对电机来说过大？

**追问 2**
- Q: How do you check your own understanding?
- A: I try to explain it in plain words. If I cannot, I haven't understood it.
- 中文：怎么检查自己的理解？——我尝试用平白的话解释；如果讲不出来，说明还没理解。

**关键词与易错点**：small examples by hand、write the assumptions、question list。不要说"我已经完全克服了"。

### Q4. Describe yourself in three words, and give an example for each.

**中文题意**：用三个词形容自己，并各举一例。

**考察点**：自我描述的证据。

**English answer**：I would say systematic, curious and reliable. Systematic: when the wheel-legged robot fell in three different ways, I separated the causes one by one: state inconsistency, torque limits and ground contact. Curious: at AIRS, when tuning could not explain the oscillation, I wanted to know why, and derived a model. Reliable: before every hardware test, I added limits and checked the state, so that tests could be repeated without damaging the robot. I chose these words because I can show an example for each, and I think examples are more useful than adjectives.

**中文翻译**：我会说系统、好奇、可靠。系统：轮腿机器人以三种不同方式倒下时，我逐个分离原因：状态不一致、力矩限幅和触地。好奇：在 AIRS，调参无法解释振荡时，我想知道原因，于是推导了模型。可靠：每次硬件测试前，我加限制并检查状态，使测试能重复而不损坏机器人。我选这些词，是因为我能为每个词给出例子，而我认为例子比形容词更有用。

**追问 1**
- Q: Would your teammates agree?
- A: I hope so. I'd ask them, and I'm ready to hear what they would add.
- 中文：队友会同意吗？——我希望如此；我会去问他们，也愿意听他们补充。

**追问 2**
- Q: Which word is least true?
- A: "Curious" can be limited by time. I sometimes have to stop exploring and finish the task.
- 中文：哪个词最不准确？——"好奇"会受时间限制，有时我要停止探索去完成任务。

**关键词与易错点**：systematic、curious、reliable + example。词可以换，但每个词都要有来自 CV 的例子。

### Q5. Are you more of a theory person or a hands-on person?

**中文题意**：你更偏理论还是偏动手？

**考察点**：自我定位与平衡。

**English answer**：I'm more hands-on at the moment, and I know it. I learn best by building and testing. But I have also seen what happens when I go back to theory: at AIRS, a model explained what tuning could not. So I would say I am a hands-on person who has learned to value theory. In this master's I want to move the balance a bit, so that theory leads more often. I believe the best engineers can do both, and I want to be one.

**中文翻译**：目前我更偏动手，我自己也清楚。我通过搭建和测试学得最好。但我也见过回到理论的结果：在 AIRS，模型解释了调参解释不了的现象。所以我是一个学会了重视理论的动手型人。在这个硕士里我希望把平衡稍微移动一点，让理论更常常领先。我相信最好的工程师两者都行，我想成为这样的人。

**追问 1**
- Q: Which do you find harder?
- A: Theory that has no example. I need to connect it to a system I know.
- 中文：哪个更难？——没有例子的理论；我需要把它连到我熟悉的系统。

**追问 2**
- Q: Do you enjoy proofs?
- A: Not by themselves. I enjoy them when they explain a behaviour I have seen.
- 中文：你喜欢证明吗？——单独不太喜欢；当它们解释了我见过的行为时我很喜欢。

**关键词与易错点**：hands-on, learned to value theory、balance。与 PS "人设" 句一致。

### Q6. Are you a perfectionist?

**中文题意**：你是完美主义者吗？

**考察点**：避免"假缺点"，展现判断力。

**English answer**：Not exactly. I'm careful where it matters, such as safety limits, state initialisation and the tests before hardware. But I know that perfect is not always the goal. In the RoboMaster season, we had to decide when a controller was good enough for the match. I learned to define clear acceptance criteria first, for example a stable launcher speed within a range, and then stop. Where the cost of a mistake is small, I'd rather move faster and iterate. Where it is large, such as on hardware, I am slower and stricter.

**中文翻译**：不完全是。在重要的地方我很仔细，比如安全限制、状态初始化和硬件测试前的检查。但我知道完美不总是目标。在 RoboMaster 赛季里，我们要判断一个控制器何时对比赛足够好。我学会先定清晰的验收标准，比如发射转速稳定在一个范围内，然后就停下。错误代价小的地方，我宁可快一点、迭代；代价大的地方，比如硬件上，我更慢、更严格。

**追问 1**
- Q: How do you decide "good enough"?
- A: By criteria set in advance, measured on repeated tests, and by the time left.
- 中文：怎么判断"足够好"？——由事先设定的标准、重复测试的测量以及剩余时间决定。

**追问 2**
- Q: Has perfectionism hurt you?
- A: Sometimes I spend too long on a detail. A weekly review with a mentor helps me to stop.
- 中文：完美主义伤害过你吗？——有时我在细节上花太久；每周与导师的回顾帮助我停下来。

**关键词与易错点**：careful where it matters、acceptance criteria、iterate。不要说"I'm a perfectionist"然后把它说成优点。

### Q7. Which of your strengths can become a weakness?

**中文题意**：你的哪项优点在某些情况下会变成缺点？

**考察点**：辩证和自知。

**English answer**：My habit of checking the whole system can become a weakness. When something fails, I look at timing, frames, limits and sensors, and this takes time. In a deadline situation, it can look slow. I also tend to explain too much detail. I have learned to prioritise: check the most likely causes first, and tell the listener the conclusion before the detail. A weekly review with my mentor also helps me to keep the pace. In an interview, I try to give a short answer first, and the detail only if asked.

**中文翻译**：我检查整个系统的习惯可能变成缺点。出问题时，我会查时序、坐标系、限制和传感器，这需要时间；在截止日期前显得慢。我也倾向于解释过多细节。我学会了排序：先检查最可能的原因，先给听者结论再给细节。每周与导师的回顾也帮助我保持节奏。在面试里，我也先给短答案，被问再给细节。

**追问 1**
- Q: Show me how you would shorten an answer.
- A: First one sentence with the conclusion, then one example, then stop.
- 中文：演示如何缩短回答。——先一句结论，再一个例子，然后停下。

**追问 2**
- Q: Have you been told you talk too long?
- A: In the past, I got that feedback in project reviews, and I'm improving it.
- 中文：有人说你讲话太长吗？——过去在项目评审中有人提过，我在改进。

**关键词与易错点**：thorough but slow、prioritise、conclusion first。第二追问的"项目评审中有人提过"是个人习惯类表述，若不属实请改成"我自己注意到这一点"。

### Q8. What is your learning style?

**中文题意**：你的学习方式是什么？

**考察点**：学习方法的具体性。

**English answer**：I learn in a cycle. First I read the idea in plain words, and try to say it in one or two sentences. Second I work a small example by hand, with clean numbers. Third I connect it to a real system, usually a robot or a simulation, and see what changes. Fourth I explain it to someone else, because if I cannot explain it, I haven't understood it. For example, I learned LQR by first seeing the cost as a trade-off between being off balance and spending effort, and only then looking at the matrices.

**中文翻译**：我按循环学习。第一，用平白的话读懂想法，并尝试用一两句话说出来。第二，用干净的数字手算一个小例子。第三，把它连到真实系统，通常是机器人或仿真，看看有什么变化。第四，讲给别人听，因为讲不出来就说明没懂。比如我学 LQR 时，先把代价理解为"偏离平衡"与"耗费力气"之间的权衡，然后才看矩阵。

**追问 1**
- Q: Do you prefer to learn alone or in a group?
- A: First alone to get the basics, then in a group to test my understanding.
- 中文：喜欢独自学还是小组学？——先独自学基础，再在小组里检验理解。

**追问 2**
- Q: How do you learn from a lecture in English?
- A: I read the slides before the class, note the key terms, and review the same day.
- 中文：怎样学习英文授课？——课前读课件，记下关键术语，当天复习。

**关键词与易错点**：plain words、small example by hand、connect to a system、explain to others。与 [英文自我介绍 7.1](../01_面试总指南/02_英文自我介绍_多版本.md) 的"如何学习"一致。

### Q9. How do you deal with ambiguity?

**中文题意**：你怎样应对不确定的情况？

**考察点**：处理模糊需求的方法。

**English answer**：I try to turn ambiguity into small questions that I can test. When I started the navigation project, the first goal was just "autonomous navigation on the real vehicle". I split it into localisation, planning and tracking, and for each I defined what a good result would look like. When something is still unclear, I ask a precise question to my mentor, and I write down my assumption, so that we can check it. I'm also comfortable making a small first version, and learning from it, instead of waiting for a perfect specification.

**中文翻译**：我努力把模糊变成可检验的小问题。开始导航项目时，最初的目标只是"真车上的自主导航"。我把它拆成定位、规划和跟踪，并为每一块定义好结果应是什么样。当事情仍不清楚时，我向导师提精准的问题，并写下自己的假设以便检查。我也习惯先做一个小的初版，从中学习，而不是等待完美的规格。

**追问 1**
- Q: Give an example of an assumption you wrote down.
- A: For the ballbot analysis, I assumed small tilt angles, so the model could be linearised.
- 中文：举一个你写下的假设。——球形机器人分析里，我假设倾角很小，所以模型可以线性化。

**追问 2**
- Q: What if your mentor is not available?
- A: I would test the most likely assumption myself, and keep notes for the next meeting.
- 中文：导师不在怎么办？——我会自己检验最可能的假设，并记下要点留待下次会议。

**关键词与易错点**：split into small questions、write the assumption、first version。避免说"I like ambiguity"。

### Q10. Do you prefer to work alone or in a team?

**中文题意**：你喜欢独自工作还是团队合作？

**考察点**：合作风格。

**English answer**：Both, in different phases. For understanding a problem and for the first implementation, I like to work alone for a while, because I think best when I can try things. But robots are team work: mechanics, electronics, software and sometimes vision. In RoboMaster, I depended on teammates for the hardware and vision, and I defined the interfaces with them. At Kaihong, I work with code reviews and a weekly sync. So I'd say: I am independent when the direction is clear, and I'm happy to work in a team when the problem crosses boundaries.

**中文翻译**：两者都可以，在不同阶段。理解问题和第一次实现时，我喜欢先独自工作一阵，因为能试验时我思考得最好。但机器人是团队工作：机械、电子、软件，有时还有视觉。在 RoboMaster，我依赖队友做硬件和视觉，并和他们定义接口。在深开鸿，我通过代码评审和每周同步合作。所以我是方向清楚时独立、问题跨越边界时乐于团队合作。

**追问 1**
- Q: What is the hardest part of teamwork for you?
- A: Waiting for others when my part depends on theirs. I try to agree early on interfaces and dates.
- 中文：团队合作对你最难的是什么？——我的部分依赖他人时的等待；我尽量早期约定接口和日期。

**追问 2**
- Q: Do you lead or follow?
- A: I lead on my modules and follow on the system decisions made by the lead. I speak up when I see a risk.
- 中文：你领导还是跟随？——我的模块上我主导，系统决策上跟随负责人；看到风险时我会说出来。

### Q11. How do you know when you need help?

**中文题意**：你怎么判断何时需要求助？

**考察点**：求助的判断与方式。

**中文翻译**：我用一个简单规则：一个问题如果花了设定的时间，比如半天，而我没有新假设可检验，我就求助。求助前我先准备：我预期什么、看到什么、试了什么、我认为原因可能是什么。这样问题简短精准，对方能很快回答。在 AIRS，导师说我会提精准的问题，我想这个习惯是原因。涉及安全时我也会早问，因为在硬件上猜错的代价可能比一个简短的问题更大。

**追问 1**
- Q: What does a precise question look like?

- 中文：精准的问题是什么样？——例如："机器人重新使能时，哪些状态被清除、哪些保留？"它说明情境，且只问一件事。

**追问 2**
- Q: What if someone does not answer?

- 中文：对方不回答怎么办？——写下我所知的，做最安全的假设，并在下次会议再问。

**关键词与易错点**：set a time、prepare the question、ask early on safety。时间设定（半天）是示例性的个人规则，可按你的真实习惯改写。

### Q12. What was the best feedback you ever received, and what was the hardest?

**中文题意**：你收到过的最好的反馈和最难接受的反馈分别是什么？

**中文翻译**：最好的反馈来自 AIRS 的导师，导师看重的是：当调参不再能解释机器人时，我回到了基于模型的分析。这鼓励我在一个问题开始时就把分析与调参结合使用。最难的反馈来自机器人本身：当我加大倾角增益想让球形机器人响应更快时，它反而变差。这让人不舒服，因为我预期的是相反的结果。但它逼我找到真正的原因：执行器饱和。我认为好的反馈是具体且真实的，即使很难接受。

**追问 1**
- Q: How did you react at that moment?
- A: I stopped changing gains, wrote down the facts, and went back to the model to explain them.
- 中文：当时你怎样反应？——我停止改增益，写下事实，并回到模型去解释它们。

**追问 2**
- Q: Do you give feedback to others?

- 中文：你给别人反馈吗？——会，在代码评审里；先说好的，再给一个清晰的建议。

**关键词与易错点**：specific and true、the robot as feedback、saturation。导师性别不确定时用 "my mentor"，不要用 he/she；这里的故事与推荐信和 PS 的饱和案例一致。

## 3. B 组：压力、失败与时间管理

### Q13. Tell me about your biggest failure.

**中文题意**：说说你最大的一次失败。

**考察点**：反思能力与真实度。

**English answer**：One failure I remember well was at AIRS. After a fall, I re-enabled the balance controller, and the robot suddenly lunged. The reason was simple: the integral term, the filter history and the target position still held values from before the fall. I had not cleared them. Nobody was hurt, but it showed me how dangerous a re-enable can be. I changed the procedure. Now, on every enable, the robot must be still, the bias is measured again for about two seconds, and the integrator, the filters and the target are cleared. I also lock the controller after a fall, and recovery needs a person.

**中文翻译**：我记得很清楚的一次失败在 AIRS。机器人倒下后，我重新使能平衡控制器，它突然猛冲。原因很简单：积分项、滤波历史和目标位置还保存着倒下之前的值，我没有清除它们。没有人受伤，但它让我看到重新使能有多危险。我改了流程：每次使能时，机器人必须静止，约两秒重新测量偏置，并清除积分器、滤波器和目标。我也在倒下后锁定控制器，恢复需要人工。

**追问 1**
- Q: What did you learn?
- A: Safety is a procedure, not only a limit. Every state that can carry over must be cleared on purpose.
- 中文：你学到了什么？——安全是一套流程，不只是限幅；每个可能残留的状态都必须被有意清除。

**追问 2**
- Q: How did you tell your mentor?
- A: I told them what happened, what I found, and what I changed, the same day.
- 中文：你怎么告诉导师？——当天说明发生了什么、发现了什么、改了什么。

**关键词与易错点**：re-enable, integral, filter history, target、clear states on purpose、manual recovery。取自 P1 自查表第 15、18、31 项；细节写成 "my design / my procedure"，不要夸大后果。不要说"机器人损坏"（没有依据）。

### Q14. Describe a serious mistake you made, and how you handled it.

**中文题意**：描述一次你犯过的严重错误，以及你如何处理。

**考察点**：诚实、责任感、修复和预防。

**English answer**：In my smart-gardening project, the pump turned on as soon as the board was powered, before any software ran. The cause was the relay module: it was triggered by a low level, and my pin was low at startup. It was a small test, but a pump running dry or overflowing is a real problem. I found it by reading the module's trigger polarity, and I fixed it by setting the pin to the safe level first and enabling the output only after the state machine started. I also added the habit to test any actuator without a load first, and to read the trigger polarity before wiring.

**中文翻译**：在智能园艺项目里，开发板一上电水泵就开了，此时还没有任何软件运行。原因是继电器模块是低电平触发，而我的引脚启动时是低电平。这是个小测试，但水泵空转或溢水是真实的问题。我通过读模块的触发极性发现原因，修复方法是先把引脚设为安全电平，状态机启动后才使能输出。我也养成了习惯：任何执行器先空载测试，接线前先读触发极性。

**追问 1**
- Q: Could that have caused damage?
- A: It could have wasted water or run the pump dry. That's why I now test without a load first.
- 中文：这可能造成损害吗？——可能浪费水或让泵空转；所以我现在先空载测试。

**追问 2**
- Q: Is this kind of mistake common in your field?
- A: Yes. Startup states are a classic source of problems, and one reason I care about initialisation.
- 中文：这类错误在你的领域常见吗？——常见；启动状态是经典的问题来源，也是我重视初始化的原因之一。

**关键词与易错点**：low-level trigger、safe level first、test without load。取自 P4 自查表第 81 项（继电器低触发极性导致上电即开）。

### Q15. How would you handle repeated experiment failures?

**中文题意**：实验反复失败，你怎么办？（调研中的澳科大高频题之一）

**考察点**：韧性和方法。

**English answer**：I would not repeat the same test and hope for a different result. First, I would stop and write down what I expected and what I saw. Second, I would change only one thing at a time, so that I can see which change matters. Third, I would check the basics: the signs, the units, the timing, and the limits. On the wheel-legged robot, balance failed in three different ways, and I found three different causes: state inconsistency, torque limits and ground-contact transitions. If after several tries I still have no hypothesis, I ask for help with a precise description. Failures are information, if I record them well.

**中文翻译**：我不会重复同一个测试并期待不同结果。第一，我会停下来，写下预期和所见。第二，一次只改一件事，才能看出哪个改动起作用。第三，检查基础：符号、单位、时序和限制。在轮腿机器人上，平衡以三种不同方式失败，我找到了三个不同原因：状态不一致、力矩限幅和触地切换。如果试了几次仍没有假设，我会带着精准的描述求助。失败是信息，只要我记录得好。

**追问 1**
- Q: How do you record failures?
- A: A short log with the setting, the recording of signals, what I changed and the result.
- 中文：怎么记录失败？——简短的日志：设置、信号录包、改了什么和结果。

**追问 2**
- Q: How do you avoid frustration?
- A: By making progress visible, for example a table of causes ruled out. It turns "nothing works" into "three causes are eliminated".
- 中文：怎样避免沮丧？——让进展可见，比如已排除原因的表格；把"什么都不行"变成"已排除三个原因"。

**关键词与易错点**：one change at a time、basics first、failures are information。这是 ★ 调研题的回答，示例取自 P7。

### Q16. How do you stay motivated after a setback?

**中文题意**：受挫后怎样保持动力？

**考察点**：心态与方法。

**English answer**：I make the next step small. After a setback, I don't think about the whole project. I ask, what is the next test that will tell me something? Seeing a small result, such as one cause ruled out, gives me energy to continue. I also remind myself of the purpose: a robot that works, or a model that explains. And I talk to someone. At AIRS, explaining the problem to my mentor often helped me to see it differently. I think motivation is not a feeling that I wait for. It comes from making clear progress in small steps.

**中文翻译**：我把下一步变小。受挫后，我不想整个项目，而是问：下一个能告诉我点什么的测试是什么？看到小结果，比如排除一个原因，会给我继续的能量。我也提醒自己目的：一个能工作的机器人，或一个能解释现象的模型。我还会找人谈。在 AIRS，向导师讲述问题常常让我换一个角度看。我认为动力不是等来的感觉，而是来自一小步一小步的明确进展。

**追问 1**
- Q: What if there is no progress for days?
- A: Then I change my approach: go back to the model, simplify the test, or ask for a second opinion.
- 中文：几天没有进展呢？——换方法：回到模型、简化测试或请第二个人看。

**追问 2**
- Q: Do you ever want to give up?
- A: Sometimes I feel tired. But I haven't wanted to give up, because there is nearly always a smaller test to run.
- 中文：想过放弃吗？——有时会累，但没想过放弃，因为几乎总有更小的测试可做。

**关键词与易错点**：next small step、visible progress、talk to someone。不要说"我从不气馁"。

### Q17. Tell me about a time you missed a deadline.

**中文题意**：说说你错过截止日期的一次经历。

**考察点**：诚实，不编造完美记录。

**English answer**：I don't remember missing a major deadline, and I'd rather say that than invent a story. What I can describe is how I handle the risk. When a test takes longer than planned, I tell my mentor early, with the reason and a new estimate. We agree what to cut or delay, usually the part that adds the least value. For example, if a hardware test is not safe to run yet, I move it later and use the time for analysis. I think the real problem is not a late delivery, but late news about it.

**中文翻译**：我不记得错过过重要的截止日期，我宁愿这样说，也不编故事。我能描述的是我如何处理这种风险。当一个测试比计划花得久，我会早点告诉导师，附上原因和新的估计。我们商量削减或推迟什么，通常是价值最小的部分。比如硬件测试还不安全时，我把它推后，用这段时间做分析。我认为真正的问题不是延迟交付，而是延迟告知。

**追问 1**
- Q: Have you ever had to cut scope?
- A: Yes, usually small things, such as extra features, to keep the main function safe and tested.
- 中文：削减过范围吗？——有，通常是小的额外功能，以保证主要功能安全并经过测试。

**追问 2**
- Q: How do you estimate time?
- A: I split the task, estimate each part, and add time for debugging. I update the estimate every week.
- 中文：怎么估计时间？——拆分任务，估计每部分，加上调试时间；每周更新估计。

### Q18. How do you respond to criticism?

**中文题意**：你怎样对待批评？

**考察点**：成熟度。

**English answer**：First, I listen to the end, without defending. Second, I ask for a specific case, because a specific case can be tested. I write a test that shows it, fix it, and say thanks. If I disagree, I bring data and not opinion. I try to separate the criticism of the work from the criticism of me. Good criticism is a gift, because it saves me time later.

**中文翻译**：首先听完，不辩解。其次，我请对方给出具体案例，因为具体案例可以检验。第三，我检查这一点是否属实，通常通过复现。比如代码评审里，同事可能说我的函数遇到旧时间戳会失败。我写一个测试复现它，修复，然后道谢。如果不同意，我拿数据而不是观点。我尽量把对工作的批评和对我本人的批评分开。好的批评是礼物，因为它为我节省之后的时间。

**追问 2**

- 中文：哪条批评改变了你？——"模型和增益是逐台机器人得出的"这一点，让我决定去学一般性理论。

### Q19. How do you manage stress?

**中文翻译**：压力下我努力让问题具体化：写下已知和未知，选择下一步。这样做本身就减轻了我的压力，因为问题变小了。我也尽量保持身体状态：睡眠充足，长时间调试时离开屏幕短暂休息。在硬件工作里，压力可能导致不安全的捷径，所以即使匆忙，我也保留我的检查清单。我也会早点和队友谈。我不总是冷静，但我知道什么对我有帮助。

### Q20. How do you decide what to do first when everything is urgent?

**中文题意**：一切都紧急时，你先做什么？

- 中文：导师不在呢？——选最安全的顺序并记录理由，之后可调整。

### Q21. How do you react when you are under pressure in an interview and meet a question you cannot answer?

### Q22. How do you manage your time?

**中文题意**：你怎样管理时间？

**考察点**：时间管理的具体做法。

**English answer**：I plan by weeks. At the start of the week, I list what must be done, and what depends on others. I use fixed dates first: lectures, assignments and meetings. Then I plan work in blocks, and keep time for debugging, which always takes longer. I also reduce repeated work with scripts. For example, a regression check that took about fifteen minutes by hand, I turned into a script that takes about one minute. A weekly review with my mentor shows me early when I am off track. At the end of each day, I write the next step, so I can start fast the next morning.

**中文翻译**：我按周计划。每周开始，我列出必须完成的事和依赖他人的事。先排固定日期：课程、作业和会议。然后按时间块计划工作，并给调试留时间，调试总是更久。我也用脚本减少重复工作。例如一个手动约十五分钟的回归检查，我做成了约一分钟的脚本。每周与导师的回顾让我早点发现偏差。每天结束时，我写下下一步，次日早上能快速开始。

**追问 1**

- A: A simple text list in the repository, plus the issue list for shared tasks.

**追问 2**
- Q: Which part of time management is hardest?

### Q24. Tell me about a time when you were wrong, and how you found out.

**中文题意**：说说你错了的一次，以及你怎么发现的。

**中文翻译**：在球形机器人上，我以为更大的倾角增益会让机器人响应更快、平衡更好。我加大了，机器人开始振荡。我尝试更多调参，没有用。录包显示轮子指令更频繁地顶到限幅。回到 LQR 分析后我看到，更大的倾角权重需要多得多的控制量。所以真正的问题是饱和，不是增益不足。我对原因判断错了。我降低了倾角参考上限和内环增益，振荡消失。

**追问 1**

- 中文：现在会怎么做不同？——在改增益之前，更早查看指令信号是否触及限幅。

**追问 2**

- 中文：怎么确认原因？——降低上限和增益后，指令不再饱和，振荡停止。

**关键词与易错点**：wrong about the cause、recordings、saturation。这是 PS 与推荐信的饱和案例（P1），一定要与它一致；LQR 只是分析，不是部署。

### Q27. How do you debug a system with many interacting parts?

**中文题意**：怎样调试有许多相互作用部件的系统？

**考察点**：调试方法论。

**English answer**：I go from the outside in. First, I look at the recordings of the signals, to see where the first sign of the problem appears. Then I check the inputs: sign, unit, timing and frame. After that I test one part at a time, with simple inputs. I change one thing at a time and keep notes. I separate the problem types: a slow drift suggests bias, a sudden fall at a step suggests saturation, and a fall after a bump suggests contact. On the wheel-legged robot, this method found three different causes. When I cannot reproduce a problem, I add logging first.

**中文翻译**：我由外向内。首先看信号录包，找到问题最早出现的位置。然后检查输入：符号、单位、时序和坐标系。之后一次测一个部分，用简单输入。一次只改一件事并记笔记。我把问题类型分开：缓慢漂移提示偏置，阶跃时突然倒下提示饱和，颠簸后倒下提示接触。在轮腿机器人上，这个方法找到了三个不同原因。无法复现问题时，我先加日志。

**追问 1**
- Q: What do you log?
- A: Raw sensor values with timestamps, the state, the command, and the limits that were active.
- 中文：你记录什么？——带时间戳的原始传感器值、状态、命令以及生效的限幅。

**追问 2**

- A: I try to predict what the fix should change in the recording, then check that it changed.
- 中文：怎样避免改错地方？——先预测修复应在录包中改变什么，再核对它是否真的改变。

**关键词与易错点**：outside in、inputs first、one change at a time、drift/saturation/contact。与 Q13 的故障分型一致。

### Q28. How do you know when a solution is good enough?

**中文题意**：怎么知道方案已经足够好？

**考察点**：验收标准意识。

**English answer**：I define the acceptance criteria before I start tuning. For example, the robot keeps its balance for a set time under a defined arm motion, or the launcher keeps its speed within a range during continuous fire. I measure it on repeated runs, not only one good run. I also check the margin: does it still work when the battery is lower, or the load is slightly different? If it passes with margin, and the failure cases are understood and safe, I stop. If not, I ask whether the problem is the design or just the tuning. I also consider the time left, and the risk of changing something late.

**中文翻译**：开始调参之前，我就定义验收标准。比如机器人在规定的手臂运动下保持平衡一段时间，或发射机构在连发时转速保持在范围内。我在重复运行上测量，而不是只看一次成功。我也检查裕度：电池电量较低或负载略有不同时是否还能工作？如果带裕度通过，失败情形也被理解且安全，我就停。如果不是，我会问问题在设计还是只是调参。我还会考虑剩余时间和后期改动的风险。

**追问 1**
- Q: What is a "margin"?
- A: Extra room before a limit, such as a smaller torque than the maximum, so small changes don't cause failure.
- 中文：什么是"裕度"？——距离限制的额外空间，比如力矩小于最大值，使小变化不会导致失败。

**追问 2**
- Q: Have you stopped too early?
- A: Possibly. That's why I record the failure cases and the conditions in which the result holds.
- 中文：过早停过吗？——可能；所以我记录失败情形和结果成立的条件。

**关键词与易错点**：acceptance criteria、repeated runs、margin、known failure cases。

**关键词与易错点**：how I learn、plain words、small example。该答案来自 [英文自我介绍 7.1](../01_面试总指南/02_英文自我介绍_多版本.md)。

### Q58. Is LQR actually better than PID? Can you prove it?

**考察点**：避免绝对化，展现理解。

**English answer**：I can't prove that LQR is better in general, and I don't think it is. LQR is optimal for a linear model and a quadratic cost, when the model is accurate and the full state is available. It handles coupled states systematically. But with model errors, delay and actuator saturation, its advantage can disappear. A well-tuned PID can be just as good for a simple loop, and it is easier to deploy. So "better" depends on the task. That is why I used LQR on the ballbot as an analysis tool, and deployed a cascaded PID. To compare them properly, I would test both on the same task with the same limits.

**中文翻译**：我无法证明 LQR 总体更好，我也不认为如此。LQR 在线性模型和二次代价下是最优的，前提是模型准确且全状态可得。它系统地处理耦合状态。但存在模型误差、延迟和执行器饱和时，它的优势可能消失。对简单回路，调得好的 PID 可以同样好，而且更容易部署。所以"更好"取决于任务。这也是我在球形机器人上把 LQR 当作分析工具、部署级联 PID 的原因。要公平比较，我会在同样任务、同样限制下测试两者。

**追问 1**
- Q: What does "optimal" mean here?
- A: It minimises the chosen cost function. If the cost does not match what we really want, optimal can still be bad.
- 中文：这里"最优"是什么意思？——最小化所选的代价函数；如果代价不符合我们真正想要的，最优也可能不好。

**追问 2**
- Q: When would you choose LQR?
- A: When I have a good linear model, several coupled states, and I want to trade off performance and effort systematically.
- 中文：什么时候选 LQR？——有好的线性模型、多个耦合状态，并想系统权衡性能和控制量时。

**关键词与易错点**：optimal for linear + quadratic、model error and saturation、depends on the task。不要说"LQR 一定更好"或"PID 更好"。

## 7. F 组：怪题与开放题

> 应对原则：先停顿 3 秒；说明你的思路（"I'll answer in two parts"）；把答案落到有依据的经历；对没有意义的怪题不要紧张，认真、简短地回答并保持友好。

### Q59. Convince me in 30 seconds that we should admit you.

**中文题意**：用 30 秒说服我们录取你。

**考察点**：浓缩和说服。

**English answer**：You should admit me for three reasons. First, evidence: ranked sixth out of 156, and real work on a ballbot, a wheel-legged robot, a navigation vehicle and a multi-robot platform. Second, honesty and rigour: I know where my knowledge stops, and I go back to models when tuning stops explaining. Third, a clear plan: I want the foundations in optimisation and learning, and then to work as a robotics algorithm engineer in the Greater Bay Area. I will work hard, I'll ask good questions, and I will help my classmates.

**中文翻译**：您应该录取我，有三个理由。第一，证据：专业排名 156 人中第 6，并在球形机器人、轮腿机器人、导航车和多机器人平台上做过真实工作。第二，诚实与严谨：我知道自己知识的边界，调参解释不了时我会回到模型。第三，清晰的计划：我想要最优化和学习的基础，然后在大湾区做机器人算法工程师。我会努力、会问好问题，也会帮助同学。

**追问 1**
- Q: What is the most important reason?
- A: The evidence of real work, because it shows I can finish things.
- 中文：最重要的理由？——真实工作的证据，因为它说明我能把事情做完。

**追问 2**
- Q: What if we have stronger candidates?
- A: I can only tell you what I can offer. I'm honest, I learn fast, and I'm ready to work.
- 中文：如果有更强的候选人呢？——我只能说我能提供什么：诚实、学得快、准备好努力。

**关键词与易错点**：evidence, honesty, plan。30 秒约 70 词；不要加"我最好"之类比较。

### Q60. If your method fails in the real world, whose fault is it?

**中文题意**：如果你的方法在真实世界里失败，是谁的错？

**考察点**：责任感，不甩锅。

**English answer**：It is the engineer's responsibility to find out, and I would not blame the world. If a method fails on a real robot, it means that an assumption did not hold, or that something was not tested. My job is to find which. On the wheel-legged robot, the balance failed because of state inconsistency, torque limits and ground-contact transitions, which were all things that I could handle after I understood them. Failure does not mean that a person is bad. It is information, and a good engineer uses it to improve the design, the tests or the safeguards.

**中文翻译**：弄清楚是工程师的责任，我不会责怪世界。方法在真机上失败，意味着某个假设不成立，或者某些东西没测试。我的工作是找出是哪个。在轮腿机器人上，平衡失败是因为状态不一致、力矩限幅和触地切换，这些在我理解之后都是我能处理的。失败不意味着人不好，它是信息，好的工程师用它来改进设计、测试或保障。

**追问 1**
- Q: And if the cause is outside your control?
- A: Then I explain it clearly, add a safeguard for it, and tell the people who can fix it.
- 中文：如果原因不在你控制之内呢？——我会清楚地解释，加一个保障，并告知能修复它的人。

**追问 2**
- Q: How do you present a failure?
- A: Honestly: what happened, what we learned, and what we changed.
- 中文：怎样呈现失败？——诚实地：发生了什么、学到了什么、改了什么。

**关键词与易错点**：assumption didn't hold、engineer's responsibility、failure is information。不要说"硬件的问题"。

### Q61. What if I tell you that your answer is wrong?

**中文题意**：如果我说你的回答是错的呢？

**考察点**：被质疑时的反应。

**English answer**：I would listen carefully, and ask which part you think is wrong. Then I would check my reasoning. If you are right, I would say so and correct it, and thank you. If I still believe my answer is right, I would explain my reason politely with an example or a simple calculation, and accept that we may see it differently. Either way, I want to learn. In an engineering team, it is better to find a mistake in a discussion than on the robot. So I welcome the challenge, even if I'm a little nervous in the moment.

**中文翻译**：我会认真听，并问您认为哪一部分错。然后检查我的推理。如果您对，我会承认并更正，同时感谢您。如果我仍相信自己的回答，我会礼貌地用例子或简单计算解释理由，并接受我们可能看法不同。无论哪种，我都想学习。在工程团队里，在讨论中发现错误好过在机器人上发现。所以我欢迎挑战，即使当下有点紧张。

**追问 1**
- Q: What would you say if you cannot decide?
- A: "I'm not sure. Could I think about it and check afterwards?"
- 中文：如果你无法判断呢？——"我不确定，能否让我想一想并在之后核对？"

**追问 2**
- Q: Have you changed an answer in an interview before?
- A: In this interview, I try to correct myself when I notice a mistake.
- 中文：面试中改过答案吗？——在这次面试里，发现错误时我会更正。

**关键词与易错点**：listen, check, correct or explain。不要立即争辩，也不要立即全盘放弃自己的答案。

### Q62. Answer in one sentence: what is robotics?

**中文题意**：用一句话回答：什么是机器人学？

**考察点**：提炼和清晰。

**English answer**：Robotics is the science and engineering of making machines that sense, decide and act in the physical world, and doing it reliably. I would add that the word "reliably" is the hard part, because the real world has noise, delay and limits that a model does not capture. That is also why I like it. In one more sentence, robotics brings together control, perception, planning, mechanics and software, so that a machine can do something useful. In my own work, it also means that a robot must stay safe when something goes wrong, not only work when everything is fine.

**中文翻译**：机器人学是让机器在物理世界里感知、决策和行动，并可靠地做到这一点的科学与工程。我想补充，"可靠"这个词是难点，因为真实世界有模型没有捕捉的噪声、延迟和限制，这也是我喜欢它的原因。再补一句，机器人学把控制、感知、规划、机械和软件结合起来，让机器能做有用的事。在我自己的工作里，这还意味着机器人在出问题时也必须保持安全，而不只是在一切正常时才能工作。

**追问 1**
- Q: And for a ten-year-old?
- A: Robotics is teaching machines to see, think and move to help people.
- 中文：给十岁孩子怎么说？——机器人学是教机器去看、去想、去动，来帮助人。

**追问 2**
- Q: What is the difference between robotics and automation?
- A: Automation repeats a fixed process. Robotics adds sensing and decisions, so the machine can respond to changes.
- 中文：机器人学和自动化有何区别？——自动化重复固定流程；机器人学加入感知与决策，让机器能应对变化。

**关键词与易错点**：sense, decide, act、reliably。一句话就说完，后面的补充用"I would add"。

### Q63. If you had one week to learn a new robot, what would you do?

**中文题意**：如果只有一周学会一个新机器人，你怎么做？

**考察点**：快速上手方法。

**English answer**：I would plan the week in stages. Day one: read the documents, the interfaces and the safety rules, and draw the system diagram with sensors, actuators and signals, units and rates. Day two: run the simplest safe test, with the robot lifted or restrained, and record the signals. Days three and four: check signs, directions and limits, and then run a basic motion. Day five: write down what I learned, the known problems and a test list. The weekend: a small, safe task, and a short report. I would ask for help early, and I would not skip the safety checks to save time.

**中文翻译**：我会分阶段规划这一周。第一天：读文档、接口和安全规则，画出带传感器、执行器、信号、单位和频率的系统图。第二天：在机器人悬空或固定的情况下运行最简单的安全测试，并记录信号。第三、四天：检查符号、方向和限制，然后运行基本动作。第五天：写下所学、已知问题和测试清单。周末：做一个小而安全的任务，并写简短报告。我会及早求助，不会为节省时间而跳过安全检查。

**追问 1**
- Q: What is the first thing you check?
- A: The emergency stop and the limits, before anything moves.
- 中文：你先检查什么？——急停和限制，在任何东西动之前。

**追问 2**
- Q: Have you done this before?
- A: Yes, in a shorter form: at Future Power, I started with an existing hardware chain and brought up teleoperation and wheel control in a short internship.
- 中文：以前这样做过吗？——做过，较短的形式：在未来动力，我在现有硬件链上，在短实习里搭起了遥操作和轮控制。

**关键词与易错点**：diagram first、safe test、signs and limits、write down。强调安全优先。

### Q64. If you were a robot, what kind of robot would you be?

**中文题意**：如果你是一个机器人，你会是什么类型的？

**考察点**：幽默感和自我认知，用结构化方式。

**English answer**：I would be a wheel-legged robot. That is a little funny, but there's a reason. A wheel-legged robot is stable and efficient most of the time, and it can adapt its height when the ground changes. I would like to be like that: steady in my daily work, and flexible when the situation changes. I have also worked on one, so I know the weaknesses: it can fall if the state is wrong, so it needs good sensors and a careful operator. And I need honest feedback, as that robot needs good sensors.

**中文翻译**：我会是一个轮腿机器人。这有点好笑，但有理由。轮腿机器人大部分时间稳定高效，地面变化时又能调整高度。我想像它一样：日常工作稳定，情况变化时灵活。我也做过一个，所以知道它的弱点：状态不对时会摔倒，需要好的传感器和细心的操作员。而我需要诚实的反馈，就像那个机器人需要好的传感器。

**追问 1**
- Q: What is the weakness?
- A: I can get too focused on details. Good feedback helps me to adjust, like a sensor.
- 中文：弱点是什么？——我可能过于专注细节；好的反馈像传感器一样帮我调整。

**追问 2**
- Q: Why not a humanoid?
- A: Because I'd rather be something I understand well and can be honest about.
- 中文：为什么不是人形？——因为我宁愿是自己了解得很好、可以诚实谈论的那一种。

**关键词与易错点**：wheel-legged、stable and adaptive、good sensors = honest feedback。怪题用一两个比喻并回到事实；不要编复杂的故事。

### Q65. If you had an unlimited budget for one robot project, what would you build?

**中文题意**：如果预算无限，你会做什么机器人项目？

**考察点**：想象力和落地性。

**English answer**：I would not start with the biggest robot. I would build a small platform for fair testing of control and learning methods: a few wheel-legged and mobile robots with the same sensors, in an arena with a motion-capture system to measure the true state, and a good simulator with the same limits. Then I could compare methods fairly, and find where learning helps and where model-based control is enough. With unlimited budget, I'd also put money into safety: gantries, emergency stops and logging. The result would be knowledge, not only a demo.

**中文翻译**：我不会从最大的机器人开始。我会搭一个公平测试控制与学习方法的小平台：几台使用相同传感器的轮腿和移动机器人，放在有动捕系统测量真实状态的场地，加上一个有相同限制的好仿真器。然后我能公平比较各方法，找到学习在哪有帮助、在哪里基于模型的控制就够了。预算无限时，我还会把钱投在安全上：龙门架、急停和日志。成果是知识，而不只是演示。

**追问 1**
- Q: Why a motion-capture system?
- A: It gives a true position and orientation, so I can measure the error of my estimates.
- 中文：为什么要动捕系统？——它给出真实位置和姿态，让我能测量估计的误差。

**追问 2**
- Q: What is the first experiment?
- A: Compare PID, MPC and a learned correction on one balance task, with the same pushes.
- 中文：第一个实验是什么？——在同一个平衡任务上，用相同的推力，比较 PID、MPC 和学习修正。

**关键词与易错点**：fair testbed、true state、safety、knowledge not demo。这是设想，不是已有经验。

### Q66. Is there a question you wish we had asked?

**中文题意**：有没有你希望我们问的问题？

**考察点**：收尾与主动性。

**English answer**：Yes. I wish you had asked, "What would you do in your first month in the programme?" My answer would be: review the mathematics I need, choose courses with my advisor, and find a small project where I can test what I learn on a real system or in simulation. I would also join a study group, and talk with people from other backgrounds. I'd like to ask you one question as well, if there is time: what do successful students in your programme have in common? It would help me to prepare well.

**中文翻译**：有。我希望您问"你在项目第一个月会做什么？"我的回答是：复习需要的数学，与学业导师选课，并找一个小项目，把所学在真实系统或仿真上检验。我也会参加学习小组，与不同背景的人交流。如果还有时间，我也想问您一个问题：贵项目里成功的学生有什么共同点？这会帮助我做好准备。

**追问 1**
- Q: Why that question?
- A: Because the first month decides the habits for the rest of the programme.
- 中文：为什么是这个问题？——因为第一个月决定之后的习惯。

**追问 2**
- Q: What would you ask the faculty next?
- A: How coursework students can join a lab project.
- 中文：接下来想问教师什么？——授课型学生如何参与实验室项目。

**关键词与易错点**：first month plan、what successful students share、a question back。反问示例见 [反问面试官的问题](../01_面试总指南/06_反问面试官的问题.md)。

## 8. 自测与练习安排

### 8.1 自测清单

| 检查项 | 通过标准 |
|---|---|
| 优缺点 | Q1、Q2 各 45 秒说完，优点有例子，缺点有计划 |
| 失败与错误 | Q13、Q14、Q24 三个故事各能在 60 秒内讲完，并且与 P1、P4 口径一致 |
| 敏感题 | Q45–Q50 六题不看稿能答，数字一致：87.63、6/156、IELTS 6.5（小分只在被问到时说） |
| 压力题 | Q57、Q58 能讲清 PID 与 LQR 的关系，不说 LQR 是部署控制器 |
| 兴趣与价值观 | Q35、Q41、Q42 的回答没有编造爱好、书籍或志愿经历 |
| 怪题 | 任选 2 个怪题，先停顿再回答，并把话题带回有依据的经历 |

### 8.2 练习安排

| 天 | 内容 | 时间 |
|---|---|---|
| 第 1 天 | A 组 Q1–Q12：每题 40 秒，录音 | 60 分钟 |
| 第 2 天 | B 组 Q13–Q24：重点练三个故事 | 75 分钟 |
| 第 3 天 | C 组 Q25–Q34：口述加追问 | 60 分钟 |
| 第 4 天 | D 组 Q35–Q44：核对每个答案都是真的，必要时改成你的真实情况 | 60 分钟 |
| 第 5 天 | E 组 Q45–Q58：模拟压力，请朋友追问两层 | 75 分钟 |
| 第 6 天 | F 组 Q59–Q66：限时 30–60 秒 | 45 分钟 |
| 第 7 天 | 综合：朋友随机抽 10 题，只给英文题目，限时回答，录像回看 | 60 分钟 |

### 8.3 常见错误回顾

1. 编造爱好、书籍、志愿经历，被追问就露馅。
2. 把弱项说成优点（"我太追求完美"）。
3. 在敏感题里防御或抱怨（"这不公平"）。
4. 数字不一致（排名、均分、雅思）。
5. 说 LQR 控制了球形机器人、说 "I won third prize"、说 "1 kHz"。
6. 为实习的结束编造具体原因。
