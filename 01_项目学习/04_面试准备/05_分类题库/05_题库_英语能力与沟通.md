# 分类题库 05：英语能力与沟通（English ability and communication）

> **一句话定位**：这一份题库专门解决"英语不够流利怎么办"：被问到英语和沟通能力时怎么答，怎样用简单的话向非专业人士解释你的项目，听不懂时怎么应对，怎样请面试官重复、被打断后怎样接上，口语卡壳时怎样恢复，以及课程语言、课堂参与和英文表达的问题。共 52 题，每题有英文回答、中文翻译和两个追问。

**本章学完你能……**

- 诚实而有分寸地评价自己的英语：说出 6.5 的事实、阅读强口语弱的特点、已有的证据和具体的提升计划；
- 用 20 个以内的简单句型，把 PID、LQR、导航链、轮腿、球形机器人、园艺系统讲给非专业人士；
- 面试中听不懂、没听清、被打断、卡壳时，立刻用得上的 30 多句现成英语；
- 对"课程语言""课堂参与""英文汇报"类问题给出可信的答案。

**前置知识**：[英文口语表达_技术词汇与句型](../01_面试总指南/07_英文口语表达_技术词汇与句型.md)、[技术口述方法](../01_面试总指南/08_技术口述方法_如何讲清一个项目.md)、[口径与事实基线 2.3](../../00_申请画像/04_口径与事实基线.md)。

## 1. 本文件怎么用

1. **英语水平的事实**：IELTS Academic 总分 6.5；小分听力 6.0、阅读 8.0、写作 6.0、口语 5.5（取自申请总览，见 [口径与事实基线 2.3](../../00_申请画像/04_口径与事实基线.md)）。主动说总分和"阅读是我最强的一项"；小分只在被直接问到时说。
2. **你已有的英语使用证据**（只能说这些）：读 ROS/ROS2、OpenHarmony 和芯片手册的英文文档；用英文写 README 和代码注释；CV 和 PS 是用英文写的；为本次面试做的出声练习和录音。与国际同学合作较少，被问到就如实说 "limited, which is one reason I want an international programme"。
3. **句子要短**：每句 8–15 个词，一个意思。长句留给书面语。这一份的所有英文答案都按"能说出口"来写。
4. **先结论后例子**：英语不流利时，结构比词汇更能让人听懂。先说一句结论，再给一个例子，最后一句收束。
5. **简单词优先**：用 "use" 不用 "utilise"，用 "show" 不用 "demonstrate"。技术词（PID、LQR、EKF、MPC）一定保留，因为面试官懂。
6. **向非专业人士解释**：B 组所有答案都用类比和生活语言，不用公式。技术评委要的时候，再换回技术说法（见各题的"技术版一句话"）。
7. **应急句型要背熟**：第 2 节的表是你"救命"的句子。练到不假思索，面试里遇到没听清就能立刻说出来，不显得慌。
8. **每题格式**：英文题目、中文题意、考察点、英文回答、中文翻译、两个追问、关键词与易错点。

> **面试要点**：面试官不期待你的英语像母语者。他们期待你能清楚地沟通，遇到困难时知道怎样处理。会礼貌地请对方重复，比假装听懂更加分。

## 2. 应急句型速查表

### 2.1 没听清、请重复

| 场景 | 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 完全没听清 | Sorry, could you repeat the question, please? | 抱歉，能请您重复一下问题吗？ |
| 请对方慢一点 | Could you say that a little more slowly, please? | 能请您说慢一点吗？ |
| 没听清一个词 | Sorry, I missed the word after "..." Could you say it again? | 抱歉，我没听清"……"后面的词，能再说一遍吗？ |
| 网络问题 | I'm sorry, the connection broke up. Could you repeat that? | 抱歉，网络断了，能重复一下吗？ |
| 听到一半 | I caught the first part, about ..., but not the second part. | 我听到前半部分（关于……），后半部分没听到。 |

### 2.2 确认理解

| 场景 | 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 复述确认 | Let me check that I understand. You are asking about ..., is that right? | 我确认一下理解：您问的是……对吗？ |
| 二选一澄清 | Do you mean A or B? | 您指的是 A 还是 B？ |
| 请举例 | Could you give me an example of what you mean? | 能给我一个您所指的例子吗？ |
| 问范围 | Do you want a short answer, or more detail? | 您要简短回答，还是更详细的？ |
| 不熟悉的词 | I'm not familiar with that term. Could you explain it briefly? | 我不熟悉这个术语，能简要解释一下吗？ |

### 2.3 争取时间、重新组织

| 场景 | 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 需要想一想 | That's a good question. Let me think for a moment. | 这是个好问题，让我想一下。 |
| 重新组织 | Let me say that in a different way. | 让我换个方式说。 |
| 想不起词 | I can't think of the exact word, but it is something like ... | 我想不起确切的词，但大概是…… |
| 说错了 | Sorry, let me correct that. I meant ... | 抱歉，让我更正，我的意思是…… |
| 回到主线 | Going back to your question, ... | 回到您的问题…… |

### 2.4 被打断、收尾

| 场景 | 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 被打断 | Of course. Please go ahead. | 当然，您请说。 |
| 接上 | May I finish my last point? It's one sentence. | 我能说完最后一点吗？只有一句。 |
| 收尾 | That's the main point. I'm happy to give more detail. | 要点就是这样，我乐意提供更多细节。 |
| 听不懂口音 | Sorry, I'm not used to that accent yet. Could you repeat? | 抱歉，我还不太习惯这个口音，能重复吗？ |
| 结束 | Thank you. That's all from me. | 谢谢，我说完了。 |

## 3. A 组：英语能力的自我评价

### Q1. How would you describe your English ability honestly?

**中文题意**：请你诚实地评价自己的英语能力。

**考察点**：自我评价的真实度、证据和计划。

**English answer**：My IELTS overall score is 6.5. My reading is my strongest part, and my speaking is the weakest. For technical topics, I'm more comfortable, because I read English documentation every day, I write code comments and READMEs in English, and I wrote my CV and statement in English. Fast conversation is harder for me, especially with unfamiliar accents. So I'd say I can communicate clearly on technical subjects, and I'm still improving my speed in open conversation. I practise speaking about my projects aloud every day, and I'm glad to practise with you now.

**中文翻译**：我的雅思总分是 6.5。阅读是我最强的部分，口语最弱。技术话题我更自如，因为我每天读英文文档，用英文写代码注释和 README，CV 和陈述也是用英文写的。快速对话对我更难，尤其是陌生口音。所以我可以说，我能在技术话题上清楚沟通，在开放对话的速度上还在提高。我每天出声讲项目来练习，也很高兴现在和您练习。

**追问 1**
- Q: Do you think that's enough for a master's?
- A: For understanding and writing, I think yes. For speaking, I'll keep improving by using English every day in class.
- 中文：你认为读硕士够吗？——理解和写作我认为够；口语我会通过课堂上每天使用英语继续提高。

**追问 2**
- Q: What was your speaking score?
- A: 5.5. It is my lowest, and I'm working on it.
- 中文：口语分是多少？——5.5，是我最低的，我在改进。

**关键词与易错点**：6.5 overall、reading strongest、speaking weakest、technical English comfortable。不要说 "my English is perfect"，也不要说 "my English is bad"。

### Q2. Can you follow lectures and read technical papers in English?

**中文题意**：你能听懂英文授课、读英文技术论文吗？

**考察点**：学习语言的实际能力。

**English answer**：I think I can follow lectures on technical topics, especially with slides, because I know most of the key terms from my projects. If a lecturer speaks very fast, I may miss some details, so I plan to read the slides before the class and review after it. For reading, I read English documentation daily, and I have not read many research papers in depth yet. I think my reading will transfer well, since reading is my strongest skill. I will start with the abstract and the figures, and then the method. I expect to improve quickly.

**中文翻译**：我认为我能听懂技术话题的授课，尤其有课件时，因为我从项目里知道大部分关键术语。如果教师说得很快，我可能漏掉一些细节，所以我计划课前读课件、课后复习。阅读方面，我每天读英文文档，但还没有深入读过很多研究论文。我认为我的阅读能力会很好地迁移，因为阅读是我最强的技能。我会先读摘要和图，再读方法。我预期会进步得很快。

**追问 1**
- Q: How do you handle a paper full of unknown terms?
- A: I read it in rounds. First the structure, then the key terms, then the method, and I keep a short term list.
- 中文：论文满是生词怎么办？——分几轮读：先结构，再关键术语，再方法，并保留一个简短的术语表。

**追问 2**
- Q: What if lectures are not recorded?
- A: I'll take notes of the key terms, then check them with classmates and the slides afterwards.
- 中文：课程没有录像呢？——记录关键术语，课后与同学和课件核对。

**关键词与易错点**：slides before class、review after、rounds of reading。不要夸大读论文的经验。

### Q3. What do you think will be the biggest challenge of studying in English?

**中文题意**：你认为用英语学习最大的挑战是什么？

**考察点**：现实的挑战认知。

**English answer**：For me, the biggest challenge is live discussion at normal speed. In a seminar, people speak fast, use idioms, and change topic quickly, and I need a moment to find the words. I may be slower to join a discussion, and I may miss a joke. Reading and writing are less of a problem. To manage it, I'll prepare questions before class, take a seat where I can hear well, and ask for repeats without feeling shy. I think that after a few weeks of daily use, my listening speed will improve a lot.

**中文翻译**：对我来说最大的挑战是正常语速的现场讨论。研讨中人们说得快，用习语，话题变化快，我需要片刻找词。我可能参与讨论慢一点，也可能错过一个玩笑。阅读和写作的问题较小。为此，我会课前准备问题，坐在听得清楚的位置，并不害羞地请对方重复。我认为经过几周的每日使用，我的听力速度会提高很多。

**追问 1**
- Q: How will you prepare questions before class?
- A: I read the slides and write two questions: one about a term, one about the main idea.
- 中文：课前怎么准备问题？——读课件，写两个问题：一个关于术语，一个关于主要思想。

**追问 2**
- Q: What if you feel shy?
- A: I remember that asking questions is normal and useful for everyone.
- 中文：害羞怎么办？——我记住提问是正常的，对每个人都有用。

**关键词与易错点**：live discussion at normal speed、idioms、prepare questions、ask for repeats。承认挑战加给对策。

### Q4. How are you improving your English?

**中文题意**：你怎样提高英语？

**考察点**：具体行动。

**English answer**：I focus on speaking, because that is my weakest part. Every day, I explain one of my projects aloud for a few minutes, and sometimes I record myself, so that I can listen to my speed and my unclear words. I prepare short answers with a clear structure: a conclusion, an example and an ending. I also read English documentation every day, which keeps my technical vocabulary active. For listening, I use technical talks and lectures in English. And I use this kind of interview practice with friends. I'll keep a list of the words that I could not say, and I'll learn them.

**中文翻译**：我专注口语，因为这是我最弱的部分。每天我出声讲解一个项目几分钟，有时录音，听自己的语速和不清楚的词。我准备结构清晰的短答案：结论、例子和收尾。我也每天读英文文档，保持技术词汇活跃。听力方面，我听英文的技术演讲和课程。我还和朋友做这类面试练习。我会记下我说不出来的词，然后学会它们。

**追问 1**
- Q: How long do you practise each day?
- A: Usually about thirty minutes to one hour at this time, before the interviews.
- 中文：每天练多久？——这段面试前的时间里，通常半小时到一小时。

**追问 2**
- Q: What did you learn from recording yourself?
- A: That I speak too fast when I'm nervous, and I drop the ends of words. So I now pause at the end of sentences.
- 中文：录音让你发现了什么？——我紧张时说得太快，吞词尾；所以现在句末会停顿。

**关键词与易错点**：speak aloud daily、record、structure、word list。时长是你面试前的练习量；听技术演讲、与朋友练习这些习惯请按你的真实做法改写。

### Q5. How did you prepare for the IELTS?

**中文题意**：你怎样准备雅思？

**考察点**：学习方法的真实度。

**English answer**：I prepared by practising each section with sample tests, and by learning the types of questions. I found that reading and writing improved well with practice, because I could study alone. Speaking and listening need more daily exposure, and I did less of that. My reading score was the highest. Looking back, my speaking would have improved more if I had spoken with other people regularly. That is the lesson that I use now: I practise speaking with partners, and I record myself.

**中文翻译**：我用样题练习每个部分，并学习题型。我发现阅读和写作通过练习提高得很好，因为可以独自学习。口语和听力需要更多的日常接触，而我在这方面做得较少。我的阅读分最高。回头看，如果我定期和其他人说话，口语会进步更多。这是我现在应用的教训：我和伙伴练习口语，并录音。

**追问 1**
- Q: Would you take the test again?
- A: If the programme requires, yes. Otherwise, I think daily practice is better.
- 中文：会再考吗？——项目要求就会；否则日常练习更好。

**追问 2**
- Q: What is the difference between the test and the real study?
- A: The test is general English. In study, I use technical English, which is my stronger area.
- 中文：考试和真实学习的区别？——考试是通用英语；学习用技术英语，这是我更强的领域。

**关键词与易错点**：sample tests、lesson learned、speak with partners。备考细节请按你的真实情况改写；此处只写了不会被核对的一般做法。

### Q6. Can you read English research papers? How do you read them?

**中文题意**：你能读英文研究论文吗？你怎么读？

**考察点**：阅读策略的真实度。

**English answer**：I can read them with effort, and I'm still building the habit. I haven't read many papers in depth, mostly technical documentation. My method is to read in three rounds. First, the abstract, the figures and the conclusion, to see what the paper claims. Second, the method, where I look for the main equation or the main step, and I try to restate it in my own words. Third, the experiments, where I ask what the baseline is and whether the test is fair. I keep notes with new terms. I plan to read papers regularly before and during the programme.

**中文翻译**：我能读，但需要努力，也还在培养习惯。我没有深入读过很多论文，主要是技术文档。我的方法是分三轮。第一轮，摘要、图和结论，看论文主张什么。第二轮，方法，找主要公式或主要步骤，并尝试用自己的话重述。第三轮，实验，问基线是什么、测试是否公平。我用笔记记录新术语。我计划在项目前后定期读论文。

**追问 1**
- Q: What is the first paper you want to read?
- A: The original description of MPC or TEB, because I used them.
- 中文：想读的第一篇论文？——MPC 或 TEB 的原始描述，因为我用过它们。

**追问 2**
- Q: How long does a paper take you?
- A: At the moment, several hours for a careful read. I expect it to become faster.
- 中文：读一篇论文要多久？——目前仔细读需要几个小时，我预期会越来越快。

**关键词与易错点**：three rounds、restate in own words、baseline and fair test。与 [03 题库 Q26](./03_题库_职业规划与研究兴趣.md) 的"没读过多少论文"保持一致。

### Q7. Describe a time you used English at work.

**中文题意**：说说你在工作中使用英语的一次经历。

**考察点**：使用英语的真实场景。

**English answer**：I use English at work almost every day, in reading and writing. For example, when I worked on ROS 2 navigation, I read the official documentation and the discussion pages to solve configuration problems. When I worked with chip manuals for the STM32 and the Hi3861 boards, I read the technical reference in English. I write my code comments and READMEs in English. My CV and my statements are in English too. I haven't worked much with international colleagues, so I haven't used spoken English at work as often. That is one reason I want an international programme.

**中文翻译**：我几乎每天在工作中用英语读写。比如做 ROS 2 导航时，我读官方文档和讨论页来解决配置问题。使用 STM32 和 Hi3861 开发板的芯片手册时，我读英文技术参考。我的代码注释和 README 用英文写。CV 和陈述也是英文的。我没有和国际同事共事很多，所以工作中用口语英语不多。这也是我想要国际化项目的原因之一。

**追问 1**
- Q: Have you given a presentation in English?
- A: Not a long one. I've explained my projects in English in practice sessions, and I'm preparing more.
- 中文：用英语做过汇报吗？——没有长的；我在练习中用英语讲过项目，并在准备更多。

**追问 2**
- Q: Do you write emails in English?
- A: Yes, short professional emails. For example, to ask a question about a tool or an application step.
- 中文：你用英语写邮件吗？——写，简短的专业邮件，比如询问工具或申请步骤。

**关键词与易错点**：reading and writing daily、no international colleagues、spoken English limited。不要夸大口语使用。

### Q8. Your reading score is much higher than your speaking score. Why?

**中文题意**：你的阅读分比口语分高很多，为什么？

**考察点**：对差距原因的诚实分析。

**English answer**：I think the reason is practice. I read English every day for my work, so my reading is strong. But I speak English less, because my daily life and my study are mostly in Chinese. In speaking, I need to find words and make sentences quickly, and I haven't had enough practice. I also feel nervous in speaking tests, which makes me slower. I understand this gap, and I know how to close it: more speaking practice, with feedback. That's why I'm practising aloud now, and why an English-taught programme is so valuable for me.

**中文翻译**：我认为原因是练习量。我工作中每天读英语，所以阅读强。但我说英语较少，因为日常生活和学习大多用中文。口语要快速找词、造句，而我练习不够。我在口语考试时也会紧张，这让我更慢。我理解这个差距，也知道如何缩小：更多有反馈的口语练习。这就是我现在出声练习的原因，也是英语授课项目对我如此有价值的原因。

**追问 1**
- Q: Does being nervous affect you a lot?
- A: A little. When I take a short pause and use a structure, I speak much better.
- 中文：紧张对你影响大吗？——有一点；稍停一下并用结构，我会说得好很多。

**追问 2**
- Q: Do you think the gap will close?
- A: Yes. Speaking is a skill, and daily practice improves it.
- 中文：你认为差距会缩小吗？——会；口语是技能，每日练习会提高它。

**关键词与易错点**：practice gap、nervous in tests、close the gap。不要把差距归咎于考官或考试。

## 4. B 组：向非专业人士解释你的项目

> 方法：**类比 + 三步**。第一句说"它是什么"，第二句用生活类比，第三句说"我做了什么"。每个答案后给出一句"技术版"，当对方是技术评委时用。

### Q9. Explain your ballbot project to a non-expert.

**中文题意**：向非专业人士解释你的球形机器人项目。

**考察点**：把平衡控制讲成生活语言。

**English answer**：A ballbot is a robot that stands on one big ball, not on two or four wheels. Three small wheels turn the ball, so the robot can move in any direction. Balancing on a ball is like balancing a broom on the palm of your hand: it falls unless you keep moving your hand. ### Q10. Explain PID without any jargon.

**中文题意**：不用术语解释 PID。

**考察点**：用类比讲清控制的基本思想。

**English answer**：Think of driving a car and trying to keep a steady speed. If you are too slow, you press the pedal. That is the first part, the present error. If you have been too slow for a long time, you press a little more. That is the second part, the history of the error. If you see that you are speeding up quickly, you ease off early. That is the third part, the trend. PID uses these three ideas together to decide how hard to push. I use it for things like keeping a gimbal pointing at a target, or the speed of a motor.

**中文翻译**：想象开车并想保持稳定的速度。如果太慢，你踩油门，这是第一部分：当前的误差。如果你已经慢了很久，你再多踩一点，这是第二部分：误差的历史。如果你发现速度上升得很快，你提前松油门，这是第三部分：趋势。PID 同时使用这三个想法来决定用多大力。我用它来让云台对准目标，或控制电机的速度。

**技术版一句话**：PID computes the control output from the proportional, integral and derivative terms of the error.

**追问 1**
- Q: What happens if the "history" part is too strong?
- A: The output can overshoot and oscillate. This is called integral windup, so we limit it.
- 中文：如果"历史"部分太强会怎样？——输出可能过冲并振荡，这叫积分饱和（windup），所以要限幅。

**追问 2**
- Q: Where else could it be used?
- A: In a thermostat or a cruise control.
- 中文：还能用在哪里？——恒温器或定速巡航。

**关键词与易错点**：present, past, trend、car speed analogy、windup。类比限于一维速度控制，说明"这是简化说法"。

### Q17. Explain the idea of a Kalman filter to a non-engineer.

**中文翻译**：想象你在黑暗的房间里行走。你有两个信息来源。一个是你自己的猜测：你数了步数，所以认为自己在这里。另一个是测量：你摸到墙，大致告诉你位置。两者都可能出错。卡尔曼滤波把猜测与测量结合起来，并按各自的可靠程度信任它们。如果你的步数通常准确，就更信它；如果摸墙很精确，就更信那个。随着你移动，它一次次更新答案。

- 中文：如果她问"危险吗？"——我会说：我们缓慢谨慎地测试，有限制和急停，所以是安全的。

**追问 2**
- Q: Can you say it in one sentence?
- A: I teach robots how to move and find their way, safely.
- 中文：能一句话吗？——我教机器人安全地移动和找路。

**关键词与易错点**：sensors give numbers, motors need numbers、find out why and fix。向家人解释是常见的怪题形式，用 "a family member" 或 "someone without a technical background" 也可以。

## 5. C 组：听不懂、请重复、被打断、卡壳

> 这一组的"English answer"是你面对这种情况时会做什么，并附上可以直接说出口的句子。答案里引号中的句子，就是你在面试里要说的原话。

### Q21. What do you do if you do not understand a question in an interview?

**中文题意**：面试中没听懂问题时，你怎么办？

**考察点**：处理听力困难的成熟度。

**English answer**：I don't pretend that I understood. I stay calm and I say, "Sorry, could you repeat the question, please?" If I got some words, I say what I caught: "I understood that you are asking about the control part, but I missed the rest." That helps the interviewer to repeat only the missing part. If the question is still unclear, I check my understanding: "Do you mean the design or the testing?" It takes only a few seconds, and I think a correct answer to the right question is much better than a fast answer to the wrong one.

**中文翻译**：我不假装听懂。我保持冷静，说："抱歉，能请您重复一下问题吗？"如果我听到了一些词，我会说出我听到的："我听到您在问控制部分，但漏掉了其余内容。"这能让面试官只重复缺失的部分。如果问题仍不清楚，我确认自己的理解："您指的是设计还是测试？"这只花几秒钟，我认为对正确问题给出正确回答，远好于对错误问题给出很快的回答。

**追问 1**
- Q: What if the interviewer repeats and you still don't understand?
- A: I say, "Sorry, could you say it in a simpler way, or give me an example?"
- 中文：重复后仍没懂呢？——我说："抱歉，能用更简单的方式说，或给我一个例子吗？"

**追问 2**
- Q: Do interviewers mind repeating?
- A: In my understanding, no. A short, polite request is normal and shows that you care about being correct.
- 中文：面试官介意重复吗？——据我的理解不介意；简短礼貌的请求是正常的，说明你在意回答正确。

**关键词与易错点**：don't pretend、say what you caught、check your understanding。不要沉默很久；不要用中文问。

### Q22. Can you rephrase what you just said in other words?

**中文题意**：你能用别的话重新表达你刚才说的吗？

**考察点**：被要求换种说法时的应变（调研英语能力题之一）。

**English answer**：Of course. Let me say it in a simpler way. "The robot has to balance while its arms move. When the arms move, the weight changes. So my program watches the arms and adjusts the balance early." In short, the program does not wait for the robot to start falling. I think rephrasing is a good habit, because it shows that I understand the idea and not only the words. If a term is difficult, I use a short example. If you would like even more detail, I can draw it on paper.

**中文翻译**：当然，让我换个更简单的说法。"机器人必须在手臂移动时保持平衡。手臂一动，重量就变了。所以我的程序观察手臂，提前调整平衡。"简而言之，程序不等机器人开始倒才反应。我认为换种说法是好习惯，因为这说明我理解的是想法，而不只是词。如果一个术语难，我用一个简短的例子。如果您想要更多细节，我可以在纸上画出来。

**追问 1**
- Q: Which part was unclear?
- A: Possibly "centre-of-mass feedforward". It means we add a correction in advance, based on where the arms are.
- 中文：哪部分不清楚？——可能是"质心前馈"；意思是根据手臂的位置，提前加一个修正。

**追问 2**
- Q: Can you say it in one sentence?
- A: The robot predicts the effect of the arms and corrects before it feels the error.
- 中文：能一句话吗？——机器人预判手臂的影响，在感觉到误差之前先修正。

**关键词与易错点**：simpler words、short example、offer to draw。这里的例子是 P1 的质心前馈，换成其他项目时保持同样结构。

### Q23. How would you politely ask the interviewer to repeat or slow down?

**中文题意**：你会怎样礼貌地请面试官重复或放慢？

**考察点**：礼貌用语。

**English answer**：I use short, polite sentences and a smile. "Sorry, could you repeat that, please?" "Could you speak a little more slowly, please? I want to be sure that I understand." "Sorry, I missed the last word. Could you say it again?" I always say "please" and "sorry" first, and I say the reason, because I want to be correct. I avoid saying "What?" or "Pardon?" with a rising, short tone, which can sound rude. After they repeat, I say "Thank you" and start my answer. I practise these sentences until they feel natural.

**中文翻译**：我用简短礼貌的句子，并带着微笑。"抱歉，能请您重复一下吗？""能请您说慢一点吗？我想确保我理解了。""抱歉，我漏了最后一个词，能再说一遍吗？"我总是先说 "please" 和 "sorry"，并说明原因：我想答得正确。我避免用短促上扬的语气说 "What?" 或 "Pardon?"，那样可能显得不礼貌。他们重复后，我说 "Thank you" 并开始回答。我反复练习这些句子，直到变得自然。

**追问 1**
- Q: What if the video lags?
- A: "I'm sorry, the connection broke up for a moment. Could you repeat the last sentence?"
- 中文：如果视频卡顿呢？——"抱歉，连接中断了一下，能重复最后一句吗？"

**追问 2**
- Q: Do you worry that asking too often looks weak?
- A: A little. But a polite request is better than a wrong answer. I also try to listen very carefully the first time.
- 中文：担心问太多显得弱吗？——有一点；但礼貌请求好过错误回答，我也尽量第一次就认真听。

**关键词与易错点**：please + sorry + reason、slowly、thank you。句型见第 2 节速查表。

### Q24. What do you do if you understood only part of the question?

**中文题意**：只听懂问题的一部分时怎么办？

**考察点**：复述确认的技巧。

**English answer**：I repeat the part that I understood, and ask about the rest. For example: "So you are asking how I tested the controller. Did you also ask about the safety checks?" This is called paraphrasing, and it has two benefits. The interviewer can correct me quickly, and I gain a few seconds to think. If the question has two parts, I answer them in order and say so: "I'll answer in two parts. First, ... Second, ..." This helps me to stay organised and shows the interviewer that I followed.

**中文翻译**：我重复我听懂的那部分，并询问其余部分。例如："所以您在问我如何测试控制器。您是否也问了安全检查？"这叫复述，有两个好处：面试官可以很快纠正我，我也赢得几秒钟思考。如果问题有两部分，我按顺序回答并说明："我分两部分回答。第一，……第二，……"这有助于我保持条理，也向面试官表明我跟上了。

**追问 1**
- Q: What if your paraphrase is wrong?
- A: The interviewer will correct it, and that is useful. It's better to know early.
- 中文：复述错了呢？——面试官会纠正，这很有用，早知道更好。

**追问 2**
- Q: How do you remember a two-part question?
- A: I note one keyword for each part on paper.
- 中文：怎么记住两部分的问题？——在纸上为每部分记一个关键词。

**关键词与易错点**：paraphrase、in two parts、keyword on paper。准备好纸笔（真人面试允许时）。

### Q25. What if the interviewer speaks fast or has an accent you are not used to?

**中文题意**：面试官说得快或口音不熟悉怎么办？

**考察点**：听力应对。

**English answer**：I do three things. First, I listen for the key words and not for every word, such as the topic and the verb. Second, I say politely, "Sorry, I'm not used to this accent yet. Could you repeat that more slowly?" Third, I paraphrase to check. I also use the context: if the topic is control, I can guess that "gain" and "loop" will appear. In the first minute, I may need a little more time to adjust to the voice, and I think that is normal for everybody. After that, it usually becomes easier.

**中文翻译**：我做三件事。第一，听关键词，而不是每个词，比如主题和动词。第二，礼貌地说："抱歉，我还不太习惯这个口音，能请您说慢一点重复吗？"第三，用复述来核对。我也利用上下文：如果主题是控制，我能猜到会出现 "gain" 和 "loop"。第一分钟我可能需要多一点时间适应声音，我想这对每个人都正常。之后通常会变得容易。

**追问 1**
- Q: How do you train for accents?
- A: By listening to technical talks from different speakers, and slowing the speed when I need.
- 中文：怎样训练口音适应？——听不同讲者的技术演讲，需要时放慢速度。

**追问 2**
- Q: What if you still cannot follow?
- A: I tell the interviewer honestly, and ask them to write the key term in the chat if there is one.
- 中文：如果仍听不懂？——如实告诉面试官，并请他们在聊天框写下关键术语（如果有）。

**关键词与易错点**：key words、context、polite request、paraphrase。听不同口音的训练是计划，按你真实情况调整。

### Q26. What do you do if you do not know a word in English?

**中文题意**：你不知道某个英文词时怎么办？

**考察点**：绕说（circumlocution）的能力。

**English answer**：I describe the thing with simple words, and I do not stop. For example, if I forget "singularity", I say "a position where the robot arm loses one direction of motion". If I forget a general word, I use a simple word with a similar meaning, or I describe what it does. I may say "I can't think of the exact word, but it's something like ...", and the interviewer often helps me. Then I continue. It is better to keep the idea moving than to stop for a perfect word. Afterwards, I write the word down and learn it.

**中文翻译**：我用简单的词描述那个东西，不会停下。例如忘了 "singularity"，我说"机器人手臂失去一个运动方向的位置"。忘了普通词，我用意思相近的简单词，或描述它的作用。我也可能说："我想不起确切的词，但大概是……"面试官常会帮我。然后我继续。让思路继续前进，好过为了一个完美的词而停下。事后，我记下这个词并学会它。

**追问 1**
- Q: Give another example.
- A: If I forget "saturation", I say "the motor is asked to give more than its maximum".
- 中文：再举个例子。——如果忘了 "saturation"，我说"电机被要求输出超过它的最大值"。

**追问 2**
- Q: Do you keep a list?
- A: Yes. I keep a list of technical words with a short example for each.
- 中文：你有词汇清单吗？——有；我保留技术词汇表，每个词附一个短例子。

**关键词与易错点**：describe, don't stop、"something like ..."、learn afterwards。保持关键技术词（PID、LQR）的发音与拼写准确。

### Q27. What if you cannot remember the English term for a technical concept, such as "gain scheduling"?

**中文题意**：想不起某个技术概念的英文，比如"增益调度"，怎么办？

**考察点**：技术词汇的应急处理。

**English answer**：I explain the idea, and I give the term if I can. For "gain scheduling", I could say: "We change the controller gains depending on the leg length, because the robot behaves differently when the leg is longer." Then the interviewer says, "Oh, gain scheduling," and I confirm: "Yes, exactly, gain scheduling." If I know the Chinese term only, I don't say it. I explain the meaning. I also prepare a list of the 30 terms that appear in my CV, with an English one-line explanation for each, so I can recover quickly.

**中文翻译**：我解释这个想法，能说出术语就说。对 "gain scheduling"，我可以说："我们根据腿长改变控制器增益，因为腿更长时机器人的行为不同。"然后面试官说 "噢，增益调度"，我确认："对，就是增益调度。"如果我只知道中文术语，我不说中文，而是解释意思。我也准备了 CV 中出现的 30 个术语清单，每个附一行英文解释，以便快速恢复。

**追问 1**
- Q: Which terms are on your list?
- A: Cascaded PID, LQR, saturation, feedforward, gain scheduling, virtual model control, EKF, AMCL, MPC, inverse kinematics, and so on.
- 中文：清单上有哪些词？——级联 PID、LQR、饱和、前馈、增益调度、虚拟模型控制、EKF、AMCL、MPC、逆运动学等。

**追问 2**
- Q: How do you pronounce "AMCL"?
- A: Letter by letter: A-M-C-L. I say "adaptive Monte Carlo localisation" the first time.
- 中文：AMCL 怎么读？——逐字母读 A-M-C-L；第一次提到时说全称 "adaptive Monte Carlo localisation"。

**关键词与易错点**：explain the idea first、confirm the term、30-term list。读音见 [英文自我介绍_多版本 第 6 节](../01_面试总指南/02_英文自我介绍_多版本.md)。

### Q28. What do you do when the interviewer interrupts you while you are answering?

**中文题意**：回答时被面试官打断，你怎么办？

**考察点**：被打断时的礼貌和灵活。

**English answer**：I stop at once and let the interviewer speak: "Of course. Please go ahead." An interruption usually means they want something specific, or that I'm taking too long. I listen to the new question, and I answer it directly. If they want more detail on one point, I give it. If they say "Let's move on", I move on without feeling bad. I don't try to finish my old sentence if it is no longer needed. In a short interview, being flexible is a strength. I would rather answer what they want to know than what I prepared.

**中文翻译**：我立刻停下，让面试官说："当然，您请说。"打断通常意味着他们想要某个具体内容，或者我说得太久了。我听新的问题，并直接回答。如果他们想要某一点的更多细节，我就给。如果他们说"我们继续吧"，我毫无负担地继续。如果旧句子已不再需要，我不会硬要把它说完。在短时面试里，灵活是优点。我宁愿回答他们想知道的，而不是我准备的。

**追问 1**
- Q: What if you were in the middle of an important point?
- A: I can say, "May I finish this point? It's one sentence." Then I finish quickly.
- 中文：如果正说到重要的一点呢？——我可以说："我能说完这一点吗？只有一句。"然后快速说完。

**追问 2**
- Q: How do you stay calm?
- A: I remember that interruptions are normal in interviews, and not a sign that I did something wrong.
- 中文：怎么保持冷静？——我记住面试中被打断是正常的，不代表我做错了。

**关键词与易错点**：stop at once、answer the new question、be flexible。不要抢话。

### Q29. After you are interrupted, how do you get back to your point?

**中文题意**：被打断后，怎样回到你的要点？

**考察点**：恢复主线的能力。

**English answer**：I first answer the interruption fully. Then I use a short bridge to return to my point, if it is still useful: "Going back to the earlier point, ..." or "To finish what I started, ...". If the interviewer has moved on, I don't force it. I can include my earlier idea later, in another answer, when it fits. To make this easy, I always start my answers with the conclusion. Then even if I'm interrupted early, I have already said the most important thing. After that, details are a bonus.

**中文翻译**：我先完整回答打断的内容。然后如果我的要点仍然有用，就用一个简短的衔接回到它："回到之前的那点……"或"为了说完我刚才开始的……"如果面试官已经转到别处，我不强求。我可以以后在合适的另一个回答里再加入之前的想法。为了做到这一点，我总是先说结论。这样即使被早早打断，我也已经说了最重要的话，细节只是加分。

**追问 1**
- Q: Give an example of a bridge.
- A: "Going back to the safety checks, I would add one point ..."
- 中文：举个衔接语的例子。——"回到安全检查，我想补充一点……"

**追问 2**
- Q: What if you forget your earlier point?
- A: I say, "Sorry, I lost my thread. Could I come back to your question?" and I restate the question in my own words.
- 中文：忘了之前的要点呢？——我说："抱歉，我丢了思路，能回到您的问题吗？"并用自己的话重述问题。

**关键词与易错点**：answer first, bridge later、conclusion first、don't force。

### Q30. What if you freeze and cannot speak for a few seconds?

**中文题意**：突然卡住几秒说不出话怎么办？

**考察点**：卡壳时的恢复。

**English answer**：First, I breathe, and I accept a short silence. A pause of three seconds feels long to me but is normal to the listener. Then I use a sentence that I have prepared: "That's a good question. Let me think for a moment." If my mind is still blank, I give a simple structure, like "I'll answer in two steps", and I begin with the easiest part. Or I restate the question in my own words, which gives me time and often brings back the ideas. If I really can't answer, I say honestly what I know and what I don't. I never panic and talk without sense.

**中文翻译**：首先，我呼吸，接受短暂的沉默。三秒的停顿对我来说很长，但对听者是正常的。然后我用一句准备好的话："这是个好问题，让我想一下。"如果脑子仍然一片空白，我给出一个简单结构，比如"我分两步回答"，并从最容易的部分开始。或者用自己的话重述问题，这既争取时间，也常能唤回想法。如果真的答不出，我诚实说出我知道什么、不知道什么。我从不慌乱地胡说。

**追问 1**
- Q: What if the freeze happens in the self-introduction?
- A: I go to my first keyword and continue from there. I practise with keywords, not a full script.
- 中文：自我介绍时卡住呢？——我回到第一个关键词继续；我练习的是关键词，而不是完整稿子。

**追问 2**
- Q: How do you practise this?
- A: I ask a friend to interrupt me with random questions, so I get used to recovering.
- 中文：怎么练习？——请朋友用随机问题打断我，习惯恢复。

**关键词与易错点**：breathe、prepared sentence、simple structure、restate the question。不要说 "I'm so nervous" 然后停住。

### Q31. What do you do when you make a grammar mistake in the middle of a sentence?

**中文题意**：说到一半语法错了怎么办？

**考察点**：对错误的心态。

**English answer**：I usually continue. The interviewer understands the meaning, and a small mistake is not important. If the mistake changes the meaning, for example a wrong tense that makes the project sound unfinished, I correct it quickly: "Sorry, I mean, I built it last year." I don't stop to apologise for a long time. I know that I make some errors with articles and with tenses, so for the important facts, like Kaihong being present tense and the old projects in the past tense, I practise those sentences specifically. Clear content is more important than perfect grammar.

**中文翻译**：我通常继续说。面试官理解意思，小错误不重要。如果错误改变了意思，比如时态错误让项目听起来没完成，我会迅速更正："抱歉，我的意思是，我去年做了它。"我不会长时间停下道歉。我知道我在冠词和时态上会出一些错，所以对重要事实，比如深开鸿用现在时、旧项目用过去时，我专门练习那些句子。清晰的内容比完美的语法更重要。

**追问 1**
- Q: Which mistakes do you make most?
- A: Articles, like "a" and "the", and sometimes the third-person "s".
- 中文：你最常犯什么错？——冠词（a 和 the），有时是第三人称 s。

**追问 2**
- Q: How do you correct them?
- A: By recording myself and listening for the same mistakes, then practising the correct sentence.
- 中文：怎么纠正？——录音并听同样的错误，然后练习正确的句子。

**关键词与易错点**：continue、correct only if meaning changes、present tense for Kaihong。最重要的时态：Kaihong 用现在时（"I'm working on..."），已结束项目用过去时。

### Q32. What do you do if the video connection is bad during an online interview?

**中文题意**：线上面试时视频连接不好怎么办？

**考察点**：技术故障的应对。

**English answer**：Before the interview I test the connection, the camera and the microphone, and I have a backup, like my phone hotspot. If the connection breaks during the interview, I say calmly: "I'm sorry, I think the connection is unstable. Could you hear my last sentence?" If the sound is bad, I ask the interviewer to type the question in the chat if possible. I speak a little slower, and I keep my answers short, so less is lost if the signal drops. If I get disconnected, I reconnect at once and apologise briefly. I follow the instructions in the invitation email.

**中文翻译**：面试前我测试连接、摄像头和麦克风，并准备备用方案，比如手机热点。如果面试中连接断了，我平静地说："抱歉，我想连接不稳定，您能听到我的上一句话吗？"如果声音不好，我请面试官在聊天框里输入问题（如果可行）。我说得稍慢，答案保持简短，这样信号掉线时损失更少。如果断线，我立即重连并简短道歉。我遵守邀请邮件里的指示。

**追问 1**
- Q: What if it is a recorded AI interview?
- A: I follow the platform's rules. I test the equipment first and keep the environment quiet and well lit.
- 中文：如果是录制式 AI 面试呢？——我遵守平台规则，先测试设备，保持环境安静、光线好。

**追问 2**
- Q: Where do you sit?
- A: In a quiet room, with a plain background, and the camera at eye level.
- 中文：你坐在哪里？——安静的房间，背景简单，摄像头与眼睛同高。

**关键词与易错点**：test before、backup、short answers、follow the email。录制式面试的规则以邀请邮件和系统提示为准。

### Q33. What if the interviewer asks a yes/no question, but the answer needs some nuance?

**中文题意**：面试官问是非题，但答案需要细微差别，怎么办？

**考察点**：简短而诚实的表达。

**English answer**：I start with the short answer, and then add the nuance in one or two sentences. For example, "Did you use LQR on the robot?" I say, "As an analysis tool, yes. As the deployed controller, no. The deployed controller was a cascaded PID." Starting with "yes" or "no" shows that I listened and that I am direct. The nuance then protects the truth. I avoid long explanations before the answer, because the interviewer may stop listening. If I'm not sure, I say, "Partly. Let me explain."

**中文翻译**：我先给简短答案，再用一两句话补充细微差别。例如，"你在机器人上用过 LQR 吗？"我说："作为分析工具，是；作为部署的控制器，不是。部署的控制器是级联 PID。"先说 "yes" 或 "no" 表明我听清了且很直接。细微差别随后保护了真相。我避免在回答前做长篇解释，因为面试官可能不再听。如果不确定，我说："部分是。让我解释。"

**追问 1**
- Q: Did you use machine learning in your projects?
- A: Yes, in one project: I deployed a detection model and built a decision chain. I haven't trained a policy on a robot.
- 中文：你的项目用过机器学习吗？——是，在一个项目里：我部署了检测模型并搭了决策链；我没在机器人上训练过策略。

**追问 2**
- Q: Did your team win a prize?
- A: Yes. Our team won third prize at the national final, and I was responsible for the control loops.
- 中文：你们团队获奖了吗？——是，我们队在全国总决赛获三等奖，我负责控制回路。

**关键词与易错点**：answer first, nuance after、yes/no then one sentence、"partly"。这是红线问题（LQR、团队奖项）的标准答法。

### Q34. How do you buy time naturally while you are thinking?

**中文题意**：思考时怎样自然地争取时间？

**考察点**：填充语的使用。

**English answer**：I use a few natural phrases, not too many. "That's a good question." "Let me think about that for a moment." "Let me see." "I'd like to organise my thoughts." I can also restate the question: "So you are asking about ... " which gives me time and checks the question. I avoid long "um" and "uh" sounds, and I avoid repeating the same phrase every time. A calm pause is better than a long filler. I practise each phrase aloud, so that it comes without effort. In a video interview with a time limit, I keep these pauses very short.

**中文翻译**：我用几句自然的短语，不用太多。"这是个好问题。""让我想一想。""让我看看。""我想整理一下思路。"我也可以重述问题："所以您问的是……"这既争取时间，也核对问题。我避免拖长的 "um" 和 "uh"，也避免每次重复同一句。平静的停顿好于拖长的填充音。我出声练习每个短语，让它们毫不费力地说出。限时的视频面试里，我把停顿控制得非常短。

**追问 1**
- Q: How long can a pause be?
- A: About three seconds in a live interview. In a one-minute video answer, only one or two.
- 中文：停顿可以多久？——真人面试约三秒；一分钟的视频回答里只有一两秒。

**追问 2**
- Q: Which phrase do you like best?
- A: "That's a good question. Let me think for a moment." It is polite and short.
- 中文：你最喜欢哪句？——"这是个好问题，让我想一下。"礼貌又简短。

**关键词与易错点**：short phrases、restate the question、calm pause。不要连续用多个填充语。

### Q35. How do you show that your answer is finished?

**中文题意**：怎样表明你的回答已经结束？

**考察点**：收尾的技巧，防止面试官不知道你是否说完。

**English answer**：I end with a clear last sentence that repeats the conclusion, in a falling tone, and then I stop. For example, "So, in short, I used LQR to understand the gains, and the deployed controller was a cascaded PID." Sometimes I add "That's my answer. I'm happy to give more detail." I do not add random sentences after the ending, because that makes the answer weaker. In a video interview, I say "Thank you" and stop speaking, even if there is time left. A short, clear answer is better than a long one.

**中文翻译**：我用一句清楚的最后一句话复述结论，语调下降，然后停止。例如，"简而言之，我用 LQR 理解增益，部署的控制器是级联 PID。"有时我加一句："这就是我的回答，我乐意提供更多细节。"结尾后我不再加随意的句子，因为那会削弱回答。视频面试里，我说 "Thank you" 然后停止说话，即使还有时间。简短清楚的回答好过长的。

**追问 1**
- Q: What if there is silence after your answer?
- A: I wait calmly for two or three seconds. If nobody speaks, I say, "Would you like more detail on any part?"
- 中文：回答后沉默怎么办？——平静等两三秒；没人说话就问："您想了解哪部分的更多细节吗？"

**追问 2**
- Q: How do you avoid adding extra sentences?
- A: I plan the last sentence before I start, so I know when I'm done.
- 中文：怎样避免多说？——开始前就想好最后一句，知道何时结束。

**关键词与易错点**：repeat the conclusion、falling tone、stop。AI 视频面试：提前说完没关系，直接结束。

### Q36. What if the interviewer asks a very broad question, like "Tell me about robots"?

**中文题意**：面试官问非常宽泛的问题，如"讲讲机器人"，怎么办？

**考察点**：缩小范围、结构化。

**English answer**：I narrow the question first, politely. "That's a broad topic. Shall I talk about what robots are, or about my own experience with them?" If the interviewer says "your experience", I give a short answer: "I work on how robots balance, navigate and move safely." If the interviewer wants a general view, I give three ideas in order: what a robot is, what makes it hard, and what I find interesting. A structure with three points is easy for me to say and easy for the listener to follow. I keep the answer within one minute.

**中文翻译**：我先礼貌地缩小范围。"这是个很宽的话题。我是讲机器人是什么，还是讲我自己的经验？"如果面试官说"你的经验"，我给短答案："我研究机器人如何平衡、导航和安全地运动。"如果面试官想要一般的看法，我按顺序讲三点：机器人是什么、什么使它困难、我觉得什么有趣。三点结构对我容易说，对听者容易跟。我把回答控制在一分钟内。

**追问 1**
- Q: What is a robot, in your words?
- A: A machine that senses, decides and acts in the physical world.
- 中文：用你的话说，什么是机器人？——在物理世界里感知、决策和行动的机器。

**追问 2**
- Q: What makes robotics hard?
- A: The real world: noise, delay, limits and contact.
- 中文：机器人学难在哪里？——真实世界：噪声、延迟、限制和接触。

**关键词与易错点**：narrow the scope、three points、within a minute。不要试图回答"所有关于机器人的事"。

### Q37. What do you do if you disagree with what the interviewer says?

**中文题意**：不同意面试官的说法怎么办？

**考察点**：礼貌表达不同意见。

**English answer**：I disagree politely and with a reason. "That's an interesting point. My experience is a little different. In my project, ..." or "I see it a bit differently, because ..." I give an example or a simple fact, and I keep my tone calm. I also ask a question: "Do you mean in the general case, or in this system?" Often we agree after clarifying. If the interviewer still disagrees, I say, "I understand your view, and I'd like to think more about it." I don't argue. I want to show that I can discuss a difference in a professional way.

**中文翻译**：我礼貌地、带着理由表示不同意。"这是个有趣的观点。我的经验有点不同。在我的项目里……"或"我的看法略有不同，因为……"我给出例子或简单事实，并保持语气平静。我也会提问："您指的是一般情况，还是这个系统？"澄清后常常能取得一致。如果面试官仍不同意，我说："我理解您的观点，我想再多想想。"我不争论。我想表明我能以专业的方式讨论分歧。

**追问 1**
- Q: What if the interviewer is correct?
- A: I say, "You're right. Thank you. I'll correct my understanding."
- 中文：如果面试官是对的呢？——我说："您说得对，谢谢，我会更正我的理解。"

**追问 2**
- Q: What if you are not sure who is right?
- A: "I'm not sure. Could we check the case together?" I'd rather be honest.
- 中文：不确定谁对呢？——"我不确定，我们能一起看看这个情形吗？"我宁愿诚实。

**关键词与易错点**：polite, reason, example、clarifying question、don't argue。不要说 "You are wrong"。

### Q38. What if the interviewer is silent or shows no reaction to your answer?

**中文题意**：如果面试官沉默或没有反应怎么办？

**考察点**：应对无反馈的场景（尤其是 AI 视频面试）。

**English answer**：I don't take silence as a bad sign. Some interviewers listen without showing reactions, and in an AI video interview there is no human reaction at all. I finish my answer clearly, and wait for two or three seconds. In a live interview, I can ask, "Would you like me to give more detail, or shall I move on?" In a recorded interview, I simply continue to the next question. I keep my energy and eye contact with the camera the same. I think the best approach is to focus on communicating clearly, and not to read the other person's face too much.

**中文翻译**：我不把沉默当作坏信号。有些面试官听而不露反应，AI 视频面试则根本没有人的反应。我清楚地结束回答，并等两三秒。真人面试里，我可以问："您想让我给更多细节，还是继续下一题？"录制式面试里，我就继续下一题。我保持同样的精神和对镜头的目光。我认为最好的做法是专注于清晰沟通，不过分解读对方的表情。

**追问 1**
- Q: Where do you look in an AI video interview?
- A: At the camera, not at the screen, so it feels like eye contact.
- 中文：AI 视频面试看哪里？——看镜头而不是屏幕，让它像眼神接触。

**追问 2**
- Q: How do you stay motivated without feedback?
- A: I treat it as a chance to say my best answer clearly, one question at a time.
- 中文：没有反馈时怎样保持状态？——把它当作清楚说出最佳回答的机会，一题一题来。

**关键词与易错点**：silence is not bad、wait, then offer、look at the camera。AI 视频面试的检查清单见 [模拟面试脚本_AI视频面试](../04_模拟与冲刺/02_模拟面试脚本_AI视频面试.md)。

## 6. D 组：课程语言、课堂参与与英文表达

### Q39. How will you take part in discussions in English-taught classes?

**中文题意**：在英语授课的课堂里，你怎样参与讨论？

**考察点**：课堂参与计划的现实性。

**English answer**：I'll prepare before each class, because that makes it easier to speak. I'll read the slides, find the key terms, and write one or two questions or comments. In class, I'll set a small goal: speak at least once in each session, even a short clarifying question. I know that I'm slower in open discussion, so I'll use a simple structure: a point, a reason, an example. If I miss something, I'll ask for a repeat. After class, I'll talk with classmates and review notes. I believe that participation grows with practice, and the first weeks are the hardest.

**中文翻译**：我会在每节课前准备，因为这让开口更容易。我会读课件，找关键术语，写一两个问题或评论。课堂上，我设定一个小目标：每次课至少发言一次，哪怕只是一个澄清性的小问题。我知道自己在开放讨论中较慢，所以我会用简单结构：一个观点、一个理由、一个例子。如果漏听了，我会请对方重复。课后，我会与同学交流并复习笔记。我相信参与会随练习增长，头几周最难。

**追问 1**
- Q: What if you have a good idea but cannot find the words?
- A: I'll say it in simple words, or write a short sentence on paper and read it. Ideas are more important than perfect words.
- 中文：有好想法但找不到词怎么办？——用简单的词说，或把短句写在纸上念出来；想法比完美的词重要。

**追问 2**
- Q: How will you measure your progress?
- A: By how many times I spoke in class, and by how easily I follow the discussion.
- 中文：怎么衡量进步？——看我在课堂上发言的次数，以及跟上讨论的难易程度。

**关键词与易错点**：prepare before class、one question per session、structure: point, reason, example。目标是现实的"每次一次"，不要承诺"积极发言每一次"。

### Q40. How will you work in group projects with international classmates?

**中文题意**：怎样和国际同学一起做小组项目？

**考察点**：跨文化协作。

**English answer**：I'll work in a clear and honest way. At the start, I'll suggest that we write down roles, interfaces and dates, because written agreements help everyone, including people with different English levels. After each meeting, I'll send a short summary of decisions and next steps in writing, which avoids misunderstanding. I'll take a module where I'm strong, probably the one connecting software to hardware, and I'll ask for help where I'm weak. I'll be on time and keep my promises. I'll also listen carefully to different working styles. I think teams work better when assumptions are written down.

**中文翻译**：我会清楚、诚实地合作。一开始，我会建议我们写下角色、接口和日期，因为书面约定对每个人都有帮助，包括英语水平不同的人。每次会议后，我会发一份决定和下一步的简短书面总结，避免误解。我会承担我擅长的模块，大概是软件与硬件相连的那个，并在薄弱处求助。我会准时并信守承诺。我也会认真倾听不同的工作风格。我认为团队在假设被写下时更好地工作。

**追问 1**
- Q: What if a teammate speaks too fast for you?
- A: I'd say, "Could you slow down a little? I want to make sure I understand." Then I confirm in writing.
- 中文：如果队友说得太快呢？——我会说："能慢一点吗？我想确保我理解了。"然后书面确认。

**追问 2**
- Q: What if there is a conflict?
- A: I'd bring it to the facts and the plan, and speak with the person directly and kindly.
- 中文：有冲突怎么办？——回到事实和计划，并直接而友善地与对方沟通。

**关键词与易错点**：written roles and interfaces、summary after meetings、ask for help。体现已有实践：RoboMaster 中与视觉组定义接口。

### Q41. How do you prepare an English presentation?

**中文题意**：你怎样准备英文汇报？

**考察点**：汇报准备方法。

**English answer**：I start with the message: what do I want the audience to remember? Then I make three main points, each with one example or a figure. My slides have few words, and I put the details in my speaking. I write a short script for the opening and the closing, and for the main points I only use keywords, so I don't sound like I'm reading. I practise aloud with a timer, at least three times, and I record myself once. I also prepare for questions: I list five possible questions with short answers. On the day, I speak slowly and pause at the end of each point.

**中文翻译**：我从信息出发：我想让听众记住什么？然后做三个主要观点，每个配一个例子或一张图。幻灯片字很少，把细节放在讲述里。我为开头和结尾写简短的稿子，对主要观点只用关键词，这样不会听起来像在读。我带计时器出声练习至少三遍，并录音一次。我还准备问答：列出五个可能的问题和简短答案。当天我说得慢，并在每个要点结束时停顿。

**追问 1**
- Q: How do you handle nerves?
- A: By practising enough, starting with a strong first sentence, and breathing before I begin.
- 中文：怎样应对紧张？——充分练习，用有力的第一句话开始，开始前深呼吸。

**追问 2**
- Q: What do you do if you forget a point?
- A: I look at my keyword, or I move to the next point and return if there is time.
- 中文：忘了一点怎么办？——看关键词，或继续下一点，有时间再回来。

**关键词与易错点**：three points、few words on slides、practise with a timer、prepare questions。与 [技术口述方法](../01_面试总指南/08_技术口述方法_如何讲清一个项目.md) 一致。

### Q42. How is your academic and technical writing in English?

**中文题意**：你的英文学术和技术写作怎么样？

**考察点**：写作能力的诚实评估。

**English answer**：For technical writing, I'm fairly comfortable. I write READMEs and code comments in English, and I wrote my CV and my statements in English. My IELTS writing score was lower than my reading, 6.0, so I know that long academic essays are something to improve. My method is to write an outline first, with the claim in each paragraph, then fill in with short sentences. I check for tense and articles, which are my usual mistakes. I ask a colleague or a tutor to read important texts. In the programme, I'll use writing support if it is offered, and I'll learn from feedback on my first reports.

**中文翻译**：技术写作我比较自如。我用英文写 README 和代码注释，CV 和陈述也是英文写的。我的雅思写作分比阅读低，是 6.0，所以我知道长的学术论述是要改进的。我的方法是先写提纲，写下每段的主张，再用短句填充。我检查时态和冠词，这是我常犯的错。重要文本我请同事或导师读。读书期间，如果提供写作支持，我会使用，并从第一批报告的反馈中学习。

**追问 1**
- Q: Did you write your statements yourself?
- A: Yes. The ideas and the facts come from my own work, and I can discuss any sentence in them.
- 中文：陈述是你自己写的吗？——是；想法和事实来自我自己的工作，里面任何一句我都能讨论。

**追问 2**
- Q: What is your plan for the dissertation?
- A: To start the outline early, write a section each week, and get feedback from my supervisor.
- 中文：dissertation 的写作计划？——早些写提纲，每周写一节，并请导师给反馈。

**关键词与易错点**：technical writing comfortable、writing 6.0、outline first、feedback。"Did you write yourself" 题需要诚实：只说属实的内容；如果有人帮助润色，如实说明，并强调内容和事实来自你自己。

### Q43. How will you ask questions in class and talk to professors?

**中文题意**：你怎样在课堂提问并与教授交流？

**考察点**：主动求助的方式。

**English answer**：I'll use two ways. In class, I'll ask short clarifying questions like, "Could you explain that term again?" or "Do you mean the general case or this example?" After class, I'll go to office hours with prepared questions: what I tried, where I got stuck, and what I think the cause is. I've learned that precise questions get quick and useful answers. I'll also send a short email when needed, with a clear subject and my specific question. I think professors respect students who prepare, and it makes my English easier, because the topic is clear.

**中文翻译**：我会用两种方式。课堂上，我问简短的澄清问题，如："您能再解释一下那个术语吗？"或"您指的是一般情形还是这个例子？"课后，我带着准备好的问题去答疑时间：我试过什么、卡在哪里、我认为原因是什么。我知道精准的问题能得到快速有用的回答。需要时我也会发简短邮件，主题清楚、问题具体。我想教授尊重有准备的学生，而且这让我的英语更容易，因为话题明确。

**追问 1**
- Q: Show me how you would write that email.
- A: "Dear Professor, I have a question about the optimisation lecture. I tried X, and I got Y. Could you explain why? Thank you."
- 中文：演示这封邮件怎么写。——"尊敬的教授，我对最优化讲座有个问题。我试了 X，得到 Y。您能解释一下为什么吗？谢谢。"

**追问 2**
- Q: What if you are afraid to ask?
- A: I'd write the question first. A written question is easier to say.
- 中文：害怕提问怎么办？——先把问题写下来；写好的问题更容易说出口。

**关键词与易错点**：short clarifying questions、office hours、what I tried / where stuck / what I think。AIRS 导师评价你"提精准的问题"，这是同一个习惯。

### Q44. What will you do if a professor explains too fast or uses a style you cannot follow?

**中文题意**：教授讲得太快或风格你跟不上，怎么办？

**考察点**：应对课堂困难。

**English answer**：I'll keep a record of the points that I missed, and not interrupt every sentence. I'll use the slides to follow the structure, and I'll try to guess unknown words from context. At the end of the class or in the break, I'll ask a short question about the missing part. After class, I'll check my notes with a classmate and with the slides, and read the textbook or the notes on the topic. If the problem is the style or the accent, I'll give myself a few sessions to adapt, because it often gets easier. If still hard, I'll talk to the professor politely and ask for advice.

**中文翻译**：我会记录漏掉的要点，而不是每句话都打断。我用课件跟随结构，并尝试从上下文猜测生词。课堂结束或休息时，我就缺失的部分问一个简短的问题。课后，我与同学和课件核对笔记，并阅读教材或该主题的讲义。如果问题是风格或口音，我给自己几节课适应，因为常常会变容易。如果仍然困难，我会礼貌地与教授沟通并请教建议。

**追问 1**
- Q: Could you ask the professor to slow down?
- A: Yes, politely, "Sorry, could you slow down a little? This part is new for me."
- 中文：你能请教授放慢吗？——能，礼貌地说："抱歉，能稍微放慢一点吗？这一部分对我是新的。"

**追问 2**
- Q: Do you record lectures?
- A: Only if the university and the professor allow it. Otherwise, I take structured notes.
- 中文：你会录课吗？——只有学校和教授允许时；否则我做结构化笔记。

**关键词与易错点**：record missed points、slides、ask later、adapt in a few sessions。录音需要许可，不要默认录课。

### Q45. Are there any cultural differences in communication that you are thinking about?

**中文题意**：你有没有考虑过沟通方面的文化差异？

**考察点**：跨文化意识。

**English answer**：Yes, I've thought about it. In my experience, Chinese technical communication can be indirect and modest: people often avoid saying "this is wrong" and talk around the problem. In an international classroom, people may be more direct, and they expect students to give opinions and to disagree politely. So I'm practising to say clearly what I think, with a reason, and to use phrases like, "I see it a little differently, because ..." I also remember that directness is not rudeness, and that modesty is not weakness. I try to be clear and respectful at the same time.

**中文翻译**：是，我考虑过。据我的经验，中文的技术交流可能比较含蓄、谦逊：人们常避免说"这是错的"，而绕着问题说。在国际化课堂里，人们可能更直接，期望学生表达意见并礼貌地不同意。所以我在练习清楚说出我的想法并附上理由，并使用 "我的看法略有不同，因为……" 这样的句式。我也记住直接不是无礼，谦逊也不是弱。我努力同时做到清楚和尊重。

**追问 1**
- Q: Give an example of a direct but polite sentence.
- A: "I'm not sure that works here, because the motor torque would saturate."
- 中文：给一个直接但礼貌的句子。——"我不确定这里行得通，因为电机力矩会饱和。"

**追问 2**
- Q: Has this been a problem for you?
- A: Sometimes I say too little. I now try to give my opinion first, and then the reason.
- 中文：这对你是个问题吗？——有时我说得太少；现在我尽量先说意见，再说理由。

**关键词与易错点**：direct vs indirect、polite disagreement、respect。避免刻板印象式贬低任何文化；用 "in my experience"。

### Q46. Talk about Shenzhen for one minute.

**中文题意**：用一分钟谈谈深圳。

**考察点**：用英语谈熟悉的话题，检验流畅度（调研中的家乡类英语题，改成你确有的深圳经历）。

**English answer**：Shenzhen is the city where I have studied and worked for the past three years. It is a fast-changing city, and it is known for electronics and technology companies. For a robotics student, that is a great place, because it is easy to find hardware, parts and people who build things. All my internships were here: a robotics institute, a robotics company and a platform team. People come from all over China, so the city feels young and busy. Sometimes it is too busy, and I try to find quiet time to study. I like it, and I plan to work in this region after my studies.

**中文翻译**：深圳是我过去三年学习和工作的城市。它变化很快，以电子和科技公司闻名。对机器人专业的学生来说，这里很棒，因为容易找到硬件、零件和动手做东西的人。我所有的实习都在这里：一个机器人研究院、一家机器人公司和一个平台团队。人们来自全国各地，所以城市感觉年轻而忙碌。有时太忙，我会找安静的时间学习。我喜欢这里，也计划在学习之后在这个地区工作。

**追问 1**
- Q: What do you not like about it?
- A: It can be crowded and expensive, and the pace is fast.
- 中文：你不喜欢它什么？——有时拥挤且贵，节奏很快。

**追问 2**
- Q: Where are you from originally?
- A: I'm happy to tell you more about my background if you like, but Shenzhen is where I have built my study and work life.
- 中文：你原来是哪里人？——如果您想了解，我乐意多说我的背景；不过深圳是我建立学习和工作生活的城市。

**关键词与易错点**：three years、electronics and technology、young and busy。老家等个人背景材料中没有，不编；如被问到，如实回答你自己的情况。

### Q47. Describe your typical day in English.

**中文题意**：用英语描述你典型的一天。

**考察点**：日常话题的流畅度和时态。

**English answer**：On a normal weekday in a project phase, I start by looking at my list for the day and choosing the most important task. In the morning, I usually work on code or analysis, and in the afternoon, I test on the robot or in simulation, if I have access. In the evening, I review what I learned, write short notes about the results, and plan the next day. I also spend some time reading documentation in English and practising speaking about my projects. I try to sleep at a regular time, because tired people make mistakes with hardware.

**中文翻译**：项目阶段的普通工作日，我先看当天清单并选最重要的任务。上午我通常写代码或做分析，下午如果能用设备，就在机器人或仿真上测试。晚上我复习所学，写简短的结果笔记，并规划第二天。我也花一些时间读英文文档和练习讲项目。我尽量规律作息，因为疲劳的人在硬件上会出错。

**追问 1**
- Q: What is different on a day with classes?
- A: Classes and assignments come first. The robot work goes in the free time.
- 中文：有课的日子有什么不同？——课程和作业优先，机器人工作放在空闲时间。

**追问 2**
- Q: Which part of the day do you like best?
- A: The test on the robot, when an idea becomes a real behaviour.
- 中文：一天里最喜欢哪一部分？——在机器人上测试，想法变成真实行为的时候。

**关键词与易错点**：present simple for routines、first / then / in the evening。作息按你的真实情况改写；这里是项目阶段的典型安排。

### Q48. Describe something you learned recently.

**中文题意**：描述你最近学到的一件事。

**考察点**：学习的具体性和表达。

**English answer**：Recently, at work, I learned how much a robot depends on consistent state. When data comes from several sensors, each has its own timestamp, frame and units. If these are not standardised, the control code cannot be reused on another robot. So we put timestamps, coordinate frames and validity checks in one place, before the data reaches the controllers. I learned to ask three questions about every signal: when was it measured, in which frame, and is it still valid? It sounds simple, but it prevents many hidden errors. I already had this experience in navigation, and now I see it at a platform level.

**中文翻译**：最近在工作中，我学到机器人对一致状态的依赖有多大。数据来自多个传感器时，每个都有自己的时间戳、坐标系和单位。如果没有标准化，控制代码就无法在另一个机器人上复用。所以我们在数据到达控制器之前，在一个地方处理时间戳、坐标系和有效性检查。我学会对每个信号问三个问题：什么时候测的？在哪个坐标系？还有效吗？听起来简单，但能避免许多隐藏的错误。我在导航中已有这种经验，现在在平台层面再次看到。

**追问 1**
- Q: Can you give an example of an invalid signal?
- A: A reading that is old, outside its normal range, or frozen with the same value for several frames.
- 中文：给一个无效信号的例子。——过期的读数、超出正常范围的读数，或连续多帧值完全相同。

**追问 2**
- Q: Is this the same as a data-quality problem in AI?
- A: It is similar. Bad inputs give bad outputs, whether it is a controller or a model.
- 中文：这和 AI 里的数据质量问题一样吗？——类似；坏输入产生坏输出，不论是控制器还是模型。

**关键词与易错点**：timestamp / frame / validity、three questions、reuse across robots。内容来自 P6 CV bullet 2，不透露内部细节。

### Q49. Describe a technology that changed your life.

**中文题意**：描述一项改变你生活的技术。

**考察点**：用英语描述并给出个人联系。

**English answer**：For me, it is open-source software and online documentation. When I started robotics, I did not have a teacher for every tool, but I could read the documentation, run the examples and ask questions in public forums. That is how I learned ROS 2, how I understood the chip manuals, and how I tried tools like MuJoCo. It allowed me to learn at my own speed and to build real systems as a student. It also taught me to read English technical text. I'm thankful to the people who write and share these resources, and I hope to contribute to them one day.

**中文翻译**：对我来说，是开源软件和在线文档。刚开始做机器人时，我没有每个工具的老师，但我可以读文档、运行示例并在公开论坛提问。我就是这样学会 ROS 2、读懂芯片手册，并尝试 MuJoCo 之类的工具。它让我按自己的速度学习，并在学生时期就搭建真实系统。它也教会我读英文技术文本。我感谢写作和分享这些资源的人，也希望有一天能为它们做贡献。

**追问 1**
- Q: What would you contribute?
- A: A small fix or a documentation improvement, as a start.
- 中文：你会贡献什么？——从小的修复或文档改进开始。

**追问 2**
- Q: Is there a downside?
- A: Documentation can be outdated or unclear, so I test the examples and read the source.
- 中文：有缺点吗？——文档可能过时或不清楚，所以我测试示例并读源码。

**关键词与易错点**：open-source、documentation、learn at my own speed。"在公开论坛提问" 若你实际没有这样做，请改成 "read discussions"；保持诚实。

### Q50. Summarise a recent robotics news story.

**中文题意**：概述一则最近的机器人新闻。

**考察点**：概述能力；不编新闻。

**English answer**：I don't follow robotics news as closely as technical documentation, so I'll be honest and not invent a story. What I can show is how I summarise: in three sentences, who did what, why it matters, and what I think. If you give me a short article, I'll do it now. For a topic I know well, here is an example: "Teams are testing vision-language-action models, which take images and instructions and output robot actions. This matters because one model may handle many tasks. But I think safety and evaluation are open problems, so I would keep checks around the model." That is my method.

**中文翻译**：我关注机器人新闻不像关注技术文档那样紧，所以我会诚实，不编故事。我能展示的是我怎么概述：三句话，谁做了什么、为什么重要、我怎么看。如果您给我一篇短文章，我现在就做。对我熟悉的话题，这里有一个例子："团队在测试视觉-语言-动作模型，它们接收图像和指令并输出机器人动作。这很重要，因为一个模型可能处理许多任务。但我认为安全和评估仍是开放问题，所以我会在模型周围保留检查。"这是我的方法。

**追问 1**
- Q: Which source do you read?
- A: Mostly technical documentation and project pages. I plan to follow a few robotics news sources regularly.
- 中文：你读哪些来源？——主要是技术文档和项目页；我计划定期关注几个机器人新闻来源。

**追问 2**
- Q: What is your opinion on the example you gave?
- A: It is promising, but I would want to see fair evaluations on real robots.
- 中文：你对这个例子的看法？——有前景，但我想看到真实机器人上的公平评估。

**关键词与易错点**：don't invent、three-sentence summary、who / why / my view。例子是一般性的趋势描述，不是某条具体新闻；面试前如果你读到了一条真实的、可复述的报道，可以替换它。

### Q51. What are the advantages and disadvantages of social media?

**中文题意**：社交媒体的优缺点是什么？

**考察点**：开放话题的结构化表达（调研英语题之一）。

**English answer**：There are advantages and disadvantages, and I'll give two of each. The advantages are connection and information. People can share knowledge quickly, and learners can find communities, tutorials and answers. The disadvantages are attention and quality. It is easy to lose time, and not all information is correct, so we need to check sources. For me, the best use is to follow technical communities and to learn, with limits on time. I think it is a tool, and its value depends on how we use it.

**中文翻译**：有优点也有缺点，我各说两点。优点是连接和信息：人们可以快速分享知识，学习者能找到社区、教程和答案。缺点是注意力和质量：容易浪费时间，信息也不都正确，所以需要核对来源。对我来说最好的用法是关注技术社区并学习，同时限制时间。我认为它是工具，价值取决于我们怎么用。

**追问 1**
- Q: Should there be rules for it?
- A: I think the user should have basic habits, like checking sources, and platforms should be clearer about how they work.
- 中文：应该有规则吗？——我认为用户应有基本习惯（如核对来源），平台应更清楚地说明运作方式。

**追问 2**
- Q: Do you use it much?
- A: Not heavily. I prefer documentation and focused communities.
- 中文：你用得多吗？——不多；我更喜欢文档和专注的社区。

**关键词与易错点**：two advantages, two disadvantages、tool、depends on use。第二追问是你的使用习惯，按真实情况改。

### Q52. Describe a place you like in Shenzhen.

**中文题意**：描述一个你在深圳喜欢的地方。

**考察点**：描述性英语，用时态和形容词。

**English answer**：The place I like most is the lab where I did my navigation and wheel-legged robot projects. It is not a famous place, but it matters a lot to me. It has a large open floor for testing, tables with tools and boards, and a few robots in different states of repair. When the robots work, it is exciting. When they don't, it is a good place to think. It is a place where I learned to find causes, to be patient and to work with others. If you want a public place, I also like walking in a park near campus to rest between long work sessions.

**中文翻译**：我最喜欢的地方是我做导航和轮腿机器人项目的实验室。它不是著名的地方，但对我很重要。那里有供测试的开阔地面、放着工具和板子的桌子，以及几个处于不同维修状态的机器人。机器人工作时令人兴奋；不工作时，这里是个适合思考的地方。我在这里学会了找原因、耐心和与人合作。如果您想要一个公共场所，我也喜欢在长时间工作之间到校园附近的公园散步休息。

**追问 1**
- Q: What do you remember most about the lab?
- A: The moment the car drove its first full route by itself.
- 中文：你对实验室记忆最深的是什么？——车第一次自己跑完整条路线的时刻。

**追问 2**
- Q: Why not a famous place?
- A: Because I'd rather speak about a place where I really spent my time.
- 中文：为什么不说名胜？——因为我宁愿讲一个我真正度过时间的地方。

**关键词与易错点**：lab, open floor, tools, robots、real place with real memory。实验室的细节（开阔地面、桌子）是常见的场景描述，若与你的实验室不同请改；"公园散步" 一句若不属实请删除。

## 7. 自测与练习安排

### 7.1 自测清单

| 检查项 | 通过标准 |
|---|---|
| 应急句型 | 第 2 节 20 句不看稿能说出，语调自然 |
| 英语自评 | Q1、Q8 各 40 秒说完，事实一致：6.5、阅读最强、口语最弱 |
| 向非专业人士解释 | Q9–Q13 五个项目，各 45 秒，用类比，不出现公式 |
| 听不懂 | 请朋友故意说得快、用不同口音，连续练 5 次"请重复/复述确认" |
| 被打断 | 请朋友在你回答到一半时打断，练习"接上 / 放下" |
| 课堂英语 | Q39–Q45 每题 40 秒，能给出具体做法 |
| 红线 | 不假装听懂；不编新闻；不编家乡；不说 "my English is perfect" |

### 7.2 一周练习安排

| 天 | 内容 | 时间 |
|---|---|---|
| 第 1 天 | 第 2 节 20 句：每句读 5 遍，录音，选 5 句最难的重练 | 40 分钟 |
| 第 2 天 | A 组 Q1–Q8：自评与证据 | 45 分钟 |
| 第 3 天 | B 组 Q9–Q14：类比讲项目，朗读加计时 | 60 分钟 |
| 第 4 天 | B 组 Q15–Q20，加上"技术版一句话" | 60 分钟 |
| 第 5 天 | C 组 Q21–Q30：和朋友模拟听不懂、被打断 | 60 分钟 |
| 第 6 天 | C 组 Q31–Q38 与 D 组 Q39–Q45 | 60 分钟 |
| 第 7 天 | D 组 Q46–Q52 的一分钟话题；综合模拟 | 60 分钟 |

### 7.3 常见错误回顾

1. 听不懂时沉默或假装听懂，结果答非所问。
2. 一个句子太长，说到一半语法乱了。
3. 向非专业人士解释时仍用缩写和公式。
4. 被打断后试图把准备好的内容全部说完。
5. 把中文术语直接说出来，而不解释英文意思。
6. 编造新闻、家乡、爱好来回答话题题。
