# 分类题库 06：行为面试与团队合作（86 题，STAR 回答，每题标明项目故事）

> **一句话定位**：行为面试问的是"你以前怎么做"。本文件把团队合作、冲突与分歧、沟通、领导与主动性、接受反馈、失败与挫折、压力与时间管理、学习新东西、成就与遗憾、诚信与安全红线共 10 类问题做成 86 道可直接口述的英文题，每题都用 STAR（情境、任务、行动、结果）加一句反思回答，故事全部取自 CV 的七个项目和各项目"20 工程化实践"里的"团队协作与冲突""工程问题"章节，并标明用的是哪个项目的故事。

**本章学完你能……**

- 在 60–90 秒内用 STAR 讲完一个有细节、有反思的故事，而且 "I" 与 "we" 分得清；
- 对 86 个问法各知道该调用哪个项目、哪个故事，避免一个故事答所有题；
- 对每个故事的第二层、第三层追问有备好的短答；
- 在没有真实事例的题目上，诚实地改用"方法型"回答，而不是编故事；
- 避开几类会扣分的话：夸大领导角色、把团队成绩说成个人、把设计取值说成实测、把读码发现说成自己的贡献。

**前置知识**：[CV 事实基线](../../06_维护说明/CV事实基线.md)（唯一权威）；[行为面试 STAR 与常见问题](../01_面试总指南/04_行为面试_STAR与常见问题.md)（STAR 骨架与 30 题）；[弱项与敏感问题应对](../01_面试总指南/05_弱项与敏感问题应对.md)；[港硕面试真题调研](../00_港硕面试真题调研.md)（本文件的题目来源之一，其中第 2.8–2.11 节与第 4 节是行为与工程类题单）。

## 1. 本文件怎么用

1. **先背故事，不背题**。第 2 节的故事库列出 30 个故事的"一句话骨架"。面试时先判断题目问的是哪一类，再从对应故事里取材。每个故事在整份文件里会被换角度使用，但**同一场面试里同一个故事最多用两次**，第二次必须换角度。
2. **每个答案都是口语稿**，90–150 词，一口气约 60–70 秒。句子短，动词具体。练习时把英文遮住，只看中文题意，自己讲一遍，再对照。
3. **数字的口径**。凡是带数字的地方，都与各项目 README 的"实机细节自查表"和"20 工程化实践"一致，是项目的统一设计取值。说的时候用 about、roughly、in our setup，不说"测得精确值"。被追问精确值时用诚实模板：*"I don't remember the exact number, but it was roughly …"*，再转回你有把握的定性结论。
4. **团队成绩说团队**。RoboMaster 全国赛三等奖、南部赛区二等奖、3V3 二等奖都是团队成绩，用 "our team"；3V3 不要说成英雄机器人的成绩。
5. **读码发现不是你的贡献**。P1、P2 里有些问题是读源码时发现的隐患（例如 P2 底盘固件的心跳标志位、P1 采集脚本的参数名），回答里一律说 "I noticed"，不说 "I fixed"；你实际做的是上位机侧兜底。
6. **P1 的 LQR 是分析工具，没有上机部署**；**P3 控制任务周期约 3 ms，不说 1 kHz**；**P5 用 ROS2 Humble**；**P7 用经典 CAN（不是 CAN FD）**；**P6 的数字都是设计取值，不是公司内部实测**。
7. **英文题目分层**：每题有一个主答案（L1）和两个追问（L2、L3）。主答案背熟，追问只需要抓住核心句。
8. **没有真实事例时**，题目后会标"方法型"。方法型回答先说 "I don't have a dramatic example, but here is how I handle it"，再讲原则、步骤，最后给一个真实小例子。

## 3. A 团队合作

### Q1. Tell me about a time you worked in a team.
**中文题意**：讲一次你在团队里工作的经历。
**考察点**：你在团队中的位置、接口意识、结果与反思。
**使用项目**：P3 RoboMaster 英雄机器人（S4、S7）。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> The clearest example is our RoboMaster team. We built a hero robot with a gimbal, a launcher and a chassis, and I was responsible for those three control loops on the STM32F407. The loops depend on each other and on the vision and mechanical teammates, so early on I wrote a one-page interface note with the vision group: target error angles and timestamps at about 100 hertz, and my attitude and bullet speed going back. We also kept a shared header for the board-to-board CAN frames. That removed most integration rework. Our team reached the national final and won third prize. What I learned is that in a team, a clear interface matters more than a clever controller.

**中文翻译**：最清楚的例子是我们的 RoboMaster 战队。我们做了一台带云台、发射机构和底盘的英雄机器人，我负责 STM32F407 上这三类控制环。这些环互相依赖，也依赖做视觉和做机械的队友，所以我很早就和视觉组写了一页接口说明：目标误差角和时间戳约 100 Hz 送给我，我把姿态和弹速回传；板间 CAN 帧也放在共享头文件里。这去掉了大部分集成返工。我们队进入全国总决赛并获得三等奖。我学到的是：在团队里，清晰的接口比聪明的控制器更重要。
**追问 1**：*What exactly was in the interface note?* Target error angle, timestamp, a validity bit and a fire command from vision; my current attitude, quaternion, bullet speed and mode back. If the target was lost for more than 100 milliseconds, I fell back to remote control.
　中文：视觉给目标误差角、时间戳、有效位和开火指令；我回传姿态、四元数、弹速和模式；目标丢失超过 100 ms 就退回遥控。
**追问 2**：*What was your personal contribution versus the team's?* The three control loops and the interface to vision were mine; mechanics, vision and strategy were my teammates'. The prize is the team's.
　中文：三类控制环和与视觉的接口是我的；机械、视觉、策略是队友的；奖项是团队的。
**关键词与易错点**：interface note、timestamp、validity bit、board-to-board CAN。不要说 "I won third prize"；不要把 3V3 二等奖说成这台英雄机器人的成绩。

### Q2. What role do you usually play in a team?
**中文题意**：你在团队里通常扮演什么角色？
**考察点**：自我认知，是否真实，是否有团队意识。
**使用项目**：P3、P7、P6（综合）。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> I am usually the person who connects an algorithm to the hardware. In the RoboMaster team I owned the gimbal, launcher and chassis loops. In the wheel-legged project I owned the balance controller and the on-robot tuning. In both cases my job was to turn a control idea into something that runs on the real machine: reading sensors, producing torques, and tuning. I explain my part clearly to teammates in other areas, and I raise integration problems early, for example sign conventions and update rates. I am not always the one who proposes the big idea, but I am reliable at making an idea work on hardware and at documenting how.

**中文翻译**：我通常是把算法和硬件连起来的人。在 RoboMaster 战队里我负责云台、发射和底盘三类环；在轮腿项目里我负责平衡控制和实机调参。两种情况下我的工作都是把一个控制想法变成能在真机上运行的东西：读传感器、输出力矩、调参。我会向其他方向的队友讲清楚我的部分，也会尽早提出集成问题，比如符号约定和更新频率。我不总是提出宏大想法的人，但我可靠地把想法在硬件上做出来并写清楚做法。
**追问 1**：*Give a concrete integration issue you raised early.* In the wheel-legged project I asked for a written table of motor directions and zero positions before any torque was applied. We found one reversed sign on the bench instead of on the floor.
　中文：轮腿项目里我在加力矩前就要求写出电机方向和零位表，结果在台架上就发现了一个符号反了，而不是在地上摔机。
**追问 2**：*Would you like to lead a team?* I would like to grow into it, but today my honest strength is ownership of a technical part.
　中文：我愿意逐步成长，但目前诚实的强项是对一个技术模块的负责。
**关键词与易错点**：ownership、integration、sign convention。不要自称 team lead。

### Q3. Describe your role in your RoboMaster team.
**中文题意**：描述你在 RoboMaster 战队中的角色。
**考察点**：个人贡献与团队的边界。
**使用项目**：P3（S3、S7）。
**English answer（口语，约 120 词）**：
> I was one of the embedded control members for the hero robot, and I was responsible for three loops: the gimbal pitch and yaw cascaded PID using the IMU, the launcher with friction-wheel speed loops and a feeder position and velocity loop, and the chassis with Mecanum wheel allocation and power scaling. Mechanical, vision and strategy members were my teammates' areas. I defined with vision the target interface, and I wrote the safety behaviour: offline detection and a relax state. Before the match I also moved the same control code and tuning procedure to the 3V3 robot. The 3V3 second prize is a team result.

**中文翻译**：我是英雄机器人的电控成员之一，负责三类环：用 IMU 的云台 pitch/yaw 串级 PID；摩擦轮速度环加拨盘位置速度环的发射机构；麦克纳姆轮分配与功率缩放的底盘。机械、视觉、策略是队友的领域。我和视觉定义了目标接口，也写了安全行为：离线检测与 RELAX 状态。赛前我还把同一套控制代码和调参流程移植到 3V3 参赛车上。3V3 二等奖是团队成绩。
**追问 1**：*How did you keep the gimbal board and the chassis board consistent?* The board-to-board CAN frames were defined once in a shared header, little-endian, with a version byte, so the two boards could not disagree about a field.
　中文：板间 CAN 帧只在一个共享头文件里定义，小端、带版本字节，两块板不会对字段理解不一致。
**追问 2**：*What was the hardest part of the role?* Keeping three loops tuned at one fixed control period, about 3 milliseconds, because changing the period would change every gain.
　中文：在一个固定控制周期（约 3 ms）下保持三类环都调好，因为改周期等于换一套增益。
**关键词与易错点**：cascaded PID、power scaling、relax state。控制周期说 about 3 ms，不说 1 kHz。

### Q4. How do you divide work in a team project?
**中文题意**：团队项目里你怎么分工？
**考察点**：计划与沟通能力。
**使用项目**：P3、P1（S9、S17）。
**English answer（口语，约 120 词）**：
> I divide by interface rather than by task size. First we list the signals that cross between people: for example, which topic, which unit, which rate. Then each person owns what is behind an interface and is responsible for it being correct. In the ballbot project I owned the balance control and the safety loop, the arm colleague owned the arm drivers and trajectories, and we agreed topic names, message types, rates and zero positions first. In my Future Power internship, a parameter used to live in three places, so we gave it a single owner and everyone else changed it through a request. Ownership plus a written interface avoids both overlap and gaps.

**中文翻译**：我按接口分工而不是按任务大小分。先列出跨人的信号：哪个话题、什么单位、什么频率。然后每个人拥有接口后面的部分并负责其正确。球形机器人项目里，我负责平衡控制和安全回路，臂同事负责臂驱动和轨迹，先约定话题名、消息类型、频率和零位。在未来动力实习中，同一个参数曾在三处定义，我们就给它指定唯一所有者，其他人通过请求修改。责任归属加书面接口，既避免重叠也避免遗漏。
**追问 1**：*What if the interface needs to change?* The owner writes the change first, with old and new fields and a date, and tells everyone who uses it before editing code.
　中文：接口所有者先写变更说明（新旧字段、日期），通知使用者之后再改代码。
**追问 2**：*Who decides when two people both think a part is theirs?* We ask the supervisor or lead for a quick decision and write it down; I would not argue about it.
　中文：请导师或组长快速裁定并写下来，不争论。
**关键词与易错点**：single owner、interface、request。

### Q5. Tell me about a time you helped a teammate.
**中文题意**：讲一次你帮助队友的经历。
**考察点**：协作与知识共享。
**使用项目**：P3（S4）。
**English answer（口语，约 120 词）**：
> A vision teammate told me the target angle he computed from my gimbal data jumped at some headings. I checked my side. Yaw wraps around at plus and minus 180 degrees, so averaging two readings across the wrap, say 179 and minus 179, gives about zero, which is wrong. I explained this to him on a whiteboard, and we agreed to filter angles on the unit circle: low-pass the sine and cosine separately, then take the arctangent, and fold errors into the range of plus and minus 180. The jumps disappeared. It also saved debugging time before the competition. I now check angle wrap whenever I see sudden jumps.

**中文翻译**：一位做视觉的队友说，他用我的云台数据算出的目标角在某些朝向会跳。我检查自己这边：yaw 在正负 180° 回绕，跨过回绕点平均两个读数（比如 179 和 −179）会得到约 0，这是错的。我在白板上给他讲清楚，我们约定在单位圆上滤波：分别低通 sin 和 cos，再取 atan2，并把误差折回 ±180°。跳变消失了，也在赛前省了调试时间。现在一看到突然跳变，我就检查角度回绕。
**追问 1**：*How did you confirm the fix?* A test where the target went around a circle at 30 degrees per second for three turns, with the error staying continuous and within 2 degrees.
　中文：目标以 30°/s 绕圈三圈，误差全程连续且不超过 2°。
**追问 2**：*Did it cost you anything?* About an afternoon, and it removed a recurring surprise for both of us.
　中文：大约一个下午，却消除了双方反复遇到的问题。
**关键词与易错点**：angle wrap、unit circle、atan2。

### Q6. How do you integrate code written by different people?
**中文题意**：你怎么集成不同人写的代码？
**考察点**：接口、测试、版本意识。
**使用项目**：P6、P5（S25、S19）。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> I integrate in small steps and I make the interfaces testable. At Kaihong, an interface change starts with a written note: old and new fields, defaults and a migration path. Code goes in through pull requests, and every adapter must pass the same contract tests, so someone else's module either fits or fails loudly. During the integration bench I replace one neighbour at a time with a simulator, so I know which layer is wrong. On the navigation vehicle, a lower-controller frame format once changed without notice, so we added a protocol version byte and the bridge refuses to run on a mismatch. A visible failure is better than silent wrong data.

**中文翻译**：我分小步集成，并让接口可测试。在深开鸿，接口变更先写说明：新旧字段、默认值、迁移路径。代码通过 PR 合入，每个适配器必须通过同一套契约测试，别人的模块要么匹配要么明显失败。联调时每次只把一个邻居换成真实的，其余用模拟器，这样知道哪一层错了。导航车上下位机曾改了帧格式没通知，于是我们加了协议版本字节，不匹配时桥接节点拒绝运行。明显的失败好过静默的错误数据。
**追问 1**：*What is a contract test?* The same test cases run against every implementation of an interface, for example forward, reverse and in-place-left-turn for every base type.

### Q11. Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate.
**中文题意**：讲一次你和队友意见不一致的经历。
**考察点**：用数据解决分歧。
**使用项目**：P3（S1）。
**English answer（口语，约 130 词）**：
> A mechanical teammate and I disagreed about why the muzzle velocity dropped during continuous fire. He thought the friction wheels were worn and wanted to replace them. We logged the wheel speed for each shot with the old wheels and with a new pair. Both showed the same dip of several hundred rpm and the same recovery of over a hundred milliseconds, so the wheels were not the cause. We kept the wheels and I changed the controller, from PID towards variable-gain PID and then sliding mode. Data settles disagreements better than opinions.

**中文翻译**：一位做机械的队友和我对连发时弹速下降的原因有分歧。他认为摩擦轮磨损，想换掉；我认为是速度环的问题，因为每发后转速下降而且恢复慢。我们没有争论，而是分别用旧轮和新轮记录每一发的轮速。两者都出现同样几百 rpm 的下跌和一百多毫秒的恢复，所以原因不在轮子。我们保留了轮子，我去改控制器，从 PID 迭代到变增益 PID 再到滑模。数据比观点更能解决分歧。
**追问 1**：*What if the data had shown the wheels were worn?* Then I would have agreed immediately and replaced them; I only wanted the test to be fair.
　中文：那我会立刻同意换轮；我只是想让测试公平。
**追问 2**：*Did the relationship change afterwards?* He started sending me logs when he had suspicions, and we agreed to test before replacing parts.
　中文：他之后有怀疑就发日志给我，我们约定先测试再换件。
**关键词与易错点**：muzzle velocity、per-shot log、controller。不要贬低队友。

### Q12. Describe a disagreement between two groups, for example vision and control, and how you helped resolve it.
**中文题意**：描述两个组之间（如视觉与电控）的分歧及你怎么帮助解决。
**考察点**：中立、可检验的方案。
**使用项目**：P3（S2）。
**English answer（口语，约 130 词）**：
> Vision thought the control side should extrapolate the target forward for the 30 to 50 milliseconds of vision delay. I thought vision, which has the timestamps, should do the compensation. Instead of debating, we ran the same constant-velocity target with both options and measured the steady lag. Vision-side compensation using timestamps and my returned attitude gave a smaller lag, and control-side extrapolation amplified noise. So we wrote it into the interface document: vision compensates; control only low-passes, limits the rate, and adds a velocity feedforward of 0.6. With that, the steady lag on a 20 degrees per second target dropped from about 0.7 to 0.3 degrees. Turning an opinion into a test made the decision easy.

**中文翻译**：视觉认为电控应对 30–50 ms 的视觉延迟向前外推目标；我认为有时间戳的视觉侧应做补偿。我们没有辩论，而是用同一个匀速靶对两种方案各测一次稳态滞后。视觉侧用时间戳加我回传的姿态补偿滞后更小，电控外推会放大噪声。于是我们写进接口文档：视觉补偿；电控只低通、限速并加 0.6 的速度前馈。这样 20°/s 目标的稳态滞后从约 0.7° 降到约 0.3°。把观点变成测试，决定就容易了。
**追问 1**：*Why 0.6 and not 1.0?* Full velocity feedforward would amplify the vision velocity noise, so I kept it below one.
　中文：全量速度前馈会放大视觉速度噪声，所以取小于 1。
**追问 2**：*Where did the 0.7 and 0.3 come from?* Teaching-style estimates consistent with our design: 20 divided by Kp of 28, and with 0.6 feedforward multiplied by 0.4. I say about, not measured to decimals.
　中文：与设计一致的估算：20/28 和乘以 0.4；我说"约"，不说测到小数。
**关键词与易错点**：delay compensation、timestamp、feedforward。0.7→0.3 是设计推算，说 about。

**关键词与易错点**：ownership、private conversation。这是方法型，不要编造具体"队友偷懒"的故事。

### Q18. What do you do when team members blame each other, for example hardware versus algorithm?
**中文题意**：队友互相归咎（比如怪硬件还是怪算法）时你怎么做？
**考察点**：事实优先，降温。
**使用项目**：P7（S27）。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> On the wheel-legged robot the body wobbled. A mechanical teammate thought it was my algorithm. I suspected loose assembly. We could have argued for a long time. I suggested a bench test first: push the body by hand to feel for backlash, then hang the robot and measure motor response. Whatever we found, we would then decide. The test showed a loose mounting that explained part of the wobble, and I found a sign problem on my side that explained another part. From then on, every claim that it was hardware had to come with a testable check. I like turning blame into a test, because it is faster and keeps the team friendly.

**中文翻译**：轮腿机器人机身晃动。一位机械队友认为是我的算法有问题，我怀疑是装配松动。我们本可以争很久。我提议先做台架测试：用手推机身感受齿隙，再吊起机器人测电机响应。无论发现什么，再据此决定。测试发现一处安装松动解释了一部分晃动，同时我在自己这边找到一个符号问题解释了另一部分。此后"怀疑硬件"必须附带一条可检验的测试。我喜欢把指责变成测试，因为更快，也能保持团队友好。
**追问 1**：*How did you check the sign problem?* A direction checklist: small torque on each motor and confirm the positive direction matches the convention.
　中文：方向检查表：对每个电机加小力矩，确认正方向与约定一致。
**追问 2**：*What did you learn?* Both sides can be partly right; separate the causes with tests before talking about fault.
　中文：双方都可能部分正确；先用测试分开原因，再谈责任。
**关键词与易错点**：bench test、backlash、direction checklist。

### Q19. What do you do when you disagree with a decision that has already been made?
**中文题意**：你对已做出的决定有异议时怎么办？
**考察点**：服从与坦诚的平衡，安全议题例外。
**使用项目**：P2（S14）。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> It depends on whether it is about safety. For preferences I follow the decision and do my best. For safety I speak up with evidence. In my Future Power internship the base firmware owner thought the host heartbeat rule was enough. While reading the code, I noticed its flag is never updated, so it would not trigger. I did not argue. I wrote a short reproduction: disconnect the host and watch the counter. He saw it and agreed to fix it later. In the meantime, I made sure the host sends zero speed if the velocity command is silent for 300 milliseconds. I protect my own node, report the risk, and the owner decides.

**中文翻译**：这要看是否涉及安全。偏好问题我服从决定并尽力做好；安全问题我用证据说话。在未来动力实习中，底盘固件负责人认为上位机心跳规则够用。读码时我注意到它的标志位从不更新，所以不会触发。我没有争论，而是写了简短复现：断开上位机，看计数器。他看到后同意稍后修复。期间我确保上位机在速度命令静默 300 ms 时发送零速。我保护好自己的节点、报告风险，由负责人决定。
**追问 1**：*Did you fix the firmware?* No. It was reading-code finding and not my code; I reported it and added a host-side protection.
　中文：没有。那是读码发现、不是我的代码；我汇报了并在上位机侧加了保护。
**追问 2**：*What if the owner had refused?* I would still protect my own node and document the risk for them to decide.
　中文：我仍会保护我的节点并记录风险，由他决定。
**关键词与易错点**：I noticed、reproduction、host-side timeout。不说 "I fixed the firmware"。

### Q20. Describe a time you changed your mind after a discussion.
**中文题意**：讲一次讨论后你改变了想法的经历。
**考察点**：开放、基于证据。
**使用项目**：P3（S2）。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> At first I believed the control side should extrapolate the vision target to hide the vision delay, because we owned the gimbal and could do it quickly. The vision teammate argued that he had the timestamps and the attitude I sent back. We agreed to test both on the same constant-velocity target. His method gave a smaller steady lag, while my extrapolation amplified measurement noise. I changed my mind, and we wrote into the interface document that vision compensates, and control only low-passes and limits rate. I think it is important to say clearly, "your approach was better", and to move on. It also made our later integration smoother.

**中文翻译**：起初我认为电控应外推视觉目标来掩盖视觉延迟，因为云台归我们、能很快做。视觉队友认为他有时间戳和我回传的姿态。我们同意用同一个匀速靶测两种方案。他的方法稳态滞后更小，而我的外推放大了测量噪声。我改变了想法，并写进接口文档：视觉补偿，电控只低通、限速。我觉得清楚地说"你的方案更好"然后继续向前很重要。这也让之后的集成更顺畅。
**追问 1**：*Was it hard to admit?* A little, but the test made it easy; there was no argument left.
　中文：有点，但测试让它变得容易；已经没有可争论的了。
**追问 2**：*What did you keep from your idea?* The low-pass and rate limit on the control side, which protected the gimbal from noisy target jumps.
　中文：电控侧的低通和限速，保护云台不受带噪目标跳变影响。

### Q21. How do you handle conflicting priorities between teams?
**中文题意**：不同团队的优先级冲突怎么办？
**考察点**：优先级、升级、沟通。
**使用项目**：P5、P7（S21）。
**English answer（口语，约 120 词）**：
> I look for the shared constraint, then make priorities visible. On the navigation project the lab had only one vehicle, and localisation, planning and control each wanted time. We agreed on a booking table. Before each slot, the person wrote what to verify, what version and how long. Each slot had one theme. Safety-related tests had priority. In the last ten minutes we copied the bag, committed parameters and cleaned up. When priorities truly conflict, I bring the facts to my supervisor and ask for a decision. What helps most is that nobody has to guess what the others need.

**中文翻译**：我先找共同约束，再让优先级可见。导航项目里实验室只有一辆车，定位、规划和控制都要用。我们做了预约表。每个时间窗前，使用者写明要验证什么、什么版本、多长时间。每个窗口只有一个主题，安全相关测试优先。最后十分钟拷贝 bag、提交参数并收拾。优先级真正冲突时，我带着事实去找导师请他定夺。最有帮助的是没人需要猜别人要什么。
**追问 1**：*Why one theme per slot?* Several changes at once make failures impossible to attribute.
　中文：同时改多个东西，失败无法归因。
**追问 2**：*Did anybody complain?* Briefly, but the rule was fair and visible, so complaints faded.
　中文：短暂有，但规则公平且可见，抱怨自然消失。
**关键词与易错点**：booking table、one theme per slot。

### Q22. Tell me about a time a colleague changed something without telling you.
**中文题意**：同事改了东西没告诉你，你怎么处理？
**考察点**：不责怪、系统性防再发。
**使用项目**：P5（S19），备选 P7（S28）。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> On the navigation vehicle, the teammate who wrote the lower-controller firmware changed a frame format without telling me, and my bridge node began rejecting frames with CRC errors. I first proved from the logs that the format had changed, because the meaning of one byte after the header was different. We then agreed the new layout together. To prevent a repeat, I added a protocol version byte, so the bridge refuses to run on a mismatch with a clear error message. We also made a one-page interface document for units, signs and timing that must be updated with each firmware change. I wanted the system to make forgetting hard, rather than blaming anybody.

**中文翻译**：导航车上，写下位机固件的队友改了帧格式没有告诉我，我的桥接节点开始因 CRC 错误拒收帧。我先用日志证明格式确实变了：帧头之后一个字节的含义不同。然后我们一起确定新格式。为防重演，我加了协议版本字节，不匹配时桥接节点拒绝运行并给出明确错误。我们还建了一页接口文档，写单位、符号和时序，固件每次改动必须更新。我想让系统让人难以忘记，而不是指责谁。
**追问 1**：*How did you tell it was a format change and not noise?* The errors were constant and started at one commit; random noise would not align with a firmware change.
　中文：错误持续且从某次提交开始；随机噪声不会与固件变更对齐。
**追问 2**：*Similar case in another project?* On the wheel-legged robot a motor firmware update changed the encoding range, so we added an interface-change register and a frame consistency test.
　中文：轮腿项目里电机固件更新改了编码范围，于是我们加了接口变更登记表和帧一致性测试。
**关键词与易错点**：CRC、version byte、interface document。

### Q23. How do you deal with a difficult team member?

**考察点**：同理心、方法。
**使用项目**：方法型。
**English answer（口语，约 110 词）**：

First I try to understand what is behind the difficulty: often it is unclear responsibilities, different pace, or a misunderstanding. I speak with the person directly and privately, focus on the task and ask what would help. If it is about unclear ownership, I propose writing down who owns what. **追问 2**：*What is your own difficult trait?* I can be too quiet when I am unsure, so I now say early what I do not know.
　中文：不确定时我可能太沉默，所以现在会早点说出我不知道什么。
**关键词与易错点**：ownership、private、task focus。方法型回答要明说 "I don't have a dramatic example"。

## 5. C 沟通

### Q24. How do you explain a technical idea to a non-technical person?
**中文题意**：你怎么向非技术人员解释技术想法？
**考察点**：沟通清晰度，类比能力。
**使用项目**：P6、P4（S30）。
**English answer（口语，约 120 词）**：
> I start from what the person cares about, not from the method. For example, to explain the safety chain at Kaihong, I say: a plan from a smart planner is like a request at a gate. The gate checks if the robot's state is fresh, if the action is inside its limits and if the mode allows it. Only then does the robot move, and we confirm by reading the state back. For my gardening pump I say: it is like a thermostat with two different numbers, one to turn on and a higher one to turn off, so it does not click on and off. Then I ask, does this match what you needed?

**中文翻译**：我从对方关心的东西出发，而不是从方法出发。比如向非技术人员解释深开鸿的安全链，我说：聪明规划器给出的计划就像到门口的请求。门口检查机器人状态是否新鲜、动作是否在限度内、当前模式是否允许；通过了机器人才动，并通过读回状态确认。对园艺水泵我说：它像一个有两个数字的恒温器，一个数字开、一个更高的数字关，所以不会"咔哒咔哒"反复开关。然后我问：这符合你需要的吗？
**追问 1**：*What is the risk of an analogy?* It hides details; so I offer to go deeper and I keep the real constraint, "nothing moves if the check fails".
　中文：类比会隐藏细节；所以我提出可深入，并保留真实约束——检查不通过就不动。
**追问 2**：*Can you do it in one sentence?* A task plan cannot reach a robot without passing a check, and the result is confirmed from the robot's actual state.
　中文：任务计划不经检查到不了机器人，结果要用机器人真实状态确认。
**关键词与易错点**：analogy、gate、hysteresis。P6 细节只讲原理。

### Q25. How do you give and receive feedback?
**中文题意**：你怎么给予和接受反馈？
**考察点**：成熟度。
**使用项目**：P6、P1（S25）。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> When I receive feedback, I treat it as information. I ask for the reason and check it against the data. At AIRS my mentor noticed that I changed several gains at once, so I could not tell which change helped. Since then I change one thing at a time and log it. When I give feedback, for example in code review at Kaihong, I comment on the code and the behaviour, not the person. I point to a case that fails, suggest an alternative, and say what is good. I also keep review comments small, so the author can fix them quickly. Good feedback is specific, kind and testable.

**中文翻译**：收到反馈时，我把它当作信息，问原因并对照数据检查。在 AIRS，导师注意到我一次改了好几个增益，无法判断哪次有效。此后我一次只改一项并记录。给反馈时，比如在深开鸿的代码评审里，我评论代码和行为，不评论人；指出会失败的用例，给出替代方案，也说哪里好。我还让评审意见保持小而具体，方便作者快速修改。好的反馈要具体、善意、可检验。
**追问 1**：*What if you disagree with feedback?* I show the data I see and ask for their view; if I am wrong I change quickly.
　中文：我展示我看到的数据并问对方看法；若我错了就迅速改。
**追问 2**：*What did the habit change?* My tuning on the real robot became faster and safer because I could attribute every change.
　中文：真机调参更快更安全，因为每次改动都能归因。
**关键词与易错点**：one change at a time、code review。

### Q26. How did you communicate a technical risk to a non-technical manager or supervisor?
**中文题意**：你如何向非技术的管理者或导师传达技术风险？
**考察点**：风险沟通，不夸大不隐瞒。
**使用项目**：P2（S16）。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> Before the first real-hardware test at Future Power, a colleague wanted to run at full gain to save time. I explained the risk in plain terms: a wrong sign or a bad zero offset at full gain would make the arm move fast and hit a person or the table. I did not say it is dangerous in general. I proposed the exact plan: ten to twenty percent gain, half the speed limit, no load, someone at the emergency stop, and a checklist. I also said what we would gain: the first run would take a little longer, but would find sign errors without risk. Everyone agreed. A risk statement should include the cost, the plan and the benefit.

**中文翻译**：在未来动力第一次上真机测试前，一位同事想用满增益运行以节省时间。我用通俗的话解释风险：满增益下符号错误或零位偏差会让手臂快速运动，撞到人或桌子。我没有泛泛地说"危险"，而是给出具体方案：10%–20% 增益、限速减半、空载、人在急停旁，并配检查清单。我也说明收益：第一次会多花一点时间，但能无风险地发现符号错误。大家都同意。风险陈述应包括代价、方案和收益。
**追问 1**：*Did the low-gain run find anything?* The usual things: a sign convention or a zero offset below 0.03 radian that needed calibration.
　中文：通常是符号约定或需要标定的小于 0.03 rad 的零位偏差。
**追问 2**：*What if the manager says there is no time?* I show the cost of a damaged arm versus an hour, and offer to prepare the checklist myself.
　中文：我对比损坏手臂的代价和一小时，并主动准备检查清单。
**关键词与易错点**：10–20% gain、emergency stop、checklist。

### Q27. How do you ask for help without wasting other people's time?
**中文题意**：你怎么求助又不浪费别人时间？
**考察点**：自主性与尊重。
**使用项目**：P1。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> I first spend a bounded time on it myself, and I make a single-variable test. Then I go with a short message: what I observed, what I have excluded, what I suspect, and what I need. At AIRS, for example, I did not just say the ballbot crawls. I said: at rest it crept about twenty centimetres in a fixed direction, the pitch mean was 0.9 degrees, the model says drift equals offset divided by position gain and that matched, and I suspect the offset was captured while the robot was not steady. My mentor answered in one minute. Good questions make answers cheap.

**中文翻译**：我先给自己限定时间自己做，并做单变量测试。然后带着简短信息去问：我观察到什么、排除了什么、怀疑什么、需要什么。比如在 AIRS，我不会只说"球形机器人在爬"，而是说：静止时朝固定方向爬了约 20 cm，pitch 均值 0.9°，模型预言漂移等于偏置除以位置增益，吻合，我怀疑偏置是在机器人没站稳时采的。导师一分钟就答了。好问题让答案变得便宜。
**追问 1**：*How long do you try before asking?* A bounded time, such as one focused session, longer if the risk is low and shorter if the robot could be damaged.
　中文：限定时间，如一个专注时段；风险低可更长，可能损坏机器人则更短。
**追问 2**：*What happened with the offset?* I changed the enable sequence: steady robot, average 200 frames, re-sample at every enable.
　中文：我改了使能流程：扶稳、取 200 帧均值、每次使能都重采。
**关键词与易错点**：observed、excluded、suspect。数值 0.9° 与 0.2 m 是项目统一取值，说 about。

### Q28. How do you document an interface so others can use it?
**中文题意**：你怎么写接口文档让别人能用？
**考察点**：文档意识。
**使用项目**：P5、P2（S15、S19）。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> One page, with the things that actually cause bugs: names, types, units, signs, rates, timeouts, versions and an example. For the navigation chassis, my page listed speed in millimetres per second as a signed 16-bit integer, steering angle in hundredths of a degree with left positive, reverse as negative, frame layout with a CRC, a 50 hertz rate, the watchdog time of 200 milliseconds, status bits and a version byte. I added one known input and the expected output, so people can test their code. And I update it in the same commit as the code. If documentation lags behind code, people stop trusting it.

**中文翻译**：一页，只写真正会引起 bug 的东西：名称、类型、单位、符号、频率、超时、版本和示例。导航底盘的那一页写了：速度为有符号 16 位整数，单位 mm/s；转角为 0.01°、向左为正；倒车为负；带 CRC 的帧布局；50 Hz 频率；200 ms 看门狗；状态位；以及版本字节。我还加了一个已知输入和期望输出，方便大家测试自己的代码。并且文档与代码在同一次提交中更新。文档落后于代码，人们就不再信任它。
**追问 1**：*Why a known input and output?* It lets the other person test their side without my robot.
　中文：让对方不需要我的机器人也能测试自己这边。
**追问 2**：*Who updates it?* The owner of the interface, in the same commit as the change.
　中文：接口所有者，与改动同一次提交。
**关键词与易错点**：units、signs、version byte、known example。

### Q29. How do you keep your supervisor informed?
**中文题意**：你怎么向导师汇报进度？
**考察点**：向上沟通。
**使用项目**：P7、P5、P6（S25、S20）。
**English answer（口语，约 120 词）**：
> I use a fixed structure for a weekly update: what I planned and the result, the biggest problem and the evidence, the plan for next week and what I need. For the wheel-legged robot it was one page and a short video. I focus on decisions and evidence: for instance, why a gain schedule instead of a fixed gain, and the experiment that supports it. I only report conclusions I can back with a plot or a number. When a risk appears, such as possible damage or a spare part to buy, I say it early. And I turn feedback into test items, for example a leg-length sweep in the regression list.

**中文翻译**：我用固定结构做每周汇报：计划与结果、最大问题与证据、下周计划与所需资源。轮腿项目是一页纸加一段短视频。重点放在决策与证据：比如为什么用增益调度而不是固定增益，以及支撑它的实验。我只汇报能用曲线或数字支持的结论。出现风险（可能损坏、要买备件）时我尽早说。我还把反馈转成测试项，比如把腿长扫描放进回归清单。
**追问 1**：*Example of feedback turned into a test?* "I'd like to see stability when the leg length changes" became a leg-length sweep from 0.10 to 0.30 metres.
　中文：「希望看到腿长变化时的稳定性」变成了 0.10–0.30 m 的腿长扫描。

### Q43. Tell me about a time your approach was wrong, and how you found out.

## 8. F 失败、挫折与遗憾

### Q44. Tell me about your biggest failure.
**中文题意**：讲讲你最大的一次失败。
**考察点**：承担责任、从失败中学习。
**使用项目**：P3（S1）。
**English answer（口语，约 130 词）**：
> My biggest setback was the launcher on our RoboMaster hero robot. I started with a PID on the friction wheels, but it was not good enough for continuous firing. **中文翻译**：我最大的挫折是 RoboMaster 英雄机器人的发射机构。我最开始在摩擦轮上用 PID，但对连续发射不够好。每发弹丸后轮速下降几百 rpm，要一百多毫秒才恢复，所以弹速变化约 1 m/s。我试了变增益 PID，然后是滑模控制，并加入裁判系统弹速反馈，在每发后修正目标转速。最终版本发射稳定，散布约 0.3 m/s。但真正的教训在流程：我应该一开始就记录每一发的速度，因为没有它我是在盲调。
**追问 1**：*Why did you change from variable-gain PID to sliding mode?* The variable gain helped large errors but near zero error it was hard to tune; sliding mode with a boundary layer shortened the recovery to about 60 to 80 milliseconds.
　中文：变增益对大误差有帮助，但近零误差难调；带边界层的滑模把恢复缩短到约 60–80 ms。
**追问 2**：*Did the three controllers run together?* No. They are an iteration history; at the end one of them was active.
　中文：没有，它们是迭代历史；最后只有一个在运行。

### Q45. Describe a serious mistake you made and what you did about it.
**中文题意**：描述你犯的一个严重错误以及你怎么处理。
**考察点**：诚实、补救、预防。
**使用项目**：P1（S11）。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> At AIRS, my first centre-of-mass feedforward had the wrong sign. With it off, the tilt peak during the arm raise was about 0.06 radians. With it on, it became about 0.09, and the robot fell once. I stopped the tests and told my mentor. A static test with one arm held out showed the correction going the wrong way, while my estimate of the centre of mass matched a hand calculation, so the sign was the problem, not the estimate. I flipped it, calibrated the gain to about one with five static poses, and then repeated the on and off comparison. To prevent a repeat, any signed feedforward now gets a static single-arm direction test before any dynamic test.

**中文翻译**：在 AIRS，我的第一版质心前馈符号是反的。关闭时，抬臂过程的倾角峰值约 0.06 rad；打开后变成约 0.09，机器人还倒了一次。我停下测试并告诉导师。一只手臂伸出并保持的静态测试显示修正方向反了，而我的质心估计与手算吻合，所以问题在符号，不在估计。我翻转符号，用五个静态姿态把增益标定到约 1，然后重做开/关对比。为防重演，现在任何带符号的前馈都要先做静态单臂方向测试，再做动态测试。
**追问 1**：*Why was the robot unsafe to continue?* A reversed feedforward adds to the disturbance instead of cancelling it; I stayed on the gantry safety stage until the sign was verified.
　中文：反向前馈会加重扰动而不是抵消；符号验证前我留在龙门架保护阶段。
**追问 2**：*How did you calibrate the gain?* Regress the steady correction needed against the gravity moment over five static poses; the slope was about 1.0.
　中文：用五个静态姿态对所需稳态修正与重力矩回归，斜率约 1.0。
**关键词与易错点**：sign error、static test、gain calibration。

### Q46. Tell me about a project that did not go as planned.
**中文题意**：讲一个没按计划进行的项目。
**考察点**：应变、诊断。
**使用项目**：P7（S29）。
**English answer（口语，约 130 词）**：
> On the wheel-legged robot, my plan was to build the controller and tune it. But at a 0.5 metre per second speed step the robot fell. The cause was not the control law. The step asked the wheels for about nine newton-metres, but the limit was three, so the controller saturated and its effective gain collapsed. I added a metric, the fraction of cycles at the limit, which was over sixty percent. I then ramped the speed command over one second with a 0.5 metres per second squared limit, lowered the speed weight and added anti-windup on the position integral. The limit fraction dropped below five percent, and the robot tracked 0.5 metres per second. I learned to budget time for limits and signals around the controller.

**中文翻译**：在轮腿机器人上，我的计划是搭好控制器并调参。但在 0.5 m/s 速度阶跃时机器人倒了。原因不是控制律：阶跃要求轮子给约 9 N·m，但限幅是 3，控制器饱和、有效增益崩塌。我加了一个指标：贴限幅的周期占比，当时超过 60%。然后我把速度指令做成 1 s 斜坡（加速度限制 0.5 m/s²），降低速度权重，并对位置积分加抗饱和。贴限幅占比降到 5% 以下，机器人能稳定跟踪 0.5 m/s。我学会了为控制器周围的限幅与信号预留时间。
**追问 1**：*Why does saturation hurt?* When the output hits the limit, the effective gain is limit divided by error, so a larger error means a smaller gain, and the loop can run away.
　中文：输出贴限幅后有效增益是限幅除以误差，误差越大增益越小，闭环可能发散。
**追问 2**：*What did you change in the plan?* I added "saturation fraction" to the regression list and always ramp commands.
　中文：把"饱和占比"加入回归清单，并始终对指令做斜坡。
**关键词与易错点**：saturation fraction、ramp、anti-windup。数值是项目统一取值。

### Q47. How would you handle repeated experiment failures?
**中文题意**：实验反复失败你怎么处理？
**考察点**：系统性思维与韧性。
**使用项目**：P1（S10）。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> I stop and make the failure reproducible before I change anything. At AIRS, the robot lurched forward every time I re-enabled it after a fall. I recorded the target, the integrator and the offset before and after the enable. Three stale things were stacked: the old target position, the integrator from the fall, and an offset captured while the robot was still being steadied. I restored them one at a time to see each contribution. Then I wrote the enable as a state machine with a five-step initialisation and made ten fall-and-re-enable cycles part of my regression list. The peak tilt after re-enabling stayed under 0.02 radians. Repeated failures usually mean I haven't isolated the cause yet.

**中文翻译**：我先停下来让失败可复现，再改东西。在 AIRS，每次跌倒后重新使能机器人都往前猛冲。我记录了使能前后的目标、积分和偏置。三个陈旧量叠加：旧的目标位置、跌倒时的积分、以及机器人还在扶稳时采集的偏置。我逐个恢复它们看各自的贡献。然后把使能写成状态机，加五步初始化，并把十次"跌倒—重新使能"循环放进回归清单。重新使能后的倾角峰值保持在 0.02 rad 以下。反复失败通常说明我还没有隔离出原因。
**追问 1**：*What are the five steps?* Wait until the body is steady, average about 200 frames for the offset, set the target to the current position, clear integrator and filters, then ramp the output in over about 0.3 seconds.
　中文：等待机身稳定、取约 200 帧均值作偏置、目标设为当前位置、清积分和滤波、约 0.3 s 斜坡放出输出。
**追问 2**：*What if you cannot reproduce it?* I add logging so that the next occurrence is explainable, such as a ring buffer of the last second.
　中文：加日志让下次可解释，比如保留最近 1 s 的环形缓冲。
**关键词与易错点**：state machine、initialisation、regression。数字是项目统一取值。

### Q49. Describe a time your hardware broke or misbehaved at a bad moment.
**中文题意**：讲一次硬件在关键时刻出问题的经历。
**考察点**：排查、预防。
**使用项目**：P2 / P3。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：

### Q50. Tell me about a time you had to start over or redo something several times.
**中文题意**：讲一次你不得不重来或多次重做的经历。
**考察点**：迭代与耐心。
**使用项目**：P7（离地切换四个版本）。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：

**中文翻译**：轮腿机器人的离地检测经历了四个版本。第一版用轮速突变，依赖于我怎么驾驶。第二版用估计支撑力低于 10 N，但在门限处来回抖。第三版加了滞回和去抖：10 N 持续 16 ms 判离地，20 N 持续 10 ms 判着地；但落地仍有力矩尖峰。第四版在腾空时把轮矩置零、冻结位置参考，落地后约 80 ms 渐入控制器。每个版本我只改一件事，这样结果可归因；每个版本都在机器人从未离地的日志上检查误触发次数。

## 12. J 道德、诚信与安全红线

### Q77. How do you make sure you report experimental results honestly?
**中文题意**：你怎么确保如实汇报实验结果？
**考察点**：科研诚信。
**使用项目**：P4、P5（S30）。
**English answer（口语，约 130 词）**：
> I report what I did and what I did not do. In the gardening project my real-hardware validation reached the full loop with camera, pump and web page, but I did not run a long-duration test, so I say clearly that I reached the full loop but not a long-run validation. One observation, soil moisture rising from 41 to 58 during watering, is a single trial, not a statistic. In the navigation project I say 22 recorded runs, 20 successful, and I describe the two failures: a localisation error at a glass door and a time-out in a narrow turn. I don't say "highly reliable". I state the scene, the conditions and the limits of the evidence.

**中文翻译**：我如实说明做了什么、没做什么。园艺项目里我的实机验证到达了摄像头、水泵和网页的完整闭环，但没有做长时间测试，所以我明确说到达了完整闭环但没有长时间验证。土壤湿度浇水时从 41 升到 58 是一次观察，不是统计。导航项目里我说 22 次记录的运行成功 20 次，并描述两次失败：玻璃门处的定位错误和窄口调头超时。我不说"高度可靠"。我说明场景、条件和证据的局限。
**追问 1**：*Why include the failures?* They show where the system is weak and how I would improve it.
　中文：它们说明系统弱在哪里，以及我会怎么改进。
**追问 2**：*What would a long-run test find?* Drift, memory growth, relay wear and Wi-Fi dropouts.
　中文：漂移、内存增长、继电器磨损和 Wi-Fi 掉线。
**关键词与易错点**：single trial、22 runs/20 successes、limits of evidence。P4 只到 V4，不说做过长时测试。

### Q78. What is a safety line you would never cross on a robot?
**中文题意**：在机器人上你绝不会越过的安全底线是什么？
**考察点**：安全意识。
**使用项目**：P1（S16）。
**English answer（口语，约 120 词）**：
> I would never run an untested change on a robot that can fall or move fast without the staged procedure. For the ballbot the rule was: lifted single-axis test, then gantry with a slack safety strap at low gain, then the floor; never skip a stage. Someone always holds the wireless emergency stop, which also cuts power through a contactor, and nobody stands in the test area. Software has limits, a fall detector, data-age checks and a zero-output lock-out, but the hardware stop does not depend on software. I would not skip any of this for a deadline, because the failure costs a damaged machine or a hurt person.

**中文翻译**：我绝不会在可能倒下或快速运动的机器人上，不经分阶段流程就跑未经测试的改动。对球形机器人的规则是：悬空单轴测试，再用松弛保险带的龙门架低增益，再落地；永不跳阶段。始终有人握着无线急停（它也通过接触器断电），测试区不站人。软件有限幅、跌倒检测、数据年龄检查和零输出锁定，但硬件急停不依赖软件。我不会为赶期限跳过其中任何一项，因为失败的代价是损坏机器或伤人。
**追问 1**：*What does the fall detector do?* Tilt above 0.5 radians for 5 cycles zeroes the output and locks the controller until a manual, re-initialised enable.
　中文：倾角超 0.5 rad 持续 5 个周期则输出清零并锁定，直到人工、重新初始化后才能使能。
**追问 2**：*Why independent of software?* The failing component could be the software itself.
　中文：出故障的可能就是软件本身。
**关键词与易错点**：staged test、hardware stop、lock-out。数值为设计取值。

### Q79. What would you do if you were pressured to skip a safety check before a demo?
**中文题意**：演示前有人催你跳过安全检查，你怎么办？
**考察点**：原则与沟通。
**使用项目**：P2 / P3（S16）。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> I would not skip it, but I would try to help with the real problem, which is time. I'd explain what the check protects, for example a wrong sign or a bad zero offset at full gain, which can hit a person. Then I'd offer a faster safe alternative: the checklist is short, the low-gain run takes minutes, and I can prepare it in advance. If it still seems that I am pushed, I'd involve my supervisor. I did exactly this at Future Power before the first hardware run: ten to twenty percent gain, half the speed limit, no load and someone at the stop. We found sign issues safely and the demo went fine.

**中文翻译**：我不会跳过，但会帮忙解决真正的问题——时间。我会解释这个检查保护什么，比如满增益下的符号错误或零位偏差可能伤人。然后给出更快的安全替代方案：清单很短，低增益运行只需几分钟，我可以提前准备。如果仍然被施压，我会请导师介入。我在未来动力第一次上真机前就是这样做的：10%–20% 增益、限速减半、空载、人在急停旁。我们安全地发现了符号问题，之后的运行也很顺利。
**追问 1**：*What if the supervisor insists?* I'd say clearly I am not comfortable, offer to take responsibility for the delay, and ask for a written decision.
　中文：我会明确说我不放心，愿意为延误负责，并请求书面决定。
**追问 2**：*Where is your line on small risks?* I accept small risks that are reversible and logged; I don't accept risks to people.
　中文：我接受可逆且有记录的小风险；不接受对人的风险。
**关键词与易错点**：safe alternative、supervisor。

### Q80. What would you do if you found an error in results you had already reported?
**中文题意**：如果发现已汇报的结果有错，你怎么办？
**考察点**：诚信与纠错。
**使用项目**：P1（S12）。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> I would tell the people who used the result right away, say what is wrong and what is affected, and fix it. At AIRS my first feedforward comparison looked bad for the feedforward: I ran all "off" tests and then all "on" tests, and the second batch looked worse because the battery had dropped and the motors had warmed up. I told my mentor that the comparison was biased by order. I then reran it with on and off interleaved, five times each, with the battery voltage logged. The corrected comparison favoured the feedforward. If I had said nothing, the mistake would have stayed in the notes. Correcting early costs little and builds trust.

**中文翻译**：我会立刻通知使用过该结果的人，说明哪里错了、影响什么，并修正。在 AIRS，我最初的前馈对比看起来对前馈不利：我先跑完所有"关"的测试再跑所有"开"的测试，后一批看起来更差，因为电量下降、电机变热。我告诉导师这个对比被顺序偏差影响了。然后我把开/关交错重做，各五次，并记录电池电压。修正后的对比支持前馈。如果我什么都不说，错误就会留在笔记里。早纠错代价小，还能建立信任。
**追问 1**：*Why interleave?* To spread trends such as battery drain and temperature equally over both conditions.
　中文：让电量、温度这类趋势均匀分布在两种条件上。
**追问 2**：*What do you record per run?* Voltage, time order, parameter file and bag name.
　中文：电压、时间顺序、参数文件和 bag 名。
**关键词与易错点**：order effect、interleaved、correct early。

### Q81. How do you separate your own contribution from what you only noticed or what the team did?
**中文题意**：你怎么区分自己的贡献、读码发现和团队工作？
**考察点**：诚实表述贡献。
**使用项目**：P1、P2、P3、P6（S14）。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> I use three verbs. "I built" is for what I wrote and tested: for example the cascaded balance controller and the safety loop at AIRS, or the relative-pose mapping and the Mink IK configuration at Future Power. "We" is for team results, such as the RoboMaster national third prize. "I noticed" is for findings from reading other people's code, for example the base firmware's heartbeat flag that was never updated, or a launch parameter name the node did not read. I reported those and added protection on my own side, but I did not fix them. If someone asks, "which part is yours?", I can answer in one sentence, and I also say where I used existing tools such as Mink or the motor library.

**中文翻译**：我用三个动词。"I built"用于我写并测试过的东西：比如 AIRS 的级联平衡控制器和安全回路，或未来动力的相对位姿映射和 Mink IK 配置。"We"用于团队成果，如 RoboMaster 全国赛三等奖。"I noticed"用于读别人代码的发现，比如底盘固件那个从不更新的心跳标志，或节点不读的 launch 参数名。我汇报了它们并在自己这边加了保护，但没有修。如果有人问"哪部分是你的"，我能用一句话回答，也会说明哪里用了现成工具，如 Mink 或电机库。
**追问 1**：*Why be so careful?* Because overclaiming is easy to detect with one follow-up question and damages trust.
　中文：因为夸大只需一个追问就会暴露，并损害信任。
**追问 2**：*What existing tools did you use?* Mink and MuJoCo for IK, an existing motor driver library, and the team's base CAN framework.
　中文：IK 用 Mink 和 MuJoCo，现成的电机驱动库，以及团队的底盘 CAN 框架。
**关键词与易错点**：I built / we / I noticed。这是全套材料的核心诚实原则。

### Q82. How do you handle confidential information from a company internship?
**中文题意**：怎么处理公司实习中的保密信息？
**考察点**：职业操守。
**使用项目**：P6。
**English answer（口语，约 110 词）**：
> I talk about design principles, my own role and the engineering method, and I do not talk about internal source code, internal interface and topic names, device lists, customers or internal performance numbers. At Kaihong I can say that the platform gates every task plan through a proposal, a policy check, execution and state feedback, and that I standardised timestamps, frames and validity for the robot state. I would not give the exact rules or thresholds. When I am asked for a number, I say that in our design it is roughly such-and-such, and that I would measure it on the target. If I am unsure whether something is public, I ask my mentor first.

**中文翻译**：我讲设计原则、我自己的角色和工程方法，不讲内部源码、内部接口和话题名、设备清单、客户或内部性能数字。在深开鸿，我可以说平台让每个任务计划都经过提议、策略检查、执行和状态反馈，以及我标准化了机器人状态的时间戳、坐标系和有效性。我不会给出具体规则或阈值。被问到数字时，我说"在我们的设计里大约是……"，并说我会在目标系统上测量。不确定是否公开时，我先问导师。
**追问 1**：*Can you tell me more detail on the rules?* I can describe categories, such as freshness, limits, mode and duplicates, but not the exact rules.
　中文：我可以讲类别，如新鲜度、限度、模式、重复，但不讲具体规则。
**追问 2**：*Are your P6 numbers measured?* They are design-level values, not company measurements; I say that.
　中文：它们是设计级数值，不是公司测量；我会明说。
**关键词与易错点**：principles not internals、ask mentor。

### Q83. A planner or a language model suggests an unsafe robot action. Whose responsibility is it, and what would you build?
**中文题意**：规划器或语言模型建议了不安全的机器人动作，谁负责？你会建什么？
**考察点**：AI 与物理执行的责任（契合 PS 主线）。
**使用项目**：P6、P4。
**English answer（口语，约 130 词）**：
> The system designer is responsible for what can reach the hardware, so the model is never the last line of defence. At Kaihong a task plan goes through an action proposal, a policy check, execution and state feedback. The check asks whether the state is fresh, whether the action is inside the device's limits, whether the mode and permission allow it, and whether it is a duplicate or expired. If the check fails, nothing moves and the reason goes back. In my gardening project the language model could only give recommendations or constrained requests, and the pump limits stayed on the device. A gate limits the envelope of actions, but it does not guarantee good decisions, so I also care about independent hardware protection.

**中文翻译**：能到达硬件的东西由系统设计者负责，所以模型从来不应是最后一道防线。在深开鸿，任务计划要经过动作提议、策略检查、执行和状态反馈。检查会问：状态是否新鲜、动作是否在设备限度内、模式和权限是否允许、是否重复或过期。检查不通过就什么都不动，并把原因返回。在园艺项目里，语言模型只能给建议或受约束的请求，水泵限制留在设备端。关口限定动作包络，但不保证决策质量，所以我也关注独立的硬件保护。
**追问 1**：*Can the gate be bypassed?* A design question: the check runs as a separate service, and the execution gateway accepts only a short-lived approved ticket.
　中文：这是设计问题：检查作为独立服务，执行网关只接受短时有效的批准票据。
**追问 2**：*What does the gate not guarantee?* That the plan is a good plan, or that the sensors are right; it needs independent limits and monitoring.
　中文：不保证计划好、传感器对；还需要独立限幅与监控。
**关键词与易错点**：proposal、policy check、envelope、hardware protection。P6 为典型设计，使用 in our design。

### Q84. What would you do if someone asked you to exaggerate your role on a CV or in an interview?
**中文题意**：如果有人要你在简历或面试里夸大角色，你怎么办？
**考察点**：诚信。
**使用项目**：综合（P3 三等奖团队成绩、P1 LQR 分析）。
**English answer（口语，约 105 词）**：
> I would decline politely and explain why. A claim that I cannot explain in detail will fail at the first follow-up question, and it hurts the people who trust me. For example, I say that LQR on the ballbot was an analysis tool, not the deployed controller; that the RoboMaster third prize and the 3V3 second prize are team results; and that a code-reading finding is something I noticed, not something I fixed. I would rather say a smaller true statement and be able to answer every question about it. Being accurate is also faster: I never need to remember a story.

**中文翻译**：我会礼貌地拒绝并解释原因。一个我无法详细解释的说法，在第一个追问时就会露馅，也会伤害信任我的人。比如我会说球形机器人上的 LQR 是分析工具，不是部署的控制器；RoboMaster 三等奖和 3V3 二等奖是团队成绩；读码发现是我注意到的，不是我修好的。我宁愿说一个更小但真实的说法，并能回答关于它的每一个问题。准确也更省事：我不需要记住一个故事。
**追问 1**：*Where do people usually overclaim?* Team results, tools they only used, and numbers they did not measure.
　中文：团队成绩、只是用过的工具、没测过的数字。
**追问 2**：*How do you answer when you don't remember a number?* "I don't remember the exact number, but it was roughly …", and then give the qualitative result.
　中文：「我不记得确切数字，大致是……」然后给定性结论。
**关键词与易错点**：accuracy、follow-up、team result。

### Q85. A teammate wants to skip a test stage to save time. What do you do?
**中文题意**：队友想跳过一个测试阶段省时间，你怎么办？
**考察点**：团队安全文化。
**使用项目**：P1、P7（S16）。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> I ask what they want to save and what risk it adds, then I show what the stage catches. In the wheel-legged project, the hanging stage caught sign errors before the robot touched the ground; the first low-torque ground test with a safety rope caught a wrong balance bias. I made the three-stage test, hanging, rope and low limits, then the floor, a written rule rather than a request, so it was not a matter of personal opinion. If a stage truly is redundant for a specific change, I'd agree to skip it only with my supervisor's approval and a note in the log. Safety rules are for everyone.

**中文翻译**：我问对方想省什么、会增加什么风险，然后展示这个阶段能发现什么。轮腿项目里，悬空阶段在机器人碰地前发现了符号错误；带保护绳的第一次低力矩落地测试发现了平衡偏置不对。我把"悬空、保护绳低限幅、再上地面"三阶段测试写成书面规则，而不是个人请求，这样它不是个人意见。如果某个阶段对某项具体改动确实多余，我只在导师批准并在日志里记一笔的情况下同意跳过。安全规则对每个人都适用。
**追问 1**：*What if the rule slows the project a lot?* Then I make the stages faster, for example scripts and checklists, not skip them.
　中文：那就让阶段更快——脚本和清单——而不是跳过。
**追问 2**：*Who can approve an exception?* The supervisor, and it goes in the log.
　中文：导师，并写入日志。
**关键词与易错点**：written rule、staged test。

### Q86. If your method fails in the real world, whose fault is it?
**中文题意**：如果你的方法在现实中失败，是谁的错？
**考察点**：担责，不推卸。
**使用项目**：P7、P5。
**English answer（口语，约 120 词）**：
> The first question is not whose fault it is, but what failed. As the person who owns the method, I take responsibility for finding out. On the wheel-legged robot, balance anomalies could have been blamed on the controller, but tracing showed state inconsistency, torque limits and ground-contact transitions. I first checked the data, then the limits, then the model assumption, and only then changed the controller. If the cause is the hardware, I show a test that proves it, as we did with the backlash check. If the cause is my assumption, I say so and revise it. A failure with a clear explanation is progress, and it is better than a success I cannot explain.

**中文翻译**：首先的问题不是谁的错，而是什么失败了。作为方法的负责人，我负责查明。在轮腿机器人上，平衡异常本可以怪控制器，但追溯显示是状态不一致、力矩限制和触地切换。我先查数据，再查限幅，再查模型假设，之后才改控制器。如果原因是硬件，我给出能证明它的测试，就像我们的齿隙检查。如果原因是我的假设，我承认并修正。解释清楚的失败就是进展，比解释不了的成功更好。
**追问 1**：*What is your debugging order?* Data, then limits, then model, then the controller; one change at a time.
　中文：数据、限幅、模型、控制器；一次改一项。
**追问 2**：*Which cause was the most common?* Interface and state problems such as timestamps, frames and signs, more often than control laws.
　中文：接口和状态问题（时间戳、坐标系、符号）比控制律更常见。
**关键词与易错点**：ownership、debug order、evidence。

### 13.2 三轮练习安排

| 轮次 | 目标 | 做法 | 用时 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 第一轮：读熟 | 熟悉 86 题和 30 个故事 | 按类别每天读 20 题，只记故事骨架，不背逐词 | 约 5 天 |
| 第二轮：开口 | 能在 60–90 秒内讲完 | 遮住英文，只看中文题意，计时说一遍并录音，对照关键词 | 约 7 天 |
| 第三轮：追问 | 能接第二、三层追问 | 找人或自己随机抽题，每题连问三次 "Why?" "How do you know?" "What would you do differently?" | 约 5 天 |

### 13.3 故事使用提醒

- 同一场面试里，一个故事最多用两次，且第二次要换角度（例如 S1 第一次答"分歧"，第二次答"失败"）。
- 最容易被追问的五个故事是 S1（摩擦轮）、S8（LQR 分析与饱和）、S10（重新使能猛冲）、S22（定位输入）、S29（0.5 m/s 阶跃）。这五个要能讲到数字、方法、反思三层。
- 面试前一天，把第 1 节第 3–6 条的口径规则再读一遍。
