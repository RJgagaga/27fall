> **一句话定位**：港澳工科硕士的教授最爱问"你具体怎么做的"。本文件把七个项目的工程化实践做成 118 道英文题：最复杂的问题怎么解决、怎么定位 bug、做过哪些优化并怎么度量和回归、控制周期与频率怎么选、抖动与实时性、延迟预算、CAN 负载、线程优先级、仿真到实机、参数与版本、测试、安全、日志、代码质量、技术选型和现场应急。每道题都标明用哪个项目，回答按"现象 → 度量 → 瓶颈 → 改动 → 结果 → 回归"组织，数字与各项目 README 的自查表和"20 工程化实践"一致。

**本章学完你能……**

- 对任意一次优化，用六步讲清：现象、度量方法、瓶颈、改动、前后对比、回归验证；

- 避免三类硬伤：把设计取值说成实测、把读码发现说成自己的修复、把团队成绩说成个人成绩。

## 1. 本文件怎么用

1. **工程题的统一骨架**：背景与目标（1 句）→ 约束（硬件、时间、安全）→ 现象 → 度量 → 瓶颈 → 改动 → 前后对比 → 回归验证 → 一句反思。工程题比行为题更看重"度量"：永远先说"我怎么量的"。
2. **数字的口径**：所有频率、延迟、阈值、前后对比，都是各项目 README 自查表和"20 工程化实践"里的统一设计取值，面试时用 about、roughly、in our setup；被追问到你记不清的精确值时用诚实模板：*"I don't remember the exact number, but it was roughly …, and here is how I measured it."*
3. **P6 的数字是设计取值，不是公司内部实测**；说 "in our design"，并转到"我会怎么测"。
4. **口径红线**：P1 的 LQR 是分析工具，没有部署；P3 控制任务周期约 3 ms（约 333 Hz），传感器 IMU 任务约 1 kHz，不要说"控制 1 kHz"；P5 使用 ROS2 Humble；P7 使用经典 CAN（8 字节帧，不是 CAN FD）；P4 的实机验证到 V4（完整闭环），没有做 V5 长时间测试；P2、P1 里读码发现的隐患只说 "I noticed"。
5. **一个故事可以服务多类题**，但同一场面试里请换角度。题后的"使用项目"告诉你本题取材于哪里，方便你回到项目文档查证。
6. **每题格式**：英文题目、中文题意、考察点、使用项目、英文口语答案（90–150 词）、中文翻译、两个追问（英文问题 + 简答 + 中文）、关键词与易错点。

## 3. A 最复杂的问题与问题分解

### Q1. Describe the most complex problem you solved and how you approached it.
**中文题意**：描述你解决过的最复杂的问题以及你的思路。
**考察点**：问题分解与因果叠加的定位。
**使用项目**：P7 轮腿机器人。
**English answer（口语，约 130 词）**：
> The most complex one was a set of balance anomalies on the wheel-legged robot. It would creep forward after being lowered, it fell when I gave a 0.5 metre per second step, and it fell when the foot left the ground. They looked like one control problem, but I separated them by checking in a fixed order: data, then limits, then model. The creep came from a pitch bias of about 1.5 degrees and unsynchronised IMU and joint data; I read the bias while hanging and took one snapshot per cycle. The step failure was torque saturation, about 9 newton-metres demanded against a limit of 3, so I ramped the command. The fall was a ground-contact switch problem, which took four versions to fix. Each fix had a regression item.

**中文翻译**：最复杂的是轮腿机器人上的一组平衡异常。放下后它会慢慢向前爬，给 0.5 m/s 阶跃会倒，脚离地时也会倒。它们看起来像同一个控制问题，但我按固定顺序检查把它们分开：先数据，再限幅，再模型。爬行来自约 1.5° 的俯仰偏置和 IMU 与关节数据不同步；我在悬空时读偏置，并且每周期取一次快照。阶跃失败是力矩饱和，需求约 9 N·m 而限幅是 3，所以我给指令加斜坡。倒下来自触地切换问题，经过四个版本才解决。每个修复都有一个回归项。
**追问 1**：*How did you separate the causes?* One variable at a time: static log for the creep, step command with a limit-fraction metric for the step, and replayed contact logs for the foot.
　中文：一次一个变量：静止日志看爬行，带贴限幅占比指标的阶跃看饱和，回放触地日志看离地。
**追问 2**：*Which one was hardest?* Contact switching, because it needed a physical quantity (support force), hysteresis, debounce and a blend-in after landing.
　中文：触地切换，因为需要物理量（支撑力）、滞回、去抖和落地渐入。
**关键词与易错点**：bias、snapshot、saturation fraction、hysteresis。数字是项目统一取值，说 about。

### Q2. How do you break down a large problem?
**中文题意**：你怎么拆解大问题？
**考察点**：系统思维。
**使用项目**：P5 导航。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> I break it into layers with a test at each boundary. For the navigation car, the goal was to drive to a goal in a known map. I split it into localisation, global planning, local trajectory, and tracking, plus safety. Each layer had a pass criterion: continuous pose with steps under 0.05 metres, a feasible path under the turning radius, a trajectory that keeps 0.3 metres from obstacles, and a lateral tracking error under 0.1 metres at 0.5 metres per second. I validated bottom-up, because a problem in a lower layer shows up as a fake problem in every layer above. Then I tested each layer on recorded bags before the car, and the car at low speed before higher speed.

**中文翻译**：我把它分成层，每个边界都有测试。对导航车，目标是在已知地图中开到目标点。我拆成定位、全局规划、局部轨迹、跟踪和安全。每层有通过标准：位姿连续且阶跃小于 0.05 m；满足转弯半径的可行路径；离障碍保持 0.3 m 的轨迹；0.5 m/s 下横向跟踪误差小于 0.1 m。我自底向上验证，因为低层的问题会在上面每一层表现为假问题。然后每层先在录制的 bag 上测试，再上车低速，最后提速。
**追问 1**：*Why bottom-up?* A timestamp error in localisation looks like bad planning and bad tracking; fixing the lowest layer first removes fake symptoms.
　中文：定位里的时间戳错误会表现为规划和跟踪不好；先修最低层去掉假症状。
**追问 2**：*What are the pass criteria based on?* The project requirements; I wrote them down before tuning.
　中文：项目需求；我在调参前就写下来。
**关键词与易错点**：layered validation、pass criteria、recorded bags。

### Q3. Describe a time when your first solution failed.
**中文题意**：讲一次你的第一个方案失败了。
**考察点**：迭代与诊断。
**使用项目**：P1 / P7。
**English answer（口语，约 120 词）**：
> On the ballbot, my first centre-of-mass feedforward made things worse: the tilt peak went from about 0.06 to 0.09 radians and the robot fell once. I did not tune the gain. I asked what could be wrong: the sign, the estimate, or the gain. A static test with one arm held out, no dynamics, separated them. The correction went the wrong way, while the estimated centre of mass was a few centimetres forward as in my hand calculation. So the sign was wrong. I flipped it, calibrated the gain to about one using five static poses, and then ran on and off comparisons. The peak dropped to about 0.026 radians. The lesson is to test signed terms statically first.

**中文翻译**：在球形机器人上，我的第一版质心前馈让情况更糟：倾角峰值从约 0.06 升到 0.09 rad，机器人还倒了一次。我没有去调增益，而是问：可能错的是符号、估计还是增益？一只臂伸出的静态测试去掉了动态因素，把它们分开。修正方向反了，而估计的质心向前几厘米，与手算一致。所以是符号错了。我翻转符号，用五个静态姿态把增益标定到约 1，然后做开/关对比，峰值降到约 0.026 rad。教训是带符号的项先做静态测试。
**追问 1**：*Why would a too-large gain look different?* A too-large gain would overshoot to the opposite direction; here the offset got larger in the same direction, which means a sign error.
　中文：增益过大会超调到反方向；这里偏移在同方向变大，说明符号错。
**追问 2**：*What did you regress?* Five static poses, then dynamic trajectories on and off, five repeats each.
　中文：五个静态姿态，然后动态轨迹开/关各五次。
**关键词与易错点**：static test、sign、gain calibration、interleaved comparison。

### Q4. How do you know when a solution is good enough?
**中文题意**：你怎么知道方案"够好了"？
**考察点**：验收标准与取舍。
**使用项目**：P5。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> I decide before I start, by writing acceptance criteria that can pass or fail. For the navigation car: pose steps between cycles under 0.05 metres; 0.5 metres per second tracking with the mean lateral error below 0.10 metres; arrival within 0.15 metres and 8 degrees; and any module failure must stop the car. I measured 0.06 metres mean at 0.5 metres per second and about 0.25 metres at the worst corner at 0.8, so I recorded 0.5 as the supported speed. Over 22 runs, 20 succeeded, and I reported the two failures. Good enough means meeting the criteria with margin, and stating honestly where it stops working.

**中文翻译**：我在开始前就写下能通过/失败的验收标准。对导航车：相邻周期位姿阶跃小于 0.05 m；0.5 m/s 下平均横向误差小于 0.10 m；到点位置 0.15 m、航向 8° 以内；任何模块失效车必须停下。我测到 0.5 m/s 下平均约 0.06 m，0.8 m/s 的最差弯道约 0.25 m，所以把 0.5 m/s 记为支持速度。22 次运行成功 20 次，我汇报了两次失败。"够好"是带余量地满足标准，并诚实说明它在哪里失效。
**追问 1**：*What were the two failures?* A relocalisation error at a glass door and a time-out in a narrow turn.
　中文：玻璃门处的重定位错误和窄口调头超时。
**追问 2**：*Would you raise the speed?* Only after those two are handled, and with three runs at each speed step.
　中文：先处理这两项，并在每档速度至少三次运行后。
**关键词与易错点**：acceptance criteria、margin、honest limits。

### Q5. How do you balance quality and speed?
**中文题意**：你怎么平衡质量与速度？
**考察点**：工程判断。
**使用项目**：P3 RoboMaster。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> I protect the parts where failure is expensive and move fast elsewhere. In the RoboMaster team, safety behaviour and the core loops were never rushed: offline detection, the relax state, output and integral limits came before features. For tuning I moved fast with small steps and a log. A week before the match we froze features, kept only bug fixes, and ran a twelve-item regression list daily, which took little time but caught regressions early. When the operator wanted a change late, I made it a remote-control parameter instead of changing firmware. Speed comes from good process, not from skipping tests.

**中文翻译**：我保护失败代价高的部分，在其他地方快速推进。在 RoboMaster 战队，安全行为和核心环从不赶工：离线检测、RELAX 状态、输出和积分限幅先于功能。调参时我用小步和日志快速推进。比赛前一周我们冻结功能，只保留 bug 修复，每天跑 12 项回归清单，花时间不多却能早早发现回退。操作手后期想要改动时，我把它做成遥控参数而不改固件。速度来自好流程，而不是跳过测试。
**追问 1**：*What is your regression list?* Steps, disturbance, wrap-around, fire test, jam, full-load driving, link losses, drift, long run.
　中文：阶跃、扰动、越界、连发、卡弹、满载行驶、链路断开、漂移、长跑。
**追问 2**：*When do you cut quality?* Never in safety; for cosmetic features, yes.
　中文：安全上绝不；外观类功能可以。
**关键词与易错点**：feature freeze、safety first、regression。

### Q6. Describe a time you identified a root cause that others missed.
**中文题意**：讲一次你发现了别人漏掉的根因。
**考察点**：深入分析。
**使用项目**：P5。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> On the navigation car the pose jumped when turning or reversing. The natural reaction was to retune the filter. I looked at the inputs instead. The jump size correlated with the yaw rate, which pointed to timing. Cross-correlating the gyro yaw rate with the scan-matching yaw rate gave a lag of about 60 milliseconds, because the laser driver stamped each scan with its arrival time. Then a reverse test showed the odometry kept moving forward, because the speed arrived as an unsigned magnitude. Fixing the stamp, the sign and the frame conventions made the steps between cycles less than 0.05 metres. The root causes were in the data, not in the algorithm, and I now run input checks first.

**中文翻译**：导航车转弯或倒车时位姿会跳。自然的反应是重调滤波器。我却去看输入。跳变大小与偏航率相关，指向时间问题。陀螺偏航率与扫描匹配偏航率互相关得到约 60 ms 滞后，因为激光驱动用到达时间给每帧打戳。接着倒车测试显示里程计一直向前，因为速度是无符号幅值。修正时间戳、符号和坐标系约定后，相邻周期阶跃小于 0.05 m。根因在数据而不在算法，我现在先做输入检查。
**追问 1**：*Why correlate yaw rates?* Both measure the same physical quantity with independent sensors; the lag at the correlation peak is the timing offset.
　中文：两者用独立传感器测同一物理量；互相关峰值处的滞后就是时间偏移。
**追问 2**：*How do you confirm the fix?* Re-run the correlation (offset under 5 ms) and a 3 metre reverse run against a tape measure (error about 5 centimetres).
　中文：重做互相关（偏移小于 5 ms）和 3 m 倒车对卷尺（误差约 5 cm）。
**关键词与易错点**：cross-correlation、timestamp、signed speed。

### Q7. How do you evaluate trade-offs between algorithms?
**中文题意**：你怎么评估算法之间的取舍？
**考察点**：选型依据。
**使用项目**：P5。
**English answer（口语，约 130 词）**：
> I list what the system needs, then compare candidates on those needs, including cost. For global planning, a plain A star path is not feasible for a car, so I used Hybrid A star, which puts heading and the turning radius into the search. For local planning I chose TEB because it expresses car kinematics and time-optimal behaviour with several homotopy classes. For tracking I wrote my own MPC, because it is the part most tied to the hardware: it handles steering angle, rate and speed constraints, and delay compensation, which geometric methods cannot. I paid in effort: tuning weights and solve-time budgeting. I also write down what would change my mind, for example a precise model and a torque interface for LQR.

**中文翻译**：我先列出系统需要什么，再按需求（含成本）比较候选方案。全局规划上，普通 A* 路径对车不可行，所以用 Hybrid A*，把朝向和转弯半径放进搜索。局部规划选 TEB，因为它能表达车的运动学和时间最优，并有多个同伦类。跟踪上我自写 MPC，因为它与硬件耦合最紧：能处理转角、转角速率和速度约束以及延迟补偿，几何方法做不到。我付出的代价是调权重和求解时间预算。我也写下什么会改变我的选择，比如有精确模型和力矩接口时用 LQR。
**追问 1**：*Why not a Pure Pursuit tracker?* It is simple but cannot handle constraints or delay in a unified way.
　中文：简单，但不能统一处理约束和延迟。
**追问 2**：*Which parts were existing packages?* Typical implementations: robot_localization EKF, nav2_amcl, Smac Hybrid A star, a ROS2 TEB port; MPC was my own node.
　中文：典型实现：robot_localization EKF、nav2_amcl、Smac Hybrid A*、ROS2 版 TEB；MPC 是我自己的节点。
**关键词与易错点**：feasibility、constraints、delay compensation。包名按"典型实现"说。

## 4. B 定位 bug 的方法

### Q8. Describe your debugging process from symptom to root cause.
**中文题意**：描述你从症状到根因的调试过程。
**考察点**：系统化排查。
**使用项目**：P7、P1。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> I use a fixed order and I change one thing at a time. First, the data: timestamps, frames, signs, units and data age. Second, the limits: is any actuator at its limit, what is the saturation fraction? Third, the model assumption: is the gain or the leg length used the one I think? Only then do I change the controller. I make the failure reproducible first, I write a hypothesis with an expected observation, and I test the cheapest one. On the wheel-legged robot this order traced the anomalies to state inconsistency, torque limits and ground-contact transitions, rather than to the control law. Each fix then became a regression item.

**中文翻译**：我用固定顺序，一次只改一件事。第一，数据：时间戳、坐标系、符号、单位和数据年龄。第二，限幅：执行器是否在限幅，饱和占比是多少？第三，模型假设：所用的增益或腿长是否是我认为的？之后才改控制器。我先让失败可复现，写下带预期观察的假设，并测试最便宜的那个。在轮腿机器人上，这个顺序把异常追溯到状态不一致、力矩限制和触地切换，而不是控制律。每个修复之后成为一个回归项。

### Q10. How do you use logs and plots to find a bug?

### Q13. How do you use controlled comparison experiments to confirm a cause?
**中文题意**：你怎么用对照实验确认原因？
**考察点**：实验设计。
**使用项目**：P1。
**English answer（口语，约 130 词）**：
> I change one thing, keep everything else fixed, and remove ordering effects. For the centre-of-mass feedforward on the ballbot I used the same fixed arm trajectory, a front raise from zero to about 0.78 radians and back over 16 seconds. **中文翻译**：我只改一件事，其余保持不变，并消除顺序效应。球形机器人的质心前馈对比用同一条固定臂轨迹：前平举从零到约 0.78 rad 再回来，16 s。我把前馈开和关交替运行，各五次，每次运行前重新初始化，并记录电池电压。第一次我先跑完所有"关"再跑所有"开"，"开"看起来更差只是因为电量下降、电机变热。交替运行去掉了这个影响。在我们的设置下，倾角峰值关时约 0.060、开时约 0.026，30 s 漂移约 0.30 m 对 0.10 m。

　中文：否则积分、偏置和滤波会带着上一次的状态。
**追问 2**：*Are these numbers measured?* They are the recorded values of our setup; I say about, and I can explain conditions.
　中文：它们是我们设置下的记录值；我说"约"，并能说明条件。
**关键词与易错点**：interleaved、fixed trajectory、re-initialise。

### Q14. How do you tell whether a failure is caused by software, hardware or the model?
**中文题意**：你怎么判断故障来自软件、硬件还是模型？

**使用项目**：P1、P7。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> I give each candidate a test that only that candidate can fail. 　中文：无风险检查实现，且每次结果相同。
**追问 2**：*What if two causes overlap?* Restore them one at a time and watch each contribution.
　中文：逐个恢复，看各自贡献。

### Q15. What would you do if the simulation works but the real robot does not?
**中文题意**：仿真能跑、真机不行怎么办？

**使用项目**：P2、P5。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> I list what the simulation does not contain and add them one at a time. In the navigation project, a controller that was fine at 1 metre per second in simulation shook at 0.5 on the car. ### Q16. How did you find a timing problem between sensors?
**中文题意**：你怎么发现传感器之间的时间问题？
**考察点**：时间同步方法。
**使用项目**：P5。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> The symptoms were wall smearing in RViz during turns, steps in the map-to-odom transform of 0.2 to 0.4 metres, and "extrapolation into the future" warnings. I measured with four tools. **中文翻译**：症状是转弯时 RViz 里墙体拖影、map→odom 出现 0.2–0.4 m 的阶跃，以及"extrapolation into the future"警告。我用四种工具测量。陀螺偏航率与扫描匹配偏航率互相关给出偏移，约 60 ms。转向脉冲显示 IMU、编码器和扫描曲线起跳时间不同。topic delay 工具显示 now 减去 header 时间戳。把同一个 bag 回放两次检查确定性。原因是激光驱动用到达时间打戳。我改为扫描起始加固定补偿，用 chrony 同步各机器，偏移降到 5 ms 以内。

　中文：取滑动窗口内偏移的最小值，因为到达时间不会早于真实发送时间。
**追问 2**：*Does TF tolerance fix it?* It hides the warning; it does not fix the misalignment.
　中文：它只隐藏警告，不能修复错位。

### Q17. How did you find a coordinate-frame error?

### Q19. How did you diagnose actuator saturation?
**中文题意**：你怎么诊断执行器饱和？
**考察点**：限幅诊断。
**使用项目**：P1、P7。
**English answer（口语，约 130 词）**：
> I use one metric: the fraction of cycles at the limit. On the wheel-legged robot, with a 0.5 metre per second step, it was above 60 percent, and the robot fell. On the ballbot, after a hand push the wheel command stayed at its limit of 8 for 0.4 seconds, a 35 percent saturation share in that push. Then I ask whether the saturation comes from too much gain, which saturates at a tiny tilt, or from too little actuator margin, which saturates near the maximum recoverable tilt. I used the LQR model for the second prediction, then reduced the tilt reference limit from 0.15 to 0.10 radians. The saturation fraction fell below 5 percent. I also check the battery voltage, because a one-volt drop shrinks the margin.

**中文翻译**：我用一个指标：贴限幅的周期占比。在轮腿机器人上，0.5 m/s 阶跃时它超过 60%，机器人倒下。在球形机器人上，手推之后轮命令在限幅 8 处停留 0.4 s，这次推动中饱和占比 35%。然后我问饱和来自增益过大（会在很小倾角就饱和）还是执行器余量不足（在最大可恢复倾角附近饱和）。第二种预测我用 LQR 模型，然后把倾角参考上限从 0.15 降到 0.10 rad，饱和占比降到 5% 以下。我也检查电池电压，因为 1 V 的压降会缩小余量。
**追问 1**：*Why does saturation make things worse?* The effective gain becomes limit divided by error, so a larger error gives a smaller gain; below about a quarter to a third of nominal gain, the loop can become unstable.
　中文：有效增益变成限幅除以误差，误差越大增益越小；低于名义增益的约四分之一到三分之一时闭环可能失稳。

**考察点**：定位振荡来源。
**使用项目**：P5、P1。
**English answer（口语，约 130 词）**：
> In the MPC the steering command shook at around 3 degrees, and the period of about 0.3 seconds lined up with TEB replanning. Two causes: TEB switching between homotopy classes made the reference jump, and the 120 millisecond actuation delay made the controller overreact to old states. Class switches fell from five to eight to at most one, and the mean cross-track error at 0.5 metres per second was about 0.06 metres. A low-pass filter would only add lag.

**中文翻译**：MPC 的转角指令抖动约 3°，约 0.3 s 的周期与 TEB 重规划对齐。两个原因：TEB 在同伦类之间切换使参考跳变；120 ms 的执行延迟使控制器对旧状态过度反应。我同时做了三件事，再逐个撤掉检查贡献：TEB 类别选择的滞回，新类别要明显更好才切换；延迟补偿，用已发指令把状态前推 120 ms；加大转角变化的权重，从 0.5 到 5。类别切换从五到八次降到至多一次，0.5 m/s 下平均横向误差约 0.06 m。低通滤波只会增加滞后。
**追问 1**：*What is the side effect of a large change weight?* Slower response in bends and some corner cutting; I raised the position weight to compensate.
　中文：弯道响应变慢、切内；我提高位置权重补偿。
**追问 2**：*On the ballbot?* A 1.5 hertz oscillation was fixed by raising the derivative gain to about half of the proportional gain.
　中文：球形机器人 1.5 Hz 振荡通过把微分增益提到比例增益约一半解决。
**关键词与易错点**：homotopy hysteresis、delay compensation、ablation。

### Q32. How did you improve the recovery speed of the launcher's friction wheels?
**中文题意**：你怎么改善摩擦轮的恢复速度？
**考察点**：控制律迭代与回归。
**使用项目**：P3。
**English answer（口语，约 135 词）**：
> The symptom was a muzzle-velocity spread of about plus or minus one metre per second in continuous fire. **中文翻译**：症状是连发时弹速散布约 ±1 m/s。我对每一发记录来自 CAN 反馈的轮速、裁判上报的弹速和电池电压。每发弹丸把轮速拖低 600–900 rpm，PID 需要约 120 ms 恢复，几乎用满 150 ms 的射击间隔。瓶颈是负载扰动下的恢复速度，而不是噪声。我从 PID 换到变增益 PID，再到带边界层的滑模，并加上慢速目标修正：高于 16.0 m/s 减 50 rpm，低于 15.7 加 50。恢复缩短到 60–80 ms，散布降到约 0.3。回归：30 发测试，并在低电量下重做。
**追问 1**：*Why a boundary layer?* To avoid chattering from the sign function.
　中文：避免符号函数引起的抖振。
**追问 2**：*Do the three controllers run together?* No, it is an iteration history; one is active.
　中文：不，是迭代历史；只有一个在运行。
**关键词与易错点**：per-shot log、recovery time、sliding mode、30-shot regression。弹速反馈来自裁判上报。

### Q33. How did you reduce noise in a velocity estimate?
**中文题意**：你怎么降低速度估计的噪声？
**考察点**：滤波与延迟权衡。
**使用项目**：P1、P7。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> On the ballbot the wheel-speed odometry gave a velocity that had spikes at rest, and the outer loop turned them into tilt-reference jitter. I measured the RMS and the spectrum of the velocity at rest for 60 seconds. The outer loop has a bandwidth of only about 0.15 hertz, so it tolerates delay; the inner loop does not. I added a first-order low-pass at 0.8 hertz, only on that slow path, with a delay of about 0.2 seconds. **中文翻译**：球形机器人用轮速里程得到的速度在静止时有毛刺，外环把它们变成倾角参考的抖动。我测了静止 60 s 速度的 RMS 和频谱。外环带宽只有约 0.15 Hz，能容忍延迟；内环不能。我只在这条慢路径上加了 0.8 Hz 一阶低通，延迟约 0.2 s。RMS 从约 0.020 降到 0.004 m/s，参考 RMS 从 0.004 降到 0.001 rad，而推扰恢复不变，说明延迟没进内环。在轮腿机器人上，我改用一维卡尔曼滤波，把滞后从约 20 降到 5 ms。
**追问 1**：*Why not filter the gyro the same way?* A lag of 0.2 seconds in the inner loop would destabilise it.
　中文：内环加 0.2 s 滞后会使它失稳。
**追问 2**：*What goes into the Kalman filter on the wheel-legged robot?* Acceleration for the prediction, and wheel speed minus the leg swing velocity for the measurement; the measurement noise is raised when slip is detected.
　中文：加速度做预测，轮速扣除腿摆动速度做量测；检测到打滑时放大量测噪声。
**关键词与易错点**：slow path、bandwidth ratio、Kalman。

### Q34. How did you reduce CAN bus load?
**中文题意**：你怎么降低 CAN 总线负载？
**考察点**：负载估算与发送策略。
**使用项目**：P3。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> Symptom: during vision integration the CAN send-failure count sometimes rose and motor feedback frames occasionally dropped. I counted full mailboxes and received frames, and I estimated the load. On the gimbal board five motors report at 1 kilohertz, which is about 55 percent, and if commands also went at 1 kilohertz the load would be about 78 percent, near 95 in the worst case. The bottleneck was commands, remote and board frames stacking in one cycle. I merged the commands of a motor group into one frame sent once per control cycle, about 333 hertz, spaced by one tick, and divided the board-to-board frames to 100 and 50 hertz. **中文翻译**：症状：视觉联调时 CAN 发送失败计数偶尔上升，电机反馈偶有丢帧。我统计了邮箱满的次数和接收帧数，并估算负载。云台板上五个电机以 1 kHz 上报，约 55%；若命令也按 1 kHz 发，负载约 78%，最坏接近 95%。瓶颈是命令、遥控和板间帧在一个周期里叠加。我把同组电机命令合并成一帧、每个控制周期（约 333 Hz）发一次、帧间隔一个 tick，并把板间帧分频到 100 和 50 Hz。负载从约 78% 降到 63%，发送失败归零。回归：运行十分钟，反馈帧间隔最大值小于 3 ms。
**追问 1**：*How do you calculate the load?* Frames per second times about 111 microseconds per frame at 1 megabit, divided by one second; five feedback frames at 1 kHz is about 55 percent.
　中文：每秒帧数乘以 1 Mbit/s 下约 111 µs/帧再除以 1 s；5 帧 × 1 kHz 约 55%。
**追问 2**：*Worst case?* With bit stuffing about 135 microseconds per frame, about 76 percent for the final design.
　中文：位填充约 135 µs/帧，最终设计最坏约 76%。
**关键词与易错点**：frames × frame time、merge commands、divide board frames。

### Q35. How did you raise the IK loop rate?

## 6. D 控制周期与频率的选择

### Q38. How did you choose the control-loop frequency, and what limited it?
**中文题意**：你怎么选控制环频率，什么限制了它？
**考察点**：三条判据：带宽、计算、通信。
**使用项目**：P7。
**English answer（口语，约 130 词）**：
> On the wheel-legged robot the loop ran at 500 hertz, a 2 millisecond period. Three criteria. First, bandwidth: the balance loop is a few hertz, so 500 hertz is two orders higher and discretisation loss is negligible. Second, compute: the control task takes about 0.3 milliseconds, about 15 percent of the period. Third, communication: on the joint bus, four commands and four replies are eight classic CAN frames, about 0.9 to 1.1 milliseconds, which is 44 to 54 percent of 2 milliseconds. At 1 kilohertz the compute would be fine but the bus would be almost full, and any retransmission would become torque delay. So the bus limited it. To go faster I would reduce frames or split the bus, not switch to CAN FD, because ours runs in classic mode.

**中文翻译**：轮腿机器人的回路是 500 Hz，周期 2 ms。三条判据。一，带宽：平衡回路只有几赫兹，500 Hz 高两个数量级，离散化损失可以忽略。二，计算：控制任务约 0.3 ms，约占周期的 15%。三，通信：关节总线上四条命令加四条回复共八个经典 CAN 帧，约 0.9–1.1 ms，占 2 ms 的 44%–54%。1 kHz 时计算没问题，但总线几乎占满，任何重发都会变成力矩延迟。所以限制来自总线。要更快我会减帧或拆总线，而不是换 CAN FD，因为我们跑的是经典模式。
**追问 1**：*Why not 250 hertz?* The delay from the zero-order hold doubles, and the leg-length loop with its 10 hertz crossover would lose more phase.
　中文：零阶保持延迟翻倍，10 Hz 穿越的腿长环会损失更多相位。
**追问 2**：*Is the CPU margin enough?* Yes, the worst-case execution time must stay below 1 millisecond, which I check in the regression.
　中文：够；最坏执行时间必须小于 1 ms，回归里检查。
**关键词与易错点**：bandwidth、compute 15%、bus 44–54%、classic CAN。不要说 CAN FD。

### Q39. Why is your ballbot control loop at 100 hertz and not 200?
**中文题意**：球形机器人控制环为什么 100 Hz 而不是 200 Hz？
**考察点**：不盲目提频。
**使用项目**：P1。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> Four reasons. The inner loop bandwidth is about 1 hertz, so 100 hertz is already a hundred times higher. The IMU and the wheel feedback both arrive at about 100 hertz, so running at 200 would only reuse the same data. The sample-and-hold delay is half a period: 5 milliseconds at 100 hertz, and 200 hertz would save just 2.5 milliseconds out of a worst-case budget of 25, because the big parts are the IMU transfer and the motor speed loop. And the node is Python, with limited jitter margin, so doubling the rate would shrink that margin. What would change my mind: a faster IMU and a faster motor loop, then the delay budget would justify it. In our setup the period jitter was about plus or minus 1 millisecond.

**中文翻译**：四个理由。内环带宽约 1 Hz，100 Hz 已是它的百倍。IMU 和轮反馈都约 100 Hz，跑 200 Hz 只会重复使用同一份数据。采样保持延迟是半个周期：100 Hz 时 5 ms，200 Hz 只省 2.5 ms，而最坏预算是 25 ms，大头是 IMU 传输和电机速度环。而且节点是 Python，抖动余量有限，提频会缩小余量。什么会改变我的想法：更快的 IMU 和更快的电机环，那时延迟预算才值得。在我们的设置里周期抖动约 ±1 ms。
**追问 1**：*Why not 50 hertz?* The discretisation difference grows (about 8 percent at a 20 millisecond period in the teaching table), and 20 milliseconds is already about a tenth of the doubling time of 0.17–0.27 seconds.
　中文：离散化偏差变大（教学表里 20 ms 周期约 8%），而 20 ms 已占倾角倍增时间 0.17–0.27 s 的约十分之一。
**追问 2**：*What dominates the delay?* IMU sampling and serial transfer (5–10 ms) and the motor speed loop (5–6 ms).
　中文：IMU 采样与串口传输（5–10 ms）和电机速度环（5–6 ms）。
**关键词与易错点**：reuse same data、half-period delay、jitter margin。

### Q40. How is your RoboMaster control period determined, and what limits it?
**中文题意**：你的 RoboMaster 控制周期怎么定，什么限制它？
**考察点**：诚实表述周期。
**使用项目**：P3。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：

The IMU task is separate, about 1 kilohertz. I do not say the control runs at 1 kilohertz. Why not faster: commands at 1 kilohertz would raise the gimbal board's CAN load from about 63 to 78 percent and double the CPU load, from about 47 percent. To go faster I would first fix the period with a fixed-delay call, measure the jitter, and only then raise the inner gains.

**中文翻译**：控制任务以约 3 ms、约 333 Hz 运行。它来自任务循环中的三处 1 tick 延时。之后我在这个周期下整定所有增益，所以它成了设计的一部分。IMU 任务是独立的，约 1 kHz。我不说控制是 1 kHz。为什么不更快：命令按 1 kHz 发会把云台板的 CAN 负载从约 63% 提到 78%，CPU 负载也会翻倍（现约 47%）。采样约为 35 Hz 内环带宽的十倍，处在常用规则的下沿。要更快，我会先用固定延时调用固定周期、测抖动，然后才提高内环增益。
**追问 1**：*Why is the period part of the controller?* The PID has no explicit time step, so gains are tied to the period; changing it means retuning.
　中文：PID 没有显式时间步，增益绑定周期；改周期就要重调。
**追问 2**：*What do you mean by 35 hertz?* The inner loop bandwidth, 30 to 40 hertz, with outer loops of about 4.5 hertz for yaw.
　中文：内环带宽 30–40 Hz；yaw 外环约 4.5 Hz。
**关键词与易错点**：about 3 ms、not 1 kHz、IMU task 1 kHz。绝不说"控制 1 kHz"。

### Q41. How did you choose the loop rates in the Future Power project?
**中文题意**：未来动力项目里各环的频率怎么选？
**考察点**：从物理与接口出发。
**使用项目**：P2。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：

**中文翻译**：我从物理和接口出发。电机内环在厂商固件里以 kHz 运行，我改不了。VR 数据约 90 Hz，所以发布超过 100 Hz 的目标不增加信息；IK 循环约 120 Hz，目标和从臂命令 100 Hz。主臂重力补偿约 200 Hz，因为手拖很慢，限制来自回包等待和 Python，而不是物理。底盘速度环与里程计共用 5 ms 周期。我还把 CAN 负载保持在 50% 以下：每臂 200 Hz 时约 36%。规则：命令频率不应超过信息频率。
**追问 1**：*How do you compute 36 percent?* 16 frames per cycle, about 111 microseconds each, 200 cycles per second.
　中文：每周期 16 帧、每帧约 111 µs、每秒 200 个周期。
**追问 2**：*What if a loop is slower than its configured step?* I use the measured time stamps in integrations and log the measured period.
　中文：积分用实测时间戳，并记录实测周期。
**关键词与易错点**：information rate、reply wait、CAN under 50%。

### Q42. How do the rates fit together in your navigation stack?
**中文题意**：导航栈里各部分的频率如何配合？
**考察点**：频率层级。
**使用项目**：P5。
**English answer（口语，约 130 词）**：

### Q47. How do you handle a 20 hertz controller using a 10 hertz reference?

**使用项目**：P5、P3。

### Q50. How do you measure jitter and execution time?
**中文题意**：你怎么测抖动和执行时间？
**考察点**：度量手段。
**使用项目**：P7、P1。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：

### Q51. How did you reduce jitter in an RTOS?
**中文题意**：你怎么在 RTOS 里降低抖动？
**考察点**：触发方式。
**使用项目**：P7。
**English answer（口语，约 120 词）**：
> On the wheel-legged robot the first version used a delay-until call, whose resolution is the system tick, 1 millisecond. A 2 millisecond period is two ticks, and the interval drifted between 1 and 3 milliseconds, about 1 millisecond peak-to-peak, which is 50 percent of the period. The symptom was an occasional "nodding" in standing balance. I changed it so that a 500 hertz hardware timer interrupt releases a semaphore that wakes the control task, and I gave the control task the highest priority and the logging task the lowest. **中文翻译**：轮腿机器人的第一版用了 delay-until 调用，其分辨率是系统节拍 1 ms。2 ms 周期是两个节拍，间隔在 1–3 ms 之间漂移，峰峰值约 1 ms，占周期 50%。症状是站立平衡时偶尔"点头"。我改成由 500 Hz 硬件定时器中断释放信号量唤醒控制任务，并让控制任务优先级最高、日志任务最低。中断里不做别的事。抖动降到约 0.1 ms，即 5%。我重跑了调参流程的第 5 到 9 步，确认没有回退。

　中文：中断要短；工作在任务里，优先级才起作用。
**追问 2**：*Did anything else change?* The log task priority was lowered, so that logging cannot delay a torque command.
　中文：日志任务优先级降低，使日志不会延迟力矩命令。

### Q52. How did you set task priorities in FreeRTOS?

**使用项目**：P7、P3。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：

　中文：低优先级任务持锁阻塞高优先级任务，同时中优先级抢占；我避免共享锁，临界区极小。

### Q53. How did you use real-time scheduling on Linux for the ballbot?
**中文题意**：球形机器人上你怎么用 Linux 实时调度？
**考察点**：进程调度与绑核。
**使用项目**：P1。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> The control node and the base driver ran with SCHED_FIFO priorities 50 and 45, pinned to dedicated cores, and the IMU driver at 40. 　中文：让日志、视觉或写盘不会与控制任务争用同一个核。

### Q54. What is priority inversion and how do you avoid it?
**中文题意**：什么是优先级反转，你怎么避免？
**考察点**：实时基础。
**使用项目**：P6、P1。

Where I must lock, I use a mutex with priority inheritance and keep the critical section to a pointer swap. And I keep blocking I/O and big allocations out of the control thread. At Kaihong, the same rule applied to the control threads in the platform process, in our design.

**中文翻译**：低优先级任务持有锁，高优先级任务等待它，中优先级任务又抢占了低优先级任务，于是高优先级任务被中优先级任务间接延迟。我有三种避免方式。完全避免共享锁：控制线程把日志写入无锁环形缓冲，低优先级线程负责取出落盘。必须加锁时，用带优先级继承的互斥量，临界区只做指针交换。并且不让阻塞 I/O 和大内存分配进入控制线程。球形机器人上，回调只存最新消息引用，计算在定时器里做。在深开鸿，同样规则适用于平台进程里的控制线程（我们的设计）。
**追问 1**：*Why a ring buffer for logs?* The control thread never waits for the disk.
　中文：控制线程永远不等磁盘。
**追问 2**：*Is it a problem with a plain Linux kernel?* It can be; priority-inheritance mutexes exist for that.
　中文：可能是；优先级继承互斥量就是为此设计的。
**关键词与易错点**：priority inheritance、lock-free ring buffer、store reference only。

### Q57. Why do you sleep to absolute times in a periodic loop?
**中文题意**：周期循环里为什么按绝对时刻睡眠？
**考察点**：避免累计漂移。
**使用项目**：P2、P6。
**English answer（口语，约 110 词）**：
> If I write "sleep for the period" at the end of each cycle, then the compute time and the wake-up latency are added to every cycle, so the real period is longer than I intended, and errors accumulate. If I compute the next wake-up time as the start time plus k periods and sleep until that absolute time, a late wake-up is not carried over: the next cycle shortens to catch up. So the average period is accurate. In the Future Power hardware node I used this, and in the Kaihong control threads, in our design, I use sleep-until with a monotonic clock. I also record the actual period and the execution time every cycle, and flag two consecutive cycles over 1.5 times the period.

**中文翻译**：如果我在每个周期末尾写"睡一个周期"，计算时间和唤醒延迟会加到每个周期里，真实周期比预想的长，误差累积。如果我把下一次唤醒时间算成起始时间加 k 个周期，并睡到那个绝对时刻，则一次迟到的唤醒不会累积：下一个周期会缩短来追赶。所以平均周期是准确的。未来动力的硬件节点用了这种方法，深开鸿的控制线程（在我们的设计里）也用带单调时钟的 sleep-until。我还在每个周期记录实际周期和执行时间，连续两个周期超过 1.5 倍周期就标记。
**追问 1**：*Why a monotonic clock?* Wall-clock jumps from time synchronisation would disturb the schedule.
　中文：校时造成的墙上时钟跳变会扰乱调度。
**追问 2**：*Does absolute-time sleeping remove jitter?* No, it removes drift; jitter needs priorities, isolation and a hardware timer.
　中文：不消除抖动，只消除漂移；抖动需要优先级、隔离和硬件定时器。
**关键词与易错点**：sleep_until、accumulated drift、monotonic clock。

The wheel bus has two motors, four frames, 22 to 27 percent. If all six motors shared one bus, 12 frames would take 67 to 81 percent, too little margin, which is why there are two buses. **中文翻译**：我们的 FDCAN 外设工作在经典模式，1 Mbps，8 字节，无比特率切换。关节总线有四个电机，每周期八帧，0.89–1.08 ms，占 2 ms 周期的 44%–54%。轮总线两个电机，四帧，22%–27%。若六个电机共用一条总线，12 帧要占 67%–81%，余量太小，所以分成两路。要更快，我会减帧（比如降低回复频率或发更少字段），或进一步拆总线。在这条硬件路径上换成 CAN FD 不是选项。
**追问 1**：*What does a retransmission do?* It adds delay in the torque command, which looks like a phase lag.
　中文：重发会给力矩命令增加延迟，表现为相位滞后。
**追问 2**：*How do you detect frame loss?* Count TX failures and RX overflows per bus and write them in the log.
　中文：每路总线统计发送失败和接收溢出并写入日志。
**关键词与易错点**：classic CAN、no BRS、8 frames、44–54%。不要说 CAN FD。

### Q66. How did you check the CAN load of the RoboMaster boards?
**中文题意**：RoboMaster 板的 CAN 负载你怎么核算？

### Q67. How did you check the CAN load of the Future Power arms?
**中文题意**：未来动力手臂的 CAN 负载怎么核算？

**使用项目**：P2。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> Each arm has eight motors. Each cycle sends eight commands and receives eight replies, 16 frames, about 1.78 milliseconds on the bus. At 200 hertz that is about 36 percent, at 100 hertz about 18. **追问 2**：*How do you notice a lost reply?* Warn after more than three missing cycles and disable the arm after ten.
　中文：缺失超过三个周期告警，十个周期失能整条臂。

### Q68. How did you check the serial link bandwidth between the PC and the lower controller?

**追问 2**：*Why a version byte?* A lower-controller format change then fails loudly instead of silently.

## 10. H 仿真到实机

### Q69. How do you validate a controller before putting it on a robot?
**中文题意**：把控制器上机前你怎么验证？
**考察点**：分层验证。
**使用项目**：P1、P7。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> In three layers with different purposes. First, a model-level simulation, with a normalised NumPy model on the ballbot, to check signs, magnitudes and trends: for example, one degree of tilt offset moves the robot about 0.2 metres. Second, a MuJoCo environment using the same URDF to check joint axes, centre-of-mass direction and arm trajectories; for the wheel-legged robot, software-in-the-loop with the firmware's algorithm code compiled on a PC and limits, quantisation and delay added. Third, the real robot: hanging single-axis tests, a gantry or safety rope at low gain, then the floor. Simulation gives predictions, the robot gives the verdict. I never copy simulated gains to the robot.

**中文翻译**：分三层，目的各不相同。一，模型级仿真：球形机器人用归一化 NumPy 模型检查符号、量级和趋势，如 1° 倾角偏置使机器人移动约 0.2 m。二，用同一 URDF 的 MuJoCo 环境检查关节轴、质心方向和臂轨迹；轮腿机器人上用软件在环：固件算法代码在 PC 上编译，并加入限幅、量化和延迟。三，真机：悬空单轴测试、低增益的龙门架或保护绳，然后落地。仿真给预言，机器人给裁决。我从不把仿真增益拷到机器人上。
**追问 1**：*What does the first layer not check?* Friction, slip, motor dynamics, noise and arm multi-body dynamics.
　中文：摩擦、打滑、电机动态、噪声和臂的多体动力学。
**追问 2**：*Why software-in-the-loop?* It separates "the design is wrong" from "the implementation is wrong".
　中文：把"设计错"和"实现错"分开。
**关键词与易错点**：signs/magnitudes/trends、SIL、hanging → rope → floor。

### Q70. What differences between simulation and the real car mattered most?
**中文题意**：仿真与真车之间哪些差异最重要？
**考察点**：已知缺口。
**使用项目**：P5。
**English answer（口语，约 120 词）**：
> Four mattered. A delay of about 120 milliseconds in steering, which the simulation did not have. Servo backlash of about 1.5 degrees, which made small steering reversals "nest" and the controller shake on straight sections. Sensor noise and time stamp errors, such as the 60 millisecond laser offset. And battery voltage: when it was low, the same speed command gave a lower speed, so the longitudinal tracking error grew. I handled them one by one: simulation with delay, backlash and noise from real bags; delay compensation in the controller; the lower controller crossing the dead zone quickly and the controller using the measured steering angle; and a lower speed cap with a voltage log. Before each real run I also note the battery voltage.

**中文翻译**：四点重要。转向约 120 ms 的延迟，仿真里没有。舵机约 1.5° 的回差，使小角度往复时转向"窝"，直行段控制器抖动。传感器噪声与时间戳误差，如 60 ms 的激光偏移。电池电压：电压低时同一速度指令给出更低速度，纵向跟踪误差变大。我逐个处理：仿真加入延迟、回差和取自真实 bag 的噪声；控制器做延迟补偿；下位机快速越过死区，控制器使用实测转角；降低速度上限并记录电压。每次上车前我也记下电池电压。
**追问 1**：*How was the backlash handled?* Backlash compensation in the lower controller when crossing zero, a larger steering-change weight in the MPC, and measured steering angle as a state.
　中文：下位机过零时回差补偿，MPC 里加大转角变化权重，并用实测转角作状态。
**追问 2**：*What did the simulation not cover?* Dynamic obstacles, glass, slip on different floors.
　中文：动态障碍、玻璃、不同地面打滑。
**关键词与易错点**：120 ms、1.5° backlash、battery voltage。

### Q71. Why did you make the simulation share the real timing in the RoboMaster project?
**中文题意**：RoboMaster 项目里为什么让仿真与实机共享时间结构？
**考察点**：仿真同构。
**使用项目**：P3。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> A sliding-mode gain that I tuned in an ideal 1 millisecond simulation with no delay chattered badly on the real robot. The reason is that the real system has a 3 millisecond period, about 2 milliseconds of feedback delay and a current loop with a time constant of about 4 milliseconds. So I made the simulation share that time structure: 3 millisecond period, 2 millisecond delay, a first-order current loop, and the same command limits. In that simulation I enlarged the boundary layer, retuned, and then needed only a small adjustment on the robot. A simulation with a different time structure only tells me how a different controller behaves.

**中文翻译**：我在理想的 1 ms、无延迟仿真里调好的滑模增益，在真机上抖得很厉害。原因是真实系统有 3 ms 周期、约 2 ms 反馈延迟，以及时间常数约 4 ms 的电流环。所以我让仿真共享这个时间结构：3 ms 周期、2 ms 延迟、一阶电流环、同样的命令限幅。在那个仿真里我加大了边界层并重调，之后真机上只需小幅调整。时间结构不同的仿真只能告诉我另一个控制器的表现。
**追问 1**：*What is the boundary layer for?* It replaces the sign function by a saturation to reduce chattering.
　中文：用饱和函数代替符号函数以减小抖振。
**追问 2**：*Where did the 4 ms time constant come from?* A first-order approximation of the current loop in our setup.
　中文：我们设置里电流环的一阶近似。
**关键词与易错点**：same time structure、boundary layer、delay in sim。

### Q72. What is software-in-the-loop, and how did you use it?
**中文题意**：什么是软件在环，你怎么用的？
**考察点**：设计与实现分离。
**使用项目**：P7。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> I wrote the algorithm layer, the estimation, LQR, leg-length PID and the VMC, as pure C functions that take structures in and return structures, with no hardware access. The system layer, with the tasks, interrupts, CAN packing and protections, calls them. Because of that split, the same algorithm code compiles on the PC for simulation and into the firmware. In the software-in-the-loop, I connect that code to a nonlinear simulation with limits, quantisation and delay, and compare the result with the offline design. If it disagrees, the implementation is wrong; if it agrees but the robot differs, the model is the issue. I also test the gain polynomial against an offline Riccati solution at five leg lengths, with error below 1 percent.

**中文翻译**：我把算法层——估计、LQR、腿长 PID 和 VMC——写成纯 C 函数，输入结构体、输出结构体，不访问硬件。带任务、中断、CAN 打包和保护的系统层调用它们。由于这种分层，同一份算法代码既能在 PC 上编译做仿真，也能编进固件。软件在环里，我把这份代码接到带限幅、量化和延迟的非线性仿真上，并与离线设计对比。如果不一致，是实现错；如果一致但机器人表现不同，问题在模型。我还在五个腿长点上用离线 Riccati 解检验增益多项式，误差小于 1%。
**追问 1**：*What bug did the single-point check find?* A mix of millimetres and metres in the gain fit.
　中文：增益拟合里毫米与米混用。
**追问 2**：*What does the pure function style give?* Easier tests and no hidden state.
　中文：更容易测试，没有隐藏状态。
**关键词与易错点**：pure C functions、SIL、single-point consistency test。

### Q73. What can simulation never tell you?
**中文题意**：仿真永远无法告诉你什么？
**考察点**：边界意识。
**使用项目**：P5、P1。
**English answer（口语，约 110 词）**：
> Simulation can falsify but not confirm. If a controller fails in simulation, it will fail on the robot. If it passes, I still need the real test. What it cannot show: unmodelled effects like friction and backlash, battery sag, slip on different floors, real sensor behaviour such as dropped frames or time stamp offsets, cable strain, and dynamic obstacles or reflective glass for the navigation car. On the ballbot, the sign of the feedforward and the URDF mass errors were discovered only on the real robot with static single-arm tests. So I add the known missing effects to the simulation one by one, and I state what remains uncovered.

**中文翻译**：仿真能证伪，不能证实。如果控制器在仿真里失败，在机器人上也会失败；如果通过了，仍需要真实测试。它无法显示：没建模的效应，如摩擦与回差、电池压降、不同地面的打滑、真实传感器行为（丢帧、时间戳偏移）、线缆拉扯，以及导航车的动态障碍和反光玻璃。球形机器人上，前馈符号和 URDF 质量误差只有在真机上通过静态单臂测试才被发现。所以我把已知缺失的效应逐个加入仿真，并说明哪些仍未覆盖。
**追问 1**：*Example of URDF error?* Masses were checked by weighing to within about 5 percent; the centre-of-mass reference point was the ball centre.
　中文：质量用称重核对到约 5% 以内；质心参考点取球心。
**追问 2**：*What do you write down?* A short list of what the simulation does not cover.
　中文：一份简短的"仿真未覆盖项"清单。
**关键词与易错点**：falsify not confirm、known gaps。

### Q74. How do you manage parameters across experiments?

**使用项目**：P7。

### Q77. How do you keep thresholds consistent between a server and a device?
**中文题意**：服务端和设备端的阈值怎么保持一致？
**考察点**：配置管理。
**使用项目**：P4。

### Q83. How do you use fault injection?

## 13. K 安全与保护

### Q87. What safeguards did you implement for a robot that can fall?
**中文题意**：对会摔倒的机器人你做了哪些保护？
**考察点**：分层保护。
**使用项目**：P1。
**English answer（口语，约 135 词）**：
> On the ballbot I had layers. First, an enable state machine with a five-step initialisation, so every enable starts clean and ramps in over about 0.3 seconds. Second, three levels of limiting: the tilt reference bound of 0.10 radians, the overall wheel-command limit scaled to keep direction, and a rate limit per cycle. Third, fall detection: roll or pitch above 0.5 radians for 5 cycles, 50 milliseconds, zeroes the output, resets the controller and locks a fault state; recovery is manual, with the robot upright and still for about a second, and then a re-initialisation. Fourth, data checks: age over 50 milliseconds for IMU and wheels, NaN checks. Fifth, a 100 millisecond driver watchdog. Last, the physical layer: lifted tests, a gantry strap, a wireless stop and a contactor.

**中文翻译**：球形机器人上我有多层保护。一，使能状态机和五步初始化，每次使能都从干净状态开始，并在约 0.3 s 内斜坡放出。二，三级限幅：0.10 rad 的倾角参考上限、保持方向的轮命令整体限幅、每周期斜率限制。三，跌倒检测：roll 或 pitch 超过 0.5 rad 持续 5 个周期（50 ms）则输出清零、复位控制器并锁定故障状态；恢复是人工的：机器人扶正并静止约 1 s，然后重新初始化。四，数据检查：IMU 和轮反馈数据年龄超过 50 ms、NaN 检查。五，驱动侧 100 ms 看门狗。最后是物理层：悬空测试、龙门架吊带、无线急停和接触器。
**追问 1**：*Why not restart automatically?* A fall may have damaged something; a person must check before the robot moves again.
　中文：跌倒可能损坏东西；机器人再动之前必须由人检查。
**追问 2**：*Which of these were most useful?* The enable state machine and the watchdog, because edges and dead controllers are where danger hides.
　中文：使能状态机和看门狗，因为危险藏在状态边沿和控制器死亡里。
**关键词与易错点**：state machine、three-level limiting、0.5 rad/5 cycles、manual recovery。以上数值为设计取值；源码快照里有两处较弱，说"设计要求如此"。

### Q88. How do you design a watchdog?
**中文题意**：你怎么设计看门狗？
**考察点**：分层、位置、测试。
**使用项目**：P5、P2、P7。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> The watchdog goes downstream, because the thing that dies could be the controller. On the navigation car: the lower controller zeroes the speed if no command arrives within 200 milliseconds, four MPC periods; the bridge node sends zero speed itself if the MPC is silent for 100 milliseconds; and a hardware emergency stop sits on the motor enable, independent of software. At Future Power: a target older than 100 milliseconds freezes the arm and older than 500 disables it; the base zeroes on 300 milliseconds without a velocity command. On the wheel-legged robot, a 20 millisecond hardware watchdog fed by the control task resets the MCU, which then comes up with zero torque. Each watchdog gets two tests: fed, and timeout.

**中文翻译**：看门狗放在下游，因为死掉的可能是控制器本身。导航车上：下位机在 200 ms（四个 MPC 周期）内没收到命令就零速；桥接节点在 MPC 静默 100 ms 时自己发零速；硬件急停串在电机使能上，不依赖软件。未来动力：目标超过 100 ms 则冻结手臂，超过 500 ms 失能；底盘在 300 ms 没有速度命令时归零。轮腿机器人：由控制任务喂的 20 ms 硬件看门狗复位 MCU，复位后以零力矩启动。每个看门狗有两个测试：被喂、和超时。

### Q89. How do you handle sensor failure or invalid data?

### Q91. How do you design a state machine for a pump or actuator and avoid chattering?

### Q92. How do you limit power or current without destroying performance?

### Q93. How do you ensure safe behaviour when a high-level planner outputs a wrong command?

**English answer（口语，约 130 词）**：

### Q97. How do you capture the moments before a rare failure?
**中文题意**：怎么捕捉罕见故障发生前的瞬间？
**考察点**：触发式记录。
**使用项目**：P7、P1。
**English answer（口语，约 120 词）**：

**中文翻译**：RAM 里的环形缓冲。轮腿机器人的连续日志是 100 Hz，但偶发摔倒的关键瞬间落在两行之间。所以我在 RAM 里保留最近 2 s、500 Hz 的数据（约一千条），触发条件出现时冻结：倾倒保护、触地切换或饱和占比过高。数据由低优先级任务分小块通过串口导出，所以不会延迟控制。串口占用保持在约 5%。球形机器人上我用类似的最近 1 s 缓冲并带原因码，否则保护触发后我只知道它倒了。这样每次故障都有它之前的两秒。
**追问 1**：*Why not log at 500 hertz all the time?* It would load the serial port and disk; the ring buffer costs RAM only.
　中文：会占串口和磁盘；环形缓冲只花 RAM。
**追问 2**：*What goes in the dump?* Tilt, rates, commands, data age, mode flags and the reason code.
　中文：倾角、角速度、命令、数据年龄、模式标志和原因码。

### Q99. How do you make a decision chain traceable and replayable?
**中文题意**：怎么让决策链可追溯、可回放？
**考察点**：trace 与回放。
**使用项目**：P4。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> Every decision is written as one JSON line with a trace id, a summary of the input snapshot, the rule or model path taken, the verdict, and the configuration version. Observations, sensor snapshots and user commands are unified into one state, so each line refers to the same state. For debugging I replay the recorded observation sequence through the decision chain offline, and for model calls I replay the recorded responses, so the same input gives the same output. ### Q101. How do you structure code so that it can be reused across hardware?

### Q105. How do you handle logging and error handling in embedded code?
**中文题意**：嵌入式代码里日志和错误处理怎么做？
**考察点**：不阻塞控制。
**使用项目**：P3。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> The control task only writes to a ring buffer: no formatting, no blocking. A low-priority task outputs in batches, with a bandwidth far below the bus, so it cannot disturb control. Critical events, such as offline detection, jams, scaling triggers and mode changes, go into an event log with time stamps. For errors, the safe state comes first: a relax state with zero current, cleared integrators and history, and the CAN heartbeat kept. Offline timeouts: 50 milliseconds for motors and 100 for remote, vision and the board link. Power-up default is relax, and the robot must be switched to a safe position before enabling. Competition builds use a compact log, bench builds the full log, switched by a macro.

**中文翻译**：控制任务只写环形缓冲：不格式化、不阻塞。低优先级任务批量输出，带宽远低于总线，所以不会干扰控制。关键事件，如离线检测、卡弹、缩放触发和模式切换，写入带时间戳的事件日志。对错误，安全状态优先：RELAX 状态——电流置零、清积分和历史，并保持 CAN 心跳。离线超时：电机 50 ms，遥控、视觉和板间 100 ms。上电默认 RELAX，必须先拨到安全位才能使能。比赛版本用精简日志，台架版本用完整日志，用宏切换。
**追问 1**：*What happened when you stopped at a breakpoint?* The offline detector declared all motors offline after resuming, so I disabled the detection while debugging or used semihosting output.
　中文：断点停住后恢复时所有电机被判离线；调试时关闭检测或使用半主机输出。
**追问 2**：*Why keep the heartbeat in relax?* The motors and the other board still need to see that the board is alive.
　中文：电机和另一块板仍需知道这块板是活的。
**关键词与易错点**：ring buffer、relax、offline timeouts、macro。

### Q108. Why did you choose gain-scheduled LQR with VMC rather than a cascade PID for the wheel-legged robot?
**中文题意**：轮腿机器人为什么选增益调度 LQR + VMC 而不是级联 PID？
**考察点**：选型比较。
**使用项目**：P7。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> Leg-length change is the core requirement. With cascade PID, the coupling between pitch, speed and leg angle must be split by experience, and after a leg-length change the whole set needs retuning. A fixed-gain LQR handles several states together but loses stability margin when the leg length is far from the design point. Gain-scheduled LQR keeps the controller linear and cheap: I solve Riccati equations offline at several leg lengths, fit each of the 12 gains as a cubic polynomial, 48 coefficients, and evaluate them on the microcontroller. The VMC decouples the support force from the joint torques. MPC and whole-body control were too expensive for 500 hertz on an STM32H7 and harder to explain. The price is offline work and model error that tuning has to absorb.

**中文翻译**：腿长变化是核心需求。用级联 PID，俯仰、速度和腿角之间的耦合要靠经验拆分，腿长变化后整套参数要重调。固定增益 LQR 同时处理多个状态，但腿长远离设计点时稳定裕度下降。增益调度 LQR 保持控制器线性且便宜：我离线在若干腿长点解 Riccati 方程，把 12 个增益各拟合成三次多项式（共 48 个系数），在单片机上求值。VMC 把支撑力与关节力矩解耦。MPC 和全身控制在 STM32H7 上 500 Hz 太贵，也更难解释。代价是离线工作和需要靠调参吸收的模型误差。
**追问 1**：*What protects against the polynomial being wrong near the ends?* Clamp the leg length to the fit interval, denser nodes near the ends, and a single-point consistency test at five leg lengths.
　中文：腿长限制在拟合区间内、两端节点更密、五个腿长点的一致性测试。
**追问 2**：*What did you fix after the first fits?* The residual at the ends was up to 8 percent; denser nodes brought it to about 2.
　中文：两端残差曾达 8%；加密节点后约 2%。
**关键词与易错点**：Riccati offline、cubic fit 48 coefficients、clamp。LQR 在 P7 是主控制器；P1 不是。

### Q110. Should you use an on-board AHRS or write your own attitude filter?

In my CV I only claim the filter for the RoboMaster robot, not for the ballbot.

**中文翻译**：这取决于风险在哪里。球形机器人上，团队已有带板载融合的 IMU 模块的可用驱动。自己写滤波要处理零偏、磁干扰和安装标定，收益很小。我把精力花在让我吃亏的地方：数据有效性检查（数据年龄、四元数模长）和使能时的偏置初始化。RoboMaster 项目里我在 BMI088 上用 Mahony 滤波，比例增益 0.5、积分关闭，因为那里我需要相对 yaw、恒温的 IMU，而磁力计未启用。在 CV 里我只对 RoboMaster 机器人声称滤波，不对球形机器人声称。
**追问 1**：*Why is the Mahony integral off?* Bias is estimated at power-up from 2000 samples with heater temperature control, and yaw is only used relative.
　中文：偏置由 2000 个样本上电估计、配合恒温；yaw 只做相对使用。
**追问 2**：*What is the yaw drift?* About 1 degree per minute order, around 0.017 degrees per second, at rest.
　中文：静置约 1°/min 量级（约 0.017°/s）。
**关键词与易错点**：on-board AHRS vs own、Mahony Kp 0.5、yaw relative。P1 不说自己写了滤波。

### Q111. Describe how you take a project from zero to running on hardware.
**中文题意**：描述你如何把一个项目从零做到上硬件。
**考察点**：完整流程。
**使用项目**：P4、P2。
**English answer（口语，约 130 词）**：
> I follow a fixed shape. First, turn the goal into measurable requirements and acceptance tests. Second, build the safest part first and test it on a bench. In the gardening project that was the Hi3861 pump loop, offline-capable, before any vision or AI. Third, add layers, replacing one neighbour at a time: use simulators or replays for the others, and swap in real hardware only one component at a time. So when it breaks I know which layer is wrong. Fourth, always single joint, low gain, no load first on any arm or motor, with a checklist and someone at the stop. Fifth, write regression scripts early. Sixth, document the interface and the limits. In the Future Power internship I built the base first because its chain is shortest.

**中文翻译**：我遵循固定的形状。一，把目标变成可衡量的需求和验收测试。二，先做最安全的部分并在台架上测试。园艺项目里那是不依赖网络的 Hi3861 水泵回路，先于任何视觉或 AI。三，逐层添加，每次替换一个邻居：其他的用模拟器或回放，只逐个换成真实硬件，这样出问题时知道是哪一层。四，任何手臂或电机总是先单关节、低增益、空载，有清单、有人守急停。五，早点写回归脚本。六，写接口和限制文档。未来动力实习里我先做了底盘，因为它的链路最短。
**追问 1**：*Why simulators first?* Because I know which layer is wrong when only one is real.
　中文：因为一次只有一层是真实的时候，知道哪层出错。
**追问 2**：*Where did you stop?* In the gardening system I reached V4, the full real-hardware loop, but I did not do a long-duration test (V5).
　中文：园艺系统到达 V4（实机完整闭环），没有做 V5 长时间测试。
**关键词与易错点**：safest part first、one real neighbour at a time、V4 not V5。

### Q112. How do you handle changing requirements?
**中文题意**：需求变化时你怎么办？
**考察点**：灵活与控制。
**使用项目**：P6、P3。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> I make the change explicit, estimate its effect on the interfaces and the tests, and decide with my mentor. At Kaihong a request to extend the proposal validity touched the de-duplication window and replay risk, so the answer was a validity per action type, written in the interface note with the reason. In the RoboMaster team, an operator wanted a different sensitivity the day before the match; I did not change firmware, I made sensitivity a runtime remote parameter inside a safe range. I keep a change small, reversible and tested by the regression list. And I ask what the real need is before I choose the implementation. Written decisions avoid the same debate twice.

**中文翻译**：我把变更显式化，估计它对接口和测试的影响，并与导师决定。在深开鸿，延长提案有效期的请求涉及去重窗口和重放风险，所以答案是按动作类型设有效期，并把理由写进接口说明。RoboMaster 战队里，操作手赛前一天想要不同的灵敏度；我没改固件，而是把灵敏度做成安全范围内的运行时遥控参数。我让变更小、可逆、并由回归清单测试。选实现方式之前，我先问真实需求是什么。写下来的决定避免同样的争论发生两次。
**追问 1**：*What if the change is large?* Split it, deliver the safe part first, and re-estimate.
　中文：拆分、先交付安全部分、重新估计。
**追问 2**：*How do you keep the history?* In the interface note and the commit message.
　中文：在接口说明和提交信息里。
**关键词与易错点**：explicit change、real need、runtime parameter。

## 17. O 与硬件同事协调及赛场现场应急

### Q113. How did you work with mechanical or electrical engineers when the hardware limited your algorithm?
**中文题意**：硬件限制了你的算法时，你怎么与机械/电气同事协作？
**考察点**：用数据协商。
**使用项目**：P1、P3。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> On the ballbot the arm colleague wanted faster, larger motions, but the base had limited margin. I did not argue. I estimated that for the slow trajectories the dynamic term is only about 3.6 percent of the static moment, but that fast motions would need an acceleration feedforward, and I showed the saturation fraction on the plots. We agreed to use the slow trajectories first and revisit fast ones after I added the acceleration term. On the RoboMaster gimbal, mechanical wanted the maximum pitch travel; I showed the motor current rising at the stop, and we agreed on a soft limit two degrees inside the mechanical stop with a damper added. Safety rules, like the three-stage test, were not negotiable.

**中文翻译**：球形机器人上，臂同事想要更快、更大的动作，但底盘余量有限。我没有争论。我估算出慢轨迹的动态项只占静态力矩的约 3.6%，但快动作需要加速度前馈，并在图上展示了饱和占比。我们约定先用慢轨迹，等我加上加速度项后再评估快动作。RoboMaster 云台上，机械想要最大的 pitch 行程；我展示了电流在限位处上升，我们约定在机械限位内收 2° 设软限位并加缓冲。安全规则（如三阶段测试）不容商量。
**追问 1**：*What was in the plots?* The saturation fraction and the tilt peak for slow and fast trajectories.
　中文：慢与快轨迹的饱和占比和倾角峰值。
**追问 2**：*What did you learn?* Put numbers on the table, agree the interfaces first, keep safety out of the negotiation.
　中文：用数字说话、先约接口、安全不参与谈判。
**关键词与易错点**：3.6% dynamic term、soft limit、non-negotiable safety。

### Q114. How do you handle an interface or protocol change by a hardware colleague?
**中文题意**：硬件同事改了接口或协议，你怎么处理？
**考察点**：接口登记。
**使用项目**：P7、P5。
**English answer（口语，约 115 词）**：
> After a motor firmware update on the wheel-legged robot, the encoding range changed and the torque came out scaled and misplaced. I identified it with a frame consistency test: encode a known torque, decode it, and compare. We then created an interface change register: any change in the motor protocol, mechanical dimensions or remote command format is written there with the date and the owner, and when the firmware version changes, the frame consistency test must be run again. On the navigation car the same idea became a protocol version byte, so a mismatch fails loudly. The goal is to make forgetting hard, not to blame anyone.

**中文翻译**：轮腿机器人上一次电机固件更新后，编码范围变了，力矩输出被缩放和错位。我用帧一致性测试识别了它：编码一个已知力矩、解码并比较。随后我们建立了接口变更登记表：电机协议、机械尺寸或遥控指令格式的任何改动都登记日期和负责人；固件版本变化时必须重跑帧一致性测试。导航车上同样的想法变成协议版本字节，不匹配时明显失败。目标是让人难以忘记，而不是指责谁。
**追问 1**：*What does the consistency test check?* Known value in, same value out, at the range edges as well.
　中文：已知值进、同值出，包括范围边界。
**追问 2**：*How does the MIT frame limit resolution?* Velocity is 12-bit over ±30 rad/s, about 0.015 rad/s per step, which shows as steps at low speed.
　中文：速度 12 位、范围 ±30 rad/s，每级约 0.015 rad/s，低速时表现为台阶。
**关键词与易错点**：change register、frame consistency test、version byte。

### Q115. How do you debug on a competition day?
**中文题意**：比赛当天你怎么调试？
**考察点**：现场纪律。
**使用项目**：P3。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> With a fixed checklist, and with the rule that between matches I only repair and never change algorithms. On arrival: check the wiring, the connectors and the CAN terminators, because loose wiring is the most common cause of offline motors. In the waiting area: power up and stand still for two minutes to check the yaw drift and the IMU temperature; check motor directions and zero positions; ten shots to verify the muzzle speed in 15.7 to 16.0 metres per second; safe-state tests like unplugging the remote and vision; and check the firmware hash in the log header. After the match I download the logs and write an event, cause and action note, and turn useful events into regression items.

**中文翻译**：用固定清单，并遵守规则：场间只修不改算法。到场：检查接线、接头和 CAN 终端电阻，因为接线松是电机离线最常见的原因。候场区：上电静置两分钟，检查 yaw 漂移和 IMU 温度；检查电机方向和零位；10 发验证弹速在 15.7–16.0 m/s 内；做拔遥控和视觉等安全态测试；核对日志头里的固件哈希。比赛后下载日志，写"事件—原因—处理"记录，把有用的事件变成回归项。
**追问 1**：*Why not change parameters in the break?* Each change is a new unknown I cannot validate.
　中文：每个改动都是我无法验证的新未知。
**追问 2**：*Why check the battery states?* Muzzle speed depends on voltage; full voltage once gave 16.2 metres per second.
　中文：弹速与电压有关；满电曾出现 16.2 m/s。
**关键词与易错点**：repair not change、checklist、hash、event notes。

### Q116. Tell me about handling a failure during a match.
**中文题意**：讲一次处理比赛中故障的经历。
**考察点**：现场纪律与设计。
**使用项目**：P3。
**English answer（口语，约 125 词）**：
> In one match the feeder jammed twice, and the launcher went to its fail state. The fail logic is: if the residual position error stays above 5000 counts for more than a second, the feeder reverses two slots, 52450 counts, once; if it fails again, the launcher stops and reports. That fail state is not a power cut: only the relax state cuts the current. The event log showed two jam events and then the fail state, as designed. The cause was a batch of larger pellets. Between matches I only cleaned the feed path and did not change the thresholds. After the competition I added a jam test to the regression list and reviewed whether the retry steps should change.

**中文翻译**：有一场比赛里拨盘卡了两次，发射机构进入失败状态。失败逻辑是：残余位置误差超过 5000 计数持续超过 1 s，拨盘反转两格（52450 计数）一次；再失败就停止并上报。该失败状态不是断电：只有 RELAX 状态才切断电流。事件日志显示两次卡弹然后进入失败状态，符合设计。原因是一批偏大的弹丸。场间我只清理了供弹路径，没有改阈值。赛后我把卡弹测试加入回归清单，并评估重试步骤是否应改变。
**追问 1**：*Why only one reverse attempt?* Unlimited retries could burn the motor; a clear failure is better.
　中文：无限重试可能烧电机；明确失败更好。
**追问 2**：*How do you tell a jam from a normal start?* The error plus its duration; current alone misjudges the high start current.
　中文：误差加持续时间；只看电流会误判较大的起步电流。
**关键词与易错点**：residual error + time、reverse once、fail state ≠ power cut。

### Q117. Tell me about a hardware wiring or connection problem you found in the field.
**中文题意**：讲一次你在现场发现的接线或连接问题。
**考察点**：硬件排查。
**使用项目**：P2、P3。
**English answer（口语，约 120 词）**：
> During Future Power testing, replies from one arm began to time out, and the arm sometimes stopped responding. I looked at the error counters, the bus-off and restart numbers, and measured the resistance between CAN high and low, which should be about 60 ohms with a terminator at each end. Then I wiggled the connectors while watching the error counters jump, which found one with intermittent contact. After remaking it and checking both terminators, a 30 minute run showed no timeouts. I then added protection: warn after three missing cycles, disable the whole arm after ten, and a power-on check that reads every motor. On the RoboMaster robot, loose wiring and supply sag dropped motors offline, and I thickened the supply wires and fixed the connectors.

**中文翻译**：未来动力测试期间，一条臂的回包开始超时，臂有时不响应。我看错误计数、bus-off 和重启次数，并测 CAN_H 与 CAN_L 之间的电阻（两端各一个终端时应约 60 Ω）。然后我边晃接头边看错误计数跳动，找到一个接触不良的接头。重做并检查两个终端后，30 分钟运行无超时。接着我加了保护：缺失三个周期告警、十个周期失能整条臂，以及逐个读电机的上电检查。RoboMaster 机器人上，接线松和供电跌落曾让电机离线，我加粗了供电线并固定了接头。
**追问 1**：*Why wiggle test?* A counter that jumps when the connector moves points straight at the physical contact.
　中文：晃动接头时计数跳变，直接指向物理接触。
**追问 2**：*Is the 30 minutes a statistic?* No; it is a regression check, not a reliability claim.
　中文：不是统计；是回归检查，不是可靠性声明。
**关键词与易错点**：termination 60 Ω、error counters、wiggle test。

### Q118. How does battery voltage affect your experiments, and what do you do about it?
**中文题意**：电池电压怎样影响实验，你怎么处理？
**考察点**：实验控制变量。
**使用项目**：P5、P7、P3。
**English answer（口语，约 120 词）**：
> Voltage changes what the actuators can deliver. On the navigation car, with a low battery the same speed command gave a lower speed, because the lower controller's speed loop saturated, and the longitudinal tracking error grew. I made the lower controller report the voltage, and the bridge lowers the speed cap below a threshold; speed estimation uses the encoder, not the command. I record the voltage before and after each experiment and do not run quantitative tests at low voltage. On the wheel-legged robot, low voltage and warm motors made the robot "softer"; I charge fully before tuning. On the RoboMaster robot, muzzle speed depends on voltage, so I test with full and lower-voltage packs.

**中文翻译**：电压改变执行器能提供的能力。导航车上，电量低时同样的速度指令给出更低的速度，因为下位机速度环饱和，纵向跟踪误差变大。我让下位机上报电压，桥接节点在低于阈值时降低速度上限；速度估计用编码器而不是指令。我在每次实验前后记录电压，低电压下不做定量实验。轮腿机器人上，低电压和电机变热使机器人变"软"；调参前我先充满电。RoboMaster 机器人上，弹速与电压有关，所以我用满电和较低电压的电池各测一遍。
**追问 1**：*Why record voltage in the log header?* It is a hidden variable that explains many "unexplained" differences.
　中文：它是能解释很多"莫名差异"的隐藏变量。
**追问 2**：*How did voltage affect the on/off feedforward comparison?* Running all "off" then all "on" let battery drop bias the result; I interleaved the runs.
　中文：先全"关"再全"开"让电量下降影响结果；我改为交错执行。
**关键词与易错点**：hidden variable、speed cap、interleave。

### 18.1 自测题

1. 不看资料，口述 P1、P7、P3 各自的控制周期，并说出为什么不是更快。（Q38–Q40）

3. 手算：球形机器人轮通道 CAN 负载；轮腿关节总线负载；云台板 CAN1 负载。（Q64–Q66）
4. 用六步讲一次优化：P1 抖动、P2 IK 频率、P3 CAN 命令频率、P4 帧龄、P5 MPC 求解时间，任选三个。（Q23、Q29、Q34、Q35、Q24）
5. 说出"数据 → 限幅 → 模型 → 控制器"的调试顺序，并各给一个项目例子。（Q8、Q9、Q19）
6. 说出配置频率与实测频率的区别和 P2 的例子。（Q48）

8. 说出三个只是"读码发现"、不能说"我修了"的例子，以及 P1 LQR 的定位。（Q78、Q80、Q102、Q39）

### 18.2 三轮练习

| 轮次 | 目标 | 做法 | 用时 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 第一轮：数字 | 记住速查卡与各项目的核心数字 | 每天默写一个项目的频率、延迟、负载表，对照第 2 节 | 约 4 天 |
| 第二轮：六步 | 能把优化题讲成六步 | 遮住英文，只看题意，90 秒内按"现象→度量→瓶颈→改动→结果→回归"说，录音 | 约 7 天 |
| 第三轮：追问 | 接住"怎么量的""为什么不是更快""不对怎么办" | 找人或自己随机抽题，每题追问三次 | 约 5 天 |

### 18.3 提醒

- 数字一律说 about、roughly、in our setup；记不清说诚实模板。
- P6 说 design budget；P4 说到 V4；P1 的 LQR 是分析工具；P3 说约 3 ms 不是 1 kHz；P7 说经典 CAN。
- 面试前一天，把第 2 节速查卡和 Q38–Q44 再读一遍。
