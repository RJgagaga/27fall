## 1. 本文件怎么用

1. **先背骨架，再背细节**。每个案例的英文叙述约 300 词，对应 2 分钟左右的口述。先记住五段的一句话（S、T、A、R、Reflection），再记数字。

## 案例 1. 控制周期抖动：把 100 Hz 回路的 99 分位抖动从 3.5 ms 降到 1.0 ms（P1）

**适用题型**：Describe an optimisation; How do you choose and verify the control frequency; Tell me about a problem that appeared only when the whole system ran.
**English story（STAR + Reflection，约 320 词）**：

> **Situation.** At AIRS I worked on the ballbot, a three-wheel balancing robot with two arms. My balance controller was a Python ROS node running a 100 hertz timer. It balanced well when it ran alone. But when the whole system was running, with the arm nodes, the data collector and rosbag, the robot sometimes twitched, and recovery after a push was a little slower.
>
> **Task.** I was responsible for the balance control and the tuning on the real robot, so I had to find out whether the loop timing was the cause, and fix it without changing the control design.
>
> **Action.** I measured first. I logged a monotonic clock in the first line of the timer callback and plotted a histogram of the intervals. About three times a minute there were outliers of 5 to 8 milliseconds, and the 99th percentile jitter was 3.5 milliseconds. I compared the node alone with the full system, using top and pidstat to see which threads were busy. I found three causes: log prints inside the loop, rosbag and a CSV collector writing to disk on the same core, and arrays being created every cycle. I removed all prints from the cycle and replaced them with a one-hertz summary, pinned the control node to its own core with a real-time FIFO priority, moved the recorder and the collector to other cores with a nice value of 10, and preallocated the NumPy arrays.
>
> **Result.** The 99th percentile jitter fell from 3.5 to 1.0 milliseconds, the maximum from 8 to 2, and the events over 5 milliseconds in one hour from about 180 to zero. I re-ran the push-recovery test, and the recovery time was back to the baseline. I also kept the period histogram in the regression list, with a limit of 1 millisecond at the 99th percentile.
>
> **Reflection.** The lesson is that the control frequency is only as good as its timing. I did not need 200 hertz; the IMU and the wheel feedback were both about 100 hertz, so a faster loop would have reused the same data. What I needed was a loop that was predictable, so I now measure jitter before I change a gain.

**中文翻译**：
情境：在 AIRS，我做的是带双臂的三轮球形机器人。平衡控制器是一个 Python 的 ROS 节点，用 100 Hz 定时器运行。它单独运行时平衡得很好。但整套系统运行时——有臂节点、数据采集和 rosbag——机器人有时会抽动，推扰之后的恢复也略慢。任务：我负责平衡控制和真机调参，所以要查清是不是回路时序造成的，并且在不改变控制设计的前提下修好它。行动：我先测量。我在定时器回调第一行记录单调时钟，画间隔的直方图。每分钟大约有三次 5–8 ms 的超时，99 分位抖动是 3.5 ms。然后我对比控制节点单独运行与整机运行，用 top 和 pidstat 看哪些线程忙。我找到三个原因：循环内的日志打印，同一个核上 rosbag 和 CSV 采集器写盘，以及每个周期都新建数组。我把周期内的打印全部去掉，换成 1 Hz 的汇总；把控制节点绑到独立的核并设实时 FIFO 优先级；把录包和采集器放到其他核并设 nice 10；NumPy 数组改为预分配。结果：99 分位抖动从 3.5 降到 1.0 ms，最大值从 8 降到 2，一小时内超过 5 ms 的事件从约 180 次降到零。我重做了推扰恢复测试，恢复时间回到基线。我还把周期直方图放进回归清单，99 分位上限 1 ms。反思：控制频率的好坏取决于它的时序。我并不需要 200 Hz；IMU 和轮反馈都约 100 Hz，更快的回路只会重复使用同一份数据。我需要的是可预测的回路，所以现在我在改任何增益之前先测抖动。

**30 秒浓缩版（English short version）**：
> At AIRS, my ballbot's 100 hertz balance loop had occasional 5 to 8 millisecond outliers when the whole system ran. I logged interval histograms, found prints, disk writes and allocations inside the loop, pinned the node to its own core with a real-time priority and moved the recorder away. The 99th percentile jitter fell from 3.5 to 1.0 milliseconds, and the histogram stays in my regression list.

中文：在 AIRS，球形机器人的 100 Hz 平衡回路在整机运行时偶有 5–8 ms 的超时。我记录了间隔直方图，发现循环里有打印、写盘和内存分配；于是把节点绑到独立核并设实时优先级，把录包移走。99 分位抖动从 3.5 降到 1.0 ms，直方图留在回归清单里。

**可能的 4 个追问**：

1. **Why 100 hertz and not 200?**
　*Answer*：The inner-loop bandwidth is about 1 hertz, so 100 hertz is already a hundred times higher. The IMU and wheel feedback arrive at about 100 hertz, so 200 would only reuse data. Sample-and-hold delay is half a period, so 200 hertz saves only 2.5 milliseconds out of a worst-case budget of 25, whose big parts are the IMU transfer and the motor speed loop. And a Python node has limited jitter margin.
　中文：内环带宽约 1 Hz，100 Hz 已是百倍；IMU 与轮反馈都约 100 Hz，200 Hz 只重复数据；采样保持延迟是半个周期，200 Hz 在 25 ms 的最坏预算里只省 2.5 ms，大头是 IMU 传输和电机速度环；而且 Python 节点抖动余量有限。
2. **How do you know the three causes were the causes?**
　*Answer*：By isolating them. Running the control node alone gave a clean histogram. Then I added load back one source at a time; the prints, the disk writes on the same core and the allocations each moved the histogram. After the changes, an hour-long full-system run gave no event above 5 milliseconds, and the push-recovery time matched the baseline.
　中文：靠隔离。控制节点单独运行时直方图干净；然后逐个加回负载，打印、同核写盘和分配各自改变直方图；改完后一小时整机运行没有超过 5 ms 的事件，推扰恢复时间与基线一致。
3. **Why not rewrite the node in C++?**
　*Answer*：It would help the worst case, but after the isolation the margin was enough. The Python code was simpler to test and to tune on the robot, and I had no measured need. If the maximum had still been a problem, the timer callback with the control computation would be the first part to move.
　中文：能改善最坏情形，但隔离后余量已够；Python 代码更易测试与整定，也没有实测需求。若最大值仍有问题，首先迁移的是带控制计算的定时器回调。
4. **What would you do differently?**
　*Answer*：Add the period histogram to the regression list from day one, and keep prints out of the real-time loop by design. Also log the measured loop period by default, because the configured rate and the real rate can differ.
　中文：第一天就把周期直方图放进回归清单，并在设计上禁止实时回路里打印；默认记录实测循环周期，因为配置频率与实际频率可能不同。

**关键词与易错点**：monotonic clock、histogram、99th percentile、SCHED_FIFO、core pinning、preallocation。数字为项目统一取值，说 about；不说 "I rewrote it in C++"；LQR 与此案例无关。

## 案例 2. CAN 命令帧按控制周期发送：云台板总线负载从 78% 降到 63%（P3）

**适用题型**：How did you reduce bus or CPU load; Tell me about a problem that appeared during integration; How do you choose communication rates.
**English story（STAR + Reflection，约 310 词）**：

> **Situation.** On our RoboMaster hero robot, the gimbal board controlled five motors on one CAN bus: yaw, pitch, two friction wheels and the feeder. When we integrated the vision link, the CAN send-failure counter sometimes rose and a motor feedback frame was occasionally lost. The gimbal sometimes twitched at those moments.
>
> **Task.** I owned the control loops and the CAN sending on this board, so I had to find the cause and make the bus reliable without changing the controller tuning.
>
> **Action.** I measured instead of guessing. I counted how often the transmit mailboxes were full and how many feedback frames arrived per second. Then I estimated the load. A standard eight-byte frame is about 111 bits, so 111 microseconds at one megabit. Five motors reporting at 1 kilohertz is 555 microseconds per millisecond, which is 55 percent. The commands for the gimbal motors and the friction wheels and the feeder were sent at several places in the cycle together with the remote and board-to-board frames. If commands had been sent at 1 kilohertz, the load would be about 78 percent, and in the worst case with bit stuffing close to 95 percent. I merged the commands of each motor group into one frame, sent it once per control cycle, about 333 hertz, spaced the frames by one tick, and divided the board-to-board frames to 100 and 50 hertz.
>
> **Result.** The load on the gimbal board fell from about 78 to about 63 percent, about 76 in the worst case. The send-failure counter went to zero and, in a ten minute run, the longest gap between feedback frames stayed under 3 milliseconds. I added the ten minute run to the regression list.
>
> **Reflection.** Faster is not better when the bus is shared. A command rate above the control rate adds no information, since the new command is computed once per cycle. I now calculate the load as frames times frame time before I raise any rate.

**中文翻译**：
情境：在我们的 RoboMaster 英雄机器人上，云台板在一条 CAN 总线上控制五个电机：yaw、pitch、两个摩擦轮和拨盘。联调视觉链路时，CAN 发送失败计数有时上升，偶尔丢失一帧电机反馈，云台在这些时刻有时会抽动。任务：这块板上的控制环和 CAN 发送归我负责，所以我要找出原因，在不改变控制器整定的前提下让总线可靠。行动：我测量而不是猜。我统计发送邮箱满的次数和每秒到达的反馈帧数。然后估算负载：一个标准 8 字节帧约 111 位，1 Mbit/s 下是 111 µs。五个电机以 1 kHz 上报是每毫秒 555 µs，即 55%。云台电机、摩擦轮和拨盘的命令与遥控帧、板间帧一起在周期的多个位置发送。如果命令按 1 kHz 发送，负载约 78%，最坏情形（位填充）接近 95%。我把每组电机的命令合并成一帧，每个控制周期（约 333 Hz）发送一次，帧间隔一个 tick，并把板间帧分频到 100 Hz 和 50 Hz。结果：云台板负载从约 78% 降到约 63%，最坏约 76%。发送失败计数归零，十分钟运行里反馈帧最长间隔保持在 3 ms 以下。我把十分钟运行加入了回归清单。反思：总线共享时更快并不更好。命令频率高于控制频率不增加信息，因为新的命令每个周期只计算一次。现在我在提高任何频率之前，先用"帧数乘以帧时间"算负载。

**30 秒浓缩版（English short version）**：
> On our RoboMaster gimbal board, CAN send failures appeared during vision integration. I calculated the load as frames times frame time, merged each motor group's commands into one frame per control cycle, and slowed the board-to-board frames. The load fell from about 78 to about 63 percent, and the send failures went to zero.

中文：RoboMaster 云台板在联调视觉时出现 CAN 发送失败。我用帧数乘以帧时间算负载，把每组电机的命令合并成每个控制周期一帧，并降低板间帧频率。负载从约 78% 降到约 63%，发送失败归零。

**可能的 4 个追问**：

1. **How exactly do you calculate the bus load?**
　*Answer*：Frames per second times the frame time, divided by one second. A frame is about 111 microseconds at one megabit, or about 135 with worst-case bit stuffing. Five feedback frames at 1 kilohertz give 555 microseconds per millisecond, and two command frames at about 333 hertz add about 7 percent, so about 63 percent in total.
　中文：每秒帧数乘以帧时间再除以 1 s。1 Mbit/s 下一帧约 111 µs，最坏位填充约 135 µs。五个反馈帧 1 kHz 是每毫秒 555 µs，两条约 333 Hz 的命令帧再加约 7%，合计约 63%。
2. **Why was it not enough to just add a second bus?**
　*Answer*：The board-to-board link already used the second bus, CAN2, at under 2 percent. Rewiring a competition robot would cost time and add hardware risk, while removing redundant frames was cheap and could be checked with the regression list.
　中文：板间链路已经用了第二条总线 CAN2（低于 2%）；给比赛用车重新接线既费时间又有硬件风险，而去掉冗余帧很便宜，还能用回归清单检验。
3. **How do you know you did not hurt the control performance?**
　*Answer*：The controller computes one command per cycle, so sending it once per cycle loses nothing. I reran the regression list: gimbal step and disturbance, the thirty-shot test, and the ten minute run, and compared with the old logs.
　中文：控制器每周期只算一个命令，所以每周期发一次不损失信息。我重跑回归清单——云台阶跃与扰动、30 发测试和十分钟运行——并与旧日志对比。
4. **What would you do if you needed a higher control rate?**
　*Answer*：First fix the period with a fixed-delay call and measure the jitter, then reduce frames or lower the feedback rate, and only then raise the inner gains. At 1 kilohertz commands the load alone would be about 78 percent.
　中文：先用固定延时调用固定周期并测抖动，再减帧或降低反馈频率，然后才提高内环增益。命令按 1 kHz 发时仅负载就约 78%。

**关键词与易错点**：frames × frame time、111 µs、63%/78%、merge commands、about 333 hertz。控制周期约 3 ms，不说 1 kHz；数字为统一设计取值；Wireshark/分析仪测量值若无就说估算。

## 案例 3. 主从示教的端到端延迟：从约 45 ms 降到约 30 ms（P2）

**适用题型**：How did you reduce latency in a sensing-to-actuation loop; Describe a performance problem you diagnosed; Tell me about teamwork on hardware.
**English story（STAR + Reflection，约 315 词）**：

> **Situation.** At Future Power I worked on a dual-arm platform. In leader-follower teaching, a person moves the leader arm by hand and the follower arm copies it. The leader had gravity compensation so it felt almost weightless. But when I moved the leader quickly, the follower trailed behind like it was chasing.
>
> **Task.** I was responsible for the gravity compensation and for tuning the follower tracking, so I had to find where the delay was coming from and reduce it without making the follower noisy.
>
> **Action.** I measured the delay first. I made a quick small step on the leader, overlaid both joint curves on the same time axis, and read the time difference between the ten and fifty percent points. The end-to-end delay was about 45 milliseconds. I then listed the stages: leader reading, publishing, receiving, the bus round trip, and the motor's inner loop. I found three problems. The follower received positions only, with no velocity feedforward, so it always reacted late. The low-pass on the velocity was too strong. And the publisher and the hardware node's timer were out of phase, so on average half a period was wasted. I added a velocity feedforward from the leader's velocity with a light low-pass, reduced the filter strength, and triggered the hardware node from the same source as the publisher.
>
> **Result.** The delay fell to about 30 milliseconds, and during motion the lag dropped from about four or five control periods to about three. The steady error stayed under 0.02 radians, the RMS error was about 0.01 radians, and the velocity noise did not cause jitter. I checked this by repeating the step test and the hover test.
>
> **Reflection.** I did not just raise the publish rate. Going from 100 to 200 hertz would save only 2.5 milliseconds on average, so I went after the big parts first. Delay budgets tell me where to look.

**中文翻译**：
情境：在未来动力，我做一个双臂平台。主从示教时，人用手移动主臂，从臂复制它。主臂有重力补偿，所以感觉几乎没有重量。但我快速移动主臂时，从臂落在后面，像在追赶。任务：我负责重力补偿和从臂跟踪的整定，所以要找出延迟来自哪里，并且在不让从臂变得有噪声的前提下降低它。行动：我先测量延迟。在主臂上做一个快速小阶跃，把两条关节曲线叠在同一时间轴上，读取 10% 到 50% 处的时间差。端到端延迟约 45 ms。然后我列出各阶段：主臂读取、发布、接收、总线往返、电机内环。我发现三个问题：从臂只收到位置，没有速度前馈，所以总是反应偏晚；速度低通太强；发布和硬件节点的定时器相位不同步，平均浪费半个周期。我加入来自主臂速度的前馈并配轻度低通，降低滤波强度，并让硬件节点与发布同源触发。结果：延迟降到约 30 ms，运动中的滞后从约四五个控制周期降到约三个。稳态误差保持在 0.02 rad 以下，RMS 误差约 0.01 rad，速度噪声没有引起抖动。我通过重复阶跃测试和悬停测试来检查。反思：我没有简单地提高发布频率。从 100 Hz 提到 200 Hz 平均只省 2.5 ms，所以我先去动大头。延迟预算告诉我该往哪里看。

**30 秒浓缩版（English short version）**：
> In leader-follower teaching, the follower trailed the leader by about 45 milliseconds. I measured it with a step overlay, added a velocity feedforward, lightened the filter and synchronised the timers. The delay fell to about 30 milliseconds without extra noise.

中文：主从示教中，从臂比主臂落后约 45 ms。我用阶跃叠加图测量，加入速度前馈、减轻滤波并同步定时器。延迟降到约 30 ms，也没有增加噪声。

**可能的 4 个追问**：

1. **How did you measure the delay?**
　*Answer*：I commanded a quick small step on the leader and overlaid the leader and follower joint angles on one time axis. I read the time difference at the ten and fifty percent levels of the rise. I also logged both the receive time and the publish time, so the delay of each stage could be computed afterwards.
　中文：在主臂上做一个快速小阶跃，把主从关节角叠在一条时间轴上，读取上升过程 10% 与 50% 处的时间差；同时记录接收时间和发布时间，事后可算各阶段延迟。
2. **Why add velocity feedforward and not just a higher gain?**
　*Answer*：A higher gain with a 30 millisecond delay costs phase: a 5 hertz crossover would lose about 54 degrees, and a 2 hertz crossover about 22 degrees, in a teaching estimate. Feedforward moves the response earlier without raising the loop gain, so it does not eat the phase margin.
　中文：30 ms 延迟下更高增益会损失相位：教学估算里 5 Hz 穿越约损失 54°，2 Hz 约 22°。前馈让响应提前而不提高回路增益，所以不消耗相位裕度。
3. **What stage dominates the 30 milliseconds?**
　*Answer*：The follower's motor inner loop, about 15 to 20 milliseconds, then the leader reading of about 5, publishing and receiving of about 5 and the bus round trip of about 2. That is a design budget; the motor inner loop is in vendor firmware and I cannot change it.
　中文：从臂电机内环约 15–20 ms，其次主臂读取约 5 ms、发布与接收约 5 ms、总线往返约 2 ms。这是设计预算；电机内环在厂商固件里，我改不了。
4. **What would you do next to improve the follow?**
　*Answer*：Add gravity feedforward on the follower so it needs less gain, and keep the measured delay in the default log. That would reduce position error without more gain.
　中文：给从臂加重力前馈，使它需要更低增益，并把实测延迟放进默认日志，这样不加增益也能减小位置误差。

## 案例 4. 激光时间戳偏移：从约 60 ms 降到 5 ms 以内（P5）

**适用题型**：How do you handle time synchronisation; Describe a bug whose root cause was in the data; How did you find a timing problem between sensors.
**English story（STAR + Reflection，约 330 词）**：

> **Situation.** On the navigation car I fused an IMU, wheel encoders and a 2D laser with an EKF and AMCL. In turns, the walls smeared in RViz, the transform from map to odom jumped by 0.2 to 0.4 metres, and the log showed "extrapolation into the future" warnings.
>
> **Task.** I was responsible for the localisation chain, and the goal was a continuous pose. I first suspected the filter noise, but nothing changed when I retuned it, so I had to find the real cause.
>
> **Action.** I recorded a bag and plotted the size of the jumps against the yaw rate; they correlated, which pointed at timing, since a time offset matters only when the car is turning. To measure it, I took the yaw rate from the gyro and the yaw rate from scan matching and cross-correlated them during a slow spin. The peak was at about 60 milliseconds. A turn pulse showed the IMU, encoder and scan curves starting at different times, and the topic delay tool showed the laser stamps were late. The driver stamped each scan with the arrival of the whole revolution, about 100 milliseconds after the scan began. I changed the driver to stamp the scan start, added a fixed delay compensation, synchronised the machines with chrony, and set the transform tolerance to 0.3 seconds. For the lower controller I mapped its tick counter to ROS time by taking the minimum of the offset over a sliding window, because arrival time is never earlier than the true send time.
>
> **Result.** The cross-correlation offset fell from about 60 milliseconds to under 5. For a wall six metres away the lateral error in a turn fell from about 16 centimetres to about 1.5. I repeated the straight-line test and a 90 degree turn, the AMCL match score did not drop, and the extrapolation warnings did not return in a thirty minute run.
>
> **Reflection.** Raising the TF tolerance would have hidden the warning but left the data misaligned. A stamp must be the measurement time, not the arrival time.

**中文翻译**：
情境：在导航车上，我用 EKF 和 AMCL 融合 IMU、轮式编码器和 2D 激光。转弯时，RViz 里墙体拖影，map 到 odom 的变换跳动 0.2–0.4 m，日志里出现 "extrapolation into the future" 警告。任务：我负责定位链，目标是连续的位姿。我起初怀疑滤波器噪声，但重调后没有任何变化，所以必须找到真正原因。行动：我录了一个 bag，把跳变大小与偏航率画在一起，二者相关，指向时间问题，因为时间偏移只在车转弯时才有影响。为了量化，我在慢速原地旋转时取陀螺偏航率和扫描匹配偏航率做互相关，峰值在约 60 ms。转向脉冲显示 IMU、编码器和扫描曲线起跳时间不同，topic delay 工具显示激光时间戳偏晚。驱动用整圈数据到达的时刻给每帧打戳，比扫描开始晚约 100 ms。我把驱动改为扫描起始时刻，加固定延迟补偿，用 chrony 同步各机器，并把变换容忍度设为 0.3 s。对下位机，我取滑动窗口内偏移的最小值把它的 tick 计数映射到 ROS 时间，因为到达时间永远不会早于真实发送时间。结果：互相关偏移从约 60 ms 降到 5 ms 以内。对于 6 m 外的墙，转弯时的横向误差从约 16 cm 降到约 1.5 cm。我重复了直行测试和 90° 转弯，AMCL 匹配分数没有下降，三十分钟运行里外推警告没有再出现。反思：提高 TF 容忍度只会隐藏警告而数据依然错位。时间戳必须是测量时刻，而不是到达时刻。

**30 秒浓缩版（English short version）**：
> Our navigation car's pose jumped in turns. Cross-correlating the gyro and scan-matching yaw rates showed a 60 millisecond offset, because the laser driver stamped the arrival time. I stamped the scan start, added a fixed compensation and synchronised the clocks, and the offset fell below 5 milliseconds.

中文：导航车转弯时位姿跳变。陀螺和扫描匹配偏航率互相关显示 60 ms 偏移，因为激光驱动打的是到达时间。我改为扫描起始时间、加固定补偿并同步时钟，偏移降到 5 ms 以内。

**可能的 4 个追问**：

1. **Why does cross-correlation give the time offset?**
　*Answer*：Both signals measure the same physical quantity, the yaw rate, with independent sensors. If one lags by a delay, the correlation peaks at that lag. It needs a motion with enough yaw rate content, like a slow spin, and it assumes a fixed delay.
　中文：两个信号用独立传感器测同一物理量（偏航率），一个滞后某延迟时，相关峰就出现在该滞后处。需要足够偏航率内容的动作（如慢速旋转），并假设延迟固定。
2. **Why not just increase the TF tolerance?**
　*Answer*：It only makes the warning go away. The scan would still be matched against a pose from a different moment, and the error would stay: roughly the yaw rate times the offset times the distance to the wall.
　中文：它只是让警告消失；扫描仍与另一时刻的位姿匹配，误差仍在，约为偏航率×偏移×到墙的距离。
3. **What if the delay changes with the load?**
　*Answer*：Then a fixed compensation is not enough; I would estimate the offset online or use hardware time stamps. In our case the main error was the driver's stamping rule, which I fixed at the source, and I would re-measure after any hardware change.
　中文：那固定补偿不够，需要在线估计偏移或用硬件时间戳。我们这里的主要误差是驱动的打戳规则，我在源头修了，并会在硬件改动后重测。
4. **How do you prevent this from coming back?**
　*Answer*：A start-up self-test that checks stamps are monotonic and recent, and the cross-correlation experiment in the regression list. Also a rule: every driver stamps the measurement time.
　中文：启动自检检查时间戳单调且新，回归清单里放互相关实验；还有规则：每个驱动都打测量时刻。

**关键词与易错点**：cross-correlation、measurement time vs arrival time、sliding-window minimum、chrony、transform tolerance。60 ms 与 16 cm→1.5 cm 为统一设计取值，说 about；不要说"调了滤波器解决"。

## 案例 5. 倒车时速度符号丢失：里程计向前积分，粒子云炸开（P5）

**适用题型**：Tell me about a bug caused by a sign or convention; Describe a mistake and how you fixed it; How do you check data before you change an algorithm.
**English story（STAR + Reflection，约 320 词）**：

> **Situation.** The navigation car needed to reverse for tight turn-arounds. Going forward it localised well. When it reversed, the odometry kept moving forward, and AMCL pulled the pose back, so the particle cloud spread out and the map-to-odom transform made a large jump.
>
> **Task.** I owned the localisation chain, so I had to make the pose continuous in reverse as well, and to find out why only reversing failed.
>
> **Action.** Because it appeared only in reverse, I suspected a sign. > **Result.** In a three metre reverse run, the odometry now agreed with the tape within about five centimetres, and the step in the map-to-odom transform during reversing disappeared. I added a forward, reverse, forward round trip, which returns to within ten centimetres of the start, and a unit test for the parser's sign and overflow behaviour.
>
> **Reflection.** My first reaction in this project was to tune the filter, but the cause was an interface losing information. I now compare the sign of a signal with an independent source before I trust it.

**中文翻译**：

## 案例 6. 质心前馈打开反而更差：用静态单臂实验找到符号错误（P1）

**适用题型**：Describe a mistake; Tell me about a time your first solution failed; How do you design a controlled experiment; Hardest technical problem.
**English story（STAR + Reflection，约 330 词）**：

> **Situation.** On the ballbot at AIRS, the arms keep moving the centre of mass, which disturbs the balance. I estimated the centre-of-mass offset from the arm joint angles with the URDF and Pinocchio, and fed a roll and pitch gravity feedforward into the balance controller. The first time I switched it on, the result was worse: during a front raise, the tilt peak went from about 0.06 to 0.09 radians, and once the robot fell.
>
> **Task.** I was responsible for the balance controller and for showing, with a test, that the feedforward helps. So I had to find out whether the sign, the estimate or the gain was wrong.
>
> **Action.** I stopped dynamic tests and used the simplest experiment: hold one arm out in a static front-raise pose and look at the steady tilt. With the feedforward off the tilt leaned forward; with it on, it leaned further in the same direction. Next I printed the centre of mass: with the arm forward it was about 3.5 centimetres ahead, which matched my hand calculation, and moving each joint gave sensible directions, so the estimate and the joint-name mapping were fine. I flipped the sign, collected five static poses, and regressed the correction needed against the gravity moment. The slope was about one, so the gain was about 1.0. Then I compared on and off with the same fixed arm trajectory, alternating, five runs each, re-initialising before every run.
>
> **Result.** With the feedforward on, the tilt peak was about 0.026 radians and the RMS about 0.010, against about 0.060 and 0.022 off. The drift over 30 seconds was about 0.10 metres against 0.30.
>
> **Reflection.** Test signed terms statically before dynamically. A static test removes everything except the sign, the estimate and the gain.

## 案例 7. 0.5 m/s 速度阶跃使机器人倒下：力矩饱和与有效增益崩塌（P7）

**适用题型**：Tell me about a project that did not go as planned; How did you diagnose actuator saturation; Describe a problem that was not in the control law.
**English story（STAR + Reflection，约 320 词）**：

> **Situation.** On the variable-leg-length wheel-legged robot, I built a gain-scheduled LQR for balance and speed tracking, on an STM32H7 with FreeRTOS and FDCAN. Standing still and small commands worked. But when I gave a half metre per second speed step, the robot leaned and fell.
>
> **Task.** I was responsible for the balance controller and its tuning on the robot. The task was to make the speed command trackable, and to find out why a controller that was stable in simulation failed on the real robot.
>
> **Action.** I did not touch the gains first. I checked the data, then the limits. The data looked consistent. Then I added one metric to the log: the fraction of control cycles in which a torque command was at its limit. During the step it was above sixty percent. I computed what the step asked for: about nine newton-metres at the wheels, while the software limit was three. When the command is clipped, the effective gain becomes the limit divided by the error, so the larger the error, the smaller the gain, and the loop can run away. That explained why the failure appeared only for a large step. I made three changes: the speed command goes through a one-second ramp, with an acceleration limit of 0.5 metres per second squared; I lowered the speed weight in the LQR a little to ask for less torque; and I added anti-windup to the position integral, so it stops accumulating when the output is clipped. I changed one thing at a time.
>
> **Result.** The limit fraction fell from over sixty percent to under five, and the robot tracked 0.5 metres per second. I re-ran standing balance for 30 seconds, where the displacement stayed under three centimetres, and a disturbance test, where the recovery time did not get longer.
>
> **Reflection.** The error was not in the control law. I learned to look at the actuator limits before I tune a gain, and to make "saturation fraction" a standard metric. I now ramp any command that a human or an upper layer can change in a step.

**中文翻译**：
情境：在腿长可变的轮腿机器人上，我在 STM32H7 与 FreeRTOS、FDCAN 上做了用于平衡和速度跟踪的增益调度 LQR。静止和小指令都正常。但我给 0.5 m/s 的速度阶跃时，机器人前倾并倒下。任务：我负责平衡控制器及其上机调参。任务是让速度指令可跟踪，并查清为什么仿真中稳定的控制器在真机上失败。行动：我没有先动增益，而是先检查数据，再检查限幅。数据看起来是一致的。然后我在日志里加了一个指标：控制周期中力矩命令贴着限幅的占比。阶跃期间它超过 60%。我算出阶跃需要什么：轮子约 9 N·m，而软件限幅是 3。命令被削顶后，有效增益变成限幅除以误差，误差越大增益越小，闭环可能发散。这解释了为什么只有大阶跃才失败。我做了三处改动：速度指令经过 1 s 斜坡，加速度限制 0.5 m/s²；稍微降低 LQR 里的速度权重，让它要求更少的力矩；并给位置积分加抗饱和，输出被削顶时停止累积。我一次只改一件事。结果：贴限幅占比从超过 60% 降到 5% 以下，机器人能跟踪 0.5 m/s。我重做了 30 s 的站立平衡，位移保持在 3 cm 以内，也重做了扰动测试，恢复时间没有变长。反思：错误不在控制律里。我学会了在调增益之前先看执行器限幅，并把"饱和占比"作为标准指标。现在任何人或上层可能阶跃改变的指令，我都加斜坡。

**30 秒浓缩版（English short version）**：
> The wheel-legged robot fell at a 0.5 metre per second speed step, because the demanded torque was about three times the limit. I tracked the saturation fraction, ramped the command over one second and added anti-windup. The fraction fell from over sixty to under five percent.

中文：轮腿机器人在 0.5 m/s 速度阶跃时倒下，因为需求力矩约为限幅的三倍。我跟踪饱和占比，把指令做成 1 s 斜坡并加抗饱和。占比从 60% 以上降到 5% 以下。

**可能的 4 个追问**：

1. **How do you know it was saturation and not a bad gain?**
　*Answer*：A too-high gain saturates at a tiny error; here the clipping happened only for the large step, and the demanded torque, about nine newton-metres, was three times the limit. The saturation fraction, above sixty percent during the step, showed it directly, and after the ramp the same gains worked.
　中文：增益过高会在很小误差时饱和；这里只有大阶跃才削顶，需求力矩约 9 N·m 是限幅的三倍。阶跃中超过 60% 的饱和占比直接显示了它，加斜坡后同样的增益就好用了。
2. **Why a ramp and not just a higher torque limit?**
　*Answer*：The limit protects the hardware; the motors can only give so much. A ramp makes the demand physically reachable. A step asks for an acceleration that no actuator can provide, and I would rather shape the request than raise the risk.
　中文：限幅保护硬件，电机能给的有限；斜坡让需求在物理上可达。阶跃要求的加速度没有执行器能给，我宁愿整形请求，而不是增加风险。
3. **What does the anti-windup do here?**
　*Answer*：When the output is clipped, the position integral stops accumulating, so after the step ends the robot does not overshoot because of stored error. The position integral is limited to plus or minus 0.3 metres.
　中文：输出被削顶时位置积分停止累积，阶跃结束后不会因累积误差而过冲；位置积分限幅 ±0.3 m。
4. **How did you make sure the fix did not hurt other behaviour?**
　*Answer*：I re-ran the regression list: direction checks, 30 seconds of standing balance with less than three centimetres of drift, a disturbance test, a leg-length sweep and the worst-case execution time. The saturation fraction became one of the metrics in the list.
　中文：重跑回归清单：方向检查、30 s 站立漂移小于 3 cm、扰动测试、腿长扫描和最坏执行时间；饱和占比成为清单里的指标之一。

**关键词与易错点**：saturation fraction、effective gain、ramp 1 s / 0.5 m/s²、anti-windup、one change at a time。数字为统一设计取值；LQR 在 P7 是主控制器；不要说成 P1。

## 案例 8. 功率限制触发后底盘一顿一顿：不对称缩放与缓冲能量（P3）

**适用题型**：How do you limit power without destroying performance; Describe a control oscillation you removed; Tell me about a problem you found in testing.
**English story（STAR + Reflection，约 315 词）**：

> **Situation.** On our RoboMaster hero robot, the match rules limit the chassis power. If the robot exceeds it, there are penalties. I distributed the body velocity to four Mecanum wheels and scaled the wheel speeds when the predicted power went over the limit. But when the limit was triggered, the chassis jerked: it sped up, slowed down and sped up again.
>
> **Task.** I was responsible for the chassis loop, and the goal was a chassis that stays manoeuvrable under the power limit without oscillating around it.
>
> **Action.** I logged the scaling factor, the referee power and the buffer energy together. The scaling factor flipped between one and a small value several times per second. The cause was the rule itself: the scale went down and came back with the same speed. When the power fell after the scale dropped, the rule released the scale immediately; the power went up again and over the limit, and the cycle repeated. A second reason was that the referee power arrives at only 50 hertz, six or seven control cycles old, so the feedback was always late. I predicted the power demand from the local motor feedback, current and speed, through a quadratic model, and used the referee's value for the limit, the reported limit minus 5 watts. Then I made the scaling asymmetric: it drops at once when needed, but recovers at 0.01 per cycle, about 0.3 seconds to full. All four wheels use the same factor, so the direction of motion is kept. When the buffer energy is below 30 joules the scale tightens, and below 10 joules it drops to 0.3. I also added a 2 degree dead zone to the chassis following, so the rotation command stopped flipping while the power was compressed.
>
> **Result.** The flipping of the factor disappeared, and the lowest buffer energy in a hard test, full-load driving plus spinning for 60 seconds, rose from about 8 to about 25 joules. The test stayed in the regression list, with the limit that it must stay above 10 joules.
>
> **Reflection.** A limiter needs hysteresis in time, just like a pump needs hysteresis in level.

**中文翻译**：
情境：在我们的 RoboMaster 英雄机器人上，比赛规则限制底盘功率，超限会受罚。我把车体速度分配到四个麦克纳姆轮，并在预测功率超限时缩放轮速。但限制触发时，底盘一顿一顿：加速、减速、再加速。任务：底盘回路归我负责，目标是让底盘在功率限制下保持机动，又不在限制附近振荡。行动：我同时记录缩放因子、裁判功率和缓冲能量。缩放因子每秒在 1 和一个小值之间翻转好几次。原因就在规则本身：缩放下降和恢复的速度相同。缩放下降后功率回落，规则立刻放开缩放；功率又升高、又超限，循环重复。第二个原因是裁判功率只有 50 Hz，已有六七个控制周期的延迟，反馈总是偏晚。我用本地电机反馈（电流和转速）通过二次模型预测功率需求，裁判值只用来给出限制：上报上限减 5 W。然后我把缩放做成不对称：需要时立即下降，但以每周期 0.01 恢复，约 0.3 s 回到满。四个轮用同一个因子，所以运动方向不变。缓冲能量低于 30 J 时缩放收紧，低于 10 J 降到 0.3。我还给底盘跟随加了 2° 死区，使功率被压缩时旋转指令不再来回翻。结果：因子的翻转消失；高强度测试（满载行驶加小陀螺 60 s）中缓冲能量最低值从约 8 J 升到约 25 J。这个测试留在回归清单里，要求保持在 10 J 以上。反思：限幅器需要时间上的滞回，就像水泵需要电平上的滞回。

**30 秒浓缩版（English short version）**：
> Our RoboMaster chassis jerked at the power limit, because the scale dropped and recovered at the same speed. I predicted the power from local feedback, made the scale drop at once and recover slowly, and added a dead zone. The lowest buffer energy rose from about 8 to about 25 joules.

中文：RoboMaster 底盘在功率限制处一顿一顿，因为缩放下降和恢复速度相同。我用本地反馈预测功率，让缩放立即下降、缓慢恢复，并加了死区。缓冲能量最低值从约 8 J 升到约 25 J。

**可能的 4 个追问**：

1. **Why not use the referee power directly as feedback?**
　*Answer*：It updates at 50 hertz while my control runs about every 3 milliseconds, so it is six or seven cycles old. I predict the demand from the fresh local current and speed and let the referee value set the limit and correct the model.
　中文：它 50 Hz 更新而我的控制约每 3 ms 一次，已有六七个周期的旧值；我用新鲜的本地电流与转速预测需求，由裁判值给出限制并修正模型。
2. **Why scale all four wheels by the same factor?**
　*Answer*：A different factor per wheel would change the direction of the body velocity, so the driver would lose control of the heading. A common factor keeps the direction and only reduces the speed.
　中文：每个轮不同的因子会改变车体速度方向，操作手会失去对朝向的控制；共同因子保持方向、只降低速度。
3. **Why recover slowly but drop quickly?**
　*Answer*：The penalty is about exceeding the limit, so the drop must be immediate. Recovery can wait until the power has really settled, so a slow slope avoids bouncing back over the limit.
　中文：惩罚针对超限，所以下降必须立即；恢复可以等功率真正稳定，慢斜率避免弹回超限。
4. **Where do the model coefficients come from?**
　*Answer*：They come from the quadratic model in the team's code snapshot, a current-and-speed form with fitted constants. I describe it as a model of that form and I do not claim I measured the coefficients myself.
　中文：来自队伍代码快照里的二次模型（电流与转速形式、带拟合常数）。我把它描述为这种形式的模型，不声称系数是我自己测的。

**关键词与易错点**：asymmetric scaling、+0.01 per cycle、buffer energy 60/30/10 J、referee 50 Hz、uniform factor。队伍代码里的功率模型系数不要当作自测；控制周期约 3 ms。

## 案例 9. 放下后机器人缓慢向前爬：俯仰偏置与数据不同步（P7）

**适用题型**：Describe a debugging process from symptom to root cause; Tell me about state inconsistency; How did you separate several causes.
**English story（STAR + Reflection，约 320 词）**：

> **Situation.** On the wheel-legged robot, I hung it up and lowered it to the ground at a fixed leg length. It balanced, but crept slowly forward, and the position error kept growing until I caught it. Sometimes there were also small twitches in the torque.
>
> **Task.** I owned the balance controller, so I had to find out why a robot that should stand still did not, and whether it was one cause or two.
>
> **Action.** I looked at the data first. At rest the body pitch mean was not zero: after I subtracted the nominal balance point it still read about one and a half degrees. A pitch offset acts like a constant push, and a position loop that tries to hold the position integrates against it, so the robot leans and creeps. The offset came from two sources: the centre of mass was ahead of the geometric centre, and the IMU was not mounted perfectly level. I measured it by hanging the robot, reading the zero position, and then finding the balance point on the ground with a safety rope. I wrote the offset into the parameter table. For the twitches, I put the time stamps of the IMU and the joint data in the log. In the same control cycle they differed by several hundred microseconds up to nearly one full period, because the control task read variables that interrupts were updating at any time. I fixed it by taking one snapshot of all inputs at the start of each cycle, with a double buffer and a time stamp, and by adding an age check: data older than three periods, 6 milliseconds, is not used.
>
> **Result.** Standing for 30 seconds, the displacement stayed under 3 centimetres. In replay, the time difference between IMU and joint data in a cycle was below 0.5 milliseconds.
>
> **Reflection.** Two different state problems, a bias and a time skew, produced two different symptoms. I now check the data first, then the limits, then the model.

**中文翻译**：
情境：在轮腿机器人上，我把它吊起来，在固定腿长下放到地面。它能平衡，但缓慢向前爬，位置误差不断增大，直到我把它抓住。有时力矩还有小幅抽动。任务：平衡控制器归我负责，所以我要查清为什么应该静止的机器人没有静止，以及原因是一个还是两个。行动：我先看数据。静止时机身俯仰均值不是零：减去名义平衡点之后仍读到约 1.5°。俯仰偏置就像持续的推力，而试图保持位置的位置环会对它积分，所以机器人前倾并爬行。偏置有两个来源：质心在几何中心前方，以及 IMU 安装不完全水平。我的测量方法是：吊起机器人读取零位，然后用保护绳在地面找到平衡点，把偏置写进参数表。对于抽动，我把 IMU 和关节数据的时间戳放进日志。在同一个控制周期里它们相差几百微秒到接近一整个周期，因为控制任务读取的变量会被中断随时更新。我的修复是：每个周期开始时对所有输入取一次快照，用双缓冲和时间戳，并加数据年龄检查：超过三个周期（6 ms）的数据不使用。结果：站立 30 s，位移保持在 3 cm 以内。回放中，一个周期里 IMU 和关节数据的时间差小于 0.5 ms。反思：偏置和时间错位这两个不同的状态问题产生了两种不同的症状。现在我先查数据，再查限幅，再查模型。

**30 秒浓缩版（English short version）**：
> My wheel-legged robot crept forward at rest. A pitch offset of about 1.5 degrees and unsynchronised IMU and joint data were the causes. I calibrated the offset, took one snapshot per cycle and checked the data age. The drift stayed under three centimetres in 30 seconds.

中文：轮腿机器人静止时缓慢前爬。原因是约 1.5° 的俯仰偏置以及 IMU 与关节数据不同步。我标定了偏置、每周期取一次快照并检查数据年龄。30 s 内漂移保持在 3 cm 以内。

**可能的 4 个追问**：

1. **Why does a pitch offset cause creeping, quantitatively?**
　*Answer*：The position loop sees a constant disturbance and keeps asking for a correction; in steady state the position error equals the offset divided by the position gain. That is the same relationship I used on the ballbot, where a one degree offset moves the robot by about 0.2 metres.
　中文：位置环看到恒定扰动并不断要求修正；稳态时位置误差等于偏置除以位置增益。这与我在球形机器人上用的关系相同，1° 偏置使机器人移动约 0.2 m。
2. **How did you find the balance point safely?**
　*Answer*：Hang the robot and read the zero, then lower it with a safety rope at low limits, and record the pitch at which it stands still. Only then I write the offset into the table. I never changed it while the robot was moving.
　中文：先吊起读零位，再用保护绳在低限幅下放到地面，记录它静止时的俯仰；然后才写入参数表。我从不在机器人运动时改它。
3. **Why a double buffer and not a lock?**
　*Answer*：A lock inside the control path can block a high-priority task. A double buffer or a short copy keeps the critical section tiny and the snapshot consistent.
　中文：控制路径里的锁可能阻塞高优先级任务；双缓冲或短拷贝让临界区极小、快照一致。
4. **Where does the 6 millisecond limit come from?**
　*Answer*：Three control periods of 2 milliseconds. It tolerates an isolated late frame, but it reacts within a few cycles; five consecutive stale cycles lead to a protection state.
　中文：三个 2 ms 控制周期；能容忍孤立的晚到帧，几个周期内就反应；连续五个陈旧周期进入保护状态。

**关键词与易错点**：pitch offset 1.5°、position gain、snapshot、double buffer、age check 3 periods。数字为统一设计取值；不要说是 CAD 精确算出的偏置。

## 案例 10. 重新使能瞬间猛冲：三个陈旧量叠加（P1）

**适用题型**：Tell me about a failure and how you learned from it; How do you debug a state-machine bug; Describe a problem with several stacked causes.
**English story（STAR + Reflection，约 330 词）**：

> **Situation.** On the ballbot at AIRS, a fall test is part of my safety procedure: I push the robot over until the protection triggers, set it upright and enable it again. The first time I did this, the robot shot forward about 0.3 metres, the tilt peak was 0.12 radians, and once it triggered the protection a second time.
>
> **Task.** I owned the balance controller and its safety logic. A robot that lunges at re-enable is dangerous, so I had to find the cause and make re-enabling clean.
>
> **Action.** I plotted the target position, the position estimate and the integrator, before and after the enable edge. Three things were stale. The target position was still the one from the previous run, 0.4 metres from where the robot stood now; with the outer-loop gain of 0.08 that is a 0.032 radian request. The integrator held a large value from the fall. And the pitch offset had been captured while I was still steadying the robot. To be sure there were three causes and not one, I restored them one at a time: with only the old target the robot moved about 0.1 metres, with only the integrator the tilt peak was about 0.05 radians, and with only the bad offset it crept slowly. All three together gave the 0.3 metres and 0.12. I wrote the enable as a state machine with a five-step initialisation: wait until the body is steady, average about 200 frames for the offset, set the target to the current position, clear the integrator and filters, and ramp the output in over about 0.3 seconds. All the variables with memory sit in one reset function, which both the enable edge and the fault trigger call.
>
> **Result.** In ten consecutive fall-and-re-enable cycles, the tilt peak stayed under 0.02 radians. I put "ten re-enables" into the regression list.
>
> **Reflection.** State transitions are where bugs and danger live. I test the whole cycle, not only the first power-up.

**中文翻译**：
情境：在 AIRS 的球形机器人上，跌倒测试是我安全流程的一部分：把机器人推倒到保护触发，扶正，再次使能。我第一次这样做时，机器人向前冲出约 0.3 m，倾角峰值 0.12 rad，有一次还触发了第二次保护。任务：平衡控制器及其安全逻辑归我负责。重新使能时猛冲是危险的，所以我要找出原因，让重新使能干净。行动：我画出使能边沿前后的目标位置、位置估计和积分。有三样东西是陈旧的。目标位置还是上一次运行的，离机器人现在的位置 0.4 m；外环增益 0.08 时这是 0.032 rad 的请求。积分保留着跌倒时的大值。俯仰偏置是在我还在扶稳机器人时采集的。为了确认是三个原因而不是一个，我逐个恢复它们：只有旧目标时机器人移动约 0.1 m，只有积分时倾角峰值约 0.05 rad，只有坏偏置时它缓慢爬行；三者同时才产生 0.3 m 和 0.12 rad。我把使能写成状态机，带五步初始化：等机身稳定；取约 200 帧均值作偏置；目标设为当前位置；清积分和滤波；约 0.3 s 斜坡放出输出。所有带记忆的变量放在一个 reset 函数里，使能边沿和故障触发都调用它。结果：连续十次跌倒—重新使能循环，倾角峰值保持在 0.02 rad 以下。我把"十次重新使能"放进了回归清单。反思：状态转换是 bug 与危险所在。我测试整个循环，而不只是第一次上电。

**30 秒浓缩版（English short version）**：
> The ballbot lunged when re-enabled after a fall, because the old target, the integrator and a bad offset were all stale. I restored them one at a time, then wrote a state machine with a five-step initialisation. The tilt peak stayed under 0.02 radians in ten cycles.

中文：球形机器人跌倒后重新使能时猛冲，因为旧目标、积分和坏偏置都是陈旧的。我逐个恢复它们，然后写了带五步初始化的状态机。十次循环里倾角峰值保持在 0.02 rad 以下。

**可能的 4 个追问**：

1. **How did you separate the three causes?**
　*Answer*：By restoring them one at a time, keeping the other two cleared, and watching each contribution: the old target gave about 0.1 metres, the old integrator about 0.05 radians, the bad offset a slow creep. Only together did they reproduce the original 0.3 metres.
　中文：逐个恢复，其余两个保持清零，观察各自贡献：旧目标约 0.1 m，旧积分约 0.05 rad，坏偏置慢速爬行；三者同时才复现最初的 0.3 m。
2. **Why a state machine instead of just clearing variables?**
　*Answer*：The state machine makes the sequence explicit and makes it impossible to run the controller in an uninitialised state. Clearing variables in several places is how one gets forgotten.
　中文：状态机让顺序显式，并使控制器不可能在未初始化状态下运行；在多处清变量正是容易漏掉一个的原因。
3. **What happens if the robot is not steady when enabling?**
　*Answer*：The initialisation waits for the gyro to be quiet. If the wait runs over the five second timeout, it gives up, returns to disabled and raises an alarm, so a new request is needed.
　中文：初始化等待陀螺安静；等待超过 5 s 超时就放弃、回到失能并报警，需要重新请求。
4. **Is this behaviour guaranteed in the original code?**
　*Answer*：I describe it as the design requirement. In the code snapshot two parts were weaker, for example a fall branch that resets rather than explicitly zeroes the output, so I list them as hardening items and I don't claim it can never enable badly.
　中文：我把它说成设计要求。代码快照里有两处较弱，比如跌倒分支只复位而没显式清零输出，所以我把它们列为加固项，不声称不可能带病使能。

**关键词与易错点**：stale target / integrator / offset、five-step initialisation、reset function、ramp 0.3 s、ten cycles。数字为统一设计取值；对"保证不会"要保守措辞。

## 案例 11. 手臂总线间歇丢回包：接头接触不良与总线时序（P2）

**适用题型**：Tell me about a hardware problem you diagnosed; How do you debug intermittent communication loss; Describe a time hardware failed at a bad moment.

## 案例 12. 从 FP16 到 INT8：用 0.03 的 mAP 换 17 ms 的推理时间（P4）

## 案例 13. 设备时钟回拨使状态被误判陈旧（P6，设计级案例）

**适用题型**：How do you handle time synchronisation; Tell me about a bug that only appeared in a deployed environment; How do you design validity checks.
**English story（STAR + Reflection，约 310 词）**：

> **Situation.** At Kaihong I work on the robot state layer of a multi-robot platform on OpenHarmony, giving the motion-control and task layers a consistent, trusted state. >
> **Task.** My job was to keep the state trustworthy without making the layer ignore real stale data, and to make sure a clock jump on one device did not look like a failure of the robot.

I added clock-jump fault injection to the test suite, forward and backward, with the expected behaviour written first: the state stays valid, a suspect flag and a health event appear, and nothing is declared stale because of the jump.
>
> **Result.** In our design review and tests, a jump no longer changed the stale decision, and the health event told the operator what happened. These are design-level values and I would measure the real numbers on the target platform.
>
> **Reflection.** Never compute freshness from a clock that someone else can change. Use a monotonic local clock for ages and treat the device's stamp as information.

## 案例 14. 重连后任务被重复下发：至少一次投递、幂等与安全执行链（P6，设计级案例）

**适用题型**：How do you ensure safe execution when a planner may be wrong; Tell me about a safety decision; What is exactly-once and why can't you have it.
**English story（STAR + Reflection，约 335 词）**：

> **Situation.** At Kaihong, task plans from schedulers, an operator console and planners reach robots over M-DDS. A plan cannot go straight to a robot. It passes an action proposal, a policy check, execution and state feedback. In a bench test of our design, a robot reconnected after a network drop and the same navigation task ran twice.
>
> **Task.** I was responsible for the proposal, check, execute and feedback chain, so I had to stop duplicates from becoming physical actions, without giving up the retries that make the system reliable.
>
> **Action.** I started from the semantics. Over an unreliable network a sender cannot tell a lost request from a lost reply, so it must retry, and that means at-least-once delivery. The effect of exactly-once comes from at-least-once plus an idempotent executor. Each proposal has an identity, a validity period and an idempotency key. The robot de-duplicates by the key, and the de-duplication table is kept about twice the proposal validity, 60 seconds by default, which is longer than the retry window of three attempts with 0.5, 1 and 2 second back-off. The table is in memory, so I snapshot it to a local file every 5 seconds, and after a restart the service stays silent for one validity period before accepting new proposals, otherwise an old proposal could look new. The execution gateway also keeps its own de-duplication, because one check is not enough. The check runs as a separate service, and only an approved proposal receives a short-lived signed ticket. Before execution the state is checked again, to reduce the gap between check and use. After execution I read the state back, instead of trusting the acknowledgement. A stop is always allowed.
>
> **Result.** In our design tests, a duplicate proposal returns the earlier result and moves nothing. These are design-level values, not company measurements.
>
> **Reflection.** I design for the failure, not for the happy path. A gate limits the envelope, so I also want independent hardware protection.

**中文翻译**：
情境：在深开鸿，来自调度器、操作台和规划器的任务计划通过 M-DDS 到达机器人。计划不能直接发给机器人，要经过动作提案、策略检查、执行和状态反馈。在我们设计的一次台架测试里，机器人在断网后重连，同一个导航任务被执行了两次。任务：提案—检查—执行—反馈链归我负责，所以我要阻止重复变成物理动作，同时又不放弃让系统可靠的重试。行动：我从语义出发。在不可靠网络上，发送方分不清是请求丢了还是回复丢了，所以必须重试，这意味着"至少一次"投递。"恰好一次"的效果来自"至少一次"加幂等的执行器。每个提案有身份、有效期和幂等键。机器人按键去重，去重表保留时间约为提案有效期的两倍（默认 60 s），比三次重试（退避 0.5、1、2 s）的窗口更长。表在内存里，所以我每 5 s 把它快照到本地文件；重启后服务先静默一个有效期再接新提案，否则旧提案可能显得像新的。执行网关也有自己的去重，因为一处检查不够。检查作为独立服务运行，只有通过的提案才获得短时有效的签名票据。执行前再次检查状态，减小"检查到使用"之间的间隙。执行后我读回状态，而不是只相信回执。停止始终放行。结果：在我们的设计测试中，重复提案返回之前的结果，不产生任何动作。这些是设计级数值，不是公司实测。反思：我为失效设计，而不只是为顺利路径。关口只限定动作包络，所以我还希望有独立的硬件保护。

**30 秒浓缩版（English short version）**：
> In our design a reconnect caused a duplicate task. Delivery is at least once, so execution must be idempotent: idempotency keys, a de-duplication window of twice the validity, a quiet period after restart, a separate check service with short-lived tickets, and a read-back of the state.

中文：在我们的设计里，重连导致任务重复。投递是至少一次，所以执行必须幂等：幂等键、两倍有效期的去重窗口、重启后的静默期、带短时票据的独立检查服务，以及状态读回。

**可能的 4 个追问**：

1. **Why can't you have exactly-once delivery?**
　*Answer*：Because the sender cannot distinguish a lost request from a lost reply over an unreliable network. So we use at-least-once delivery and make the execution idempotent. The result looks like exactly-once, provided the de-duplication window is longer than the retry window.
　中文：因为在不可靠网络上发送方无法区分请求丢了还是回复丢了；所以用至少一次投递并让执行幂等，只要去重窗口长于重试窗口，效果就像恰好一次。
2. **Why a separate check service and a ticket?**
　*Answer*：A library call inside the same process can be bypassed by a bug or a shortcut. A separate process, and a gateway that accepts only commands with a signed short-lived ticket, means a bypass has to cross a process boundary. The ticket also expires, which narrows the time-of-check to time-of-use gap.
　中文：同进程的库调用可被 bug 或捷径绕过；独立进程加只接受带短时签名票据命令的网关，意味着绕过要跨越进程边界；票据过期也缩小了检查到使用的时间间隙。
3. **What does the gate not guarantee?**
　*Answer*：It does not guarantee that the plan is a good plan or that the sensor data is correct. It limits the envelope of actions. So I also rely on device-side limits, a watchdog and independent hardware protection, such as an emergency stop.
　中文：不保证计划好，也不保证传感器数据正确；它限定动作包络。所以我还依赖设备侧限制、看门狗和独立的硬件保护（如急停）。
4. **What did you do in the gardening project that is similar?**
　*Answer*：There the language model could only give recommendations or constrained requests, which passed a whitelist and policy checks. The device keeps the hard limits: a 60 second maximum run, a cooldown and a safe power-up default, and the device limit is the floor.
　中文：园艺项目里语言模型只能给建议或受约束的请求，需通过白名单和策略检查；设备端保留硬限制：最长运行 60 s、冷却、安全的上电默认值，设备限值是底线。

**关键词与易错点**：at-least-once + idempotency key、dedup window 2× validity (60 s)、snapshot 5 s、quiet period after restart、signed ticket、read-back、stop always allowed。P6 一律说 in our design；不说 exactly-once delivery；不讲内部接口名与具体规则。

## 4. 自测与练习安排

### 4.1 自测题

1. 用一句话说出 14 个案例的"数字钩子"：例如 99 分位抖动 3.5→1.0 ms（案例 1）、负载 78%→63%（案例 2）、延迟 45→30 ms（案例 3）、60 ms→5 ms（案例 4）。

3. 饱和类案例怎么区分"增益过大"与"执行器余量不足"？（案例 7、案例 8；P1 参考上限 0.15→0.10 rad 可作补充）
4. 两个"状态不一致"案例（9、10）的区别是什么？（偏置 + 时间错位 vs 三个陈旧量叠加）
5. 案例 11 的两个原因（物理与软件时序）怎么分开？
6. 案例 12 为什么接受 mAP 下降？事件门做了什么？
7. 案例 13 与案例 14 里哪些话必须加 "in our design"？
8. 把案例 10 改写成"最难的 bug""失败""团队协作"三种问法下的 90 秒开场。

### 4.2 三轮练习

| 轮次 | 目标 | 做法 | 用时 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 第一轮：骨架 | 记住 14 个案例的 S/T/A/R/Reflection 一句话 | 每天读 3 个案例，只写五句话骨架，不看原文 | 约 5 天 |
| 第二轮：口述 | 每个案例 2 分钟讲完 | 遮住英文，只看标题和数字钩子，计时说并录音，对照关键词 | 约 7 天 |
| 第三轮：追问 | 接住四类追问：度量、取舍、回归、重来 | 找人或自己随机抽案例，每个案例连续追问四次 | 约 5 天 |

### 4.3 提醒

- 一场面试里同一个案例最多用两次，且第二次必须换角度。
- 最容易被追问的案例：4（时间同步）、6（前馈符号与对照实验）、7（饱和）、10（重新使能）、12（量化）。这五个要能讲到数字、方法、局限三层。
- 每个数字前加 about，每个设计级案例前加 in our design。
