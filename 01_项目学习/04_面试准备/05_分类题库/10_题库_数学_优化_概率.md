## 2. A 线性代数（Q1–Q16）

### Q1. What are eigenvalues and eigenvectors, and why do they matter in control?
**中文题意**：什么是特征值和特征向量？为什么在控制里重要？
**考察点**：能否把定义和"系统极点"联系起来。
**项目关联**：P1（线性化四状态倒立摆的不稳定模态）、P7（LQR 闭环极点）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> An eigenvector of a matrix A is a direction that A only stretches or flips, without turning it. So A v equals lambda v, and lambda is the eigenvalue, the stretch factor. In control this is very useful. For a linear system x dot equals A x, the eigenvalues of A are the poles of the system. If every eigenvalue has a negative real part, all modes decay and the system is stable. If one has a positive real part, one mode grows exponentially. For example, the linearised inverted pendulum has one positive real eigenvalue, which is the falling mode. That is why the upright position is unstable without feedback, and why feedback gain K tries to move the eigenvalues of A minus B K into the left half plane.

**中文翻译**：矩阵 A 的特征向量是 A 只拉伸或翻转、不旋转的方向，即 A v 等于 lambda v，lambda 是特征值，也就是拉伸倍数。在控制里很有用：对线性系统 x 的导数等于 A x，A 的特征值就是系统的极点。所有特征值实部为负时各模态衰减，系统稳定；有一个实部为正时就有一个指数增长的模态。例如线性化的倒立摆有一个正实特征值，就是倒下的模态，所以直立位置不加反馈是不稳定的，反馈增益 K 的作用就是把 A 减 B K 的特征值移到左半平面。
**追问 1**：*Do complex eigenvalues matter?* Yes. A complex pair means oscillation: the imaginary part is the oscillation frequency and the real part is the decay rate. A pair with small negative real part rings for a long time.
　中文：重要。复共轭对表示振荡，虚部是振荡频率，实部是衰减速率；实部负得很小的一对会振铃很久。
**追问 2**：*Are eigenvalues enough to judge stability for a time-varying system?* No. For a time-varying system the frozen-time eigenvalues do not guarantee stability, so you need a Lyapunov argument or a slow-variation condition.
　中文：不够。时变系统的"冻结时刻"特征值不能保证稳定，需要李雅普诺夫论证或缓变条件。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In my ballbot work, the linearised model has an unstable pole, and the LQR analysis showed how much control effort is needed to bring the closed-loop poles into the left half plane.*
　中文：在我的球形机器人工作里，线性化模型有一个不稳定极点，LQR 分析说明了把闭环极点拉回左半平面需要多大控制量。
**关键词与易错点**：eigenvalue、pole、left half plane、real part。不要说"特征值就是极点"而不加 "of A"；也不要说有一个特征值为零就一定"不稳定"（它是临界稳定或需要进一步分析）。

### Q2. What is the singular value decomposition, and where have you used the idea?
**中文题意**：什么是奇异值分解？你在哪里用过这个思想？
**考察点**：SVD 的几何含义，与雅可比、伪逆的联系。
**项目关联**：P2（雅可比最小奇异值监控、阻尼最小二乘）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Any matrix A can be written as U Sigma V transpose, where U and V are orthogonal and Sigma is diagonal with non-negative singular values. Geometrically, A first rotates in the input space, then stretches along each axis by a singular value, then rotates again in the output space. The largest singular value is the biggest gain, and the smallest one is the weakest direction. For a robot Jacobian, the smallest singular value tells me how close the arm is to a singularity. When it goes to zero, some end-effector direction needs very large joint speed. That is exactly what I watch in the teleoperation project.

**中文翻译**：任何矩阵 A 都可写成 U Sigma V 转置，U 和 V 是正交矩阵，Sigma 是对角的、元素非负的奇异值矩阵。几何上，A 先在输入空间旋转，再沿各个轴按奇异值拉伸，最后在输出空间再旋转。最大奇异值是最大增益，最小奇异值是最弱的方向。对机器人雅可比来说，最小奇异值告诉我机械臂离奇异位形有多近：它趋于零时，某个末端方向需要极大的关节速度。这正是我在遥操作项目里监控的量。
**追问 1**：*How is SVD related to the pseudo-inverse?* The pseudo-inverse is V Sigma plus U transpose, where Sigma plus inverts the non-zero singular values and keeps zeros as zeros.
　中文：伪逆是 V Sigma 加 U 转置，Sigma 加是把非零奇异值取倒数、零保持为零。
**追问 2**：*How is it different from eigen-decomposition?* Eigen-decomposition needs a square matrix and may have complex values. SVD works for any shape and always gives real, non-negative singular values.
　中文：特征分解要求方阵，可能出现复数；SVD 对任意形状的矩阵都成立，奇异值总是实的、非负的。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P2 I flagged the arm as near-singular when the smallest singular value of the Jacobian dropped below about 0.05, and reduced the target speed.*
　中文：在 P2 里，当雅可比最小奇异值低于约 0.05 时，我把手臂标为近奇异并降低目标速度。
**关键词与易错点**：singular value、orthogonal、pseudo-inverse、near-singular。不要把奇异值说成特征值的平方根而不说"of A transpose A"；对非对称矩阵两者不同。

### Q3. Explain rank, column space and null space in plain words.
**中文题意**：用通俗语言解释秩、列空间和零空间。
**考察点**：基本概念，以及与冗余机械臂的联系。
**项目关联**：P2（七自由度机械臂的零空间）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The column space of a matrix is the set of all outputs it can produce. The rank is the dimension of that space, which is the number of independent directions the matrix can reach. The null space is the set of inputs that the matrix sends to zero. By the rank-nullity theorem, the rank plus the dimension of the null space equals the number of columns. In robotics this is very concrete. For a seven-joint arm tracking a six-dimensional pose, the Jacobian is six by seven. Its rank is at most six, so there is at least a one-dimensional null space. Those are joint motions that do not move the end effector, and we can use them for secondary goals like staying away from joint limits.

**中文翻译**：矩阵的列空间是它能产生的所有输出；秩是这个空间的维数，即矩阵能到达的独立方向数；零空间是被矩阵映射成零的所有输入。由秩—零度定理，秩加零空间维数等于列数。在机器人里很具体：七个关节的臂跟踪六维位姿，雅可比是 6 乘 7，秩至多为 6，所以零空间至少一维。那些关节运动不移动末端，可以用来实现次要目标，比如远离关节限位。
**追问 1**：*What happens to the rank at a singularity?* The Jacobian loses rank, so some end-effector direction becomes unreachable and the corresponding joint velocity blows up in the inverse.
　中文：奇异位形处雅可比降秩，某个末端方向变得不可达，求逆时对应的关节速度会发散。
**追问 2**：*How do you project a secondary task into the null space?* Use the projector I minus J pseudo-inverse times J, applied to the secondary joint velocity.
　中文：用投影矩阵 I 减 J 伪逆乘 J，作用到次要任务的关节速度上。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P2 each arm has seven joints, so a one-dimensional null space exists; I mainly relied on joint limits and damping rather than a hand-written null-space task.*
　中文：P2 里每条臂七个关节，所以存在一维零空间；我主要依靠关节限位和阻尼，而不是手写零空间任务。
**关键词与易错点**：rank、null space、rank-nullity、redundant。不要说"零空间就是零向量"；它是被映射到零的全体输入。

### Q4. When is a matrix positive definite, and why does it matter?
**中文题意**：矩阵什么时候正定？为什么重要？
**考察点**：正定的判别和在 LQR、协方差、凸优化中的角色。
**项目关联**：P1（LQR 的 Q 与 R）、P5（协方差矩阵、MPC 的 QP）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A symmetric matrix P is positive definite if x transpose P x is positive for every non-zero x. Equivalent tests: all eigenvalues are positive, or all leading principal minors are positive, or it has a Cholesky factorisation. It matters in three places. In LQR, R must be positive definite so the control cost is well defined, and Q should be at least positive semi-definite. In estimation, a covariance matrix must be positive semi-definite, otherwise it makes no sense as a variance. In optimisation, a quadratic cost with a positive definite Hessian is strictly convex, so it has a single global minimum and QP solvers are reliable.

**中文翻译**：对称矩阵 P 正定，是指对所有非零 x，x 转置 P x 都为正。等价判别：所有特征值为正，或所有顺序主子式为正，或存在 Cholesky 分解。它在三处重要：LQR 里 R 必须正定，控制代价才有意义，Q 至少要半正定；估计里协方差矩阵必须半正定，否则不能作为方差；优化里 Hessian 正定的二次代价是严格凸的，有唯一全局最小值，QP 求解器可靠。
**追问 1**：*Why can a covariance matrix lose positive definiteness in a Kalman filter?* Rounding errors and a subtractive update like P minus K H P can make it slightly asymmetric or negative. The Joseph form or symmetrising keeps it valid.
　中文：舍入误差和 P 减 K H P 这种相减式更新会让它略不对称甚至出现负特征值，用 Joseph 形式或对称化可保持有效。
**追问 2**：*Is positive semi-definite enough for Q in LQR?* Yes, if the pair A and the square root of Q is detectable, so unstable modes are penalised.
　中文：够，只要 A 与 Q 的平方根构成的对可检测，使不稳定模态都被惩罚。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 I used a diagonal Q with entries four, four, one hundred and one, and R equal to zero point two five, so both are clearly positive.*
　中文：P1 里我用对角 Q，元素 4、4、100、1，R 取 0.25，所以两者都明确为正。
**关键词与易错点**：positive definite、semi-definite、Cholesky、strictly convex。不要把"所有元素为正"当成正定的判据，两者无关。

### Q5. How do you solve a least-squares problem, and what is the pseudo-inverse?
**中文题意**：如何解最小二乘？伪逆是什么？
**考察点**：正规方程、QR、SVD 的取舍。
**项目关联**：P2（IK 的最小二乘）、P5（EKF/位姿图本质上是加权最小二乘）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> For an over-determined system A x equals b with more equations than unknowns, I minimise the squared residual. Setting the gradient to zero gives the normal equations: A transpose A x equals A transpose b. If A has full column rank, x equals A transpose A inverse times A transpose b, and that matrix in front of b is the pseudo-inverse. In practice I would not form A transpose A, because it squares the condition number. I would use a QR factorisation or the SVD, which are more stable numerically. For an under-determined system, the pseudo-inverse gives the solution with the smallest norm, which is the natural choice for inverse kinematics.

**中文翻译**：方程多于未知数的超定系统 A x 等于 b，我最小化残差平方和。令梯度为零得到正规方程：A 转置 A x 等于 A 转置 b。A 列满秩时，x 等于 A 转置 A 的逆乘 A 转置 b，b 前面的矩阵就是伪逆。实际中我不会显式构造 A 转置 A，因为它会把条件数平方，我会用 QR 分解或 SVD，数值上更稳。对欠定系统，伪逆给出范数最小的解，这正是逆运动学的自然选择。
**追问 1**：*Why does A transpose A square the condition number?* Because the singular values of A transpose A are the squares of those of A, so the ratio of largest to smallest is squared.
　中文：因为 A 转置 A 的奇异值是 A 的奇异值的平方，最大与最小之比也被平方。
**追问 2**：*How do you weight the residuals?* Minimise the sum of residual transpose W residual, where W is the inverse covariance. This is the maximum-likelihood solution under Gaussian noise.
　中文：最小化残差转置 W 残差之和，W 取噪声协方差的逆，这是高斯噪声下的极大似然解。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5, localisation and trajectory optimisation are both nonlinear least-squares problems at heart, solved by linearising and repeating this step.*
　中文：P5 里，定位和轨迹优化本质上都是非线性最小二乘，通过线性化并重复这一步来求解。
**关键词与易错点**：normal equations、QR、pseudo-inverse、minimum norm。不要说伪逆"总是"存在逆；它总存在，但不等于普通逆。

### Q6. What is damped least squares, and why do you use it near singularities?
**中文题意**：什么是阻尼最小二乘？为什么在奇异位形附近使用？
**考察点**：对 P2 核心方法的理解，包含推导。
**项目关联**：P2（约束 IK，全局阻尼 10 的负 3 次方，近奇异监控）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The plain pseudo-inverse of the Jacobian J has singular values one over sigma. Near a singularity sigma is tiny, so a small task error becomes a huge joint velocity. Damped least squares adds a penalty on the joint velocity. Instead of only minimising the task error, I minimise the task error squared plus lambda squared times the joint velocity squared. The solution is J transpose times J J transpose plus lambda squared I, inverse. Each singular value sigma now becomes sigma over sigma squared plus lambda squared, which is bounded by one over two lambda. So the speed cannot explode. The price is a small tracking error, and I choose lambda as a trade-off between smoothness and accuracy.

**中文翻译**：雅可比 J 的朴素伪逆含有 1/sigma 项，奇异点附近 sigma 很小，很小的任务误差就会变成极大的关节速度。阻尼最小二乘对关节速度加惩罚：不只最小化任务误差，而是最小化任务误差平方加 lambda 平方乘关节速度平方。解为 J 转置乘 (J J 转置加 lambda 平方 I) 的逆。每个奇异值 sigma 变成 sigma 除以 (sigma 平方加 lambda 平方)，其上界是 1/(2 lambda)，所以速度不会发散。代价是出现小的跟踪误差，我把 lambda 当作平滑与精度的折中来选。
**追问 1**：*How did you choose the damping value?* I used a small global damping of about ten to the minus three, and a larger task-level damping of about zero point zero five, then checked by slowly reaching toward the workspace boundary twenty times and watching joint speeds.
　中文：全局阻尼取约 10 的负 3 次方，任务级阻尼约 0.05，然后缓慢伸向工作空间边界 20 次，观察关节速度是否超限。
**追问 2**：*Is a fixed damping optimal?* Not really. Adaptive damping, which grows only when the smallest singular value is small, gives less error far from singularities.
　中文：不是最优。自适应阻尼只在最小奇异值小时增大，远离奇异点时误差更小。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*That is exactly the trade-off in my VR teleoperation task: I accept a small lag near the boundary to avoid velocity amplification.*
　中文：这正是我 VR 遥操作任务中的折中：在边界附近接受小的滞后，以避免速度放大。
**关键词与易错点**：damping、Levenberg–Marquardt、bounded、velocity amplification。不要说阻尼让解"更准"；阻尼牺牲精度换取有界性。

### Q7. What is a condition number, and what does a large one mean?
**中文题意**：什么是条件数？它大意味着什么？
**考察点**：数值稳定性，与奇异性的联系。
**项目关联**：P2（雅可比条件数）、P1/P7（病态模型）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The condition number of a matrix is the ratio of its largest singular value to its smallest one. It measures how much a relative error in the input can be amplified in the output when I solve A x equals b. If the condition number is about ten to the six, then I may lose up to six decimal digits in the solution. A large condition number means the matrix is nearly singular or badly scaled. Typical fixes are rescaling the variables, using a more stable factorisation, or adding regularisation. For a Jacobian, a large condition number means the arm is close to a singular configuration.

**中文翻译**：矩阵的条件数是最大奇异值与最小奇异值之比，衡量求解 A x 等于 b 时输入的相对误差能被放大多少。条件数约 10 的 6 次方，意味着解可能损失多达六位十进制有效数字。条件数大说明矩阵接近奇异或尺度很差。常见办法是重新缩放变量、用更稳定的分解，或加正则化。对雅可比来说，条件数大意味着手臂接近奇异位形。
**追问 1**：*Does a large determinant mean a well-conditioned matrix?* No. Determinant depends on scale. A matrix can have a tiny determinant but be perfectly conditioned, like zero point one times the identity.
　中文：不是。行列式依赖尺度，0.1 乘单位阵行列式很小但条件数完美。
**追问 2**：*What is the condition number of an orthogonal matrix?* It is one, the best possible, which is why rotations do not amplify errors.
　中文：为 1，是最佳值，所以旋转不会放大误差。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 the state variables have different units, so I normalise them before the LQR analysis; otherwise the Riccati solver can be badly conditioned.*
　中文：P1 里各状态的量纲不同，所以在 LQR 分析前先做归一化，否则 Riccati 求解可能病态。
**关键词与易错点**：condition number、ill-conditioned、scaling、regularisation。不要把条件数和行列式混为一谈。

### Q8. What are the properties of a rotation matrix?
**中文题意**：旋转矩阵有哪些性质？
**考察点**：SO(3) 的基本定义。
**项目关联**：P1/P2/P5/P6（所有坐标变换）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A rotation matrix R in three dimensions is orthogonal, so R transpose times R equals the identity, and its determinant is plus one. That means it preserves lengths and angles, and the inverse is simply the transpose. The columns are the axes of the rotated frame expressed in the original frame, and they are mutually perpendicular unit vectors. Rotations are not commutative, so the order of multiplication matters. It has nine numbers but only three degrees of freedom, because of the six constraints from orthogonality. Its eigenvalues are one and a complex pair of unit magnitude, and the eigenvector for one is the rotation axis.

**中文翻译**：三维旋转矩阵 R 是正交矩阵，R 转置乘 R 等于单位阵，行列式为正 1。因此它保持长度和角度，逆就是转置。各列是旋转后坐标系的坐标轴在原坐标系中的表示，是两两垂直的单位向量。旋转不可交换，所以乘法顺序很重要。它有九个数但只有三个自由度，因为正交性带来六个约束。特征值是 1 和一对模为 1 的复数，特征值 1 对应的特征向量就是旋转轴。
**追问 1**：*What if the determinant is minus one?* Then it is a reflection, not a rotation. This often shows up as a sign mistake when a frame is left-handed.
　中文：那是反射而不是旋转，常在坐标系为左手系时作为符号错误出现。
**追问 2**：*How do you fix a rotation matrix that drifted from orthogonality?* Re-orthonormalise it with the SVD or Gram–Schmidt, or keep a quaternion and normalise it.
　中文：用 SVD 或 Gram–Schmidt 重新正交化，或者用四元数并归一化。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P2 I wrote the VR axis mapping as a rotation matrix with determinant plus one, and I checked this because a determinant of minus one would mean a mirrored motion.*
　中文：P2 里我把 VR 轴映射写成行列式为 +1 的旋转矩阵，并专门检查，因为 −1 意味着镜像运动。
**关键词与易错点**：orthogonal、determinant plus one、SO(3)、non-commutative。不要说旋转矩阵有九个自由度。

### Q9. What does the determinant mean geometrically?
**中文题意**：行列式的几何意义是什么？
**考察点**：体积缩放与可逆性。
**项目关联**：P2（坐标系手性检查）、P7（五连杆雅可比奇异）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The determinant of a square matrix is the factor by which it scales volume. If I apply the matrix to a unit cube, the determinant is the signed volume of the result. The sign tells me whether orientation is preserved. If the determinant is zero, the matrix squashes space into a lower dimension, so it is not invertible. For a Jacobian of a square mapping, determinant zero means a singularity. I also use it as a quick sanity check: a rotation must have determinant one, and a proper transform between right-handed frames cannot flip the sign.

**中文翻译**：方阵的行列式是它对体积的缩放因子。把矩阵作用在单位立方体上，行列式就是结果的带符号体积。符号表示方向是否被保持。行列式为零表示矩阵把空间压到更低维度，所以不可逆。对方形雅可比，行列式为零就是奇异。我也把它当作快速检查：旋转的行列式必须为 1，右手系之间的合法变换不会翻转符号。
**追问 1**：*Is the determinant a good measure of nearness to singularity?* Not alone, because it depends on scale. The smallest singular value or the condition number is better.
　中文：单独不好，因为依赖尺度；最小奇异值或条件数更好。
**追问 2**：*How is the determinant related to eigenvalues?* It is the product of the eigenvalues.
　中文：等于所有特征值之积。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P7 the closed-chain leg Jacobian becomes singular when the five-bar linkage is fully stretched, so I keep the leg length inside its working range.*
　中文：P7 里五连杆完全伸直时腿的雅可比奇异，所以我把腿长限制在工作范围内。
**关键词与易错点**：determinant、signed volume、invertible、handedness。不要说行列式小就一定接近奇异。

### Q10. How do you solve a linear system numerically? Compare LU, QR and Cholesky.
**中文题意**：数值上怎么解线性方程组？比较 LU、QR 和 Cholesky。
**考察点**：数值线性代数常识。
**项目关联**：P2（QP 求解内部）、P5（OSQP、位姿图）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I never compute the explicit inverse. For a general square system I use LU factorisation with pivoting, which costs about two thirds n cubed operations. For a symmetric positive definite system, such as a covariance or a Hessian, I use Cholesky, which is about twice as fast and very stable. For least-squares problems I use QR, which avoids squaring the condition number. When the matrix may be rank-deficient, I use the SVD, which is the slowest but the most informative. In embedded settings, I also like to precompute the factorisation once when the matrix is constant, as in a linear MPC with fixed model.

**中文翻译**：我从不显式求逆。一般方阵用带主元的 LU 分解，运算量约 2/3 n 的三次方。对称正定系统（如协方差或 Hessian）用 Cholesky，大约快一倍且很稳定。最小二乘用 QR，避免条件数平方。矩阵可能秩亏时用 SVD，最慢但信息最多。在嵌入式场景，矩阵固定时我喜欢只预先分解一次，例如模型固定的线性 MPC。
**追问 1**：*Why avoid the explicit inverse?* It is slower, less accurate, and loses sparsity.
　中文：更慢、更不准，而且破坏稀疏性。
**追问 2**：*What is pivoting for?* It prevents division by small numbers, which would amplify rounding errors.
　中文：避免除以很小的数而放大舍入误差。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 the MPC solver OSQP factorises its matrices once and warm-starts, which is why one solve takes only a few milliseconds.*
　中文：P5 里 MPC 求解器 OSQP 只分解一次并热启动，所以单次求解只要几毫秒。
**关键词与易错点**：LU、Cholesky、QR、pivoting、sparse。不要说"求逆再乘"是标准做法。

### Q11. Why are the eigenvalues of a symmetric matrix real and the eigenvectors orthogonal?
**中文题意**：为什么对称矩阵的特征值是实数、特征向量正交？
**考察点**：谱定理，与协方差/主成分的联系。
**项目关联**：P5（协方差椭圆）、P4（PCA/ML 背景）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> That is the spectral theorem. A real symmetric matrix can be written as Q Lambda Q transpose, where Q is orthogonal and Lambda is a real diagonal matrix. The short reason is that for a symmetric matrix, the quantity x transpose A x is real, and eigenvectors for different eigenvalues satisfy v1 transpose A v2 computed in two ways, which forces them to be orthogonal. The practical consequence is great. A covariance matrix is symmetric, so its eigenvectors are the principal axes of the uncertainty ellipse, and its eigenvalues are the variances along those axes. That is also the basis of principal component analysis.

**中文翻译**：这就是谱定理。实对称矩阵可写成 Q Lambda Q 转置，Q 正交，Lambda 是实对角矩阵。简要理由是：对称矩阵的 x 转置 A x 是实数，而不同特征值的特征向量，用两种方式计算 v1 转置 A v2，会迫使它们正交。实际意义很大：协方差矩阵对称，所以其特征向量是不确定性椭圆的主轴，特征值是沿主轴的方差。这也是主成分分析的基础。
**追问 1**：*What if the matrix is not symmetric?* Eigenvalues may be complex and eigenvectors need not be orthogonal. A normal matrix is still diagonalisable by a unitary matrix.
　中文：特征值可能是复数，特征向量不一定正交；正规矩阵仍可被酉矩阵对角化。
**追问 2**：*How would you draw the uncertainty ellipse of a 2D pose?* Take the eigen-decomposition of the position covariance, and scale the axes by the square roots of the eigenvalues times a confidence factor.
　中文：对位置协方差做特征分解，轴长取特征值的平方根乘置信系数。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 I look at the AMCL covariance ellipse in RViz to see if the localisation is converging.*
　中文：P5 里我在 RViz 中看 AMCL 的协方差椭圆来判断定位是否收敛。
**关键词与易错点**：spectral theorem、symmetric、principal axes、covariance ellipse。注意椭圆轴长是特征值的平方根（标准差），不是特征值本身。

### Q12. What is the difference between the Frobenius norm, the spectral norm and the trace?
**中文题意**：Frobenius 范数、谱范数和迹有什么区别？
**考察点**：矩阵范数基本概念。
**项目关联**：P5（协方差迹用于判定定位置信）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The trace is the sum of the diagonal entries, which also equals the sum of the eigenvalues. The Frobenius norm is the square root of the sum of all squared entries, equal to the square root of the sum of squared singular values. The spectral norm is the largest singular value, which is the maximum gain of the matrix on a unit vector. For uncertainty, the trace of a covariance matrix is a handy single number: it is the total variance. In my navigation work I used the trace of the pose covariance as a simple confidence check before accepting a large pose correction.

**中文翻译**：迹是对角元素之和，也等于特征值之和。Frobenius 范数是所有元素平方和的平方根，等于奇异值平方和的平方根。谱范数是最大奇异值，即矩阵对单位向量的最大增益。对不确定性来说，协方差的迹是一个方便的单一数字，即总方差。在导航工作中，我在接受较大的位姿修正之前用位姿协方差的迹做简单的置信检查。
**追问 1**：*Which norm bounds the gain of a system?* The spectral norm gives the worst-case gain; the Frobenius norm is an upper bound but can be loose.
　中文：谱范数给出最坏情形增益；Frobenius 范数是上界但可能很松。
**追问 2**：*Is the trace invariant under a change of basis?* Yes, it is invariant under similarity transforms.
　中文：是，在相似变换下不变。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 a large pose jump was only accepted after several consistent updates and when the covariance trace was below about zero point three.*
　中文：P5 里大的位姿跳变只有在多次一致更新且协方差迹低于约 0.3 时才被接受。
**关键词与易错点**：trace、Frobenius norm、spectral norm、total variance。不要说迹等于"最大特征值"。

### Q13. What is an orthogonal projection, and where does it appear?
**中文题意**：什么是正交投影？出现在哪里？
**考察点**：最小二乘的几何解释。
**项目关联**：P2（零空间投影）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> An orthogonal projection maps a vector to the closest point in a subspace. If the subspace is spanned by the columns of A, the projection matrix is A times A transpose A inverse times A transpose. It is symmetric and idempotent, so applying it twice equals applying it once. Least squares is exactly a projection: the fitted value A x hat is the projection of b onto the column space, and the residual is perpendicular to that space. In robotics, the null-space projector I minus J pseudo-inverse J projects a joint velocity onto motions that do not disturb the end effector.

**中文翻译**：正交投影把向量映到子空间里最近的点。若子空间由 A 的列张成，投影矩阵为 A 乘 (A 转置 A) 的逆乘 A 转置。它对称且幂等，作用两次与一次相同。最小二乘恰好是投影：拟合值 A x hat 是 b 在列空间上的投影，残差垂直于该空间。在机器人里，零空间投影器 I 减 J 伪逆 J 把关节速度投影到不影响末端的运动上。
**追问 1**：*Why is the residual perpendicular?* Because at the minimum the gradient is zero, which gives A transpose times the residual equals zero.
　中文：因为最小值处梯度为零，即 A 转置乘残差等于零。
**追问 2**：*What are the eigenvalues of a projection matrix?* Only zero and one.
　中文：只有 0 和 1。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In teleoperation, a secondary posture preference can be added through the null space so it never disturbs the hand target.*
　中文：在遥操作里，次要姿态偏好可通过零空间加入，不影响手部目标。
**关键词与易错点**：projection、idempotent、column space、orthogonal residual。

### Q14. What is manipulability, and how does it relate to the Jacobian?
**中文题意**：可操作度是什么？它与雅可比有何关系？
**考察点**：速度椭球。
**项目关联**：P2（近奇异监控）、P7（腿雅可比）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Take all joint velocities with unit norm. Through the Jacobian they map to an ellipsoid of end-effector velocities. The axes of this ellipsoid are the singular values of the Jacobian, along the left singular vectors. A big ellipsoid in a direction means the arm moves fast there; a flat ellipsoid means it is nearly singular. Yoshikawa's manipulability measure is the product of the singular values, which is the square root of the determinant of J J transpose. I often prefer the smallest singular value, because it shows the weakest direction directly.

**中文翻译**：取所有范数为 1 的关节速度，经雅可比映射成末端速度椭球。椭球的轴长是雅可比的奇异值，方向是左奇异向量。某方向椭球大表示手臂在该方向能快速运动；椭球扁平则接近奇异。Yoshikawa 可操作度是奇异值之积，即 J J 转置行列式的平方根。我常更喜欢最小奇异值，因为它直接指出最弱的方向。
**追问 1**：*What is the force ellipsoid?* It is dual: its axes are the inverses of the singular values, so directions that move easily are weak in force.
　中文：是对偶的，轴长为奇异值的倒数，容易运动的方向承力弱。
**追问 2**：*Why is it relevant for a wheel-legged robot?* Because leg stretch changes the Jacobian and the force the leg can output at the wheel.
　中文：因为腿伸缩会改变雅可比和腿在轮端能输出的力。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P2 I recorded the smallest singular value at ten hertz and coloured it red below about zero point zero five.*
　中文：P2 里我以 10 Hz 记录最小奇异值，低于约 0.05 时标红。
**关键词与易错点**：manipulability ellipsoid、singular values、weakest direction。

### Q15. What is the matrix exponential, and why do we need it?
**中文题意**：矩阵指数是什么？为什么需要？
**考察点**：线性系统解与离散化。
**项目关联**：P1/P7（离散化 LQR）、P3（数字实现）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The matrix exponential e to the A t is defined by the same power series as the scalar exponential. It is the solution operator of the linear system x dot equals A x: the state at time t is e to the A t times the initial state. We need it for exact discretisation. With a sampling time T and a zero-order hold input, the discrete matrices are A d equals e to the A T, and B d is the integral of e to the A s times B over zero to T. In practice I compute it with a library routine, such as a Padé approximation, rather than the series. For small T, A d is close to I plus A T.

**中文翻译**：矩阵指数 e 的 A t 次方，由与标量指数相同的幂级数定义。它是线性系统 x 导数等于 A x 的解算子：时刻 t 的状态等于 e 的 A t 次方乘初始状态。我们用它做精确离散化：采样时间 T 且输入零阶保持时，离散矩阵 A d 等于 e 的 A T 次方，B d 等于 e 的 A s 次方乘 B 在 0 到 T 上的积分。实际中用库函数（例如 Padé 近似）计算，而不是级数。T 很小时 A d 近似 I 加 A T。
**追问 1**：*How do the eigenvalues of A d relate to those of A?* They are e to the lambda T, so stability moves from the left half plane to the unit disk.
　中文：是 e 的 lambda T 次方，稳定性判据由左半平面变为单位圆内。
**追问 2**：*When is the Euler approximation not good enough?* When T times the fastest eigenvalue magnitude is not small, or for stiff or lightly damped systems.
　中文：当 T 乘最快特征值的模不够小，或系统刚性、阻尼很小时。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P7 the control loop runs at about 500 hertz, so T is about two milliseconds, which is small enough that the choice between Euler and zero-order hold matters little for the gains.*
　中文：P7 里控制循环约 500 Hz，T 约 2 ms，足够小，所以欧拉与零阶保持的选择对增益影响不大。
**关键词与易错点**：matrix exponential、zero-order hold、unit disk、Padé。不要说 e 的 A 次方等于逐元素取指数。

### Q16. How would you check that a system is controllable using linear algebra?
**中文题意**：怎么用线性代数检验系统可控？
**考察点**：可控性矩阵的秩。
**项目关联**：P1/P7（倒立摆可控）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> For a linear system with n states, I build the controllability matrix: B, A B, A squared B, and so on up to A to the n minus one times B. The system is controllable if this matrix has full rank n. Intuitively, the columns are the directions I can push the state into, directly or after the dynamics propagate the push. If the rank is less than n, some mode cannot be influenced by the input. For the four-state inverted pendulum, the rank is four, so we can place poles or design an LQR. By duality, observability is the same test with C and A transpose.

**中文翻译**：对 n 个状态的线性系统，构造可控性矩阵：B、A B、A 平方 B，一直到 A 的 n 减 1 次方乘 B。若该矩阵满秩 n，则系统可控。直观上各列是我能直接推、或经动力学传播后推到的状态方向。秩小于 n 时，有些模态无法被输入影响。四状态倒立摆的秩为 4，所以可以配置极点或设计 LQR。由对偶性，可观性用 C 和 A 转置做同样的检验。
**追问 1**：*Is full rank enough in practice?* Mathematically yes, but a nearly rank-deficient matrix means poor controllability, so check the singular values or the Gramian.
　中文：数学上够，但近似秩亏意味着可控性很差，要看奇异值或可控性格拉姆矩阵。
**追问 2**：*What if some unstable mode is uncontrollable?* The system cannot be stabilised. If it is only stable modes, it is stabilisable.
　中文：若不稳定模态不可控则无法镇定；若只是稳定模态不可控，则系统可镇定。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 the four-state pendulum model is controllable, which is why the LQR analysis is meaningful; the same rank test is a quick check whenever I change the model.*
　中文：P1 里四状态倒立摆模型是可控的，所以 LQR 分析才有意义；改模型时我也用同样的秩检验做快速检查。
**关键词与易错点**：controllability matrix、full rank、stabilisable、duality。不要把"可控"与"稳定"混淆。

### Q17. Why do we linearise a nonlinear system, and how do you do it?
**中文题意**：为什么要把非线性系统线性化？怎么做？
**考察点**：泰勒展开、平衡点、适用范围。
**项目关联**：P1（四状态倒立摆线性化）、P7（腿长不同的线性化点）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Most control tools, such as pole placement, LQR and frequency analysis, are developed for linear systems. So I take the nonlinear model x dot equals f of x and u, pick an equilibrium point, and expand with a first-order Taylor series. The matrix A is the partial derivative of f with respect to x at that point, and B is the partial derivative with respect to u. For the inverted pendulum, I use sine theta approximately equal to theta, and cosine theta approximately one for small tilt. The result is only valid near the equilibrium. If the robot is pushed far away, the model error grows, so I need margins, saturation handling, or gain scheduling.

**中文翻译**：大多数控制工具（极点配置、LQR、频域分析）都是为线性系统建立的。所以我取非线性模型 x 导数等于 f(x, u)，选一个平衡点，做一阶泰勒展开。矩阵 A 是 f 对 x 在该点的偏导，B 是对 u 的偏导。对倒立摆，小倾角下用 sin theta 近似 theta，cos theta 近似 1。结果只在平衡点附近有效。机器人被推到远处时模型误差变大，所以需要裕度、饱和处理或增益调度。
**追问 1**：*How do you know the linear model is valid enough?* Compare the nonlinear and linear responses in simulation for the largest tilt I expect, and check the closed loop on the nonlinear model.
　中文：在仿真中对预期的最大倾角比较非线性与线性响应，并在非线性模型上验证闭环。
**追问 2**：*What if the equilibrium is not at zero?* Shift coordinates so the deviation from the equilibrium is the state, and linearise there.
　中文：平移坐标，把偏离平衡点的量作为状态，在那里线性化。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 I linearised a four-state inverted pendulum around the upright position and used the result for the LQR analysis; in P7 the linearisation changes with leg length, which is why the gains are scheduled.*
　中文：P1 在直立位置线性化四状态倒立摆，用于 LQR 分析；P7 里线性化随腿长变化，所以增益需要调度。
**关键词与易错点**：Taylor series、equilibrium、small-angle、valid region。不要说线性化模型对全工作范围都成立。

### Q18. What are the gradient, the Jacobian and the Hessian?
**中文题意**：梯度、雅可比和 Hessian 分别是什么？
**考察点**：多元微积分基本概念。
**项目关联**：P2（运动学雅可比）、P5（EKF 雅可比、优化的 Hessian）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> For a scalar function of many variables, the gradient is the vector of partial derivatives, and it points in the direction of steepest increase. For a vector function, the Jacobian is the matrix of all partial derivatives, with one row per output and one column per input. It is the best linear approximation of the function near a point. The Hessian is the matrix of second derivatives of a scalar function, and it describes curvature. At a minimum, the gradient is zero and the Hessian is positive semi-definite. In robotics the Jacobian maps joint velocity to end-effector velocity, and in the EKF it linearises the motion and measurement models.

**中文翻译**：对多变量标量函数，梯度是偏导数组成的向量，指向上升最快的方向。对向量函数，雅可比是所有偏导数组成的矩阵，每个输出一行、每个输入一列，是函数在某点附近的最佳线性近似。Hessian 是标量函数二阶导数组成的矩阵，描述曲率。极小值处梯度为零、Hessian 半正定。机器人里雅可比把关节速度映到末端速度，EKF 里用它线性化运动模型和观测模型。
**追问 1**：*What is the second-order condition for a local minimum?* Gradient zero and Hessian positive definite is sufficient; positive semi-definite is necessary.
　中文：梯度为零且 Hessian 正定是充分条件，半正定是必要条件。
**追问 2**：*Why do EKF Jacobians sometimes cause trouble?* Because the linearisation is only accurate near the current estimate; a poor estimate gives a poor Jacobian and the filter can diverge.
　中文：线性化只在当前估计附近准确，估计差则雅可比差，滤波器可能发散。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 the EKF uses the Jacobian of the car-like motion model, and in P2 and P7 the kinematic Jacobian maps joint speeds or forces to the task space.*
　中文：P5 的 EKF 使用车式运动模型的雅可比；P2、P7 里运动学雅可比把关节速度或力映射到任务空间。
**关键词与易错点**：gradient、Jacobian、Hessian、curvature。注意 Jacobian 的行列方向约定：行是输出、列是输入。

### Q19. Explain the chain rule and how it connects to backpropagation.
**中文题意**：解释链式法则及其与反向传播的联系。
**考察点**：深度学习的数学基础。
**项目关联**：P4（YOLO 训练）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The chain rule says that if y depends on u, and u depends on x, then the derivative of y with respect to x is the product of dy over du and du over dx. A neural network is a composition of many functions, so the gradient of the loss with respect to a weight in an early layer is a long product of local derivatives. Backpropagation computes this efficiently: it goes through the network once forward to store the activations, and then once backward, reusing the partial products. So the cost of the gradient is only a small multiple of the forward pass, not one pass per weight.

**中文翻译**：链式法则说，若 y 依赖 u，u 依赖 x，则 y 对 x 的导数等于 dy/du 乘 du/dx。神经网络是许多函数的复合，所以损失对早期层某个权重的梯度是一长串局部导数的乘积。反向传播高效地计算它：先前向走一遍并保存激活值，再反向走一遍，复用中间乘积。所以梯度的计算代价只是前向的几倍，而不是每个权重一次前向。
**追问 1**：*Why do gradients vanish in deep networks?* Repeated multiplication of derivatives smaller than one, for example from saturating activations, shrinks the gradient exponentially. Residual connections and normalisation help.
　中文：小于 1 的导数反复相乘（如饱和激活）使梯度指数缩小，残差连接和归一化可缓解。
**追问 2**：*What is automatic differentiation?* A systematic way of applying the chain rule on a computational graph; backpropagation is its reverse mode.
　中文：在计算图上系统地应用链式法则；反向传播是其反向模式。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P4 I fine-tuned a YOLOv5s model, so I used backpropagation through the framework; my own work was the data, the deployment and the INT8 conversion.*
　中文：P4 里我微调了 YOLOv5s，反向传播由框架完成；我自己的工作是数据、部署和 INT8 转换。
**关键词与易错点**：chain rule、local derivative、reverse mode、vanishing gradient。

### Q20. What is the time constant of a first-order system?
**中文题意**：一阶系统的时间常数是什么？
**考察点**：基础动态响应。
**项目关联**：P1（轮速低通）、P4（土壤传感）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> For a first-order system tau times y dot plus y equals u, a step input makes the output rise as one minus e to the minus t over tau. The time constant tau is the time to reach about sixty-three percent of the final value. After three tau it is about ninety-five percent, and after four tau about ninety-eight percent. The pole is at minus one over tau, so a smaller tau means a faster system. A first-order low-pass filter is the common example: the cutoff frequency is one over two pi tau in hertz. I use this to relate a filter delay to its bandwidth.

**中文翻译**：对一阶系统 tau 乘 y 导数加 y 等于 u，阶跃输入使输出按 1 减 e 的负 t/tau 次方上升。时间常数 tau 是达到终值约 63% 的时间；3 tau 时约 95%，4 tau 时约 98%。极点在负 1/tau，tau 越小系统越快。一阶低通滤波器是常见例子：截止频率为 1/(2 pi tau) 赫兹。我用它把滤波延迟与带宽联系起来。
**追问 1**：*How would you measure tau from data?* Apply a step, find the time at which the output reaches sixty-three percent, or fit a line to the logarithm of the remaining error.
　中文：施加阶跃，找输出到 63% 的时刻，或对剩余误差取对数后拟合直线。
**追问 2**：*What is the relation between a low-pass cutoff and delay?* Roughly, the lower the cutoff, the larger the lag, about one over two pi times the cutoff in seconds for a first-order filter.
　中文：截止频率越低滞后越大，一阶滤波器的时间常数约为 1/(2 pi 乘截止频率) 秒。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 I used a roughly 0.8 hertz low-pass on the wheel-speed signal for the outer loop, which gives a delay of about 0.2 seconds, acceptable for an outer loop at about 0.15 hertz.*
　中文：P1 里外环速度估计用约 0.8 Hz 低通，延迟约 0.2 s，对约 0.15 Hz 的外环可接受。
**关键词与易错点**：time constant、sixty-three percent、low-pass、pole。不要把时间常数说成"调节时间"。

### Q21. For a second-order system, explain damping ratio, natural frequency, overshoot and settling time.
**中文题意**：对二阶系统，解释阻尼比、固有频率、超调和调节时间。
**考察点**：经典控制指标与极点位置的关系。
**项目关联**：P1/P3/P7（PID 与 LQR 整定）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A standard second-order system has a natural frequency omega n and a damping ratio zeta. If zeta is less than one, the response oscillates. The percent overshoot depends only on zeta: it equals e to the minus pi zeta over the square root of one minus zeta squared. For zeta equal to zero point seven, overshoot is about four to five percent. The two-percent settling time is roughly four divided by zeta times omega n. So a higher natural frequency makes the response faster, and a higher damping makes it smoother. These are exact for a pure second-order system and only a guide for higher-order systems with extra poles and zeros.

**中文翻译**：标准二阶系统有固有频率 omega n 和阻尼比 zeta。zeta 小于 1 时响应振荡。百分比超调只由 zeta 决定，等于 e 的负 pi zeta 除以根号下 (1 减 zeta 平方) 次方。zeta 取 0.7 时超调约 4% 到 5%。2% 调节时间约为 4 除以 (zeta 乘 omega n)。所以固有频率高则响应快，阻尼高则更平滑。这些对纯二阶系统是精确的，对有额外极零点的高阶系统只是参考。
**追问 1**：*Where are the poles for zeta less than one?* At minus zeta omega n plus or minus j omega n times the square root of one minus zeta squared.
　中文：在 负 zeta omega n 加减 j omega n 乘根号(1 减 zeta 平方)。
**追问 2**：*What happens to overshoot if there is a zero in the system?* A left-half-plane zero near the poles typically increases the overshoot; a right-half-plane zero causes initial undershoot.
　中文：靠近极点的左半平面零点通常增大超调，右半平面零点会造成初始反向。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*When I tuned the P1 attitude loop, I looked for a damping ratio around zero point seven; adding more derivative gain removed the oscillation of about 1.5 hertz that I saw.*
　中文：整定 P1 姿态环时我追求约 0.7 的阻尼比；增加微分增益消除了我看到的约 1.5 Hz 振荡。
**关键词与易错点**：damping ratio、natural frequency、overshoot、settling time。不要说超调取决于固有频率。

### Q22. What is the Laplace transform for, and what is a transfer function?
**中文题意**：拉普拉斯变换有什么用？传递函数是什么？
**考察点**：经典控制的语言。
**项目关联**：P3（PID 的频域分析）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The Laplace transform turns differential equations into algebra. Differentiation in time becomes multiplication by s, so a linear system with zero initial conditions becomes an output equal to a transfer function times the input. The transfer function G of s is the ratio of output to input in the s domain. Its roots in the denominator are the poles, which decide stability and speed, and the roots in the numerator are the zeros. If I put s equal to j omega, I get the frequency response, which tells me gain and phase at each frequency. That is how I discuss bandwidth and phase margin for a PID loop.

**中文翻译**：拉普拉斯变换把微分方程变成代数。时域的微分变为乘 s，所以初始条件为零的线性系统，输出等于传递函数乘输入。传递函数 G(s) 是 s 域里输出与输入之比。分母的根是极点，决定稳定性和速度；分子的根是零点。令 s 等于 j omega 就得到频率响应，给出各频率的增益和相位。我就是这样讨论 PID 回路的带宽和相位裕度的。
**追问 1**：*Why is the transfer function only for linear time-invariant systems?* Because superposition and time invariance are needed for the output to be a product in the s domain.
　中文：因为需要叠加性和时不变性，输出才能写成 s 域的乘积。
**追问 2**：*How does it relate to state space?* G of s equals C times s I minus A inverse times B plus D.
　中文：G(s) 等于 C 乘 (s I 减 A) 的逆乘 B 再加 D。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P3 I reasoned about the gimbal loops in the frequency domain: the inner loop bandwidth was about thirty to forty hertz and the outer loop about four to five hertz.*
　中文：P3 里我用频域分析云台回路：内环带宽约 30–40 Hz，外环约 4–5 Hz。
**关键词与易错点**：Laplace transform、transfer function、poles、frequency response。

### Q23. How do you solve x dot equals A x, and what do the modes mean?
**中文题意**：如何求解 x 导数等于 A x？模态是什么意思？
**考察点**：线性系统的模态分解。
**项目关联**：P1/P7（倒立摆模态）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The solution is x of t equals e to the A t times x zero. If A is diagonalisable, I write the initial state as a combination of eigenvectors. Each eigenvector evolves independently as e to the lambda t, so the state is a sum of modes. A real negative eigenvalue gives a pure decay, a complex pair gives a decaying oscillation, and a positive eigenvalue gives growth. This view is useful in practice: when a system misbehaves, I ask which mode is excited, and which state combination it corresponds to. For the pendulum on wheels, the unstable mode is a combination of tilt and tilt rate.

**中文翻译**：解为 x(t) 等于 e 的 A t 次方乘 x(0)。若 A 可对角化，我把初始状态写成特征向量的组合。每个特征向量独立地按 e 的 lambda t 次方演化，所以状态是各模态之和。负实特征值给出纯衰减，复共轭对给出衰减振荡，正特征值给出增长。这个视角在实践中很有用：系统表现异常时，我问是哪个模态被激发、对应哪种状态组合。对轮子上的倒立摆，不稳定模态是倾角和倾角速率的组合。
**追问 1**：*What if A is not diagonalisable?* Then there are Jordan blocks and terms like t times e to the lambda t appear, which grow polynomially on top of the exponential.
　中文：存在 Jordan 块，出现 t 乘 e 的 lambda t 次方这类项，在指数之上再带多项式增长。
**追问 2**：*Can transient growth happen with all eigenvalues stable?* Yes, with non-normal matrices the state can grow temporarily before decaying.
　中文：可以，非正规矩阵的状态可能先暂时增长再衰减。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P7, I look at which state error grows first when the robot falls; it is usually pitch and pitch rate, which fits the unstable mode.*
　中文：P7 里机器人倒下时我看哪个状态误差先增长，通常是俯仰和俯仰角速度，符合不稳定模态。
**关键词与易错点**：mode、eigenvector、matrix exponential、non-normal。

### Q24. Why can we say sine theta is approximately theta, and how accurate is it?
**中文题意**：为什么 sin theta 近似 theta？精度如何？
**考察点**：泰勒展开的具体应用与误差估计。
**项目关联**：P1/P7（小倾角假设）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> From the Taylor series, sine theta equals theta minus theta cubed over six plus higher terms. For small angles the cubic term is tiny. At zero point one radian, about 5.7 degrees, the relative error is about 0.17 percent. At zero point three radian, about 17 degrees, it is about 1.5 percent. Cosine theta is one minus theta squared over two, so it is approximately one. So the approximation is excellent at the angles where a balancing robot normally operates. It breaks down for big recoveries, which is why a fall detector and torque limits are still needed.

**中文翻译**：由泰勒级数，sin theta 等于 theta 减 theta 立方除以 6 再加高阶项。小角度时立方项很小。0.1 弧度（约 5.7 度）时相对误差约 0.17%；0.3 弧度（约 17 度）时约 1.5%。cos theta 等于 1 减 theta 平方除以 2，所以近似为 1。因此在平衡机器人通常工作的角度范围，这个近似非常好；大幅度恢复时失效，所以仍需要跌倒检测和力矩限幅。
**追问 1**：*What is the error of the cosine approximation at 0.3 radian?* About four and a half percent, since theta squared over two is 0.045.
　中文：约 4.5%，因为 theta 平方除以 2 为 0.045。
**追问 2**：*What do you do for large angles?* Keep the full nonlinear terms in the model and use nonlinear control or scheduling.
　中文：在模型中保留完整非线性项，使用非线性控制或调度。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 the tilt reference is bounded to about zero point one radian, which keeps the robot in the region where the small-angle model is accurate.*
　中文：P1 里倾角参考上限约 0.1 弧度，使机器人保持在小角度模型准确的范围内。
**关键词与易错点**：Taylor series、small angle、relative error。注意角度必须用弧度。

### Q25. What is the Euler–Lagrange equation and how is it used for robot dynamics?
**中文题意**：什么是欧拉—拉格朗日方程？如何用于机器人动力学？
**考察点**：能量法建模。
**项目关联**：P1/P7（倒立摆动力学）、P2（二连杆）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I define the Lagrangian L as kinetic energy minus potential energy, using generalised coordinates q. The Euler–Lagrange equation says d by d t of partial L over partial q dot, minus partial L over partial q, equals the generalised force tau. This avoids computing constraint forces. For a manipulator it leads to M of q times q double dot plus C of q and q dot times q dot plus g of q equals tau. M is the inertia matrix, C collects Coriolis and centrifugal terms, and g is gravity. I like this method because it is systematic: I only need energies, and the equations of motion come out by differentiation.

**中文翻译**：我定义拉格朗日量 L 为动能减势能，用广义坐标 q。欧拉—拉格朗日方程说：L 对 q 导数的时间导数，减 L 对 q 的偏导，等于广义力 tau。这样不用计算约束力。对机械臂，得到 M(q) q 的二阶导加 C(q, q 导数) q 导数加 g(q) 等于 tau。M 是惯性矩阵，C 包含科氏力和离心力项，g 是重力。我喜欢这个方法因为很系统：只需要能量，运动方程通过微分得到。
**追问 1**：*What is special about M?* It is symmetric and positive definite, and the matrix M dot minus 2 C is skew-symmetric, which is useful in stability proofs.
　中文：M 对称正定，且 M 的导数减 2C 是反对称的，这在稳定性证明中有用。
**追问 2**：*Newton–Euler versus Lagrange?* Newton–Euler is recursive and efficient for computation; Lagrange is clean for derivation and gives structure.
　中文：牛顿—欧拉是递归的、计算高效；拉格朗日适合推导且结构清晰。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P2, gravity compensation uses the g of q term from the URDF and KDL; in P1 and P7 the pendulum dynamics come from this kind of derivation.*
　中文：P2 的重力补偿用 URDF 与 KDL 给出的 g(q) 项；P1、P7 的摆动力学来自这种推导。
**关键词与易错点**：Lagrangian、generalised coordinates、inertia matrix、Coriolis。

### Q26. Why is differentiating a noisy signal dangerous, and what do you do about it?
**中文题意**：为什么对带噪声信号求导很危险？怎么办？
**考察点**：微分项噪声放大。
**项目关联**：P3（PID 的 D 项）、P1（角速度滤波）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A derivative has a gain proportional to frequency. Measurement noise is usually high-frequency, so differentiation amplifies it. A tiny jitter in position becomes a large spike in velocity. Integration is the opposite: it smooths high-frequency noise but accumulates bias and drifts. In practice, I avoid pure differentiation. I use a sensor that measures the rate directly, such as the gyroscope, or I use a filtered derivative, which is a derivative with a first-order low-pass. In a PID controller I also take the derivative on the measurement rather than on the error, so a setpoint step does not create a derivative kick.

**中文翻译**：微分的增益与频率成正比，测量噪声通常是高频的，所以微分会放大它。位置上一点小抖动，在速度上变成大尖峰。积分正相反：平滑高频噪声，但累积偏置并漂移。实际中我避免纯微分：用直接测量速率的传感器（如陀螺仪），或使用带一阶低通的滤波微分。在 PID 里我还对测量值而不是误差求微分，避免设定值阶跃造成微分冲击。
**追问 1**：*How do you choose the derivative filter cutoff?* A few times the loop crossover frequency, but below the noise-dominated band.
　中文：取回路穿越频率的几倍，但低于噪声占主导的频段。
**追问 2**：*Why does integration drift?* Any constant bias in the input becomes a linear ramp in the output, and a random walk for white noise.
　中文：输入中任何常值偏置会变成输出的线性斜坡，白噪声则变成随机游走。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P3 and P1 the angular rate comes from the gyroscope directly, so the derivative term does not differentiate an angle.*
　中文：P3 和 P1 里角速度直接来自陀螺仪，所以微分项不需要对角度求导。
**关键词与易错点**：derivative kick、filtered derivative、drift、gyroscope。

### Q27. What does the Fourier transform tell you, and how do you choose a cutoff frequency?
**中文题意**：傅里叶变换告诉你什么？如何选截止频率？
**考察点**：频域直觉与滤波选择。
**项目关联**：P1/P3/P5（滤波截止）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The Fourier transform decomposes a signal into sinusoids, so I can see how much energy sits at each frequency. For a sensor signal, I look at the spectrum to separate the useful band, such as the motion of the robot, from the noise band. I choose the cutoff above the highest frequency of the motion I need to follow, and well below the frequency where noise dominates. I also check the delay, because every low-pass adds phase lag. If the loop bandwidth is, say, one hertz, a filter at twenty-five hertz adds little lag, but a filter at one hertz would destroy the phase margin.

**中文翻译**：傅里叶变换把信号分解成正弦分量，让我看到各频率的能量。对传感器信号，我看频谱来区分有用频段（如机器人运动）和噪声频段。截止频率取在我需要跟踪的最高运动频率之上、噪声占主导的频率之下。我还检查延迟，因为每个低通都增加相位滞后。若回路带宽约 1 Hz，25 Hz 的滤波器几乎不增加滞后，而 1 Hz 的滤波器会破坏相位裕度。
**追问 1**：*What is aliasing?* When the sampling rate is less than twice the highest frequency, high-frequency content folds into lower frequencies and cannot be removed afterward.
　中文：采样率低于最高频率的两倍时，高频成分折叠到低频，之后无法去除。
**追问 2**：*FFT or filter for a single noisy reading?* A causal filter is needed in real time; the FFT is for offline analysis of logs.
　中文：实时需要因果滤波器；FFT 用于离线分析日志。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 I applied a light first-order low-pass of about 25 hertz on the gyro, or none, and a 0.8 hertz low-pass only for the slow wheel-speed path.*
　中文：P1 里陀螺仪只加约 25 Hz 的轻度一阶低通或不滤波，仅对慢速的轮速路径用 0.8 Hz 低通。
**关键词与易错点**：spectrum、cutoff、phase lag、aliasing。

### Q28. State Bayes' theorem and give a robotics example.
**中文题意**：陈述贝叶斯定理并举一个机器人例子。
**考察点**：先验、似然、后验。
**项目关联**：P5（AMCL、EKF 本质是贝叶斯滤波）、P4（检测置信度）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Bayes' theorem says the probability of a hypothesis A given evidence B equals the likelihood of B given A, times the prior of A, divided by the probability of B. In words: posterior is proportional to likelihood times prior. In localisation, the hypothesis is the robot pose. The prior is where I think the robot is after applying odometry. The likelihood is how well the laser scan matches the map at each candidate pose. The posterior is the updated belief. AMCL does this with particles: it predicts using odometry, weights particles by the laser likelihood, and resamples. The Kalman filter is the same idea with Gaussians.

**中文翻译**：贝叶斯定理说，给定证据 B 时假设 A 的概率，等于给定 A 时 B 的似然，乘 A 的先验，再除以 B 的概率。用话说：后验正比于似然乘先验。在定位中，假设是机器人位姿；先验是应用里程计后我认为机器人所在位置；似然是激光扫描与地图在每个候选位姿上匹配的程度；后验是更新后的信念。AMCL 用粒子实现：用里程计预测、按激光似然给粒子加权、再重采样。卡尔曼滤波是同样的思想，只是用高斯分布。
**追问 1**：*What is the base-rate trap?* If a detector has a ninety-five percent hit rate but the event is rare, most alarms can still be false, because the prior is small.
　中文：检测器命中率 95%，但事件很罕见时，多数报警仍可能是误报，因为先验很小。
**追问 2**：*What is the evidence term used for?* It is a normaliser, so the posterior sums to one; in many filters we skip it and just normalise.
　中文：它是归一化常数，使后验和为 1；许多滤波器直接归一化而不显式计算。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 my EKF and AMCL are both Bayesian filters; the EKF carries a Gaussian belief and AMCL carries a sample-based belief on the map.*
　中文：P5 里 EKF 和 AMCL 都是贝叶斯滤波器；EKF 携带高斯信念，AMCL 携带地图上的样本信念。
**关键词与易错点**：prior、likelihood、posterior、normaliser。不要把似然说成概率分布本身（它是参数的函数）。

### Q29. Why is the Gaussian distribution so common in robotics?
**中文题意**：为什么高斯分布在机器人里这么常见？
**考察点**：中心极限定理、封闭性、数学便利。
**项目关联**：P5（EKF 假设高斯）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> There are three reasons. First, the central limit theorem: the sum of many small independent errors tends to look Gaussian, which fits sensor noise. Second, it is closed under linear operations: a linear transform of a Gaussian is Gaussian, and the product of two Gaussians is also Gaussian up to scale. That makes the Kalman filter exact for linear systems. Third, it is fully described by the mean and covariance, so it is cheap to store and compute. The limits are real, though. Outliers, multi-modal beliefs such as being in one of two similar corridors, and strongly nonlinear maps break the Gaussian assumption, which is why particle filters exist.

**中文翻译**：有三个原因。第一，中心极限定理：许多小的独立误差之和趋向高斯，符合传感器噪声。第二，它在线性运算下封闭：高斯的线性变换仍是高斯，两个高斯之积在差一个常数下也是高斯，这使卡尔曼滤波对线性系统是精确的。第三，它完全由均值和协方差描述，存储和计算都便宜。但局限也真实存在：离群点、多峰信念（比如处在两个相似走廊之一）以及强非线性映射会破坏高斯假设，所以才有粒子滤波。
**追问 1**：*How do you handle outliers in a Kalman filter?* Gate the innovation with a Mahalanobis distance test, or use a robust loss.
　中文：用马氏距离对新息做门限，或使用鲁棒损失。
**追问 2**：*Is a sum of two Gaussians a Gaussian?* A sum of two independent Gaussian random variables is Gaussian; a mixture of two Gaussians is not.
　中文：两个独立高斯随机变量之和是高斯；两个高斯的混合则不是。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 I used AMCL next to the EKF exactly because global pose can be multi-modal at start, which a single Gaussian cannot represent.*
　中文：P5 里我在 EKF 之外使用 AMCL，正是因为初始全局位姿可能多峰，单个高斯无法表示。
**关键词与易错点**：central limit theorem、closed under linear maps、multi-modal、mixture。

### Q30. What is a covariance matrix and how do you read it?
**中文题意**：协方差矩阵是什么？怎么读？
**考察点**：对角元、非对角元、相关。
**项目关联**：P5（位姿协方差）、P2（odom 协方差）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> For a random vector, the covariance matrix has the variances on the diagonal and the covariances off the diagonal. The diagonal entries tell me how uncertain each component is, and the square root gives the standard deviation. The off-diagonal entries tell me how two components vary together; dividing by the two standard deviations gives the correlation between minus one and one. In a pose estimate, a large correlation between x and yaw means that if I am wrong about the heading, I am also wrong about the position in a predictable direction. The matrix is symmetric and positive semi-definite, and its eigenvectors give the principal axes of the uncertainty ellipse.

**中文翻译**：对随机向量，协方差矩阵对角线是方差，非对角线是协方差。对角元告诉我每个分量有多不确定，开方就是标准差；非对角元告诉我两个分量如何一起变化，除以两个标准差得到 −1 到 1 之间的相关系数。在位姿估计中，x 与航向之间的大相关意味着如果我的航向错了，位置也会沿可预测的方向错。该矩阵对称半正定，特征向量给出不确定性椭圆的主轴。
**追问 1**：*Can the correlation be exactly zero while the variables are dependent?* Yes. Zero correlation means no linear relation only.
　中文：可以。零相关只表示没有线性关系。
**追问 2**：*Why is a diagonal measurement covariance an approximation?* Because real noise is often correlated across components or in time, and ignoring it makes the filter over-confident.
　中文：因为真实噪声常在分量间或时间上相关，忽略会让滤波器过度自信。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P2 the odometry message carries a position standard deviation starting at about two centimetres, growing with distance, and a yaw standard deviation starting at about 0.05 radian, growing with rotation.*
　中文：P2 里里程计消息携带位置标准差（起步约 2 cm，随距离增长）和航向标准差（起步约 0.05 rad，随转角增长）。
**关键词与易错点**：variance、covariance、correlation、symmetric。不要把方差和标准差混用。

### Q31. What is the difference between uncorrelated and independent?
**中文题意**：不相关和独立有什么区别？
**考察点**：概率基础。
**项目关联**：P5（噪声独立性假设）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Independent means the joint distribution factorises into the product of the marginals, so knowing one variable tells me nothing about the other. Uncorrelated only means the covariance is zero, which rules out a linear relationship. Independence implies uncorrelated, but not the other way around. A classic example: take X symmetric around zero and Y equal to X squared. They are perfectly dependent, but the covariance is zero. For jointly Gaussian variables, uncorrelated does imply independent, which is one more reason why Gaussian assumptions are convenient in filtering.

**中文翻译**：独立指联合分布分解为边缘分布之积，知道一个变量对另一个毫无信息。不相关只指协方差为零，排除线性关系。独立蕴含不相关，反之不成立。经典例子：X 关于零对称，Y 等于 X 平方，两者完全相关依赖，但协方差为零。对联合高斯变量，不相关确实蕴含独立，这是高斯假设在滤波里方便的又一原因。
**追问 1**：*Why does the Kalman filter need the noises to be independent?* The standard derivation assumes process and measurement noise are uncorrelated and white; otherwise the gain formula needs extra cross terms.
　中文：标准推导假设过程噪声与测量噪声不相关且为白噪声，否则增益公式需要额外交叉项。
**追问 2**：*Give an example of time-correlated noise.* Gyroscope bias drifting slowly is a coloured noise, not white.
　中文：陀螺仪偏置缓慢漂移就是有色噪声，不是白噪声。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*When I fuse wheel speed and gyro yaw rate in P5, I treat them as independent, which is reasonable because they are different sensors.*
　中文：P5 融合轮速和陀螺偏航率时把它们当作独立，因为是不同的传感器。
**关键词与易错点**：independent、uncorrelated、joint distribution、marginal。

### Q32. What are expectation and variance, and how do they behave under linear transforms?
**中文题意**：期望和方差是什么？线性变换下如何变化？
**考察点**：基础概率运算。
**项目关联**：P5（协方差传播）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The expectation is the probability-weighted average, and the variance is the expected squared deviation from the mean. Expectation is linear: the expectation of a X plus b equals a times the expectation of X plus b. The variance scales with the square: the variance of a X plus b equals a squared times the variance of X, and the shift b does not matter. For vectors, the covariance of A X plus b is A times the covariance of X times A transpose. This single formula is the heart of covariance propagation in the Kalman filter. For the sum of independent variables, the variances add, so the standard deviations add in quadrature.

**中文翻译**：期望是按概率加权的平均，方差是偏离均值的平方的期望。期望是线性的：a X 加 b 的期望等于 a 乘 X 的期望加 b。方差按平方缩放：a X 加 b 的方差等于 a 平方乘 X 的方差，平移 b 没有影响。对向量，A X 加 b 的协方差等于 A 乘 X 的协方差乘 A 转置。这个公式是卡尔曼滤波协方差传播的核心。独立变量之和的方差相加，所以标准差按平方和开根号相加。
**追问 1**：*If you average N independent measurements, what happens to the noise?* The variance falls by N, so the standard deviation falls by the square root of N.
　中文：方差降为 1/N，标准差降为 1/根号 N。
**追问 2**：*What if the measurements are correlated?* The improvement is smaller; with perfect correlation, averaging does not help.
　中文：改善变小；完全相关时平均没有帮助。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 I averaged about two hundred IMU frames at start-up to estimate the roll and pitch bias, which reduces the noise by roughly the square root of two hundred.*
　中文：P1 里启动时平均约 200 帧 IMU 数据估计横滚与俯仰偏置，噪声大约降低根号 200 倍。
**关键词与易错点**：expectation、variance、linearity、A P A transpose。

### Q33. Explain maximum likelihood and MAP estimation.
**中文题意**：解释极大似然估计和最大后验估计。
**考察点**：估计的概率视角，与正则化的关系。
**项目关联**：P5（加权最小二乘）、P4（训练）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Maximum likelihood chooses the parameter that makes the observed data most probable. With Gaussian noise, maximising the likelihood is the same as minimising the sum of squared residuals, weighted by the inverse covariance. So least squares is a maximum-likelihood estimator. MAP adds a prior on the parameter and maximises the posterior, which is likelihood times prior. With a Gaussian prior, MAP becomes least squares plus a quadratic penalty, which is ridge regularisation. With a Laplace prior, it becomes an L1 penalty. So regularisation has a probabilistic meaning: it encodes what I believe before seeing the data. When there is a lot of data, the likelihood dominates and the two estimates become close.

**中文翻译**：极大似然选择使观测数据最可能出现的参数。高斯噪声下，最大化似然等价于最小化按协方差逆加权的残差平方和，所以最小二乘是极大似然估计。MAP 加入参数先验并最大化后验，即似然乘先验。高斯先验下，MAP 变成最小二乘加二次惩罚，即岭回归；拉普拉斯先验下变成 L1 惩罚。所以正则化有概率意义：编码了看数据之前的信念。数据很多时似然占主导，两种估计趋于接近。
**追问 1**：*Is the Kalman filter MLE or MAP?* It computes the MAP (and the minimum mean-square error) estimate for linear Gaussian models, with the prediction acting as the prior.
　中文：在线性高斯模型下它给出 MAP（也是最小均方误差）估计，预测起先验作用。
**追问 2**：*Why use the log-likelihood?* It turns products into sums, which is numerically stable and easier to differentiate.
　中文：把乘积变成求和，数值更稳定，也更易求导。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*The damping in my IK and the regularisation in training are both prior beliefs in this sense: prefer small joint speeds, or prefer small weights.*
　中文：我 IK 里的阻尼和训练中的正则化在这个意义下都是先验信念：偏好小的关节速度，或偏好小权重。
**关键词与易错点**：maximum likelihood、MAP、prior、regularisation。

### Q34. You have two noisy measurements of the same quantity. How do you fuse them?
**中文题意**：对同一个量有两个带噪测量，如何融合？
**考察点**：逆方差加权，卡尔曼增益的由来。
**项目关联**：P5（轮速与陀螺融合）、P7（速度估计）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> If the measurements are independent Gaussians with variances sigma one squared and sigma two squared, the optimal fused estimate is the inverse-variance weighted average. The weight of each measurement is proportional to one over its variance. The fused variance is one over the sum of one over sigma one squared and one over sigma two squared, so it is always smaller than either input. A small example: variances of four and one give weights of zero point two and zero point eight. This is exactly a one-dimensional Kalman update: the gain K equals the prior variance divided by the prior variance plus the measurement variance, and the new estimate moves toward the measurement by K times the innovation.

**中文翻译**：若两个测量是独立高斯，方差分别为 sigma1 平方和 sigma2 平方，最优融合估计是逆方差加权平均。每个测量的权重正比于其方差的倒数。融合方差是 1/sigma1 平方与 1/sigma2 平方之和的倒数，总是小于任一输入。小例子：方差 4 和 1 给出权重 0.2 和 0.8。这正是一维卡尔曼更新：增益 K 等于先验方差除以先验方差加测量方差，新估计向测量值移动 K 乘新息。
**追问 1**：*What if one sensor is biased?* Fusion cannot remove a common bias; you estimate the bias as a state or calibrate it.
　中文：融合无法消除偏置；要把偏置作为状态估计或做标定。
**追问 2**：*What if the measurement errors are correlated?* The weights must use the full covariance, and naive averaging becomes over-confident.
　中文：权重必须用完整协方差，朴素平均会过度自信。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 the EKF fuses wheel speed with a standard deviation of about 0.05 metres per second and the gyro at about 0.01 radian per second, each weighted by its noise level.*
　中文：P5 里 EKF 融合标准差约 0.05 m/s 的轮速和约 0.01 rad/s 的陀螺，各按噪声水平加权。
**关键词与易错点**：inverse-variance weighting、Kalman gain、innovation。注意权重大的是方差小的那个。

### Q35. How does uncertainty propagate through a nonlinear function?
**中文题意**：不确定性如何通过非线性函数传播？
**考察点**：EKF、UKF 的核心。
**项目关联**：P5（EKF 预测步）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> For a linear map the covariance becomes A P A transpose. For a nonlinear function f, the standard approach is to linearise around the mean. The Jacobian F of f plays the role of A, so the new covariance is approximately F P F transpose, plus the process noise. This is the extended Kalman filter prediction. It works when the function is nearly linear over the spread of the uncertainty. If it is strongly nonlinear, there are alternatives. The unscented transform picks a small set of sigma points, pushes them through the true function, and recomputes the mean and covariance. Particle methods use many samples and can represent non-Gaussian shapes.

**中文翻译**：线性映射下协方差变为 A P A 转置。对非线性函数 f，标准做法是在均值处线性化。f 的雅可比 F 起 A 的作用，新协方差约为 F P F 转置加过程噪声，这就是扩展卡尔曼滤波的预测步。当函数在不确定性范围内近似线性时有效。强非线性时有替代方法：无迹变换选一小组 sigma 点，通过真实函数传播，再重新计算均值和协方差；粒子方法用很多样本，可表示非高斯形状。
**追问 1**：*Why can the EKF be inconsistent?* Linearisation errors and wrong noise models make the covariance too small, so the filter trusts itself too much.
　中文：线性化误差和错误的噪声模型使协方差偏小，滤波器过度自信。
**追问 2**：*How do you check filter consistency?* Look at the normalised innovation squared; its average should match the measurement dimension.
　中文：看归一化新息平方，其平均值应与测量维数匹配。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 the car-like model uses the heading, so the Jacobian depends on the current yaw, and a wrong yaw would give a wrong prediction of the covariance.*
　中文：P5 里车式模型依赖航向，雅可比取决于当前 yaw，航向错会导致协方差预测错。
**关键词与易错点**：Jacobian、linearisation、unscented transform、consistency。

### Q36. What is the Mahalanobis distance, and how is it used for gating?
**中文题意**：马氏距离是什么？如何用于门限（gating）？
**考察点**：异常值检测。
**项目关联**：P5（定位跳变门限）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The Mahalanobis distance of a vector x from a distribution with mean mu and covariance S is the square root of x minus mu, transpose, S inverse, x minus mu. It measures distance in units of standard deviation, taking correlations into account. For a Gaussian, its square follows a chi-square distribution with as many degrees of freedom as the dimension. So I can set a gate: accept a measurement only if its squared Mahalanobis distance is below a chi-square threshold, for example about 9.2 for two dimensions at ninety-nine percent. Measurements outside the gate are treated as outliers. This protects an EKF from a wrong match or a bad laser return.

**中文翻译**：向量 x 到均值 mu、协方差 S 的分布的马氏距离，是 (x 减 mu) 转置乘 S 的逆乘 (x 减 mu) 的平方根。它以标准差为单位衡量距离，并考虑相关性。对高斯分布，其平方服从自由度等于维数的卡方分布。所以可设门限：只有马氏距离平方低于卡方阈值（例如二维 99% 约 9.2）才接受测量；门外的测量视为离群点。这保护 EKF 免受错误匹配或坏的激光回波影响。
**追问 1**：*Why not use the Euclidean distance?* It ignores the different uncertainty in each direction and the correlations.
　中文：它忽略各方向不同的不确定性和相关性。
**追问 2**：*What happens if the gate is too tight?* Good measurements are rejected and the filter can drift or lose track.
　中文：好的测量被拒绝，滤波器可能漂移或丢失。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 I used a similar idea for the map-to-odom correction: large jumps, above about half a metre or ten degrees, were only accepted after three consistent updates with a small covariance trace.*
　中文：P5 里对 map 到 odom 的修正用了类似思想：超过约 0.5 m 或 10° 的大跳变需连续三次一致且协方差迹较小才接受。
**关键词与易错点**：Mahalanobis、chi-square、gate、outlier。

### Q37. Explain the law of large numbers and the central limit theorem.
**中文题意**：解释大数定律和中心极限定理。
**考察点**：统计基础，与蒙特卡洛的联系。
**项目关联**：P4（评估集大小）、P5（粒子数）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The law of large numbers says that the sample mean of independent identically distributed variables converges to the true mean as the sample size grows. The central limit theorem goes further: the distribution of the sample mean, properly scaled, approaches a Gaussian, with standard deviation sigma over the square root of N. Together they explain why averaging more data helps, and why the error decreases only as one over root N. To halve the error, I need four times as many samples. This is also the reason Monte Carlo methods, such as particle filters, converge slowly but do not suffer from the dimension in the same way as grid methods.

**中文翻译**：大数定律说，独立同分布变量的样本均值随样本量增加收敛到真实均值。中心极限定理更进一步：适当缩放后样本均值的分布趋向高斯，标准差为 sigma 除以根号 N。两者共同解释了为什么多平均数据有帮助，以及误差只按 1/根号 N 下降：误差减半需要四倍样本。这也是蒙特卡洛方法（如粒子滤波）收敛慢、但不像网格方法那样受维数灾难影响的原因。
**追问 1**：*Does the CLT hold for any distribution?* It needs finite variance; heavy-tailed distributions like Cauchy break it.
　中文：需要有限方差；柯西这样的重尾分布不满足。
**追问 2**：*How many particles do you need?* It depends on the dimension and how peaked the likelihood is; there is no universal number, so I tune with the effective sample size.
　中文：取决于维数和似然的尖锐程度，没有通用数字，我通过有效样本数来调。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 the AMCL particle count was allowed to vary between about five hundred and three thousand, using the adaptive scheme.*
　中文：P5 里 AMCL 粒子数在约 500 到 3000 之间自适应变化。
**关键词与易错点**：law of large numbers、CLT、one over root N、effective sample size。

### Q38. What is the bias–variance trade-off?
**中文题意**：什么是偏差—方差权衡？
**考察点**：估计与学习的共同原理。
**项目关联**：P4（YOLO 过拟合）、P3（滤波强度）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The expected squared error of an estimator is the sum of the squared bias, the variance, and the irreducible noise. Bias is systematic error from a wrong or too simple model. Variance is the sensitivity to the particular data sample. A flexible model has low bias but high variance, so it may overfit. A simple model has high bias and low variance, so it underfits. Regularisation, more data, or averaging trades a little bias for a big drop in variance. The same trade-off appears in filtering: a heavy low-pass filter has low noise variance but adds bias in the form of lag; a light filter is the opposite.

**中文翻译**：估计量的期望平方误差等于偏差平方、方差和不可约噪声之和。偏差是模型错误或过于简单造成的系统误差；方差是对具体数据样本的敏感度。灵活的模型偏差低但方差高，可能过拟合；简单的模型偏差高方差低，会欠拟合。正则化、更多数据或平均，用一点偏差换取方差大幅下降。滤波里也有同样权衡：重低通滤波噪声方差低，但以滞后的形式引入偏差；轻滤波则相反。
**追问 1**：*How do you detect overfitting?* Training error keeps falling while validation error rises; a held-out set is the check.
　中文：训练误差持续下降而验证误差上升；用留出集检验。
**追问 2**：*How do you reduce variance without adding bias?* Collect more data or average several models.
　中文：收集更多数据或平均多个模型。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P4 I used an 8 to 2 train-validation split of about eighteen hundred leaf images and reported validation mAP, so the numbers are not training numbers.*
　中文：P4 里我把约 1800 张叶片图像按 8:2 划分训练/验证，报告验证集 mAP，而不是训练集数字。
**关键词与易错点**：bias、variance、overfitting、regularisation、validation set。

### Q39. What is white noise versus a random walk, and what is Allan variance used for?
**中文题意**：白噪声与随机游走有什么区别？Allan 方差用来做什么？
**考察点**：IMU 噪声模型。
**项目关联**：P1/P3/P7（陀螺与加速度计）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> White noise is uncorrelated from sample to sample. If I integrate gyro white noise, I get a random walk in angle, whose standard deviation grows like the square root of time. Bias instability, or a random walk in the rate itself, causes a faster growing drift in the angle. Allan variance is a tool for separating these noise types from a long static recording: plot the Allan deviation against averaging time on a log-log chart, and read angle random walk, bias instability and rate random walk from the slopes. I would use it to choose filter noise parameters, rather than guessing them. For my projects, I mostly rely on a static bias calibration at start-up, because a long Allan test needs hours.

**中文翻译**：白噪声在样本间不相关。对陀螺白噪声积分，得到角度上的随机游走，其标准差随时间的平方根增长。偏置不稳定性（或速率本身的随机游走）使角度漂移增长更快。Allan 方差是从长时间静态记录中分离这些噪声类型的工具：在对数—对数图上画 Allan 偏差对平均时间的曲线，从斜率读出角度随机游走、偏置不稳定性和速率随机游走。我会用它为滤波器选择噪声参数，而不是猜测。在我的项目里，我主要靠启动时的静态偏置标定，因为完整 Allan 测试要几个小时。
**追问 1**：*Why is yaw drift unavoidable with only a gyro?* Yaw has no absolute reference from gravity, so bias and noise integrate without correction.
　中文：yaw 没有重力这样的绝对参考，偏置和噪声积分后无法被修正。
**追问 2**：*How would a magnetometer help?* It gives an absolute heading but is easily disturbed by motors and metal.
　中文：给出绝对航向，但易受电机和金属干扰。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P3 the yaw drifted about one degree per minute at rest, and the magnetometer was not enabled, so I relied on start-up bias calibration and a temperature-controlled IMU.*
　中文：P3 里 yaw 静置漂移约每分钟 1 度，未启用磁力计，靠启动偏置标定和恒温 IMU。
**关键词与易错点**：white noise、random walk、bias instability、Allan variance。

### Q40. What is a Markov process, and what does the Markov assumption give a filter?
**中文题意**：什么是马尔可夫过程？马尔可夫假设给滤波器带来什么？
**考察点**：递归估计的基础。
**项目关联**：P5（EKF、AMCL）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A process is Markov if the future depends on the past only through the present state. In a filter, this means the current state contains all the information I need, so I do not have to store the whole history. The Bayes filter then has two steps: predict the next state using the motion model, and update with the new measurement. Both use only the previous belief. The assumption holds if my state is rich enough. If I forget a state, for example a sensor bias or a time delay, the hidden variable breaks the Markov property and shows up as correlated errors. A common fix is to augment the state.

**中文翻译**：若未来只通过当前状态依赖于过去，则该过程是马尔可夫的。在滤波器里这意味着当前状态包含我需要的全部信息，不必存储整个历史。贝叶斯滤波因此有两步：用运动模型预测下一个状态，再用新测量更新，两步都只用上一时刻的信念。这个假设在状态足够丰富时成立。如果遗漏一个状态（如传感器偏置或时间延迟），隐藏变量会破坏马尔可夫性，表现为相关误差，常用的补救是扩增状态。
**追问 1**：*Give an example where the Markov assumption fails.* A sensor with a slowly varying bias, or a delayed measurement.
　中文：带缓慢变化偏置的传感器，或有延迟的测量。
**追问 2**：*What is a hidden Markov model?* A Markov chain of hidden states with observations emitted from each state; filtering infers the hidden state.
　中文：隐状态构成马尔可夫链，每个状态发出观测；滤波用于推断隐状态。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5, timestamp and direction inconsistencies acted like missing state: the filter assumed one thing and the sensors did another, which is why I fixed the inputs rather than the filter.*
　中文：P5 里时间戳和方向的不一致相当于缺失的状态：滤波器假设一回事、传感器是另一回事，所以我修的是输入而不是滤波器。
**关键词与易错点**：Markov property、belief、predict and update、augment the state。

### Q41. What are entropy, cross-entropy and KL divergence?
**中文题意**：熵、交叉熵、KL 散度分别是什么？
**考察点**：信息论在机器学习中的角色。
**项目关联**：P4（分类损失）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Entropy measures the average uncertainty of a distribution: minus the sum of p times log p. Cross-entropy between a true distribution p and a model q is minus the sum of p times log q. KL divergence is the difference, cross-entropy minus entropy. It is always non-negative, zero only when the two distributions match, and it is not symmetric, so it is not a true distance. In classification, minimising cross-entropy with one-hot labels is the same as maximising the log-likelihood of the correct class, and it is the same as minimising the KL divergence from the data to the model. That is why cross-entropy is the standard classification loss.

**中文翻译**：熵衡量分布的平均不确定性：负的 p 乘 log p 之和。真实分布 p 与模型 q 的交叉熵是负的 p 乘 log q 之和。KL 散度是两者之差，即交叉熵减熵。它总是非负，仅在两个分布相同时为零，且不对称，所以不是真正的距离。在分类中，用 one-hot 标签最小化交叉熵，等价于最大化正确类的对数似然，也等价于最小化数据到模型的 KL 散度，所以交叉熵是标准分类损失。
**追问 1**：*Why not use squared error for classification?* With a sigmoid or softmax output, squared error gives small gradients when the prediction is confidently wrong; cross-entropy does not.
　中文：sigmoid 或 softmax 输出下，预测自信地错时平方误差梯度很小，交叉熵不会。
**追问 2**：*Is KL divergence symmetric?* No; KL of p from q is generally different from KL of q from p.
　中文：不对称，两个方向一般不同。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P4 the YOLO classification and objectness terms use cross-entropy style losses, but I used the framework's implementation, and my focus was deployment.*
　中文：P4 中 YOLO 的分类与目标性项使用交叉熵类损失，但我用的是框架实现，我的重点是部署。
**关键词与易错点**：entropy、cross-entropy、KL divergence、log-likelihood。不要说 KL 是距离。

### Q42. What is Monte Carlo integration, and what is importance sampling?
**中文题意**：什么是蒙特卡洛积分？什么是重要性采样？
**考察点**：粒子滤波的数学基础。
**项目关联**：P5（AMCL）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Monte Carlo integration estimates an expectation by averaging a function over random samples from the distribution. The error falls like one over the square root of the number of samples, regardless of dimension. Importance sampling is used when I cannot sample directly from the target distribution. I sample from an easy proposal distribution, and weight each sample by the ratio of target to proposal. In a particle filter, the proposal is the motion model prediction and the target is the posterior, so the weight becomes the measurement likelihood. Then resampling removes particles with tiny weights and duplicates the good ones, so the computation is not wasted.

**中文翻译**：蒙特卡洛积分通过对从分布中随机采样的样本取函数平均来估计期望，误差按 1/根号样本数下降，与维数无关。当无法直接从目标分布采样时用重要性采样：从容易采样的提议分布抽样，用目标与提议之比给每个样本加权。在粒子滤波中，提议分布是运动模型的预测，目标是后验，所以权重就是测量似然。之后重采样去掉权重很小的粒子并复制好的粒子，避免计算浪费。
**追问 1**：*What is particle degeneracy?* After a few steps, almost all weight sits on a few particles; the effective sample size becomes tiny, which is why we resample.
　中文：几步之后几乎所有权重集中在少数粒子上，有效样本数变得很小，所以要重采样。
**追问 2**：*What is the kidnapped robot problem?* The robot is moved without the filter knowing; adding some random particles helps recovery.
　中文：机器人被搬走而滤波器不知道；注入一些随机粒子有助于恢复。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 the AMCL used the likelihood field model with about sixty beams per scan and updated only after the robot moved about fifteen centimetres or turned about 0.2 radian.*
　中文：P5 的 AMCL 使用似然场模型，每次扫描约 60 条射线，机器人移动约 15 cm 或旋转约 0.2 rad 才更新。
**关键词与易错点**：Monte Carlo、importance sampling、resampling、effective sample size。

### Q43. A teammate says "my change improved the result". How do you check that the improvement is real?
**中文题意**：队友说"我的改动改善了结果"。你怎么验证改善是真实的？
**考察点**：实验设计与统计常识。
**项目关联**：P1（前馈开/关各重复 5 次）、P3（连发弹速）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I look at three things. First, the comparison must be controlled: same trajectory, same conditions, and only one change. Second, repeatability: one run can be luck, so I repeat each condition several times and compare the mean and the spread. If the difference is much larger than the run-to-run variation, I trust it. For formal checks, I can use a confidence interval or a simple test, but the key is that the effect is large relative to the noise. Third, I report the metric clearly, such as peak, RMS or drift, and I avoid claiming more precision than I have measured.

**中文翻译**：我看三件事。第一，对比必须受控：同样的轨迹、同样的条件、只改一个变量。第二，可重复性：单次运行可能是运气，所以每个条件重复多次，比较均值和离散程度；如果差异远大于运行间波动，我才相信。需要形式化时可用置信区间或简单检验，但关键是效应相对噪声要足够大。第三，清楚报告指标（峰值、均方根或漂移），不声称比实测更高的精度。
**追问 1**：*How many repeats are enough?* It depends on the variation; five is a practical minimum for a clear effect, more for a small one.
　中文：取决于波动；明显效应下 5 次是实用下限，小效应需要更多。
**追问 2**：*What is a confounder?* A hidden variable that changes with your treatment, such as battery level or temperature.
　中文：随处理一起变化的隐藏变量，如电量或温度。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 I toggled the centre-of-mass feedforward on and off under the same fixed arm trajectory, five repeats each; the peak tilt dropped from about 0.06 to about 0.026 radian. It is a repeated bench comparison, not a statistical study.*
　中文：P1 里我在同一固定手臂轨迹下开关质心前馈，每种状态重复 5 次；倾角峰值从约 0.06 降到约 0.026 rad。这是重复的台架对比，不是统计研究。
**关键词与易错点**：controlled comparison、repeatability、confidence interval、confounder。不要把 5 次说成"统计显著"。

### Q44. What is a convex function, and why do we care about convexity?
**中文题意**：什么是凸函数？为什么关心凸性？
**考察点**：优化理论的核心概念。
**项目关联**：P5（MPC 的 QP）、P2（IK 的 QP）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A function is convex if the line segment between any two points on its graph lies above the graph. For a twice-differentiable function, that means the Hessian is positive semi-definite everywhere. The big consequence is that any local minimum is a global minimum, and the set of minimisers is convex. So if I can write my problem as a convex optimisation, I can solve it reliably and quickly, with no worry about bad initial guesses. A quadratic program with a positive semi-definite cost matrix and linear constraints is convex. That is one reason MPC with a linear model and linear constraints can run in real time, while nonlinear problems like trajectory optimisation can be stuck in local minima.

**中文翻译**：如果函数图像上任意两点之间的线段位于图像上方，则函数是凸的。对二阶可微函数，等价于 Hessian 处处半正定。重大结果是：任何局部最小值都是全局最小值，最小值点集合是凸集。所以如果能把问题写成凸优化，就能可靠且快速地求解，不用担心坏的初值。代价矩阵半正定且约束线性的二次规划是凸的。这就是线性模型、线性约束的 MPC 能实时运行的一个原因，而像轨迹优化这样的非线性问题可能陷入局部极小。
**追问 1**：*Is the sum of convex functions convex?* Yes, and so is a non-negative scaling or a maximum of convex functions.
　中文：是，非负数乘以凸函数、凸函数的最大值也仍是凸的。
**追问 2**：*Give a non-convex example in robotics.* Inverse kinematics with joint-angle variables has many solutions; collision avoidance with obstacles makes the feasible set non-convex.
　中文：以关节角为变量的逆运动学有多个解；避障使可行集非凸。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 the MPC step is convex because the model is linearised around the reference and the constraints are linear; the TEB step is not, so it depends on a good initial path from Hybrid-A star.*
　中文：P5 里 MPC 这一步是凸的，因为模型围绕参考轨迹线性化且约束线性；TEB 不是，所以依赖 Hybrid-A* 提供好的初始路径。
**关键词与易错点**：convex、global minimum、positive semi-definite Hessian、feasible set。不要说"凸的就一定有唯一解"；只是局部即全局。

### Q45. Explain gradient descent. How do you choose the step size?
**中文题意**：解释梯度下降。步长怎么选？
**考察点**：一阶方法及其局限。
**项目关联**：P4（训练）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Gradient descent repeatedly moves the parameters a small step opposite to the gradient: x new equals x minus alpha times the gradient. If alpha is too small, convergence is slow. If it is too large, the iterates oscillate or diverge. For a quadratic with Hessian eigenvalues between mu and L, a safe step is below two over L, and the best constant step gives a convergence rate that depends on the condition number L over mu. So badly scaled problems converge slowly, with a zig-zag path along a narrow valley. That is why I normalise the inputs, and why people use momentum, adaptive step sizes like Adam, or second-order methods.

**中文翻译**：梯度下降反复沿梯度反方向走一小步：x 新值等于 x 减 alpha 乘梯度。alpha 太小收敛慢；太大则迭代振荡或发散。对 Hessian 特征值在 mu 到 L 之间的二次函数，安全步长小于 2/L，最佳常数步长的收敛速率取决于条件数 L/mu。所以尺度糟糕的问题收敛慢，沿狭窄山谷走之字形。这就是为什么要归一化输入，以及为什么使用动量、Adam 这样的自适应步长或二阶方法。
**追问 1**：*Why is the zig-zag?* The gradient is perpendicular to the contour lines, and in a narrow valley it points mostly across the valley, not along it.
　中文：梯度垂直于等值线，在狭窄山谷里主要指向横穿山谷而不是沿谷底。
**追问 2**：*What is a line search?* Choosing alpha at each iteration so the cost decreases sufficiently, for example by backtracking.
　中文：每次迭代选择 alpha 使代价充分下降，例如回溯法。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P4 the training used the standard optimiser with a decaying learning rate in the framework; I did not write the optimiser myself.*
　中文：P4 的训练使用框架中标准的优化器和衰减学习率；优化器不是我自己写的。
**关键词与易错点**：step size、condition number、momentum、line search。

### Q46. How does Newton's method differ from gradient descent?
**中文题意**：牛顿法和梯度下降有何不同？
**考察点**：二阶信息的作用。
**项目关联**：P2（迭代 IK）、P5（优化）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Gradient descent uses only the slope. Newton's method also uses the curvature. At each step it fits a quadratic model using the Hessian and jumps to its minimum: x new equals x minus Hessian inverse times gradient. Near a solution it converges quadratically, which means the number of correct digits roughly doubles each iteration. The costs are that I need the Hessian, I need to solve a linear system at each step, and far from a minimum, the Hessian may be indefinite and the step may go the wrong way. So in practice we use damped or trust-region Newton, or quasi-Newton methods such as BFGS that approximate the Hessian from gradients.

**中文翻译**：梯度下降只用斜率。牛顿法还用曲率：每步用 Hessian 拟合二次模型，并跳到其最小点：x 新值等于 x 减 Hessian 逆乘梯度。靠近解时二次收敛，即每次迭代正确位数大约翻倍。代价是需要 Hessian，每步要解线性方程组，且远离极小值时 Hessian 可能不定，步长方向可能错误。所以实际使用阻尼牛顿或信赖域牛顿，或者像 BFGS 这样用梯度近似 Hessian 的拟牛顿法。
**追问 1**：*What does Newton do on a quadratic?* It converges in one step.
　中文：对二次函数一步收敛。
**追问 2**：*Why is it unsuitable for huge neural networks?* The Hessian has the square of the number of parameters in entries, which is too large to store or invert.
　中文：Hessian 的元素数是参数数的平方，无法存储或求逆。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*Iterative inverse kinematics in P2 is a Newton-like process on the task error with a damped Jacobian; the damping plays the role of a trust region.*
　中文：P2 的迭代逆运动学是对任务误差的类牛顿过程，阻尼雅可比，阻尼起信赖域的作用。
**关键词与易错点**：Hessian、quadratic convergence、BFGS、trust region。

### Q47. What are Gauss–Newton and Levenberg–Marquardt?
**中文题意**：高斯—牛顿与 Levenberg–Marquardt 是什么？
**考察点**：非线性最小二乘。
**项目关联**：P5（EKF 迭代、TEB 的 g2o）、P2（DLS）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> For a nonlinear least-squares problem, minimise the sum of squared residuals r of x. Gauss–Newton linearises the residual with its Jacobian J, so the step solves J transpose J delta equals minus J transpose r. It ignores the second derivative of the residual, which is cheap and works well when the residuals are small near the solution. Levenberg–Marquardt adds a damping term, lambda times the identity, to J transpose J. With a large lambda it behaves like gradient descent, safe but slow; with a small lambda it behaves like Gauss–Newton, fast near the solution. The damped least squares in inverse kinematics is the same idea.

**中文翻译**：对非线性最小二乘，最小化残差 r(x) 的平方和。高斯—牛顿用雅可比 J 线性化残差，步长 delta 满足 J 转置 J delta 等于负 J 转置 r。它忽略残差的二阶导数，计算便宜，在解附近残差小时效果好。LM 在 J 转置 J 上加阻尼项 lambda 乘单位阵：lambda 大时像梯度下降，安全但慢；lambda 小时像高斯—牛顿，靠近解时快。逆运动学里的阻尼最小二乘就是同样的思想。
**追问 1**：*How is lambda adapted?* Increase it when a step fails to reduce the cost, decrease it when it succeeds.
　中文：步长未能降低代价时增大，成功时减小。
**追问 2**：*When does Gauss–Newton fail?* With large residuals, strong nonlinearity, or a rank-deficient J transpose J.
　中文：残差大、非线性强或 J 转置 J 秩亏时。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*TEB in P5 is a sparse nonlinear least-squares problem solved with this family of methods; I tuned its weights rather than writing the solver.*
　中文：P5 的 TEB 是用这类方法解的稀疏非线性最小二乘；我调的是权重，而不是写求解器。
**关键词与易错点**：Gauss–Newton、Levenberg–Marquardt、residual、Jacobian。

### Q48. Explain Lagrange multipliers with an equality constraint.
**中文题意**：用等式约束解释拉格朗日乘子。
**考察点**：约束优化的核心思想。
**项目关联**：P1/P7（LQR 的变分推导）、P2（IK 约束）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> To minimise f of x subject to g of x equals zero, I form the Lagrangian f plus lambda times g. At a constrained optimum, the gradient of f must be parallel to the gradient of g, because I cannot decrease f without leaving the constraint surface. So the condition is: gradient of f plus lambda times gradient of g equals zero, together with g equals zero. The multiplier lambda has a meaning: it is the sensitivity of the optimal cost to a small relaxation of the constraint. A simple example is to minimise x squared plus y squared subject to x plus y equals one. The answer is x and y both zero point five, with lambda equal to minus one.

**中文翻译**：要在 g(x) 等于零的约束下最小化 f(x)，我构造拉格朗日函数 f 加 lambda 乘 g。在约束最优点，f 的梯度必须与 g 的梯度平行，因为不离开约束面就无法再降低 f。所以条件是：f 的梯度加 lambda 乘 g 的梯度等于零，同时 g 等于零。乘子 lambda 有意义：它是最优代价对约束小幅放松的敏感度。简单例子：在 x 加 y 等于 1 的条件下最小化 x 平方加 y 平方，答案是 x 和 y 都是 0.5，lambda 等于负 1。
**追问 1**：*Check the example.* The gradient is 2x and 2y, plus lambda times 1 and 1, equal to zero, so x equals y equals minus lambda over two. The constraint gives x plus y equals one, so x equals y equals 0.5 and lambda is minus one.
　中文：梯度 2x、2y 加 lambda 乘 (1, 1) 为零，所以 x 等于 y 等于负 lambda/2；约束 x 加 y 等于 1，得到 x 等于 y 等于 0.5，lambda 为 −1。
**追问 2**：*What is the physical meaning in a mechanical system?* The multiplier is the constraint force.
　中文：在机械系统中，乘子就是约束力。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*A closed-chain leg in P7 has loop constraints; the multipliers are the internal forces, though I used the virtual-leg model so I did not solve them explicitly.*
　中文：P7 的闭链腿有回路约束，乘子就是内力；不过我用的是虚拟腿模型，没有显式求解。
**关键词与易错点**：Lagrangian、multiplier、gradient parallel、sensitivity。

### Q49. What are the KKT conditions?
**中文题意**：KKT 条件是什么？
**考察点**：不等式约束的最优性条件。
**项目关联**：P5（MPC 约束）、P2（关节限位）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> For a problem with inequality constraints, the Karush–Kuhn–Tucker conditions are necessary for optimality under mild regularity conditions, and they are also sufficient for convex problems. There are four parts. Stationarity: the gradient of the Lagrangian is zero. Primal feasibility: all constraints are satisfied. Dual feasibility: the multipliers for inequality constraints are non-negative. And complementary slackness: for each inequality, either the constraint is active, meaning it holds with equality, or its multiplier is zero. In words, a constraint only pushes back if it is touching. This is how MPC with limits works: a speed limit only matters at the moments when the optimal solution would exceed it.

**中文翻译**：对含不等式约束的问题，KKT 条件在温和的正则性假设下是最优性的必要条件，对凸问题还是充分条件。共四部分。平稳性：拉格朗日函数梯度为零。原始可行性：所有约束成立。对偶可行性：不等式约束的乘子非负。互补松弛：每个不等式要么是起作用的（等号成立），要么乘子为零。通俗说，约束只有在"碰到"时才会推回。MPC 带限制就是这样工作的：速度限制只在最优解会超过它的时刻才起作用。
**追问 1**：*What is an active set?* The set of inequality constraints that hold with equality at the solution.
　中文：解处取等号成立的不等式约束集合。
**追问 2**：*What if the constraints are not regular?* KKT may fail; constraint qualifications such as Slater's condition for convex problems guard against this.
　中文：KKT 可能不成立；凸问题中的 Slater 条件等约束规范性假设可避免。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 the steering-angle limit of about 0.5 radian is active in tight turns, which is where the multiplier is non-zero.*
　中文：P5 里转向角约 0.5 rad 的限制在急转弯时起作用，此时乘子非零。
**关键词与易错点**：KKT、complementary slackness、active set、dual feasibility。

### Q50. What is a quadratic program, and what makes it easy to solve?
**中文题意**：什么是二次规划？为什么容易求解？
**考察点**：QP 标准形。
**项目关联**：P2（Mink 约束 IK）、P5（MPC）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A quadratic program minimises one half z transpose H z plus g transpose z, subject to linear constraints: lower bound less than or equal to A z less than or equal to upper bound. If H is positive semi-definite, the problem is convex, so any solver that finds a KKT point finds the global optimum. QPs are solved reliably by active-set methods, interior-point methods, or ADMM-based solvers like OSQP. For small problems the solve time is milliseconds, and warm starting from the last solution makes it even faster, which is why QPs are a natural fit for real-time control.

**中文翻译**：二次规划是在线性约束（下界小于等于 A z 小于等于上界）下最小化二分之一 z 转置 H z 加 g 转置 z。H 半正定时问题是凸的，所以任何找到 KKT 点的求解器都找到全局最优。QP 可由有效集法、内点法或像 OSQP 这样基于 ADMM 的求解器可靠地求解。小规模问题求解时间为毫秒级，用上一次的解热启动更快，因此 QP 很适合实时控制。
**追问 1**：*What does it mean if the QP is infeasible?* The constraints contradict each other; a real controller needs a fallback, or soft constraints.
　中文：约束相互矛盾；实际控制器需要后备策略或软约束。
**追问 2**：*How do you handle that in MPC?* Use slack variables with a large penalty, so the state constraints can be violated slightly rather than making the solver fail.
　中文：使用带大惩罚的松弛变量，使状态约束可略微违反，而不是让求解失败。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5, if the solver failed, I reused the shifted previous solution for three cycles and then slowed the car to a stop. In P2 the IK problem is a QP with velocity and position limits each cycle.*
　中文：P5 里求解失败时，我把上一次的解平移复用三个周期，然后减速停车；P2 的 IK 是每周期带速度和位置限制的 QP。
**关键词与易错点**：quadratic program、convex、OSQP、warm start、slack variable。

### Q51. Why is linear MPC a quadratic program?
**中文题意**：为什么线性 MPC 是二次规划？
**考察点**：MPC 的核心形式。
**项目关联**：P5（MPC 轨迹跟踪）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> In MPC I predict the state over a horizon of N steps using a linear model: x at k plus one equals A x at k plus B u at k. If I substitute this into the cost, which is a sum of quadratic terms in the state error and the control, the cost becomes a quadratic function of the stacked control sequence. The model equations are linear equalities, and the limits on speed, steering and rate are linear inequalities. A quadratic cost with linear constraints is a quadratic program. I solve it at each cycle, apply only the first control, and repeat with the new measurement. For a nonlinear car model I linearise it around the reference, so each step remains a QP.

**中文翻译**：MPC 里我用线性模型在 N 步时域内预测状态：x(k+1) 等于 A x(k) 加 B u(k)。把它代入代价（状态误差和控制量的二次项之和），代价就成为堆叠控制序列的二次函数。模型方程是线性等式，速度、转向、变化率限制是线性不等式。二次代价加线性约束就是二次规划。我每个周期求解一次，只执行第一个控制，用新测量再重复。对非线性车辆模型，我围绕参考轨迹线性化，使每一步仍是 QP。
**追问 1**：*Condensed or sparse formulation?* Condensed eliminates the states, giving a small dense QP; sparse keeps states as variables, giving a larger sparse QP that solvers like OSQP exploit well.
　中文：压缩形式消去状态，得到小而稠密的 QP；稀疏形式保留状态变量，得到更大但稀疏的 QP，OSQP 等能很好利用。
**追问 2**：*Why only apply the first input?* Because the model is imperfect; re-solving with new feedback corrects for disturbances and model error.
　中文：因为模型不完美；用新反馈重新求解可以纠正扰动和模型误差。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 the state is x, y, heading and speed, the input is acceleration and steering angle, the horizon is fifteen steps of 0.1 second, and OSQP solves it in about two to five milliseconds at 20 hertz.*
　中文：P5 里状态是 x、y、航向、速度，输入是加速度和转向角，时域 15 步、步长 0.1 s，OSQP 在 20 Hz 下单次约 2–5 ms。
**关键词与易错点**：receding horizon、condensed、sparse、linearise、first input。

### Q52. How does LQR come from an optimisation problem, and what is the Riccati equation?
**中文题意**：LQR 如何来自优化问题？Riccati 方程是什么？
**考察点**：LQR 的数学推导思路。
**项目关联**：P1（LQR 分析）、P7（LQR 增益调度）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> LQR minimises the cost J, the integral of x transpose Q x plus u transpose R u, subject to x dot equals A x plus B u. The solution has the form u equals minus K x. The gain is K equals R inverse B transpose P, where P is the positive definite solution of the algebraic Riccati equation: A transpose P plus P A minus P B R inverse B transpose P plus Q equals zero. The idea of the derivation is that the optimal cost-to-go is a quadratic function x transpose P x, and substituting that into the Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman equation gives the Riccati equation. In practice I call a library solver. The weights Q and R set the trade-off between state error and control effort.

**中文翻译**：LQR 在 x 导数等于 A x 加 B u 的约束下最小化代价 J，即 x 转置 Q x 加 u 转置 R u 的积分。解的形式为 u 等于负 K x。增益 K 等于 R 逆乘 B 转置乘 P，其中 P 是代数 Riccati 方程的正定解：A 转置 P 加 P A 减 P B R 逆 B 转置 P 加 Q 等于零。推导思路是：最优代价函数（cost-to-go）是二次型 x 转置 P x，代入 Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman 方程就得到 Riccati 方程。实际中我调用库函数求解。权重 Q 和 R 设定状态误差与控制量之间的折中。
**追问 1**：*What guarantees does LQR have?* For a stabilisable and detectable system, the closed loop is stable; for single-input systems it has at least sixty degrees of phase margin and infinite upward gain margin.
　中文：对可镇定可检测系统闭环稳定；单输入系统至少有 60 度相位裕度和无限的增益上裕度。
**追问 2**：*Do those margins survive a delay or an observer?* No. The guarantee is for full state feedback without delay; with an observer or delay they can vanish.
　中文：不一定。保证是针对无延迟的全状态反馈；加观测器或延迟后可能消失。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 I used Q equal to diag of four, four, one hundred, one and R equal to zero point two five on a normalised four-state model, as an analysis tool to understand weights and saturation; the robot itself ran a PID cascade.*
　中文：P1 里我在归一化四状态模型上用 Q 等于 diag(4, 4, 100, 1)、R 等于 0.25，作为理解权重与饱和的分析工具；机器人实际运行的是级联 PID。
**关键词与易错点**：Riccati、cost-to-go、gain matrix、stabilisable。不要说 P1 部署了 LQR。

### Q53. Walk me through a scalar LQR example by hand.
**中文题意**：请手算一个标量 LQR 例子。
**考察点**：会算，而不只会背。
**项目关联**：P1/P7（离散 LQR）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Take a discrete scalar system: x next equals x plus u, so A and B are both one, with cost weights Q equal to one and R equal to one. The discrete Riccati equation in the scalar case is P equals Q plus A squared P, minus the square of A B P divided by R plus B squared P. With all the numbers equal to one, that is P equals one plus P minus P squared over one plus P. This simplifies to P squared equals one plus P, so P is the golden ratio, about 1.618. The gain K equals B P A over R plus B squared P, which is 1.618 over 2.618, about 0.618. The closed-loop pole is A minus B K, which is about 0.382. It is inside the unit circle, so the closed loop is stable and faster than the open loop, whose pole is at one.

**中文翻译**：取离散标量系统：x 下一步等于 x 加 u，所以 A 和 B 都是 1，权重 Q 等于 1、R 等于 1。离散 Riccati 方程为 P 等于 Q 加 A 平方 P 减 (A B P) 平方除以 (R 加 B 平方 P)；标量情形 P 等于 1 加 P 减 P 平方除以 (1 加 P)。化简得 P 平方等于 1 加 P，所以 P 是黄金比例，约 1.618。增益 K 等于 B P A 除以 (R 加 B 平方 P)，即 1.618 除以 2.618，约 0.618。闭环极点为 A 减 B K，约 0.382，在单位圆内，所以闭环稳定，并且比开环（极点在 1）更快。
**追问 1**：*What happens if I increase R to ten?* The gain falls, so the controller is gentler, and the closed-loop pole moves closer to one, so the response is slower.
　中文：增益下降，控制器更温和，闭环极点移向 1，响应更慢。
**追问 2**：*Why is the open-loop pole at one marginally stable?* A pole on the unit circle means the state neither grows nor decays; a constant input would make it drift.
　中文：单位圆上的极点表示状态既不增长也不衰减；恒定输入会使其漂移。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*It is the same logic as tuning Q and R in P1: the ratio matters, not the absolute numbers, and a bigger R means smaller commands and slower correction.*
　中文：这与 P1 里整定 Q 和 R 的逻辑相同：重要的是比例而非绝对数值，R 大则指令小、纠正慢。
**关键词与易错点**：discrete Riccati、golden ratio、closed-loop pole、unit circle。

### Q54. What is regularisation? Compare L2 and L1.
**中文题意**：什么是正则化？比较 L2 和 L1。
**考察点**：优化与学习的联系。
**项目关联**：P2（阻尼）、P4（权重衰减）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Regularisation adds a penalty to the objective so the solution does not become extreme or overfit. L2 regularisation adds the squared norm of the parameters. It shrinks all weights smoothly and makes ill-posed problems well-posed; in least squares it is ridge regression, and with a Jacobian it is damped least squares. L1 regularisation adds the absolute values. It gives sparse solutions, where many weights become exactly zero, because the L1 ball has corners on the axes. So L2 is good for stability and smoothness, and L1 for feature selection. Both have a Bayesian reading: L2 corresponds to a Gaussian prior, L1 to a Laplace prior.

**中文翻译**：正则化在目标中加入惩罚，使解不致极端或过拟合。L2 正则加参数的平方范数，平滑地缩小所有权重，并使病态问题变得适定；在最小二乘里就是岭回归，用在雅可比上就是阻尼最小二乘。L1 正则加绝对值，给出稀疏解，许多权重恰好为零，因为 L1 球在坐标轴上有尖角。所以 L2 适合稳定和平滑，L1 适合特征选择。两者都有贝叶斯解读：L2 对应高斯先验，L1 对应拉普拉斯先验。
**追问 1**：*Why does L1 give sparsity geometrically?* The contours of the loss are likely to first touch the diamond-shaped L1 ball at a corner, where some coordinates are zero.
　中文：损失等值线很可能先在菱形 L1 球的角上接触，那里某些坐标为零。
**追问 2**：*Is weight decay the same as L2?* For plain SGD, yes; for adaptive optimisers like Adam they differ, which is why decoupled weight decay is used.
　中文：对普通 SGD 相同；对 Adam 等自适应优化器不同，所以才有解耦权重衰减。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*The damping in my inverse kinematics is exactly an L2 regulariser on the joint velocity.*
　中文：我逆运动学里的阻尼正是对关节速度的 L2 正则。

### Q55. What is stochastic gradient descent, and why do Adam and momentum help?
**中文题意**：什么是随机梯度下降？为什么动量和 Adam 有帮助？
**考察点**：深度学习优化。
**项目关联**：P4。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Stochastic gradient descent computes the gradient on a small random batch instead of the full dataset. It is a noisy but unbiased estimate, much cheaper per step, and the noise can even help escape poor local regions. Momentum keeps a running average of past gradients, so it smooths the noise and accelerates along consistent directions, which helps in narrow valleys. **中文翻译**：随机梯度下降用小批量随机样本而非全部数据计算梯度，是有噪声但无偏的估计，每步便宜得多，噪声甚至可能帮助逃离糟糕区域。动量保持过去梯度的滑动平均，因此平滑噪声并沿一致方向加速，有利于窄谷。Adam 还跟踪梯度平方的滑动平均，对每个参数缩放步长：梯度大的参数步长更小。通常收敛快、调参少。学习率调度仍然重要，在某些视觉任务上，调好的带动量 SGD 泛化略好。
**追问 1**：*Why shuffle the data?* Correlated consecutive samples bias the gradient estimate and hurt convergence.
　中文：相邻样本相关会使梯度估计有偏并损害收敛。
**追问 2**：*What is the effect of batch size?* Larger batches give less noisy gradients and better hardware use; very large batches may need a larger learning rate and can generalise worse.
　中文：批量大梯度噪声小、硬件利用率高；批量过大需要更大学习率，且泛化可能变差。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P4 I fine-tuned YOLOv5s from COCO weights for about one hundred epochs on my own leaf images, using the framework's default recipe.*
　中文：P4 里我从 COCO 权重出发，在自己的叶片图像上微调 YOLOv5s 约 100 个 epoch，使用框架的默认训练配方。

### Q56. Line search versus trust region: what is the difference?
**中文题意**：线搜索与信赖域有什么区别？
**考察点**：全局化策略。
**项目关联**：P5（非线性优化）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Both are ways to make an optimiser converge from a poor starting point. In a line search, I first choose a descent direction, then choose how far to go along it, for example by backtracking until the cost decreases enough. In a trust-region method, I first choose a region around the current point where I trust my local quadratic model, then find the best step inside it. If the actual reduction matches the predicted one, I enlarge the region; otherwise I shrink it. Trust-region methods handle indefinite Hessians more gracefully. Levenberg–Marquardt is closely related to a trust-region method, since the damping lambda is like the inverse of the region size.

**中文翻译**：两者都是让优化器从糟糕初值收敛的办法。线搜索先选下降方向，再选沿它走多远，例如回溯直到代价充分下降。信赖域方法先选当前点周围我信任局部二次模型的区域，再在区域内找最佳步。若实际下降与预测一致就放大区域，否则缩小。信赖域方法更好地处理不定 Hessian。LM 与信赖域密切相关，阻尼 lambda 类似区域大小的倒数。
**追问 1**：*What is the Armijo condition?* A sufficient-decrease test: the cost must drop at least by a fraction of what the linear model predicts.
　中文：充分下降判据：代价下降量至少是线性模型预测值的某个比例。
**追问 2**：*Which would you use for IK?* A damped step is simple and effective; a line search on the task error is an optional safeguard.
　中文：阻尼步简单有效；对任务误差做线搜索是可选保护。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*My damped IK in P2 behaves like a small trust region, which keeps joint steps bounded near singularities.*
　中文：P2 的阻尼 IK 表现得像小信赖域，使奇异点附近关节步长有界。
**关键词与易错点**：line search、trust region、Armijo、damping。

### Q57. Compare active-set, interior-point and ADMM methods for QPs.
**中文题意**：比较有效集法、内点法和 ADMM 求解 QP。
**考察点**：求解器选择常识。
**项目关联**：P5（OSQP）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Active-set methods guess which constraints are active and move along the boundary; they are very good with warm starts and small problems, and they give exact vertices. Interior-point methods stay inside the feasible region and follow a central path, and they scale well with a predictable number of iterations, but each iteration is expensive and warm starting is harder. ADMM-based solvers, like OSQP, split the problem into easy sub-problems. They factorise a matrix once, and each iteration is cheap, so they are fast to a moderate accuracy and good for embedded use, but they may need many iterations for high accuracy. For MPC, I usually choose OSQP or a similar solver with warm starts.

**中文翻译**：有效集法猜测哪些约束起作用并沿边界移动，擅长热启动和小规模问题，给出精确的顶点解。内点法留在可行域内部沿中心路径前进，扩展性好、迭代数可预测，但每次迭代代价大、热启动较难。基于 ADMM 的求解器（如 OSQP）把问题拆成易解的子问题，矩阵只分解一次，每次迭代便宜，达到中等精度很快，适合嵌入式，但高精度可能需要很多迭代。对 MPC，我通常选 OSQP 或类似的热启动求解器。
**追问 1**：*Why does OSQP converge to only moderate accuracy quickly?* ADMM is a first-order-like method; the primal and dual residuals fall quickly at first and then slowly.
　中文：ADMM 类似一阶方法，原始与对偶残差起初下降快，之后变慢。
**追问 2**：*What is solver time-out handling?* Cap the iterations or time, and use the best feasible iterate or fall back to a safe action.
　中文：限制迭代数或时间，使用最好的可行迭代或退回安全动作。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 the OSQP solve took about two to five milliseconds, and if it failed, the controller used a fallback for three cycles and then stopped the car.*
　中文：P5 里 OSQP 单次约 2–5 ms，失败时控制器用后备方案三个周期，然后停车。
**关键词与易错点**：active set、interior point、ADMM、warm start。

### Q58. What are local minima, and how do you deal with them in planning and optimisation?
**中文题意**：什么是局部极小值？规划与优化中如何应对？
**考察点**：非凸问题的工程处理。
**项目关联**：P5（Hybrid-A* 提供 TEB 初值；同伦类）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> In a non-convex problem, a local minimum is a point that is better than all its neighbours but not the best overall. A gradient-based method will stop there. In practice I do three things. First, choose a good initial guess from a global method, for example a graph search path used to initialise a local optimiser. Second, explore several starting points or topologies in parallel and pick the best. Third, add randomisation or restarts. In navigation, a local planner can be stuck in front of a wall if the goal is behind it, which is why we have a global planner to give the overall route.

**中文翻译**：在非凸问题中，局部极小值是比所有邻点都好、但不是全局最优的点，基于梯度的方法会停在那里。实际中我做三件事：第一，用全局方法提供好初值，例如用图搜索路径初始化局部优化器；第二，并行探索多个初值或拓扑，选最好的；第三，加入随机化或重启。在导航里，如果目标在墙后，局部规划器可能卡在墙前，这就是为什么需要全局规划器给出整体路线。
**追问 1**：*What is a homotopy class in TEB?* A group of paths that can be continuously deformed into each other without crossing an obstacle, for example passing an obstacle on its left or its right.
　中文：可在不穿越障碍的前提下连续变形为彼此的一组路径，例如从障碍物左边或右边绕过。
**追问 2**：*What can go wrong when switching between classes?* The reference can jump, which makes a tracking controller jitter.
　中文：参考轨迹会跳变，使跟踪控制器抖动。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5, TEB switching between homotopy classes made the reference jump, and combined with about 120 milliseconds of delay it made the steering jitter. I added a selection hysteresis, larger input-rate weights and delay compensation.*
　中文：P5 里 TEB 在同伦类之间切换使参考跳变，加上约 120 ms 延迟导致转向抖动；我加了选择滞回、更大的输入变化率权重和延迟补偿。
**关键词与易错点**：local minimum、initial guess、homotopy class、hysteresis。

### Q59. How do you write constrained inverse kinematics as a QP?
**中文题意**：如何把带约束的逆运动学写成 QP？
**考察点**：Mink 类方法的数学。
**项目关联**：P2（核心）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> At each control cycle, I choose the joint velocity v to minimise the weighted task error plus a damping term. In symbols: minimise the norm of J v minus the desired task velocity, weighted by a matrix W, plus lambda squared times the norm of v squared. The constraints are linear in v: joint velocity limits, and position limits turned into velocity bounds, such as the lower limit minus q divided by the time step being less than or equal to v, and v less than or equal to the upper limit minus q over the time step. This is a QP in v with a positive definite Hessian, so it is convex and has a unique solution. I integrate v over the time step to get the next joint targets.

**中文翻译**：每个控制周期我选关节速度 v，最小化加权任务误差加阻尼项。写成符号：最小化 J v 减期望任务速度的范数（用矩阵 W 加权），加 lambda 平方乘 v 范数的平方。约束对 v 是线性的：关节速度限制，以及转成速度界的位置限制，例如 (下限减 q) 除以时间步长小于等于 v，且 v 小于等于 (上限减 q) 除以时间步长。这是关于 v 的 QP，Hessian 正定，所以是凸的且解唯一。我在时间步上积分 v 得到下一步关节目标。
**追问 1**：*Why velocity-level rather than position-level IK?* The task is linear in velocity through the Jacobian, so the QP is convex; position-level IK is nonlinear.
　中文：任务通过雅可比对速度是线性的，QP 是凸的；位置层 IK 是非线性的。
**追问 2**：*How do multiple tasks get priorities?* With weights, or hierarchically with null-space projection.
　中文：用权重，或用零空间投影做层级处理。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P2 the global damping was about ten to the minus three, the task damping about 0.05, and I added extra speed limits in the target generator and the driver node because I did not rely on a solver default.*
　中文：P2 里全局阻尼约 10 的负 3 次方，任务阻尼约 0.05，并在目标生成端与驱动节点另加速度限制，因为我不依赖求解器默认设置。
**关键词与易错点**：velocity-level IK、damping、joint limits as bounds、unique solution。

### Q60. What is duality in optimisation, and what do the dual variables mean?
**中文题意**：优化中的对偶是什么？对偶变量的含义？
**考察点**：高级但常被问到的概念。
**项目关联**：P5（OSQP 内部的对偶变量）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> For a constrained problem, the dual problem maximises a lower bound on the optimal cost. I form the Lagrangian, minimise it over the primal variables for fixed multipliers, and then maximise the resulting dual function over the multipliers. Weak duality says the dual value never exceeds the primal optimum. For convex problems under mild conditions, strong duality holds, so the gap is zero. The dual variables are the shadow prices: they tell me how much the optimal cost would change if I relaxed a constraint slightly. In a solver, the size of the gap between primal and dual is a convenient stopping criterion.

**中文翻译**：对约束问题，对偶问题是最大化最优代价的下界。我构造拉格朗日函数，对固定乘子关于原变量最小化，再对乘子最大化所得对偶函数。弱对偶说对偶值不超过原问题最优值；凸问题在温和条件下强对偶成立，间隙为零。对偶变量是影子价格：告诉我若稍微放松某个约束，最优代价会变多少。在求解器里，原始与对偶之间的间隙是方便的停止判据。
**追问 1**：*Give an intuitive example of a shadow price.* If a power limit is the active constraint, the multiplier tells me how much extra speed I could get per extra watt.
　中文：若功率限制是起作用的约束，乘子告诉我多一瓦功率能多得到多少速度。
**追问 2**：*When does strong duality fail?* For non-convex problems, or convex ones without a constraint qualification.
　中文：非凸问题，或凸问题缺少约束规范性条件时。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*I did not implement duality myself; I rely on the solver reporting primal and dual residuals when I check convergence.*
　中文：对偶我没有自己实现；检查收敛时我依赖求解器报告的原始与对偶残差。
**关键词与易错点**：dual problem、weak and strong duality、shadow price、duality gap。

### Q61. What is the difference between LP, QP, SOCP and SDP, and which appear in robotics?
**中文题意**：LP、QP、SOCP、SDP 有何区别？机器人里常见哪些？
**考察点**：凸优化家族。
**项目关联**：P5（QP）；其余为常识。
**English answer（口语）**：
> They form a hierarchy of convex problems. A linear program has a linear cost and linear constraints. A quadratic program has a quadratic cost with linear constraints. A second-order cone program allows cone constraints such as a norm being less than a linear function, which is useful for friction cones and robust constraints. A semidefinite program has matrix variables constrained to be positive semi-definite, and it appears in certain stability certificates and relaxations. In robotics, QPs are everywhere: MPC, inverse kinematics, whole-body control. Second-order cones appear in contact force models with friction. I have used QPs, not the last two.

**中文翻译**：它们构成凸问题的层级。线性规划是线性代价和线性约束；二次规划是二次代价加线性约束；二阶锥规划允许锥约束，比如范数小于线性函数，可用于摩擦锥和鲁棒约束；半定规划的变量是矩阵，约束为半正定，出现在某些稳定性证书和松弛里。机器人里 QP 无处不在：MPC、逆运动学、全身控制；二阶锥出现在带摩擦的接触力模型。我用过 QP，没用过后两者。
**追问 1**：*Why is a friction cone a second-order cone?* The tangential force norm must be at most mu times the normal force, which is exactly a norm bounded by a linear function.
　中文：切向力范数不超过 mu 乘法向力，正是范数被线性函数界住。
**追问 2**：*Are all LPs QPs?* Yes, with a zero Hessian.
　中文：是，Hessian 为零即可。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*My experience is with QPs in P2 and P5. I know the others at the concept level.*
　中文：我的经验是 P2 和 P5 中的 QP，其余的只在概念层面了解。
**关键词与易错点**：linear program、second-order cone、semidefinite、friction cone。诚实说明未用过。

### Q62. What is a pose graph, and how is it optimised?
**中文题意**：什么是位姿图？如何优化？
**考察点**：非线性最小二乘在 SLAM 的应用。
**项目关联**：P5（定位与建图背景；使用 slam_toolbox 离线建图）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A pose graph has robot poses as nodes and relative-motion measurements as edges, from odometry or scan matching. Loop closures are extra edges between distant poses that see the same place. Each edge gives a residual: the difference between the measured relative pose and the one implied by the current node estimates, weighted by the information matrix. The optimisation minimises the sum of squared weighted residuals over all poses, using Gauss–Newton or Levenberg–Marquardt on a sparse problem. One pose is fixed to remove the gauge freedom. Sparse Cholesky makes it fast even with thousands of nodes. A wrong loop closure can badly distort the map, so robust kernels are often used.

**中文翻译**：位姿图以机器人位姿为节点、以来自里程计或扫描匹配的相对运动测量为边。回环是连接看到同一地点的远距离位姿的额外边。每条边给出一个残差：测量的相对位姿与当前节点估计所隐含的相对位姿之差，用信息矩阵加权。优化是在所有位姿上最小化加权残差平方和，对稀疏问题用高斯—牛顿或 LM。固定一个位姿以消除规范自由度。稀疏 Cholesky 使上千节点也很快。错误的回环会严重扭曲地图，所以常用鲁棒核函数。
**追问 1**：*Why does the problem have a gauge freedom?* Only relative poses are measured, so the whole graph can be moved without changing the residuals.
　中文：只测量了相对位姿，整个图可以平移而不改变残差。
**追问 2**：*Filter SLAM versus graph SLAM?* A filter marginalises past poses and keeps only the current one; a graph keeps the history and can re-linearise, usually giving better accuracy.
　中文：滤波边缘化过去位姿只保留当前；图优化保留历史并可重新线性化，通常精度更好。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 I built the map offline with slam_toolbox, which uses graph optimisation internally, and my own work was localisation on that map with EKF and AMCL.*
　中文：P5 里我用 slam_toolbox 离线建图，其内部使用图优化；我自己的工作是在该地图上用 EKF 和 AMCL 定位。
**关键词与易错点**：pose graph、loop closure、information matrix、gauge freedom、robust kernel。

### Q63. Compare Euler and Runge–Kutta 4 integration.
**中文题意**：比较欧拉法和四阶龙格—库塔积分。
**考察点**：精度阶数与计算量。
**项目关联**：P1/P7（仿真）、P2（MuJoCo 积分）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Explicit Euler advances the state by the step size times the derivative at the start of the interval. Its local error is of order h squared, and the global error of order h, so it is first-order accurate. Runge–Kutta 4 evaluates the derivative four times per step and combines them, with global error of order h to the fourth. For the same accuracy, RK4 can take much larger steps, although each costs four function evaluations. For a stable physical simulation I might prefer semi-implicit Euler, which is also first order but conserves energy better for oscillators. The right choice depends on whether I need accuracy or just a cheap, stable update.

**中文翻译**：显式欧拉用区间起点的导数乘步长推进状态，局部误差 h 平方量级，全局误差 h 量级，是一阶精度。RK4 每步计算四次导数并组合，全局误差为 h 的四次方。达到同样精度时 RK4 可以取大得多的步长，尽管每步需要四次函数计算。对稳定的物理仿真，我可能偏好半隐式欧拉，它同样是一阶，但对振荡系统能量保持更好。选择取决于我需要精度还是只需要便宜而稳定的更新。
**追问 1**：*Why does explicit Euler gain energy on an oscillator?* The amplification factor has a magnitude above one, so the amplitude grows.
　中文：放大因子的模大于 1，振幅增长。
**追问 2**：*How would you check the step size?* Halve it; if the result changes noticeably, the step was too large.
　中文：把步长减半，结果明显变化说明原步长太大。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In my work the controller loops are discrete by nature, such as the 500 hertz loop in P7; integration accuracy mostly matters when I use simulation to compare designs.*
　中文：我的控制回路本来就是离散的，如 P7 的 500 Hz 循环；积分精度主要在用仿真比较方案时才重要。
**关键词与易错点**：order of accuracy、RK4、semi-implicit Euler、step size。

### Q64. What are zero-order hold and Tustin discretisation, and when do you use each?
**中文题意**：零阶保持与 Tustin 离散化各是什么？何时使用？
**考察点**：连续到离散。
**项目关联**：P3（数字 PID）、P1（滤波器）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Zero-order hold assumes the input is constant during each sample period, as it is when a microcontroller writes a value to the actuator. For a state-space plant this is exact, with A d equal to e to the A T. Tustin, or the bilinear transform, replaces s by two over T times z minus one over z plus one. It maps the whole left half plane into the unit disk, so a stable continuous filter stays stable, and I use it to discretise controllers or filters designed in continuous time. It warps the frequency axis, so I may pre-warp at the critical frequency. For slow loops with a fast sample rate, even simple forward Euler is often close enough.

**中文翻译**：零阶保持假设输入在每个采样周期内为常数，就像微控制器把数值写给执行器那样。对状态空间被控对象这是精确的，A d 等于 e 的 A T 次方。Tustin（双线性变换）用 (2/T) 乘 (z 减 1) 除以 (z 加 1) 代替 s，把整个左半平面映射到单位圆内，所以稳定的连续滤波器保持稳定，我用它离散化连续时间设计的控制器或滤波器。它会扭曲频率轴，因此可在关键频率处预畸变。对采样率很高的慢回路，简单的前向欧拉常常足够接近。
**追问 1**：*What does forward Euler do to stability?* It can map a stable continuous pole outside the unit circle if T is not small enough.
　中文：T 不够小时，它可能把稳定的连续极点映射到单位圆外。
**追问 2**：*What is the sampling-time rule of thumb?* Sample at least ten to twenty times faster than the closed-loop bandwidth.
　中文：采样率至少比闭环带宽快十到二十倍。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P3 the PID gains are tied to the control period, which is about three milliseconds, so changing the period means re-tuning the gains.*
　中文：P3 的 PID 增益与控制周期（约 3 ms）绑定，改周期就要重新整定增益。
**关键词与易错点**：zero-order hold、Tustin、bilinear transform、pre-warping。

### Q65. What is a stiff system, and why does it matter for simulation?
**中文题意**：什么是刚性系统？为什么对仿真重要？
**考察点**：数值稳定性。
**项目关联**：P2（MuJoCo 接触）、P7（轮地接触）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A stiff system has dynamics at very different time scales, for example a fast decaying mode and a slow mode of interest. An explicit method must use a step size small enough for the fastest mode to remain stable, even if that mode has already vanished, so the simulation becomes slow. For explicit Euler on x dot equals lambda x, the stability condition is the absolute value of one plus h lambda below one. For a large negative lambda this means a tiny h. Implicit methods, such as implicit Euler, are stable for any step size on that test problem. Contact with a stiff spring model is a typical source of stiffness in robot simulators.

**中文翻译**：刚性系统有时间尺度差异很大的动力学，例如一个快速衰减的模态和一个关注的慢模态。显式方法必须取足够小的步长使最快模态保持稳定，即使该模态早已消失，所以仿真变慢。对 x 导数等于 lambda x 的显式欧拉，稳定性条件是 1 加 h lambda 的绝对值小于 1；lambda 为大负数时，h 必须很小。隐式方法（如隐式欧拉）在该测试问题上对任意步长都稳定。刚性弹簧接触模型是机器人仿真器中刚性的典型来源。
**追问 1**：*What is the cost of an implicit method?* Each step solves a nonlinear or linear system.
　中文：每步要解一个非线性或线性方程组。
**追问 2**：*How do simulators like MuJoCo deal with contact?* They use a soft constraint formulation solved with convex optimisation, so the contact is stable at reasonable time steps.
　中文：用软约束形式并通过凸优化求解，所以在合理的步长下接触稳定。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P7 the wheel–ground contact transitions were a source of trouble on the real robot, and the stiffness idea explains why a landing can create a torque spike unless the output is faded in.*
　中文：P7 里轮—地接触切换是实机问题来源，刚性的思想解释了为什么落地会产生力矩尖峰，除非让输出渐入。
**关键词与易错点**：stiff、stability region、implicit Euler、contact。

### Q66. What numerical problems can floating-point arithmetic cause in control code?
**中文题意**：浮点运算会在控制代码里造成什么数值问题？
**考察点**：工程数值常识。
**项目关联**：P3（STM32 浮点）、P1。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Floating-point numbers have limited precision: about seven decimal digits for float32 and about sixteen for float64. Three problems come up. Catastrophic cancellation: subtracting two nearly equal numbers loses most of the significant digits, so I rewrite formulas to avoid it. Accumulation of rounding errors: adding a very small number to a large one, such as a timestamp in seconds since the epoch, loses the small part, so I use relative times or integer ticks. Comparisons: I never test floating-point equality, I use a tolerance. On a microcontroller with a single-precision FPU, I also pay attention to unit scaling and to accidental double-precision operations that are slow.

**中文翻译**：浮点数精度有限：float32 约 7 位十进制有效数字，float64 约 16 位。会出现三类问题。灾难性抵消：两个相近的数相减会丢失大部分有效数字，所以我改写公式来避免。舍入误差累积：把一个很小的数加到很大的数上（如自纪元以来的秒数时间戳）会丢失小数部分，所以我用相对时间或整数计数。比较：我从不测试浮点相等，而是用容差。在只有单精度 FPU 的微控制器上，我还留意单位缩放，以及意外的双精度运算会很慢。
**追问 1**：*What is machine epsilon?* The gap between one and the next representable number, about 1.2 times ten to the minus seven for float32.
　中文：1 与下一个可表示数之间的间隔，float32 约 1.2 乘 10 的负 7 次方。
**追问 2**：*Why is a long-running integrator in float32 risky?* Once the sum is large, small increments are rounded away.
　中文：和变大之后，小的增量会被舍入掉。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P3 the controller runs in single precision on the STM32F407, and I used a hard-coded nominal dt, a point I flag as a known approximation.*
　中文：P3 的控制器在 STM32F407 上用单精度运行，并使用写死的名义 dt，我把它当作已知的近似。
**关键词与易错点**：machine epsilon、cancellation、tolerance、single precision。

### Q67. State the Nyquist sampling theorem and explain aliasing.
**中文题意**：陈述奈奎斯特采样定理并解释混叠。
**考察点**：采样基础。
**项目关联**：P3（陀螺 1 kHz）、P1（100 Hz IMU）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The Nyquist theorem says that to represent a signal without loss, the sample rate must be more than twice its highest frequency. If it is not, the high-frequency content folds back as a false lower frequency, which is aliasing. For example, a 60 hertz vibration sampled at 100 hertz appears as a 40 hertz component. After sampling, aliasing cannot be removed, so the fix is an analogue anti-aliasing filter before the sampler, or oversampling inside the sensor with digital filtering. For control loops, I also need the sample rate to be much higher than the closed-loop bandwidth, typically ten times or more, to keep phase lag small.

**中文翻译**：奈奎斯特定理说，要无损表示一个信号，采样率必须高于其最高频率的两倍。否则高频成分会折叠成虚假的低频，这就是混叠。例如 60 Hz 振动以 100 Hz 采样，会表现为 40 Hz 分量。采样之后混叠无法去除，所以要在采样前加模拟抗混叠滤波器，或在传感器内过采样并数字滤波。对控制回路，我还要求采样率远高于闭环带宽，通常十倍以上，以保持小的相位滞后。
**追问 1**：*Why do IMUs sample at kilohertz and output at 100 hertz?* They oversample internally and filter digitally before decimating.
　中文：它们在内部过采样并数字滤波后再抽取。
**追问 2**：*What does aliasing look like in a log?* A slow oscillation that does not match any physical motion.
　中文：日志里出现与任何物理运动都不符的缓慢振荡。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P3 the IMU task ran at about one kilohertz and the control task at about three milliseconds; in P1 the IMU module output at about 100 hertz, matched to the control loop.*
　中文：P3 里 IMU 任务约 1 kHz，控制任务约 3 ms；P1 里 IMU 模块输出约 100 Hz，与控制回路匹配。
**关键词与易错点**：Nyquist、aliasing、anti-aliasing filter、oversampling。

### Q68. How do finite differences work, and how do you choose the step?
**中文题意**：有限差分如何工作？步长怎么选？
**考察点**：数值微分的误差权衡。
**项目关联**：P3（速度由编码器差分）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A forward difference approximates the derivative as f of x plus h minus f of x, divided by h, with truncation error of order h. A central difference uses f of x plus h minus f of x minus h, divided by two h, with error of order h squared. There is a trade-off. A small h reduces truncation error, but amplifies rounding error and measurement noise, because we divide a small difference by a small number. A good step is around the square root of machine epsilon for forward differences. With noisy encoder data, I either differentiate over a longer window or use a filtered derivative, and I accept a small delay.

**中文翻译**：前向差分用 (f(x+h) 减 f(x)) 除以 h 近似导数，截断误差为 h 量级。中心差分用 (f(x+h) 减 f(x−h)) 除以 2h，误差为 h 平方量级。这里存在权衡：h 小减小截断误差，却放大舍入误差和测量噪声，因为用很小的差除以很小的数。前向差分合适的步长大约是机器精度的平方根。对带噪声的编码器数据，我要么在更长窗口上微分，要么用滤波微分，并接受一点延迟。
**追问 1**：*How do you get speed from an encoder?* Count pulses over a fixed window (M method), or time between pulses (T method), or combine them.
　中文：在固定窗口内计脉冲数（M 法）、测脉冲间隔时间（T 法），或两者结合。
**追问 2**：*Which is better at low speed?* The time-between-pulses method has better resolution at low speed.
　中文：低速时测间隔时间的方法分辨率更好。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P2 the wheel speed comes from the driver as rpm every five milliseconds, so I did not difference positions myself.*
　中文：P2 里轮速由驱动器每 5 ms 以 rpm 给出，所以我没有自己做位置差分。
**关键词与易错点**：truncation error、rounding error、central difference、window。

### Q69. When would you use cubic spline interpolation instead of linear interpolation?
**中文题意**：什么时候用三次样条而不是线性插值？
**考察点**：轨迹平滑性。
**项目关联**：P1（手臂轨迹三次样条）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Linear interpolation connects the points with straight lines. The position is continuous but the velocity jumps at each knot, which means an infinite acceleration and a jerk for the motors. A cubic spline uses a cubic polynomial between knots with continuous position, velocity and acceleration at each knot. So the motion is smooth, with bounded acceleration, which is kinder to the actuators and the structure. The price is possible overshoot between points and a need to solve a tridiagonal system for the coefficients. For robot trajectories with waypoints, I use cubic or quintic profiles, or time-optimal scaling when limits matter.

**中文翻译**：线性插值用直线连接各点，位置连续但速度在每个结点跳变，意味着无穷大的加速度和对电机的冲击。三次样条在结点之间用三次多项式，并在结点处保证位置、速度、加速度连续，因此运动平滑、加速度有界，对执行器和结构更友好。代价是点间可能过冲，并需要解三对角方程组来求系数。对带路点的机器人轨迹，我用三次或五次轨迹，限制重要时用时间最优缩放。
**追问 1**：*Why a quintic polynomial?* It lets me specify position, velocity and acceleration at both ends, giving zero acceleration at start and stop.
　中文：可以在两端指定位置、速度、加速度，使起停时加速度为零。
**追问 2**：*Why is a smooth reference important for a balancing robot?* Sudden steps in the reference produce large torque spikes and can saturate the actuators.
　中文：参考突变会产生大的力矩尖峰并使执行器饱和。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 the fixed arm trajectory for the feedforward test was a smooth cubic spline interpolation between a zero pose, a raised pose of about 0.78 radian at eight seconds, and the zero pose at sixteen seconds.*
　中文：P1 里前馈测试用的固定手臂轨迹是三次样条插值：零位、8 s 时抬到约 0.78 rad、16 s 回零位。
**关键词与易错点**：cubic spline、continuity of acceleration、quintic、overshoot。

### Q70. How does Newton's method find roots, and what are its failure modes?
**中文题意**：牛顿法如何求根？失效模式有哪些？
**考察点**：迭代求解与收敛。
**项目关联**：P2（迭代 IK 的内核）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> To solve f of x equals zero, Newton's method replaces f by its tangent line at the current guess and jumps to where the tangent crosses zero: x new equals x minus f over f prime. For a vector function, I solve the Jacobian times delta equals minus f. Near a simple root it converges quadratically. The failure modes are: a poor starting point, a derivative close to zero which creates a huge step, a singular Jacobian, and cycling or divergence for some functions. Safeguards are damping the step, bounding it, or falling back to bisection on a bracket. That is the same logic as damped least squares in inverse kinematics.

**中文翻译**：要解 f(x) 等于零，牛顿法在当前猜测处用切线代替 f，并跳到切线过零的位置：x 新值等于 x 减 f 除以 f 的导数。对向量函数，我解雅可比乘 delta 等于负 f。靠近单根时二次收敛。失效模式包括：初值差、导数接近零造成巨大步长、雅可比奇异，以及某些函数上的循环或发散。保护措施是阻尼步长、限制步长，或在括号区间内退回二分法。这与逆运动学中的阻尼最小二乘逻辑相同。
**追问 1**：*What is the convergence rate of bisection?* Linear: it halves the interval each step.
　中文：线性：每步区间减半。
**追问 2**：*What is the secant method?* Newton with the derivative approximated from the last two points; superlinear convergence without needing a derivative.
　中文：用最近两点近似导数的牛顿法；无需导数，超线性收敛。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*An iterative IK step in P2 solves the Jacobian system with damping and joint-speed limits, so one cycle is a safeguarded Newton step.*
　中文：P2 里迭代 IK 的一步就是带阻尼和关节速度限制的雅可比方程求解，也就是一次受保护的牛顿步。
**关键词与易错点**：tangent、quadratic convergence、bisection、damped step。

## 7. F 李群与旋转的数学（Q71–Q76）

### Q71. Why can't you just add or average rotation matrices?
**中文题意**：为什么不能直接对旋转矩阵做加法或平均？
**考察点**：SO(3) 是流形而非向量空间。
**项目关联**：P1/P2/P6（姿态融合与滤波）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Rotations form a group called SO(3), not a vector space. The sum of two rotation matrices is generally not a rotation matrix: it is not orthogonal and its determinant is not one. Rotations also do not commute, so the order of composition matters. The set of rotations is a curved three-dimensional manifold sitting inside nine-dimensional matrix space. To average rotations properly, I can average on the manifold, for example by taking the mean in the tangent space and mapping back, or by averaging quaternions with sign alignment and then normalising, which is fine for small differences. A raw element-wise mean followed by projection with the SVD also works for a quick fix.

**中文翻译**：旋转构成称为 SO(3) 的群，不是向量空间。两个旋转矩阵之和一般不是旋转矩阵：不正交，行列式也不为 1。旋转还不可交换，所以组合顺序很重要。旋转集合是嵌在九维矩阵空间中的弯曲三维流形。要正确平均旋转，可在流形上做平均，例如在切空间取均值再映射回去，或对四元数先做符号对齐再平均并归一化（差异小时可行）。逐元素平均再用 SVD 投影回 SO(3) 也是快速的补救办法。
**追问 1**：*Why do quaternion signs need alignment?* The quaternions q and minus q represent the same rotation, so averaging them naively could cancel out.
　中文：q 与 −q 表示同一旋转，朴素平均可能相互抵消。
**追问 2**：*How would you interpolate between two orientations?* Spherical linear interpolation, slerp, which moves at constant angular speed along the great circle.
　中文：球面线性插值 slerp，沿大圆以恒定角速度运动。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P2 the VR hand orientation is applied as a relative rotation composed on the robot end-effector pose, rather than added.*
　中文：P2 里 VR 手的姿态是作为相对旋转复合到机器人末端位姿上，而不是相加。
**关键词与易错点**：SO(3)、manifold、non-commutative、slerp、quaternion sign。

### Q72. What is the Lie algebra so(3), the hat map and the exponential map?
**中文题意**：李代数 so(3)、hat 映射与指数映射是什么？
**考察点**：旋转的指数坐标。
**项目关联**：P1/P6（角速度积分、姿态误差）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The Lie algebra so(3) is the set of three-by-three skew-symmetric matrices. It is the tangent space of rotations at the identity. The hat map turns a three-vector omega into the skew-symmetric matrix omega hat, such that omega hat times v equals the cross product of omega and v. The exponential map goes from the algebra to the group: for a rotation vector theta times a unit axis n, the rotation matrix is given by Rodrigues' formula, R equals I plus sine theta times n hat, plus one minus cosine theta times n hat squared. Its inverse, the logarithm, gives the axis and angle of a rotation. I use them to integrate angular velocity and to define small attitude errors.

**中文翻译**：李代数 so(3) 是 3×3 反对称矩阵的集合，是旋转群在单位元处的切空间。hat 映射把三维向量 omega 变成反对称矩阵 omega hat，使 omega hat 乘 v 等于 omega 与 v 的叉积。指数映射从李代数到群：对由角度 theta 和单位轴 n 构成的旋转向量，旋转矩阵由 Rodrigues 公式给出，R 等于 I 加 sin theta 乘 n hat，再加 (1 减 cos theta) 乘 n hat 平方。其逆映射（对数映射）给出旋转的轴和角。我用它们来积分角速度并定义小的姿态误差。
**追问 1**：*How do you integrate a gyro reading into an orientation?* Compute the rotation vector omega times dt, exponentiate to a small rotation, and multiply on the correct side.
　中文：计算旋转向量 omega 乘 dt，指数映射成小旋转，再乘到正确的一侧。
**追问 2**：*Does the order of left and right multiplication matter?* Yes; body-frame angular velocity multiplies on the right, world-frame on the left.
　中文：重要；机体系角速度右乘，世界系角速度左乘。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*My AHRS work in P1 and P3 used an onboard or Mahony filter which does this integration internally; I used the resulting orientation and angular rate.*
　中文：P1、P3 的姿态估计用板载或 Mahony 滤波器，它内部完成了这个积分；我使用的是输出的姿态和角速度。
**关键词与易错点**：so(3)、hat map、Rodrigues、exponential map、logarithm。

### Q73. How do homogeneous transforms and SE(3) work?
**中文题意**：齐次变换与 SE(3) 如何工作？
**考察点**：刚体变换的复合与求逆。
**项目关联**：P2/P5/P6（坐标系、TF）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A rigid transform in three dimensions is a rotation R and a translation p. I pack them into a four-by-four matrix T with R in the top-left block, p in the top-right, and a bottom row of zero, zero, zero, one. Applying it to a point in homogeneous coordinates gives R x plus p. Composition is matrix multiplication, so a chain of frames is a product of transforms. The inverse is not the transpose of T; it is R transpose in the rotation block and minus R transpose p for the translation. The set of these matrices is the group SE(3), with six degrees of freedom. TF in ROS is essentially a tree of such transforms.

**中文翻译**：三维刚体变换由旋转 R 和平移 p 组成。我把它们打包成 4×4 矩阵 T：左上角是 R，右上角是 p，底行是 0, 0, 0, 1。作用在齐次坐标下的点上得到 R x 加 p。复合就是矩阵乘法，所以一串坐标系就是变换的乘积。逆不是 T 的转置，而是旋转块为 R 转置、平移为负 R 转置 p。这类矩阵的集合是群 SE(3)，有六个自由度。ROS 的 TF 本质上就是这类变换构成的树。
**追问 1**：*How do you transform a velocity or a wrench?* Use the adjoint of the transform, which combines the rotation and the translation in a six-by-six matrix.
　中文：用变换的伴随矩阵，它把旋转和平移组合成 6×6 矩阵。
**追问 2**：*What is the most common mistake with frames?* Applying a transform in the wrong direction, which confuses frame A in B with B in A.
　中文：把变换方向用反，混淆"A 在 B 中的位姿"与"B 在 A 中的位姿"。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 and P6 many of my bugs were frame and timestamp inconsistencies rather than algorithm errors, so I make the direction explicit in every variable name.*
　中文：P5、P6 里我的许多 bug 是坐标系与时间戳不一致而不是算法错误，所以我在每个变量名里写明方向。
**关键词与易错点**：homogeneous transform、SE(3)、inverse、adjoint、frame direction。

### Q74. Why do we use quaternions, and what are their pros and cons?
**中文题意**：为什么用四元数？优缺点？
**考察点**：姿态表示的取舍。
**项目关联**：P3（视觉接口回传四元数）、P1。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A unit quaternion has four numbers with unit norm. It represents a rotation by an angle theta around a unit axis n as cosine of theta over two, and n times sine of theta over two. The pros: no gimbal lock, only four numbers, easy to normalise, cheap to compose, and smooth to interpolate. The cons: it is less intuitive than Euler angles, and it double-covers the rotation group, since q and minus q are the same rotation. Euler angles are easy to read and good for display and for setting limits like pitch, but they have singularities. So I use quaternions inside the estimator, and convert to Euler angles for display and for simple control channels.

**中文翻译**：单位四元数是模为 1 的四个数。绕单位轴 n 旋转角度 theta，对应 cos(theta/2) 与 n 乘 sin(theta/2)。优点：没有万向锁、只有四个数、容易归一化、复合便宜、插值平滑。缺点：不如欧拉角直观，并且它对旋转群是双覆盖，q 与 −q 表示同一旋转。欧拉角易读，适合显示和设置俯仰等限制，但有奇异性。所以我在估计器内部用四元数，显示和简单控制通道里转成欧拉角。
**追问 1**：*What is gimbal lock?* With Euler angles, when the middle angle reaches plus or minus ninety degrees, two axes align and one degree of freedom is lost.
　中文：欧拉角在中间角达到正负 90 度时两个轴对齐，丢失一个自由度。
**追问 2**：*How do you rotate a vector with a quaternion?* Compute q times the vector as a pure quaternion times the conjugate of q.
　中文：把向量当作纯四元数，计算 q 乘向量再乘 q 的共轭。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P3 the board sent the attitude quaternion back to the vision computer, and I avoided Euler-angle jumps near plus or minus 180 degrees by filtering on the unit circle with sine and cosine.*
　中文：P3 里电控板把姿态四元数回传给视觉计算机，并用 sin、cos 在单位圆上滤波，避免 ±180° 附近的欧拉角跳变。
**关键词与易错点**：unit quaternion、gimbal lock、double cover、conjugate。

### Q75. How do you represent an attitude error in an estimator or controller?
**中文题意**：在估计器或控制器里如何表示姿态误差？
**考察点**：误差状态、小角度。
**项目关联**：P1（roll/pitch 平衡）、P3（云台）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I define the error as the relative rotation between the desired and the actual orientation: R error equals R desired transpose times R actual. For small errors I take the rotation vector of this relative rotation, which is a three-vector, and use it in a normal linear estimator or feedback law. This is the error-state idea: the full orientation lives on the manifold, but the error is small and lives in a three-dimensional vector space. It avoids subtracting angles, which gives wrong results near wrap-around, and it avoids the redundant four-number quaternion in the covariance, which would be singular.

**中文翻译**：我把误差定义为期望姿态与实际姿态之间的相对旋转：R 误差等于 R 期望转置乘 R 实际。对小误差，取这个相对旋转的旋转向量，它是一个三维向量，可用于普通线性估计器或反馈律。这就是误差状态的思想：完整姿态在流形上，但误差很小，处于三维向量空间。它避免了直接相减角度（在回绕处会出错），也避免了在协方差中使用冗余的四元数（会奇异）。
**追问 1**：*What does a multiplicative EKF do?* It estimates a small error rotation, then applies it to the nominal quaternion and resets the error to zero.
　中文：估计小的误差旋转，把它作用到名义四元数上，然后把误差重置为零。
**追问 2**：*Why is the covariance of a four-number quaternion singular?* The unit norm constraint leaves only three independent degrees of freedom.
　中文：单位模约束使独立自由度只有三个。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In my balance work, the roll and pitch errors are small tilt angles, so a direct small-angle difference after bias removal is valid; yaw needs wrap-around handling.*
　中文：我的平衡工作中，roll 和 pitch 误差是小倾角，去偏置后直接做小角差即可；yaw 需要处理回绕。
**关键词与易错点**：error state、relative rotation、rotation vector、multiplicative update。

### Q76. How do you handle angle wrap-around, for example yaw jumping from 179 to minus 179 degrees?
**中文题意**：如何处理角度回绕，例如 yaw 从 179 度跳到 −179 度？
**考察点**：工程上最常见的角度 bug。
**项目关联**：P3（yaw ±180° 跳变故障案例）、P5。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The problem is that 179 and minus 179 degrees are only two degrees apart, but a naive subtraction gives 358. For errors, I always wrap the difference into the range minus pi to pi, for example with atan2 of sine and cosine of the difference. For filtering or averaging angles, I do not filter the angle itself. I filter the sine and cosine, or the unit vector, and recover the angle with atan2 at the end. I also unwrap continuous signals when I need a monotonic angle for differentiation or plotting. These checks are cheap and prevent the sudden full-speed spins that wrap-around bugs can cause.

**中文翻译**：问题在于 179 度和 −179 度只相差 2 度，但朴素相减得到 358。对误差，我总是把差值回绕到 −pi 到 pi 范围，例如对差值的 sin 和 cos 取 atan2。对角度的滤波或平均，我不直接滤波角度本身，而是滤波 sin 和 cos（或单位向量），最后用 atan2 恢复角度。需要单调角度来做微分或绘图时，我还对连续信号做展开。这些检查很便宜，能防止回绕 bug 引起的突然全速旋转。
**追问 1**：*Why not just use degrees from 0 to 360?* The same wrap problem simply moves to the 0 and 360 boundary.
　中文：同样的回绕问题只是移到了 0 与 360 的边界。
**追问 2**：*What about the mean of 359 and 1 degrees?* The arithmetic mean is 180, which is wrong; the circular mean is 0.
　中文：算术平均为 180，是错的；圆周平均为 0。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*This was a real case in P3: the gimbal yaw jumped at plus or minus 180 degrees and the vision angle averaging was wrong, and I fixed it by filtering with sine and cosine on the unit circle.*
　中文：这是 P3 的真实案例：云台 yaw 在 ±180° 处跳变，视觉角度平均出错，我用单位圆上 sin、cos 滤波修复。
**关键词与易错点**：wrap-around、atan2、circular mean、unwrap。

- 最容易被问到的 12 题：Q1（特征值）、Q2（SVD）、Q4（正定）、Q5（最小二乘）、Q6（阻尼最小二乘）、Q17（线性化）、Q21（二阶系统）、Q28（贝叶斯）、Q34（融合）、Q44（凸性）、Q51（MPC 是 QP）、Q52（LQR 与 Riccati）。
- 与你项目绑定最紧的 8 题：Q6、Q14（P2）；Q17、Q52、Q53（P1/P7）；Q36（P5 门限）；Q42（AMCL）；Q76（P3 yaw 回绕）。这些题要能讲到"数字、方法、反思"三层。
- 数学题的通用收尾："I know the idea and where I used it; if you want the full proof, I would need a moment to write it out." 不要为了显得懂而硬推。
