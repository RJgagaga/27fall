## 2. A 反馈、PID 与经典控制（Q1–Q14）

### Q1. Why do we use feedback instead of open-loop control?
**中文题意**：为什么用反馈而不是开环控制？
**考察点**：反馈的基本价值：抗扰动、抗模型误差、镇定不稳定系统。
**项目关联**：P1、P7（不稳定对象必须反馈）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Open-loop control sends a command computed from a model and hopes for the best. It cannot correct for disturbances or model errors. Feedback measures the output, compares it with the reference, and corrects the command based on the error. That gives three benefits. First, it rejects disturbances, like a push on a robot. Second, it makes the system less sensitive to parameter errors, such as a wrong mass. Third, it can stabilise an unstable plant, which open loop never can. The price is that feedback needs sensors, adds noise and delay into the loop, and can itself cause instability if the gain is too high. So the design is a balance between accuracy, speed and robustness.

**中文翻译**：开环控制根据模型算出指令，然后寄希望于结果，无法纠正扰动和模型误差。反馈测量输出，与参考比较，并根据误差修正指令。这带来三个好处：第一，抑制扰动，比如机器人被推了一下；第二，降低对参数误差（如质量不准）的敏感性；第三，可以镇定不稳定的对象，这是开环永远做不到的。代价是需要传感器，会把噪声和延迟带入回路，增益过高时反馈本身也会导致不稳定。所以设计是在精度、速度和鲁棒性之间的平衡。
**追问 1**：*Can you give an example where open loop is acceptable?* A stepper motor within its torque limit, or a feedforward move on a well-modelled system, where the cost of sensors is not worth it.
　中文：步进电机在力矩限度内，或模型很准的系统上的前馈运动，传感器不值得。
**追问 2**：*What is the sensitivity function?* S equals one over one plus the loop gain L; it tells how much disturbances and model errors at the output are suppressed.
　中文：S 等于 1 除以 (1 加回路增益 L)，表示输出端扰动和模型误差被抑制的程度。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*A ballbot or a wheel-legged robot is an unstable plant, so in P1 and P7 feedback was not optional: without it the robot falls in a fraction of a second.*
　中文：球形机器人和轮腿机器人都是不稳定对象，所以 P1、P7 里反馈不是可选项，没有它机器人不到一秒就倒。
**关键词与易错点**：feedback、disturbance rejection、sensitivity、unstable plant。不要说反馈"总是"提高稳定性；增益过高会失稳。

### Q2. Explain PID control and what each term does.
**中文题意**：解释 PID 控制以及每一项的作用。
**考察点**：最基本的控制题，要讲清物理意义。
**项目关联**：P1、P2、P3（全部使用 PID）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A PID controller computes the command as three parts of the error. The proportional term is the gain Kp times the current error; it reacts immediately, but alone leaves a steady-state error for some plants. The integral term accumulates the error over time, so it removes steady-state error and rejects constant disturbances, but it adds phase lag and can wind up. The derivative term reacts to the rate of change of the error, so it adds damping and predicts where the error is heading, but it amplifies noise. In practice I tune Kp first for speed, add Kd to damp oscillation, and add a little Ki last to remove the steady offset.

**中文翻译**：PID 控制器把指令算成误差的三个部分。比例项是增益 Kp 乘当前误差，反应立即，但对某些对象单独使用会留下稳态误差。积分项随时间累积误差，所以消除稳态误差并抑制恒定扰动，但增加相位滞后并可能积分饱和。微分项响应误差的变化率，增加阻尼并预测误差的走向，但会放大噪声。实践中我先调 Kp 求速度，加 Kd 抑制振荡，最后加一点 Ki 消除稳态偏差。
**追问 1**：*Why is the integral term needed if the plant has no integrator?* With only P control, a constant disturbance needs a constant error to balance it; integral action drives that error to zero.
　中文：被控对象没有积分环节时，只用 P 控制，恒定扰动需要恒定误差来平衡；积分作用把误差驱动到零。
**追问 2**：*When would you use PD instead of PID?* When the plant already has an integrator or when the steady offset is acceptable or handled by feedforward, to avoid integral lag.
　中文：对象本身有积分环节，或稳态偏差可接受、可由前馈处理时，用 PD 避免积分滞后。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 the inner attitude loop is mainly PD, with a small integral term of about two to remove a residual mounting bias; in P3 the friction wheel loop used Kp about twelve and Ki about zero point two.*
　中文：P1 里姿态内环主要是 PD，带约 2 的小积分项消除残余安装偏置；P3 摩擦轮回路用 Kp 约 12、Ki 约 0.2。
**关键词与易错点**：proportional、integral、derivative、steady-state error、windup。不要把 PID 说成"万能"。

### Q3. What happens when you increase Kp, Ki or Kd? How do you tune a PID?
**中文题意**：增大 Kp、Ki、Kd 分别会怎样？你如何整定 PID？
**考察点**：整定经验与方法。
**项目关联**：P1、P3、P7（腿长 PID）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Increasing Kp makes the response faster and reduces the steady error, but increases overshoot and can cause oscillation. Increasing Ki removes the offset faster, but increases overshoot and can destabilise the loop. Increasing Kd reduces overshoot and adds damping, but amplifies noise and can excite high-frequency vibration. My procedure is to start with all gains at zero, raise Kp until the response is fast with slight oscillation, then back off. Then I add Kd until the oscillation is damped, and finally a small Ki. I always tune the inner loop first, then the outer loop, and I test on the real robot with limited gains and a safety rig.

**中文翻译**：增大 Kp 使响应更快、稳态误差更小，但超调增大并可能振荡；增大 Ki 更快消除偏差，但超调增加并可能失稳；增大 Kd 减少超调、增加阻尼，但放大噪声并可能激发高频振动。我的步骤是：所有增益从零开始，提高 Kp 直到响应快且略有振荡，然后回退；再加 Kd 抑制振荡；最后加小的 Ki。我总是先调内环再调外环，并在真机上用受限增益和保护装置测试。
**追问 1**：*Do you use Ziegler–Nichols?* It gives a starting point from the ultimate gain and period, but it tends to be aggressive; I prefer manual tuning with the plots.
　中文：它能从临界增益和周期给出起点，但往往偏激进；我更喜欢看曲线手动整定。
**追问 2**：*What do you look at to judge a tuning?* Step response overshoot and settling time, the control signal for saturation, and the noise in the output.
　中文：看阶跃响应的超调与调节时间、控制信号是否饱和、输出噪声。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*For P1 I went from a hanging-gantry test with low gains to touching the ground, and I used the LQR weight analysis to decide that the derivative gain should be about half of the proportional gain, which removed a 1.5 hertz oscillation.*
　中文：P1 里我先在龙门架吊挂下用低增益测试再落地，并用 LQR 权重分析判断微分增益应为比例增益的一半左右，消除了 1.5 Hz 振荡。
**关键词与易错点**：tuning、overshoot、inner loop first、safety rig。不要说"随便调"；要说步骤。

### Q4. What is integral windup and how do you prevent it?
**中文题意**：什么是积分饱和（windup）？如何防止？
**考察点**：实际 PID 最常见的问题。
**项目关联**：P1（积分限幅）、P3（积分限幅 3000）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Windup happens when the actuator is saturated, but the integrator keeps accumulating the error. When the error finally changes sign, the integrator is huge, and it takes a long time to unwind, causing a big overshoot or even a crash. The remedies are: clamp the integral term to a limit; stop integrating when the output is saturated, which is conditional integration; or use back-calculation, feeding the difference between the saturated and unsaturated output back into the integrator. I also clear the integrator when the controller is disabled or re-enabled, because stale integral memory can cause a jump at start-up.

**中文翻译**：当执行器饱和而积分器仍继续累积误差时就会发生 windup。误差终于变号时，积分量已经很大，需要很久才能释放，造成大超调甚至失控。对策有：把积分项钳位在限值内；输出饱和时停止积分（条件积分）；或使用反算法，把饱和前后输出之差反馈给积分器。控制器失能或重新使能时我还会清空积分器，因为旧的积分记忆会在启动时造成跳变。
**追问 1**：*Which method do you prefer?* Back-calculation behaves smoothly; clamping plus a stop-on-saturation rule is simplest and what I used.
　中文：反算法最平滑；钳位加饱和时停止积分最简单，也是我使用的。
**追问 2**：*Where else does windup appear?* In any controller with memory when the plant cannot follow, for example a position loop commanding a speed that a motor cannot reach.
　中文：任何带记忆的控制器在对象跟不上时都会，例如位置环要求电机达不到的速度。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 I used an integral clamp plus output saturation, not back-calculation, and I cleared the integrator, filters and target position on every re-enable. A missing clear caused a lunge on re-enable in one of my debug cases.*
　中文：P1 里我用积分钳位加输出限幅，没用反算法，并在每次重新使能时清积分、滤波和目标位置；有一次因为没清造成重新使能时猛冲。
**关键词与易错点**：windup、clamping、back-calculation、conditional integration。

### Q5. What is derivative kick, and why filter the derivative term?
**中文题意**：什么是微分冲击？为什么要对微分项滤波？
**考察点**：PID 的实际实现细节。
**项目关联**：P3、P1。
**English answer（口语）**：
> If the derivative acts on the error and the setpoint changes in a step, the derivative of the error is a spike, which gives a large kick to the output. The fix is to differentiate the measurement instead of the error, so a setpoint step does not create a spike. Second, a pure derivative amplifies measurement noise, so I use a first-order low-pass on the derivative term, with the corner frequency a few times the loop crossover. In many of my loops I use the gyro rate directly as the derivative of the angle, which is both cleaner and has no differentiation delay.

**中文翻译**：如果微分作用在误差上，设定值阶跃变化时误差的导数是尖峰，给输出一个大冲击。解决办法是对测量值而不是误差微分，这样设定值阶跃不产生尖峰。其次，纯微分放大测量噪声，所以我对微分项加一阶低通，转折频率取回路穿越频率的几倍。在我的很多回路里，直接用陀螺角速率作为角度的导数，既更干净又没有微分延迟。
**追问 1**：*What is two-degree-of-freedom PID?* It weights the setpoint differently in the proportional and derivative terms, so tracking and disturbance rejection can be tuned separately.
　中文：在比例项和微分项里对设定值使用不同权重，使跟踪和抗扰可分别整定。
**追问 2**：*How do you set the filter?* Choose the time constant as Kd over Kp divided by N, with N about 5 to 20.
　中文：时间常数取 Kd 除以 Kp 再除以 N，N 约 5 到 20。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P3 the gimbal inner loop feeds the gyro rate as the derivative signal, and in P1 the angular rate comes from the gyro as well, so no angle differentiation is needed.*
　中文：P3 的云台内环用陀螺角速率作为微分信号，P1 的角速率也来自陀螺，所以不需要对角度微分。
**关键词与易错点**：derivative kick、filtered derivative、measurement vs error。

### Q6. What are steady-state error and system type?
**中文题意**：什么是稳态误差和系统型别？
**考察点**：经典控制基础。
**项目关联**：P3（弹速偏差）、P4（湿度）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The steady-state error is the remaining error after transients die out. The system type is the number of pure integrators in the open-loop transfer function. A type-zero system has a finite steady error for a step reference. A type-one system has zero steady error for a step, and a finite error for a ramp. A type-two system tracks a ramp with zero error. So adding an integrator, which is what the integral term does, increases the type and removes steady errors. For constant disturbances, what matters is the number of integrators before the point where the disturbance enters. A common example is a motor speed loop: the plant is type zero, so P control leaves an offset and PI removes it.

**中文翻译**：稳态误差是瞬态消失后剩下的误差。系统型别是开环传递函数中纯积分器的个数。0 型系统对阶跃参考有有限稳态误差；1 型系统对阶跃为零误差，对斜坡有有限误差；2 型系统对斜坡零误差跟踪。所以增加积分器（积分项正是这个作用）提升型别，消除稳态误差。对恒定扰动，关键是扰动进入点之前的积分器个数。常见例子是电机速度环：对象是 0 型，P 控制有偏差，PI 消除它。
**追问 1**：*What is the steady error of a type-zero system?* One over one plus the DC loop gain Kp, for a unit step.
　中文：对单位阶跃，是 1 除以 (1 加直流回路增益 Kp)。
**追问 2**：*Is an integrator in the controller enough for disturbance rejection at the plant input?* Yes, if it is upstream of where the disturbance enters; it will drive the error to zero.
　中文：是，只要它在扰动进入点之前，就会把误差驱动到零。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P3 the friction wheel speed loop is a type-zero plant, so a purely proportional loop leaves a speed offset, and that is why the loop uses a small integral term.*
　中文：P3 里摩擦轮速度环是 0 型对象，纯比例会留下转速偏差，所以回路带了小积分项。
**关键词与易错点**：steady-state error、system type、integrator、ramp。

### Q7. Why do you use cascaded control loops?
**中文题意**：为什么使用级联控制？
**考察点**：带宽分离与结构。
**项目关联**：P1（位置—姿态级联）、P3（角度—角速度级联）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Cascade control puts a fast inner loop inside a slower outer loop. The outer controller does not command the actuator directly; it gives a reference to the inner loop. There are three benefits. The inner loop handles fast disturbances and nonlinearity before they reach the outer loop. Each loop is a simpler plant, so it is easier to tune. And I can limit the inner reference, which gives a natural way to bound the tilt or the speed. The rule of thumb is that the inner loop should be at least three to five times faster than the outer one, so the outer loop sees the inner one as an almost instantaneous gain. In my ballbot, the ratio was about six to seven.

**中文翻译**：级联控制把快的内环放在慢的外环里面。外环控制器不直接指挥执行器，而是给内环一个参考。好处有三：内环在快速扰动和非线性到达外环之前就处理掉；每个环对应的对象更简单，更好整定；还可以限制内环的参考，自然地给倾角或速度设界。经验法则是内环至少比外环快三到五倍，这样外环把内环看成近乎瞬时的增益。我的球形机器人里，比例约为六到七倍。
**追问 1**：*What happens if the inner and outer bandwidths are too close?* The loops interact, the outer loop sees the inner dynamics, and the system can oscillate.
　中文：两个环相互作用，外环能看到内环动态，系统可能振荡。
**追问 2**：*Which loop do you tune first?* The inner loop, with the outer loop open, then the outer.
　中文：先调内环（外环断开），再调外环。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 the outer position loop is at about 0.15 hertz, the attitude inner loop at about 1 hertz, and the motor speed loop at about 20 hertz, so each pair is separated by roughly a factor of six or more.*
　中文：P1 里位置外环约 0.15 Hz，姿态内环约 1 Hz，电机速度环约 20 Hz，每对之间约相差六倍以上。
**关键词与易错点**：cascade、bandwidth separation、inner loop、bounded reference。

### Q8. Explain gain margin and phase margin. Why do they matter?
**中文题意**：解释增益裕度和相位裕度，为什么重要？
**考察点**：经典稳定性裕度。
**项目关联**：P3（延迟与相位代价）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Look at the open-loop frequency response. The phase margin is how much extra phase lag I could add at the gain crossover frequency, where the loop gain is one, before the loop becomes unstable. The gain margin is how much I could increase the gain at the frequency where the phase is minus 180 degrees before instability. Typical targets are a phase margin of 45 to 60 degrees and a gain margin above six decibels. They matter because a real system has delay, unmodelled dynamics and parameter errors, and margins tell how much of that the loop can tolerate. A small phase margin means a lightly damped, oscillatory response.

**中文翻译**：看开环频率响应。相位裕度是在增益穿越频率（回路增益为 1 处）还能再增加多少相位滞后回路才不稳定；增益裕度是在相位为 −180 度的频率处还能增大多少增益才不稳定。典型目标是相位裕度 45 到 60 度、增益裕度大于 6 dB。它们重要，是因为真实系统有延迟、未建模动态和参数误差，裕度反映回路能容忍多少。相位裕度小意味着响应阻尼小、振荡。
**追问 1**：*How does a pure delay affect the phase margin?* It adds phase lag of omega times the delay at each frequency, reducing the margin at crossover.
　中文：延迟在各频率增加 omega 乘延迟的相位滞后，降低穿越频率处的裕度。
**追问 2**：*How are margins related to damping ratio?* For a standard second-order loop, a phase margin of about 60 degrees corresponds to a damping ratio of about 0.6 to 0.7.
　中文：对标准二阶回路，约 60 度相位裕度对应约 0.6 到 0.7 的阻尼比。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P3 the loop delay was a few milliseconds; at the gimbal inner loop's roughly 35 hertz crossover this costs about 50 degrees of phase, so I would not add more filtering than necessary.*
　中文：P3 里回路延迟几毫秒；在云台内环约 35 Hz 穿越频率处约损失 50 度相位，所以不应添加多余滤波。
**关键词与易错点**：gain margin、phase margin、crossover frequency、delay。

### Q9. What do Bode, Nyquist and root locus plots tell you?
**中文题意**：伯德图、奈奎斯特图、根轨迹各告诉你什么？
**考察点**：经典设计工具。
**项目关联**：概念题。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A Bode plot shows the gain and phase of the open loop against frequency, so I can read the crossover frequency and the stability margins and see the effect of a filter or a delay. The Nyquist plot traces the open-loop frequency response in the complex plane; the Nyquist criterion counts encirclements of minus one to decide closed-loop stability, which also works for open-loop unstable plants. The root locus shows how the closed-loop poles move as one gain increases from zero to infinity; it is good for seeing the effect of a gain and for placing poles. In daily work I use step responses and Bode-like reasoning most, and the others as a check.

**中文翻译**：伯德图画出开环增益和相位随频率的变化，我可以读出穿越频率和稳定裕度，看滤波器或延迟的影响。奈奎斯特图在复平面上画出开环频率响应；奈奎斯特判据通过统计对 −1 点的包围圈数判断闭环稳定性，对开环不稳定对象也适用。根轨迹显示某个增益从零增到无穷时闭环极点的移动，适合看增益的影响和配置极点。日常工作中我最常用阶跃响应和类似伯德图的推理，其他的作为核对。
**追问 1**：*Why is Nyquist needed for unstable plants?* The simple Bode margin rules assume an open-loop stable system; Nyquist handles the general case.
　中文：简单的伯德裕度规则假设开环稳定；奈奎斯特处理一般情形。
**追问 2**：*When is root locus misleading?* With significant delay or high-order unmodelled dynamics, because it only covers the model's poles.
　中文：有明显延迟或高阶未建模动态时，因为它只覆盖模型的极点。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*For my projects I mostly checked time-domain behaviour on the real robot, and used the frequency view to reason about delay and filter choice.*
　中文：我的项目里主要在真机上看时域行为，用频域视角分析延迟和滤波选择。
**关键词与易错点**：Bode plot、Nyquist criterion、root locus、encirclement。

### Q10. What is the difference between BIBO stability and asymptotic stability?
**中文题意**：BIBO 稳定与渐近稳定有何区别？
**考察点**：稳定性定义。
**项目关联**：概念题。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Asymptotic stability is about the internal state: if I start away from the equilibrium with no input, the state converges back to it. BIBO stability, bounded-input bounded-output, is about the input–output behaviour: every bounded input produces a bounded output. For a linear time-invariant system, asymptotic stability implies BIBO stability. The reverse can fail when there is an unstable mode hidden from the input–output map, because of a pole–zero cancellation, an uncontrollable or unobservable mode. That is why in practice I care about internal stability of the state space model, not only the transfer function.

**中文翻译**：渐近稳定关乎内部状态：从偏离平衡点的位置出发、无输入，状态收敛回平衡点。BIBO 稳定（有界输入有界输出）关乎输入输出行为：每个有界输入都产生有界输出。对线性时不变系统，渐近稳定蕴含 BIBO 稳定。反过来可能不成立：当存在被输入输出映射隐藏的不稳定模态（极零点相消、不可控或不可观模态）时。所以实践中我关心状态空间模型的内部稳定性，而不仅是传递函数。
**追问 1**：*What is Lyapunov stability?* The state stays near the equilibrium if it starts near it; asymptotic stability adds convergence.
　中文：从平衡点附近出发则状态保持在附近；渐近稳定在此基础上再加收敛。
**追问 2**：*Give an example of hidden instability.* A controller that cancels an unstable plant pole with a zero: the transfer function looks stable but an internal state grows.
　中文：控制器用零点抵消对象的不稳定极点：传递函数看似稳定，但内部状态增长。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*This is why, for the inverted pendulum, I never cancel the unstable pole; the controller moves it into the left half plane by feedback.*
　中文：所以对倒立摆，我从不相消不稳定极点；控制器通过反馈把它移到左半平面。
**关键词与易错点**：BIBO、asymptotic stability、internal stability、pole–zero cancellation。

### Q11. How does time delay affect a control loop?
**中文题意**：时间延迟如何影响控制回路？
**考察点**：延迟与相位裕度。
**项目关联**：P5（120 ms 延迟）、P2（30 ms 主从延迟）、P1（15 ms）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A pure delay of T seconds has unit gain and a phase lag of omega times T that grows linearly with frequency. So it does not change the gain but erodes the phase margin at the crossover frequency, and it limits the achievable bandwidth. A rough rule is that the loop bandwidth should be much lower than one over the delay. Practical ways to cope: reduce the delay at the source, such as faster communication and less filtering; reduce the loop gain; add a predictor, such as a Smith predictor or a model-based forward prediction of the state; or use feedforward, which is not affected by feedback delay.

**中文翻译**：延迟 T 秒的纯延迟增益为 1，相位滞后为 omega 乘 T，随频率线性增长。所以它不改变增益，但会削减穿越频率处的相位裕度，并限制可达带宽。粗略规则是回路带宽应远低于延迟的倒数。实际对策：从源头减小延迟，如更快的通信和更少的滤波；降低回路增益；加预测器，如 Smith 预测器或基于模型的状态前向预测；或使用不受反馈延迟影响的前馈。
**追问 1**：*Why can high-gain feedback with delay be dangerous?* The phase at high frequency becomes very negative, so a larger gain pushes the crossover to where the margin is negative.
　中文：高频相位变得很负，增益更大会把穿越频率推到裕度为负的位置。
**追问 2**：*What is a Smith predictor?* It uses a model without delay to predict the output, and corrects with the difference between the real and modelled outputs; it relies on a good model.
　中文：用无延迟模型预测输出，并以真实与模型输出之差修正；依赖准确模型。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 the total delay was about 120 milliseconds, and I compensated by predicting the vehicle state forward with the commands already sent before solving the MPC.*
　中文：P5 里总延迟约 120 ms，我在求解 MPC 之前用已发出的指令把车辆状态前推来补偿。
**关键词与易错点**：delay、phase lag、Smith predictor、state prediction。

### Q12. What is bandwidth, and how do you choose the sampling rate relative to it?
**中文题意**：什么是带宽？采样率相对带宽怎么选？
**考察点**：数字控制频率选择。
**项目关联**：P1（100 Hz vs 1 Hz）、P7（500 Hz）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The closed-loop bandwidth is roughly the frequency up to which the output follows the reference well, often defined where the gain drops by three decibels. It is related to the speed of the response: higher bandwidth means a faster response, and also more sensitivity to noise and delay. As a rule of thumb, I sample at least ten times, and preferably twenty to thirty times, faster than the closed-loop bandwidth, so the discretisation delay of about half a sample does not hurt the phase margin. A faster sample rate costs CPU and bus load, so I pick the lowest rate that satisfies the margin.

**中文翻译**：闭环带宽大致是输出能良好跟随参考的频率上限，通常定义为增益下降 3 dB 处。它与响应速度有关：带宽越高响应越快，也对噪声和延迟更敏感。经验法则是采样率至少为闭环带宽的 10 倍，最好 20 到 30 倍，使约半个采样周期的离散化延迟不损害相位裕度。更高的采样率消耗 CPU 和总线负载，所以我选满足裕度要求的最低速率。
**追问 1**：*Where does the half-sample delay come from?* The zero-order hold holds the command for the full period, equivalent to an average delay of half a period.
　中文：零阶保持把指令保持一个完整周期，相当于平均半个周期的延迟。
**追问 2**：*Is a higher rate always better?* No; it increases noise sensitivity in the derivative term, CPU use and bus load, and quantisation effects may appear.
　中文：不是；它会增加微分项的噪声敏感度、CPU 与总线负载，还可能出现量化效应。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 the loop runs at 100 hertz with the outer bandwidth around 0.15 hertz and the attitude loop around 1 hertz, which is well within the rule; in P7 the 500 hertz loop leaves plenty of room for a fast balance loop.*
　中文：P1 里回路 100 Hz、外环带宽约 0.15 Hz、姿态环约 1 Hz，远在规则之内；P7 的 500 Hz 回路给快速平衡环留足余量。
**关键词与易错点**：bandwidth、sampling rate、ten times rule、half-sample delay。

### Q13. What are poles and zeros, and what is a non-minimum-phase system?
**中文题意**：极点和零点是什么？什么是非最小相位系统？
**考察点**：传递函数性质。
**项目关联**：P1/P7（倒立摆的零点）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Poles are the roots of the denominator of the transfer function and determine the natural modes and stability. Zeros are the roots of the numerator and shape how the input excites the modes. A non-minimum-phase system has a zero in the right half plane. Its step response starts in the wrong direction before going the right way, like steering a car backwards. Such zeros limit the achievable bandwidth: I cannot make the loop faster than roughly the zero frequency without losing stability margins. In balancing systems, the cart position seen from the push force has right-half-plane zeros, which is why to move a balancing cart forward it must first move back a little to tip the pendulum forward.

**中文翻译**：极点是传递函数分母的根，决定固有模态和稳定性；零点是分子的根，决定输入如何激发模态。非最小相位系统在右半平面有零点，阶跃响应先朝错误方向走再走向正确方向，就像倒车转向。这类零点限制可达带宽：回路速度不能超过零点频率附近，否则失去稳定裕度。在平衡系统中，从推力看小车位置有右半平面零点，所以平衡中的小车要想前进，必须先稍微后退，把摆向前倾倒。
**追问 1**：*Why can't a right-half-plane zero be cancelled?* Cancelling it would need an unstable pole in the controller, which gives hidden instability.
　中文：相消需要控制器含不稳定极点，造成隐藏不稳定。
**追问 2**：*What do dominant poles mean?* The poles closest to the imaginary axis, which dominate the slow response; faster poles can often be ignored.
　中文：最靠近虚轴的极点，主导慢响应；更快的极点常可忽略。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In my balance robots, to start moving forward the wheels first drive backward to tip the body forward, so the position response to the wheel command starts in the wrong direction. That is part of why position control sits in the slow outer loop.*
　中文：我的平衡机器人要开始前进，轮子必须先后退让机体前倾，所以位置对轮指令的响应一开始方向相反。这也是位置控制放在慢外环的原因之一。
**关键词与易错点**：poles、zeros、non-minimum phase、right half plane、dominant pole。

### Q14. What is the difference between Routh–Hurwitz and eigenvalue checks for stability?
**中文题意**：劳斯—赫尔维茨判据与直接算特征值有什么区别？
**考察点**：稳定性判据的实际用法。
**项目关联**：概念题。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Both answer the same question for a linear system: are all roots of the characteristic polynomial in the left half plane? The eigenvalue check computes the roots numerically and looks at the real parts. The Routh–Hurwitz criterion builds a table from the polynomial coefficients and counts sign changes in the first column, so it decides stability without finding the roots. It is valuable when the coefficients contain a symbolic parameter such as a gain, because I can derive the range of the gain that keeps the system stable. With numbers, I simply call an eigenvalue routine. For discrete systems, the corresponding test is that all eigenvalues are inside the unit circle, with the Jury criterion as the table-based version.

**中文翻译**：对线性系统，两者回答同一个问题：特征多项式的所有根是否在左半平面？特征值检验是数值求根再看实部。劳斯—赫尔维茨判据用多项式系数建表并数第一列的符号变化，不需要求根就能判断稳定性。当系数含有符号参数（如增益）时它很有价值，因为可以推出保持稳定的增益范围。有具体数值时我直接调用特征值函数。离散系统的对应检验是所有特征值在单位圆内，表格形式是 Jury 判据。
**追问 1**：*A simple use?* For a third-order polynomial with a gain K, the table gives the maximum K before instability.
　中文：对含增益 K 的三阶多项式，表格给出失稳前的最大 K。
**追问 2**：*What if a root is on the imaginary axis?* The system is marginally stable at best; the Routh table shows a row of zeros.
　中文：最多是临界稳定；劳斯表出现全零行。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In practice I check the closed-loop eigenvalues of A minus B K numerically for my pendulum analysis, and for discrete designs I check that they are inside the unit circle.*
　中文：实践中，在倒立摆分析里我数值检查 A 减 B K 的闭环特征值，离散设计则检查它们在单位圆内。
**关键词与易错点**：Routh–Hurwitz、characteristic polynomial、unit circle、Jury criterion。

### Q15. What is a state-space model, and why use it instead of a transfer function?
**中文题意**：什么是状态空间模型？为什么不只用传递函数？
**考察点**：现代控制的出发点。
**项目关联**：P1、P7。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A state-space model describes the system with a state vector x, which holds everything I need to predict the future given the inputs. The model is x dot equals A x plus B u, and the output is y equals C x plus D u. Compared with a transfer function, it handles multiple inputs and outputs naturally, shows internal states, and allows tools like pole placement, LQR, observers and MPC. It also works directly in the time domain, which is closer to simulation and code. The transfer function is still great for single-input single-output frequency thinking, but for a balancing robot with several coupled states, state space is the natural language.

**中文翻译**：状态空间模型用状态向量 x 描述系统，x 包含给定输入后预测未来所需的全部信息。模型为 x 导数等于 A x 加 B u，输出 y 等于 C x 加 D u。与传递函数相比，它自然处理多输入多输出、显示内部状态，并支持极点配置、LQR、观测器和 MPC。它直接工作在时域，更贴近仿真和代码。传递函数仍然适合单输入单输出的频域思考，但对具有多个耦合状态的平衡机器人，状态空间是自然的语言。
**追问 1**：*How do you go from state space to a transfer function?* G of s equals C times s I minus A inverse times B plus D.
　中文：G(s) 等于 C 乘 (s I 减 A) 的逆乘 B 再加 D。
**追问 2**：*Is the state vector unique?* No; any invertible change of coordinates gives an equivalent model, but the eigenvalues stay the same.
　中文：不唯一；任何可逆坐标变换给出等价模型，特征值不变。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 I used a four-state model of position, velocity, tilt and tilt rate; in P7 it is six states including the leg angle and body pitch.*
　中文：P1 用位置、速度、倾角、倾角速率四个状态；P7 是包含腿角和机身俯仰的六个状态。
**关键词与易错点**：state vector、A B C D、MIMO、coordinate change。

### Q16. What is an observer, and what is the separation principle?
**中文题意**：什么是观测器？分离原理是什么？
**考察点**：状态估计与控制的关系。
**项目关联**：P7（速度估计）、P5（EKF）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I often cannot measure all states, for example velocity. An observer is a model-based estimator: it runs a copy of the system and corrects it with the measurement error, x hat dot equals A x hat plus B u plus L times y minus C x hat. The observer gain L sets how fast the estimate converges. The separation principle says that for a linear system, I can design the state-feedback gain K and the observer gain L independently, and the combined closed loop has the union of both sets of poles. The Kalman filter is the optimal observer for known noise statistics. In practice the observer should be faster than the controller, but not so fast that it amplifies noise.

**中文翻译**：我常常无法测量所有状态，例如速度。观测器是基于模型的估计器：它运行系统的副本，并用测量误差来修正，x hat 导数等于 A x hat 加 B u 加 L 乘 (y 减 C x hat)。观测器增益 L 决定估计收敛的快慢。分离原理说，对线性系统，状态反馈增益 K 与观测器增益 L 可以独立设计，合成闭环的极点是两组极点的并集。卡尔曼滤波器是已知噪声统计下的最优观测器。实践中观测器应比控制器快，但不能快到放大噪声。
**追问 1**：*What condition is required for an observer?* The pair A and C must be observable, or at least detectable.
　中文：A 与 C 须可观，至少可检测。
**追问 2**：*Does separation hold for nonlinear systems or with delay?* Not in general; the performance can degrade and needs separate verification.
　中文：一般不成立；性能可能下降，需要单独验证。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P7 I estimated body velocity with a one-dimensional Kalman filter fusing wheel speed and acceleration, with a process noise of about 0.001 and a measurement noise of about 0.01.*
　中文：P7 里我用一维卡尔曼滤波融合轮速和加速度估计机身速度，过程噪声约 0.001、测量噪声约 0.01。
**关键词与易错点**：observer、observer gain、separation principle、observable。

### Q17. Compare pole placement and LQR.
**中文题意**：比较极点配置与 LQR。
**考察点**：两种状态反馈设计思路。
**项目关联**：P1、P7。
**English answer（口语）**：
> In pole placement I choose the closed-loop pole locations directly, and the formula gives K. It is intuitive for a single-input system, but it is not obvious where to place the poles for a multi-state system, and aggressive poles cause very large gains. LQR chooses K by minimising a quadratic cost with state weights Q and control weight R. It gives a stable closed loop with good margins, and it automatically balances many states and many inputs. The tuning knobs are Q and R, which have a physical meaning: how much I care about each state error versus control effort. For multi-state robots I prefer LQR; for a quick check of speed and damping on a small model I may use pole placement.

**中文翻译**：极点配置直接选定闭环极点位置，公式给出 K。对单输入系统直观，但对多状态系统不清楚该把极点放在哪里，而且激进的极点会带来很大的增益。LQR 通过最小化带状态权重 Q 与控制权重 R 的二次代价来选 K，给出稳定且裕度良好的闭环，自动平衡多个状态和多个输入。调参旋钮是 Q 与 R，物理意义明确：对每个状态误差与控制量的重视程度。对多状态机器人我偏好 LQR；在小模型上快速检查速度与阻尼时可能用极点配置。
**追问 1**：*How does the size of K relate to pole location?* Faster poles need larger gains and more control effort, so actuator limits cap how fast I can place them.
　中文：更快的极点需要更大的增益和控制量，因此执行器限制决定了极点能放多快。
**追问 2**：*Is LQR always better?* It is optimal for its cost, but the cost is a design choice and the model must be right; it does not guarantee good behaviour with saturation or delay.
　中文：对其代价最优，但代价是设计选择且模型必须准确；不能保证饱和或延迟下的良好表现。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P7 LQR weights let me express that pitch matters most, with an entry of about five thousand on pitch compared to about a hundred on leg angle, and the solver turns that into gains.*
　中文：P7 里 LQR 权重让我表达"俯仰最重要"，俯仰权重约 5000、腿角约 100，由求解器转成增益。
**关键词与易错点**：pole placement、LQR、Q and R、control effort。

### Q18. How do you choose the Q and R matrices in LQR?
**中文题意**：LQR 的 Q 和 R 怎么选？
**考察点**：整定思路。
**项目关联**：P1（Q = diag(4, 4, 100, 1)，R = 0.25）、P7。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I start with Bryson's rule: set each diagonal entry to one over the square of the maximum acceptable value of that state, and R to one over the square of the maximum acceptable control. That gives a normalised starting point. Then I adjust by physical intent. If tilt must stay small, I raise its weight; if the commands saturate or the response is too aggressive, I raise R or lower the weights on fast states. I check the closed-loop poles, the simulated response, and the peak control. Because only the ratio matters, I change one entry at a time and watch how the gain K changes. It is an engineering exercise, not a pure optimisation.

**中文翻译**：我从 Bryson 法则开始：每个对角元取该状态最大可接受值平方的倒数，R 取最大可接受控制量平方的倒数，得到归一化的起点。然后按物理意图调整：倾角必须小则提高其权重；指令饱和或响应太激进则提高 R 或降低快状态的权重。我检查闭环极点、仿真响应和峰值控制量。由于只有比例重要，我一次只改一个元素，观察增益 K 怎么变。这是工程活，而不是纯优化。
**追问 1**：*What happens if Q is much larger than R?* The gains become large, the response becomes fast, and the control effort and noise sensitivity rise.
　中文：增益变大，响应变快，控制量和噪声敏感度上升。
**追问 2**：*Do the absolute values matter?* Scaling Q and R by the same factor leaves K unchanged.
　中文：Q 和 R 同比例缩放，K 不变。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 I used Q equal to diag of four, four, one hundred and one with R equal to zero point two five on a normalised four-state model, giving gains of about minus four, minus 10.5, minus 46 and minus 21 in my convention; the tilt gain is the largest, as expected.*
　中文：P1 里在归一化四状态模型上用 Q 等于 diag(4, 4, 100, 1)、R 等于 0.25，按我的符号约定增益约为 −4、−10.5、−46、−21；倾角增益最大，符合预期。
**关键词与易错点**：Bryson's rule、weighting、normalised model、ratio matters。

### Q21. Walk me through the four-state inverted pendulum model.
**中文题意**：请讲一下四状态倒立摆模型。
**考察点**：能否口述建模流程。
**项目关联**：P1（核心）、P7。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The four states are position, velocity, tilt angle and tilt rate. I write the equations of motion with Newton–Euler or Lagrange for a body of mass m at height l above the contact, driven by a horizontal force or wheel torque. Then I linearise around the upright position, with sine tilt approximately equal to tilt and cosine approximately one, and drop the products of small terms. The result is x dot equals A x plus B u. The tilt rate row of A has a positive coefficient of about g over l on tilt, which is the unstable mode. The B column shows how a wheel command accelerates the base and, by reaction, tilts the body. Controllability is full, so LQR is well defined.

**中文翻译**：四个状态是位置、速度、倾角和倾角速率。我用牛顿—欧拉或拉格朗日写出质量为 m、质心在接触点上方 l 高度处、由水平力或轮力矩驱动的物体的运动方程。然后围绕直立位置线性化，sin 倾角近似倾角、cos 近似 1，并忽略小量的乘积。结果是 x 导数等于 A x 加 B u。A 中倾角速率那一行在倾角上有约 g/l 的正系数，就是不稳定模态。B 列显示轮指令如何加速底座并通过反作用使机体倾斜。可控性满秩，所以 LQR 定义良好。
**追问 1**：*Why is the coefficient positive?* Gravity pulls the centre of mass further away from the vertical once it leans; the restoring effect is absent.
　中文：一旦倾斜，重力使质心更远离竖直，没有恢复作用。
**追问 2**：*Which assumptions are hidden in this model?* Rigid body, no slip at the contact, constant parameters, small tilt, and no actuator dynamics.
　中文：刚体、接触无滑移、参数恒定、小倾角、无执行器动态。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 the true robot is a ball with three omni wheels and arms moving, so the model is a simplified reference; the arm motion shifts the centre of mass, which I handled with feedforward.*
　中文：P1 的真实机器人是三个全向轮驱动的球且手臂在动，所以模型是简化的参考；手臂运动使质心移动，我用前馈处理。

### Q22. Why is an inverted pendulum unstable, and what do its poles look like?
**中文题意**：倒立摆为什么不稳定？极点长什么样？
**考察点**：物理直觉与数学对应。
**项目关联**：P1、P7。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Physically, when the pendulum leans, gravity creates a torque that increases the lean. There is no restoring torque, unlike a hanging pendulum. For a simple pendulum linearised about the upright position, theta double dot equals g over l times theta. The characteristic equation is s squared equals g over l, so the poles are plus and minus the square root of g over l. One is positive, so a disturbance grows like e to the power of the square root of g over l times t. For a length of half a metre, this is a growth rate of about four and a half per second, meaning that the lean doubles roughly every 0.15 seconds. That is why the control loop must be fast.

**中文翻译**：物理上，摆一倾斜，重力产生使倾斜增加的力矩，不像悬挂摆那样有恢复力矩。对在直立位置线性化的简单摆，theta 二阶导数等于 g/l 乘 theta。特征方程为 s 平方等于 g/l，极点为正负根号下 g/l。其中一个为正，所以扰动按 e 的 (根号 g/l 乘 t) 次方增长。长度 0.5 米时，增长率约每秒 4.5，意味着倾斜大约每 0.15 秒翻倍。这就是控制回路必须快的原因。
**追问 1**：*Check the number.* g over l is about 19.6 per second squared for half a metre, so the square root is about 4.4 per second; doubling time is ln 2 over 4.4, about 0.16 seconds.
　中文：0.5 米时 g/l 约 19.6，开方约 4.4 每秒；翻倍时间为 ln2/4.4，约 0.16 秒。
**追问 2**：*Does a longer pendulum fall slower?* Yes; the pole is the square root of g over l, so a taller body is easier to balance.
　中文：是；极点是根号 g/l，所以更高的机体更容易平衡。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*That is why my loop rate matters: with a 100 hertz loop in P1 and a 500 hertz loop in P7, the unstable pole is far below the sampling rate.*
　中文：所以回路频率很关键：P1 的 100 Hz 和 P7 的 500 Hz 都远高于不稳定极点。
**关键词与易错点**：restoring torque、unstable pole、doubling time、square root of g over l。

### Q23. What is gain scheduling, and why did the wheel-legged robot need it?
**中文题意**：什么是增益调度？轮腿机器人为什么需要？
**考察点**：参数变化系统的控制。
**项目关联**：P7（核心）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Gain scheduling designs a family of controllers for different operating points and switches or interpolates between them using a scheduling variable. In the wheel-legged robot, the leg length changes the height of the centre of mass and the inertia, so the linearised model changes. A single LQR gain designed at one leg length loses stability margin when the leg is far from that length. So I solved LQR at several leg lengths, fitted each gain element as a cubic polynomial of the leg length, and evaluated the polynomial online. That is twelve gain elements, four coefficients each, so forty-eight coefficients, and I clamp the leg length to the fitted range from about 0.10 to 0.30 metres.

**中文翻译**：增益调度为不同工作点设计一族控制器，并用调度变量在它们之间切换或插值。轮腿机器人里，腿长改变质心高度和转动惯量，因此线性化模型也变化。在某一腿长设计的单个 LQR 增益，在腿长远离该值时稳定裕度下降。所以我在多个腿长上求解 LQR，把每个增益元素拟合成腿长的三次多项式，在线计算多项式。共 12 个增益元素、每个 4 个系数，一共 48 个系数，并把腿长限制在约 0.10 到 0.30 米的拟合区间内。
**追问 1**：*What is the risk of gain scheduling?* Stability is only guaranteed for frozen operating points; fast changes of the scheduling variable can destabilise the loop, so I limit the leg-length rate.
　中文：稳定性仅对"冻结"工作点保证；调度变量快速变化可能使回路失稳，所以我限制腿长变化速率。
**追问 2**：*Why a polynomial rather than a lookup table?* Smooth, cheap to evaluate on a microcontroller, and no interpolation discontinuities.
　中文：平滑、在微控制器上计算便宜，没有插值不连续。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*A fixed gain designed at one leg length clearly lost margin when the leg length moved far away, which is why I scheduled it.*
　中文：单一腿长下设计的固定增益在腿长远离时明显损失裕度，所以我做了调度。
**关键词与易错点**：gain scheduling、scheduling variable、cubic polynomial、frozen operating point。

### Q24. How do you make an LQR track a reference, not just regulate to zero?
**中文题意**：如何让 LQR 跟踪参考而不只是调节到零？
**考察点**：LQR 的扩展。
**项目关联**：P7（速度指令跟踪）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Standard LQR drives the state to zero. For time-varying references, I use a feedforward from the reference trajectory, or switch to MPC, which handles tracking and constraints natively.

**中文翻译**：标准 LQR 把状态驱动到零。为了跟踪恒定参考，我平移状态坐标：用 u 等于负 K 乘 (x 减 x 参考)，再加一个保持平衡点的前馈项。对速度指令，参考状态包含期望速度和零倾角。存在恒定扰动或模型误差时，我通过把跟踪误差的积分扩增进状态来加入积分作用，并在扩增系统上设计 LQR。对时变参考，我用来自参考轨迹的前馈，或切换到天然处理跟踪与约束的 MPC。

　中文：LQR 理论针对偏离平衡点的偏差，所以参考必须是一个平衡点。
**追问 2**：*Is a large step command safe?* No; I shape it with a ramp and an acceleration limit, because a step can saturate the actuators.
　中文：不安全；我用斜坡和加速度限制整形，因为阶跃会使执行器饱和。

### Q25. Continuous-time versus discrete-time LQR: which do you use and why?
**中文题意**：连续时间与离散时间 LQR，你用哪个？为什么？

　中文：用上一次施加的输入扩增状态，在扩增模型上设计 LQR。
**追问 2**：*What if the sample time jitters?* The gains no longer match the model; I keep the loop time-triggered and measure the jitter.
　中文：增益与模型不再匹配；我保持时间触发并测量抖动。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P7 the loop runs at about 500 hertz on the STM32H7 under FreeRTOS, so the difference between the continuous and discrete gains is small for the balance states.*
　中文：P7 里循环在 FreeRTOS 下的 STM32H7 上约 500 Hz，平衡状态的连续与离散增益差别很小。

### Q26. What is LQG? Does it inherit the good margins of LQR?
**中文题意**：什么是 LQG？它继承 LQR 的好裕度吗？
**考察点**：理论局限的认知。
**项目关联**：概念题；P7 的估计+控制。
**English answer（口语）**：
> LQG combines an LQR controller with a Kalman filter. The Kalman filter estimates the states from noisy measurements, and the LQR acts on the estimate. By the separation principle, I can design the two independently. The surprising result, shown by Doyle in 1978, is that LQG has no guaranteed robustness margins: the nice margins of LQR can disappear once the estimator is in the loop. To recover robustness I can use loop transfer recovery, which tunes the estimator to approach the LQR loop, or design with robust methods. In practice I test the margins of the whole loop, including delay, instead of trusting the theory of its parts.

**中文翻译**：LQG 把 LQR 控制器和卡尔曼滤波器结合：卡尔曼滤波器从带噪测量估计状态，LQR 作用在估计值上。由分离原理，两者可独立设计。令人意外的结果（Doyle 1978）是 LQG 没有保证的鲁棒裕度：估计器进入回路后，LQR 的良好裕度可能消失。恢复鲁棒性可用回路传递恢复，让估计器趋近 LQR 回路，或使用鲁棒方法设计。实践中我测试整个回路（含延迟）的裕度，而不是信任各部分的理论。
**追问 1**：*What is the famous paper?* Doyle's 1978 note titled "Guaranteed margins for LQG regulators", whose answer is that there are none.
　中文：Doyle 1978 年的短文，题为"LQG 调节器的保证裕度"，结论是没有保证。
**追问 2**：*What is the practical test?* Break the loop at the plant input and measure or compute the gain and phase margins.
　中文：在对象输入处断开回路，测量或计算增益和相位裕度。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In my robots the estimator is a simple filter feeding the controller, and I check the closed loop by experiment on the robot rather than by theory alone.*
　中文：我的机器人里估计器是馈入控制器的简单滤波器，我靠真机实验而不只是理论来检查闭环。
**关键词与易错点**：LQG、Kalman filter、separation principle、loop transfer recovery。

### Q27. How robust is a state-feedback controller to model errors, and how do you test it?
**中文题意**：状态反馈对模型误差有多鲁棒？怎么测试？
**考察点**：鲁棒性的工程检验。
**项目关联**：P1（质量、质心不准）、P7。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A state-feedback controller is only as good as the model near the operating point. Model errors in mass, centre of mass, friction and actuator lag change the effective A and B. To test robustness, I do three things. In simulation, I perturb the parameters by plus or minus twenty percent or more and check that the closed-loop poles stay stable. On the robot, I do a disturbance test, a gentle push, and watch recovery and saturation. And I look at the sensitivity to delay by adding an artificial delay in the loop. If the system is too sensitive, I lower the gains, add damping, or use integral action and feedforward to take the load off the feedback.

**中文翻译**：状态反馈控制器的表现取决于工作点附近的模型。质量、质心、摩擦和执行器滞后的误差会改变有效的 A 和 B。测试鲁棒性我做三件事：仿真中把参数扰动正负 20% 或更多，检查闭环极点仍稳定；真机上做扰动测试（轻推），观察恢复与饱和；并通过在回路里加人为延迟来观察对延迟的敏感性。如果系统太敏感，我就降低增益、增加阻尼，或用积分和前馈减轻反馈负担。
**追问 1**：*What is structured versus unstructured uncertainty?* Structured: known parameters vary within ranges. Unstructured: unmodelled dynamics, usually at high frequency.
　中文：结构化：已知参数在范围内变化。非结构化：未建模动态，通常在高频。
**追问 2**：*What is mu-analysis?* A tool from robust control that checks stability against structured uncertainty, beyond what I used.
　中文：鲁棒控制工具，检验对结构化不确定性的稳定性；超出我用过的范围。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 I checked robustness by physical push tests of about twenty to thirty newtons, from which the robot recovered with a peak tilt of about 0.15 radian.*
　中文：P1 里我用约 20–30 N 的物理推扰测试鲁棒性，机器人恢复，倾角峰值约 0.15 rad。
**关键词与易错点**：robustness、parameter perturbation、delay margin、push test。

### Q28. How do you interpret the gain vector K physically?
**中文题意**：如何从物理上解释增益向量 K？
**考察点**：理解而非背诵。
**项目关联**：P1、P7。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Each entry of K is a gain from one state to the control. In the inverted pendulum, the tilt gain says how hard to push the base per radian of lean; the tilt-rate gain acts as damping on the lean; the velocity and position gains slowly pull the base back to a desired speed and place. In a typical solution the tilt gain is the biggest, then the tilt rate, and the position gain is the smallest, which gives a fast inner stabilisation and a slow outer correction. That structure looks like a cascade: the position and velocity terms produce a small tilt demand, and the tilt terms balance. So LQR and the cascaded PID are actually saying the same thing in different forms.

**中文翻译**：K 的每个元素是从某个状态到控制量的增益。在倒立摆里，倾角增益说明每弧度倾斜要多用力推底座；倾角速率增益起到对倾斜的阻尼作用；速度和位置增益缓慢地把底座拉回期望的速度和位置。典型解中倾角增益最大，其次是倾角速率，位置增益最小，因此内部稳定快、外部修正慢。这种结构看起来像级联：位置和速度项产生一个小的倾角需求，倾角项负责平衡。所以 LQR 和级联 PID 其实是用不同形式说同一件事。
**追问 1**：*What does a sign flip in K mean?* A positive-feedback error: the controller pushes the wrong way, usually caused by a wrong sign convention of a state.
　中文：正反馈错误：控制器推错了方向，通常由状态符号约定错误造成。
**追问 2**：*How can the structure help debugging?* If the base drifts slowly, look at the position and velocity terms; if it oscillates quickly, look at the tilt-rate term.
　中文：底座缓慢漂移看位置和速度项；快速振荡看倾角速率项。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 the LQR gains had the largest magnitude on tilt, about 46, and this matched my cascaded PID, where the inner loop has the largest gains and the outer loop only requests a small, bounded tilt.*
　中文：P1 的 LQR 增益在倾角上量级最大，约 46，与我的级联 PID 一致：内环增益最大，外环只请求小而有界的倾角。
**关键词与易错点**：gain vector、tilt gain、damping、cascade equivalence。

## 4. C 模型预测控制 MPC（Q29–Q38）

### Q29. What is MPC, and why is it useful?
**中文题意**：什么是 MPC？为什么有用？
**考察点**：MPC 的概念与优势。
**项目关联**：P5（核心）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Model predictive control repeatedly solves an optimisation problem over a finite horizon. At each step, I use a model to predict the future states for a sequence of inputs, minimise a cost on tracking error and effort, subject to constraints, apply the first input, and then repeat with the new measurement. The key advantages are that it handles constraints explicitly, like steering limits and speed limits; it handles multi-input multi-output systems; and it uses preview of the reference, so it can anticipate a turn. The costs are computation time and the need for a decent model. For a car-like robot following a path, those features fit very well.

**中文翻译**：模型预测控制在有限时域上反复求解优化问题。每一步，我用模型预测一串输入下的未来状态，在约束下最小化跟踪误差与控制量的代价，执行第一个输入，然后用新测量重复。主要优势：显式处理约束，如转向与速度限制；处理多输入多输出系统；利用参考轨迹的预瞻信息，能预判转弯。代价是计算时间和需要一个像样的模型。对跟随路径的车式机器人，这些特性非常契合。
**追问 1**：*What is a receding horizon?* The prediction window moves forward by one step at each cycle.
　中文：预测窗口每个周期向前移动一步。
**追问 2**：*Why not apply the entire optimal sequence?* Because of model error and disturbances, feedback from re-solving is essential.
　中文：由于模型误差和扰动，重新求解带来的反馈至关重要。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 I used MPC to track the TEB trajectory with a car-like kinematic model and constraints on position, heading, speed and steering, closing the loop on the real vehicle.*
　中文：P5 里我用车式运动学模型和位置、航向、速度、转向约束的 MPC 跟踪 TEB 轨迹，并在真车上闭环。
**关键词与易错点**：receding horizon、constraints、preview、first input。

### Q30. MPC versus LQR: what is the difference?
**中文题意**：MPC 与 LQR 有何区别？
**考察点**：选型。
**项目关联**：P5 vs P7。
**English answer（口语）**：
> LQR is the infinite-horizon unconstrained special case. It gives a fixed gain matrix computed offline, so it is extremely cheap online. MPC solves a finite-horizon problem online, so it can include constraints and time-varying references, but it costs computation. Without constraints, and with a terminal cost equal to the Riccati solution, MPC reproduces the LQR control law. So I use LQR when the dynamics are fast, the constraints are not binding and the computing budget is tight, like balance control at 500 hertz. I use MPC when constraints matter and there is time to solve, like path tracking at 20 hertz.

**中文翻译**：LQR 是无限时域、无约束的特例，增益矩阵在线下算好，在线计算极其便宜。MPC 在线求解有限时域问题，可以包含约束和时变参考，但需要计算。无约束且终端代价取 Riccati 解时，MPC 复现 LQR 控制律。所以动力学快、约束不起作用、算力紧张时用 LQR，如 500 Hz 的平衡控制；约束重要且有时间求解时用 MPC，如 20 Hz 的路径跟踪。
**追问 1**：*Can MPC run at 500 hertz?* For small problems with a fast solver it is possible, but the computation time, jitter and worst case must be bounded; a fixed gain is simpler and safer.
　中文：小问题配快速求解器可行，但必须限定计算时间、抖动和最坏情形；固定增益更简单也更安全。
**追问 2**：*What is explicit MPC?* The solution is precomputed as a piecewise affine function of the state, trading memory for online time.
　中文：把解预先计算成状态的分段仿射函数，用内存换在线时间。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*My two choices were consistent with this: gain-scheduled LQR for P7's fast balance, MPC for P5's slower tracking with steering constraints.*
　中文：我的两个选择与此一致：P7 快速平衡用增益调度 LQR，P5 带转向约束的较慢跟踪用 MPC。
**关键词与易错点**：infinite horizon、finite horizon、constraints、computation。

### Q31. How do you choose the MPC horizon and time step?
**中文题意**：MPC 的预测时域和步长怎么选？
**考察点**：设计取舍。
**项目关联**：P5（N = 15，0.1 s）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The horizon should be long enough to see the consequences of my decisions, and short enough to solve in time. A rule of thumb is to cover the slowest important dynamics, like the time the car needs to steer into a curve, and the braking time at the maximum speed. The time step should be at most a fraction of the fastest dynamics I care about, and at least the control period. In my vehicle MPC, I used fifteen steps of 0.1 second, so a horizon of 1.5 seconds. At about one metre per second that looks ahead 1.5 metres, enough for the curvature of the TEB trajectory. Longer horizons increase the cost with little benefit if the model is inaccurate.

**中文翻译**：时域要足够长，能看到决策的后果；又要足够短，能及时求解。经验法则是覆盖最慢的重要动态，如车转入弯道所需时间，以及最高速度下的制动时间。步长至多是我关心的最快动态的一部分，至少是控制周期。我的车辆 MPC 用 15 步、每步 0.1 秒，时域 1.5 秒。以约 1 m/s 的速度，向前看 1.5 米，足以覆盖 TEB 轨迹的曲率。时域更长会增加计算量，而模型不准时收益很小。
**追问 1**：*What happens with too short a horizon?* The controller is short-sighted and may oscillate or become unstable, or cut corners.
　中文：控制器短视，可能振荡、失稳或切弯。
**追问 2**：*Can you use a non-uniform time step?* Yes, finer steps early and coarser later, but the model discretisation must match.
　中文：可以，早期细、后期粗，但模型离散化必须匹配。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*The solve time in P5 was about two to five milliseconds at N equal to fifteen, well inside the 50 millisecond period of the 20 hertz loop.*
　中文：P5 里 N 等于 15 时求解约 2–5 ms，远小于 20 Hz 的 50 ms 周期。
**关键词与易错点**：horizon、time step、look-ahead、solve time。

### Q32. How is stability of MPC guaranteed?
**中文题意**：如何保证 MPC 的稳定性？
**考察点**：理论知识。
**项目关联**：概念题。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A finite-horizon MPC is not automatically stable. The classical way to get a guarantee is to add a terminal cost and a terminal constraint. The terminal cost approximates the cost-to-go beyond the horizon, often the LQR Riccati solution, and the terminal set is a region where a local stabilising controller is feasible and keeps the state inside. Then the optimal cost works as a Lyapunov function. Another approach is a long enough horizon, or an infinite-horizon-equivalent cost. In practice, with a tracking task on a car, I rely on a reasonable horizon, a terminal weight, and extensive testing, and I accept that the guarantee is not formal.

**中文翻译**：有限时域 MPC 并非自动稳定。获得保证的经典做法是加入终端代价和终端约束。终端代价近似时域之外的代价，常取 LQR 的 Riccati 解；终端集是局部稳定控制器可行并能把状态保持在内部的区域。这样最优代价就可作为李雅普诺夫函数。另一种做法是足够长的时域或等价于无限时域的代价。实际中，对车的跟踪任务，我依靠合理的时域、终端权重和大量测试，并承认这并非形式化保证。
**追问 1**：*What is recursive feasibility?* If the problem is feasible now, it stays feasible at the next step; it is needed for stability proofs.
　中文：现在可行则下一步仍可行；这是稳定性证明所需。
**追问 2**：*What did you use as a terminal weight?* Twice the state weight Q.
　中文：状态权重 Q 的两倍。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 the terminal weight was two times Q, a practical choice rather than the Riccati solution, so I describe the stability as tested, not proven.*
　中文：P5 里终端权重取 2Q，是实用选择而不是 Riccati 解，所以我把稳定性说成"测试过"而不是"证明过"。
**关键词与易错点**：terminal cost、terminal set、recursive feasibility、Lyapunov function。

### Q33. How do you make MPC run in real time on a robot?
**中文题意**：如何让 MPC 在机器人上实时运行？
**考察点**：工程实现。
**项目关联**：P5（OSQP、热启动）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I keep the problem small and convex, with a short horizon and a linearised model, and I use a solver designed for embedded or real-time use, like OSQP. I warm-start from the shifted previous solution, which usually saves most iterations. I pre-compute the matrices that do not change and update only the parts that depend on the current state and reference. I cap the iteration count and the time per solve, and I monitor the solve time and the failure rate. If a solve fails, I have a fallback: reuse the shifted old solution for a few cycles, then decelerate to a stop. The key is to bound the worst case, not just the average.

**中文翻译**：我让问题保持小而凸，用短时域和线性化模型，并使用面向嵌入式或实时的求解器，如 OSQP。我用平移后的上一次解热启动，通常能省下大部分迭代。我预先计算不变的矩阵，只更新依赖当前状态和参考的部分。我限制迭代次数和每次求解时间，并监控求解时间与失败率。若求解失败，我有后备：把平移的旧解复用几个周期，然后减速停车。关键是限定最坏情形，而不只是平均。
**追问 1**：*What is your latency budget?* The whole cycle should be well below the period; I used an alarm at 10 milliseconds and treat 30 milliseconds as a failure.
　中文：整个周期应远小于控制周期；我在 10 ms 处告警，30 ms 视为失败。
**追问 2**：*Why not just use the old solution forever?* The open-loop sequence drifts from reality, so after a few cycles it must stop.
　中文：开环序列会偏离实际，几个周期后必须停车。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 the solve was about two to five milliseconds, and after a failure I reused the shifted solution for three cycles before slowing down.*
　中文：P5 里求解约 2–5 ms，失败后复用平移解三个周期，然后减速。
**关键词与易错点**：warm start、OSQP、worst case、fallback。

### Q34. How do you handle delay in an MPC controller?
**中文题意**：MPC 里如何处理延迟？
**考察点**：延迟补偿的具体做法。
**项目关联**：P5（120 ms 延迟补偿）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> If the total delay between the command and its effect is, say, 120 milliseconds, the state I measure now is the result of commands applied before that. If I ignore it, the MPC plans from the wrong initial state and will oscillate. The standard fix is to predict the current state forward over the delay, using the model and the commands that have been sent but have not yet taken effect, and use that predicted state as the initial condition of the optimisation. Alternatively, I augment the model with delay states. This needs a decent model, so I also keep the weights on the input rate large enough to damp the effect of any prediction error.

**中文翻译**：如果指令到生效的总延迟是 120 毫秒，我现在测到的状态是此前施加的指令的结果。忽略它，MPC 会从错误的初始状态开始规划并振荡。标准做法是用模型和已经发出但尚未生效的指令把当前状态前推一个延迟时间，用预测的状态作为优化的初始条件。另一种做法是用延迟状态扩增模型。这需要一个像样的模型，所以我也让输入变化率的权重足够大，以抑制预测误差的影响。
**追问 1**：*Where did 120 ms come from?* About 20 milliseconds from the serial link and the low-level board, and about 100 milliseconds from the steering servo and motor response.
　中文：串口与下位机约 20 ms，舵机和电机响应约 100 ms。
**追问 2**：*How did you verify it?* By comparing the predicted and measured vehicle states, and by seeing the steering jitter reduce.
　中文：比较预测与测量的车辆状态，并观察转向抖动减少。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5, the steering jitter came from the TEB reference jumping together with a 120 millisecond delay; delay compensation, a larger rate weight and selection hysteresis fixed it.*
　中文：P5 里转向抖动由 TEB 参考跳变加 120 ms 延迟引起；延迟补偿、更大的变化率权重和选择滞回解决了它。
**关键词与易错点**：delay compensation、state prediction、augmented model、rate weight。

### Q35. How do you handle infeasibility and soft constraints in MPC?
**中文题意**：MPC 中如何处理不可行与软约束？
**考察点**：工程鲁棒性。
**项目关联**：P5。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A QP can become infeasible when the state is already outside a constraint, for example when a disturbance pushes the car past a boundary, or the constraints contradict each other. To avoid a solver failure, I make state constraints soft by adding a slack variable with a large penalty, so they can be violated slightly when necessary. Hard constraints I keep only for things that must never be violated, such as the actuator limits on steering angle and acceleration. I also detect failure and fall back: reuse the shifted solution, then brake. In logging, I record the slack values, because they show where the controller is under pressure.

**中文翻译**：当状态已经在某约束之外时（如扰动把车推过边界），或约束相互矛盾时，QP 可能不可行。为避免求解失败，我用带大惩罚的松弛变量把状态约束做成软约束，必要时可略微违反。只对绝不能违反的量保留硬约束，如转向角与加速度的执行器限制。我还检测失败并启用后备：复用平移后的解，然后制动。日志里我记录松弛量，因为它显示控制器在哪里承压。
**追问 1**：*Why not make all constraints soft?* Actuator limits are physical; violating them has no meaning because the hardware cannot do it.
　中文：执行器限制是物理的，违反没有意义，因为硬件做不到。
**追问 2**：*How large is the penalty?* Large enough to dominate the cost, but not so large that it badly conditions the QP.
　中文：大到压过其他代价，但不能大到使 QP 病态。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P5 the steering angle (about 0.5 radian), its rate (about 0.6 radian per second), speed and acceleration were hard actuator limits.*
　中文：P5 里转向角（约 0.5 rad）、转向速率（约 0.6 rad/s）、速度和加速度是硬的执行器限制。
**关键词与易错点**：infeasible、slack variable、soft constraint、fallback。

### Q36. What is nonlinear MPC, and how does it differ from linear time-varying MPC?
**中文题意**：什么是非线性 MPC？与线性时变 MPC 有何区别？
**考察点**：MPC 的变种。
**项目关联**：P5（线性化的运动学模型）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Nonlinear MPC uses the nonlinear model directly, so the optimisation is a nonlinear program, solved for example by sequential quadratic programming. It is more accurate over large manoeuvres, but it is heavier and may stop in local minima. Linear time-varying MPC linearises the nonlinear model along the reference trajectory at each step, so each solve is a convex QP. It is fast and reliable, and accurate if the vehicle stays near the reference, which is typical for path tracking. In my project, a car-like kinematic model linearised around the TEB trajectory worked well at speeds up to about 0.8 metres per second. For aggressive manoeuvres, I would consider nonlinear MPC.

**中文翻译**：非线性 MPC 直接使用非线性模型，优化是非线性规划，如用序列二次规划求解。大机动下更准确，但更重，可能停在局部极小。线性时变 MPC 在每一步沿参考轨迹线性化非线性模型，使每次求解是凸 QP，快速可靠，车辆保持在参考附近时足够准确，这正是路径跟踪的典型情形。在我的项目里，围绕 TEB 轨迹线性化的车式运动学模型在最高约 0.8 m/s 的速度下表现良好。对激烈机动，我会考虑非线性 MPC。
**追问 1**：*What is a kinematic versus dynamic model for a car?* Kinematic assumes no slip, valid at low speed; dynamic includes tyre forces for high speed.
　中文：运动学模型假设无滑移，低速有效；动力学模型包含轮胎力，适用于高速。
**追问 2**：*What is SQP?* It repeatedly solves a QP approximation of the nonlinear problem.
　中文：反复求解非线性问题的 QP 近似。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*At my speeds, up to 0.8 metres per second, the no-slip kinematic model is reasonable, which is why I did not need a dynamic or nonlinear MPC.*
　中文：在我的速度（最高 0.8 m/s）下无滑移运动学模型合理，所以不需要动力学或非线性 MPC。
**关键词与易错点**：nonlinear MPC、linear time-varying、SQP、kinematic model。

### Q37. Describe the kinematic model used in your car-like MPC.
**中文题意**：描述你车式 MPC 使用的运动学模型。
**考察点**：车辆模型。
**项目关联**：P5（核心）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I use the kinematic bicycle model with the reference point at the rear axle. The states are x, y, heading psi and speed v. The inputs are acceleration a and steering angle delta. The equations are: x dot equals v cosine psi, y dot equals v sine psi, psi dot equals v over L times tangent of delta, and v dot equals a. L is the wheelbase, 0.40 metres for our car. I discretise it with 0.1 second steps and linearise it around the reference to get a QP at each cycle.

　中文：后轴沿简单路径运动（无侧滑），简化方程。
**追问 2**：*What is the limit of this model?* It ignores tyre slip and dynamics, so it is accurate at low speed only.
　中文：忽略轮胎滑移和动力学，仅在低速准确。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In planning I used a turning radius of 0.8 metres instead of the 0.69 metre geometric minimum, to leave a margin.*
　中文：规划中我取转弯半径 0.8 米，而不是 0.69 米的几何下限，留出余量。

### Q38. How do you tune MPC weights, and why does the input-rate weight matter?
**中文题意**：MPC 权重怎么调？为什么输入变化率权重重要？
**考察点**：整定经验。
**项目关联**：P5（Q, R, Rd）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I treat the weights as a hierarchy. Q penalises tracking error in position, heading and speed, and sets how tightly the vehicle follows the path. R penalises the size of the inputs and keeps the commands moderate. Rd penalises the change of the inputs between steps, and it controls how smooth and how fast the commands can vary. I tune Q first for tracking, then increase Rd on the steering until the commands are smooth without too much lag. In my vehicle, I used diag of ten, ten, five and one for Q, diag of 0.5 and 1 for R, and diag of 0.1 and 5 for Rd, so the steering rate was penalised much more than the acceleration rate.

**中文翻译**：我把权重当作层级。Q 惩罚位置、航向、速度的跟踪误差，决定车沿路径跟得多紧。R 惩罚输入的大小，使指令适中。Rd 惩罚相邻步之间输入的变化，控制指令变化的平滑度和速度。我先调 Q 保证跟踪，再增大转向的 Rd，直到指令平滑且滞后不大。我的车里 Q 取 diag(10, 10, 5, 1)，R 取 diag(0.5, 1)，Rd 取 diag(0.1, 5)，所以转向变化率的惩罚比加速度变化率大得多。
**追问 1**：*What happens with Rd too small?* The steering chatters and excites the servo's dynamics and delay.
　中文：转向抖动，激发舵机动态和延迟。
**追问 2**：*And too large?* The vehicle responds sluggishly and cuts corners.
　中文：响应迟钝、切弯。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*The steering jitter in P5 was reduced partly by raising Rd for steering to 5, in combination with delay compensation.*
　中文：P5 的转向抖动部分通过把转向 Rd 提到 5 来降低，并配合延迟补偿。
**关键词与易错点**：Q R Rd、input-rate weight、smoothness、tracking。

## 5. D 滑模、李雅普诺夫、鲁棒与自适应（Q39–Q50）

### Q39. Explain sliding-mode control and how you used it.
**中文题意**：解释滑模控制，以及你怎么用它。
**考察点**：理解原理，并能说出项目参数与迭代动机。
**项目关联**：P3（发射机构摩擦轮速度环，核心）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Sliding-mode control defines a sliding surface s, a combination of the error and its derivative. In my case s equals e dot plus c times e, with c equal to 5. The control has two parts: an equivalent part that keeps the state on the surface, and a switching part, rho times a sign-like function of s, that pushes the state toward the surface despite model uncertainty. Once on the surface, the error decays like e to the minus c t, independent of the disturbance, as long as rho is larger than the disturbance bound. In P3 the friction-wheel speed dropped after each shot, and the sliding-mode version recovered faster than PID, from about 120 to about 60 or 80 milliseconds, with less speed scatter.

**中文翻译**：滑模控制定义一个滑模面 s，是误差及其导数的组合。我的例子中 s 等于 e 导数加 c 乘 e，c 取 5。控制有两部分：保持状态在面上的等效部分，以及切换部分 rho 乘 s 的类符号函数，在模型不确定下把状态推向滑模面。一旦到达滑模面，只要 rho 大于扰动上界，误差就按 e 的负 c t 次方衰减，与扰动无关。P3 里每次发射后摩擦轮转速会下降，滑模版本比 PID 恢复更快，从约 120 ms 缩短到约 60–80 ms，弹速离散也更小。
**追问 1**：*What are the parameters in your implementation?* c equal to 5, rho equal to 2, a boundary layer epsilon of 1, and an output limit of 10000.
　中文：c 为 5，rho 为 2，边界层 epsilon 为 1，输出限幅 10000。
**追问 2**：*What is the reaching condition?* s times s dot is less than zero, so the distance to the surface decreases.
　中文：s 乘 s 的导数小于零，到滑模面的距离减小。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*It was a progression in P3: PID, then variable-gain PID, then sliding mode; they are iterations in time, not controllers running together. Muzzle velocity scatter went from about plus or minus 1 to about plus or minus 0.3 metres per second.*
　中文：P3 里是一个迭代过程：PID、变增益 PID、滑模；它们是时间上的迭代，不是同时运行。弹速离散从约 ±1 m/s 降到约 ±0.3 m/s。
**关键词与易错点**：sliding surface、equivalent control、switching term、reaching condition。不要把三个控制器说成同时运行。

### Q40. What is chattering, and how do you reduce it?
**中文题意**：什么是抖振？如何减轻？
**考察点**：滑模的主要缺点与对策。
**项目关联**：P3（边界层 epsilon = 1）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Chattering is a high-frequency oscillation of the control signal caused by the discontinuous switching term. In real systems with finite sampling and actuator dynamics, the state cannot slide exactly on the surface, so it bounces across it. That excites unmodelled dynamics, wears actuators and makes noise. The common cure is a boundary layer: replace the sign function by a saturation function or a smooth function, linear inside a thin layer of width epsilon around the surface and saturated outside. The price is a small steady error inside the layer. Other options are higher-order sliding modes, or reducing rho to the smallest value that still dominates the disturbance. I used a boundary layer.

**中文翻译**：抖振是由不连续切换项引起的控制信号高频振荡。真实系统有有限采样和执行器动态，状态无法精确地沿滑模面滑动，而是在面两侧来回跳。这会激发未建模动态、磨损执行器并产生噪声。常用的对策是边界层：用饱和函数或光滑函数代替符号函数，在滑模面附近宽度为 epsilon 的薄层内线性，层外饱和。代价是层内有小的稳态误差。其他选择有高阶滑模，或把 rho 降到仍能压过扰动的最小值。我使用的是边界层。
**追问 1**：*How do you choose epsilon?* Large enough to remove visible chatter at the sampling rate, small enough that the steady error is acceptable.
　中文：大到在该采样率下看不到抖振，小到稳态误差可接受。
**追问 2**：*Is the sampling period a factor?* Yes, a slow loop makes the discrete switching overshoot the surface more.
　中文：是，采样慢时离散切换更容易越过滑模面。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P3 the control period was about 3 milliseconds and I used a boundary layer of 1 in the error units, with the output limited to 10000.*
　中文：P3 的控制周期约 3 ms，我在误差单位下用 1 的边界层，输出限幅 10000。
**关键词与易错点**：chattering、boundary layer、saturation function、higher-order sliding mode。

### Q41. Why is sliding-mode control robust, and what are its limits?
**中文题意**：滑模为什么鲁棒？局限是什么？
**考察点**：匹配不确定性。
**项目关联**：P3。
**English answer（口语）**：
> In sliding mode, once the state is on the surface, its motion depends only on the surface design, here the constant c, and not on the plant parameters or on a matched disturbance. The disturbance only needs to be bounded and enter through the same channel as the control. That is the robustness. The limits are: the disturbance bound must be known to set the switching gain; unmatched disturbances are not rejected; the discontinuous action causes chattering; and a larger switching gain demands more actuator authority, which can saturate. Also, the performance in the boundary layer is only approximate. So it fits well with disturbances of uncertain size on a fast actuator, like the ball loading on a friction wheel.

**中文翻译**：滑模中，一旦状态到达滑模面，其运动只取决于滑模面的设计（这里是常数 c），而与对象参数和匹配扰动无关。扰动只需有界，并与控制从同一通道进入。这就是鲁棒性。局限：必须知道扰动界才能设定切换增益；不匹配扰动不被抑制；不连续作用引起抖振；更大的切换增益需要更大的执行器能力，可能饱和；边界层内的性能也只是近似。所以它很适合快速执行器上大小不确定的扰动，比如弹丸对摩擦轮的负载。
**追问 1**：*What is a matched disturbance?* One that enters through the same channel as the input, such as a load torque on a motor.
　中文：与输入同通道进入的扰动，如电机上的负载力矩。
**追问 2**：*What alternative to sliding mode for disturbance rejection?* A disturbance observer or extended state observer with feedforward cancellation.
　中文：扰动观测器或扩张状态观测器加前馈抵消。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*The load from the projectile on the friction wheel is a torque on the same shaft as the motor input, which is why a sliding-mode design fit that loop well.*
　中文：弹丸对摩擦轮的负载是与电机输入同轴的力矩，所以滑模设计很适合这个环。
**关键词与易错点**：matched disturbance、switching gain、robustness、bounded disturbance。

### Q42. Explain Lyapunov stability in simple terms.
**中文题意**：用简单的话解释李雅普诺夫稳定性。
**考察点**：非线性稳定性分析的核心思想。
**项目关联**：概念题；P2（PD+重力补偿）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The idea is to find an energy-like function V of the state that is positive everywhere except at the equilibrium, where it is zero, and that never increases along the system trajectories. If the derivative of V along the trajectories is negative semi-definite, the equilibrium is stable. If it is negative definite, the state converges to the equilibrium, so it is asymptotically stable. The beauty is that I do not need to solve the differential equation. For physical systems, the total energy is a natural candidate. When the derivative is only negative semi-definite, LaSalle's invariance principle helps to conclude convergence.

**中文翻译**：思路是找一个类似能量的状态函数 V，除平衡点处为零外处处为正，并且沿系统轨迹永不增加。若沿轨迹 V 的导数半负定，则平衡点稳定；若负定，状态收敛到平衡点，即渐近稳定。妙处在于不需要解微分方程。对物理系统，总能量是自然的候选。当导数只是半负定时，LaSalle 不变集原理可帮助推出收敛。
**追问 1**：*Is a failed Lyapunov function a proof of instability?* No; it only means I have not found a function.
　中文：不是；只表示我没找到合适的函数。
**追问 2**：*What is the Lyapunov equation for linear systems?* A transpose P plus P A equals minus Q, with P positive definite if and only if A is stable.
　中文：A 转置 P 加 P A 等于负 Q，当且仅当 A 稳定时存在正定 P。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*I use Lyapunov thinking as a way to reason about designs, such as why PD plus gravity compensation is stable for an arm; I did not do a formal proof for my deployed controllers.*
　中文：我把李雅普诺夫当作分析设计的思路，比如为什么 PD 加重力补偿对机械臂稳定；已部署的控制器我没有做形式化证明。
**关键词与易错点**：Lyapunov function、negative definite、LaSalle、energy。

### Q43. Why is PD control with gravity compensation stable for a robot arm?
**中文题意**：为什么带重力补偿的 PD 控制对机械臂是稳定的？
**考察点**：经典证明思路。
**项目关联**：P2（从臂、主臂的 PD 与重力补偿）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The arm dynamics are M of q q double dot plus C q dot plus g of q equals tau. With the control tau equals g of q minus Kp q minus Kd q dot, taking the target as the origin, gravity is cancelled exactly. I take V as the kinetic energy plus one half q transpose Kp q. Its derivative along trajectories is minus q dot transpose Kd q dot, because the Coriolis term does no work, as M dot minus 2 C is skew-symmetric. So V decreases whenever the speed is non-zero, and by LaSalle the only invariant set is q dot equal to zero and then q equal to zero. This is global asymptotic stability, and it needs only a good gravity model, not the full dynamics.

**中文翻译**：机械臂动力学为 M(q) q 二阶导加 C q 导数加 g(q) 等于 tau。取目标为原点，控制 tau 等于 g(q) 减 Kp q 减 Kd q 导数，重力被精确抵消。取 V 为动能加二分之一 q 转置 Kp q。沿轨迹的导数是负的 q 导数转置 Kd q 导数，因为科氏项不做功（M 的导数减 2C 是反对称的）。所以速度非零时 V 下降，由 LaSalle，唯一不变集是 q 导数为零，进而 q 为零。这是全局渐近稳定，且只需要好的重力模型，而不需要完整动力学。
**追问 1**：*What if the gravity model is wrong?* There will be a steady-state error that depends on the mismatch divided by Kp; adding integral action or a larger Kp reduces it.
　中文：会有稳态误差，大小约为失配量除以 Kp；加积分或增大 Kp 可减小。
**追问 2**：*Why not use full computed torque?* It needs accurate inertia and Coriolis models, and is more sensitive to errors; PD plus gravity is simple and robust.
　中文：需要精确的惯量和科氏模型，对误差更敏感；PD 加重力补偿简单且鲁棒。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P2 the follower uses position and velocity tracking with PD-like gains, and the leader arm uses gravity compensation with a ratio of 1 on six joints and 0.8 on the seventh, so it feels light but does not drift.*
　中文：P2 里从臂用类 PD 增益做位置速度跟踪，主臂做重力补偿，前六个关节比例为 1、第七关节为 0.8，手感轻但不漂移。
**关键词与易错点**：gravity compensation、skew-symmetric、LaSalle、Lyapunov function。

### Q44. What is computed torque control, and what are its weaknesses?
**中文题意**：什么是计算力矩控制？它的弱点是什么？
**考察点**：反馈线性化。
**项目关联**：P2（概念对比）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Computed torque cancels the nonlinear dynamics. I compute tau equals M of q times a desired acceleration plus C q dot plus g of q, where the desired acceleration is q double dot desired plus Kd times velocity error plus Kp times position error. If the model is exact, the closed loop becomes a simple linear error equation with a chosen decay. The weakness is model dependence: any error in M, C or g leaves a residual nonlinear coupling, and the computation needs the full dynamics online, which depends on accurate inertia parameters and also on friction. So in practice I prefer PD plus gravity compensation, or add robust or adaptive terms, unless the model is identified well.

**中文翻译**：计算力矩抵消非线性动力学。我计算 tau 等于 M(q) 乘期望加速度加 C q 导数加 g(q)，其中期望加速度等于期望 q 二阶导加 Kd 乘速度误差加 Kp 乘位置误差。若模型精确，闭环成为选定衰减特性的简单线性误差方程。弱点是依赖模型：M、C 或 g 的任何误差都会留下残余非线性耦合，并且需要在线计算完整动力学，依赖准确的惯量参数，也受摩擦影响。所以除非模型辨识得很好，实践中我更偏好 PD 加重力补偿，或加入鲁棒或自适应项。
**追问 1**：*Is it the same as feedback linearisation?* Computed torque is feedback linearisation applied to a robot arm.
　中文：计算力矩就是对机械臂应用的反馈线性化。
**追问 2**：*How do you make it more robust?* Add a robust term, a sliding-mode term, or adaptation of the parameters.
　中文：加鲁棒项、滑模项或参数自适应。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*I used URDF and KDL for gravity terms in P2, not for a full computed-torque law.*
　中文：P2 里我用 URDF 和 KDL 计算重力项，而不是完整的计算力矩律。
**关键词与易错点**：computed torque、feedback linearisation、model dependence、residual。

### Q45. What is a disturbance observer or an extended state observer?
**中文题意**：什么是扰动观测器或扩张状态观测器？
**考察点**：鲁棒控制的工程手段。
**项目关联**：P3（与滑模对比）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The idea is to treat the unknown disturbance and the model error as an extra state, estimate it, and cancel it in the control. A disturbance observer uses the nominal model of the plant, compares the measured output with the model output, and estimates the lumped disturbance. An extended state observer adds the total disturbance as an extended state to the plant and estimates it together with the states. Then the control is the nominal control minus the estimated disturbance, so the plant looks like the nominal one. The bandwidth of the observer sets a trade-off: faster estimation rejects disturbances more quickly but amplifies noise. It is the core of active disturbance rejection control, a popular alternative to sliding mode with less chattering.

**中文翻译**：思路是把未知扰动和模型误差当作额外的状态，估计它，并在控制中抵消。扰动观测器使用对象的名义模型，比较测量输出与模型输出，估计集总扰动。扩张状态观测器把总扰动作为扩张状态加到对象里，与其他状态一起估计。然后控制量等于名义控制减去估计扰动，使对象看起来像名义对象。观测器带宽决定权衡：估计更快则抑制扰动更快，但放大噪声。这是自抗扰控制的核心，是滑模的一个流行替代，抖振更少。
**追问 1**：*Compare it with integral action.* Integral action is a slow disturbance estimator in disguise; an observer can be faster and structured.
　中文：积分作用相当于一个慢的扰动估计器；观测器可以更快且更有结构。
**追问 2**：*What limits the observer bandwidth?* Sensor noise, delay and sampling rate.
　中文：传感器噪声、延迟和采样率。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*I did not use an ESO on the hardware; for the friction wheel I chose sliding mode, and I know the observer-based alternative as a way to get a similar disturbance rejection.*
　中文：我没有在硬件上用 ESO；对摩擦轮我选了滑模，并了解基于观测器的方案可以获得类似的抗扰效果。
**关键词与易错点**：disturbance observer、extended state observer、lumped disturbance、ADRC。诚实说明未使用。

### Q46. What is adaptive control, and when is it needed?
**中文题意**：什么是自适应控制？何时需要？
**考察点**：概念理解。
**项目关联**：概念题；P1（质心变化）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Adaptive control adjusts the controller online as the plant changes or is uncertain. In model reference adaptive control, I define a reference model with the desired response. The adaptation law changes the controller parameters to make the plant output follow the reference model output, usually derived from a Lyapunov function so the tracking error converges. It is needed when the parameters change a lot or are unknown, such as a payload mass that varies. Its risks are parameter drift with noise, the need for persistent excitation, and the difficulty of tuning and proving robustness in practice. When the changes are slow and measurable, gain scheduling or feedforward is often simpler and safer.

**中文翻译**：自适应控制在对象变化或不确定时在线调整控制器。模型参考自适应控制中，我定义一个具有期望响应的参考模型，自适应律改变控制器参数，使对象输出跟随参考模型输出，通常由李雅普诺夫函数推导，使跟踪误差收敛。当参数变化大或未知时需要，比如变化的负载质量。风险包括噪声下参数漂移、需要持续激励，以及实践中调参和证明鲁棒性的困难。当变化缓慢且可测时，增益调度或前馈通常更简单更安全。
**追问 1**：*What is persistent excitation?* The input must be rich enough for the parameters to be identifiable.
　中文：输入必须足够丰富，使参数可辨识。
**追问 2**：*Could the centre-of-mass shift in a ballbot be handled adaptively?* Yes in principle, but since the shift is measurable from the arm joints, a feedforward from the kinematics is simpler.
　中文：原则上可以，但质心偏移可由手臂关节测量，所以基于运动学的前馈更简单。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 I used centre-of-mass feedforward computed from the arm joint states with URDF, not adaptation, and that reduced the tilt peak from about 0.06 to about 0.026 radian.*
　中文：P1 里我用手臂关节状态结合 URDF 计算的质心前馈而不是自适应，倾角峰值从约 0.06 降到约 0.026 rad。
**关键词与易错点**：MRAC、adaptation law、persistent excitation、parameter drift。诚实说明未用自适应。

### Q47. What is robust control, and what does H-infinity mean?
**中文题意**：什么是鲁棒控制？H 无穷指什么？
**考察点**：概念与灵敏度函数。
**项目关联**：概念题。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Robust control designs a controller that keeps stability and performance for a whole set of plants, not only the nominal model. H-infinity control formalises this: it minimises the largest gain, the infinity norm, of a transfer function from disturbances to the errors across all frequencies. In loop-shaping terms, the sensitivity S should be small at low frequencies for tracking and disturbance rejection, and the complementary sensitivity T should be small at high frequencies for noise and model uncertainty. S plus T equals one, so I cannot have both small at the same frequency. The peak of S is a direct measure of robustness, and a peak below about two is a good design target. I know this at the concept level; my projects used PID, LQR and sliding mode.

**中文翻译**：鲁棒控制设计的控制器要对一整族对象（而不只是名义模型）保持稳定和性能。H 无穷控制将此形式化：它最小化从扰动到误差的传递函数在所有频率上的最大增益（无穷范数）。用回路整形的话说，灵敏度 S 在低频应小，以保证跟踪与抗扰；互补灵敏度 T 在高频应小，以抑制噪声和模型不确定性。S 加 T 等于 1，所以不能在同一频率让两者都小。S 的峰值是鲁棒性的直接度量，峰值低于约 2 是好的设计目标。这部分我是概念层面的了解；我的项目用的是 PID、LQR 和滑模。
**追问 1**：*What is the waterbed effect?* Reducing sensitivity at some frequencies necessarily increases it at others.
　中文：在某些频率降低灵敏度必然在别处提高。
**追问 2**：*How does the peak of S relate to margins?* A peak of S of 2 guarantees a gain margin of at least 2 and a phase margin of about 29 degrees.
　中文：S 峰值为 2 保证增益裕度至少 2、相位裕度约 29 度。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*I have not designed an H-infinity controller; my way of robustness was conservative gains, saturation handling and testing on the real robot.*
　中文：我没有设计过 H 无穷控制器；我的鲁棒性做法是保守增益、饱和处理和真机测试。
**关键词与易错点**：robust control、H-infinity、sensitivity function、waterbed effect。诚实说明经验边界。

### Q48. What is variable-gain PID, and what was its problem in your launcher?
**中文题意**：什么是变增益 PID？你的发射机构里它的问题是什么？
**考察点**：PID 迭代历史的真实理解。
**项目关联**：P3（变增益 PID 阶段）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Variable-gain PID makes the gain depend on the size of the error. In my launcher, the proportional gain was Kp equals k0 times log base ten of k1 times the absolute error, plus k2, with k0 equal to 1, k1 equal to 8, and k2 equal to 5. A large error gives a larger gain for a quick recovery after the speed dropped, and a smaller error gives a smaller gain to avoid overshoot. The difficulty is near zero error: the logarithm goes to minus infinity, so it needs a floor, and the gain near zero becomes too low or too high, so it is hard to tune. That was one reason I moved on to sliding mode, which handles the disturbance more directly.

**中文翻译**：变增益 PID 让增益取决于误差大小。我的发射机构里，比例增益 Kp 等于 k0 乘 log10(k1 乘误差绝对值) 再加 k2，k0 取 1、k1 取 8、k2 取 5。误差大时增益大，转速下降后能快速恢复；误差小时增益小，避免超调。难点在误差接近零时：对数趋于负无穷，需要设下限，且零附近的增益过低或过高，很难调。这是我转向滑模的原因之一，滑模更直接地处理扰动。
**追问 1**：*How do you protect the logarithm at zero?* Clamp the absolute error to a small minimum before taking the logarithm, and clamp the gain to a range.
　中文：取对数前把误差绝对值钳在一个小下限，并把增益钳在范围内。
**追问 2**：*Is a variable gain still a linear controller?* No; it is nonlinear, so linear stability margins no longer strictly apply.
　中文：不是；它是非线性的，线性稳定裕度不再严格适用。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P3 the three stages were PID with a 120 millisecond recovery and about plus or minus 1 metre per second scatter, variable-gain PID with better large-error response, and sliding mode with a 60 to 80 millisecond recovery and about plus or minus 0.3.*
　中文：P3 的三个阶段：PID（恢复约 120 ms、离散约 ±1 m/s）、变增益 PID（大误差响应更好）、滑模（恢复约 60–80 ms、离散约 ±0.3）。
**关键词与易错点**：variable gain、logarithmic gain、near-zero error、nonlinear controller。

### Q49. How does on–off control with hysteresis work, and where did you use it?
**中文题意**：带滞回的开关控制如何工作？你在哪里用？
**考察点**：简单控制器的正确实现。
**项目关联**：P4（水泵状态机）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> On–off control switches an actuator fully on or off based on a threshold. If the same threshold is used for both switching directions, noise around the threshold makes the actuator chatter. Hysteresis uses two thresholds: turn on below a low threshold and turn off above a higher one, so the actuator stays in its state while the value is in the band between them. In my gardening system the pump turns on when the soil moisture is below 56 and turns off when it is above 65, a band of 9. The state machine also has manual priority, a maximum on-time of 60 seconds with a cooldown, and a sensor-fault rule to turn the pump off.

**中文翻译**：开关控制根据阈值把执行器完全接通或断开。如果两个切换方向使用同一个阈值，阈值附近的噪声会让执行器抖动。滞回使用两个阈值：低于低阈值时开启，高于较高阈值时关闭，湿度处于两者之间的带内时执行器保持原状态。我的园艺系统中，土壤湿度低于 56 时水泵开，高于 65 时关，滞回带宽为 9。状态机还有手动优先、最长开泵 60 秒加冷却、传感器故障规则时关泵。
**追问 1**：*How do you pick the band width?* Larger than the noise and the effect of one pump cycle on the reading, small enough to keep the moisture in a useful range.
　中文：大于噪声和一次开泵对读数的影响，又小到足以把湿度保持在有用范围内。
**追问 2**：*Why a median filter on the sensor?* To reject occasional spikes before the threshold decision.
　中文：在阈值判断之前剔除偶发尖峰。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P4 the sensor was sampled every 200 milliseconds, with a median of five samples giving one moisture value per second, and a reading outside the valid range or three failures in a row turned the pump off and reported a fault.*
　中文：P4 里传感器每 200 ms 采样，5 次中位数得到每秒一个湿度值，读数超出有效范围或连续 3 次失败就关泵并上报故障。
**关键词与易错点**：hysteresis、two thresholds、chattering、manual priority。

### Q50. How do you deal with friction, backlash and dead zones in control?
**中文题意**：控制中如何处理摩擦、间隙和死区？
**考察点**：实际非线性。
**项目关联**：P2（低阻力示教）、P3。
**English answer（口语）**：
> These are common nonlinearities. Static friction creates stick–slip and a steady error that integral action then slowly breaks, with limit cycles as a result. Backlash is the gap in a gear train, so there is a dead time for torque transmission when the direction reverses. A dead zone is an input range that produces no output. To handle them: model-based friction feedforward, such as Coulomb plus viscous terms, with a small dither or a break-away boost; integral action with anti-windup; a high enough loop gain, or sliding-mode style switching; and, for backlash, having the output encoder in the loop and avoiding direction chatter. For a low-resistance leader arm, I keep the friction small by the choice of gravity compensation ratio and by not adding artificial damping.

**中文翻译**：这些是常见的非线性。静摩擦造成粘滑和稳态误差，积分作用会缓慢突破它，由此产生极限环。间隙是齿轮传动里的空隙，方向反转时力矩传递有死区时间。死区是不产生输出的输入范围。处理办法：基于模型的摩擦前馈（库仑加黏性项），配合小的抖动或突破力矩；带抗饱和的积分作用；足够高的回路增益，或滑模式的切换；对间隙，把输出端编码器放进回路并避免方向抖动。对低阻力的主臂，我通过重力补偿比例的选择和不加人工阻尼来保持摩擦小。
**追问 1**：*What is a limit cycle?* A sustained oscillation caused by a nonlinearity such as friction in a loop with integral action.
　中文：由摩擦等非线性在带积分的回路中引起的持续振荡。
**追问 2**：*How do you identify friction?* Move slowly at different constant speeds and plot torque versus speed; the intercept is Coulomb friction and the slope is viscous friction.
　中文：以不同恒速缓慢运动，画力矩—速度曲线；截距是库仑摩擦，斜率是黏性摩擦。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P2 the gravity compensation ratio of 0.8 on the last joint, rather than 1, was to avoid a slow upward drift when the payload mass was uncertain, and in practice values above about 0.9 started to drift.*
　中文：P2 里最后一个关节的重力补偿比例取 0.8 而不是 1，是为了在负载质量不确定时避免缓慢上漂，实践中超过约 0.9 就开始漂移。
**关键词与易错点**：stick–slip、backlash、dead zone、friction feedforward、limit cycle。

### Q51. What is feedforward control, and why combine it with feedback?
**中文题意**：什么是前馈控制？为什么与反馈结合？
**考察点**：前馈与反馈的分工。
**项目关联**：P1（质心前馈）、P7（腿长力前馈）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Feedforward computes the command from the reference or from a measured disturbance, using a model, without waiting for an error. It reacts immediately, so it can cancel known effects, such as gravity or a predictable load, before they cause an error. But it is only as good as the model, and it cannot correct for unmeasured disturbances. Feedback is slower, since it needs an error first, but it corrects whatever is left. Combining the two is standard: feedforward removes the predictable part so the feedback has only a small residual to handle, which allows a lower feedback gain and thus better noise and stability margins.

**中文翻译**：前馈用模型根据参考或测得的扰动计算指令，无需等待误差。它反应立即，所以能在已知影响（如重力或可预测的负载）造成误差之前抵消它们。但它只与模型一样好，无法纠正未测量的扰动。反馈较慢，因为需要先有误差，但能纠正剩下的一切。两者结合是标准做法：前馈消除可预测的部分，反馈只需处理很小的残余，这样可以降低反馈增益，从而获得更好的噪声和稳定裕度。
**追问 1**：*How do you check a feedforward is correct?* Toggle it on and off under a repeatable condition and compare the error; the sign and the gain are the first things to check.
　中文：在可重复条件下开关前馈并比较误差；首先检查符号和增益。
**追问 2**：*What if the sign is wrong?* It adds error instead of removing it; this happened to me once.
　中文：会增加而不是减少误差；我遇到过一次。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 the centre-of-mass feedforward cut the peak tilt from about 0.060 to about 0.026 radian and the 30-second drift from about 0.30 to about 0.10 metres, with five repeats each, and I found one sign error in January through exactly this on/off test.*
　中文：P1 里质心前馈把倾角峰值从约 0.060 降到约 0.026 rad，30 s 漂移从约 0.30 降到约 0.10 m，每种状态重复 5 次，我还在一月份通过这个开关测试发现过一次符号错误。
**关键词与易错点**：feedforward、model-based、residual、sign check。

### Q52. Explain gravity compensation and what happens if it is wrong.
**中文题意**：解释重力补偿，补偿不准会怎样？
**考察点**：模型准确度的影响。
**项目关联**：P2（主臂重力补偿，核心）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Gravity compensation applies, at each joint, the torque that cancels the gravity torque in the current configuration, computed as g of q from the robot model, here URDF and KDL. With it, the leader arm feels almost weightless and can be moved by hand with little resistance, which is what makes teaching comfortable. If the mass or centre of mass in the model is wrong, the arm either sags, when under-compensated, or floats up when over-compensated. Over-compensation is more dangerous, because it is a positive feedback with the user's hand, so I use a ratio slightly below one on the joint with the most uncertain payload, and I monitor motor temperature.

**中文翻译**：重力补偿在每个关节施加抵消当前构型下重力矩的力矩，由机器人模型（这里是 URDF 和 KDL）计算 g(q)。有了它，主臂几乎感觉不到重量，可以用手轻松移动，示教才舒适。模型里的质量或质心不对，手臂就会下垂（欠补偿）或上浮（过补偿）。过补偿更危险，因为它与用户的手形成正反馈，所以对负载最不确定的关节我取略小于 1 的比例，并监控电机温度。
**追问 1**：*How did you find the ratio of 0.8?* On the seventh joint, with a gripper or handle behind it, the mass is the least certain; above about 0.9 it drifted upward slowly, so I left a 10 to 20 percent margin.
　中文：第七关节后面有夹爪或手柄，质量最不确定；超过约 0.9 它缓慢上漂，所以留 10%–20% 余量。
**追问 2**：*How do you verify the model?* Hold the arm at several poses, release, and see if it stays; compare the commanded torque with the expected value.
　中文：把手臂停在多个姿态后放手，看是否保持；比较指令力矩与期望值。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*The compensation loop ran at about 200 hertz, with ratios 1 on joints one to six and 0.8 on joint seven, and I logged temperature at 1 hertz with a warning at 60 degrees.*
　中文：补偿循环约 200 Hz，关节 1–6 比例为 1，关节 7 为 0.8，温度 1 Hz 记录，60 度告警。
**关键词与易错点**：gravity compensation、URDF、over-compensation、payload uncertainty。

### Q53. What is actuator saturation, and how does it affect a control loop?
**中文题意**：什么是执行器饱和？对控制回路有什么影响？
**考察点**：饱和的后果。
**项目关联**：P1（核心）、P7（案例 B）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Every actuator has limits on torque, speed or current. When the controller asks for more than the limit, the actual input is clipped and the effective loop gain drops, so the loop is no longer behaving as designed. Consequences are windup of integral terms, slower recovery, and, for an unstable plant like a balancing robot, loss of the ability to recover when the required control is bigger than the available one, so it falls. I handle it in three layers: design references so they do not demand more than the actuator can give, such as a bounded tilt reference and a speed ramp; limit the output in a way that keeps direction; and use anti-windup. Then I log the saturation share to see how often it happens.

**中文翻译**：每个执行器在力矩、速度或电流上都有限制。控制器要求超过限制时，实际输入被截断，有效回路增益下降，回路不再按设计运行。后果包括积分项饱和、恢复变慢，而对平衡机器人这样的不稳定对象，当所需控制量超过可用控制量时就失去恢复能力而倒下。我分三层处理：设计参考使其不要求超过执行器能力，比如有界的倾角参考和速度斜坡；以保持方向的方式限幅；使用抗积分饱和。再记录饱和占比，看它发生得多频繁。
**追问 1**：*Why is saturation especially dangerous for unstable plants?* The region from which the plant can be stabilised is limited by the available control; beyond it, no controller can save it.
　中文：能够被镇定的区域受可用控制量限制；超出后任何控制器都救不了。
**追问 2**：*How do you measure it?* Log the share of cycles where the command is at the limit.
　中文：记录指令处于限幅的周期占比。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P7 a 0.5 metre per second step needed about 9 newton metres of wheel torque against a 3 newton metre limit and the robot fell; in P1 the saturation share of wheel commands was about 6 percent without the feedforward and about 1 percent with it.*
　中文：P7 里 0.5 m/s 阶跃需要约 9 N·m 轮力矩而限幅是 3 N·m，机器人倒下；P1 里轮指令饱和占比无前馈时约 6%，有前馈时约 1%。
**关键词与易错点**：saturation、effective gain、controllable region、saturation share。

### Q54. How do you limit the output when there are several channels, such as three wheels?
**中文题意**：多通道（如三个轮）时如何限幅？
**考察点**：限幅方式对方向的影响。
**项目关联**：P1（整体 L2 限幅）、P3（功率缩放）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> If I clip each channel independently, then when one channel hits its limit, the direction of the combined command changes. For three omni-wheels, this would turn a pure forward push into a push with a sideways component, so the robot would be pushed in the wrong direction at exactly the moment the correction matters most. So I scale the whole vector: I compute the norm of the command vector, and if it exceeds the limit, I multiply all components by the same factor. That keeps the direction and reduces only the magnitude. I also limit the change per cycle to avoid steps. The same idea is behind the chassis power scaling in my RoboMaster project, where all four wheel speeds are scaled together.

**中文翻译**：如果独立裁剪每个通道，当一个通道触及限幅时，合成指令的方向就改变了。对三个全向轮，这会把纯前向的推力变成带侧向分量的推力，在最需要修正的时候把机器人推向错误方向。所以我整体缩放向量：计算指令向量的范数，若超过限值，所有分量乘以同一系数。这样保持方向，只降低幅值。我还限制每周期的变化量以避免阶跃。RoboMaster 项目里的底盘功率缩放用了同样的思想，四个轮速一起缩放。
**追问 1**：*What norm do you use?* The L2 norm for a smooth ellipse-like limit; the infinity norm would correspond to per-channel clipping.
　中文：L2 范数给出平滑的类椭圆限制；无穷范数对应逐通道裁剪。
**追问 2**：*Is scaling always better?* It keeps direction but also reduces the authority of the unsaturated channels; in some cases a priority scheme is better.
　中文：保持方向，但也降低了未饱和通道的能力；有时优先级方案更好。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 the wheel command norm was limited to about 8 in wheel-side radians per second with proportional scaling plus a per-cycle slope limit.*
　中文：P1 里轮指令范数限制在约 8（轮端 rad/s 量级），等比缩放并加每周期斜率限制。
**关键词与易错点**：vector scaling、L2 norm、direction preserving、slope limit。

### Q55. How do you implement a PID digitally? What is the difference between positional and incremental forms?
**中文题意**：数字 PID 如何实现？位置式与增量式有何不同？
**考察点**：数字 PID 细节。
**项目关联**：P3（数字 PID）、P2（板端轮速 PID）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> In the positional form, I compute the full output each cycle: Kp times the error, plus Ki times the accumulated sum of errors times the sample time, plus Kd times the difference of errors divided by the sample time. In the incremental, or velocity, form, I compute only the change of output from the change of the error terms, then add it to the previous output. The incremental form has no explicit integral accumulator, so windup is naturally limited by the output clamp, and switching modes is smooth. The positional form is easier to read. In either form I need integral clamping, output limiting, a fixed sample time, and a reset on enable.

**中文翻译**：位置式每个周期计算完整输出：Kp 乘误差，加 Ki 乘误差累积和乘采样时间，加 Kd 乘误差差分除以采样时间。增量式只根据误差项的变化计算输出的变化量，再加到上一次输出上。增量式没有显式积分累加器，所以积分饱和自然受输出钳位限制，模式切换也更平滑。位置式更易读。无论哪种，都需要积分钳位、输出限幅、固定采样时间和使能时复位。
**追问 1**：*What if dt is not constant?* The integral and derivative terms are then wrong, so I either use the measured dt or keep the loop time-triggered; the gains implicitly assume a nominal dt otherwise.
　中文：积分与微分项会出错，所以要么使用测得的 dt，要么保持时间触发；否则增益隐含了名义 dt。
**追问 2**：*Where do you reset the controller?* When the controller is disabled, re-enabled, or when a fault is cleared.
　中文：控制器失能、重新使能或故障清除时。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P3 the PID gains were tied to the control period of about 3 milliseconds and the code used a hard-coded nominal dt, a limitation I flag, and in P2 the board-side wheel PID ran at 200 hertz with Kp equal to 5.0 and Ki equal to 0.5.*
　中文：P3 的 PID 增益绑定约 3 ms 的控制周期，代码使用写死的名义 dt，这是我明确指出的局限；P2 板端轮速 PID 以 200 Hz 运行，Kp 为 5.0、Ki 为 0.5。
**关键词与易错点**：positional form、incremental form、clamp、nominal dt、reset。

### Q56. How does the sampling period change the controller design?
**中文题意**：采样周期如何影响控制器设计？
**考察点**：数字控制中的延迟与离散化。
**项目关联**：P3、P7。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Sampling adds delay. A zero-order hold holds the command for a whole period, which is like an average extra delay of half a period, and the measurement is also taken at a certain instant, so the effective delay is about one period in total. Phase margin is lost in proportion to this delay times the crossover frequency. So, the shorter the period, the smaller the penalty, but the heavier the CPU and the bus load. Also, discretised gains differ from continuous-time ones: the integral gain is multiplied by the period and the derivative gain divided by it. A controller designed in continuous time should be checked after discretisation, and gains are tied to the period, so changing the period means retuning.

**中文翻译**：采样会增加延迟。零阶保持把指令保持整个周期，相当于平均额外延迟半个周期，测量也是在某一时刻取得，所以总的有效延迟约一个周期。相位裕度损失与该延迟乘穿越频率成正比。所以周期越短代价越小，但 CPU 和总线负载越重。离散化后的增益也不同于连续时间增益：积分增益乘周期，微分增益除以周期。在连续时间设计的控制器应在离散化之后再检查，增益与周期绑定，改周期就要重新整定。
**追问 1**：*Estimate the phase loss for a 3 ms period at 35 Hz.* Delay about one period plus a half: 4.5 ms times 360 times 35 is about 57 degrees; my estimate for the real loop was about 54 degrees, consistent with the order of magnitude.
　中文：延迟约 1.5 个周期：4.5 ms 乘 360 乘 35 约 57 度；我对实际回路的估计约 54 度，量级一致。
**追问 2**：*What is multi-rate control?* Different loops run at different rates to save resources.
　中文：不同回路以不同速率运行以节省资源。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P3 the control task was about 3 milliseconds while the IMU task was about 1 kilohertz; I do not say the control loop was 1 kilohertz.*
　中文：P3 的控制任务约 3 ms，而 IMU 任务约 1 kHz；我不会说控制回路是 1 kHz。
**关键词与易错点**：zero-order hold、phase loss、discretised gains、retuning。

### Q57. How does timing jitter affect a controller, and how did you measure and reduce it?
**中文题意**：时序抖动如何影响控制器？你如何测量与降低？
**考察点**：实时性能。
**项目关联**：P1（抖动 99 分位 3.5 → 1.0 ms）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> If the real sampling period varies, then the integral and derivative terms are computed with the wrong dt, and the effective delay varies, which erodes the phase margin and adds noise. For a loop running at 100 hertz, a few milliseconds of jitter is significant. I measured it by timestamping the first line of each callback with a monotonic clock, logging it, and computing the histogram of period differences offline. Then I reduced it: real-time priority with SCHED_FIFO, CPU core pinning, no logging or memory allocation inside the control cycle, and moving heavy work to other processes. In my case the ninety-ninth percentile jitter dropped from about 3.5 to about 1 millisecond.

**中文翻译**：如果实际采样周期变化，积分和微分项会用错误的 dt 计算，有效延迟也会变化，削弱相位裕度并增加噪声。对 100 Hz 的回路，几毫秒的抖动就很显著。我的测量方法是在每个回调的第一行用单调时钟打时间戳并记录，离线计算周期差分的直方图。然后降低它：用 SCHED_FIFO 实时优先级、CPU 核绑定、控制周期内不打日志不分配内存，把重活放到其他进程。我的案例中第 99 百分位抖动从约 3.5 ms 降到约 1 ms。
**追问 1**：*Why use a monotonic clock?* Wall clocks can jump when synchronised, which would give false jitter.
　中文：墙上时钟同步时会跳变，造成虚假抖动。
**追问 2**：*Did the maximum improve too?* Yes, from about 8 to about 2 milliseconds, and events above 5 milliseconds in one hour went from about 180 to zero.
　中文：是，最大值从约 8 降到约 2 ms，一小时内超过 5 ms 的事件从约 180 次降到 0。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*These are the P1 numbers: the control node ran with real-time priority 50 pinned to core 3, and no logging happened inside the control cycle.*
　中文：这是 P1 的数字：控制节点以实时优先级 50 绑定在核 3 上运行，控制周期内不打日志。
**关键词与易错点**：jitter、SCHED_FIFO、monotonic clock、percentile。

### Q58. How do you choose the rates for the different loops in a system?
**中文题意**：系统中各回路的频率怎么选？
**考察点**：多速率架构设计。
**项目关联**：P3、P5、P6、P7。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I go from the bottom: the innermost loop closes around the fastest dynamics, like motor current or speed, and runs the fastest. Each outer loop is slower by a factor of at least three to ten, as it sees the inner loop as nearly ideal. The sensor rate has to be at least as fast as the loop that uses it, and the communication has to carry it with margin. I check the bus load and the CPU budget. For example, in my RoboMaster hero the IMU task ran at about 1 kilohertz, the control task at about 3 milliseconds, the vision link at 100 hertz down and 200 hertz up, and the board-to-board frames at 100 and 50 hertz. Each rate has a reason, and the slowest link sets the outermost loop.

**中文翻译**：我从底层开始：最内环围绕最快的动态（如电机电流或速度）闭合，运行最快。每个外环至少慢三到十倍，使其把内环看作近似理想。传感器频率至少要和使用它的回路一样快，通信要有裕度地承载它。我会检查总线负载和 CPU 预算。例如我的 RoboMaster 英雄车里，IMU 任务约 1 kHz，控制任务约 3 ms，视觉链路下行 100 Hz、上行 200 Hz，板间帧 100 Hz 和 50 Hz。每个频率都有理由，最慢的链路决定最外环。
**追问 1**：*What happens if a sensor is slower than the loop?* The loop sees repeated old data, which looks like a delay and a staircase; I flag it with a freshness check.
　中文：回路看到重复的旧数据，表现为延迟和阶梯；我用新鲜度检查标记。
**追问 2**：*How do you check the bus?* Estimate the load from frame size, rate and bit rate; the target is below about fifty percent.
　中文：用帧长、频率和比特率估算负载；目标低于约 50%。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P7 the control and estimation run at 500 hertz with the IMU at 1 kilohertz and the FDCAN torque commands at 500 hertz, while the remote command is about 50 hertz.*
　中文：P7 里控制与估计 500 Hz，IMU 1 kHz，FDCAN 力矩指令 500 Hz，遥控指令约 50 Hz。
**关键词与易错点**：multi-rate、inner loop first、bus load、freshness。

### Q59. How does sensor quantisation or resolution limit control?
**中文题意**：传感器量化与分辨率如何限制控制？
**考察点**：数字化的影响。
**项目关联**：P2（编码器分辨率、MIT 帧量化）、P3（编码器 8192）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A finite-resolution sensor adds quantisation noise, and, after differentiation, even a larger one. An encoder with N counts per turn has an angular step of 360 degrees over N. Speed from differences over one period has a resolution of that step divided by the period, so at low speeds the speed signal is coarse and can look like zero then a spike. Remedies are a longer difference window, a filtered estimate, or timing the pulses. The same applies to the command side: a motor protocol with 12-bit torque has a finite step, and limited position resolution of 16 bits over plus or minus 12.5 radians gives about 0.022 degrees. I always clamp before packing into the frame.

**中文翻译**：有限分辨率的传感器引入量化噪声，微分之后更大。每圈 N 个计数的编码器角度步长为 360 度除以 N。用一个周期的差分得到的速度分辨率是该步长除以周期，所以低速时速度信号很粗，可能表现为零然后一个尖峰。对策是更长的差分窗口、滤波估计或测脉冲时间。命令侧也一样：12 位力矩的电机协议步长有限，16 位位置在 ±12.5 rad 范围内分辨率约 0.022 度。打包进帧前我总是先钳位。
**追问 1**：*What is the speed resolution of a 4096-line encoder with quadrature at 5 ms?* The count per turn is 16384, so about 0.022 degrees per count, giving about 4.4 degrees per second, or about 0.7 rpm.
　中文：4096 线四倍频每圈 16384 计数，每计数约 0.022 度，5 ms 内分辨率约 4.4 度/秒，约 0.7 rpm。
**追问 2**：*Why does clamp before packing matter?* An out-of-range value would wrap around in the integer field and create a huge wrong command.
　中文：越界值会在整数字段里回绕，产生巨大的错误指令。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P2 the wheel encoder is 4096 lines with 16384 counts per turn and the board samples every 5 milliseconds; the MIT frame uses 16-bit position and 12-bit speed, torque and gains.*
　中文：P2 里轮编码器 4096 线、每圈 16384 计数，板端每 5 ms 采样；MIT 帧位置 16 位，速度、力矩、增益 12 位。
**关键词与易错点**：quantisation、resolution、clamp、wrap-around。

### Q60. What is the trade-off when you put a filter in a control loop?
**中文题意**：在控制回路里加滤波器有什么取舍？
**考察点**：噪声与相位的权衡。
**项目关联**：P1（0.8 Hz 低通）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A low-pass filter removes noise, but it adds phase lag and reduces the stability margin if its corner is close to the loop crossover. So I choose the cutoff by asking two questions: how much noise do I want to remove, and how much lag can the loop tolerate? For a signal used by a slow outer loop, a heavy filter is fine. For a signal inside a fast inner loop, I use a light filter or none. In my ballbot, the wheel-speed estimate for the outer loop had a 0.8 hertz low-pass, which gives about 0.2 seconds of delay, acceptable for a 0.15 hertz loop. The tilt itself was not filtered further, and the gyro had at most a 25 hertz low-pass.

**中文翻译**：低通滤波器去除噪声，但增加相位滞后，若转折频率靠近回路穿越频率，就会降低稳定裕度。所以我用两个问题选截止频率：想去掉多少噪声？回路能容忍多少滞后？用于慢外环的信号可以重滤波；快内环里的信号则用轻滤波或不滤波。我的球形机器人里，外环用的轮速估计加 0.8 Hz 低通，约 0.2 秒延迟，对 0.15 Hz 的回路可接受；倾角本身不再额外滤波，陀螺最多加 25 Hz 低通。
**追问 1**：*Result of that change?* The static RMS of the speed estimate dropped from about 0.020 to 0.004 metres per second.
　中文：速度估计的静止均方根从约 0.020 降到 0.004 m/s。
**追问 2**：*What about notch filters?* They remove a specific resonance with less phase loss elsewhere.
　中文：陷波滤波器去除特定谐振，对其他频率的相位损失较小。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*That 0.8 hertz path was one of the five optimisations I made in P1; it was chosen for the slow outer loop only, not for the attitude loop.*
　中文：这条 0.8 Hz 路径是 P1 五项优化之一；它只用于慢外环，不用于姿态环。
**关键词与易错点**：low-pass、phase lag、cutoff、crossover frequency。

### Q61. Why do you rate-limit or smooth commands?
**中文题意**：为什么要对命令做限速或平滑？
**考察点**：保护执行器与对象。
**项目关联**：P6（加速度限幅）、P7（速度斜坡）、P2（目标限速）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A command that jumps asks for infinite acceleration, which no actuator can supply. It causes torque spikes, saturation, mechanical stress, and for balancing robots it can tip the machine. So I put a rate limiter, a ramp, or a low-pass on the commands. The limit should come from the physics: the maximum acceleration the actuators can deliver, and the stopping distance, which is v squared over two a. I also use smoothing for operator input, for example a teleoperation command with noise. The cost is a bit of lag, so I keep the limits as loose as the hardware allows.

**中文翻译**：跳变的指令等于要求无穷大的加速度，没有执行器能提供。它造成力矩尖峰、饱和、机械应力，对平衡机器人还可能使机器倾倒。所以我对指令加限速器、斜坡或低通。限值应来自物理：执行器能提供的最大加速度，以及停车距离 v 平方除以 2a。我也对操作者输入（比如带噪声的遥操作指令）做平滑。代价是一点滞后，所以我让限值在硬件允许的范围内尽量宽松。
**追问 1**：*How did you pick 0.8 metres per second and 1 metre per second squared?* They are the default profile for the platform, derived from the stopping distance and the jitter allowance, kept in the device configuration.
　中文：它们是平台的默认档位，由停车距离和抖动余量反推，放在设备配置里。
**追问 2**：*Is a ramp the same as a low-pass?* A ramp limits the rate of change linearly; a low-pass has an exponential shape and no hard limit on the rate.
　中文：斜坡线性限制变化率；低通是指数形状，对变化率没有硬限制。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P6 the design default was a speed limit of 0.8 metres per second and acceleration limits of 1.0 metres per second squared and 2.0 radians per second squared; in P7 the velocity command was ramped over about one second after a failure.*
　中文：P6 的设计默认值是速度上限 0.8 m/s、加速度限幅 1.0 m/s² 和 2.0 rad/s²；P7 在一次失败后把速度指令加了约 1 s 的斜坡。
**关键词与易错点**：rate limiter、ramp、stopping distance、acceleration limit。P6 数字是设计取值。

### Q62. How do you design watchdogs and fail-safes for a controller?
**中文题意**：如何为控制器设计看门狗与故障安全？
**考察点**：安全工程。
**项目关联**：P1（100 ms 看门狗）、P3（离线检测）、P2（失联策略）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I assume that any link can fail, so each source of data and each command path has a timeout. If the command is too old, the actuator side sets a safe output, usually zero velocity or zero torque; if feedback is missing, the controller disables itself. For the safe state I choose the one that cannot hurt: zero command and, for a balancing robot, a controlled stop or locked fault. The thresholds follow the signal rate: about three to four periods. I also require manual re-enable after a fault, with a clean initialisation, and I log the cause code. A watchdog is only useful if it is tested, so I inject faults by unplugging a cable on the bench.

**中文翻译**：我假设任何链路都可能失败，所以每个数据源和每条命令路径都有超时。命令太旧时，执行器侧设为安全输出，通常是零速度或零力矩；反馈缺失时，控制器自行失能。安全状态我选不会伤人的那个：零指令，对平衡机器人则是受控停止或锁定故障。阈值随信号速率而定：约三到四个周期。故障后还要求人工重新使能并干净地初始化，同时记录原因码。看门狗只有经过测试才有用，所以我在台架上拔线注入故障。
**追问 1**：*What are your timeouts?* In P1 the driver-side command watchdog is 100 milliseconds, and a missing wheel feedback for more than 50 milliseconds counts as a motor drop.
　中文：P1 驱动侧命令看门狗 100 ms，轮反馈缺失超过 50 ms 判为电机掉线。
**追问 2**：*In P3?* Offline thresholds were about 100 milliseconds for the remote control, vision and board-to-board link, and 50 milliseconds for motor feedback; the safe state was zero current with cleared integrators while keeping the CAN heartbeat.
　中文：P3 里遥控、视觉、板间链路约 100 ms，电机反馈 50 ms；安全状态为电流置零并清积分，同时保持 CAN 心跳。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P2 the chassis heartbeat logic that I read in the firmware never actually triggered, so I noticed it and relied on the host-side 300 millisecond cmd_vel timeout instead; I noticed the problem, I did not fix the firmware.*
　中文：P2 里我读固件时发现底盘心跳逻辑实际不会触发，所以改依赖上位机 300 ms 的 cmd_vel 超时；我是发现问题，并没有修改固件。
**关键词与易错点**：watchdog、timeout、safe state、manual re-enable、fault injection。读码发现说 "I noticed"。

## 7. F 机器人控制专题：VMC、阻抗、力控、遥操作（Q63–Q82）

### Q63. How does leader–follower position–velocity tracking work?
**中文题意**：主从（leader–follower）位置—速度跟踪如何工作？
**考察点**：PD 加速度前馈的遥操作结构。
**项目关联**：P2（核心）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The leader arm is moved by the human and gravity-compensated, so it feels light. I read its joint positions and velocities and send them as targets to the follower. On the follower, each joint runs a motor-level impedance law: torque equals kp times position error, plus kd times velocity error, plus a feedforward torque. I send the leader velocity as the velocity target, low-pass filtered, so the follower moves with the leader instead of lagging behind a position-only target. The gains differ per joint, larger for the heavy shoulder joints and smaller for the wrist. This gives low-resistance hand-guided teaching with the follower moving in sync, and the tracking error was around 0.01 radian RMS in steady motion.

**中文翻译**：主臂由人移动并做重力补偿，所以手感轻。我读取它的关节位置和速度，作为目标发给从臂。从臂每个关节运行电机层的阻抗律：力矩等于 kp 乘位置误差，加 kd 乘速度误差，再加前馈力矩。我把主臂速度（低通后）作为速度目标，使从臂跟着主臂运动，而不是落后于只含位置的目标。增益按关节不同，沉重的肩部关节大、手腕小。这实现了低阻力的手把手示教，从臂同步运动，稳态运动时跟踪误差均方根约 0.01 rad。
**追问 1**：*What are the follower gains?* kp is 50 on the first four joints and 10 on the last three; kd is 1.2 on the first four and about 0.2 to 0.3 on the last three.
　中文：前四个关节 kp 为 50，后三个为 10；kd 前四个为 1.2，后三个约 0.2–0.3。
**追问 2**：*What is the end-to-end latency?* About 30 milliseconds: reading the leader about 5, publishing and receiving about 5, bus round trip about 2, and the motor inner loop about 15 to 20.
　中文：约 30 ms：读主臂约 5、发布接收约 5、总线往返约 2、电机内环约 15–20。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*I tuned the follower gains and the leader gravity compensation parameters; the motor driver encapsulation and the CAN framework were from the team.*
　中文：我整定了从臂增益和主臂重力补偿参数；电机驱动封装和 CAN 框架来自团队。
**关键词与易错点**：leader–follower、position–velocity tracking、velocity feedforward、per-joint gains。

### Q64. What is impedance control, and how does it differ from admittance control?
**中文题意**：什么是阻抗控制？与导纳控制有何区别？
**考察点**：力交互控制的两种形式。
**项目关联**：P2（MIT 模式的关节阻抗）；概念题。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Impedance control makes the robot behave like a mass–spring–damper toward the environment. The robot is commanded in position, but the force it exerts depends on the position error: F equals K times the position error plus D times the velocity error. It is implemented by torque control at the joints, so it has good back-drivability and handles contact softly. Admittance control is the dual: it measures the contact force and computes a motion, the position the robot should move to, then tracks it with a stiff position loop. Impedance suits torque-controlled, light robots. Admittance suits stiff, position-controlled industrial arms with a force sensor. The joint-level PD law on the follower arm is, in effect, a joint-space impedance.

**中文翻译**：阻抗控制让机器人对环境表现得像质量—弹簧—阻尼。机器人按位置指令运行，但它施加的力取决于位置误差：F 等于 K 乘位置误差加 D 乘速度误差。它由关节力矩控制实现，所以反向驱动性好，接触柔顺。导纳控制是对偶：测量接触力，计算运动（机器人应移动到的位置），再用刚性的位置环跟踪。阻抗适合力矩控制的轻型机器人；导纳适合带力传感器的刚性位置控制工业臂。从臂上关节层的 PD 律实际上就是关节空间的阻抗。
**追问 1**：*Which is more stable in contact with a stiff environment?* Admittance can be unstable against stiff environments if the force loop is slow; impedance is generally more robust against stiff contact if the inner torque loop is fast.
　中文：导纳在力环慢时对刚性环境可能不稳定；内部力矩环快时阻抗对刚性接触通常更稳健。
**追问 2**：*What parameters set the feel?* Stiffness K and damping D (and virtual mass in admittance).
　中文：刚度 K 和阻尼 D（导纳里还有虚拟质量）。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*My arms used a motor protocol with kp, kd, position, velocity and torque in one frame, so the joint impedance runs inside the motor driver at a high rate, and I set the gains.*
　中文：我的机械臂使用把 kp、kd、位置、速度、力矩放在同一帧的电机协议，所以关节阻抗在电机驱动内部高速运行，我负责设定增益。
**关键词与易错点**：impedance、admittance、back-drivability、stiffness、damping。

### Q65. What is hybrid position/force control?
**中文题意**：什么是位置/力混合控制？
**考察点**：接触任务的控制思想。
**项目关联**：概念题。
**English answer（口语）**：
> When a robot contacts a surface, the directions normal to the surface and along the surface are different. Along the normal, I cannot control position freely, because the surface constrains it, so I control force. Along the tangent, I control position or velocity. Hybrid position/force control splits the task space with a selection matrix: some axes are position-controlled and others force-controlled, and the two controllers' outputs are combined. A typical example is wiping a table: control force normal to the table, to push with a constant pressure, and control the motion along the table. A practical difficulty is the force sensing quality and the stiffness of the contact, and I know this at the concept level; my projects used position and impedance modes.

### Q66. What is virtual model control (VMC)?
**中文题意**：什么是虚拟模型控制（VMC）？
**考察点**：理解概念并能解释用途。
**项目关联**：P7（核心）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Virtual model control lets me think in the task space with virtual physical components, like springs, dampers and forces, instead of joint torques. I decide what virtual force or torque I want at a point on the robot, for example a force along the leg to support the body, or a torque to hold the body pitch. Then I map that task-space force to joint torques with the transposed Jacobian: tau equals J transpose times F. This is nice because the control design stays intuitive and decoupled in task space, and the kinematics handles the joint details. For a wheel-legged robot, the five-bar leg is mapped into a virtual leg with a length and an angle, and the VMC converts the support force and the pitch torque to the two hip motors.

**中文翻译**：虚拟模型控制让我在任务空间用虚拟的物理元件（弹簧、阻尼、力）来思考，而不是关节力矩。我决定机器人某点上想要的虚拟力或力矩，比如沿腿方向支撑机身的力，或保持机身俯仰的力矩。然后用转置雅可比把任务空间的力映射为关节力矩：tau 等于 J 转置乘 F。这样的好处是控制设计在任务空间保持直观和解耦，由运动学处理关节细节。对轮腿机器人，五连杆腿被映射成有长度和角度的虚拟腿，VMC 把支撑力和俯仰力矩转换为两个髋关节电机的力矩。
**追问 1**：*Why is the transpose the right map?* By virtual work, the power at the task point equals the power at the joints, so F transpose times the task velocity equals tau transpose times the joint velocity, which gives tau equal to J transpose F.
　中文：由虚功原理，任务点功率等于关节功率，所以 F 转置乘任务速度等于 tau 转置乘关节速度，得到 tau 等于 J 转置 F。
**追问 2**：*What goes wrong near a singularity?* J becomes rank deficient, so some force directions require huge torques or cannot be generated.
　中文：J 降秩，某些方向的力需要极大力矩或无法产生。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P7 I computed the virtual leg length and angle from the closed-chain kinematics, mapped support force and pitch torque to the joints via the Jacobian, and coordinated leg extension with a leg-length PID and force feedforward.*
　中文：P7 里我由闭链运动学计算虚拟腿长和角度，通过雅可比把支撑力和俯仰力矩映射到关节，并用腿长 PID 和力前馈协调腿的伸缩。
**关键词与易错点**：virtual model、Jacobian transpose、virtual work、task space。

### Q67. Walk me through the VMC leg-length control in the wheel-legged robot.

**追问 2**：*What are the torque limits?* Joint torque is limited to about 6 newton metres in software and the wheel to about 3.

### Q69. Describe the control architecture of the ballbot.

**English answer（口语）**：
> It is a cascade running at 100 hertz. ### Q70. Why bound the tilt reference of the outer loop?
**中文题意**：为什么要限制外环输出的倾角参考？
**考察点**：级联结构的安全与线性假设。
**项目关联**：P1。
**English answer（口语）**：
> There are three reasons. First, the tilt reference is the interface between the loops, and a large reference would demand a large lean, which needs a large wheel acceleration; the actuators saturate and the robot falls. Second, the linear model I rely on is valid only for small tilt, so I stay inside it. Third, the bound limits the worst-case acceleration, because the maximum acceleration is roughly g times the tilt angle: with 0.1 radian, I get about one metre per second squared, which is comfortably above the 0.5 metre per second squared acceleration limit I used. After the LQR analysis showed that the earlier limit of 0.15 radian could ask for too much, I lowered it to 0.1.

**中文翻译**：有三个原因。第一，倾角参考是两个环之间的接口，参考过大意味着需要大幅倾斜和很大的轮加速度，执行器饱和而机器人倒下。第二，我依赖的线性模型只在小倾角下有效，要待在其中。第三，这个界限了最坏情形的加速度，因为最大加速度约为 g 乘倾角：0.1 rad 时约 1 m/s²，足以高于我用的 0.5 m/s² 加速度限制。LQR 分析表明此前 0.15 rad 的限制可能要求过多后，我把它降到了 0.1。
**追问 1**：*Check the acceleration estimate.* g times 0.1 radian is about 0.98 metres per second squared.
　中文：g 乘 0.1 rad 约 0.98 m/s²。
**追问 2**：*What happens if the reference is bounded but the plant needs more?* The robot cannot recover from large disturbances, so the bound also defines the maximum push it can take; in my case a push of 20 to 30 newtons.
　中文：机器人无法从大扰动中恢复，所以界限也决定了能承受的最大推力；我的情形是 20–30 N。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*The bound of 0.10 radian, about 5.7 degrees, together with a 0.6 metre per second speed limit and a 0.5 metre per second squared acceleration limit, is part of my P1 design values.*
　中文：0.10 rad（约 5.7 度）的界限，加上 0.6 m/s 速度上限和 0.5 m/s² 加速度上限，是我 P1 的设计取值。
**关键词与易错点**：interface、bounded reference、small-angle validity、maximum acceleration。

### Q71. How do you convert a body velocity command into three omni-wheel speeds?
**中文题意**：如何把机体速度指令转换成三个全向轮速度？
**考察点**：逆运动学与分配。
**项目关联**：P1。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Each wheel drives the ball at its contact point, in the direction tangent to the wheel's rolling direction. The three wheels are 120 degrees apart in azimuth and touch the ball at a fixed latitude, here 45 degrees. For a desired ball surface velocity, I project it on each wheel's driving direction, which gives a three-by-two matrix of unit vectors; then I scale by the ratio of ball radius to wheel radius, which is 4 for our ball and wheels, to get the wheel angular speeds. The inverse problem uses the pseudo-inverse of that matrix. The matrix has full rank, and it is evaluated once. The important points are the sign conventions and the wheel numbering, which I verified with a bench test, wheel by wheel, before going to the robot.

**中文翻译**：每个轮在接触点驱动球，方向是轮滚动方向的切线方向。三个轮方位角相隔 120 度，在固定纬度（这里 45 度）接触球。对期望的球表面速度，我把它投影到每个轮的驱动方向上，得到由单位向量构成的 3×2 矩阵；再乘球半径与轮半径之比（我们的球和轮为 4），得到轮角速度。逆问题用该矩阵的伪逆。矩阵满秩，只需计算一次。重要的是符号约定和轮序，我在上机前用台架逐轮验证。
**追问 1**：*What are the radii?* The ball is about 0.40 metre in diameter, so R is 0.20 metre, and each wheel has r equal to 0.05 metre.
　中文：球直径约 0.40 m，R 为 0.20 m，轮 r 为 0.05 m。
**追问 2**：*What are common mistakes?* A wrong wheel order or a reversed sign gives a robot that spins instead of translating.
　中文：轮序错或符号反会导致机器人旋转而不是平移。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 I allocated the controller output to three wheels via kinematics; the wheel azimuths were 0, 120 and 240 degrees from the body forward direction.*
　中文：P1 里我通过运动学把控制输出分配到三个轮；轮方位相对机体前方为 0、120、240 度。

**English answer（口语）**：

### Q73. How do you handle the gimbal's gravity, vision latency and lead?

**English answer（口语）**：

　中文：视觉侧有图像时间戳和姿态历史，可以在正确时刻表示目标。

　中文：没有单独的前馈；内环的积分项吸收它。

### Q74. How does the feeder (loader) work, and how do you handle jams?
**中文题意**：拨盘（供弹）如何工作？卡弹怎么处理？
**考察点**：位置—速度双环与故障检测。
**项目关联**：P3。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The feeder motor has a position loop on the outside and a speed loop on the inside. The disc has six slots, so each shot is one sixtieth of a revolution, which with the 19.2 to 1 gearbox and 8192 counts per turn is about 26,000 counts. The position loop has a proportional gain of 0.1 from counts to rpm, with the output limited to 2500 rpm, and the speed loop has a Kp of 8 and Ki of 0.3. A jam is detected when the remaining position error stays above 5000 counts for more than one second. Then I reverse by two slots, only once; if that fails, the state goes to a failure mode, stops firing and reports, instead of retrying forever.

**中文翻译**：拨盘电机外环是位置环，内环是速度环。拨盘有六个格，每发为一圈的六分之一，结合 19.2:1 减速箱和每圈 8192 计数，约 26000 计数。位置环比例增益 0.1（计数到 rpm），输出限制 2500 rpm；速度环 Kp 为 8、Ki 为 0.3。残余位置误差持续超过 5000 计数且超过 1 秒判为卡弹。然后反转两个格，只尝试一次；若失败，状态进入故障模式，停止发射并上报，而不是无限重试。

### Q75. How does the chassis power limit work in your hero robot?
**中文题意**：你的英雄机器人底盘功率限制是怎么做的？
**考察点**：约束下的缩放控制。
**项目关联**：P3（核心）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> The rules limit the chassis power, and the referee reports the limit, the measured power and the buffer energy. If the predicted power exceeds the limit, which is the reported limit minus 5 watts, I scale the wheel targets by a common factor, which keeps the direction of motion. 　中文：缩放保持运动方向和轮速比例。

　中文：裁判的缓冲能量反馈才是真正的安全网；模型只用于预测。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*This matches the CV: I estimated power demand from real-time power and buffer-energy feedback and scaled the wheel speeds, so the chassis stayed manoeuvrable under power limits.*
　中文：这与 CV 一致：我根据实时功率与缓冲能量反馈估计功率需求并缩放轮速，使底盘在功率限制下仍可机动。

### Q76. Explain Mecanum wheel kinematics and the chassis-follow mode.
**中文题意**：解释麦克纳姆轮运动学与底盘跟随模式。
**考察点**：运动学与跟随控制。
**项目关联**：P3。
**English answer（口语）**：
> A Mecanum wheel has rollers at 45 degrees, so each wheel produces a force along a diagonal. For the body velocities vx, vy and yaw rate omega, each wheel speed is one over the wheel radius times vx plus or minus vy plus or minus d times omega, with signs set by the wheel position, and d is the sum of half the wheelbase and half the track width, here 0.38 metre. The wheel radius is 0.076 metre. The chassis-follow mode makes the body yaw track the gimbal: I take the gimbal yaw relative to the chassis as the error, apply a PID with Kp equal to 4 and Kd equal to 0.2, and limit omega to 6 radians per second with a dead zone of 2 degrees. In the spinning mode, omega is constant at about 3.5 radians per second, and the translation vector is rotated by the relative angle.

**中文翻译**：麦克纳姆轮的滚子成 45 度，所以每个轮沿对角方向产生力。对机体速度 vx、vy 和偏航角速度 omega，每个轮速等于 1/轮半径乘 (vx 加减 vy 加减 d 乘 omega)，符号由轮的位置决定，d 为半轴距加半轮距，这里是 0.38 米。轮半径 0.076 米。底盘跟随模式让车体 yaw 跟踪云台：取云台相对底盘的 yaw 为误差，用 PID（Kp 为 4，Kd 为 0.2），限制 omega 在 6 rad/s，死区 2 度。小陀螺模式下 omega 恒定约 3.5 rad/s，平移向量按相对角旋转。
**追问 1**：*Why the dead zone?* To avoid hunting around zero because of IMU noise and backlash.
　中文：避免由 IMU 噪声和间隙造成的零点附近来回抖动。
**追问 2**：*Why does spinning need the rotated translation vector?* Because the gimbal frame stays fixed in the world while the body spins; the operator's command is in the gimbal frame.
　中文：因为车体旋转时云台坐标系固定在世界中，操作者指令在云台坐标系里。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*I allocated body velocity to four Mecanum wheels in P3, and the wheel speeds were then scaled by the power control.*
　中文：P3 里我把机体速度分配到四个麦克纳姆轮，轮速再由功率控制缩放。
**关键词与易错点**：Mecanum、45-degree rollers、chassis follow、dead zone、rotated command。

### Q77. How does the differential-drive base turn body velocity into wheel speeds, and how is odometry computed?
**中文题意**：差速底盘如何把机体速度转换为轮速？里程计如何计算？
**考察点**：差速运动学。
**项目关联**：P2（差速底盘）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> For a differential drive with track width L, the body speed v is the average of the two wheel speeds and the yaw rate omega is the right speed minus the left speed over L. Inverting: the left wheel speed is v minus omega L over two, and the right is v plus omega L over two. Each wheel speed target is converted to rpm by the wheel radius, 0.13 metres, and closed with a PID on the board using the encoder feedback at 200 hertz, with Kp 5.0, Ki 0.5 and Kd zero. For odometry, I integrate the measured v and omega: x increases by v cosine theta times dt, y by v sine theta times dt, and theta by omega times dt, at 200 hertz on the board, published at 50 hertz.

**中文翻译**：对轮距为 L 的差速驱动，机体速度 v 是两轮速度的平均，偏航角速度 omega 是右轮速度减左轮速度再除以 L。反过来：左轮速度是 v 减 omega L/2，右轮是 v 加 omega L/2。每个轮速度目标按轮半径 0.13 米换算为 rpm，在板端用编码器反馈以 200 Hz 做 PID 闭环，Kp 5.0、Ki 0.5、Kd 为零。里程计中我积分测得的 v 和 omega：x 增加 v cos theta 乘 dt，y 增加 v sin theta 乘 dt，theta 增加 omega 乘 dt，板端 200 Hz 积分，50 Hz 发布。
**追问 1**：*What is the odometry accuracy?* A 5 metre straight run had about 2 percent distance error, and ten in-place turns accumulated about 30 degrees of heading error.
　中文：5 m 直线距离误差约 2%，原地转 10 圈航向累积误差约 30°。
**追问 2**：*How did you calibrate?* Driving 5 metres to calibrate the wheel radius and ten in-place turns to calibrate the track width, each repeated three times.
　中文：直行 5 m 标定轮半径，原地转 10 圈标定轮距，各重复三次。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*This is the CV statement: body velocity to wheel targets by differential kinematics, a PID loop with encoder feedback, and integration into odometry for teleoperated and autonomous modes.*
　中文：这就是 CV 的表述：差速运动学把机体速度转为轮目标，用编码器反馈做 PID 闭环，并积分进里程计，用于遥操作和自主模式。
**关键词与易错点**：differential drive、track width、encoder、odometry、calibration。

### Q78. How does VR teleoperation map hand motion to the end effector, and how do you avoid velocity blow-up near singularities?
**中文题意**：VR 遥操作如何把手部运动映射到末端？如何避免奇异点附近速度爆炸？
**考察点**：相对位姿映射与约束 IK。
**项目关联**：P2（核心）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> When the user presses the trigger, I record the controller pose and the end-effector pose as the reference. After that, I take the controller's displacement relative to that reference, rotate it into the robot base frame with a fixed axis-mapping matrix whose determinant is plus one, scale it, and add it to the reference end-effector pose. The same is done for orientation, as a relative rotation. The target is limited in workspace, speed and angular rate, and frozen if the data is older than 200 milliseconds. Then a constrained inverse kinematics QP in MuJoCo with Mink finds the joint velocity. **中文翻译**：用户按下扳机时，我记录手柄位姿和末端位姿作为参考。之后取手柄相对该参考的位移，用行列式为 +1 的固定轴映射矩阵旋转到机器人基座系，缩放后加到参考末端位姿上。姿态也同样作为相对旋转处理。目标在工作空间、速度和角速度上被限制，数据超过 200 ms 则冻结。然后用 MuJoCo 的 Mink 中的约束逆运动学 QP 求关节速度。奇异点附近 QP 中的阻尼使关节速度有界，再加上关节速度和位置限制，所以手臂会减速而不是乱甩。
**追问 1**：*What are the limits?* The workspace box is about plus or minus 0.4 metre, the end-effector speed 0.5 metre per second, and the angular rate 1.5 radians per second.
　中文：工作空间盒约 ±0.4 m，末端速度 0.5 m/s，角速度 1.5 rad/s。
**追问 2**：*Why relative mapping rather than absolute?* The user's hand and the robot frame do not coincide; a relative mapping with a clutch lets the operator reposition their hand comfortably.

### Q79. How does communication delay affect a teleoperation system, and what can be done?
**中文题意**：通信延迟对遥操作系统有何影响？可以怎么做？
**考察点**：延迟与稳定性。
**项目关联**：P2（约 30 ms 主从延迟）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> With a delay in the loop, a system with only a motion command from the user to the robot (unilateral teleoperation) stays stable because there is no force feedback loop closing through the delay; the delay just shows up as lag. When force feedback is added (bilateral teleoperation), the loop closes through the delay and can become unstable, even if each part is passive. The classical solutions are passivity-based methods such as wave variables, scaling down the gains, or a predictor. In my project, the delay between the leader and follower was about 30 milliseconds and the system is unilateral, with the follower tracking the leader and no force reflected. **追问 1**：*What is passivity?* A system that does not generate energy; passive systems connected in feedback are stable.
　中文：不产生能量的系统；无源系统反馈连接是稳定的。
**追问 2**：*Would you add force feedback?* It would improve the feel but needs force sensing or estimation and a stability analysis against the delay.
　中文：会改善手感，但需要力感知或估计，以及针对延迟的稳定性分析。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*I checked synchrony by overlaying leader and follower joint curves: steady-state error below about 0.02 radian, and a lag of about three control periods during motion.*
　中文：我通过叠加主从关节曲线检查同步性：稳态误差小于约 0.02 rad，运动中滞后约三个控制周期。

### Q80. What is operational-space control, and what does the null space do?
**中文题意**：什么是操作空间控制？零空间起什么作用？
**考察点**：冗余机械臂控制概念。
**项目关联**：P2（概念）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Operational-space control, from Khatib, writes the robot dynamics in the task space: the end-effector acceleration relates to the task force through an operational-space inertia matrix, Lambda, which equals the inverse of J times M inverse times J transpose. So I can design the controller directly for the end effector as if it were a free mass, and map the force to joint torques with J transpose. For a redundant arm, the control has a second part in the null space of the Jacobian: it produces joint torques that do not affect the end effector, and can pursue secondary goals such as posture or joint-limit avoidance. The projector must be dynamically consistent to avoid disturbing the primary task. I know this at the concept level.

**中文翻译**：Khatib 提出的操作空间控制，把机器人动力学写在任务空间：末端加速度通过操作空间惯量矩阵 Lambda 与任务力相联系，Lambda 等于 (J 乘 M 逆乘 J 转置) 的逆。所以我可以把末端当成自由质点直接设计控制器，再用 J 转置把力映射为关节力矩。对冗余机械臂，控制还有雅可比零空间里的第二部分：产生不影响末端的关节力矩，可追求次要目标，如姿态或避开关节限位。投影器必须动力学一致，才不会干扰主任务。这部分我是概念层面的了解。
**追问 1**：*What is the difference to kinematic null-space control?* The kinematic version projects velocities; the dynamic version projects torques with the mass-weighted pseudo-inverse.
　中文：运动学版本投影速度；动力学版本用质量加权伪逆投影力矩。
**追问 2**：*Why not use it directly in teleoperation?* The velocity-level QP IK handles limits easily and the motor-level impedance handles the dynamics.
　中文：速度层 QP 的 IK 容易处理限位，电机层阻抗处理动力学。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*I used the velocity-level approach, constrained IK as a QP, rather than operational-space torque control.*
　中文：我用的是速度层方法，即作为 QP 的约束 IK，而不是操作空间力矩控制。
**关键词与易错点**：operational space、Lambda、null space、dynamically consistent。诚实说明未用。

### Q81. How do you detect that the wheels have left the ground, and what do you do then?
**中文题意**：如何检测轮子离地？离地后怎么办？
**考察点**：接触切换的工程处理。
**项目关联**：P7（案例 C）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I estimate the ground support force from the leg force and the body acceleration: the support force is roughly the leg force minus the wheel weight plus the inertial term. When the estimated force stays below 10 newtons for 8 cycles, that is 16 milliseconds, I declare the wheels airborne. To declare landing, the force must exceed 20 newtons for 5 cycles, 10 milliseconds. These two thresholds give hysteresis and the debounce counts reject noise. In the air, the wheel torque is set to zero, because a wheel spinning freely would corrupt the speed estimate, and the position integral is frozen. On landing, the estimators are reset and the torque is faded in over about 80 milliseconds to avoid a torque spike.

**中文翻译**：我根据腿力和机身加速度估计地面支撑力：支撑力大致是腿力减轮重再加惯性项。估计力连续 8 个周期（16 ms）低于 10 N 就判为离地。判定着地需要力连续 5 个周期（10 ms）高于 20 N。两个阈值提供滞回，计数消抖抑制噪声。腾空时轮力矩置零，因为空转的轮子会污染速度估计，同时冻结位置积分。落地时重置估计器，并在约 80 ms 内渐入力矩，避免力矩尖峰。

　中文：过 3 cm 门槛后落地冲出，因为轮子空转，速度估计和位置误差都错了。
**追问 2**：*Why two thresholds?* To avoid chattering between modes when the force is near a single threshold.
　中文：避免力在单一阈值附近时模式来回切换。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*This is case C in my P7 notes; after the fix, the landing was without a torque spike. It matches the CV statement about tracing anomalies to ground-contact transitions.*
　中文：这是我 P7 笔记里的案例 C；修复后落地无力矩尖峰。它与 CV 里把异常追溯到着地切换的描述一致。

## 8. G 调试、选型与安全（Q83–Q96）

### Q83. A control loop oscillates on the real robot. How do you debug it?
**中文题意**：控制回路在真机上振荡，你怎么排查？
**考察点**：系统化调试。
**项目关联**：P1（1.5 Hz 振荡）、P2、P3、P7。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I work from the data to the cause. First, I record the signals: reference, measurement, command, and whether the command is saturated. Second, I measure the oscillation frequency, because it points to the source. A frequency near the loop crossover suggests too much gain or too little phase margin, from delay or filtering. A much lower frequency, with a command at the limit, suggests windup or saturation. A high-frequency buzz suggests noise through the derivative term, or a resonance. Third, I change one thing at a time, like a lower gain, a lower filter cutoff, or a bigger derivative term, and check the effect. Last, I add a regression test or a log check so the problem is noticed next time.

**中文翻译**：我从数据到原因。第一，记录信号：参考、测量、指令，以及指令是否饱和。第二，测量振荡频率，因为它指向来源。频率接近回路穿越频率，提示增益太大或相位裕度太小（来自延迟或滤波）；频率低得多且指令在限幅处，提示积分饱和或饱和；高频嗡嗡声提示噪声通过微分项传入或有谐振。第三，一次只改一件事，如降低增益、降低滤波截止频率或增大微分项，并检查效果。最后，添加回归测试或日志检查，使下次能发现该问题。
**追问 1**：*Example from your work?* In P1 the inner loop oscillated at about 1.5 hertz because the derivative gain was too small; raising it to about half the proportional gain removed it.
　中文：P1 内环在约 1.5 Hz 振荡，因为微分增益太小；提高到比例增益的一半左右后消除。
**追问 2**：*What if the oscillation depends on the arm pose?* Check the model change: centre of mass, inertia, or a coupling that needs scheduled gains or feedforward.
　中文：检查模型变化：质心、惯量，或需要调度增益或前馈的耦合。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*I keep a baseline of parameters and a log with each test, so I can compare with the last good state.*

### Q84. How do you commission a new controller safely on real hardware?
**中文题意**：你如何在真机上安全地调试新控制器？
**考察点**：安全流程。
**项目关联**：P1、P2、P7。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I go step by step with increasing risk. First, I check signs and directions of every sensor and actuator with the robot lifted or the motors disconnected from the load. Second, I run with low gains, half the speed limit, no load, and a person at the emergency stop. For a balancing robot, I use a gantry or a safety line. **中文翻译**：我按风险递增逐步进行。第一，在机器人悬空或电机与负载断开的情况下，检查每个传感器和执行器的符号与方向。第二，以低增益、一半速度限制、空载运行，且有人在急停旁。对平衡机器人用龙门架或安全绳。第三，在整定之前先检查安全功能，如限位、超时和跌倒检测。然后逐步提高增益并记录每一步，先调内环。最后测试扰动和边界情形：传感器丢失、链路丢失、触及限位。每一步都有通过准则，只有通过才继续。

　中文：目标增益的约 10%–20%，速度限制取一半。
**追问 2**：*What is your checklist?* Directions and signs, zero offsets, limits, timeouts, emergency stop, then the gains.
　中文：方向与符号、零偏、限位、超时、急停，然后才是增益。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 the sequence was single-axis check with the robot lifted, gantry with low gains, then ground; the emergency stop was a wireless remote plus a power contactor, with a person holding the robot.*
　中文：P1 里顺序是悬空单轴自检、龙门架低增益、再落地；急停是无线遥控加电源接触器，并有人手持机器人。

### Q85. What is system identification, and how would you identify a motor?
**中文题意**：什么是系统辨识？你如何辨识一个电机？
**考察点**：从数据得到模型。
**项目关联**：P3（电机/摩擦轮）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> System identification builds a model from measured input–output data. For a motor speed loop, I would apply a step or a chirp of voltage, record the speed, and fit a first-order model with a gain and a time constant. From the step response, the time constant is where the speed reaches 63 percent. For better data I use a rich input, like a chirp or a pseudo-random sequence, which excites many frequencies, and fit by least squares in the time or frequency domain. I validate on a different data set, not the one used for fitting. I also check the friction and the saturation, since a linear model cannot capture them.

**中文翻译**：系统辨识从测得的输入输出数据建立模型。对电机速度环，我会施加电压阶跃或扫频，记录转速，拟合带增益和时间常数的一阶模型。由阶跃响应，时间常数是转速达到 63% 的时刻。为获得更好的数据，我使用激励丰富的输入，如扫频或伪随机序列，在时域或频域用最小二乘拟合。我用不同于拟合的数据集验证。我还检查摩擦与饱和，因为线性模型无法捕捉它们。
**追问 1**：*Why validate on separate data?* To avoid overfitting, so the model predicts new behaviour.
　中文：避免过拟合，使模型能预测新行为。
**追问 2**：*Open-loop or closed-loop identification?* Open loop is simpler for stable plants; for unstable plants like a balancing robot, one must identify in closed loop.
　中文：稳定对象开环更简单；对平衡机器人这样的不稳定对象必须闭环辨识。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In my projects I mostly estimated a time constant or checked a step response rather than doing a full identification; for the arm and robot models I used the URDF and CAD values.*
　中文：我的项目主要估计时间常数或看阶跃响应，而不是完整辨识；机械臂和机器人模型用的是 URDF 和 CAD 数值。
**关键词与易错点**：system identification、chirp、least squares、validation、closed-loop identification。

### Q86. Given a new robot control problem, how do you choose the control method?
**中文题意**：给你一个新的机器人控制问题，如何选择控制方法？
**考察点**：选型框架。
**项目关联**：全部项目。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I ask five questions. Is the plant stable or unstable? How good is my model? Are there important constraints, like limits on torque or steering? Is the problem single-loop or multi-variable and coupled? And how much computation and time do I have? For a stable single-loop plant with a poor model, PID plus feedforward is the answer. For an unstable coupled plant with a decent model, like a balancing robot, state feedback or LQR. When constraints and previews matter and there is time to compute, such as path tracking for a car, MPC. For fast uncertain disturbances on a good actuator, sliding mode or a disturbance observer. And I prefer the simplest method that meets the requirement.

**中文翻译**：我问五个问题：对象稳定还是不稳定？我的模型有多好？是否有重要的约束，如力矩或转向限制？问题是单回路还是多变量耦合？有多少计算量和时间？对模型差的稳定单回路对象，答案是 PID 加前馈。对模型尚可的不稳定耦合对象（如平衡机器人），用状态反馈或 LQR。约束和预瞻重要且有时间计算时（如车的路径跟踪），用 MPC。对好执行器上快速不确定的扰动，用滑模或扰动观测器。我偏好满足需求的最简单方法。
**追问 1**：*Give a mapping to your projects.* P3 friction wheel: PID, then variable gain, then sliding mode; P1: cascaded PID; P7: gain-scheduled LQR with VMC; P5: MPC.
　中文：P3 摩擦轮：PID、变增益、滑模；P1：级联 PID；P7：增益调度 LQR 加 VMC；P5：MPC。
**追问 2**：*And when is learning-based control appropriate?* When the model is hard to obtain and data and a safe testing setup are available, with a classical safety layer around it.
　中文：模型难以获得、且有数据和安全测试条件时，并在外面加经典安全层。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*My controllers in the seven projects were chosen exactly this way, and I can give the reason for each.*
　中文：我七个项目的控制器正是这样选的，每个我都能给出理由。
**关键词与易错点**：model quality、constraints、coupling、computation budget、simplest method。

### Q87. How do you evaluate the performance of a controller?
**中文题意**：你如何评估控制器的性能？
**考察点**：指标与证据。
**项目关联**：P1、P5、P7。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I pick metrics that match the task and measure them under repeatable conditions. For regulation, I look at overshoot, settling time, steady-state error and peak error under a disturbance. For tracking, the RMS and maximum error along a trajectory, such as cross-track error for a vehicle. For effort and robustness, the peak and RMS of the command, the share of cycles at saturation, and the response to a push. I also record the timing, like cycle time and jitter. Every number comes with the condition and the number of repeats. For example, in P5 at 0.5 metre per second the mean lateral error was about 6 centimetres and the maximum about 15, and 20 of 22 full runs succeeded.

**中文翻译**：我选取与任务匹配的指标，并在可重复的条件下测量。对调节，看超调、调节时间、稳态误差和扰动下的峰值误差。对跟踪，看轨迹上的均方根和最大误差，比如车辆的横向误差。对控制量和鲁棒性，看指令的峰值与均方根、饱和周期占比，以及对推扰的响应。我还记录时序，如周期和抖动。每个数字都附带条件和重复次数。例如 P5 在 0.5 m/s 下平均横向误差约 6 cm、最大约 15 cm，22 次整程运行成功 20 次。
**追问 1**：*Why report both mean and maximum?* The mean shows typical quality, the maximum shows the worst case which matters for safety.
　中文：均值显示典型质量，最大值显示对安全重要的最坏情形。
**追问 2**：*What were the two failures?* One relocalisation error at a glass door and one timeout while turning around in a narrow place.
　中文：玻璃门处一次重定位错误，窄处调头一次超时。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*These are my P5 design-record numbers; at 0.8 metre per second the maximum error in curves was about 25 centimetres.*
　中文：这些是我 P5 的记录数字；0.8 m/s 下弯道最大误差约 25 cm。
**关键词与易错点**：RMS、maximum error、saturation share、repeatability、condition。

### Q88. In P7 you traced a balance anomaly to state inconsistency. What does that mean and how did you find it?
**中文题意**：P7 里你把平衡异常追溯到状态不一致，这是什么意思？怎么发现的？
**考察点**：调试思路，从控制律转向数据。
**项目关联**：P7（案例 A）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> State inconsistency means the controller's inputs do not describe one moment in time or one frame consistently. In my case the robot was lifted and put down, and then it crept slowly forward with a position error that kept growing. Two causes: the pitch equilibrium offset was about 1.5 degrees, a combination of the centre of mass being slightly forward and the IMU mounting angle; and the IMU and joint data were not sampled at the same instant. A small constant offset in tilt is, to the controller, a request to move. My fix was to lift the robot, read the zero pose, and calibrate the offset, and to take one snapshot of all states at the start of each cycle. After that, the static drift was less than 3 centimetres in 30 seconds.

**中文翻译**：状态不一致指控制器的输入不是在同一时刻、同一坐标系下一致地描述系统。我的案例里，机器人被吊起再放下后，缓慢向前爬行，位置误差不断增大。两个原因：俯仰平衡点偏置约 1.5 度，由质心略靠前和 IMU 安装角度共同造成；以及 IMU 与关节数据不是在同一时刻采样的。倾角上一个小的常值偏置，对控制器来说就是一个移动请求。我的修复是吊起机器人读取零位并标定偏置，并在每个周期开始时对所有状态做一次快照。此后静止 30 秒的漂移小于 3 cm。
**追问 1**：*How did you notice it was not the controller gains?* The drift was slow and always in one direction, and it vanished after calibrating the offset, without changing any gain.
　中文：漂移缓慢且方向恒定，标定偏置后消失，而没有改任何增益。
**追问 2**：*Why a snapshot?* To make sure the controller sees consistent data from one instant, not a mix of old and new samples.
　中文：确保控制器看到的是同一时刻一致的数据，而不是新旧样本的混合。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*This is the CV statement: tracing balance anomalies to state inconsistency, torque limits and ground-contact transitions.*
　中文：这就是 CV 的描述：把平衡异常追溯到状态不一致、力矩限幅和着地切换。
**关键词与易错点**：state consistency、equilibrium offset、snapshot、calibration。

### Q89. How do you deal with the gap between simulation and the real robot?

### Q90. What is coupling in a multi-variable system, and how do you handle it?
**中文题意**：多变量系统中的耦合是什么？如何处理？
**考察点**：MIMO 控制。
**项目关联**：P1（三个轮的耦合）、P3（麦轮）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Coupling means one input affects several outputs. In a ballbot, each wheel torque influences the ball's motion in a direction that mixes x, y and yaw, so tuning three wheel loops separately would not make sense. The standard approach is decoupling by a change of variables: control the physical quantities, for example the two tilt angles and yaw, and then allocate to the actuators with the kinematic inverse. In the Mecanum chassis, the body velocity commands are decoupled into wheel speeds by the inverse kinematic matrix. When the decoupling is imperfect, I check the interaction with a relative gain array or step tests on each channel, and if needed use a state-space design such as LQR that handles the whole matrix.

**中文翻译**：耦合指一个输入影响多个输出。球形机器人里，每个轮力矩影响球在混合 x、y 和 yaw 的方向上的运动，所以分别整定三个轮回路没有意义。标准做法是变量变换解耦：控制物理量（比如两个倾角和 yaw），再用运动学逆分配到执行器。麦克纳姆底盘里，机体速度指令通过逆运动学矩阵解耦成轮速。当解耦不完美时，我用相对增益阵列或对每个通道做阶跃测试检查交互，必要时使用处理整个矩阵的状态空间设计，如 LQR。
**追问 1**：*What is a relative gain array?* A matrix that shows how much a loop's gain changes when other loops are closed; values near one on the diagonal mean weak interaction.
　中文：显示其他回路闭合时某回路增益变化多少的矩阵；对角线接近 1 表示交互弱。
**追问 2**：*Why not just tune three wheel PIDs?* The wheels are not independent controllers of independent quantities; the physical coordinates are.
　中文：三个轮不是独立量的独立控制器；物理坐标才是。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 the output of the attitude loop in physical coordinates was allocated to three wheels via kinematics, as in the CV.*
　中文：P1 里姿态环在物理坐标下的输出通过运动学分配到三个轮，如 CV 所述。
**关键词与易错点**：coupling、decoupling、allocation、relative gain array、change of variables。

### Q91. How do you get a velocity estimate for control when you have only wheel encoders and an IMU?
**中文题意**：只有轮编码器和 IMU 时，如何得到控制用的速度估计？
**考察点**：估计与控制的接口。
**项目关联**：P7（一维卡尔曼）、P1（轮速低通）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Wheel speed from encoders is accurate when the wheel is rolling without slip, but noisy at low speed and wrong when the wheel slips or leaves the ground. The IMU acceleration is smooth and fast but drifts when integrated and is polluted by tilt. A one-dimensional Kalman filter fuses them: predict the velocity by integrating the acceleration, and correct it with the wheel-speed measurement. The process noise sets how much I trust the acceleration, and the measurement noise how much I trust the wheel speed. In the wheel-legged robot I used values of about 0.001 and 0.01. When the ground contact is lost, the correction is turned off. For a slow outer loop, simple low-pass filtered wheel speed is enough, as in the ballbot.

**中文翻译**：编码器给出的轮速在轮子无滑动滚动时准确，但低速时噪声大，轮子打滑或离地时错误。IMU 加速度平滑且快，但积分会漂移并受倾角污染。一维卡尔曼滤波器融合它们：用加速度积分预测速度，用轮速测量修正。过程噪声决定我对加速度的信任度，测量噪声决定我对轮速的信任度。轮腿机器人里我用了约 0.001 和 0.01 的值。失去着地时，关闭修正。对慢外环，简单的低通轮速就足够，如球形机器人那样。
**追问 1**：*What is the limitation of this estimate?* It estimates velocity relative to the ground assuming no slip; there is no absolute position reference, so position drifts.
　中文：它估计的是无滑移假设下相对地面的速度；没有绝对位置参考，所以位置漂移。
**追问 2**：*How did you limit position drift?* The position integral is clamped to plus or minus 0.3 metre in the wheel-legged robot.
　中文：轮腿机器人里位置积分限制在 ±0.3 m。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 the position estimate was wheel odometry integration with no external reference, with a drift of about 5 centimetres in 60 seconds at rest and about 3 to 5 percent of the path when moving.*
　中文：P1 的位置估计是轮里程计积分，无外部参考，静止 60 s 漂移约 5 cm，行走约路程的 3%–5%。
**关键词与易错点**：sensor fusion、one-dimensional Kalman filter、process noise、drift、slip。

### Q92. When would you replace a classical controller with a learned policy, and what safeguards would you keep?
**中文题意**：什么时候会用学习得到的策略取代经典控制器？你会保留哪些保护？
**考察点**：经典控制与学习控制的判断力（契合 PS 的"AI 如何变成物理动作"主线）。
**项目关联**：P6（安全执行链）；概念题。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I would consider a learned policy when the dynamics or the contacts are too complex to model accurately, such as legged locomotion over rough terrain or dexterous manipulation, and when I can collect enough data, in simulation or on the robot. But I would keep a classical layer around it. A policy can output unexpected actions, especially out of distribution, and it comes with no stability proof. So I would keep limits on torque and speed, rate limits, a state-validity check, a watchdog, and an emergency stop beneath the policy. That is the same logic as the safe-execution chain in my platform work: whatever produces the action, a proposal passes a policy check before execution.

**中文翻译**：当动力学或接触复杂到无法准确建模时，如崎岖地形上的足式运动或灵巧操作，且我能在仿真或机器人上收集足够数据时，我会考虑学习策略。但我会在外面保留经典层。策略可能输出意外的动作，尤其在分布之外，而且没有稳定性证明。所以我会在策略之下保留力矩与速度限制、变化率限制、状态有效性检查、看门狗和急停。这与我平台工作中的安全执行链逻辑相同：不管谁产生动作，提案都要先经过策略校验才能执行。
**追问 1**：*Can you prove a learned policy is safe?* In general not; you can combine it with a safety filter such as a control barrier function or a shield, and test extensively.
　中文：一般不能；可以结合控制障碍函数等安全滤波器或"护盾"，并大量测试。
**追问 2**：*What data do you need?* Diverse data covering the operating range, including failures.
　中文：覆盖工作范围（包括失败情形）的多样数据。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*My platform work in P6 gates every task plan through proposal, policy check, execution and state feedback; the same chain would sit between a learned policy and the actuators.*
　中文：P6 的平台工作让每个任务计划经过提案、策略校验、执行、状态回传；同样的链会放在学习策略与执行器之间。
**关键词与易错点**：learned policy、out of distribution、safety layer、control barrier function、policy check。不要说学习一定比经典好。

### Q93. How do you manage controller parameters and versions across tests?
**中文题意**：你如何跨测试管理控制器参数与版本？
**考察点**：工程纪律。
**项目关联**：P1、P2。
**English answer（口语）**：
> Controller parameters belong in configuration files with one owner, not scattered through the code. Each change to the parameters is committed together with the code that uses them, and each robot test records the commit hash, the parameter snapshot and a short log, often a rosbag. I keep a baseline from the last good run, so when something gets worse I can compare. I write down the reason for a change next to the value, because three weeks later I will not remember why a gain is 0.2. Before big changes, I tag the version. This sounds boring, but most time lost in tuning comes from not knowing what changed between a good run and a bad one.

**中文翻译**：控制器参数应放在有唯一所有者的配置文件里，而不是散落在代码中。每次参数改动与使用它的代码一同提交，每次机器人测试记录提交哈希、参数快照和简短日志（常是 rosbag）。我保留上一次良好运行的基线，变差时便于比较。我在数值旁写下改动原因，因为三周后我记不得为什么增益是 0.2。大改动前打标签。听起来枯燥，但整定中大部分时间损失来自不知道好运行与坏运行之间改了什么。
**追问 1**：*What do you record per test?* The git hash, the parameter file, the date and the conditions, and a bag file with the key signals.
　中文：git 哈希、参数文件、日期与条件，以及含关键信号的 bag 文件。
**追问 2**：*Do you keep a regression test?* A short scripted test of the main behaviours, which cut a regression from about fifteen minutes to one in P1.
　中文：主要行为的简短脚本化测试，在 P1 里把一次回归从约 15 分钟缩短到 1 分钟。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*In P1 the parameter YAML files and code were committed together with a rosbag baseline; in P2 each real-robot test got a tag and a configuration hash.*
　中文：P1 里参数 YAML 与代码一同提交并附 rosbag 基线；P2 里每次真机测试都打标签并记录配置哈希。
**关键词与易错点**：configuration、commit hash、baseline、regression test、rosbag。

### Q94. Which control result are you proudest of, and why?
**中文题意**：你最自豪的控制成果是什么？为什么？
**考察点**：叙事、有证据的自豪点。
**项目关联**：P1 / P3 / P7（任选其一）。
**English answer（口语）**：
> I am most proud of the centre-of-mass feedforward in the ballbot, because it was a clean example of using a model to explain and then fix a problem. When the arms moved, the robot tilted and drifted. I used the URDF and Pinocchio to estimate the centre-of-mass offset from the arm joints, and fed a roll and pitch feedforward into the balance loop. To prove it worked, I toggled it on and off under the same fixed arm trajectory, five times each. The peak tilt fell from about 0.06 to about 0.026 radian, and the 30-second drift from about 0.3 to 0.1 metres. I also found a sign error through the same on/off test. The result is modest, but it is measured and explained.

**中文翻译**：我最自豪的是球形机器人的质心前馈，因为它是用模型解释并解决问题的干净例子。手臂运动时机器人会倾斜并漂移。我用 URDF 和 Pinocchio 根据手臂关节估计质心偏移，并把 roll 和 pitch 前馈加入平衡回路。为证明有效，我在同样的固定手臂轨迹下开关它，各重复五次。倾角峰值从约 0.06 降到约 0.026 rad，30 秒漂移从约 0.3 降到 0.1 m。我还通过同样的开关测试发现过符号错误。结果不算夸张，但是有测量、有解释。
**追问 1**：*How was the offset estimated?* Four joints per arm took part in the centre-of-mass computation, with the root at the ball centre, and the model mass was checked against the weighed total within about 5 percent.
　中文：每臂 4 个关节参与质心计算，根坐标在球心，模型质量与称重总质量核对在约 5% 以内。
**追问 2**：*What if both arms move asymmetrically?* The roll direction showed a bias and saturation; I restricted that pose range and lowered the speed, and calibrated the roll feedforward gain separately.
　中文：roll 方向出现偏置和饱和；我限制了该姿态范围、降低速度，并单独标定 roll 前馈增益。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*I know this result is a bench comparison on one fixed trajectory, and I say so.*
　中文：我知道这个结果是在一条固定轨迹上的台架对比，我会如实说明。
**关键词与易错点**：centre-of-mass feedforward、toggle test、peak tilt、drift。数字是统一设计取值，用 about。

### Q95. If you had more time on your ballbot or wheel-legged project, what would you improve?
**中文题意**：如果有更多时间，你会改进球形机器人或轮腿项目的哪些方面？
**考察点**：反思与研究兴趣。
**项目关联**：P1 / P7。
**English answer（口语）**：
> For the ballbot, I would bring the LQR design from an analysis tool into a tested controller on the real robot, starting with the inner loops in a safe setup, and compare it against the cascaded PID under the same arm trajectories. I would also improve the state estimate with a better fusion than wheel odometry alone. For the wheel-legged robot, I would try a model predictive controller for the leg-length and jump transitions, where constraints matter. In both cases, I would aim for a better model by identifying the real inertia and friction, and use that to schedule or adapt the gains. The goal is the same: make the model explain the robot more accurately, so tuning is less trial and error.

**中文翻译**：对球形机器人，我会把 LQR 设计从分析工具推进到真机上经过测试的控制器，先在安全设置下做内环，并在同样的手臂轨迹下与级联 PID 比较。我还会用比单纯轮里程计更好的融合来改进状态估计。对轮腿机器人，我会在约束重要的腿长和跳跃过渡上尝试模型预测控制。两种情况下我都会通过辨识真实惯量和摩擦来改进模型，并用它调度或自适应增益。目标相同：让模型更准确地解释机器人，使整定少一些试错。
**追问 1**：*What would be the main risk of deploying LQR on the ballbot?* The wheel interface is a speed command, not a torque, so the model needs an actuator layer, and saturation would need careful handling.
　中文：轮接口是速度指令而不是力矩，所以模型需要执行器层，且饱和需要谨慎处理。
**追问 2**：*How would you judge success?* Same trajectories, same metrics: peak tilt, drift, saturation share, repeated several times.
　中文：同样的轨迹、同样的指标：倾角峰值、漂移、饱和占比，重复多次。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*This links to my goal for the master's: to deepen the modelling and learning side of robot control.*
　中文：这与我读研的目标相连：加深机器人控制中建模与学习的部分。
**关键词与易错点**：model-based control、system identification、comparison、next step。这是计划，不是已完成工作。

### Q96. How could control theory and learning be combined in robots?
**中文题意**：控制理论与学习如何在机器人中结合？
**考察点**：研究视野（契合 MSc AI/Embodied Intelligence 方向）。
**项目关联**：P6、P1 + P4 的经验。
**English answer（口语）**：
> There are several ways. Learning can tune the parameters of a controller, like MPC weights or PID gains, automatically. Learning can estimate or correct a model, such as friction or aerodynamic terms, which a model-based controller then uses. A learned policy can provide a high-level reference or a residual on top of a classical controller, so the classical part keeps stability while the learned part adds performance. Safety filters like control barrier functions can constrain a learned policy. And MPC can be used with learned dynamics or a learned cost. My own background is on the practical side: I have built classical controllers and an agent decision chain with strict execution gates, and I want to study the theory behind combining them.

**中文翻译**：有几种方式。学习可以自动整定控制器的参数，如 MPC 权重或 PID 增益。学习可以估计或修正模型（如摩擦项），再由基于模型的控制器使用。学习策略可以提供高层参考，或在经典控制器之上提供残差，经典部分保持稳定性，学习部分增加性能。控制障碍函数等安全滤波器可以约束学习策略。MPC 也可以与学习的动力学或学习的代价结合。我自己的背景偏实践：我做过经典控制器和带严格执行闸门的智能体决策链，我想研究把它们结合起来的理论基础。
**追问 1**：*What is a residual policy?* The policy outputs a correction added to a baseline controller's command.
　中文：策略输出一个修正量，加到基线控制器的指令上。
**追问 2**：*What are the risks?* Distribution shift, no stability guarantee, sample inefficiency, and difficulty verifying safety.
　中文：分布偏移、没有稳定性保证、样本效率低、安全性难以验证。
**如果追问到你的项目**：*My PS says my machine learning experience is mostly practical and that I want to build the theoretical foundation; this question is exactly the direction I want to study.*
　中文：我的 PS 说我的机器学习经验以实践为主，想补理论基础；这个问题正是我想研究的方向。
**关键词与易错点**：residual policy、safety filter、learned dynamics、MPC with learning、distribution shift。
