## 1. 本文件怎么用

1. **按你被邀请面试的项目找到对应部分**。每部分开头有“项目一页要点”（只写有依据的信息）、“面试形式与证据”和“面试前核对清单”，请先读。
2. **每个项目 25 题**，分五类：A 项目匹配度与动机（约 5 题），B 课程与研究方向（约 4 题），C 该校面试形式可能出现的题（约 3 题），D 技术考点预测（约 9 题），E 项目深挖与收尾（约 4 题）。
3. **英文回答 90–120 词**，是示范而不是台词。请先读、再合上书用自己的话说；说不下去的地方回到 [07_英语学习](../../07_英语学习/README.md) 查词句。
4. **官方信息的表述规则**（重要）：凡课程名、学分、面试形式等不能 100% 确认的内容，回答里一律使用 “From the programme page, my understanding is …”、“As far as I know …”、“I will check the official page before the interview.” 一类说法；每部分的“面试前核对清单”写明要核对什么。**不编造课程名、教授名、研究组名**。
5. **事实规则**：项目数字取自各项目 README 的实机细节自查表；用 about / roughly；不记得就用诚实模板。P1 的 LQR 是分析工具而非部署；P3 控制任务约 3 ms，不说 1 kHz；3V3 二等奖和全国赛三等奖是团队成绩；P5 用 ROS2 Humble；P7 是经典 CAN；P6 的 IMU 为 100 Hz；P4 是 YOLOv5/RKNN INT8；读码发现的隐患不是你的贡献。
6. **与其他题库的关系**：通用的“为什么这个项目”见 [02 题库](./02_题库_为什么这个项目_学校_地区.md)；控制、运动学、规划、ML 的技术深挖见 [11](./11_题库_控制理论与机器人控制.md)、[12](./12_题库_运动学_动力学_状态估计_SLAM.md)、[13](./13_题库_规划_导航_感知.md)、[14](./14_题库_机器学习_深度学习_强化学习_大模型.md)；CS 基础见 [15](./15_题库_嵌入式_RTOS_通信_软件_CS基础.md)。

## 2. 答题通用规则：官方信息怎么说、技术回答怎么答

| 情况 | 怎么说 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 课程名没有 100% 把握 | 只说方向词，加“我了解到” | “From the programme page, my understanding is that there is a course on robot manipulation.” |
| 面试形式不确定 | 不预设；被问到时说准备了几种 | “I have prepared for both a technical conversation and a written test.” |
| 被问学校排名/比较 | 不比较，只谈匹配 | “I focus on how the programme fits my gap.” |
| 技术题不会 | 先说边界，再给推理，再问对方 | “I haven’t used it directly, but from the principle I would expect … Is that along the right lines?” |
| 数字记不清 | 诚实模板 | “I don’t remember the exact number, but roughly …” |
| 被问是否申请了其他学校 | 诚实、不排名 | “Yes, I applied to several programmes in the region; this one fits my plan well.” |

> **面试要点**：评委不会因为你“知道很多课程名”给高分，但会因为你说错课程名或编造信息扣分。安全的做法是：知道的说清，不确定的明说“我会再核对”。

## 3. 第一部分：港中深 MSc in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics（Q1–Q25）

### 3.1 项目一页要点与面试形式

| 项 | 内容（来源与等级） |
|---|---|
| 项目 | MSc in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics（MAIR），港中深；2 年；36 units；AI 与机器人两个方向（官方核对表） |
| 课程方向（只用方向词） | 机器人操作（Robot Manipulation）、机器人数学基础（Math Fundamentals for Robotics）；可选的优化、强化学习、移动机器人、控制等方向（PS 引用，**课程名面试前以官网核对**） |
| 评估形式 | 官方：材料初审后按邮件通知评估；**不能预设为面试**。二手：可能是真人群面或单面；唯一一份一手面经（2023fall）有 6 个环节：自我介绍、专业题与毕设、围绕材料的提问（本科学校等）、对 AI 未来的看法、职业规划、反问（见 [真题调研](../00_港硕面试真题调研.md) 1.2） |
| 语言 | 全英文准备；中介称自我介绍后可能转中文，按全英文答，听到中文问题也先用英文回答，除非对方要求 |
| 与你的匹配 | PS 主线“AI 决策如何变成物理动作”；P1/P7/P5 的控制与导航；P2 的操作与逆运动学；P4/P6 的 AI 落地 |

**面试前核对清单**：① 评估到底是口试、笔试还是在线测试（以邮件为准）；② Robotics 方向 2027 年的必修与选修名单；③ 你同时申请 MAIR 与 CS 两个项目，材料口径要一致（见 Q26）。

**A. 项目匹配度与动机（Q1–Q5）**

### Q1. Please introduce yourself and your background in robotics and AI.

**中文题意**：请介绍你自己，以及你在机器人和 AI 方面的背景。
**考察点**：自我介绍与项目匹配（2023 面经的第一个环节）。
**English answer（约 115 词）**：My name is Yongyi Lai. I am a computer science student at Shenzhen Technology University, ranked sixth out of 156. My main work is in robot control and navigation on real hardware. At AIRS, I built the balance controller of a ballbot with two arms. In a lab project, I built a navigation chain for a car-like robot, from localisation to planning to tracking. At Kaihong, I am working on a safe-execution chain, which checks AI task plans before they become robot actions. My machine learning experience is mostly practical, for example a YOLOv5 model on an edge chip. So I want a programme that combines AI and robotics with solid foundations, and I think this one does.
**中文翻译**：我叫赖咏怡，深圳技术大学计算机专业学生，排名 156 人中第六。我的主要工作是在真实硬件上的机器人控制与导航。在 AIRS，我为带双臂的球形机器人做了平衡控制器。在一个实验室项目里，我为车式机器人做了导航链，从定位、规划到跟踪。在深开鸿，我在做一条安全执行链，在 AI 任务计划变成机器人动作之前先做检查。我的机器学习经验主要是实践，例如边缘芯片上的 YOLOv5 模型。所以我想要一个把 AI 和机器人与扎实基础结合起来的项目，我认为这个项目做到了。
**追问 1**：What do you mean by "practical" machine learning? — I trained and deployed a detector, but I have less experience in the theory behind learning algorithms. *（中文：“实践型”机器学习指什么？— 我训练并部署过检测器，但对学习算法背后的理论经验较少。）*
**追问 2**：Which part of AI interests you most? — How learned decisions become safe physical actions. *（中文：AI 的哪部分最让你感兴趣？— 学习得到的决策如何变成安全的物理动作。）*
**关键词与易错点**：practical ML、decision to action；不要夸大学习经验；不要把全国赛三等奖说成个人奖。

### Q2. Why do you choose a programme that combines AI and robotics, rather than a pure AI or pure robotics one?

**中文题意**：为什么选择把 AI 与机器人结合的项目，而不是纯 AI 或纯机器人项目？
**考察点**：动机与项目定位的匹配。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：My work sits exactly at the joint. On the robotics side, I have built controllers and navigation on real robots. On the AI side, I have used a vision model and a decision chain, and my statement asks how an AI decision becomes a physical action. A pure robotics programme would give me less on learning, and a pure AI programme would give me less on hardware and control. From the programme page, my understanding is that this programme has both an AI stream and a robotics stream, and it has mathematics for robotics, which is the theory I need. I will check the exact courses on the official page before the interview.
**中文翻译**：我的工作恰好处在这个交界处。机器人方面，我在真实机器人上做过控制器和导航。AI 方面，我用过视觉模型和决策链，我的陈述问的是 AI 决策如何变成物理动作。纯机器人项目在学习方面给我的少，纯 AI 项目在硬件和控制方面给我的少。就我从项目页面了解的，这个项目既有 AI 方向又有机器人方向，还有机器人数学，这正是我需要的理论。面试前我会在官网核对具体课程。
**追问 1**：Which stream would you pick? — Robotics, with AI courses as electives. *（中文：你选哪个方向？— 机器人方向，AI 课程作选修。）*
**追问 2**：Why not only study AI? — Because I enjoy systems where decisions have physical consequences. *（中文：为什么不只学 AI？— 因为我喜欢决策有物理后果的系统。）*
**关键词与易错点**：joint of AI and robotics、both streams；课程名不确定时只说“我了解到”；不要贬低纯 AI 或纯机器人项目。

### Q3. Why CUHK-Shenzhen? Why not a programme in Hong Kong?

**中文题意**：为什么选港中深，而不是香港本部的项目？
**考察点**：地点与学校选择的务实理由。
**English answer（约 100 词）**：There are practical and academic reasons. Practically, I have built my robotics experience in Shenzhen and I want to work in the Greater Bay Area, so studying in Shenzhen lets me keep my internship contacts and learn from the local industry. Academically, I want a programme that is designed for AI and robotics together, and as far as I know this one is jointly run by two schools. I applied to several programmes, including some in Hong Kong, and I judge each by its fit with my plan. For this one, the fit is the combination of AI and robotics with strong foundations.
**中文翻译**：有务实和学术两方面的原因。务实上，我的机器人经验是在深圳积累的，我想在大湾区工作，在深圳学习能让我保持实习联系并了解本地产业。学术上，我想要为 AI 和机器人共同设计的项目，据我所知这个项目由两个学院联合开设。我申请了包括香港在内的几个项目，并根据与我计划的匹配程度评判每一个。对这个项目来说，匹配点是 AI 与机器人的结合以及扎实的基础。
**追问 1**：Did you apply to Hong Kong programmes as well? — Yes, I did, and I would be glad to accept a place here. *（中文：也申请了香港的项目吗？— 是的，也乐意接受这里的名额。）*
**追问 2**：What do you know about Longgang or the campus? — Only a little from the website; I would visit before enrolling. *（中文：对龙岗校区了解多少？— 只从网站上了解一点，入学前会去参观。）*
**关键词与易错点**：Greater Bay Area、jointly run（据官方核对表为数据科学学院 + 理工学院）；不要评价校园细节；不要说“Shenzhen is easier”。

### Q4. Your university is not one of the top-ranked ones. Why should we consider you?

**中文题意**：你的大学不是顶尖排名的学校，为什么我们要考虑你？
**考察点**：背景问题的应对（2023 面经里被问到本科学校）。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：I understand the concern, and I would like to be judged on what I have done. My university is less famous, but I am ranked sixth out of 156 in my programme, with an average score of 87.63. More importantly, I have worked on seven robotics and embedded projects, mostly validated on real hardware, and I reached the national final of RoboMaster with my team. **中文翻译**：我理解这个顾虑，我希望被评价的是我做过的事。我的学校知名度较低，但我在专业里排名 156 人中第六，平均分 87.63。更重要的是，我做过七个机器人和嵌入式项目，多数在真实硬件上验证，并与团队进入了 RoboMaster 全国赛。我抓住了身边的机会：竞赛战队、导师的实验室，以及深圳的实习。我知道我的理论比来自更强大学的学生弱，所以我计划提前准备，并从第一个学期起努力。
**追问 1**：How will you close the theory gap? — Linear algebra, probability and state-space control, with exercises tied to my projects. *（中文：怎么补理论缺口？— 线性代数、概率和状态空间控制，并做与项目相关的练习。）*
**追问 2**：Is grade 87.63 good in your university? — It is the average, and it puts me at 6 of 156, about the top 4 percent. *（中文：87.63 在你们学校算好吗？— 这是平均分，位列 156 人中第 6，约前 4%。）*
**关键词与易错点**：be judged on what I did、6 of 156、top 4%；不要抱怨学校；不要说“my university is bad”；排名统一用 6/156。

### Q7. Which concentration would you choose, AI or Robotics, and why?

**中文题意**：你会选 AI 还是机器人方向，为什么？

At the same time, I want enough machine learning, including reinforcement learning, to understand how learned policies can be combined with control. **中文翻译**：我会选机器人方向，并用选修课覆盖学习方面。我的经验在控制、状态估计和导航，在项目中我想深化操作及其背后的数学，如运动学和优化。同时我想要足够的机器学习，包括强化学习，以理解学习得到的策略如何与控制结合。如果 AI 方向能给我更强的学习基础，我也会考虑。最终选择我会在与课程负责人了解两个方向的安排之后再定。
**追问 1**：What would you not take? — Courses far from my plan, such as pure language processing. *（中文：你不会选什么？— 离我计划很远的课，比如纯自然语言处理。）*
**追问 2**：Do you have experience in manipulation? — Yes, in dual-arm teleoperation and teaching at Future Power, using constrained inverse kinematics and gravity compensation. *（中文：有操作经验吗？— 有，在未来动力做过双臂遥操作和示教，用了带约束的逆运动学和重力补偿。）*
**关键词与易错点**：choose and justify；留有余地；不要贬低 NLP。

### Q8. What would you like to research or build in this programme?

**中文题意**：你想在项目里研究或做出什么？
**考察点**：研究兴趣的具体程度。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：I would like to work on how learned decisions can be executed safely on a robot. In practice, I would start from a manipulation or mobile task, use a learned module for perception or decision, and keep a classical controller and a checking layer around it. ### Q17. How does backpropagation work, in simple terms?

### Q19. Explain the Kalman filter in simple terms.

**中文题意**：简单解释卡尔曼滤波。
**考察点**：状态估计理解（P5）。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：A Kalman filter keeps an estimate of the state and how uncertain it is. In each step, it first predicts the new state with a motion model, and the uncertainty grows. Then it receives a measurement and corrects the estimate. The correction depends on the Kalman gain, which weighs the prediction and the measurement by their uncertainties: if the sensor is accurate, we trust it more. For a nonlinear system, the extended Kalman filter linearises the model around the current estimate. In my navigation robot, I fused wheel speed and gyro with an EKF, and used AMCL for map-based localisation with LiDAR.
**中文翻译**：卡尔曼滤波维护状态估计及其不确定性。每一步先用运动模型预测新状态，不确定性增大。然后收到测量值并修正估计。修正量取决于卡尔曼增益，它按不确定性给预测和测量加权：传感器准确，就更信任它。对非线性系统，扩展卡尔曼滤波在当前估计附近对模型做线性化。在我的导航机器人中，我用 EKF 融合轮速和陀螺，并用 AMCL 结合激光做基于地图的定位。
**追问 1**：What are Q and R in the filter? — Process noise covariance and measurement noise covariance. *（中文：滤波里的 Q 和 R 是什么？— 过程噪声协方差和测量噪声协方差。）*
**追问 2**：What happens if timestamps are wrong? — The measurement is applied to the wrong state, causing jumps and drift. *（中文：时间戳错了会怎样？— 测量被用到错误的状态上，造成跳变和漂移。）*
**关键词与易错点**：predict–correct、Kalman gain、EKF；用 “fused wheel speed and gyro”（P5 自查表）；不要说 EKF 是你自己写的实现（用的是 robot_localization 的 ekf_node）。

### Q20. How would you represent orientation in 3D? What are the pros and cons?

**中文题意**：三维姿态如何表示？各有什么优缺点？
**考察点**：机器人数学基础。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：There are three common ways. A rotation matrix is a three by three orthogonal matrix. It is easy to compose and to apply to vectors, but it has nine numbers for three degrees of freedom. Euler angles use three numbers and are intuitive, but they have gimbal lock and discontinuities, so they are not good for computation. A unit quaternion uses four numbers, has no gimbal lock, and is compact and good for interpolation and filtering, but it is less intuitive and has a double cover, since q and minus q are the same rotation. In my IMU processing and robot models, I use quaternions or matrices inside, and convert to Euler angles for display.
**中文翻译**：有三种常见方式。旋转矩阵是 3×3 正交矩阵，易于组合并作用于向量，但用九个数表示三个自由度。欧拉角用三个数，直观，但有万向节锁和不连续，不适合计算。单位四元数用四个数，没有万向节锁，紧凑且适合插值和滤波，但不直观，并且有双重覆盖，即 q 与 −q 是同一旋转。在我的 IMU 处理和机器人模型里，内部用四元数或矩阵，显示时转成欧拉角。

**追问 2**：How do you keep a rotation matrix valid? — Re-orthonormalise it from time to time. *（中文：怎么保持旋转矩阵有效？— 定期重新正交化。）*
**关键词与易错点**：orthogonal、gimbal lock、double cover；不要说“Euler angles are wrong”，要说在哪不适合。

### Q21. How does YOLO detect objects, and what did INT8 quantisation change in your project?

**中文题意**：YOLO 如何检测物体？INT8 量化在你的项目里改变了什么？
**考察点**：深度学习理解与边缘部署实践（P4）。
**English answer（约 110 词）**：YOLO is a single-stage detector. A convolutional network looks at the whole image once and predicts, on a grid at several scales, boxes, an objectness score and class probabilities. Then non-maximum suppression removes duplicate boxes. In my gardening project, I used YOLOv5s with three classes, trained on my own plant images. I converted it to an INT8 model with the RKNN toolkit for the RK3588 chip. Quantisation makes the model smaller and faster on the NPU, but costs some accuracy; in my case, mAP at 0.5 went from about 0.86 to about 0.83. The model ran at about 15 frames per second end to end.
**中文翻译**：YOLO 是单阶段检测器。卷积网络只看一次整幅图像，在多个尺度的网格上预测边框、目标性分数和类别概率，然后用非极大值抑制去除重复框。在园艺项目里，我用 YOLOv5s，三个类别，用自己的植物图像训练。我用 RKNN 工具把它转成 INT8 模型，部署到 RK3588 芯片。量化让模型在 NPU 上更小更快，但损失一些精度；我这里 mAP@0.5 从约 0.86 降到约 0.83。端到端约 15 帧每秒。
**追问 1**：What is non-maximum suppression? — Keep the highest-scoring box and remove overlapping ones above an IoU threshold. *（中文：什么是非极大值抑制？— 保留得分最高的框，去除与之 IoU 超过阈值的重叠框。）*
**追问 2**：How did you choose the calibration data? — About 200 images covering day, cloudy, supplemental light and night. *（中文：校准数据怎么选？— 约 200 张，覆盖白天、阴天、补光和夜间。）*
**关键词与易错点**：single-stage、NMS、INT8；模型训练在 PC GPU，不是在 RK3588 上；数字取自 P4 自查表，说 about。

### Q23. How would you combine learning with classical control in a robot?

### Q24. Tell me about the safe-execution chain at Kaihong. Why is it needed?

**中文题意**：讲讲深开鸿的安全执行链。为什么需要它？
**考察点**：PS 主线的核心，项目与 AI 方向的接口。
**English answer（约 115 词）**：The idea is that a high-level plan, from a scheduler, an operator or an AI planner, must never go directly to the robot. **中文翻译**：思路是：来自调度器、操作员或 AI 规划器的高层计划，绝不能直接发给机器人。首先它变成带标识、来源、参数和有效期的动作提案。然后策略检查看格式、权限、允许的动作、有效期、模式、机器人状态新鲜度和数值范围。通过后提案被执行，机器人状态与确认被回传。每个决策都带原因码记录。需要它是因为计划可能出错、过期或越界，而机器人在物理世界行动。我可以讲设计，但不讲内部细节。

### Q36. Compare Dijkstra, A* and BFS. What are their time complexities?

**中文题意**：比较 Dijkstra、A* 和 BFS，时间复杂度如何？
**考察点**：算法基础（与导航规划联系）。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：BFS finds shortest paths in an unweighted graph by expanding level by level; its time is order V plus E. Dijkstra works with non-negative edge weights, using a priority queue; with a binary heap its time is order E log V. A* is Dijkstra with a heuristic: it expands nodes by cost so far plus an estimate of the remaining cost. If the heuristic is admissible, never overestimating, A* finds an optimal path, usually exploring far fewer nodes. In my navigation project, the global planner is a Hybrid-A*, which is A* on a grid with a continuous heading and kinematically feasible motions.
**中文翻译**：BFS 在无权图中逐层扩展找最短路径，时间为 O(V+E)。Dijkstra 适用于非负边权，使用优先队列，二叉堆实现时间为 O(E log V)。A* 是带启发函数的 Dijkstra：按已走代价加剩余代价估计来扩展节点。如果启发函数可采纳（不高估），A* 找到最优路径，通常探索的节点少得多。在我的导航项目里，全局规划是 Hybrid-A*，即在栅格上、带连续航向和运动学可行动作的 A*。
**追问 1**：What if the heuristic overestimates? — The path may not be optimal. *（中文：启发函数高估会怎样？— 路径可能不是最优的。）*
**追问 2**：Dijkstra with negative weights? — It can fail; Bellman–Ford handles negative edges. *（中文：负权边怎么办？— Dijkstra 可能失败；Bellman–Ford 可处理负边。）*
**关键词与易错点**：priority queue、admissible heuristic；复杂度记 O(E log V)；Hybrid-A* 的实现细节参见 [P5](../../01_项目课程/P5_车式移动机器人自主导航/README.md)。

### Q37. Explain how a hash table works and what happens with collisions.

**中文题意**：解释哈希表如何工作，以及冲突如何处理。
**考察点**：数据结构。
**English answer（约 100 词）**：A hash table maps keys to array positions using a hash function, so lookup, insert and delete are on average constant time. Two different keys can map to the same position, which is a collision. Two common ways to handle it are chaining, where each position holds a list of entries, and open addressing, where we probe for another free position, for example linearly. When the table gets full, the load factor rises and performance drops, so we resize and rehash. The worst case is linear time if many keys collide. A good hash function spreads keys evenly. In embedded systems, I avoid dynamic resizing in real-time code, because it is hard to bound.
**中文翻译**：哈希表用哈希函数把键映射到数组位置，所以查找、插入和删除平均为常数时间。两个不同的键可能映射到同一位置，即冲突。常见处理有链地址法，每个位置存一个条目链表；以及开放寻址，探测另一个空位，例如线性探测。表变满时负载因子上升、性能下降，所以要扩容并重新哈希。如果很多键冲突，最坏情况是线性时间。好的哈希函数让键分布均匀。在嵌入式系统里，我在实时代码中避免动态扩容，因为难以保证上界。
**追问 1**：What is the load factor? — The number of entries divided by the number of buckets. *（中文：什么是负载因子？— 条目数除以桶数。）*
**追问 2**：Why avoid it in real-time code? — Resizing takes unbounded time and may allocate memory. *（中文：为什么实时代码避免？— 扩容耗时没有上界，还可能分配内存。）*
**关键词与易错点**：chaining、open addressing、load factor；平均 O(1)、最坏 O(n)。

### Q38. Explain quicksort and its complexity. When does it perform badly?

**中文题意**：解释快速排序及其复杂度。什么时候表现差？
**考察点**：排序算法。
**English answer（约 100 词）**：Quicksort picks a pivot, partitions the array into elements smaller and larger than the pivot, and then sorts the two parts recursively. On average the time is order n log n, because the partitions are reasonably balanced. In the worst case, when the pivot is always the smallest or largest element, for example on an already sorted array with a naive pivot choice, it is order n squared. Choosing a random pivot or the median of three reduces the risk. It sorts in place, with order log n extra space for recursion on average, and it is not stable. Merge sort guarantees n log n and is stable, but needs extra memory.
**中文翻译**：快速排序选一个枢轴，把数组分成小于和大于枢轴的两部分，然后递归排序两部分。平均时间为 O(n log n)，因为划分比较均衡。最坏情况是枢轴总是最小或最大元素，例如对已排序数组且枢轴选择朴素时，为 O(n²)。随机枢轴或三数取中可降低风险。它原地排序，平均递归需要 O(log n) 额外空间，不稳定。归并排序保证 O(n log n) 且稳定，但需要额外内存。
**追问 1**：How do you make it safer? — Randomised pivot and switching to heapsort when recursion is too deep. *（中文：怎么更安全？— 随机枢轴，递归过深时切换到堆排序。）*
**追问 2**：Which sort for nearly sorted data? — Insertion sort is fast on nearly sorted input. *（中文：近乎有序的数据用什么？— 插入排序在近乎有序时很快。）*
**关键词与易错点**：pivot、partition、worst case O(n²)；不要说“quicksort is always the fastest”。

### Q39. What is dynamic programming? Give an example.

**中文题意**：什么是动态规划？举个例子。
**考察点**：算法思想。
**English answer（约 100 词）**：Dynamic programming solves a problem by breaking it into overlapping subproblems and storing their results, so each is solved only once. It needs optimal substructure: the best solution contains best solutions of subproblems. A classic example is the number of ways to climb stairs, or the minimum cost path in a grid: the cost at a cell equals its own cost plus the minimum of the cost from above and from the left. The table has n times m entries, so the time is order n times m. Compared with naive recursion, which repeats work exponentially, dynamic programming is polynomial. I have mostly used the idea in graph search, where costs-so-far are reused.
**中文翻译**：动态规划把问题拆成重叠的子问题并存储结果，每个子问题只解一次。它需要最优子结构：最优解包含子问题的最优解。经典例子有爬楼梯的方法数，或网格中的最小代价路径：某格的代价等于其自身代价加上来自上方和左方的最小代价。表有 n×m 项，时间为 O(nm)。与重复计算呈指数增长的朴素递归相比，动态规划是多项式的。我主要在图搜索中使用这种思想，即复用已累计的代价。
**追问 1**：What is memoisation? — Caching the results of a recursive function. *（中文：什么是记忆化？— 缓存递归函数的结果。）*
**追问 2**：Top-down vs bottom-up? — Top-down uses recursion with a cache; bottom-up fills the table in order. *（中文：自顶向下与自底向上？— 前者递归加缓存，后者按顺序填表。）*
**关键词与易错点**：overlapping subproblems、optimal substructure；给出一个能说清状态转移的例子。

### Q40. What is the difference between a process and a thread?

**中文题意**：进程和线程有什么区别？
**考察点**：操作系统（课程 Operating Systems）。
**English answer（约 95 词）**：A process is an instance of a running program with its own address space and resources, like open files. A thread is a unit of execution inside a process; threads of the same process share the address space, which makes communication cheap but also requires synchronisation. Creating and switching between threads is lighter than between processes. A crash in one process normally does not affect another, but a bad thread can bring down the whole process. In my robot software, I use separate processes for isolation, for example for policy checking, and threads inside a node for concurrent I/O.
**中文翻译**：进程是运行程序的实例，有自己的地址空间和资源，如打开的文件。线程是进程内的执行单元；同一进程的线程共享地址空间，通信廉价，但需要同步。创建和切换线程比进程轻量。一个进程崩溃通常不影响另一个，但一个出错的线程可能拖垮整个进程。在我的机器人软件里，我用独立进程做隔离，例如策略检查，用节点内的线程处理并发 I/O。
**追问 1**：What is context switching? — Saving one task's state and restoring another's so the CPU can run it. *（中文：什么是上下文切换？— 保存一个任务的状态并恢复另一个，使 CPU 能运行它。）*
**追问 2**：How do processes communicate? — Pipes, sockets, shared memory and message queues. *（中文：进程如何通信？— 管道、套接字、共享内存和消息队列。）*
**关键词与易错点**：address space、isolation；P6 中策略校验是独立进程是“设计级描述”，不要透露内部细节。

### Q41. What are the four conditions for deadlock, and how do you avoid it?

**中文题意**：死锁的四个必要条件是什么？如何避免？
**考察点**：并发基础。
**English answer（约 100 词）**：The four conditions are mutual exclusion, hold and wait, no preemption, and circular wait. A deadlock needs all four. To avoid it, break one condition. The simplest in practice is to impose a global order on locks, so that every thread acquires them in the same order; this breaks circular wait. Another is to use timeouts or try-lock, so a thread does not wait forever. Or hold fewer locks at a time, and keep critical sections short. In embedded code I try not to call blocking functions while holding a lock, and I keep the data shared between tasks small and well-defined.
**中文翻译**：四个条件是互斥、持有并等待、不可抢占和循环等待。死锁需要四个同时成立。避免时打破其一。实践中最简单的是对锁规定全局顺序，使每个线程按同样顺序获取，这就打破了循环等待。另一种是用超时或 try-lock，使线程不会永远等待。或者一次少持有锁，并让临界区保持很短。在嵌入式代码里，我尽量不在持锁时调用阻塞函数，并让任务间共享的数据小而明确。
**追问 1**：What is priority inversion? — A low-priority task holds a lock needed by a high-priority task; priority inheritance helps. *（中文：什么是优先级反转？— 低优先级任务持有高优先级任务需要的锁；优先级继承可缓解。）*
**追问 2**：Livelock vs deadlock? — In livelock, threads keep changing state but make no progress. *（中文：活锁与死锁？— 活锁中线程不断改变状态却无进展。）*
**关键词与易错点**：four conditions、lock ordering；不要把死锁与饥饿混为一谈。

### Q42. What is the difference between a mutex and a semaphore? When did you use them?

**中文题意**：互斥锁和信号量有什么区别？你在哪里用过？
**考察点**：同步原语（FreeRTOS 经验）。
**English answer（约 100 词）**：A mutex protects a critical section: only one task holds it at a time, and the same task that locks it should unlock it. A semaphore is a counter used for signalling or for limiting access to a number of resources. A binary semaphore is often used to signal from an interrupt handler to a task, for example "new data is ready". A counting semaphore tracks several available resources. In FreeRTOS, mutexes also support priority inheritance, which helps against priority inversion. In my embedded work, I use queues and flags to pass data between tasks, and I keep interrupt handlers short.
**中文翻译**：互斥锁保护临界区：同一时刻只有一个任务持有，且加锁的任务应由自己解锁。信号量是用于通知或限制对若干资源访问的计数器。二值信号量常用于从中断处理程序向任务发信号，例如“新数据就绪”。计数信号量跟踪多个可用资源。在 FreeRTOS 里，互斥锁还支持优先级继承，有助于应对优先级反转。在我的嵌入式工作中，我用队列和标志在任务之间传数据，并让中断处理程序保持简短。
**追问 1**：Can you use a mutex in an interrupt? — No; use a signalling mechanism instead. *（中文：中断里能用互斥锁吗？— 不能，用通知机制。）*
**追问 2**：Why keep interrupts short? — To keep latency low and avoid blocking other interrupts. *（中文：为什么中断要短？— 降低延迟，避免阻塞其他中断。）*
**关键词与易错点**：mutex ownership、binary semaphore signalling；不要说二者完全相同。

### Q43. Explain virtual memory and paging in simple terms.

**中文题意**：简单解释虚拟内存和分页。
**考察点**：OS 概念。
**English answer（约 100 词）**：Virtual memory gives each process its own address space, which the operating system maps to physical memory using page tables. Memory is divided into fixed-size pages. The mapping is handled by the memory management unit, and a translation lookaside buffer caches recent translations to make it fast. If a page is not in memory, a page fault occurs, and the operating system loads it from disk, possibly evicting another page. The benefits are isolation, the ability to run programs larger than physical memory, and simpler programming. The cost is overhead and, with heavy paging, slow performance. Small microcontrollers like my STM32 typically have no such virtual memory.
**中文翻译**：虚拟内存给每个进程独立的地址空间，操作系统用页表把它映射到物理内存。内存被划分为固定大小的页。映射由内存管理单元处理，转换后备缓冲（TLB）缓存最近的转换以加速。如果页不在内存中，就发生缺页，操作系统从磁盘加载，可能换出另一页。好处是隔离、能运行比物理内存更大的程序、编程更简单。代价是开销，以及频繁换页时性能变慢。像我用的 STM32 这样的小型微控制器通常没有这种虚拟内存。
**追问 1**：What is thrashing? — The system spends most of its time swapping pages. *（中文：什么是抖动（颠簸）？— 系统大部分时间花在换页上。）*
**追问 2**：Why does real-time code avoid paging? — It makes timing unpredictable. *（中文：实时代码为何避免换页？— 它让时序不可预测。）*
**关键词与易错点**：page table、TLB、page fault；STM32F407 / H7 没有 MMU 是常识性说明，说 “typically”。

### Q44. Compare TCP and UDP. Where did you use them?

**中文题意**：比较 TCP 和 UDP。你在哪里用过？
**考察点**：网络基础。
**English answer（约 100 词）**：TCP is connection-oriented and reliable: it gives ordered delivery, retransmission and flow control, at the price of more latency. UDP is connectionless and light: no guarantee of delivery or order, but low latency. TCP suits file transfer or commands that must not be lost; UDP suits streaming or high-rate sensor data where an old sample is useless. In my gardening project, the devices and the server exchange data over sockets, and the web page uses WebSocket. In robotics middleware, publish-subscribe systems can be configured as reliable or best-effort, which is the same trade-off, and I chose settings by data type.
**中文翻译**：TCP 面向连接且可靠：提供有序交付、重传和流量控制，代价是更高延迟。UDP 无连接且轻量：不保证交付或顺序，但延迟低。TCP 适合文件传输或不能丢的命令；UDP 适合流媒体或高频传感器数据，旧样本没有用。在我的园艺项目里，设备和服务器通过套接字交换数据，网页使用 WebSocket。在机器人中间件里，发布订阅系统可配置为可靠或尽力而为，这是同样的权衡，我按数据类型选择设置。
**追问 1**：Why best-effort for sensor state? — A newer sample replaces an older one; retransmitting the old one wastes time. *（中文：为什么传感状态用尽力而为？— 新样本替代旧样本，重传旧的浪费时间。）*
**追问 2**：What is the TCP handshake? — Three steps: SYN, SYN-ACK, ACK. *（中文：TCP 握手？— 三步：SYN、SYN-ACK、ACK。）*
**关键词与易错点**：reliable vs low latency；P6 的 QoS 为设计级描述，说 “in our design”；不要说 DDS 就是 UDP。

### Q45. What is a memory leak, and how do you avoid memory problems in C and C++?

**中文题意**：什么是内存泄漏？在 C/C++ 中怎么避免内存问题？
**考察点**：系统编程素养。
**English answer（约 100 词）**：A memory leak happens when memory is allocated and never freed, so usage grows over time. Other typical problems are use after free, double free, buffer overflow and uninitialised memory. In C++, I prefer RAII and smart pointers, such as unique pointer, so that resources are released automatically. I avoid raw new and delete in application code. In embedded firmware, I allocate memory statically at start-up and avoid dynamic allocation in the control loop, to keep timing predictable and prevent fragmentation. For checking, I use tools like address sanitiser or valgrind on the Linux side, and code review for the rest.
**中文翻译**：内存泄漏指分配了内存却从不释放，所以使用量随时间增长。其他典型问题有释放后使用、重复释放、缓冲区溢出和未初始化内存。在 C++ 里，我偏好 RAII 和智能指针，如 unique_ptr，使资源自动释放；应用代码中避免裸 new/delete。在嵌入式固件里，我在启动时静态分配内存，避免在控制循环里动态分配，以保持时序可预测并避免碎片。检查方面，在 Linux 侧用 address sanitiser 或 valgrind，其余靠代码评审。
**追问 1**：What is RAII? — Tying a resource's lifetime to an object's lifetime. *（中文：什么是 RAII？— 让资源生命周期与对象生命周期绑定。）*
**追问 2**：Why avoid malloc in a control loop? — Its time is not bounded and it can fragment memory. *（中文：为什么控制循环里避免 malloc？— 耗时无上界且会产生碎片。）*
**关键词与易错点**：RAII、static allocation、sanitiser；工具“用过”要真实，不确定就说 “I know the tools and use them where available”。

### Q46. How do you test and review your code?

**中文题意**：你如何测试和评审代码？
**考察点**：软件工程实践。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：On the platform at Kaihong, we use unit tests with a fake clock and fake devices, so tests are fast and repeatable. On my robot projects, I also keep the configuration in versioned files and note the git hash with each hardware test. I think a test that can be re-run is worth much more than one that was run once.
**中文翻译**：在深开鸿的平台上，我们使用带假时钟和假设备的单元测试，使测试快速且可重复。对每个设备适配器，我们运行同一套契约测试，包括运动学的往返：命令输入、底层命令输出，再返回。对故障处理，我们注入延迟、丢包、杀进程和时钟跳变，并回放录制的状态流。代码通过合并请求并经评审后才合入。在我的机器人项目里，我也把配置放进版本化文件，并在每次硬件测试时记下 git 哈希。我认为可重复运行的测试比只运行过一次的测试有价值得多。
**追问 1**：What is a fake clock for? — To control time in tests and avoid real waiting. *（中文：假时钟的作用？— 在测试中控制时间，避免真实等待。）*
**追问 2**：What did you build yourself? — The capability model, state management, and the coordination and safe-execution modules; the platform base belongs to the company. *（中文：你自己做了什么？— 能力模型、状态管理、协同与安全执行模块；平台底座属于公司。）*
**关键词与易错点**：fake clock、contract tests、fault injection；测试方式为设计级描述；不要说所有测试都是你一个人写的。

### Q47. Describe, step by step, how you would reverse a singly linked list.

**中文题意**：一步步说明如何反转单链表。
**考察点**：口述编码题（技术面常见）。
**English answer（约 95 词）**：I would use three pointers: previous, current and next. Start with previous as null and current as the head. In a loop, while current is not null: save the next node, point current's next to previous, then move previous to current and current to the saved next. When the loop ends, previous is the new head. The time is order n and the extra space is constant. I would test with an empty list, a single node and two nodes. A recursive version is possible, but it uses order n stack space, so for long lists I prefer the iterative one.
**中文翻译**：我会用三个指针：previous、current 和 next。开始时 previous 为空，current 为头节点。循环中，当 current 非空：先保存下一个节点，把 current 的 next 指向 previous，然后把 previous 移到 current，current 移到保存的下一个节点。循环结束时，previous 就是新的头。时间为 O(n)，额外空间为常数。我会用空链表、单节点和两节点来测试。递归版本也可以，但使用 O(n) 栈空间，所以长链表我更喜欢迭代版本。
**追问 1**：How do you detect a cycle? — Fast and slow pointers; if they meet, there is a cycle. *（中文：怎么检测环？— 快慢指针，相遇即有环。）*
**追问 2**：What edge cases do you test? — Empty list, one node, two nodes. *（中文：测试哪些边界？— 空链表、一个节点、两个节点。）*
**关键词与易错点**：three pointers、O(n)/O(1)、edge cases；口述时先说思路再说边界；不要直接写代码不解释。

### Q48. How would you find the shortest path in a grid with obstacles?

**中文题意**：如何在带障碍的网格里找最短路径？
**考察点**：BFS 口述与工程联系。
**English answer（约 100 词）**：If every move costs the same, I would use breadth-first search. I put the start cell in a queue and mark it visited. I repeatedly take a cell from the queue, look at its free neighbours, mark them visited, store their parent, and add them to the queue, until I reach the goal. I rebuild the path by following parents back from the goal. The time is order of the number of cells. If moves have different costs, I use Dijkstra, and with a good heuristic, A*. This is the same idea as the global planner in my navigation project, with the grid being the costmap.
**中文翻译**：如果每步代价相同，我会用广度优先搜索。把起点放入队列并标记已访问。反复从队列取一个格子，查看其空闲邻居，标记已访问，记录父节点，并加入队列，直到到达目标。再沿父节点从目标回溯重建路径。时间与格子数同阶。如果各步代价不同，用 Dijkstra；有好的启发函数时用 A*。这与我导航项目里全局规划的思想相同，网格就是代价地图。
**追问 1**：What about diagonal moves? — Add them as neighbours, with a larger cost for Dijkstra or A*. *（中文：斜向移动呢？— 加入邻居，在 Dijkstra/A* 中给更大代价。）*
**追问 2**：Why does a car-like robot need Hybrid-A*? — Because heading and turning radius matter, and plain grid paths may not be drivable. *（中文：车式机器人为什么要 Hybrid-A*？— 因为航向和转弯半径重要，普通栅格路径可能开不了。）*
**关键词与易错点**：queue、visited、parent；解释完说复杂度；不要漏掉“记录父节点”。

### Q49. Design a ring buffer for sensor data. What problems must you handle?

**中文题意**：设计一个传感器数据的环形缓冲区。要处理哪些问题？
**考察点**：数据结构设计与嵌入式经验。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：A ring buffer is a fixed-size array with a head and a tail index that wrap around. The producer writes at the head and advances it; the consumer reads at the tail and advances it. To tell empty from full, I either keep a count or leave one slot empty. When it is full, I must decide a policy: drop the newest data or overwrite the oldest. For sensor streams, overwriting the oldest is often better, since new data matters more. If a producer is an interrupt and a consumer is a task, I need to keep the updates safe, with atomic indices or a short critical section. I used a similar idea for event-triggered logs that keep the last second before a fault.
**中文翻译**：环形缓冲区是固定大小的数组，头尾下标循环回绕。生产者在头部写入并前移；消费者在尾部读取并前移。区分空和满，可以保留计数，或空出一个槽位。满时必须决定策略：丢弃最新数据还是覆盖最旧数据。对传感器流，覆盖最旧数据通常更好，因为新数据更重要。如果生产者是中断、消费者是任务，我需要让更新保持安全，用原子下标或很短的临界区。我在事件触发日志中用了类似思想，保留故障前的最后一秒。
**追问 1**：How big would you make it? — Enough for the longest delay of the consumer at the maximum data rate. *（中文：多大？— 足够容纳最大数据率下消费者最长延迟的数据。）*
**追问 2**：Single producer single consumer without locks? — Possible if indices are updated carefully with proper memory ordering. *（中文：单生产者单消费者能无锁吗？— 可以，只要下标更新有正确的内存序。）*
**关键词与易错点**：head/tail、overwrite policy、thread safety；事件触发日志来自 P1 自查表；不要声称实现了无锁队列库。

### Q62. Why does gain scheduling help, and how do you avoid problems when switching gains?

**中文题意**：增益调度为什么有用？切换增益时如何避免问题？
**考察点**：控制设计细节。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：When the operating point changes, such as the leg length, the linear model changes, so one gain set is only good near one point. Gain scheduling computes LQR gains at several leg lengths and varies them with leg length. To avoid jumps, I do not switch between discrete sets; I fit the gains as smooth polynomials of the leg length and clamp the leg length to the fitted range. I also limit how fast the leg length changes, so that the scheduling variable moves slowly compared with the balance dynamics. This keeps the frozen-model assumption reasonable. A fixed gain far from the design point has a much smaller stability margin.
**中文翻译**：当工作点（如腿长）改变时线性模型改变，所以一组增益只在一个点附近好用。增益调度在若干腿长下计算 LQR 增益，并随腿长变化。为避免跳变，我不在离散增益组之间切换，而是把增益拟合成腿长的平滑多项式，并把腿长钳在拟合范围内。我还限制腿长变化的速度，使调度变量相对平衡动力学变化缓慢。这样“冻结模型”的假设仍合理。固定增益远离设计点时稳定裕度小得多。
**追问 1**：Does scheduling guarantee stability? — No, only when the scheduling variable varies slowly; I tested on the robot. *（中文：调度保证稳定吗？— 不保证，仅在调度变量变化缓慢时合理；我在机器人上测试了。）*
**追问 2**：What if leg length changes quickly? — I limit the rate, or the model assumptions break down. *（中文：腿长变化快呢？— 我限制变化速率，否则模型假设失效。）*
**关键词与易错点**：slowly varying、smooth fit、clamp；不要说“scheduling guarantees stability”。

### Q63. Linearise an inverted pendulum and explain how to check controllability.

**中文题意**：线性化倒立摆，并说明如何检查可控性。
**考察点**：建模与控制基础。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：For an inverted pendulum, I write the equation of motion in terms of the tilt angle theta. Around the upright equilibrium, theta is small, so sine of theta is about theta, and cosine is about one. This gives a linear model, x dot equals A x plus B u, with states like position, velocity, tilt and tilt rate. To check controllability, I build the matrix with columns B, A B, A squared B and so on, up to A to the n minus one times B, and check that its rank is n. If the rank is full, all states can be steered with the input. LQR needs this, and the model is only valid near upright.
**中文翻译**：对倒立摆，我用倾角 θ 写出运动方程。在直立平衡点附近，θ 很小，所以 sinθ≈θ，cosθ≈1。得到线性模型 x' = Ax + Bu，状态如位置、速度、倾角和倾角速度。检查可控性时，我构造由 B、AB、A²B 直到 A^(n−1)B 组成的矩阵，检查其秩为 n。若满秩，所有状态都可通过输入操纵。LQR 需要这一点，而且模型只在直立附近有效。
**追问 1**：What does a rank-deficient controllability matrix mean? — Some state directions cannot be influenced by the input. *（中文：可控性矩阵降秩意味着什么？— 某些状态方向无法被输入影响。）*
**追问 2**：Observability? — The dual check with the observability matrix. *（中文：可观性？— 用可观性矩阵做对偶检查。）*
**关键词与易错点**：small-angle、rank test；球形机器人的四状态是位置、速度、倾角、倾角速度；说明模型只在近直立有效。

### Q64. Compare differential-drive and Ackermann kinematics. Where did you use each?

**中文题意**：比较差速和阿克曼运动学。你分别在哪里用过？

### Q65. What is the difference between EKF and AMCL? What happens when localisation fails?

**中文题意**：EKF 和 AMCL 有什么区别？定位失败会怎样？
**考察点**：定位理论与实践。
**English answer（约 110 词）**：An EKF estimates the state as a Gaussian, with a prediction from the motion model and corrections from measurements; it is good for fusing wheel speed, gyro and IMU in a smooth way. AMCL is a particle filter that represents the pose with many samples and matches the LiDAR scan to a map; it can represent multiple hypotheses, which helps when the position is uncertain. In my project, EKF gave a smooth local estimate, and AMCL corrected the global drift. If localisation fails, for example after a bad timestamp or reversing with the wrong sign, the pose jumps, and the particles spread. I handled this by fixing the inputs and by limiting the correction rate.
**中文翻译**：EKF 把状态估计为高斯分布，用运动模型预测、用测量修正；适合平滑地融合轮速、陀螺和 IMU。AMCL 是粒子滤波，用许多样本表示位姿，并把激光扫描与地图匹配；它能表示多个假设，在位置不确定时有帮助。在我的项目里，EKF 给出平滑的局部估计，AMCL 修正全局漂移。定位失败时，例如时间戳错或倒车符号错，位姿会跳变，粒子会散开。我通过修复输入并限制修正速率来处理。
**追问 1**：What is the kidnapped robot problem? — The robot is moved without the filter knowing; a particle filter can recover with global relocalisation. *（中文：什么是绑架机器人问题？— 机器人被移动而滤波器不知道；粒子滤波可通过全局重定位恢复。）*
**追问 2**：Why limit the correction rate? — Large jumps in the map-to-odom transform make the controller react to the jump. *（中文：为什么限制修正速率？— map→odom 的大跳变会让控制器对跳变作出反应。）*
**关键词与易错点**：Gaussian vs particles、multiple hypotheses；robot_localization 的 ekf_node；不要说自己实现了粒子滤波。

### Q66. Compare A*, Hybrid-A*, RRT* and TEB for a car-like robot.

**中文题意**：针对车式机器人比较 A*、Hybrid-A*、RRT* 和 TEB。
**考察点**：规划算法比较。
**English answer（约 100 词）**：A* works on a grid and ignores vehicle kinematics, so its path may not be drivable. Hybrid-A* keeps a continuous heading in each cell and expands with feasible motions, so the path respects the minimum turning radius; it also allows reversing with penalties. RRT* samples the space, scales to high dimensions and is asymptotically optimal, but paths are irregular and need smoothing, and the planning time is variable. TEB is a local optimiser: it deforms a path into a time-parameterised trajectory under obstacle and kinematic constraints. In my chain, Hybrid-A* gives the global path, TEB the local trajectory, and MPC tracks it.
**中文翻译**：A* 在栅格上工作且忽略车辆运动学，所以路径可能开不了。Hybrid-A* 在每个格子里保留连续航向并用可行动作扩展，所以路径满足最小转弯半径；它还允许倒车并加惩罚。RRT* 对空间采样，可扩展到高维且渐近最优，但路径不规则，需要平滑，规划时间不稳定。TEB 是局部优化器：在障碍和运动学约束下把路径变形成带时间参数的轨迹。在我的链条里，Hybrid-A* 给全局路径，TEB 给局部轨迹，MPC 负责跟踪。
**追问 1**：Why both TEB and MPC? — TEB handles obstacles and timing; MPC handles tracking with constraints on the actual vehicle. *（中文：为什么 TEB 和 MPC 都要？— TEB 处理障碍和时间；MPC 在实际车辆上带约束地跟踪。）*
**追问 2**：Which implementation did you use? — A typical Nav2 Smac Hybrid-A* and a ROS2 port of TEB. *（中文：用的什么实现？— 典型的 Nav2 Smac Hybrid-A* 和 TEB 的 ROS2 移植。）*
**关键词与易错点**：kinematic feasibility、local vs global；P5 使用 ROS2 Humble；不要说 TEB 是自研。

### Q67. Describe the sensors on your robots, their noise, and how you calibrated them.

**中文题意**：介绍你机器人上的传感器、噪声以及标定方法。
**考察点**：传感与标定（项目关键词 sensing）。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：On my robots, the main sensors are IMUs, wheel encoders, joint encoders and 2D LiDAR. An IMU has gyro bias and noise, and the accelerometer is noisy under motion, so I estimate attitude with a complementary filter or Mahony-style filter. For bias, I average the gyro for a short time at start-up while the robot is still. Encoders give a precise but drifting odometry, so I calibrated the wheel radius with a straight drive and the track width with in-place turns. For LiDAR, the main issue was the timestamp, which I corrected to the scan start. Each sensor has a frame, a unit and a time, and I write them down.
**中文翻译**：我的机器人上主要传感器有 IMU、轮式编码器、关节编码器和二维激光雷达。IMU 有陀螺偏置和噪声，加速度计在运动中噪声大，所以我用互补滤波或 Mahony 类滤波估计姿态。偏置方面，启动时机器人静止，对陀螺平均一小段时间。编码器给出精确但会漂移的里程，所以我用直线行驶标定轮径，用原地转向标定轮距。对激光，主要问题是时间戳，我改为扫描起始时刻。每个传感器都有坐标系、单位和时间，我都写下来。
**追问 1**：Why does the gyro need a bias estimate? — A constant bias integrates into a growing angle error. *（中文：为什么陀螺需要偏置估计？— 常值偏置积分后变成不断增大的角度误差。）*
**追问 2**：How accurate was your odometry? — About two percent over a five-metre straight run. *（中文：里程计多准？— 五米直线约百分之二的误差。）*
**关键词与易错点**：bias、complementary filter、calibration；数字取自 P2 自查表（约 2%），说 about；不同项目的传感器细节不要混。

### Q68. What is virtual model control, and when does it fail?

**中文题意**：什么是虚拟模型控制？什么时候会失效？
**考察点**：VMC 的理解边界。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：Virtual model control designs the behaviour as if virtual springs and dampers were attached to the robot, in task space. For my leg, I specify a desired virtual leg force and pitch torque, and I map them to joint torques through the Jacobian transpose. It makes the design intuitive and separates the balance logic from the leg geometry. It can fail near singular configurations, where the Jacobian loses rank and some forces cannot be produced. It also fails when torque limits are reached, when the model of the linkage is wrong, or when the foot loses contact, because the force can no longer be applied. I handled contact with a support-force estimate.
**中文翻译**：虚拟模型控制把行为设计成好像在任务空间里给机器人接了虚拟弹簧和阻尼器。对我的腿，我给出期望的虚拟腿力和俯仰力矩，并通过雅可比转置映射为关节力矩。它使设计直观，并把平衡逻辑与腿的几何分开。在奇异位形附近它会失效，因为雅可比降秩，某些力产生不了。当达到力矩限制、连杆模型错误、或足端失去接触时也会失效，因为力无法再施加。我通过支撑力估计来处理接触。
**追问 1**：How do you detect leaving the ground? — The estimated support force stays below a threshold for several cycles. *（中文：怎么检测离地？— 估计的支撑力连续数个周期低于阈值。）*
**追问 2**：What do you do in the air? — Set wheel torque to zero and freeze position integration, then blend back in after landing. *（中文：腾空时怎么做？— 轮矩置零、冻结位置积分，落地后渐入。）*
**关键词与易错点**：virtual spring-damper、Jacobian transpose、contact；阈值取自 P7 自查表（低于约 10 N 持续 8 个周期），说 about。

### Q69. How do you tune a controller on a real robot safely?

**中文题意**：你如何在真实机器人上安全地调控制器？
**考察点**：实验安全意识。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：I start conservatively. For the first test, I use ten to twenty percent of the target gains, half of the speed limit, no load, and a person at the emergency stop. I check the signs and directions before enabling the loop: whether the joint direction matches the encoder. I add output limits, a fall or fault detection, and a watchdog that stops the output if commands stop. Then I increase gains step by step and change one parameter at a time, logging the data. I stop when I see oscillation or saturation and look at the logs before continuing. For a balancing robot, I use a gantry or a hand to catch it.
**中文翻译**：我从保守开始。首次测试用目标增益的百分之十到二十、一半的速度限制、空载，并让人守在急停旁。启用回路前先检查符号和方向：关节方向是否与编码器一致。我加上输出限幅、跌倒或故障检测，以及命令停止时切断输出的看门狗。然后逐步增大增益，一次只改一个参数，并记录数据。出现振荡或饱和时就停下来看日志再继续。对平衡机器人，我用龙门架或用手来接住它。
**追问 1**：What is a watchdog? — A timer that triggers a safe action if it is not refreshed. *（中文：看门狗是什么？— 若没有被刷新就触发安全动作的定时器。）*
**追问 2**：Have you had a real incident? — Falls during early tests, which is why I add limits and recovery. *（中文：出过真实事故吗？— 早期测试中有过跌倒，所以我加了限制和恢复。）*
**关键词与易错点**：low gains first、watchdog、emergency stop；数值来自 P2 自查表的做法；不要把不安全的做法说成常规。

### Q70. What are imitation learning and reinforcement learning, and how would they apply to your robots?

**中文题意**：什么是模仿学习和强化学习？如何用于你的机器人？
**考察点**：Robot learning 概念水平，并诚实。
**English answer（约 110 词）**：In imitation learning, a policy learns from demonstrations, for example by supervised learning from state and action pairs. It is data-efficient, but it can fail when the robot leaves the demonstrated situations. In reinforcement learning, the robot learns by trial and error to maximise a reward. It can discover behaviours that are hard to design, but it needs many samples, careful reward design, and safety measures, so it is often trained in simulation first. For my robots, I think of imitation for manipulation, using my leader–follower teaching system as a way to produce demonstrations, and RL for locomotion or balance in simulation. I should say that I know this at the concept level, not from hands-on training.
**中文翻译**：模仿学习中，策略从示范中学习，例如对状态–动作对做监督学习。它数据效率高，但机器人离开示范情形时可能失败。强化学习中，机器人通过试错学习最大化奖励。它能发现难以设计的行为，但需要大量样本、细致的奖励设计和安全措施，所以常先在仿真中训练。对我的机器人，我设想把模仿学习用于操作，用我的主从示教系统产生示范；把强化学习用于仿真中的运动或平衡。我应该说明，这是概念层面的了解，不是动手训练得来的。
**追问 1**：What is distribution shift? — The policy meets states that were not in the training data. *（中文：什么是分布偏移？— 策略遇到训练数据中没有的状态。）*
**追问 2**：Have you collected demonstration data? — Not for training; the teaching system could produce it. *（中文：收集过示范数据吗？— 没有用于训练的，但示教系统可以产生。）*
**关键词与易错点**：demonstration、reward、sim-to-real；诚实说明概念层面；P2 主从示教“可以产生示范”，不要说已做过数据集。

### Q71. Explain how your leader–follower teaching system works.

**中文题意**：解释你的主从示教系统如何工作。
**考察点**：机械臂动力学与遥操作（P2）。
**English answer（约 110 词）**：There are two arms. The leader arm is held by the operator, and it needs gravity compensation, so it feels light. I built the dynamics from the URDF with KDL, computed the gravity torque for the current joint angles, and commanded it to the leader motors, with a ratio slightly below one for the last joint, whose load is uncertain. The follower arm tracks the leader's joint positions and velocities with a position–velocity controller. The result is low-resistance hand guiding with synchronous follower motion. The leader–follower delay was about thirty milliseconds end to end. The motor driver and the bus code were provided by the team.
**中文翻译**：有两根机械臂。主臂由操作员握持，需要重力补偿，让它手感很轻。我用 KDL 基于 URDF 建立动力学，对当前关节角计算重力力矩并发给主臂电机，最后一个关节的比例略小于一，因为它的负载不确定。从臂用位置–速度控制器跟踪主臂的关节位置和速度。结果是低阻力的手动引导和同步的从臂运动。主从端到端延迟约三十毫秒。电机驱动和总线代码由团队提供。
**追问 1**：Why a ratio below one? — Over-compensation makes the joint drift upward, so I leave a margin. *（中文：为什么比例小于一？— 过补偿会使关节向上漂，所以留余量。）*
**追问 2**：Which gains did the follower use? — Position and velocity gains that differ per joint, with velocity feedforward. *（中文：从臂用什么增益？— 逐关节不同的位置和速度增益，带速度前馈。）*
**关键词与易错点**：gravity compensation、KDL、leader–follower；比例 0.8 和 30 ms 取自 P2 自查表，说 about；电机驱动是团队的。

### Q72. Tell us about a real robot failure you debugged.

**中文题意**：讲一次你调试的真实机器人故障。
**考察点**：调试方法。
**English answer（约 115 词）**：On the wheel-legged robot, after I hung the robot and set it down, it slowly crept forward and the position error kept growing. I read the logs and found that the pitch equilibrium had a bias of about one and a half degrees, from the centre of mass being forward and the IMU mounting. Also, the IMU and joint data were not sampled at the same moment. I measured the zero while hanging, corrected the bias, and took one snapshot of all states per control cycle. After that, the robot stayed within a few centimetres over thirty seconds. The lesson is that a small state inconsistency can look like a control problem.
**中文翻译**：在轮腿机器人上，把机器人吊起再放下后，它缓慢向前爬，位置误差不断增大。我读日志发现俯仰平衡点有约一点五度的偏置，来自质心靠前和 IMU 安装偏差。另外，IMU 和关节数据不是在同一时刻采样的。我在吊起时测量零位，修正偏置，并在每个控制周期对所有状态做一次快照。之后，机器人在三十秒内保持在几厘米内。教训是：小小的状态不一致可能看起来像控制问题。
**追问 1**：How did you find the bias? — The robot crept in one direction even with a zero command. *（中文：怎么发现偏置？— 零指令下机器人也朝一个方向爬。）*
**追问 2**：Did the controller gains change? — No, I fixed the state first. *（中文：控制器增益改了吗？— 没有，我先修复状态。）*
**关键词与易错点**：bias、snapshot、state consistency；取自 P7 案例 A（约 1.5°、30 s 小于 3 cm），说 about。

### Q73. How would you design an experiment to compare two controllers on a robot?

**中文题意**：如何设计实验在机器人上比较两个控制器？
**考察点**：实验设计。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：First, define the question and the metrics, for example settling time after a push, peak tilt, tracking error and control effort. Second, keep everything else the same: same robot, battery level, floor, trajectory and disturbance. Third, apply the disturbance in a repeatable way, for example a fixed push from a mechanism or a fixed command step, and run several trials per controller in a random order. Fourth, log the data, report the mean and the spread, and show failures too. Fifth, check that the difference is bigger than the run-to-run variation. I did a small version of this for centre-of-mass compensation, five runs per setting.
**中文翻译**：首先，定义问题和指标，例如受推后的稳定时间、峰值倾角、跟踪误差和控制量。其次，保持其他一切相同：同一机器人、电池电量、地面、轨迹和扰动。第三，以可重复方式施加扰动，例如固定的推力装置或固定的指令阶跃，每个控制器随机顺序运行若干次。第四，记录数据，报告均值和离散度，也展示失败。第五，检查差异是否大于多次运行之间的波动。我对质心补偿做过小型版本，每种设置五次。
**追问 1**：Why random order? — To avoid battery or temperature effects favouring one controller. *（中文：为什么随机顺序？— 避免电量或温度效应偏向某个控制器。）*
**追问 2**：How many trials are enough? — It depends on the variation; five is a minimum for a first look. *（中文：多少次够？— 取决于波动；五次是初步观察的下限。）*
**关键词与易错点**：metrics、repeatability、variation；不要编造统计显著性数字。

**D. 技术考点预测（Q88–Q96）**

### Q88. Explain the attention mechanism in a Transformer in simple terms.

**中文题意**：简单解释 Transformer 里的注意力机制。
**考察点**：深度学习基础，与 VLA 相关。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：Attention lets each element of a sequence look at all the others and decide how much to use each one. For every token, the model computes a query, a key and a value. The similarity between the query of one token and the keys of all tokens, after a softmax, gives weights. The output is the weighted sum of the values. This lets the model capture long-range relations, and it runs in parallel on a GPU. The cost grows with the square of the sequence length. Multi-head attention uses several sets in parallel, so the model can look at different relations at once. I know this at the concept level from reading and from using pretrained models.
**中文翻译**：注意力让序列中的每个元素查看所有其他元素，并决定使用每个的程度。对每个词元，模型计算查询、键和值。一个词元的查询与所有词元的键的相似度，经 softmax 后给出权重，输出是值的加权和。这使模型能捕捉长程关系，并能在 GPU 上并行。成本随序列长度的平方增长。多头注意力并行使用几组，使模型能同时关注不同关系。我在概念层面了解，来自阅读和使用预训练模型。
**追问 1**：Why divide by the square root of the dimension? — To keep the dot products from becoming too large before softmax. *（中文：为什么除以维度的平方根？— 避免点积在 softmax 前过大。）*
**追问 2**：Where is it used in robotics? — Vision-language models and some policies use transformers. *（中文：机器人里哪里用？— 视觉-语言模型和一些策略使用 Transformer。）*
**关键词与易错点**：query/key/value、softmax、quadratic cost；概念水平；不要说训练过大模型。

### Q89. What is PPO, at a high level, and why is it popular for robot learning in simulation?

**中文题意**：简要说，PPO 是什么，为什么它在仿真中的机器人学习里流行？
**考察点**：RL 概念水平。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：PPO, proximal policy optimisation, is a policy-gradient method. The robot collects experience with the current policy, and the policy is updated to increase the reward. The key idea is to limit how far each update can move the policy, using a clipped objective, so that learning is more stable. It is relatively simple, works with large parallel simulations, and is robust to hyperparameters, which is why it is widely used for locomotion in simulation. It is on-policy, so it needs fresh data and is less sample-efficient than off-policy methods. I know it at a conceptual level and have not trained policies with it.
**中文翻译**：PPO，即近端策略优化，是一种策略梯度方法。机器人用当前策略收集经验，并更新策略以提高奖励。核心想法是用截断目标限制每次更新能让策略移动多远，使学习更稳定。它相对简单，适合大规模并行仿真，对超参数较鲁棒，所以在仿真中的足式运动里广泛使用。它是同策略的，需要新数据，样本效率不如异策略方法。我在概念层面了解，没有用它训练过策略。
**追问 1**：On-policy vs off-policy? — On-policy learns from data of the current policy; off-policy can reuse old data. *（中文：同策略与异策略？— 同策略用当前策略的数据；异策略可复用旧数据。）*
**追问 2**：What is a reward function? — A signal that defines what the robot should achieve. *（中文：什么是奖励函数？— 定义机器人应实现什么的信号。）*
**关键词与易错点**：clipped objective、on-policy；概念水平；诚实说明未训练。

### Q90. What causes the sim-to-real gap, and how do you reduce it?

**中文题意**：仿真到现实的差距由什么造成？如何缩小？
**考察点**：具身智能核心问题。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：The gap comes from differences between simulation and the real robot: friction and contact models, actuator dynamics and torque limits, sensor noise and delay, and unmodelled effects like cable or backlash. To reduce it, I would first identify the main parameters from real data, so the simulation is closer. Then I would use domain randomisation, which varies parameters like mass, friction and delay during training, so the policy becomes robust. I would model delays and saturation explicitly, and test on hardware step by step with limits. From my own work, I know that torque limits and state inconsistency often decide the real behaviour, and those must be in the simulator too.
**中文翻译**：差距来自仿真与真实机器人的差异：摩擦和接触模型、执行器动力学和力矩限制、传感器噪声和延迟，以及线缆或齿隙等未建模效应。为缩小它，我会先用真实数据辨识主要参数，使仿真更接近。然后用域随机化，在训练中改变质量、摩擦和延迟等参数，使策略更鲁棒。我会显式建模延迟和饱和，并带着限制在硬件上逐步测试。从我自己的工作看，力矩限制和状态不一致常常决定真实行为，这些也必须放进仿真。
**追问 1**：What is domain randomisation? — Randomising simulation parameters so that the policy cannot rely on exact values. *（中文：什么是域随机化？— 随机化仿真参数，使策略不依赖精确值。）*
**追问 2**：What did you simulate before? — MuJoCo for kinematics checks and URDF-based models; the gains were tuned on the real robot. *（中文：你仿真过什么？— 用 MuJoCo 做运动学检查和 URDF 模型，增益是在真机上调的。）*
**关键词与易错点**：identification、randomisation、delay & saturation；不要说用仿真增益直接上机（P1：增益以实机为准）。

### Q91. What are common pitfalls in reward design?

**中文题意**：奖励设计常见的陷阱有哪些？
**考察点**：RL 理解。
**English answer（约 100 词）**：A common pitfall is reward hacking: the policy finds an unintended way to get a high reward, such as moving in a strange way that the simulator allows. Another is a sparse reward, where the robot rarely gets any signal, so learning is slow; shaping the reward helps, but a badly shaped reward can bias the behaviour. Terms can conflict, for example speed against energy use, so the weights matter. Finally, the reward may ignore safety, so I would add constraints, such as torque and joint limits, and check the behaviour by watching rollouts and not only the numbers. I understand this conceptually and want to practise it in the programme.
**中文翻译**：常见陷阱是奖励投机：策略找到非预期的方式拿高奖励，例如用仿真器允许的怪异方式运动。另一个是稀疏奖励，机器人很少得到信号，学习缓慢；奖励塑形有帮助，但塑形不当会使行为偏置。各项可能冲突，如速度与能耗，所以权重很重要。最后，奖励可能忽略安全，所以我会加入约束，如力矩和关节限制，并通过观看回放而不只是数字来检查行为。我在概念上理解，想在项目中练习。
**追问 1**：Give an example of reward hacking. — A walking robot that falls forward fast to get a distance reward. *（中文：举个奖励投机的例子。— 行走机器人为了获得距离奖励而快速向前倒下。）*
**追问 2**：How to detect it? — Watch videos of the rollouts and check multiple metrics. *（中文：怎么发现？— 观看回放视频并检查多个指标。）*
**关键词与易错点**：reward hacking、sparse reward、constraints；概念水平；例子要通俗。

### Q92. Describe a pipeline for a robot to pick up an object from a table.

**中文题意**：描述机器人从桌上抓取物体的流水线。
**考察点**：系统视角，结合感知、规划、控制。
**English answer（约 110 词）**：First, perception: a camera detects the object and estimates its pose in the camera frame; with calibration, I transform it into the robot base frame. Second, grasp planning: choose a grasp pose and an approach direction, avoiding collisions. Third, motion planning and inverse kinematics: compute a collision-free trajectory to a pre-grasp pose, then to the grasp, using joint limits and damping near singularities. Fourth, control: track the trajectory with joint position and velocity control, close the gripper, and check the grasp using gripper feedback. Last, lift and verify, with a retry. The weak links are usually calibration, timing between camera and arm, and contact.
**中文翻译**：首先是感知：相机检测物体并估计其在相机坐标系中的位姿；有了标定，就变换到机器人基座坐标系。其次是抓取规划：选择抓取位姿和接近方向，避免碰撞。第三是运动规划和逆运动学：计算到预抓取位姿再到抓取位姿的无碰撞轨迹，考虑关节限位和奇异点附近的阻尼。第四是控制：用关节位置和速度控制跟踪轨迹，闭合夹爪，并用夹爪反馈检查抓取。最后抬起并验证，失败则重试。薄弱环节通常是标定、相机与机械臂之间的时间同步，以及接触。
**追问 1**：What is hand–eye calibration? — Finding the transform between the camera and the robot. *（中文：什么是手眼标定？— 求相机与机器人之间的变换。）*
**追问 2**：Which parts have you done? — Constrained IK and tracking control for a dual-arm robot; I have not done grasp planning. *（中文：哪些做过？— 双臂机器人的约束 IK 和跟踪控制；没有做过抓取规划。）*
**关键词与易错点**：camera frame to base frame、IK、calibration；明确自己做过和没做过的部分。

### Q93. Explain the Kalman filter and why timestamps matter for sensor fusion.

**中文题意**：解释卡尔曼滤波，以及为什么时间戳对传感器融合重要。
**考察点**：算法原理追问（港科广面试官会“对算法原理再次提问”）。
**English answer（约 110 词）**：The Kalman filter alternates between predicting and correcting. The prediction uses a motion model to propagate the state and its covariance. The correction compares a measurement with the predicted measurement, and the Kalman gain weighs them by their uncertainties. Timestamps matter because the correction assumes the measurement describes the state at a particular time. If the measurement is delayed, for example a LiDAR scan stamped at arrival time and not scan time, the filter applies it to the wrong state, and the estimate jumps. In my navigation robot, a delay of about sixty milliseconds caused this, and I fixed it by using the scan start time and unifying the clock.
**中文翻译**：卡尔曼滤波在预测和修正之间交替。预测用运动模型传播状态及其协方差。修正将测量与预测测量比较，卡尔曼增益按不确定性对两者加权。时间戳重要，因为修正假设测量描述的是特定时刻的状态。如果测量被延迟，例如激光扫描按到达时间而不是扫描时间打戳，滤波器就把它用在错误的状态上，估计会跳变。在我的导航机器人里，约六十毫秒的延迟造成了这一点，我通过改用扫描起始时间并统一时钟修复了它。
**追问 1**：How do you handle delayed measurements? — Compensate the stamp, or use a buffered or smoothing approach. *（中文：怎么处理延迟的测量？— 补偿时间戳，或使用缓冲或平滑方法。）*
**追问 2**：What does the covariance tell you? — How uncertain the estimate is. *（中文：协方差告诉你什么？— 估计有多不确定。）*
**关键词与易错点**：predict-correct、gain、timestamp；约 60 ms 取自 P5 自查表；不要把 AMCL 和 EKF 混为一谈。

### Q94. How could MPC be combined with a learned dynamics model?

**中文题意**：MPC 如何与学习得到的动力学模型结合？
**考察点**：控制与学习的结合。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：MPC needs a prediction model. If the nominal model is inaccurate, for example because of contact or friction, a learned model can correct it: either as a residual added to the physical model, or as a full learned model of the dynamics. The MPC then optimises over the horizon with the learned prediction, and still applies explicit constraints on states and inputs. The challenges are accuracy outside the training data, computation time, and making sure the optimisation remains well-behaved. I would start with a residual on a model I trust, compare to the nominal MPC, and keep constraints and fallbacks. I have used MPC with a kinematic model, not with a learned model.
**中文翻译**：MPC 需要预测模型。如果名义模型不准，例如因接触或摩擦，学习得到的模型可以修正它：要么作为加到物理模型上的残差，要么作为完整的学习动力学模型。MPC 然后用学习的预测在时域内优化，并仍对状态和输入施加显式约束。挑战在于训练数据之外的精度、计算时间，以及保证优化仍然表现良好。我会从一个我信任的模型上加残差开始，与名义 MPC 比较，并保留约束和回退。我用过基于运动学模型的 MPC，没有用过学习模型。
**追问 1**：How would you solve it fast? — Keep the model small and use a warm start. *（中文：怎么算得快？— 保持模型小，并用热启动。）*
**追问 2**：Your MPC settings? — A car-like model, horizon of fifteen steps of 0.1 s, solved with OSQP at about 20 Hz. *（中文：你的 MPC 设置？— 车式模型，十五步、步长 0.1 秒，用 OSQP 约 20 Hz 求解。）*
**关键词与易错点**：residual、constraints、horizon；你用过的是运动学模型 MPC；不要说用过学习模型。

### Q95. Why does a safety layer matter when a learned policy controls a robot, and how would you build one?

**中文题意**：学习策略控制机器人时，为什么需要安全层？你会怎么搭？
**考察点**：与 PS 主线一致，工程落点。
**English answer（约 110 词）**：A learned policy can output unexpected actions in situations it has not seen, and it gives no guarantee. A safety layer sits between the policy and the actuators. It checks each command against rules: valid format, allowed action, value ranges, rate limits, freshness of the robot state, and the current mode. It can reject, clamp or ask for confirmation. Below that, the low-level controller keeps its own limits and timeouts, so that if commands stop, the robot stops. Every decision is logged with a reason code. The check and the limits should be independent from the policy, so a failure of one does not remove the other. This mirrors the chain I worked on at Kaihong.
**中文翻译**：学习策略在没见过的情形下可能输出意外动作，也没有保证。安全层位于策略与执行器之间。它依据规则检查每条命令：格式有效、动作允许、数值范围、速率限制、机器人状态新鲜度和当前模式。它可以拒绝、钳位或请求确认。再往下，底层控制器保持自己的限制和超时，命令停止时机器人就停。每个决策都带原因码记录。检查和限制应独立于策略，一方失效不会使另一方消失。这与我在深开鸿做的链条相呼应。
**追问 1**：What does "clamp" mean? — Limit a value to an allowed range instead of rejecting the command. *（中文：“钳位”指什么？— 把值限制在允许范围内，而不是拒绝命令。）*
**追问 2**：Can a safety layer hurt performance? — Yes, if the limits are too tight, so I tune them with data. *（中文：安全层会损害性能吗？— 会，如果限制太紧，所以我用数据调整。）*
**关键词与易错点**：independent、reject/clamp/confirm、reason codes；设计级描述；不讲内部细节。

### Q96. How do you evaluate whether a robot policy is good?

**中文题意**：如何评估机器人策略好不好？
**考察点**：评估意识。
**English answer（约 105 词）**：I would define success before the experiment: for example task success rate, time to complete, tracking error, and safety events such as limit hits or falls. I would test on the same setup for each method, with several trials, and report the mean and the spread, including failures. I would also test under variations, such as different positions, lighting or disturbances, to see robustness. For learned policies, I would add out-of-distribution tests. I would compare with a simple baseline, and watch videos, because numbers can hide strange behaviour. From my own tests, I know that five repeats per setting is a minimum to see a clear difference.
**中文翻译**：我会在实验前定义成功：例如任务成功率、完成时间、跟踪误差，以及安全事件如触及限位或跌倒。对每种方法在同一设置下测试，做多次试验，报告均值和离散度，包括失败。我也会在变化条件下测试，如不同位置、光照或扰动，以看鲁棒性。对学习策略，我会增加分布外测试。我会与简单基线比较，并观看视频，因为数字可能掩盖怪异行为。从我自己的测试看，每种设置五次重复是看到明显差异的最低限度。
**追问 1**：What is out-of-distribution? — Situations different from the training data. *（中文：什么是分布外？— 与训练数据不同的情形。）*
**追问 2**：How do you handle randomness? — Use several seeds and report the spread. *（中文：怎么处理随机性？— 用多个种子并报告离散度。）*
**关键词与易错点**：success criteria、variations、baseline；不要编造统计检验。
