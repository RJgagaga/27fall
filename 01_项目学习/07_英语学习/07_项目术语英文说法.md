## 2. P1 AIRS：三全向轮 ballbot 双臂平衡控制

**项目一句话**

> I worked on dynamic balance control of a three-omni-wheel ballbot with a dual-arm upper body. The chassis keeps balance while both arms move along fixed trajectories.
>
> 我做的是带双臂上半身的三全向轮 ballbot 的动态平衡控制。双臂沿固定轨迹运动时，底盘保持平衡。

**核心术语（18 个）**

| # | 英文 | 中文 | 一句话说法 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ballbot | 球形平衡机器人 | A ballbot balances on a single ball, like a person standing on a ball. |
| 2 | omni wheel | 全向轮 | Three omni wheels drive the ball in any horizontal direction. |
| 3 | cascade (PID) | 级联 PID | The outer loop controls position, and the inner loop controls attitude. |
| 4 | position outer loop | 位置外环 | The position loop turns the position error into a tilt reference. |
| 5 | attitude inner loop | 姿态内环 | The attitude loop reacts about six times faster than the outer loop. |
| 6 | bounded reference | 有界参考 | We cap the tilt reference at about 0.1 radian so the inner loop is never overloaded. |
| 7 | output allocation | 输出分配 | The output is allocated to the three wheels through the kinematics. |
| 8 | IMU / AHRS | 惯性测量/姿态解算 | The IMU module provides the attitude at about 100 Hz. |
| 9 | wheel-speed feedback | 轮速反馈 | Wheel speed is used to estimate the velocity for the outer loop. |
| 10 | four-state inverted pendulum | 四状态倒立摆 | The linearised model has position, velocity, tilt and tilt rate. |
| 11 | linearisation | 线性化 | We linearise the model around the upright equilibrium. |
| 12 | LQR | 线性二次型调节器 | LQR makes the trade-off between tilt error and control effort explicit. |
| 13 | state weights (Q, R) | 状态权重 | A higher weight on tilt makes the controller react more strongly to tilt. |
| 14 | actuator saturation | 执行器饱和 | The model helped me identify a case where the command hit the limit. |
| 15 | centre-of-mass offset | 质心偏移 | When both arms reach forward, the centre of mass moves forward by a few centimetres. |
| 16 | gravity feedforward | 重力前馈 | The roll and pitch feedforward shifts the balance point to cancel the arm load. |
| 17 | URDF / Pinocchio | 机器人描述 / 动力学库 | Pinocchio computes the centre of mass from the URDF and the joint states. |
| 18 | fall recovery / safeguards | 跌倒恢复与保护 | We zero the output and lock the fault state when the tilt stays too large for several cycles. |

**常见追问的回应**

1. *Why a cascade rather than one loop?* — The two loops run at different speeds. The fast inner loop keeps the robot upright, and the slow outer loop moves it back to the target position. 中文：两个回路速度不同；快的内环保持直立，慢的外环把它拉回目标位置。
2. *What did LQR do for you?* — It was not deployed on the robot. I used it as a model reference to understand the deployed gains and to diagnose saturation. 中文：它没有上机，我把它当模型参照，用来理解已部署增益并诊断饱和。
3. *How did you verify the feedforward?* — I toggled it on and off under the same fixed arm trajectory and compared the tilt peak and drift. 中文：我在同一固定手臂轨迹下开关前馈，比较倾角峰值和漂移。

## 3. P2 未来动力：VR 遥操作、主从示教与差速底盘

**项目一句话**

> I worked on a dual-arm platform with a differential-drive base. I built VR teleoperation with constrained inverse kinematics, leader–follower teaching with gravity compensation, and wheel-speed control with odometry, all validated on the real hardware through a ROS 2–CAN chain.
>
> 我做了带差速底盘的双臂平台：基于带约束逆运动学的 VR 遥操作、带重力补偿的主从示教，以及带里程计的轮速控制，全部通过 ROS 2–CAN 链在真机上验证。

**核心术语（18 个）**

| # | 英文 | 中文 | 一句话说法 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VR teleoperation | VR 遥操作 | The operator moves the VR controller, and the robot hand follows. |
| 2 | relative pose mapping | 相对位姿映射 | Only the hand motion since the trigger press is applied to the robot. |
| 3 | end-effector target | 末端目标 | The VR pose is converted into an end-effector target in the base frame. |
| 4 | constrained IK | 带约束逆运动学 | The IK respects joint position and velocity limits. |
| 5 | MuJoCo / Mink | 物理引擎 / 微分 IK 库 | Mink builds the IK problem on top of MuJoCo. |
| 6 | singularity | 奇异点 | Near a singularity, a small hand motion can demand a huge joint velocity. |
| 7 | damped least squares | 阻尼最小二乘 | Damping bounds the joint velocity near singularities at a small cost in accuracy. |
| 8 | leader–follower | 主从 | The follower arm copies the motion of the leader arm. |
| 9 | gravity compensation | 重力补偿 | The leader arm is compensated so it feels almost weightless. |
| 10 | URDF / KDL | 机器人描述 / 动力学库 | We build the dual-arm dynamics from the URDF with KDL. |
| 11 | position–velocity tracking | 位置速度跟踪 | The follower uses a position term and a velocity term to track the leader. |
| 12 | MIT motor interface | MIT 电机接口 | One frame carries position, velocity, gains and a feedforward torque. |
| 13 | differential-drive kinematics | 差速运动学 | Body velocity is mapped to left and right wheel speeds. |
| 14 | wheel-speed PID | 轮速 PID | The PID closes the loop on encoder feedback on the chassis board. |
| 15 | odometry | 里程计 | Wheel motion is integrated into odometry for both teleoperated and autonomous modes. |
| 16 | ROS 2–CAN chain | ROS 2–CAN 执行链 | ROS 2 commands are converted into CAN frames to the motors. |
| 17 | FDCAN / STM32H7 | CAN 外设 / 单片机 | The chassis firmware sends and receives frames on FDCAN. |
| 18 | timeout and freeze | 超时与冻结 | If the target is older than the timeout, the driver freezes at the current position. |

**常见追问的回应**

1. *Why damped least squares?* — Near a singularity the plain inverse gives huge velocities. Damping bounds the velocity, and the price is a small tracking error. 中文：奇异点附近直接求逆会给出巨大速度；阻尼使速度有界，代价是小的跟踪误差。
2. *Why only compensate part of the gravity on the last joint?* — Its load is the least certain because of the gripper and the handle, so I under-compensate slightly to avoid drifting upward. 中文：它的负载最不确定，我略微欠补偿以避免上漂。
3. *Which part was yours?* — I handled the VR mapping and the IK setup and tuning, the leader gravity compensation and the follower gains, and the differential-drive control and its ROS 2 integration. 中文：我负责 VR 映射与 IK 配置整定、主臂重力补偿与从臂增益、差速控制及其 ROS 2 集成。

相关：[P2 README](../01_项目课程/P2_未来动力双臂遥操作与差速底盘/README.md)、[P2 英文口述](../01_项目课程/P2_未来动力双臂遥操作与差速底盘/99_英文面试口述与深挖问答.md)

## 4. P3 RoboMaster 英雄机器人嵌入式控制

**项目一句话**

> I did the embedded control for our RoboMaster hero robot on an STM32F407 with FreeRTOS and CAN. I was responsible for the gimbal, the launcher and the chassis loops, and the robot won third prize at the 2025 national final.
>
> 我为队里的 RoboMaster 英雄机器人做嵌入式控制，平台是 STM32F407、FreeRTOS 和 CAN。我负责云台、发射和底盘回路，机器人获得 2025 年全国赛三等奖。

**核心术语（18 个）**

| # | 英文 | 中文 | 一句话说法 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | gimbal | 云台 | The gimbal rotates the launcher in pitch and yaw. |
| 2 | cascaded PID | 串级 PID | The outer loop controls the angle; the inner loop controls the angular rate. |
| 3 | IMU / AHRS / Mahony | 姿态解算 | A Mahony filter gives the attitude from the gyro and the accelerometer. |
| 4 | vision target | 视觉目标 | The vision system sends the target error angles, and the controller turns them into continuous commands. |
| 5 | friction wheel | 摩擦轮 | Two friction wheels spin at about 6000 rpm to launch the projectile. |
| 6 | feeder | 供弹机构 | The feeder moves one projectile at a time with a position–velocity dual loop. |
| 7 | variable-gain PID | 变增益 PID | The gain depends on the size of the error. |
| 8 | sliding-mode control | 滑模控制 | It brings the speed back faster after each shot. |
| 9 | muzzle-velocity feedback | 枪口初速反馈 | The referee system reports the speed, and we adjust the target speed in small steps. |
| 10 | Mecanum wheel | 麦克纳姆轮 | Four Mecanum wheels let the chassis move sideways. |
| 11 | power limit | 功率限制 | The chassis must stay within the power limit set by the referee system. |
| 12 | buffer energy | 缓冲能量 | When buffer energy drops, the wheel speeds are scaled down. |
| 13 | power estimation | 功率估计 | We estimate the power demand and scale the wheel speeds before the limit is hit. |
| 14 | FreeRTOS task | 实时任务 | Each loop is a task with its own priority. |
| 15 | CAN bus | CAN 总线 | The motors are on CAN at 1 Mbit/s. |
| 16 | stall detection | 堵转检测 | If the error stays large for about a second, the feeder reverses once. |
| 17 | offline detection | 离线检测 | A source that times out sets its mechanism to the relaxed safe state. |
| 18 | yaw wrap-around | 偏航回绕 | Filtering with sine and cosine avoids the jump at 180 degrees. |

**常见追问的回应**

1. *Why move from PID to sliding mode?* — During continuous fire the friction wheels lose speed on every shot. PID recovered slowly. Sliding-mode control brought the recovery time down in our tests. 中文：连发时每发都会掉速；PID 恢复慢，滑模在测试中缩短了恢复时间。
2. *How did you keep the chassis under the power limit?* — I estimated the power demand from the real-time power and buffer-energy feedback, and then scaled the wheel speeds before the limit was hit. 中文：我根据实时功率和缓冲能量反馈估计需求，在触限前缩放轮速。
3. *What was your role in the 3V3 match?* — The 3V3 result belongs to the team. My part was moving the same gimbal, launcher and chassis control code and tuning flow to that robot. 中文：3V3 成绩属于团队；我负责把同一套控制代码和调参流程移植到那台车。

## 5. P4 OpenHarmony 智能园艺系统

**项目一句话**

> I built a smart gardening system on OpenHarmony: edge vision on an RK3588, an agent decision chain with LangGraph, and a local irrigation controller on a Hi3861, so the whole loop from observation to decision to execution runs end to end.
>
> 我在 OpenHarmony 上做了智能园艺系统：RK3588 上的边缘视觉、LangGraph 智能体决策链、Hi3861 上的本地灌溉控制，实现从观察到决策再到执行的完整闭环。

**核心术语（18 个）**

| # | 英文 | 中文 | 一句话说法 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | edge inference | 边缘推理 | The model runs on the device, close to the camera. |
| 2 | YOLOv5 | 目标检测模型 | We fine-tuned YOLOv5s on our own leaf images. |
| 3 | INT8 quantisation | INT8 量化 | Quantisation made the model smaller and faster with a small accuracy loss. |
| 4 | RKNN | 瑞芯微模型格式 | We converted the model to RKNN to run on the NPU. |
| 5 | calibration set | 校准集 | About 200 images, including night-time and fill-light scenes. |
| 6 | letterbox | 等比缩放填边 | Letterbox keeps the aspect ratio and the boxes are restored after decoding. |
| 7 | NMS | 非极大值抑制 | NMS removes duplicate boxes with an IoU threshold around 0.45. |
| 8 | structured observation | 结构化观测 | Each observation has a class, a confidence and a timestamp. |
| 9 | event gate | 事件门限 | Three consecutive frames must agree before an event is reported. |
| 10 | LangGraph | 决策图框架 | The decision chain is a graph of nodes with a shared state. |
| 11 | rule-based fast path | 规则快速通道 | Extreme states are handled by rules, without waiting for the model. |
| 12 | whitelist | 白名单 | Only whitelisted actions can be issued to the device. |
| 13 | recommendation / constrained request | 建议 / 受约束请求 | The agent only recommends or makes a constrained request. |
| 14 | pump state machine | 水泵状态机 | The pump has independent start and stop thresholds. |
| 15 | hysteresis | 迟滞 | The gap between the two thresholds prevents rapid switching. |
| 16 | manual override | 手动优先 | A manual command always has priority over the automatic mode. |
| 17 | ADC calibration | ADC 标定 | A two-point calibration maps the raw reading to a moisture percentage. |
| 18 | traceable system state | 可追溯系统状态 | Every decision is stored with the inputs that led to it. |

**常见追问的回应**

1. *Why INT8?* — The NPU is built for 8-bit integers, so the model is smaller and faster. We checked the accuracy loss on a validation set. 中文：NPU 针对 8 位整型设计，模型更小更快；我们在验证集上核对了精度损失。
2. *Why does the pump run locally instead of through the LLM?* — Irrigation must work even when the network or the model fails, and a local state machine is simple and predictable. 中文：网络或模型失效时灌溉也必须工作，本地状态机简单且可预测。
3. *How do you stop the LLM from doing something unsafe?* — The LLM can only make recommendations or constrained requests, and every request goes through a whitelist and a policy check. 中文：LLM 只能建议或提受约束请求，每个请求都要过白名单和策略校验。

## 6. P5 车式移动机器人自主导航

**项目一句话**

> I built the algorithm chain for a car-like robot, from multi-sensor localisation and obstacle-aware planning to MPC tracking, and it navigated autonomously on the real vehicle.
>
> 我做了车式机器人的算法链：从多传感器定位、避障规划到 MPC 跟踪，并在真车上实现自主导航。

**核心术语（18 个）**

| # | 英文 | 中文 | 一句话说法 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | car-like / Ackermann | 车式/阿克曼 | The robot steers its front wheels and drives its rear wheels. |
| 2 | wheel odometry | 轮式里程计 | It integrates the wheel speed with the steering angle. |
| 3 | EKF | 扩展卡尔曼滤波 | The EKF fuses the wheel speed, the yaw rate and the gyro into a smooth local pose. |
| 4 | AMCL | 自适应蒙特卡洛定位 | AMCL corrects the drift on a known map with a particle filter. |
| 5 | map→odom transform | map 到 odom 变换 | The transform absorbs the drift of the odometry. |
| 6 | pose jump | 位姿跳变 | We fixed jumps by unifying timestamps, frames and motion direction. |
| 7 | timestamp alignment | 时间戳对齐 | LiDAR timestamps were moved to the scan start and compensated. |
| 8 | signed speed | 带符号速度 | The reverse direction was lost in the speed message, so odometry drifted. |
| 9 | costmap / footprint | 代价地图 / 车体轮廓 | The footprint is a rectangle that the planner treats as a constraint. |
| 10 | Hybrid A* | 混合 A* | It searches over x, y and heading, so the path respects the turning radius. |
| 11 | minimum turning radius | 最小转弯半径 | We plan with about 0.8 metre, slightly above the physical minimum. |
| 12 | TEB | 时间弹性带 | TEB optimises a time-parameterised local trajectory. |
| 13 | homotopy class | 同伦类 | TEB can keep several classes, for example passing an obstacle on the left or the right. |
| 14 | MPC | 模型预测控制 | MPC solves a constrained problem over a short horizon at each step. |
| 15 | kinematic bicycle model | 运动学自行车模型 | The state is x, y, heading and speed; the inputs are acceleration and steering angle. |
| 16 | receding horizon | 滚动时域 | Only the first control is applied, and then the problem is solved again. |
| 17 | delay compensation | 延迟补偿 | We propagate the state forward by the known actuation delay. |
| 18 | reference smoothness | 参考平滑性 | We add a rate penalty and a selection hysteresis so the steering does not twitch. |

**常见追问的回应**

1. *Why both EKF and AMCL?* — The EKF gives a smooth, continuous pose for control, but it drifts. AMCL uses the map to correct the drift, but its output can jump. Together we keep the control in the odom frame and let the map→odom transform carry the correction. 中文：EKF 平滑连续但会漂；AMCL 用地图纠漂但输出会跳。控制放在 odom 系，纠正量由 map→odom 变换承载。
2. *What was the hardest bug?* — Pose jumps when turning and reversing. The causes were inconsistent timestamps, a lost speed sign, and static transforms. 中文：转弯和倒车时的位姿跳变，原因是时间戳、速度符号和静态变换不一致。
3. *Why MPC rather than pure pursuit?* — MPC handles the speed and steering constraints directly and can use the delay model. 中文：MPC 直接处理速度与转向约束，并能使用延迟模型。

## 7. P6 深开鸿 M-Robots 平台软件

**项目一句话**

> I am a robotics platform intern on the M-Robots multi-robot platform on OpenHarmony. I built the device abstraction, the state management, the coordination over M-DDS and the safe-execution chain that every task plan has to pass.
>
> 我是 M-Robots 多机器人平台（OpenHarmony）的机器人平台实习生，做设备抽象、状态管理、基于 M-DDS 的协同，以及每个任务计划都必须经过的安全执行链。

**核心术语（18 个）**

| # | 英文 | 中文 | 一句话说法 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | unified capability model | 统一能力模型 | Sensors, actuators and controllers are described by what they can do. |
| 2 | device abstraction | 设备抽象 | One interface hides the differences between hardware. |
| 3 | command mapping | 命令映射 | ROS and ROS 2 motion commands are mapped to low-level drivers. |
| 4 | code reuse across hardware | 跨硬件复用 | The same motion-control code runs on different robots. |
| 5 | robot state management | 机器人状态管理 | The state layer gives control and task layers a consistent, trusted state. |
| 6 | timestamp standardisation | 时间戳标准化 | All sources are put on one clock before use. |
| 7 | coordinate-frame consistency | 坐标系一致 | Every measurement is expressed in a declared frame. |
| 8 | validity check | 有效性检查 | Stale or implausible data is marked invalid. |
| 9 | M-DDS | M-DDS 通信 | The platform exchanges states and tasks between robots over M-DDS. |
| 10 | device discovery | 设备发现 | A new robot appears in the network automatically. |
| 11 | capability registration | 能力注册 | A device registers what it can do. |
| 12 | state synchronisation | 状态同步 | Every node sees the same robot state. |
| 13 | task dispatch | 任务分发 | A task goes to the robot with the right capability. |
| 14 | action proposal | 动作提案 | A planner proposes an action; it does not command the robot directly. |
| 15 | policy check | 策略校验 | The policy layer accepts, modifies or rejects a proposal. |
| 16 | execution | 控制执行 | Only an accepted proposal reaches the motion interface. |
| 17 | state feedback | 状态回传 | The result is returned so that the planner knows what actually happened. |
| 18 | safe-execution chain | 安全执行链 | It separates deciding from acting. |

**常见追问的回应**

1. *Why can't the AI planner send commands directly?* — A plan can be wrong or out of date. The policy check makes sure it fits the robot's real state and limits before anything moves. 中文：计划可能错误或过期；策略校验确保它符合机器人真实状态和限制后才动。
2. *What is the unified capability model for?* — It lets us write motion code once and reuse it across different hardware, because the code depends on capabilities, not on devices. 中文：让运动代码写一次就能跨硬件复用，因为代码依赖能力而不是设备。
3. *Can you share the details of the internal platform?* — I can describe the design ideas and my part. I would rather not go into internal details of the company's platform. 中文：我可以讲设计思想和我的部分；公司平台的内部细节不便展开。

## 8. P7 可变腿长轮腿机器人

**项目一句话**

> I worked on balance control for a wheel-legged robot whose leg length changes: a gain-scheduled LQR, virtual model control on the legs, and on-robot tuning on an STM32H7 with FreeRTOS and FDCAN.
>
> 我做了腿长可变的轮腿机器人平衡控制：增益调度 LQR、腿部虚拟模型控制，以及在 STM32H7（FreeRTOS、FDCAN）上的真机调参。

**核心术语（18 个）**

| # | 英文 | 中文 | 一句话说法 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | wheel-legged robot | 轮腿机器人 | It combines the speed of wheels and the adaptability of legs. |
| 2 | variable leg length | 可变腿长 | The leg changes length while the body stays balanced. |
| 3 | state vector | 状态向量 | The state has leg angle, its rate, position, speed, body pitch and its rate. |
| 4 | gain-scheduled LQR | 增益调度 LQR | The LQR gains are fitted as polynomials of the leg length. |
| 5 | gain scheduling | 增益调度 | The controller switches its behaviour with the operating point. |
| 6 | polynomial fit | 多项式拟合 | A cubic polynomial keeps the gains smooth between design points. |
| 7 | wheel-hub torque | 轮毂力矩 | The wheel torque balances and moves the robot. |
| 8 | hip torque | 髋关节力矩 | The hip torque handles the leg angle and the body pitch. |
| 9 | closed-chain kinematics | 闭链运动学 | The five-bar leg needs a closed-chain solution. |
| 10 | virtual leg | 虚拟腿 | The virtual leg is the line from the hip to the wheel centre. |
| 11 | VMC | 虚拟模型控制 | VMC maps a virtual force and torque to the joint torques through the Jacobian. |
| 12 | support force | 支撑力 | It is estimated from the joint torques and used for ground-contact detection. |
| 13 | leg-length PID | 腿长 PID | PID plus a force feedforward holds the commanded height. |
| 14 | force feedforward | 力前馈 | It carries the body weight so that the PID only corrects the error. |
| 15 | ground-contact transition | 接地切换 | At take-off, the wheel torque is set to zero. |
| 16 | torque limit | 力矩限制 | When the limit is reached, the effective gain drops. |
| 17 | state inconsistency | 状态不一致 | Unsynchronised IMU and joint data caused slow creeping. |
| 18 | on-robot tuning | 真机调参 | We started with a low gain and a safety support. |

**常见追问的回应**

1. *Why schedule the gains by leg length?* — The dynamics change with the leg length, so one fixed gain loses margin away from the design point. 中文：动力学随腿长变化，固定增益在设计点之外裕度明显下降。
2. *What was the worst failure?* — A step in the velocity command hit the torque limit and the robot fell. We fixed it with a ramp, an acceleration limit and a lower weight on velocity. 中文：速度指令阶跃撞上力矩限制导致倒下；用斜坡、加速度限制和降低速度权重解决。
3. *How is this different from the course code you studied?* — Mine uses six states and two outputs, schedules by leg length, and estimates the state from the IMU, wheel speeds and joint feedback; the public course code is a simpler four-state version. 中文：我的是六状态两输出、按腿长调度、融合估计状态；公开课程代码是更简单的四状态版本。
