# 面试英语应急与礼貌用语

> **一句话定位**：面试里最容易慌的不是难题，而是“没听懂”“卡壳了”“说错了”“网络出问题了”。本章把这些突发状况分成 12 种场景，每种给出 6–10 句可以直接说的礼貌英文（带中文译文），让你在紧张时不会沉默、不会失礼，也不会为了不丢脸而乱编。

**本章学完你能……**

- 在没听懂、听不清时，礼貌地请对方重复或换个说法；
- 在卡壳、忘词、想不起数字时，用固定句型争取时间并稳住节奏；
- 在说错话后，从容地纠正自己；在面试官说错或记错时，礼貌地澄清；
- 应对网络、音视频故障；用得体的开场寒暄和结束语给面试画上句号。

**前置知识**：[08_高频句型库](./08_高频句型库.md) 中 F、G、H 类。

## 1. 总原则：礼貌、简短、不道歉过度

1. **一句话解决问题**：不要为一个小问题连说三个 sorry。一个 “Sorry” 加一个请求就够了。
2. **用 Could / Would**：Could you … ? 比 Can you … ? 更礼貌。
3. **不要说 “I don't know”就停**：后面接 “but I can tell you …”。
4. **说清楚需要什么**：重复、慢一点、换说法、举例。
5. **保持微笑与语气平稳**：面试官更在意你是否能沟通，而非是否零失误。
6. **不要编造**：不确定时用 “about / I believe / if I remember correctly”。

> **面试要点**：沉默超过 3 秒就用缓冲句；但不要用一连串的 “um”——一个短暂停顿加一句缓冲就够。

## 2. 没听懂 / 听不清（1–15）

1. Sorry, I didn't catch that. — 抱歉，我没听清。
2. Sorry, I didn't quite catch the last part. — 抱歉，最后一部分我没听清。
3. I'm sorry, I didn't understand the question. Could you rephrase it? — 抱歉，我没理解这个问题，能换个说法吗？
4. Could you put that in a different way, please? — 能换个方式表达吗？
5. I'm not sure I understand what you mean by "[stability]". — 我不太确定你说的“[稳定性]”是什么意思。
6. Do you mean [the mathematical stability] or [how stable the robot was in practice]? — 你是指[数学上的稳定性]，还是[机器人实际有多稳]？
7. Could you give me an example of what you're looking for? — 你能举个例子说明你想了解什么吗？
8. Sorry, the audio cut out for a moment. Could you repeat the last sentence? — 抱歉，声音断了一下，能重复最后一句吗？
9. I only caught the first half of the question. Could you say it again? — 我只听到了问题的前半部分，能再说一遍吗？
10. Just to make sure I understood, you're asking about [the delay], correct? — 为确保我理解了，你问的是[延迟]，对吗？
11. Is it about [the theory] or [my own implementation]? — 是关于[理论]还是[我自己的实现]？
12. I think I understand, but let me check: [you want to know why we used two loops]. — 我想我明白了，但让我确认一下：[你想知道为什么用两个回路]。
13. Sorry, could you speak a little more slowly? I want to be sure I follow. — 抱歉，能说慢一点吗？我想确保跟上。
14. I'm not familiar with that term. Could you explain it briefly? — 我对那个术语不熟悉，能简单解释一下吗？
15. Thank you, that makes it clearer. — 谢谢，这样清楚多了。

## 3. 请求重复 / 慢一点 / 拼写（16–28）

16. Could you repeat the question, please? — 能重复一下问题吗？
17. Would you mind repeating that? — 您介意重复一下吗？
18. Could you say that again, a little more slowly? — 能再说一遍，慢一点吗？
19. Sorry, one more time, please. — 抱歉，请再说一次。
20. Could you spell that word for me? — 能帮我拼一下那个词吗？
21. How do you spell that? — 那个词怎么拼？
22. Is that "[K-A-L-M-A-N]"? — 是 “K-A-L-M-A-N” 吗？
23. Could you write it in the chat? — 能打在聊天框里吗？
24. Could you repeat the second part of the question? — 能重复问题的第二部分吗？
25. I'd like to confirm: you said [three loops], is that right? — 我想确认：您说的是[三个回路]，对吗？
26. Sorry, did you say [fifteen] or [fifty]? — 抱歉，您说的是 fifteen 还是 fifty？
27. Could you give me the number once more? — 能再给我一次那个数字吗？
28. Thank you for repeating. — 谢谢您重复。

## 4. 卡壳 / 需要时间（29–42）

29. That's a good question. Let me think for a moment. — 这个问题很好，让我想一下。
30. Give me a second to organise my thoughts. — 请给我一秒整理思路。
31. Let me think about how to explain this clearly. — 让我想想怎么讲清楚。
32. Hmm, let me put it another way. — 嗯，让我换种说法。
33. I need a moment to recall the details. — 我需要一点时间回忆细节。
34. May I take a few seconds to think? — 我可以花几秒钟思考吗？
35. Let me start from the beginning, so my answer is clear. — 让我从头说起，这样我的回答更清楚。
36. I'd like to answer in two steps: first the idea, then the details. — 我想分两步回答：先说思路，再说细节。
37. Sorry, I lost my train of thought. Let me restart. — 抱歉，我思路断了，让我重新开始。
38. Let me go back to the main point. — 让我回到主要观点。
39. Sorry, I went off topic. The key point is [the timing]. — 抱歉，我跑题了。关键点是[时序]。
40. What I mean is [the output is limited]. — 我的意思是[输出被限制]。
41. To put it simply, [we avoid asking for too much]. — 简单说，[我们避免要求过多]。
42. Let me try again with a simpler explanation. — 让我用更简单的方式再试一次。

## 5. 忘词 / 想不起英文（43–54）

43. How do you say "[抖振]" in English? I think the word is "chattering". — “抖振”英文怎么说？我想那个词是 chattering。
44. I can't remember the exact word, but it means [the output switches very fast]. — 我想不起确切的词，但意思是[输出切换得非常快]。
45. The word is on the tip of my tongue. — 那个词就在嘴边。
46. Let me describe it in another way: [it is the error between the measurement and the prediction]. — 让我换种方式描述：[它是测量和预测之间的差]。
47. It's like [a spring and a damper]. — 它像[弹簧加阻尼器]。
48. What I'm looking for is the term for [the delay in the loop]. — 我在找[回路中的延迟]的术语。
49. I'm not sure about the English term, but in Chinese we call it "[积分饱和]", which means [the integral keeps growing when the output is saturated]. — 我不确定英文术语，但中文叫“积分饱和”，意思是[输出饱和时积分项持续增长]。
50. If I remember correctly, the term is "integral windup". — 如果我没记错，术语是 integral windup。
51. Is "[saturation]" the right word here? — 这里用 saturation 对吗？
52. Let me use a simpler word: [limit]. — 让我用个更简单的词：[limit]。
53. I'll say it in a simple way, and please correct me if the term is wrong. — 我用简单的方式说，术语若不对请纠正我。
54. Thank you for the word. — 谢谢您给我的词。

## 6. 想不起数字或细节（55–66）

55. I don't have the exact number in front of me, but it was on the order of [a few milliseconds]. — 我手边没有确切数字，但量级是[几毫秒]。
56. If I remember correctly, [the loop ran at about 100 hertz]. — 如果我没记错，[回路大约 100 赫兹]。
57. I think it was around [fifteen milliseconds], but I would need to check the log. — 我想大约是[15 毫秒]，但需要查日志确认。
58. I don't remember the exact value, but I remember the trend: [it dropped clearly]. — 我记不清确切值，但记得趋势：[明显下降了]。
59. Let me say "about" because I don't want to give a number I can't back up. — 我说“大约”，因为不想给出无法佐证的数字。
60. The exact figure is in my logs; the order of magnitude is [centimetres]. — 确切数字在我的日志里，量级是[厘米]。
61. I'd rather not guess the number. What I can say is [how I measured it]. — 我不想猜数字，能说的是[我怎么测的]。
62. I measured it by [recording the signal and computing the difference]. — 我通过[记录信号并计算差值]来测量。
63. I know the setting was [low at the start and higher later], but not the exact values. — 我知道设置是[开始低、后来高]，但不记得确切值。
64. I can send you the details after the interview, if that helps. — 如果有帮助，面试后我可以把细节发给您。
65. That detail was in the team's configuration file. — 这个细节在团队的配置文件里。
66. Would it be okay if I describe the method instead of the exact value? — 我描述方法而不是确切值可以吗？

> **注意**：不要随口编数字。面试官可能追问“怎么测的”，编造的数字会让后面的回答出现矛盾。

## 7. 纠正自己（67–78）

67. Sorry, let me correct that. — 抱歉，让我更正一下。
68. Sorry, I misspoke. I meant [the inner loop]. — 抱歉，我说错了，我的意思是[内环]。
69. Let me rephrase that. — 让我重新表述。
70. Actually, I'd like to change what I said: [it was the position loop that was slower]. — 其实我想改一下刚才说的：[是位置环更慢]。
71. I made a mistake there. The correct statement is [the gain depends on the leg length]. — 我那里说错了，正确的说法是[增益取决于腿长]。
72. Wait, let me think again. — 等一下，让我再想想。
73. I said [fifty], but I meant [fifteen]. — 我说了 fifty，但意思是 fifteen。
74. Let me restate that more precisely. — 让我更精确地重述。
75. To be more accurate, [it is a linearised model, not the full nonlinear one]. — 更准确地说，[是线性化模型，不是完整非线性模型]。
76. I should clarify that [LQR was a reference and was not deployed]. — 我应该澄清，[LQR 是参照，没有部署]。
77. I'd like to add one correction: [the 3V3 result belongs to the team]. — 我想补充一个更正：[3V3 成绩属于团队]。
78. Thanks for letting me correct that. — 谢谢允许我更正。

> **提示**：纠正自己显示的是诚实和严谨，面试官通常会加分，不会扣分。

## 8. 礼貌纠正对方 / 澄清误解（79–90）

79. If I understood correctly, [LQR was deployed on the robot]. Actually, I used it as a model reference. — 如果我理解对的话，[LQR 部署在了机器人上]。其实我把它当作模型参照。
80. Just to clarify, [the Mecanum chassis was on the hero robot, not on the 3V3 robot]. — 澄清一下，[麦克纳姆底盘在英雄机器人上，不是 3V3 机器人]。
81. That's close, but not exactly what I did. — 很接近，但不完全是我做的。
82. May I add a small clarification? — 我可以补充一个小澄清吗？
83. I think there may be a small misunderstanding. I worked on [the control], and a teammate worked on [the vision]. — 我想可能有个小误会：我做[控制]，队友做[视觉]。
84. That is a common approach, but in my project I used [a different one]. — 这是常见方法，但我的项目里用的是[另一种]。
85. If I may, I'd like to restate what the project covers. — 如果可以，我想重述一下项目涵盖的内容。
86. I'm not sure that's what I said, but let me explain again. — 我不确定那是不是我说的，让我再解释一下。
87. In my experience, [the delay was smaller than that]. — 据我的经验，[延迟比那个小]。
88. That's an interesting point. In my case, [the data was already filtered]. — 这是个有意思的点。在我的情况里，[数据已经滤波了]。
89. I respectfully see it a bit differently. — 我有稍微不同的看法。
90. Thank you for the feedback. I'll keep that in mind. — 谢谢反馈，我会记住。

## 9. 不方便透露 / 不属于自己的部分（91–100）

91. That part was handled by a teammate, so I can only describe the interface. — 那部分是队友负责的，我只能描述接口。
92. I'd prefer not to go into the internal details of the company's platform, but I can explain the design idea. — 我不便展开公司平台的内部细节，但可以讲设计思想。
93. Those details are covered by the company's confidentiality rules. — 那些细节涉及公司的保密规定。
94. I can describe what my part did and how I tested it. — 我可以描述我的部分做了什么以及如何测试。
95. I can't share the exact numbers, but I can describe the order of magnitude. — 我不能分享确切数字，但可以描述量级。
96. This was a team project, and my own contribution was [the control loops]. — 这是团队项目，我自己的贡献是[控制回路]。
97. I was not responsible for [the mechanical design], so I'll focus on [the control]. — 我不负责[机械设计]，所以我聚焦[控制]。
98. I only worked on [the algorithm side]; [the hardware] was designed by others. — 我只做[算法部分]，[硬件]是别人设计的。
99. I want to be accurate about what I did myself. — 我想对自己做的部分保持准确。
100. If you'd like, I can explain how that module works in general. — 如果您愿意，我可以讲这类模块的一般工作原理。

## 10. 技术故障：网络、麦克风、屏幕（101–115）

101. Sorry, I think my connection is unstable. Could you hear me clearly? — 抱歉，我想我的网络不稳定，您能听清我吗？
102. Can you hear me? — 您能听到我吗？
103. Can you see my screen? — 您能看到我的屏幕吗？
104. Sorry, I think you're on mute. — 抱歉，您好像静音了。
105. I think my microphone isn't working. Let me check. — 我想我的麦克风坏了，我检查一下。
106. Could you give me a moment to reconnect? — 能给我一点时间重连吗？
107. Sorry, the video froze. Could you repeat that? — 抱歉，视频卡住了，能重复一下吗？
108. I'm having a little trouble with the audio. Is it okay if I turn off the video for a moment? — 我的音频有点问题，我可以暂时关闭视频吗？
109. Let me switch to my phone. — 让我换到手机上。
110. I'm back. Where were we? — 我回来了。我们说到哪儿了？
111. Let me share my screen in a second. — 我马上共享屏幕。
112. Sorry about the interruption. — 抱歉打断了。
113. Could we continue? I can answer the last question again. — 我们可以继续吗？我可以重新回答最后一个问题。
114. Is the sound quality okay on your side? — 您那边音质还好吗？
115. Thank you for your patience. — 谢谢您的耐心。

## 11. 开场寒暄与过渡（116–128）

116. Good morning. Thank you for having me. — 早上好，谢谢您接待我。
117. Good afternoon. It's a pleasure to meet you. — 下午好，很高兴见到您。
118. I'm doing well, thank you. How are you? — 我很好，谢谢。您呢？
119. I'm a little nervous, but I'm excited to be here. — 我有点紧张，但很高兴来这里。
120. Thank you for the opportunity. — 谢谢这个机会。
121. Would you like me to start with a short introduction? — 您希望我先做个简短的自我介绍吗？
122. Where would you like me to start? — 您希望我从哪里开始？
123. Shall I begin? — 我可以开始了吗？
124. Let me introduce myself briefly. — 让我简单自我介绍一下。
125. Would you like me to go into more detail on any part? — 您希望我对哪部分多讲一些吗？
126. Please feel free to interrupt me if you have a question. — 如果您有问题，请随时打断我。
127. I'll try to keep it short. — 我会尽量讲短一点。
128. Thank you. I'm ready for the next question. — 谢谢，我准备好了，请问下一个问题。

## 12. 结束语与感谢（129–145）

129. Thank you very much for your time. — 非常感谢您的时间。
130. Thank you for the interesting questions. — 谢谢这些有意思的问题。
131. I enjoyed our conversation. — 我很享受我们的交流。
132. I learned a lot from your questions. — 我从您的问题中学到很多。
133. Is there anything else you'd like to know about my projects? — 关于我的项目，您还有想了解的吗？
134. May I ask one or two questions about the programme? — 我可以问一两个关于项目的问题吗？
135. I'd like to ask about [the research directions of the faculty]. — 我想问问[教师的研究方向]。
136. What kind of support is available for [students who want to do research]? — [想做研究的学生]有哪些支持？
137. How do students usually choose their projects or supervisors? — 学生通常如何选择项目或导师？
138. I'm very interested in this programme and I hope to join. — 我对这个项目很感兴趣，希望能加入。
139. I look forward to hearing from you. — 期待您的回复。
140. Thank you again, and have a great day. — 再次感谢，祝您愉快。
141. It was nice meeting you. — 很高兴见到您。
142. Please let me know if you need any further information. — 如需更多信息请告诉我。
143. I'd be glad to send additional materials if helpful. — 如有帮助，我很乐意发送额外材料。
144. Goodbye, and thank you. — 再见，谢谢。
145. Have a good evening. — 祝您晚上愉快。

## 13. AI 视频面试专用（146–155）

AI 视频面试没有人实时回应，也没有追问，所以要自己控制节奏。

146. Hello, my name is Yongyi Lai, and I'm applying for [the MSc programme]. — 您好，我叫 Yongyi Lai，申请[硕士项目]。
147. I'll answer this question in three parts. — 我分三部分回答这个问题。
148. First, the background. Second, my method. Third, the result. — 第一，背景；第二，方法；第三，结果。
149. In short, [I built a cascade controller and used a model as a reference]. — 简言之，[我做了级联控制器并用模型作参照]。
150. Let me add one more point. — 让我再补充一点。
151. That is my answer. Thank you. — 这就是我的回答，谢谢。
152. I would like to restart my answer. — 我想重新开始回答。（如果平台允许重录）
153. Let me summarise in one sentence: [timing and frames mattered most]. — 让我用一句话总结：[时序和坐标系最重要]。
154. I have about thirty seconds left, so I'll finish with [the key result]. — 我还剩约 30 秒，所以用[关键结果]收尾。
155. Thank you for listening. — 谢谢聆听。

> **提示**：对着摄像头而不是屏幕说话；每题开始前停顿 1 秒；语速比平时略慢；回答完整结构：一句结论 + 2–3 个要点 + 一句收尾。

## 本章要点速记

1. 请求重复用 “Could you repeat …?”，确认理解用 “Just to confirm …”。
2. 卡壳用缓冲句，但控制在一句话内，随后立刻回到主线。
3. 忘词时用“描述 + 类比”绕开，不要停下。
4. 想不起数字：量级、趋势、测量方法，不要编。
5. 说错就纠正：Sorry, let me correct that.
6. 澄清对方误解时用 “If I understood correctly …” 开头，语气委婉。
7. 不属于自己的部分明确说 “handled by a teammate”。
8. 网络故障：先确认对方能不能听到，再请求重复。
9. 结束时感谢、提问、再感谢，保持积极。
10. AI 视频面试：结构清晰、对着摄像头、放慢语速。

## 自测题

1. 你没听清最后一个词，礼貌地说一句。（Sorry, I didn't catch the last word. Could you repeat it?）
2. 你想不起 “integral windup” 的英文，怎么绕开？（描述：it is when the integral keeps growing while the output is saturated）
3. 面试官说 “你把 LQR 部署到机器人上了对吧？” 你如何委婉澄清？（I used it as a model reference and didn't deploy it on the robot.）
4. 你说错了数字，如何更正？（Sorry, I misspoke. I meant fifteen.）
5. 网络断了，重连后第一句说什么？（Sorry about that. Can you hear me now?）
